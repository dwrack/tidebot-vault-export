## CONTENT (Markdown body)

```markdown
We get some version of this question a few times a month, usually from someone who Googled us, found two different websites, and isn't sure which one is real.

Fair question. Here's the actual answer, no spin.

## The short answer

Yes. Austin Kayak Tours is Lone Star Kayak Tours LLC. Same company, same dock, same guides. We changed the public-facing name to Austin Kayak Tours because it's clearer about what we do and where we do it. The legal entity behind the business never changed, and neither did anything about how the tours run.

## Why two names exist for one company

Before the rebrand, the business operated as Lone Star Kayak Tours, with its own website at lonestarkayaktours.com. When we moved to the Austin Kayak Tours name, the new site went live at austinkayaktours.com, but the old site never got fully redirected. It's still out there, still indexed, still showing up in search results next to the current one.

That's on us, not a mystery, and not a scam. It's a rebrand where the technical cleanup, pointing the old domain permanently to the new one, hasn't fully happened yet. If you land on lonestarkayaktours.com, you found the old front door to the same business. austinkayaktours.com is the one we actively maintain and the one to book through.

## What actually changed, and what didn't

The name changed. The website changed. A few things about how we present the business online changed.

What didn't change: the launch point at 200 E Riverside Dr on Lady Bird Lake, the guides, the tours themselves (skyline, bat bridge, rentals), the phone number, 512-766-5640, and the email, austinkayaktours@gmail.com. If you booked with Lone Star Kayak Tours two years ago and you're booking with Austin Kayak Tours today, you're dealing with the same people.

## How to know you're booking the real thing

Austin has grown as a kayak-tour market, which is good for the city and mildly annoying for anyone trying to figure out who's who online. A few names in local search results are close enough to ours that people mix them up, sometimes by a single word.

Here's the honest way to check:

- **Confirm the launch address.** We launch from 200 E Riverside Dr. If a listing shows a different location for "Austin Kayak Tours," call before you book.
- **Book through austinkayaktours.com or FareHarbor directly.** That's our actual booking system, not a third-party reseller page.
- **Call if anything feels off.** 512-766-5640 goes to us. If someone answers differently than you expect, you're probably not talking to us.
- **Check for our name in the confirmation email.** Our confirmations reference Austin Kayak Tours and the Lady Bird Lake launch point specifically.

We're not going to name-check every operator in the city here. Most of them are running honest businesses too. The point of this page isn't to throw shade, it's to make sure you end up on the tour you actually meant to book.

## If you already booked under the old name

If you have an old confirmation from Lone Star Kayak Tours, it's still us. The tour details, the launch point, and the contact number are unchanged. If you're unsure whether a booking is current, call 512-766-5640 and we'll sort it out directly instead of leaving you guessing.

If you're planning a first trip and just want to skip the naming confusion entirely: book through [our sunset bat tour](/bat-bridge-kayak-tour/) or [kayak and paddle board rentals](/rental/) directly on austinkayaktours.com. Same company either way, we'd just rather you find us on the first try.
```
