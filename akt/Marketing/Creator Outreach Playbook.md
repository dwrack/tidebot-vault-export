# Creator Outreach Playbook: Austin Kayak Tours

**Owner:** Connor Champaign
**Business Instagram:** @austinkayaktours (or current handle)

---

## What This Is

Proactive outreach to local content creators, offering them a free kayak tour in exchange for nothing. No contract, no posting requirements, no talking points. If they make something cool, great. If they just enjoy the paddle, that's fine too.

This is different from the UGC Repost Playbook (which is reactive, finding content that already exists). This is about building relationships with creators before they've ever been in your kayak.

## Why This Works for Austin Kayak Tours

We sell the experience creators want to film. Kayaking on Lady Bird Lake at sunset, watching 1.5 million bats fly out from under the Congress Avenue Bridge. That's content. The bat bridge tour is inherently viral: it's weird, it's dramatic, it looks incredible on camera, and most people don't even know it exists until they see it on social.

Beyond the bats, the downtown Austin skyline from the water at golden hour is the kind of backdrop creators actively seek out.

The cost is nearly zero. An empty seat on a tour that's going out anyway costs us nothing. The content a creator produces from that seat would cost $500-2,000 to produce ourselves.

## Who to Target

### The Sweet Spot: Micro-Influencers (2K-50K followers)

Don't chase big accounts. You want local creators who:
- Live in or frequently visit Austin
- Post outdoor, lifestyle, date night, sunset/nature, or city-life content
- Have real engagement (comments from real people, not bot spam)
- Show original storytelling, not just lip-syncs and trending audio reposts
- Would genuinely enjoy a kayak tour at sunset

## How to Reach Out

### DM from the Business Instagram Account

Not your personal account. Not email. A direct message from the business page.

### First DM: Cold Outreach

Keep it short. No pitch deck energy.

**Template A: Simple invite:**

> Hey [name]! Love your content, especially [reference a specific recent post]. We run kayak tours on Lady Bird Lake here in Austin, including a sunset tour where you watch 1.5 million bats fly out from under Congress Bridge. Pretty wild. We'd love to have you out. No strings, no posting requirements, just come enjoy it. Want us to set something up?

**Template B: For travel creators:**

> Hey [name]! Saw you're hitting Austin soon. We run sunset kayak tours on Lady Bird Lake where you paddle right up to the Congress bat bridge at flight time. Totally unique, looks insane on camera. We'd love to have you out while you're here. Free, no catch. Let us know if you want us to hold a spot.

**Template C: For creators who posted about a competitor or similar experience:**

> Hey [name]! Saw your [post about Lady Bird Lake/similar activity]. We run guided kayak tours out there including a bat bridge sunset tour, you paddle right under Congress Ave as the bats come out. Would love to have you out. On us, no strings. Interested?

### Rules for Outreach
- **Reference something specific** about their content. "Love your content" alone sounds automated.
- **Don't ask them to post.** The moment you say "in exchange for a post," it becomes transactional and the content suffers.
- **One follow-up max.** If they don't reply after 3-5 days, send one casual follow-up. After that, move on.

**Follow-up DM (if no reply after 5 days):**

> Hey just bumping this in case it got buried. No pressure either way!

### If They Say Yes

> Awesome! When works for you? We run tours [days/times]. The bat bridge tour goes out at sunset, so timing is weather-dependent, but I'll get you on a good one. You can bring a friend too if you want.

Then:
- Hold the spot, confirm the day before
- Let the guide know a creator is coming (just so they're aware)
- After the tour, send a quick thank you DM: "Hope you had a good time! No ask from us, just glad you came out."

### If They Post Something

- Like it immediately
- Leave a genuine comment (something specific about their content, not "Thanks for sharing!")
- Repost using the UGC Playbook process (ask permission, download, repost with tag)
- Flag top performers for ads per the UGC playbook
- Add them to your "relationship" tier for future invites

### If They Don't Post

That's fine. Don't follow up asking where their post is. The relationship still exists. They had a good experience, they'll mention you organically at some point, and you can invite them back next season.

### Relationship Tiers

- **One-timers**: Came, enjoyed it, maybe posted, no ongoing relationship. That's fine.
- **Repeat creators**: Loved it, posted, want to come back. Invite them quarterly or for seasonal bat emergence (March-October is bat season).
- **Brand friends**: Genuinely love what you do, mention you unprompted, bring friends. These are gold. Give them first access to everything.
