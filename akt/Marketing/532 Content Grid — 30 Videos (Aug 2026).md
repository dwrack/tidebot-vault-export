## The 5 topics

1. **Spontaneity** — decide at 11am, on the water at 2pm. Low friction is the product.
2. **The bats** — Congress Bridge from the water vs standing room only on the bridge
3. **The skyline from Lady Bird Lake** — the view locals don't have
4. **"Outdoor Austin" vs actual outdoor Austin** — the gap between the brand and the trip
5. **Beginner logistics** — parking, what to wear, how long, is it hard

## The 3 formats

- **Comparison** (easy) — bridge vs water, Barton Springs line vs the lake
- **How-to** (easy) — the friction-removal format, matches the brand
- **POV** (ambitious) — spontaneity is a feeling, POV sells feelings

## The 2 layouts

- **A — Walk-and-talk / talking head at the launch.** Casual, unplanned energy. Match the brand.
- **B — GoPro POV + text on screen.** Sound off.

## Cover type

Rounded sans, warm, casual, lowercase where it fits. This brand should never look like an expedition outfitter.

## Notes

- **The bats are the highest-volume search demand this brand touches and the differentiator writes itself:** everyone else is standing on a bridge shoulder to shoulder. Rows 7-12 should go out first, timed to the season.
- Row 22 is the sharpest line here. Half of Austin moved for the outdoors and doesn't use them. That's the starving crowd, per the ad opener framework.
- Don't oversell. Per the brand story, this is an urban paddle that happens to be beautiful, not a wilderness experience. Cover lines that promise adventure will produce disappointed reviews.
- Boost the highest-**save** post as a social-proof ad.
