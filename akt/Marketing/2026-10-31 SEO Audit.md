# Austin Kayak Tours: SEO Audit, 2026-10-31

**Report only. Nothing was changed on the live site, WordPress, GBP, or any ad account.** Full site audit of austinkayaktours.com.

**Data used (all pulled 2026-10-31):**
- Google Search Console via the google-austin MCP, 2026-08-01 to 2026-10-29 (90 days). GSC connected this time; the four August audits could not reach it.
- Live crawl of all 79 sitemap URLs (status, canonical, robots meta, title, meta description, H1) plus the homepage, 57 blog posts' body links, and the main money pages.
- DataForSEO Maps SERP (mobile, signed-out) and organic SERP.
- GBP read-only: listing details and the latest 40 reviews (30 with text).
- Vault: SEO Action Plan & Blog Pipeline, Ranking Baseline 2026-06-07, the four August audits, staged drafts folder.
- Gaps: GSC `gsc_index_status` returned permission info only (no indexed / not-indexed counts), so index coverage is unverified. Viator/GYG/ChatGPT were not re-sampled (bot-blocked and need the mbp-2 browser lane); the OTA table below carries the 2026-10-16 baseline and is labelled as such. Competitor backlink data was not pulled.

## Executive summary

The bat cluster is working and is the business. The bat tour page is the top GSC page (259 clicks, 11,162 impressions, position 6.7) and the bat-by-kayak queries sit at positions 2.9 to 5.4. Since the 2026-06-07 baseline, "austin bat tours" went from position 23 to 8.4, "bat tour austin" from 19.6 to 8.9, and the /rental/ page went from position 42.8 to 10.1 with impressions up from 647 to 4,774. The June rewrites paid off.

Three things hold it back. (1) The generic head terms are stuck on page one's bottom half with 0.2% to 0.5% CTR ("austin kayak" 1,297 impressions at 7.5, "kayak austin" 1,031 at 7.9, "austin kayaking" 1,651 at 8.9, 14 clicks combined). (2) 55 of 57 blog posts contain zero in-body links to a tour or rental page, including four posts about the bat colony. (3) Duplicate-property sprawl: three exact-duplicate URL pairs on this site, plus lonestarkayaktours.com and austinpaddle.com (same company, same FareHarbor account) both outranking or sharing the SERP; austinpaddle.com's own bat page is #1 organic for "austin bat kayak tour".

Top priorities: ship the schema fixes (30 minutes of copy-paste), 301 the duplicate URLs, and add contextual links from the bat posts to the bat tour page. Mobile speed is poor (see the Tech SEO report filed today).
