# Kayaking on Lake Travis: A Local's Guide

Lake Travis is the big sister of Lady Bird Lake. It's much bigger, deeper, has motorized boats, and gives you a completely different experience: Hill Country shoreline, swimming, cliff jumping, parties.

If you've only kayaked Lady Bird Lake, Travis will feel different. Here's the honest local guide.

## Lake Travis basics

- **Size:** 19,000+ acres at full pool, 64 miles long
- **Drive from downtown:** 30-45 minutes northwest
- **Motorized boats:** allowed (and there are a lot)
- **Swimming:** yes (unlike Lady Bird)
- **Best time:** weekdays in spring or fall
- **Worst time:** summer Saturdays (chaos)

## Where to launch

### Pace Bend Park
Far west end. Multiple coves, free entrance to the park (pay parking). Cliffjumper Cove is famous for cliff diving. Calmer water on the south side of the peninsula.

**Best for:** swimming, cliff jumping, longer day trips.

### Mansfield Dam Park
East side. Real boat ramp, parking lot, picnic areas. Closest to downtown of the major launches.

**Best for:** convenient access, families, first-time Travis paddlers.

### Hippie Hollow
Clothing-optional. Adults only (18+). Limestone cliffs, no beach, paddle from a small launch. Park entrance fee.

**Best for:** experienced kayakers who want something different. Be respectful.

### Devil's Cove
The party cove. Memorial Day through Labor Day, hundreds of pontoon boats and party boats anchor in the cove. Loud, fun, chaotic.

**Best for:** people who want a party scene from a kayak. Not first-timers.

### Sandy Creek Park
Smaller, quieter, west side. Less crowded than Pace Bend.

**Best for:** quiet day, less driving than Pace Bend.

## What to expect on the water

**Wake from motorboats** is the biggest difference vs. Lady Bird Lake. Boat wakes can rock a kayak pretty hard. Stay close to shore in calmer coves.

**Open water** means wind can pick up fast. Check the forecast.

**Distances feel bigger.** You can paddle for an hour and not really go anywhere on Travis. Plan accordingly.

**The water's clear** in most places. You can see the bottom in shallow areas.

## Best routes

### Pace Bend Park loop (3-4 hours)
Launch at the boat ramp, paddle around the peninsula, cliff jumping stops along the way, return.

### Mansfield Dam to Devil's Cove (1-2 hours)
Watch the chaos at Devil's Cove from a kayak. Don't anchor in the cove itself.

### Sandy Creek Park to nowhere (1-2 hours)
A quieter paddle along the west shore. Good for fishing.

## What to bring

- Same as Lady Bird Lake plus:
- Cooler with snacks (you'll be out longer)
- Anchor or rope (so you can stop and swim)
- Floatie if you want to swim and not paddle
- Extra water (more than you think for the longer time on water)

## Rentals on Lake Travis

Fewer on-water rentals than Lady Bird. A few options:
- **Texas Adventure Tours** in west Austin (they shuttle kayaks)
- **REI Outpost** for some rentals if you have a vehicle
- Most paddlers bring their own boat or a roof rack rental

## When to go

**Best:** weekday in late April, May, September, or October. Warm enough to swim, not crowded.

**Decent:** weekdays in summer (avoid weekends entirely).

**Avoid:** weekends Memorial Day through Labor Day.

**Off-season:** winter is doable but cold. The lake is empty but the wind is cold.

## What's better here than Lady Bird Lake

- Swimming (allowed and great)
- Cliff jumping
- Bigger views (Hill Country)
- Less urban
- Camping options at Pace Bend
- Boat-watching (if you're into that)

## What's better at Lady Bird

- No wakes
- Easier for beginners
- Closer to downtown
- The skyline
- The bat colony
- Year-round consistent water level

## When the lake is low

Travis fluctuates dramatically with rainfall. In drought years, certain ramps are unusable. Hippie Hollow can be 30 feet below normal. Check current lake level before you drive out.

## Common questions

**Can you kayak Lake Travis as a beginner?**
Yes, if you stay in calm coves and avoid weekends. Beginners on open water in summer with boat traffic is rough.

**Where can I rent a kayak on Lake Travis?**
Limited on-water rentals. Most paddlers bring their own boat or rent a roof-rack kayak from REI or similar.

**Can you swim in Lake Travis?**
Yes. Unlike Lady Bird Lake, swimming is allowed everywhere on Travis.

**Where's the best cliff jumping on Lake Travis?**
Pace Bend Park (Cliffjumper Cove). Be smart, check water depth, don't drink and jump.

**Is Lake Travis or Lady Bird Lake better for kayaking?**
Different goals. Lady Bird is easier and has the skyline. Travis is bigger and has swimming.
