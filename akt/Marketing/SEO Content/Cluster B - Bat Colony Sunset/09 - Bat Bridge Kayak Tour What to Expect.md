# Bat Bridge Kayak Tour: What to Expect, What to Wear, What to Bring

If you've booked our [sunset bat bridge kayak tour](/bat-bridge-kayak-tour/) or you're trying to decide whether to, here's the actual play-by-play. No marketing copy, just what happens.

## The timeline

**45 minutes before sunset:** arrive at the launch (200 E Riverside Drive). Park, sign the waiver, get fitted for a life jacket and a paddle.

**30 minutes before sunset:** brief safety talk on the dock, basic paddle stroke walkthrough, then you're on the water. The first 10 minutes is just getting comfortable.

**15-20 minutes before sunset:** we paddle west toward the Congress Avenue Bridge. Your guide stops you under or near the bridge and waits with the group.

**0-5 minutes before sunset:** the bats start. In peak summer (July, August) it's a 30-40 minute emergence. In shoulder season (April, May, September, October) it's 10-20 minutes.

**After the bats:** we paddle back east as the sun sets, with the skyline lighting up. The whole thing wraps about 2 hours from launch to land.

## What it actually looks like

You're under the bridge, looking up. The first bats start to drop out of the structure. Then more. Then a steady stream that spirals out and heads east-southeast over your head. You can hear the wings. The whole colony, 1.5 million bats, takes 30+ minutes to clear out at peak.

The skyline behind you is in full sunset mode. The lake is calm. There are 10-15 other paddlers near you, mostly quiet, mostly looking up.

It's one of those experiences that doesn't really translate to a photo. We're not promising you a wilderness moment. We're promising you something you've never seen and probably won't see anywhere else.

## What to wear

- **Quick-dry shorts or athletic pants.** Cotton dries slowly and feels gross.
- **A t-shirt** in summer. A light long-sleeve in spring or fall.
- **Sandals with a back strap** (Tevas, Chacos), or water shoes. No flip-flops.
- **A hat with a strap.** It will blow off otherwise.
- **Sunglasses with a strap or croakies.**
- **Sunscreen, applied before launch.** The sun is brutal at 7pm in July.

In April or October bring an extra layer. After sunset on the water it cools off fast.

## What to bring

- **Water bottle.** Bring more than you think.
- **Phone in a dry bag or floating case.** We sell these at the launch if you forget. Phones in the lake happen every season.
- **Snacks if it's a late tour and you skipped dinner.** Crackers, granola bar, something easy.
- **A small dry towel.**

## What NOT to bring

- Glass containers (banned)
- Alcohol (open container is a ticket)
- Anything you can't afford to lose if it goes overboard
- Big camera bags that won't fit in a dry pouch

## What we provide

- Kayak (single or tandem)
- Paddle
- Life jacket (USCG-approved)
- Dry bag for keys/wallet (small one)
- Brief instruction
- A guide who has done this hundreds of times

## What you'll see beyond the bats

- The Austin skyline lit up at golden hour
- Frost Bank Tower, the Independent, the Austonian
- Other paddlers and rowing teams winding down
- Turtles on logs east of the bridge (not always)
- A sunset most visitors fly home without ever seeing

## Who this tour is right for

- Anyone visiting Austin between March and October who hasn't booked it yet
- Couples
- Families with kids 5+
- Solo travelers who want to do something
- People who'd rather pay $50 than fight for a spot on the bridge

## Who it's not right for

- Wilderness purists (this is downtown)
- People scared of bats (bats fly above, not at, you)
- Anyone hoping to see a wide-open natural setting
- Toddlers under 5 (age limit)

## Common questions

**How long is the bat bridge kayak tour?**
Two hours total, from check-in to off the water.

**Do I need experience to do the bat tour?**
No. Most guests have never kayaked. The water is calm.

**Are the bats actually scary?**
No. They fly above you and head east. They have no interest in you. The "scary" part is built up in pop culture and isn't real on this colony.

**What if I get there and the bats don't come out?**
On rare nights with bad weather, the bats delay or skip emergence. We still run the paddle. Your trip becomes a sunset skyline tour instead. We're transparent about it on the day.

**Can I bring kids on the bat tour?**
Yes, ages 5 and up. Younger kids ride tandem with a parent.

**What time does the bat tour start?**
Varies by season. Summer (July-August): around 7:30pm check-in. Spring/fall: 6:00-7:00pm. Check the [bat tour page](/bat-bridge-kayak-tour/) for current times.

**Should I tip the guide?**
If you had a great time, yes. $5-10 per person is normal.
