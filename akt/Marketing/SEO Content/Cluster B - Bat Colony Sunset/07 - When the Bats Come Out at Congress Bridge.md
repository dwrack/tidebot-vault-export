# When Do the Austin Bats Come Out at Congress Bridge? (Monthly Schedule for 2026)

The bats fly out of the Congress Avenue Bridge roughly 30 minutes before sunset, give or take. The exact emergence time shifts every month with the season, the bat population, and the weather. If you're planning a trip around it, here's the full schedule.

## How emergence time actually works

The Mexican free-tailed bats living under the Congress Avenue Bridge come out to feed on insects. They wait until just before sunset to leave the bridge. In peak summer, the colony is so big (1.5 million bats) that the emergence can last over 45 minutes.

In April or October when the colony is smaller, you'll see a 10-minute window of bats and then it's done.

## Monthly schedule

These are typical emergence start times. They shift earlier or later by 5-15 minutes depending on cloud cover, temperature, and how hungry the colony is.

| Month | Emergence start (typical) | Bat colony size | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **March** | 7:00–7:30 PM | Returning, growing | First arrivals, smaller colony |
| **April** | 7:30–7:50 PM | Building | Worth going if you're already here |
| **May** | 8:00–8:20 PM | Strong | Pups born late May |
| **June** | 8:25–8:35 PM | Strong, pups in roost | Peak summer crowds begin |
| **July** | 8:30–8:40 PM | Largest colony | Best viewing of the season |
| **August** | 8:15–8:35 PM | Largest colony | Peak month, peak crowds |
| **September** | 7:45–8:10 PM | Strong | Crowds thin, weather perfect |
| **October** | 7:00–7:30 PM | Decreasing | Bats start migrating south late October |
| **Nov–Feb** | No bats | Migrated | Skyline tours still excellent |

## When to actually book

**Best months:** July and August, no question. Peak colony, longest emergence, dramatic sunset light.

**Best months for fewer crowds:** late April, May, September. Strong colony, smaller crowd on the bridge and on the water.

**Best months to avoid:** November through February. The bats are in Mexico. The skyline at sunset is still gorgeous, but no bats.

## Bridge view vs. water view

You can watch the bats two ways, and they're genuinely different experiences:

**From the bridge:** crowded, free, you watch the bats fly out from above. Most people never see anything but a stream of bats heading east. You don't see the colony itself.

**From the water:** less crowded, you're looking up at the underside of the bridge as the bats spiral out. You see them at eye level. This is what we run on the [bat bridge kayak tour](/bat-bridge-kayak-tour/).

If you're going to go once, go from the water. The view is what makes the experience worth it.

## How early to arrive

For a kayak tour, we have you on the water 30-45 minutes before emergence. For viewing from the bridge, get there at least 30 minutes early on a summer Saturday or you'll be on the second row of people fighting for sightlines.

## Weather effects

- **Light rain:** bats might delay emergence by 15-30 minutes or skip it entirely
- **Cool fronts (under 65°F):** bats may emerge late or in lower numbers
- **Strong winds:** can delay emergence by 10-20 minutes
- **Hot summer evenings (90°F+):** earliest, biggest emergence

The colony is more reliable than the weather lets on, but a serious storm will pause the show.

## Common questions

**What time do the bats fly out at Congress Bridge?**
Roughly 30 minutes before sunset. In summer that's 8:00-8:40pm. In spring and fall it's earlier.

**When is the best month to see the Austin bats?**
July and August for peak colony size. Late April-May or September for smaller crowds.

**Are the Austin bats there year-round?**
No. They migrate to Mexico in late October and return in March. November through February there are no bats to see.

**How long does the bat emergence last?**
In peak summer, 30-45 minutes. In shoulder season, 10-20 minutes.

**Do the bats come out every night?**
Usually yes, March through October. Occasionally a heavy storm or cold front delays or cancels emergence.

**Where's the best place to see the Austin bats?**
From the water on a kayak. The bridge is crowded and gives you a worse view of the actual colony.
