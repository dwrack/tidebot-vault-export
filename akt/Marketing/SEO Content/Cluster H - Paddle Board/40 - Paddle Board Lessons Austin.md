# Paddle Board Lessons in Austin: Where to Start and What to Expect

Most people don't actually need a paddle board lesson. SUP is one of those activities you can figure out in 10 minutes by trial and error. But if you want to skip the falling-in phase and get to confident paddling fast, a 60-minute lesson is worth the money.

Here's the honest take.

## Do you actually need a lesson?

**No, if:** you've done any board sport (skateboarding, surfing, snowboarding) and you have decent balance. You'll figure it out in 10 minutes on your own.

**Yes, if:** you're nervous about water, you want to nail technique fast, you want to learn yoga or fitness on a board, or you have a specific goal like SUP racing.

For Lady Bird Lake recreational paddling, most people are fine without a lesson.

## What a lesson actually covers

### Basics (Lesson 1, 60 min)
- Stance and posture
- Paddle technique (correct grip, stroke mechanics)
- Standing up from kneeling
- Falling correctly (sideways, not forward)
- Recovery (getting back on the board)
- Turning (forward sweep, reverse sweep, draw stroke)

You'll spend the first 15 minutes on the dock, then the next 45 on the water.

### Intermediate (Lesson 2-3)
- Refinement of stroke
- Steering precision
- Wind handling
- Touring technique for distance

### Specific tracks
- **SUP yoga:** balance work, yoga poses on the board, breathing through wobble
- **SUP fitness:** strength training elements on the water
- **Racing:** efficient stroke, turning, drafting

## Cost

- 60-min private lesson: $50-75
- 60-min group lesson: $40-50
- SUP yoga class: $25-40

Includes board, paddle, life jacket.

## What to wear

- Quick-dry shorts and rash guard
- Hat with strap
- Sunglasses with strap
- Sunscreen
- Sandals with back strap or barefoot
- Bring a towel for after

You will fall in. Plan accordingly.

## What to expect first time

- 10 minutes on the dock learning grip and stance
- 5 minutes practicing on your knees
- 5 minutes standing for the first time (likely with a fall)
- 30+ minutes paddling around with corrections from the instructor
- 10 minutes wrap-up / questions

## Common first-lesson struggles

1. **Looking at your feet.** Look at the horizon. Looking down throws balance.
2. **Stiff knees.** Bend them. Stiff legs = wobble.
3. **Death-grip on the paddle.** Loose hands, let your body do the work.
4. **Paddling only on one side.** You'll go in circles. Switch every few strokes.
5. **Standing with feet too close.** Feet shoulder-width, not together.

## When NOT to book a lesson

- It's windy (10+ mph). Lessons get cut short.
- It's freezing. The water is fine but if you fall, you'll be miserable.
- It's a thunderstorm forecast. We cancel.
- You have less than 60 minutes available. The lesson can't be rushed.

## What you can do after one lesson

- Confidently rent a SUP and paddle Lady Bird Lake on your own
- Join a SUP tour without holding the group back
- Try SUP yoga or fitness with confidence
- Explain the basics to a friend

## What requires more than one lesson

- SUP yoga at intermediate level
- Distance touring (8+ miles)
- Racing technique
- Surfing on a SUP (not really a Lady Bird Lake thing)
- White water SUP (not a Lady Bird Lake thing either)

Not necessary on Lady Bird Lake. The water is calm and you'll figure it out.

Most people are confidently paddling within 10-15 minutes of trying.

$50-75 for a private 60-minute lesson. $40-50 group.

**Is paddle boarding easy to learn?**
Yes for most people. The standing part takes some adjustment. The paddling is intuitive.
