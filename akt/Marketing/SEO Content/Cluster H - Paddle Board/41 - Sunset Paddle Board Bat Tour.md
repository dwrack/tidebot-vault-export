# The Sunset Paddle Board Bat Tour: A Different Way to See the Bats

Most people who watch the Austin bats are either on the bridge with hundreds of strangers or on a kayak. There's a third option: a stand-up paddle board. The view is the best, the experience is more athletic, and you'll have a story most of your friends don't.

Here's how the [paddle board bat tour](/paddleboard-tour/) actually works.

## What you're getting

- 2-hour SUP tour at sunset
- Sunset paddle on Lady Bird Lake
- Position under or near the Congress Avenue Bridge for bat emergence
- Skyline lit up at golden hour
- All gear and a guide

The bat tour from a SUP is taller, more visible, and more engaging than from a kayak. You're standing, paddling, balancing, and watching all at once.

## Why a paddle board specifically

**Better view of the bats overhead.** Standing puts you closer to the colony's altitude. The bats fly above you and you see them at eye level instead of looking up from a sitting position.

**More memorable experience.** Standing on a board for 2 hours while paddling under a bridge full of bats is a different memory than sitting in a kayak.

**More physical engagement.** You're using your whole body. By the end you've had a real workout in addition to the experience.

## Who this tour is right for

- People who already SUP and want to do the iconic Austin paddle on a board
- Travelers who want a workout and an experience combined
- Couples or friends who'd rather be active than passive
- Anyone who's done the kayak version and wants something different

## Who should pick the kayak version instead

- First-time paddlers (SUP balance + bat watching is a lot)
- People with knee or balance issues
- Photographers who want stable shots
- Anyone who'd rather sit for 2 hours

For the comparison, see [the bat tour: bridge vs. water](/blog/the-best-way-to-see-the-austin-bats-from-the-bridge-vs-from-the-water/).

## Difficulty level

This is more athletic than the kayak version, but Lady Bird Lake is calm enough that beginner SUPers can usually handle it. Most of our guests have done some paddle boarding before, but we've had complete first-timers do the tour without falling in.

If you've never SUP'd, we'd still recommend a [SUP lesson](/paddle-board-lessons/) or 30 minutes of rental practice before booking the bat tour.

## What to wear

Same as the kayak bat tour, with one note: you will probably get wetter on a SUP because falling is more common.

- Quick-dry shorts and rash guard
- Hat with strap
- Sunglasses with strap (cheap pair, in case)
- Sunscreen
- Sandals with back strap or barefoot
- A light layer for after sunset

## What to bring

- Phone in waterproof case (bat photos!)
- Water bottle
- Bug spray (mosquitoes at dusk in summer)
- Cash for tip
- Headlamp for the paddle back if it's getting dark

## What it actually feels like

You're standing on a board, paddle in hand, on glassy water. The skyline is lighting up. You're under the Congress Avenue Bridge. The bats start to drop, then ribbon out east. You can hear the wings. The air gets cooler. You're paddling slowly, partly to maintain balance, partly because you don't want to leave.

It's a 90-minute experience compressed into one of those moments you'll remember.

## When it runs

March through October only. The bats are in Mexico the rest of the year. Standard runs:
- Spring: starts around 7 PM
- Summer peak: starts 7:30-7:45 PM
- Fall: starts around 6:30 PM

Check the [bat tour page](/paddleboard-tour/) for current times.

## What can go wrong

- **Wind picks up.** SUP catches wind. We watch the forecast. If gusts are 15+ mph, we move you to the kayak version.
- **You fall in mid-emergence.** It happens. We help you remount.
- **Bats are light.** In shoulder season the colony is smaller. Still worth it for the SUP experience and the sunset.

## Photos

A SUP gives you a higher angle and a wider view than a kayak. Phone in a waterproof case, you can shoot one-handed while standing. Some of the best bat-tour photos we've seen came from SUPs.

## Common questions

**Can you watch the Austin bats from a paddle board?**
Yes. Our [paddle board bat tour](/paddleboard-tour/) takes you to the same spot as the kayak version, on a SUP.

**Is the SUP bat tour harder than the kayak version?**
A bit. You're standing for 2 hours and balancing while watching. Beginners can do it on calm days.

**Can a beginner do the SUP bat tour?**
Yes if the conditions are calm and you're comfortable on a board for short stretches. We recommend 30+ minutes of SUP rental practice first.

**What if I fall in during the bat tour?**
You'll be fine. We help you remount the board. The water is in the 70s in summer.

**How much does the SUP bat tour cost?**
Same as the kayak version, $50-ish.
