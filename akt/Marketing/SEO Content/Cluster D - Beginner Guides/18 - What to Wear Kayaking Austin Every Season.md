# What to Wear Kayaking in Austin (Every Season)

The right kayak outfit on Lady Bird Lake depends almost entirely on the time of year. Show up to a January paddle in shorts and you'll be miserable. Show up in July in jeans and you'll be miserable in a different way.

Here's the season-by-season breakdown.

## The universal rules (every season)

- **No flip-flops.** They fall off the second you push off. Sandals with a back strap (Tevas, Chacos) or water shoes only.
- **No cotton if you can avoid it.** Cotton stays wet for hours. Quick-dry synthetics or wool.
- **Hat with a strap.** A regular hat blows off and goes in the lake.
- **Sunglasses with a strap.** Same.
- **Sunscreen, applied before you launch.** The reflection off the water doubles UV exposure.
- **A dry bag or waterproof case for your phone.** This is non-negotiable.

## Summer (June, July, August)

The heat is the enemy. Lady Bird Lake in mid-July at 2pm is brutal even on the water.

**Wear:**
- Quick-dry shorts (athletic shorts, board shorts)
- Light-colored t-shirt or rash guard (sun protection)
- Wide-brim hat with strap
- Polarized sunglasses with strap
- Reef-safe sunscreen (SPF 30+, applied 20 min before launch)
- Sandals with back strap

**Skip:**
- Cotton t-shirts (they get soaked and stay wet)
- Anything black (heat absorbing)
- Jeans (obvious)
- Closed-toe shoes (your feet will sweat through them)

**Pro tip:** wet your shirt before launching. Evaporation cooling for the first 20 minutes.

## Spring (March, April, May) and Fall (September, October)

Best paddling weather of the year. Mostly, dress like a casual outdoor day.

**Wear:**
- Quick-dry shorts or light pants
- T-shirt or light long-sleeve
- A light pullover or fleece for early morning or evening tours
- Hat, sunglasses, sunscreen
- Sandals or water shoes

**Bring:**
- A light jacket if it's a sunset tour (it cools off after sundown)
- A small towel

**Pro tip:** April mornings can be cooler than expected. May and September are usually shorts weather.

## Winter (November, December, January, February)

Yes, people kayak Lady Bird Lake in winter. The crowds are gone, the skyline is dramatic, and the cool air is honestly pleasant. You just need to dress for it.

**Wear:**
- Wool or synthetic base layer (no cotton)
- Quick-dry pants or leggings
- Fleece or insulated mid-layer
- Light waterproof jacket on top
- Wool or synthetic socks
- Closed-toe water shoes or neoprene booties
- Beanie or warm hat

**Skip:**
- Cotton anything
- Down jackets (if they get wet, you're miserable)
- Bare ankles

**Bring:**
- Spare gloves
- A spare shirt for after (to change into a dry one)

**Pro tip:** the air is cooler than the water in winter. If you flip (you won't), the water is actually around 60°F, which is survivable for the few seconds it takes to get back to a kayak. The bigger risk is windchill on the open lake. Layer up.

## Special cases

### Sunset tours
Bring a layer regardless of season. After sunset, the lake cools off fast.

### Bat tours (March-October)
Same as your seasonal base, but bring bug spray. Mosquitoes appear right at dusk in summer.

### Sunrise tours
Cooler than the rest of the day. Add one layer to your seasonal default.

### Rentals where you're staying out longer
Pack a small dry bag with: extra water, snack, sunscreen reapplication, phone case, and a buff or bandana for your neck.

## Common questions

**Can I kayak in jeans?**
You can, but you shouldn't. They get heavy when wet, take hours to dry, and chafe.

**Do I need a wetsuit for winter kayaking in Austin?**
Not for most of winter. Layered synthetics and a waterproof jacket are usually enough. A wetsuit is overkill unless temperatures dip into the 30s with strong wind.

**What shoes should I wear kayaking?**
Sandals with a back strap (Tevas, Chacos) or water shoes. Closed-toe in winter. No flip-flops.

**Can I kayak in a swimsuit?**
You can but it's uncomfortable for 2 hours of sun exposure. We'd recommend at least a rash guard over the swimsuit.

**Should I bring a change of clothes?**
For most tours, no. Bring a small towel. For longer rentals or full-day trips, yes.
