## Voice guardrails (every piece follows these)

- Friend with a tip, not a brochure
- Honest: this is an urban paddle, not a wilderness experience
- "The water is right there" — spontaneity is the product
- Use: guests, paddle, get out on the water, Lady Bird, downtown, real Austin
- Don't use: customers, adventure of a lifetime, extreme, must-do, top 10
- No em dashes. No double dashes. No throat-clearing openers
- Take a position. Real prices. Real timing. Real constraints
- Plain ASCII, no curly quotes when copying to the CMS
