# Things to Do With Your Dog in Austin (Including Kayaking With a Pup)

Austin is one of the best cities in the country to travel with a dog. Most outdoor experiences allow dogs. Most patios allow dogs. Most hotels allow dogs. Here's the actual list of things worth doing with your dog in Austin, including the kayak version.

## On the water

### Kayak Lady Bird Lake with your dog
Yes, this works. Dogs are allowed on [our rentals](/rental/) (not most guided tours). The lake is calm, motorboat-free, and the shore is shallow if your dog jumps in.

What works:
- Calm dogs that don't panic
- Smaller dogs that fit in a kayak cockpit
- Dogs that have been on a paddle board or boat before
- Anyone with a canine life jacket (we rent these)

What doesn't:
- High-energy dogs that won't stop moving
- Dogs that aren't comfortable with strangers (the lake has paddleboarders close by)
- Anyone without voice control

### Paddle board with your dog
Same deal as kayak. SUP works for calm, smaller dogs especially. Some dogs love the standing platform.

### Barton Springs Pool — dog days
Once a year (usually late summer) Barton Springs has a "dog day" where dogs are allowed in the pool. Otherwise dogs aren't allowed in the swim area.

Auditorium Shores has off-leash access to the lake (see below).

## Off-leash dog parks

### Auditorium Shores Off-Leash Area
The big one. 8 acres along Lady Bird Lake near downtown. Direct lake access, well-shaded, social. Dogs swim in the lake here (allowed in the off-leash area, not technically in other parts of the lake).

### Red Bud Isle
Off-leash island in Lake Austin (not Lady Bird Lake). Forested, lake access, dogs love it.

### Walnut Creek Metro Park
North Austin. Big trail system, off-leash sections.

### Bull Creek District Park
Northwest Austin. Off-leash trail along the creek.

## Trails (leashed)

### Ann and Roy Butler Hike-and-Bike Trail
10-mile loop around Lady Bird Lake. Almost every dog in Austin walks this trail. Crowded but social.

### Barton Creek Greenbelt
Limestone trails, swimming holes (when water's there), more "real outdoors" feel. Leashed but loose enforcement.

### Mount Bonnell
Quick hike up, leash required, great photo with the dog and skyline.

### Pennybacker Bridge overlook
Short hike, good views, leashed.

## Patios and breweries

Most Austin patios allow dogs. Specifically dog-friendly:

**Yard Bar** (6700 Burnet Rd): dog park + bar combo. Concept is built around it.

**Banger's** (Rainey Street): big patio, dog water bowls, sausages your dog will smell.

**Lazarus Brewing** (1902 E 6th): outdoor seating, dog water, casual.

**Hops & Grain** (507 Calles St): brewery patio, dog-friendly.

**Easy Tiger** (709 E 6th): outdoor seating, dog welcome.

**Cosmic Coffee + Beer Garden** (121 Pickle Rd): outdoor space, dog-friendly.

## Restaurants

Patio dog policies vary. Generally allowed at:

- Banger's
- Lazarus
- Hopdoddy
- The Picnic food truck park
- Easy Tiger
- Most casual patios

Always confirm before sitting down with your dog.

## What to bring for your dog

For Austin specifically:
- **Water bowl + lots of water** (heat is real)
- **Cooling vest or bandana** if visiting in summer
- **Booties or paw wax** (sidewalks get hot)
- **Leash** (most places require it)
- **Poop bags** (everywhere)
- **Canine PFD if you're doing the kayak**

## Heat warning

Summer Austin is brutal for dogs. Sidewalks reach temperatures that burn paws. Cars heat up dangerously fast.

Rules:
- **No mid-day walks in summer.** Sunrise or post-sunset only.
- **Test the sidewalk with your hand** before walking your dog. If you can't hold it for 7 seconds, it's too hot.
- **Hydration constantly.**
- **Skip the kayak in mid-day summer** if it's over 95°F.

## Kayaking with your dog: best times

- **Sunrise** is ideal. Cool, calm, less crowded.
- **Spring and fall** are easiest. Mild temperatures.
- **Avoid mid-day in summer** (your dog will overheat).
- **Avoid winter cold snaps** (your dog can get hypothermic).

## Dog water rules on Lady Bird Lake

- **Allowed on rentals** in our boats with a life jacket
- **Not on most guided tours** (boat configuration)
- **No swimming** in Lady Bird Lake (the city ban applies to dogs too, except in the off-leash area at Auditorium Shores)
- **Cleanup is on you** at the launch and the dock

## Common questions

**Can I bring my dog kayaking in Austin?**
Yes on rentals if your dog is calm. Generally no on guided tours due to boat configuration.

**Where can dogs swim in Austin?**
The off-leash area at Auditorium Shores. Red Bud Isle on Lake Austin. Barton Creek pools (when water's there).

**Are dogs allowed at Austin restaurants?**
Most patios yes, some indoor sections yes. Always confirm.

**Is Austin a good city for dogs?**
Yes, it's one of the most dog-friendly cities in the country.

**Where can I take my dog kayaking on Lady Bird Lake?**
Any rental dock that allows dogs. [Our rentals at 200 E Riverside](/rental/) accept calm dogs with life jackets.
