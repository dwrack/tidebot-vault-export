# The Austin Tourist's One-Day Itinerary: Skyline, Bats, BBQ

If you've got exactly one day in Austin and you want to hit the iconic stuff without wasting time on tourist filler, here's the version that actually works. Built around three things Austin does better than anyone: the skyline from the water, BBQ, and the bat colony.

## 7 AM — Wake up

If you're going to do this in one day, you have to start early. Sleep in and you lose the morning light, the BBQ line, or the sunset window.

## 7:30 AM — Coffee + boardwalk walk

Coffee at **Jo's Coffee** (1300 S Congress) or **Bouldin Creek Cafe** (1900 S 1st St). Quick.

Walk the **Ann and Roy Butler Hike-and-Bike Trail** boardwalk section for 30 minutes. Skyline reflections in the morning light. Free, sets the tone for the day.

## 9 AM — Mount Bonnell sunrise alternative (optional)

If you missed sunrise from the boardwalk and want better elevation, drive 15 minutes to **Mount Bonnell** for the panorama. Climb the stairs (about 100), 5 minutes up.

If you've already gotten your skyline shots, skip this. We're going to be on the water at 10.

## 10 AM — Kayak skyline tour

Book the **[skyline kayak tour](/austin-skyline-tour/)** at 10 AM. Two hours. $50.

This is the trip's anchor experience. You see the downtown skyline from the water, get the architectural commentary, and leave understanding the city in a way most tourists don't.

If you're visiting March-October and you'd rather see the bats, skip this and book the [sunset bat tour](/bat-bridge-kayak-tour/) for the evening. Then do something else mid-day.

## 12:30 PM — Franklin BBQ (or alternative)

The legendary BBQ stop. **Franklin Barbecue** (900 E 11th St) opens at 11 AM and sells out by mid-afternoon. The line is real.

If you don't want to wait in line:

- **LeRoy & Lewis** (food truck on Cesar Chavez): comparable quality, shorter line
- **La Barbecue** (E Cesar Chavez): excellent, less famous
- **Cooper's BBQ** (downtown): old-school cafeteria style
- **Stiles Switch BBQ** (north Lamar): great quality, almost no line

Franklin is the icon, but the others are 90% as good with 10% the wait.

## 2 PM — South Congress walk

Drive or Uber to South Congress. Walk from the **"I Love You So Much" wall** at Jo's Coffee south to **Allens Boots** and back. Maybe duck into:
- **Uncommon Objects** for vintage
- **South Austin Popular Culture Center** (free, weird)
- **Big Top Candy Shop** if you're with kids

Pace yourself. The afternoon is for slow walking, not power-shopping.

## 4 PM — Barton Springs Pool or Pennybacker Bridge

If it's hot:

**Barton Springs Pool** (2201 Barton Springs Rd). $5-10 entry, 68°F spring-fed pool. The Austin afternoon swim. Worth it any season.

If it's cold or you'd rather see views:

**Pennybacker Bridge overlook** off Loop 360. 15-minute hike up to the best free skyline view in Austin.

## 6 PM — Sunset

Two options depending on whether you've done the kayak tour yet:

**If you did the morning kayak tour:**
Mount Bonnell or Pennybacker for sunset. Free, beautiful, photo-worthy.

**If you swapped to the sunset bat tour:**
This is your prime activity now. Check in at 6:30 PM, on the water by 7 PM, bats around 8 PM, back on land by 8:30 PM.

## 7-8 PM — Dinner

Reserve ahead. The picks:

**Eberly** (615 S Lamar): the upscale move
**Loro** (2115 S Lamar): Aaron Franklin's Asian-Texas spot
**Suerte** (1800 E 6th): modern Mexican
**Joann's Fine Foods** (1224 S Congress): retro casual
**Veracruz All Natural**: best tacos if you want casual

For BBQ at dinner: **Stubb's** if you want live music with it. Otherwise BBQ is a lunch thing.

## 9 PM — Drinks on Rainey Street

A 5-block stretch of bars in converted bungalows. 10-minute walk from most downtown spots.

Picks:
- **Container Bar** (small, lively)
- **Banger's** (food + beer)
- **Half Step** (cocktails)
- **Lustre Pearl** (the original)

Don't try to do all of them. Pick 2.

## 10:30 PM — Decide

If you have energy: **6th Street** for live music, or **Continental Club** on South Congress.

If you're done: walk the boardwalk one more time, take a final photo, sleep.

## Total cost

Roughly:
- Coffee + breakfast: $15
- Kayak tour: $50
- BBQ lunch: $25-35
- Barton Springs: $10
- Dinner: $40-60
- Drinks: $25-40

**Total:** $165-210 per person.

## What we'd skip

- **6th Street before 9 PM** (touristy)
- **Hop-on hop-off bus tours** (waste of time)
- **Texas State Capitol guided tour** (do it yourself, free, faster)
- **Generic Austin "experiences"** that compete with the BBQ + kayak combo for time

## What we'd add if you have an extra day

- **East Side breweries** (Live Oak, Lazarus, Hops & Grain)
- **South Lamar shopping** (Antonelli's Cheese, Wally Workman Gallery)
- **Live music at the Continental Club** or **The Saxon Pub**
- **A second kayak tour** (sunrise version)

## Compressed version (if you have 6 hours)

- 9 AM: Kayak skyline tour
- 11 AM: BBQ at LeRoy & Lewis or La Barbecue
- 12:30 PM: Mount Bonnell or Pennybacker for the view
- 2 PM: SoCo walk
- 3:30 PM: Done

Hits the high points in less than half a day.

## Common questions

**What's a perfect one-day Austin itinerary?**
Skyline kayak in the morning, BBQ for lunch, SoCo walk in the afternoon, sunset view, dinner downtown, drinks on Rainey.

**Is Franklin BBQ worth the wait?**
The food is genuinely great. The 2-3 hour wait is real. If you don't want to wait, LeRoy & Lewis or La Barbecue are 90% as good.

**Should I do a kayak tour or just rent if I only have one day?**
Tour. The 2-hour structure with a guide is the right move for a tight schedule.

**Can I see the bats and kayak in the same day?**
Yes. Skyline tour at 10 AM, bat tour at sunset, dinner in between.

**Where should I stay for one day in Austin?**
South Congress for a SoCo-focused trip. Downtown for a 6th Street / Rainey trip. East Austin for a less touristy feel.
