# The Best Cheap Outdoor Things to Do in Austin (Under $50)

Most "things to do in Austin" lists are full of restaurants and bars. The actual best Austin experiences are outside, mostly free or very cheap, and not what tourist sites push at you. Here's the honest list of outdoor things under $50.

## Free

### Watch the bats from Congress Avenue Bridge
Roughly 30 minutes before sunset, March-October. Stand on the bridge or in the Statesman Bat Observation Center. Free.

The view from a kayak is better (see below) but the bridge view is still cool.

### Hike the Barton Creek Greenbelt
Limestone bluffs, swimming holes (when water's there), trail miles in the western edge of the city. Free entry. Bring water.

### Walk the Ann and Roy Butler Hike-and-Bike Trail
10-mile loop around Lady Bird Lake. Best Austin walk. Free. Take a phone for photos of the skyline.

### Visit the State Capitol
Free entry, free dome tours, picnic on the grounds. Most tourists skip this and shouldn't.

### Mount Bonnell sunset
Drive up, walk the stairs (about 100), get one of the best free skyline views. Free parking, free entry.

### Pennybacker Bridge overlook
Hike up the trail off Loop 360, west view of the skyline at sunset. Free, 15-min hike.

### Zilker Park
102 acres in the middle of the city. Free entry. Kite flying, picnics, walking. ACL Festival happens here in October.

## Under $25

### Kayak or paddle board rental on Lady Bird Lake
$20-25/hour for a basic rental. [Our rental page](/rental/) covers what we offer at 200 E Riverside.

This is one of the best outdoor experiences in the city, full stop. The skyline view from the water doesn't exist on any free option.

### Barton Springs Pool
$5-10 entry. 68°F spring-fed pool, 3 acres, in the middle of Zilker Park. Locals swim here daily. Tourists sometimes miss it.

### Deep Eddy Pool
$5-10 entry. Smaller than Barton, also spring-fed, west Austin. Less crowded.

### Texas Memorial Museum
Free entry, $0 reservation needed. Dinosaurs, fossils, regional natural history.

### LBJ Library
$10 entry. Presidential library on the UT campus. Real artifacts.

## $25-50

### Kayak tour on Lady Bird Lake
$50/person. [Skyline tour](/austin-skyline-tour/), [bat tour](/bat-bridge-kayak-tour/), or [sunrise tour](/sunrise-tour/). 2 hours, includes everything.

### Day pass at McKinney Falls State Park
$6-12 entry. Camping, swimming holes, hiking. 30 minutes from downtown.

### Inks Lake State Park (day trip)
$7 entry. 1.5 hours from Austin. Granite outcrops, swimming, kayak rentals on-site.

### San Marcos River tubing
$15-25 for a tube and shuttle. 45 minutes south of Austin. Spring-fed water, summer staple.

### San Marcos River kayak rental
$30-50 for a half-day with shuttle. Same destination, different vehicle.

### Group fitness class outdoors
Various yoga and bootcamp classes happen at Auditorium Shores or Zilker. $15-25/class typically.

## Cheap dinners outdoors

### Food trucks
Multiple lots downtown and east. Most plates are $10-15. Eat outside. Best: South Austin (south of the river), East 6th Street.

### Picnic at Zilker Park
Pack a sandwich, cooler with non-alcoholic drinks, find a tree, watch sunset.

### Boardwalk picnic
The boardwalk along Lady Bird Lake has benches. Bring food, watch joggers, watch the skyline.

## What's not worth it

- **Tourist trap "Austin experiences"** that charge $80+ for things you can do free
- **Hop-on-hop-off bus tours** (you're better off walking)
- **Generic dinner cruises** on the lake (overpriced and mediocre)

## The honest "best $50 day in Austin" itinerary

- 8 AM: kayak rental on Lady Bird Lake ($25)
- 10 AM: breakfast at a food truck ($12)
- 12 PM: walk the boardwalk
- 2 PM: Barton Springs Pool ($10)
- 5 PM: Mount Bonnell sunset (free)
- 7 PM: dinner at Joann's Fine Foods or a cheaper food truck

Total: under $50, full day outdoors, you've done the city right.

## The honest "best $50 night in Austin" plan

- 6 PM: skyline kayak tour ($50)
- 8:30 PM: drinks on Rainey Street
- 10 PM: 6th Street if you're young, home if you're not

That's it. The kayak tour at $50 covers the marquee outdoor experience. Drinks are extra.

## Common questions

**What can you do in Austin for free?**
Bat watching from the bridge, hiking the greenbelt, walking the boardwalk, Mount Bonnell, Pennybacker Bridge overlook, the Capitol grounds.

**Are kayak rentals in Austin cheap?**
$20-25/hour. Among the cheaper outdoor activities in the city.

**What's the cheapest outdoor activity in Austin?**
Walking the boardwalk or hiking the greenbelt — both free.

**Is Barton Springs Pool worth it?**
Yes. $5-10 for one of the best swims in Texas, year-round.

**Can you visit Austin on a budget?**
Easily. Most of the best Austin experiences are outdoor and either free or under $25.
