# Parking Near Lady Bird Lake for Kayakers: An Honest Guide

Parking near the lake on a sunny Saturday is the worst part of kayaking in Austin. Once you know where to go and when, it's solvable. If you don't, you can spend 30 minutes circling and end up paying $20 to park in a sketchy lot.

Here's how locals actually do it.

## The free lots (and when they fill)

**Festival Beach (1730 Bergman Ave):** the main free lot. Big, paved, right next to a real boat ramp. Fills by 10am on summer weekends. Get there by 8 or skip it.

**Holly Shores (off East 5th):** smaller lot, easier to find a spot, but you're carrying gear across grass. Doesn't fill as fast.

**Roy G. Guerrero Park (east end):** big lot, almost never full, but it's a longer paddle to the skyline.

**Auditorium Shores (800 W Riverside):** small lot, paid on event days, free other times. Fills fast.

## The paid options

**Downtown garages near Cesar Chavez and S. Congress:** $5-15 for a few hours. Reliable but you're walking 5-10 minutes with your gear.

**Hotel garages with public parking:** the Hilton on E Cesar Chavez and the Four Seasons both have public parking. Premium prices.

**Riverside Drive paid lots:** several around our area at 200 E Riverside. $10-15 for the day.

## The cheat code: book with an outfitter that includes parking

If you don't have your own boat, the simplest play is to skip the parking puzzle entirely. We include free parking at our launch (200 E Riverside Drive) for guests on [tours](/austin-skyline-tour/) and [rentals](/rental/).

This isn't a sales pitch, it's just how local outfitters set themselves up. The math: gas + 30 minutes circling + paid garage often costs more than the parking premium baked into a rental.

## Weekend vs. weekday

**Weekday morning:** parking is easy at every option above. You'll find a spot.

**Weekend morning before 10am:** still mostly fine. Festival Beach starts to thin out around 9:30.

**Weekend midday-afternoon:** rough. Festival Beach is full. Garages downtown are your best bet.

**Weekend pre-sunset:** worst window. Plan for paid parking or hike from a few blocks out.

**Sunday morning:** almost as easy as a weekday morning. Locals are sleeping in.

## Special events that make parking impossible

- **ACL Festival** (early October): downtown is locked down for two weekends
- **SXSW** (March): downtown is a parking nightmare for two weeks
- **F1 weekend** (October): traffic is awful, parking is worse
- **Major UT football Saturdays:** stay east

If your visit overlaps any of these, walk, scooter, or take a rideshare.

## The scooter / rideshare workaround

If you're staying in a downtown hotel, don't drive. Walk the 10 minutes from a hotel near Rainey Street, or grab a Lime/Bird scooter for the last mile. We've had guests show up on scooters more times than I can count.

## Loading and unloading

If you have your own kayak:

- Festival Beach has a real ramp; back your trailer right up.
- Roy G. Guerrero same.
- Auditorium Shores you'll have to carry from the lot.
- Plan 5-10 minutes for loading/unloading on top of your paddle time.

## What to bring (parking-related)

- Cash or card for paid lots and garages
- Phone with the ParkMobile app (some Austin lots use it)
- A bike rack or kayak rack you actually trust
- A buddy if you're loading a tandem alone

## Common questions

**Is parking free at Lady Bird Lake?**
At Festival Beach, Holly Shores, and Roy G. Guerrero, yes. Most other downtown lots are paid.

**Where do I park to kayak Lady Bird Lake?**
Festival Beach is the most reliable free option. Private outfitters like ours include parking with bookings.

**How early should I arrive for parking on a weekend?**
Before 9am if you want a free spot at Festival Beach in summer.

**Is there parking at the Auditorium Shores kayak launch?**
Yes, small lot, fills early on weekends. Paid during events.

**Can I park downtown and walk to the lake?**
Yes. Many downtown garages are 5-10 minutes from a launch. Bring a kayak cart if you have your own boat.
