# Lady Bird Lake Kayak Rules, Permits, and What's Actually Allowed

The rules for kayaking Lady Bird Lake aren't complicated, but a few of them surprise people every season. Park rangers do enforce. This is the honest version of what you can and can't do, and what'll actually get you a ticket vs. what's a gray area.

## The hard rules

**No motorized boats.** This is what makes the lake calm enough for beginners. Electric trolling motors are allowed for accessibility, but recreational gas or electric watercraft (jet skis, motorboats, etc.) are banned.

**No swimming.** This one trips up a lot of visitors. The city has banned swimming in Lady Bird Lake for decades. The water is mostly fine, but the policy is enforced. You can wade in shallows at the launches when getting in your boat. You cannot go for a swim. If you want to swim, head to Barton Springs or Deep Eddy a few minutes away.

**No alcohol on the water.** Open container laws apply. Beer in the cooler back at the launch is fine. Beer in the kayak isn't. You'll get a ticket.

**Life jackets required for kids under 13.** Strongly recommended for everyone. Most rental and tour operators include them.

**No paddling after dark without lights.** If you're on the water past sunset, you need a white light visible 360 degrees (a basic headlamp pointed up, or a deck light) per Coast Guard rules.

## The soft rules (etiquette)

These aren't fines but they keep the peace:

- Stay right when passing
- Yield to rowers (the rowing teams have right-of-way and they're moving fast)
- Don't paddle into the dragon boat practice lanes (they meet near the boardwalk on certain weeknights)
- Don't crowd the bat colony at sunset; everyone wants the same view

## Dogs

**Allowed on rentals if calm and wearing a life jacket.** Most rental places (us included) rent canine PFDs.

**Generally not on guided tours** because the boat configuration doesn't accommodate them safely.

**Cleanup is on you.** Bring a bag.

## Fishing

You can fish from a kayak on Lady Bird Lake, but:

- You need a current Texas fishing license (over 17 years old)
- Standard Texas freshwater rules apply
- Bass, sunfish, catfish, gar are common
- Catch and release is the local norm; a lot of fishermen don't eat the fish from this section

## Permits for commercial use

If you're trying to run a tour, do a paid photo shoot, or sell something on the water, you need commercial permits from the city. This doesn't apply to recreational paddlers.

## What gets people ticketed

In order of how often we see it:

1. **Visible alcohol on the water** (beers, wine, even canned cocktails)
2. **Swimming** (people overheating and jumping in)
3. **No life jacket on a child**
4. **Paddling after dark with no light** in winter when sunset comes early

Park rangers and Austin PD lake patrol both work the water. Tickets aren't huge but they're real.

## Common questions

**Do I need a permit to kayak on Lady Bird Lake?**
No. Recreational kayaking is free and unpermitted. Bring your boat or rent one.

**Can you drink beer while kayaking on Lady Bird Lake?**
No. It's a public park area and open container is enforced.

**Can you swim in Lady Bird Lake?**
No. The city has banned swimming for decades. The water is fine, the rule isn't about water quality.

**Are dogs allowed on Lady Bird Lake kayaks?**
Yes on rentals if they're calm and wearing a PFD. Generally no on guided tours.

**Can I fish from my kayak on Lady Bird Lake?**
Yes, with a Texas fishing license.

**Do you need a life jacket on Lady Bird Lake?**
Required for paddlers under 13. Strongly recommended for everyone, regardless of skill level.
