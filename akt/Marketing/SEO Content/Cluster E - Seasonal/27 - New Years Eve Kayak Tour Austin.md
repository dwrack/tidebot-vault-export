# New Year's Eve Kayak Tour in Austin: A Different Way to Ring In the Year

Most New Year's Eve plans involve a bar, a crowd, and an Uber surge. Ours involves a kayak on Lady Bird Lake, the Austin skyline reflected on the water, and the Auditorium Shores fireworks going off above you.

It's a niche tour. It's also one of the most memorable nights on our calendar. Here's how the [New Year's Eve fireworks kayak tour](/new-years-eve-fireworks-tour/) works.

## What you're getting

A roughly 2-hour evening tour that includes:
- Sunset paddle down the lake (sunset is around 5:45 PM in late December)
- Skyline tour with the city fully lit for the holidays
- Position for the New Year's Eve fireworks
- Paddle back after the show

The fireworks are part of Austin's New Year Celebration on Auditorium Shores. They go off around 10 PM (separate from the midnight crowd at downtown bars), so it's an early-evening tour.

## The temperature reality

This is a winter tour. Late December in Austin can be:
- 50-65°F on a warm year
- 35-50°F on a cold year
- Below freezing during a cold snap (we cancel)

You will need real layers. The water is in the high 50s. The air will be cool. The wind on the open lake feels colder than the temperature reads.

## What to wear

- **Wool or synthetic base layer** (top and bottom)
- **Fleece or insulated mid-layer**
- **Waterproof jacket**
- **Wool socks**
- **Closed-toe water shoes or neoprene booties**
- **Beanie**
- **Light gloves**
- **Buff or scarf** for your neck

Skip cotton entirely. Skip down (loses insulation when wet).

## What to bring

- Phone in waterproof case (you'll want photos of fireworks)
- Headlamp for the paddle back in the dark
- Insulated water bottle (water bottles can freeze on cold nights)
- Snack
- Spare gloves in a dry bag

## What you'll see

- The Austin skyline lit for the holidays
- The Frost Bank Tower at night with its crown lit
- The Independent and the Austonian fully lit
- Fireworks launched from the Auditorium Shores area
- A few other dedicated paddlers braving the cold

You will NOT see the bat colony. The bats are in Mexico in winter.

## How long it runs

About 2 hours total:
- 4:30 PM: arrive, check in, gear up
- 5:00 PM: launch
- 5:45 PM: sunset, glassy water, skyline lighting up
- 6:30-7:00 PM: position for fireworks
- ~10 PM (varies): fireworks (we adjust based on the year's official schedule)
- 10:30 PM: back at the dock

Note: schedule depends on the year's official Austin New Year fireworks time. Some years it's earlier in the evening.

## What it's not

- Not midnight. We don't paddle in pitch dark. Show is earlier.
- Not for someone who hates being cold
- Not large group friendly (limited spots)
- Not budget-tier. Premium tour, premium pricing.

## What it is

- A real "first thing of the year" kind of experience
- Quiet by NYE standards
- A way to start the year on the water instead of in a crowd

## What if it's too cold?

We monitor the forecast. If the temp drops below 40°F or there's freeze warning, we cancel and offer rescheduling. Refunds for weather cancellations are full.

## Why this tour exists

Honestly, because we wanted to do it ourselves. It started as a thing we did on the water with friends. Then guests asked if they could come. Now it's a thing we run.
