# Fall Kayaking in Austin: The Underrated Best Season

If we had to pick one season to recommend Austin paddling, it'd be fall. September through early November on Lady Bird Lake is everything spring gives you minus the spring break crowd, plus dramatic light and the last weeks of the bat colony.

## Why fall is the underrated peak

**The heat finally breaks.** September is still warm but you can feel it dropping. October is the goldilocks month. Highs in the 70s and 80s.

**Crowds vanish.** Tourists drop after Labor Day. Locals are back at school. The lake gets noticeably emptier.

**Bat colony is still strong.** Bats are migrating south through October but the colony is still impressive in early-to-mid October.

**Light is dramatic.** Lower sun angle, longer golden hour, more dramatic shadows. Fall light on the Austin skyline is unbeatable.

**No mosquitoes (mostly).** The bugs taper off through September.

## Best fall months

**September:** still warm. Often hits the 90s. Tourist crowds dropping. Bats strong. A great month if you can handle some lingering heat.

**October:** the absolute best month. Cool mornings, warm afternoons, dramatic light, the bat colony's last hurrah. ACL Festival weekend (early October) is the one busy weekend.

**November:** cool to cold. Bats are gone. Skyline tours are spectacular and uncrowded. Layer up for sunrise paddles.

## What to wear in fall

**September:** same as summer. Light layers, sun protection.

**October:** quick-dry pants or shorts depending on the day. Light long-sleeve. Bring a fleece for sunrise or sunset.

**November:** layers. Base layer, mid-layer, light jacket for early morning. By midday you can shed.

## Best fall tours

**[Sunset bat tour](/bat-bridge-kayak-tour/)** through mid-October. The colony is migrating but still emerging in good numbers.

**[Sunset skyline tour](/sunset-skyline-tour/)** late October through November once the bats leave. The light is the best of the year.

**[Sunrise tour](/sunrise-tour/)** in October. The dawn fog over the lake is dramatic.

**[Skyline tour](/austin-skyline-tour/)** mid-day in October is genuinely comfortable.

## When the bats leave

The Mexican free-tailed bats start migrating south in late October. The colony shrinks rapidly through the month.

- **Early October:** colony is still strong, emergence is dramatic
- **Mid-October:** noticeable decline, smaller emergence
- **Late October:** scattered bats, sometimes no emergence
- **November:** gone

If seeing the bats is the priority, book by mid-October.

## Fall events to plan around

- **ACL Festival** (first two weekends of October): downtown is busy, parking is impossible, bookings sell out
- **Texas-OU Weekend** (varies): heavy traffic in town
- **F1 Weekend** (mid-October usually): downtown traffic chaos
- **Halloween:** downtown gets weird, Lady Bird Lake stays normal

If your trip overlaps any of these, book early.

## What it feels like in October

The water is mirror-flat by 5pm. The skyline is lit by the lower-angle sun. The bat colony is still emerging. There are 30% fewer paddlers than July. The temperature is 78°F instead of 95°F.

This is what we recommend to anyone visiting Austin who has flexibility on dates.

## What feels different about fall paddling vs. spring

Spring is bright and growing. Fall is golden and slowing down. Light is warmer in fall. Wildlife behavior shifts (turtles bask longer, herons hunt later). The water is warm from summer carryover, not just warming up.

Both are great. Fall has slightly better light. Spring has slightly better weather variability. Take whichever fits your trip.

## Common questions

**Is it warm enough to kayak Lady Bird Lake in October?**
Yes. October is one of the best months of the year. Highs in the 70s and 80s.

**Are the Austin bats still around in October?**
Until late October, mostly. Early October is your best bet for fall bat tours.

**Is November too cold to kayak Austin?**
No. Layered up, November is comfortable. Mornings can be in the 50s, afternoons in the 70s.

**When does the bat colony leave Austin?**
Late October. By November they've migrated to Mexico.

**Should I book a fall kayak tour ahead?**
ACL weekends and weekends near Texas-OU or F1 yes. Other weekends, you can often book 2-3 days out.
