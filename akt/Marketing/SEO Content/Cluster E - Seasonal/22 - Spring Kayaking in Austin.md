# Spring Kayaking in Austin: The Underrated Best Season

Most visitors think summer is the time to kayak Austin. Locals know better. Spring (March through May) is the underrated peak season on Lady Bird Lake. The weather is perfect, the crowds haven't built up yet, and the bats are back.

## Why spring beats summer

**Weather is ideal.** Highs in the 70s and low 80s. Mornings cool, afternoons warm, evenings perfect. None of the brutal July heat that pushes paddling to dawn or dusk only.

**Bat colony returns.** The Mexican free-tailed bats are back in March from their Mexico migration. Numbers grow through spring. By May the colony is strong.

**Wildflowers along the shore.** Bluebonnets and Indian paintbrush along the trail. The Hike-and-Bike Trail is at peak prettiness in late March to mid-April.

**Crowds are manageable.** Spring break (mid-March) is the one busy week. The rest of the season has tourist-but-not-overwhelmed crowds.

**Water temperature warms up.** From the 50s in early March to the low 70s by May. By late spring you can fall in without it being miserable.

## Best months in spring

**March:** still cool. Water is cold. Bats arriving. Tourists thin. Best for locals or anyone who likes empty water.

**April:** the goldilocks month. Warm but not hot. Bat colony growing. Wildflowers peak. Easter and spring break are crowded; rest of the month is great.

**May:** approaching summer. Bat colony solid. Water warm. Last comfortable mid-day paddling before summer heat hits.

## What to wear in spring

- Light layers (mornings and evenings can still be cool)
- Quick-dry shorts in April-May
- Pants in early March mornings
- Long-sleeve sun shirt for sun protection
- Sandals with back strap

Bring a fleece for sunset and sunrise tours. The lake cools off fast after sundown in spring.

## What to expect on the water

- Other paddlers, but not the wall-to-wall traffic of July
- Rowing teams during the week
- Spring sailboats and rowing regattas occasionally
- Wildlife: turtles waking up from winter, herons fishing, kingfishers diving
- Pollen if it's a dry spring (oak pollen can dust the water yellow)

## Spring weather watch

Two things to track:

**Cold fronts:** Texas spring cold fronts can drop temps 30 degrees overnight. Check the forecast 24 hours out. The lake is fine if you layer up.

**Severe weather:** spring is severe weather season in Texas. Tornadoes, hail, lightning. Always check the radar before launch. We don't run tours through severe weather.

## When the bats start emerging

- **Early March:** scattered. First arrivals. Worth seeing if you're already here.
- **Mid-March:** more bats, smaller crowds.
- **April:** strong colony. Less crowded than summer. Best ratio of bats to people.
- **Late May:** pups born. Colony starts approaching peak summer size.

## Common questions

**Is it warm enough to kayak Lady Bird Lake in March?**
Yes, with layers. Highs in the 70s by midday. Mornings cool.

**When do the Austin bats come back in spring?**
Early March, with numbers building through May. April is the sweet spot for fewer crowds.

**Are kayak tours running in spring?**
Yes, year-round. Spring tours are some of our favorites.

**Is Lady Bird Lake crowded in spring?**
Less than summer. Spring break (mid-March) is the only crowded week.

**What's the best month to kayak in Austin?**
April or October are the two best months. Both have ideal weather and moderate crowds.
