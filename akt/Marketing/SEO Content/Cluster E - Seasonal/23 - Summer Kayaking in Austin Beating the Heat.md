# Summer Kayaking in Austin: How to Beat the Heat on Lady Bird Lake

Austin summers are brutal. June through September the highs are in the 90s, often 100s, and the sun off the water doubles the heat. People still kayak. They just have to be smart about when.

Here's how to do summer kayaking on Lady Bird Lake without melting.

## The summer rule: paddle at the bookends

In June, July, August, and into September, the only comfortable windows to be on the water are:

- **Sunrise to 9 AM**
- **5:30 PM to 9 PM** (post-bat-emergence)

Mid-day (11 AM to 3 PM) is the worst. The sun is direct, the water reflects heat up at you, and there's no shade on most of the lake. People do it. We don't recommend it.

## Why mornings are best in summer

- Coolest part of the day (often in the 70s)
- Glassy water before wind picks up
- Almost no crowd
- You're done before the heat builds

Sunrise tours and morning rentals book up fast in summer. Reserve early.

## Why pre-sunset is the second best window

- Heat is breaking
- Skyline lights up at golden hour
- Bat colony emergence is the peak experience
- Tour operators run this slot heavily

The catch: this is the busiest window on the lake. Parking is harder, tours sell out, and the bridge crowd is significant. But the experience is worth it.

## What to wear in summer

- **Light-colored sun shirt** (UPF 50+ if you have one)
- **Quick-dry shorts**
- **Wide-brim hat with strap**
- **Polarized sunglasses with strap**
- **Sandals with back strap**
- **Reef-safe sunscreen reapplied every 90 minutes**

**Skip:** anything black, cotton t-shirts, baseball caps without straps.

## Hydration in summer

This isn't a joke. Texas summer plus paddling plus sun reflection means you're losing more water than you realize.

- **Bring at least 24 oz of water for a 2-hour tour.** 32 oz is better.
- **Drink water before launch, not just during.**
- **Add electrolytes.** Liquid IV, Nuun, or just a pinch of salt in your water bottle.
- **Avoid alcohol the morning of a paddle.** It dehydrates you and the heat compounds.

We've had guests get sun sick and stop short. It's almost always under-hydration.

## Sun protection

The sun off Lady Bird Lake in July is brutal. The water reflects UV up at you. A 10am paddle can give you a sunburn most people only get on a beach.

- Apply sunscreen 20 minutes before launch
- Reapply at the halfway point
- Cover the back of your neck and ears (people miss these)
- Wear a hat, not a baseball cap (the back of your neck burns)
- Long-sleeve sun shirts are warmer-feeling but actually keep you cooler than bare skin

## Best summer tour pick

**[Sunset bat bridge kayak tour](/bat-bridge-kayak-tour/)** is the marquee. The bat colony peaks in July and August. The 2-hour tour lands you at the bridge right at emergence. Heat is breaking, sky is colored, the experience is iconic.

The [sunrise tour](/sunrise-tour/) is the runner-up if you can't do sunset.

The [skyline tour](/austin-skyline-tour/) at midday is doable but uncomfortable in July and August. We recommend skipping it for the season.

## What about cooling tricks

A few small ones that help:

- **Wet your shirt before launching.** Evaporation cooling for the first 30 minutes.
- **Soak a buff or bandana in cold water** and wear around your neck.
- **Bring a small ice pack in a dry bag** if it's a 3+ hour rental.
- **Take a few minutes in the shade under a bridge** if you're feeling hot.
- **Splash water on your forearms** every 10 minutes.

## When to skip the paddle

- Heat advisories from NWS (usually 105°F+ heat index)
- Excessive humidity (over 70%)
- If you're hungover or dehydrated
- If you're not used to Texas heat (acclimate before you paddle)

We don't typically cancel tours for heat alone, but we send extra water and watch guests carefully on the hottest days.

## Bug situation in summer

- **Mosquitoes** show up at dusk. Bring bug spray for sunset tours.
- **Wasps and bees** can land on your kayak occasionally. Don't swat. They'll fly off.
- **Aquatic bugs** are mostly harmless. The lake doesn't have leeches or anything alarming.

## What's actually peak summer on Lady Bird Lake

**Bat tour:** absolute peak. 1.5 million bats, peak emergence size, biggest sky lighting up.

**Sunrise tour:** the most peaceful version of Austin you'll find. Fog rising off the water, joggers on the trail, no boats.

**Family rental at 9am:** a great way to spend a Saturday morning without battling the afternoon heat.

## Common questions

**Is it too hot to kayak Lady Bird Lake in July?**
Mid-day, yes. Sunrise and post-5pm are fine.

**What time should I kayak in Austin in summer?**
Before 9am or after 5:30pm.

**Are kayak tours canceled for heat?**
Rarely. We send extra water and adjust pace. Guests can opt out if they're uncomfortable.

**Is the water cooler than the air in summer?**
Yes. Lady Bird Lake water is in the high 70s/low 80s in summer. Air is in the 90s. Splashing yourself helps.

**Can I bring my dog kayaking in summer?**
Yes, on rentals, but bring extra water for the dog and watch for overheating. Sunrise and post-sunset are best. Mid-day isn't safe for dogs.
