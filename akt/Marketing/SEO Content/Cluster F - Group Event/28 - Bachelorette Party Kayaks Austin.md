# Bachelorette Party on Kayaks in Austin: A Different Take

If you're planning an Austin bachelorette and you're already tired of "Rainey Street, the SoCo crawl, the rooftop bar, the matching cowboy hats" before you've even started, here's a thought: do something on the water instead.

A private kayak tour on Lady Bird Lake works for groups of 6-20 and gets you something none of the other Austin bachelorette options give you: photos that don't look exactly like everyone else's.

## Why it works for bachelorettes

**It's something you do, not just something you go to.** Photos of your group on the water with the Austin skyline beat photos of you holding drinks at a bar.

**It's the morning option.** Schedule the kayak for the first activity of the day. Get sun, sweat a little, then go to brunch at South Congress, then to whatever else is on the agenda. You've got energy and a story before noon.

**Hangover-friendly.** Kayaking outside on calm water is one of the best things you can do hungover, in our experience.

**The bridesmaids who never paddle love it.** Lady Bird Lake is calm enough that even your friend who's terrified of water will be fine.

## Group sizes that work

- **6-10 people:** ideal. Single kayaks for everyone, one guide, easy logistics.
- **10-16 people:** still good. Mix of singles and tandems. One or two guides.
- **16-20 people:** doable. Multiple guides. We can usually accommodate.
- **20+:** possible but requires advance planning. Reach out to [our corporate events team](/corporate-events/).

## What we set up

For a typical bachelorette booking:
- Private 2-hour tour (no other paddlers in your group)
- Pick of route (skyline tour, sunrise tour, or sunset tour)
- All gear (kayaks, paddles, life jackets, dry bags)
- A guide who knows the lake
- Optional: photo stops at the boardwalk and skyline backdrop

We don't do drinks on the boats (open container is enforced on the lake). The post-paddle bar is on you.

## Best tour to pick

**Sunrise tour (8 AM):**
- Calm water, glassy reflections, no crowds
- Best photos
- Lets you do brunch and SoCo after
- Best for groups that wake up early

**Skyline tour (10 AM, 1 PM, 4 PM):**
- Mid-day, warmer, more energy
- Solid for any size group
- Standard package

**Sunset tour (6-8 PM):**
- Golden hour photos
- Pairs well with a dinner reservation after
- Most "vibe" of the three options

For most bachelorettes we'd recommend the sunrise or sunset tour for the photos.

## Photos

We can take group photos for you during the tour. Bring a phone in a waterproof case if you want shots from on the water.

The best skyline shot is from about 200 yards south of the South Congress Bridge, looking north at the downtown cluster. We stop there.

## What it costs

Pricing varies by group size and tour type. Single kayak rentals/tours are $50/person. Larger groups get custom pricing.

For a 10-person bachelorette, you're looking at roughly $500-700 total for a 2-hour private tour with all gear and a guide.

## Logistics

- **Parking:** free at our launch (200 E Riverside) for the group
- **Bathrooms:** on-site
- **Changing:** quick changing area on-site, but most groups arrive in their tour clothes
- **Storage:** we hold purses and phones during the tour
- **After:** we're a 5-minute Uber from Rainey Street, SoCo, and downtown bars

## What to wear

Bachelorette-specific:
- **Matching swimsuits or athletic outfits** photograph well on the water
- **Sash or veil:** fine, just attach securely (or it ends up in the lake)
- **Hat with strap** for the bride
- **Sunglasses** that don't cost $400 (in case they fall in)
- **Water shoes or sandals with back strap** (no flip-flops)

## What NOT to do

- Don't bring drinks on the water (ticketable)
- Don't bring glass anything (banned)
- Don't show up drunk (we'll have you sit out)
- Don't book on a holiday or ACL weekend without booking 2+ months ahead

## Pairing with the rest of the trip

A typical bachelorette day with kayak built in:
- 8 AM: sunrise kayak tour (2 hours)
- 10:30 AM: brunch at South Congress (Snooze, Jacoby's, etc.)
- 1 PM: shopping on SoCo
- 4 PM: pool party or hotel time
- 7 PM: dinner downtown
- 9 PM: bars on Rainey or 6th
- Midnight: out

The kayak is the morning anchor. Everything else falls into place around it.

## Common questions

**Can you do a bachelorette party on a kayak in Austin?**
Yes. Private group tours of Lady Bird Lake work well for 6-20 person groups. Book direct.

**How much does a bachelorette kayak tour cost?**
Around $50/person for standard tours. Larger groups get custom pricing.

**Can we drink on the kayaks?**
No. Open container is enforced on Lady Bird Lake.

**What if some of the bachelorettes have never kayaked?**
Lady Bird Lake is flat calm water. Beginners are fine. The 5-minute brief covers everything.

**How early should we book a bachelorette kayak tour?**
2-4 weeks ahead in spring/fall. 6+ weeks ahead for summer weekends. Longer if it overlaps ACL or any major Austin event.
