# Hosting a Group of 20+ on Lady Bird Lake: How We Do It

A 20-person group on Lady Bird Lake is bigger than it sounds. Most rental docks struggle with that volume. The lake itself can absolutely accommodate it. The question is whether your operator can.

We run [groups up to 60](/corporate-events/) regularly. Here's how the logistics actually work.

## What "20+" means in practice

- **20-30 people:** standard large group. One private tour, 2-3 guides, single-launch dock cycle.
- **30-50 people:** still doable single launch, multiple guides, staggered start times.
- **50-60 people:** multiple launch waves, full dock takeover. We close to other paddlers during your window.
- **60+:** possible with significant lead time and multi-day or split events.

## What a typical 25-person group looks like

- 25 paddlers
- Mix of singles and tandems (we adjust based on who can paddle and who can't)
- 2 guides
- 2-hour tour
- Free parking on-site for the group
- Restrooms accessible
- Optional post-tour catered lunch

Total event time including check-in and gear: 2.5-3 hours.

## Lead time you actually need

- **4 weeks:** minimum for a 20-30 person group
- **8 weeks:** for groups over 30
- **12 weeks:** if your event overlaps any major Austin event (ACL, F1, SXSW)
- **6 months:** for July 4th or New Year's Eve

Most groups underestimate this. Last-minute large bookings are usually impossible.

## Costs

Rough pricing (always confirm direct):

- 20-person group: $1,000-1,400 total for a 2-hour tour
- 30-person group: $1,500-2,000
- 50-person group: $2,500-3,500

Includes all gear, guides, parking, and the private tour structure. Add-ons (catering, photography, custom merch) are extra.

## What's harder with bigger groups

**Pace mismatch.** In any group of 20+, you'll have fast paddlers and slow paddlers. Guides have to manage this. We split the group into two paces if needed.

**Launch logistics.** Putting 25 boats in the water takes 15-20 minutes. We stagger to avoid chaos.

**Photography.** Group photos with 25 boats requires positioning. We use the boardwalk as a backdrop.

**Bathrooms.** 25 people show up, half need the bathroom right before launch. We recommend the group hit a bathroom before arrival.

**Skill range.** Some groups have an experienced paddler, some have all beginners. We adjust the route accordingly.

## What we do behind the scenes

For a 25-person event, our team:
- Reserves the dock window 2+ hours
- Assigns 2-3 guides
- Pre-rigs all boats with paddles and life jackets
- Briefs guides on the group profile
- Coordinates parking with the lot manager
- Has a backup plan for weather

You see a smooth event. We see a checklist running in the background.

## Group types that book us

- **Wedding parties** (rehearsal dinner alternative or post-wedding "decompress")
- **Conferences** (SXSW, ACL Live events, custom)
- **Family reunions** (we've done a 50-person reunion across 3 generations)
- **Student groups** (UT classes, study abroad orientations)
- **Travel groups** (Airbnb Experiences, tour operators bringing a bus group)

## What works for the planner

If you're the one organizing this:

- **Get a single point of contact at the operator.** Dealing with multiple staff slows everything.
- **Send a group roster ahead.** Names, emergency contacts, weight ranges (for boat assignment).
- **Tell us about anyone with special needs.** Pregnancy, mobility limitations, fear of water.
- **Build in 30 minutes of buffer time.** Some guests will be late.
- **Plan post-tour clearly.** Where's lunch? Where's the bus? Where do guests go?

## When to consider splitting the group

If your group is 40+, you have two options:
- One big private tour with multiple waves
- Two smaller tours back-to-back

Smaller tours give a more intimate experience but require more total time. Bigger tours are more cost-efficient. We'll help you decide.

## What can go wrong

- **Weather.** We watch the forecast. Light rain we go. Lightning we cancel.
- **A few late arrivals.** We can launch in waves.
- **Skill mismatch.** A non-swimmer in the group means they go in a tandem.
- **Group dynamics.** We've had a few groups where two factions don't get along; the lake doesn't fix that, but it spreads people out which helps.

## Common questions

**Can you accommodate a group of 30+ on Lady Bird Lake?**
Yes, with 4-8 weeks lead time. We've done up to 60 in a single event.

**How much does a 25-person kayak tour cost?**
Roughly $1,200-1,800 for a 2-hour private tour with all gear and 2 guides.

**Can I get a private group tour?**
Yes. Groups of 10+ can book a private tour with no other paddlers from outside the group.

**Do you offer catering with corporate events?**
We partner with local caterers. Confirm 1-2 weeks ahead.

**What's the maximum group size you can handle?**
60 in a single event with planning. Larger possible with multi-wave structure.
