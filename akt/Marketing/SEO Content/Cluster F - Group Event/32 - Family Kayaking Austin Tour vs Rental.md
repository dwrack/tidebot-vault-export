# Family Kayaking in Austin: Tour vs. Rental With Kids

Lady Bird Lake is one of the easier places to kayak with kids. It's calm, shallow near the shore, and short enough that kids don't tap out before you're done. Here's the honest version of how families actually do it, by age.

## Age guidelines

- **Under 5:** not recommended on a tour. Possible on a tandem rental with a parent for 30 minutes max.
- **5-7:** tandem kayak with a parent. Half-hour to 90 minutes max.
- **8-10:** tandem with a parent for tours. Solo possible on rentals if confident.
- **11-13:** solo kayak in calm conditions. Life jacket required for under 13.
- **14+:** solo kayak fine, can join standard tours.

## Tandem vs. single by family size

**One adult, one kid (5-12):** tandem kayak. Adult in back, kid in front.

**Two adults, one kid:** kid in tandem with one adult, second adult in single.

**Two adults, two kids:** two tandems, one parent per kid, OR one tandem (parent+kid) and one single each for the older kid and other parent.

**Two adults, three+ kids:** mix of tandems based on swim ability. Reach out and we'll set up the right combo.

## Tour vs. rental for families

**Take a tour if:**
- Kids are 8+
- You want context and stories ([the skyline tour](/austin-skyline-tour/) commentary works for kids)
- You want a guide to handle safety
- You don't want to figure out routes and timing

**Rent if:**
- Kids are 5-7 (shorter window of attention)
- You want to bail early if a kid melts down
- You want flexibility on pace and stops
- You're a confident paddler yourself

For families with mixed ages, [renting with one or two tandems](/rental/) usually beats a tour. You can adjust on the fly.

## Best tours for families

**Sunrise tour (8 AM):** quiet water, no crowds, kids are fresh. Works well if you can get them up early.

**Skyline tour (10 AM):** mid-morning, comfortable temps, easy 2-hour structure.

**Skyline tour (1 PM):** post-lunch, but kids may flag late. Mid-day heat in summer is a factor.

**Bat tour (sunset):** kids 8+ generally do well. Younger kids tap out from the long stretch on the water.

We don't recommend the sunrise tour for kids under 7 (early wake-up) or the bat tour for kids under 6 (long late tour).

## What kids actually like

In rough order:
1. **Turtles on logs** along the east side of the lake
2. **Going under bridges** and the echo
3. **The bats** (older kids, 6+)
4. **Paddling fast** for short bursts
5. **The skyline** (older kids who care about cities)

What kids don't love: long stretches of paddling without a stop, hot sun, getting splashed in cold water, being told to slow down.

## Tips for parents

- **Pack snacks.** A granola bar mid-tour buys you 30 more minutes of paddling.
- **Bring extra water.** Kids drink more in the heat than you'd think.
- **Apply sunscreen 20 minutes before launch.** Re-apply at the halfway point.
- **Plan for 90 minutes max** for kids under 8 (even if you booked 2 hours).
- **Don't push through a meltdown.** The lake is short. Land at the nearest public access if needed.
- **Bring a small toy or fidget** if you have a kid who needs distraction.

## What to wear (kids)

- Quick-dry shorts (no jeans)
- Rash guard or t-shirt
- Sun hat with strap
- Sunglasses with strap (kid-sized)
- Water shoes or sandals with back strap
- Sunscreen reapplied

Bring a change of clothes for after. Kids end up wetter than adults.

## Safety with kids

- **Life jackets are required for everyone under 13** by law on Texas waters
- We size them properly before launch
- Lady Bird Lake is shallow near the shore (kids can stand)
- No motorboats means no wakes
- Guide stays close to families on tours

If a kid is anxious about water, brief them ahead of time. Show them photos. Talk about the life jacket. The 5 minutes of prep makes a big difference.

## What can go wrong

- Kid gets cold (bring layers)
- Kid gets sunburned (sunscreen, hat)
- Kid drops phone in the lake (use a dry bag, don't hand them the phone)
- Kid wants to swim (banned on Lady Bird Lake)
- Kid gets seasick (extremely rare on calm water; happens occasionally)

## What we provide for kids

- Kid-sized paddles (lighter, shorter)
- Tandem kayaks for kid-parent pairs
- A guide who's worked with kids before

## Common questions

**What's the youngest age for kayaking in Austin?**
5 years old in a tandem with a parent. Younger only on private rentals at parent's discretion.

**Are life jackets required for kids on Lady Bird Lake?**
Yes. Required for all paddlers under 13.

**Can young kids do a kayak tour?**
Yes, ages 5+ in tandems. We recommend the morning skyline tour for younger kids.

**Should I rent or take a tour with my family?**
Rent if you want flexibility. Tour if you want context and structure.

**How long can kids paddle before they're done?**
60-90 minutes for kids 5-8. 2 hours for older kids.
