# Corporate Team Building on Lady Bird Lake: Why It Actually Works

Most corporate team-building activities are bad. Trust falls, escape rooms, painting classes. The kayak version of team building works for a specific reason: it's a real thing you do together, not a manufactured exercise.

We run [corporate kayak events](/corporate-events/) on Lady Bird Lake for groups of 10-60. Here's how they actually work.

## Why kayaking specifically

**Everyone participates.** No one sits on the sidelines. There's no "I'll just watch."

**Real shared experience.** You're all paddling the same water for the same two hours. The team conversation in the office on Monday is "remember when we paddled past Frost Bank Tower and the wind kicked up?" not "remember when we did the trust fall and I felt awkward?"

**It's outdoors.** A lot of office workers don't get outdoor time during the week. The break is genuinely valuable.

**It's downtown.** No 90-minute drive each way. We're 5 minutes from most downtown offices.

**Photos are good.** Corporate Slack channels eat this content up.

## Group sizes that work

- **10-20:** ideal. Mix of singles and tandems. One or two guides. Two-hour tour.
- **20-40:** still good. Multiple guides. Same two-hour structure.
- **40-60:** doable. Multiple guides, staggered launches. Plan ahead.
- **60+:** possible with significant lead time. Reach out directly.

- Private 2-hour tour (just your team, no other paddlers)

What we can add for an additional cost:

## What we don't do

- **Drinking on the water.** Open container is enforced. We can recommend a downtown bar for after.
- **Pure motivational speeches.** We're a kayak company. We give a safety brief, not a TED talk.
- **Risk-management exercises.** We focus on a real shared experience.
