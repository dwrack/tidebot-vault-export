# Birthday Party on Kayaks in Austin: How to Plan One

A kayak tour on Lady Bird Lake works for almost any kind of birthday party: a 10-year-old's, a 30th, a 50th. Here's how we set them up and what to think about.

## Kid birthdays (ages 5-12)

**What works:**
- Group of 6-12 kids plus adult chaperones
- Tandem kayaks where each kid is paired with a parent or guide
- A 90-minute paddle (kids tap out around 2 hours)
- Sunrise or 10am tour to beat the heat
- Cake at the dock or a picnic table after

**Age limits:**
- We start at age 5 in tandems with a parent
- Solo kayaks for kids 10+ if they can demonstrate basic paddling
- Life jackets required for everyone under 13

**What we set up:**
- Tandem kayaks for kid-parent pairs
- Adult-sized life jackets and youth life jackets
- A guide who's good with kids
- Slower pace, shorter route

## Teen birthdays (ages 13-17)

The age where kayaking becomes a real party option. Teens can paddle solo, the social dynamic of being on the water works, and you can do a 2-hour standard tour.

**What works:**
- Sunrise or sunset tour
- 8-15 person group
- Mix of singles and tandems
- Pizza after at South Austin spots

## 21st-30th birthdays

Bachelorettes are usually different (see [bachelorette guide](/blog/bachelorette-party-on-kayaks-in-austin-a-different-take/)) but generic 20s/30s birthdays work great as kayak parties.

**What works:**
- Sunset bat tour or sunset skyline tour
- Group of 6-15
- Singles for adults, tandems for couples
- Drinks after (not on the water; open container is enforced)
- Dinner reservation downtown right after the tour

## Milestone birthdays (40, 50, 60)

We see a lot of these. A milestone birthday on the water with the people you actually want to be there beats a big-restaurant booking 90% of the time.

**What works:**
- Smaller group (6-12)
- Sunrise tour for the meditative version
- Sunset tour for the party version
- Photographer along the shore for milestone shots
- Dinner at a nice downtown spot after

## What to plan for

**Group size:** 6-15 is the sweet spot for birthday parties. Smaller is intimate. Larger gets logistically harder (multiple guides, staggered launches).

**Tour pick:**
- **Sunrise:** quiet, glassy water, photo-friendly
- **Skyline (10am, 1pm, 4pm):** standard package, best for groups with kids
- **Sunset bat:** the iconic Austin experience (March-October)
- **Sunset skyline:** romantic version (year-round)

**Pre/post tour:**
- Brunch before sunrise tours (many spots open at 8am)
- Lunch picnic at Auditorium Shores after a 10am tour
- Dinner reservation downtown after a sunset tour

## Cost

Roughly:
- 6-person birthday: $300-450 for a 2-hour tour
- 12-person birthday: $600-800
- 20-person birthday: $1,000-1,400

Custom pricing for larger or unusual setups.

## What we don't do (yet)

- We don't do cakes or catering (you arrange separately)
- We don't have a dedicated party room (the dock is communal)
- We don't decorate the boats with banners or balloons (visibility safety)

What we can do:
- Sing happy birthday on the water (guides love this)
- Bring a small portable speaker for music on the dock
- Help coordinate post-tour dinner spots

## What to bring

Same as a normal tour, plus:
- Cake or cupcakes for after (in a real cooler if hot)
- Plates and forks
- Speaker if you want music at the dock
- Any party signage for the parking lot, not on the water
- Camera (or photographer if it's a milestone)

## Common questions

**Can you have a kid's birthday party on a kayak in Austin?**
Yes, ages 5+ in tandem kayaks with a parent. Ages 10+ can solo if comfortable.

**What's a good age for a kayak birthday?**
8-12 for a kid party, 16+ for a teen party. We do all ages above 5 with the right setup.

**Can we have cake on the water?**
We discourage food on the boats. Cake works at the dock or a nearby picnic table after.

**How much does a kayak birthday party cost?**
$300-1,400 depending on group size. About $50/person on average.

**Can we play music?**
Not on the water (sound carries and disturbs other paddlers). At the dock yes.
