# Austin Kayak Tours — SOP: Pre-Tour Preparation
*2026-04-21 | Vessel type: kayak/paddle vessel*

> **Purpose:** Ensure every tour launches safely, legally, and professionally. This SOP covers everything from the day before through post-tour closeout.

---

## Day-Before Checklist

- [ ] Confirm all bookings in FareHarbor: headcount, special requests, tour type (kayak vs SUP)
- [ ] Check weather forecast: wind, precipitation, heat index
- [ ] Send 48-hour reminder to all confirmed guests (see Guest Comms SOP)
- [ ] Confirm guide/staff assignments for each tour slot
- [ ] Verify all gear is staged and accounted for (kayaks, SUPs, paddles, PFDs)

## Morning-Of Checklist (Staff Arrival)

- [ ] All kayaks and SUPs inspected for hull damage, drain plugs secure
- [ ] Paddles counted and assigned (1 spare per 10 guests)
- [ ] PFDs counted and sorted by size, all USCG-approved Type III
- [ ] First aid kit stocked and accessible
- [ ] Safety whistle on every guide
- [ ] Communication device on guide (cell phone in waterproof case)
- [ ] Launch site at 200 E Riverside Dr clear and set up

## Guest Check-In Procedure

- [ ] Greet guests by name when possible
- [ ] Verify headcount against FareHarbor booking manifest
- [ ] **Check waiver status in FareHarbor.** The manifest shows who has and hasn't signed. Most guests sign pre-trip via the automated email link. For anyone who hasn't:
  - Pull up that guest's booking-specific waiver on your phone or tablet
  - Hand it to them to sign on the spot
  - Do NOT use a generic QR code. Every waiver must be tied to that guest's specific booking
  - No guest launches without a signed waiver. No exceptions
- [ ] Fit PFDs: guide checks every guest individually
- [ ] Deliver safety briefing (see script below)
- [ ] Confirm any medical or physical limitations (kayak weight limit: 450 lbs combined for tandems)
- [ ] Photo opportunity at launch (social media + guest memory)

## Safety Briefing Script

> *"Welcome, everyone! Before we head out, a few safety points. Your life jacket must be worn at all times on the water. It's required and it's what keeps you safe. Stay within sight of your guide at all times. If you flip, stay calm: your kayak floats, you float. We'll help you get back in. If you hear two short whistle blasts, stop paddling and look to your guide. Any questions before we launch?"*

## Weather Hold Protocol

**Decision maker:** Lead guide / Manager on duty

| Condition | Action |
|---|---|
| Wind > 15mph sustained | Hold, reschedule |
| Lightning within 10 miles | Cancel / shelter immediately |
| Visibility < 0.25 mile | Hold |
| Extreme heat advisory (heat index >110) | Evaluate, add water breaks or cancel |

**Weather hold communication:** Use guest communication template in Guest Comms SOP.

## Post-Tour Checklist

- [ ] All gear returned, rinsed, and stored properly
- [ ] Incident report filed if any injury or incident occurred
- [ ] Guest count confirmed out (everyone accounted for)
- [ ] Review request triggered (auto if connected via GHL, otherwise manual SMS)
- [ ] Any feedback noted for manager coaching log
