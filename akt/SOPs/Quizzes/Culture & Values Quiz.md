# Culture & Values Quiz
## Austin Kayak Tours

**Passing score: 80% (8 of 10 correct)**

---

### Question 1
A guest asks you to recommend a restaurant nearby after the tour. You don't know the area super well yet. What do you do?

A) Say "I'm not really from around here, sorry"
B) Tell them to check Yelp
C) Recommend 2-3 places you've looked up ahead of time as part of being prepared for this job
D) Make something up so you seem knowledgeable

**Answer: C**
Part of being a guide in Austin is knowing Austin. You should have a short list of nearby restaurant and bar recommendations ready to go. Guests expect local knowledge, and "I don't know" makes the whole operation feel amateur.

---

### Question 2
You're running a tour and one guest is clearly not having a good time. They're lagging behind and look frustrated. What's your move?

A) Keep the tour moving, they'll catch up
B) Drop back, check in privately, offer help with technique or a switch to a different boat
C) Announce to the group that everyone needs to keep up
D) Ignore it, some people just don't enjoy kayaking

**Answer: B**
A guide who notices and quietly addresses a struggling guest is the difference between a 3-star and 5-star review. Never call someone out in front of the group.

---

### Question 3
A group of college students wants to bring a cooler of beer on the kayak tour. What do you tell them?

A) "Go for it, just don't go overboard"
B) "Alcohol isn't allowed on our tours, but there are plenty of great spots to grab drinks after"
C) "I'll look the other way as long as you're responsible"
D) "Let me check with my manager"

**Answer: B**
No alcohol on tours, period. But redirect positively. Don't be preachy about it, just be clear and offer an alternative.

---

### Question 4
You arrive at the launch site and notice another tour company's gear is taking up part of your staging area. What do you do?

A) Move their gear out of the way
B) Complain about them to your guests
C) Work around it and mention it to management later
D) Confront their guide about it

**Answer: C**
Don't badmouth other companies and don't create a confrontation at the launch site. Adapt, run your tour, and let management handle the space issue.

---

### Question 5
A guest on a bat tour is loudly talking and using their phone flashlight as the bats start emerging. What's your approach?

A) Yell at them to stop
B) Quietly paddle over and remind them that bats are sensitive to noise and light, ask them to turn off the flash
C) Let it go, they paid for the tour
D) End the tour early because they ruined it for everyone

**Answer: B**
Handle it quietly and directly. The other guests will appreciate you protecting the experience without making a scene.

---

### Question 6
You're scheduled for a morning tour but you're feeling slightly under the weather. Not terrible, but not 100%. What do you do?

A) Tough it out, the tour needs a guide
B) Text management as early as possible, explain the situation, and let them decide
C) No-show and text an apology after
D) Show up and tell the guests you're not feeling great so they set lower expectations

**Answer: B**
Communicate early. Management can find a replacement if they have time. Showing up sick or no-showing both hurt the business.

---

### Question 7
A guest gives you a $50 tip and asks if you can take them on a private off-schedule paddle session. What do you say?

A) "Meet me here tomorrow at 6 AM"
B) "I appreciate that! Private tours can be booked through our website, and I'd love to be your guide"
C) "I can't do that, sorry"
D) Take the money and figure it out later

**Answer: B**
Redirect to the proper booking channel. Never run side trips using company equipment or brand. Be grateful for the tip and channel the enthusiasm into a legitimate booking.

---

### Question 8
What's the right time to arrive before a scheduled tour?

A) 5 minutes
B) 15 minutes
C) 30 minutes
D) An hour

**Answer: C**
30 minutes before trip start. You need time to stage gear, inspect equipment, and be ready when guests arrive. Showing up 5 minutes before is basically showing up late.

---

### Question 9
A guest makes an insensitive comment about another guest's body size during PFD fitting. What do you do?

A) Laugh it off to ease tension
B) Ignore it and keep moving
C) Shut it down calmly and directly: "We fit everyone properly here, let's get you set up" and redirect
D) Ask the offending guest to leave

**Answer: C**
Don't let it slide and don't escalate. A brief, confident redirect protects the targeted guest and signals that kind of thing isn't tolerated, without derailing the experience.

---

### Question 10
You overhear a guest saying they expected the tour to be different based on what they saw online. How do you handle it?

A) Tell them to take it up with customer service
B) Acknowledge their expectation, focus on making this tour the best it can be, and mention it to management after
C) Get defensive about the business
D) Offer them a refund on the spot

**Answer: B**
Acknowledge without being defensive. You can't fix a marketing mismatch on the water, but you can make sure this guest has a great experience anyway. Flag it to management so they can review what's online.
