### Question 1
You arrive for morning inspection and discover you're short two paddles for today's bookings. What do you do?

A) Cancel the last two bookings
B) Check if any kayak trips can be switched to SUPs (which use a different paddle), check spare inventory, and contact management if you're still short
C) Have two guests share one paddle per tandem
D) Borrow paddles from another company at the launch site

**Answer: B**
Problem-solve with what you have first. SUP swaps, spare inventory, tandem combinations. If you're still short after exhausting options, contact management. Don't cancel bookings without trying.

---

### Question 2
A guest booked a SUP tour but shows up and says they'd rather kayak instead. You have extra kayaks available. What do you do?

A) Tell them they booked SUP and that's what they're getting
B) Accommodate the switch if equipment allows. Note the change in FareHarbor.
C) Charge them extra for the swap
D) Tell them to rebook online

**Answer: B**
If you've got the gear, make the swap. It takes 30 seconds and the guest leaves happy. Log it in FareHarbor so the records are accurate.

---

### Question 3
FareHarbor goes down right before a tour. Guests are arriving and you can't pull up bookings or waivers. What do you do?

A) Cancel the tour until the system is back
B) Collect names, confirm verbally that they booked, have them sign paper waivers, and run the tour. Update FareHarbor later.
C) Just run the tour without waivers
D) Send everyone home

**Answer: B**
Tech fails sometimes. Have paper waivers as backup. Collect names, run the tour, and reconcile in FareHarbor when it's back online. Never skip waivers entirely.

---

### Question 4
You're running a tour from the secondary meeting point at 7 East Ave and a guest shows up at the primary location (200 E Riverside Dr). They call you confused. What do you do?

A) Tell them they should have read the booking confirmation
B) Give them clear directions to 7 East Ave, confirm how long it'll take to get there, and hold the launch a few minutes if possible
C) Tell them to rebook for another day
D) Ignore the call, you're busy prepping

**Answer: B**
Meeting point confusion happens. Guide them over calmly and hold a few minutes if you can without impacting the group. If they can't make it in time, help them reschedule.

---

### Question 5
At end-of-day count, you're missing one PFD. What's the right response?

A) It'll probably turn up tomorrow
B) Check the launch area, check inside all kayaks/SUPs, check vehicles. If it's still missing, report it to management immediately.
C) Order a replacement online and don't mention it
D) Borrow one from another operator

**Answer: B**
Missing equipment gets a thorough search and an immediate report. PFDs don't just disappear. Either it was left somewhere or a guest took it. Management needs to know today, not tomorrow.

---

### Question 6
A bat tour is scheduled for 8:15 PM and at 5 PM you see thunderstorms forecast for the evening. What do you do?

A) Wait until 8 PM and see what happens
B) Make the call by 5 PM: contact guests to cancel/reschedule, notify management, log the cancellation
C) Hope the forecast is wrong
D) Run the tour and come back early if it storms

**Answer: B**
Bat tours require an early weather call because guests are planning their evening around it. By 5 PM you have enough forecast data to make the call. Don't let guests drive to the site just to hear it's cancelled.

---

### Question 7
You're a Rental Agent and a walk-up customer wants to rent a kayak, but all available kayaks are reserved for upcoming guided tours. What do you tell them?

A) "Sorry, we're fully booked for rentals today"
B) "Let me check what's available. All kayaks are reserved for guided tours right now, but I can check availability later today or help you book a rental for another time."
C) Let them take one of the tour kayaks, the guide can figure it out
D) Send them to a competitor

**Answer: B**
Be transparent about inventory without overpromising. Help them find an alternative time. Never pull equipment allocated to a booked tour.

---

### Question 8
During a tour, your radio dies and you're leading a multi-guide trip. The other guide is 200 yards behind you with the back of the group. What do you do?

A) Keep going, they know the route
B) Slow down or stop, let the groups merge back together, and use visual or voice signals for the rest of the tour
C) Paddle back to the other guide and leave your group unattended
D) End the tour early

**Answer: B**
If comms fail, close the distance between groups and stay within visual/voice range for the rest of the trip. Don't leave your portion of the group unattended.

---

### Question 9
A guest left their car keys in the kayak hatch and the kayak has already been racked and locked up in the trailer. They're standing in the parking lot. What do you do?

A) Tell them to come back tomorrow
B) Open the trailer, retrieve the keys from the kayak, return them to the guest
C) Give them the trailer key to get them themselves
D) Call management and make the guest wait

**Answer: B**
Just help them. Open the storage, find the keys, hand them over. It takes 2 minutes and that person will remember the experience positively. Never give a guest access to your storage area.

---

### Question 10
You notice another guide has been consistently showing up 10 minutes before tour start (instead of 30) and rushing through safety briefings. What do you do?

A) Mind your own business
B) Mention it to management privately. Safety briefings and prep time exist for a reason.
C) Confront the guide in front of guests
D) Start showing up 10 minutes before too, since it seems to work fine

**Answer: B**
Don't confront them in front of guests, but don't ignore it either. Rushed briefings and late arrivals are safety risks. Management needs to know so they can address it directly.
