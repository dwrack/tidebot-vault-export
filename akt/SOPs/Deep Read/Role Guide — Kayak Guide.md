# Role Guide — Kayak Guide
## Austin Kayak Tours

### What This Role Is

You're the face of Austin Kayak Tours on the water. Guests remember their guide more than anything else about the experience. Your job is to keep people safe, show them a great time on Lady Bird Lake, and make them feel like they're in good hands from the first handshake to the last paddle stroke.

Austin Kayak Tours operates two roles:

**Kayak/SUP Guide** — leads tours on the water. Paid per trip. 1099 contractor.

**Rental Agent** — handles walk-up and pre-booked rentals at the launch site. Paid $15/hr. 1099 contractor.

Some people do both. Either way, you're independent contractors under Davey Jones' Locker.

### Requirements

- Current CPR/First Aid certification (must be on file before your first trip)
- Clear background check
- Swim proficiency (you need to be comfortable in the water, not just poolside)
- Paddle experience preferred but not strictly required. If you can't paddle, you'll learn fast during orientation.
- Must be comfortable on Lady Bird Lake in varying conditions: heat, wind, occasional chop

### What Makes a Good Guide Here

Technical skill matters, but personality carries the day. The guides guests rave about in reviews tend to share a few things:

They read the group fast. A bachelorette party needs a different energy than a family with young kids. Adjust on the fly.

They know Austin. Guests ask about restaurants, bars, things to do. Having 3-4 solid recommendations in your pocket goes a long way. You don't need to be an encyclopedia, but "I don't really know the area" is a bad look.

They don't rush. The tour has a route and a time window, but it shouldn't feel like a forced march. Let people take photos. Pause for wildlife. The bat tours especially need this, the whole point is the moment.

They stay calm when something goes sideways. Someone capsizes, weather rolls in, a guest panics. Your composure is the thing that keeps it from escalating.

### Training Progression

1. **Orientation** — Equipment overview, launch site walkthrough (200 E Riverside Dr and the secondary point at 7 East Ave), FareHarbor basics, Weather Hold Protocol, Safety Briefing Script memorization
2. **Shadow trip** — You ride along and observe an experienced guide run a full tour
3. **Co-guide trip** — You lead the tour with an experienced guide there as backup
4. **Debrief** — Honest conversation about what went well and what needs work
5. **Solo sign-off** — Manager or senior guide observes you running a trip independently. If you pass, you're on the schedule.

This usually takes 1-2 weeks depending on trip availability and your schedule.

### Code of Conduct

- Show up 30 minutes before your trip start time. Every time.
- No alcohol or drugs before or during trips. Zero tolerance.
- Treat every guest like they chose your tour over dozens of other things they could've done in Austin. Because they did.
- Keep your phone in a dry bag and use it only for safety, navigation, or logging.
- No badmouthing other tour companies, guides, or businesses.
- If you can't make a trip, notify management as early as possible. Last-minute cancellations hurt the whole operation.

### Pay and Scheduling

Guides are paid per trip. Rate depends on tour type and group size. Details are covered during onboarding.

Rental agents are paid $15/hr for time at the launch site.

Scheduling happens through FareHarbor. You'll see your assigned trips there. If you want more trips, communicate that to management and stay reliable with the ones you've got. Consistency earns more bookings.

### Ongoing Development

- Bat tour certification (additional training for evening departures)
- SUP-specific guiding if you're kayak-only to start
- First responder upgrade courses (optional but encouraged)
- Seasonal check-ins with management on performance and feedback
- Guest review highlights shared with you so you know what's landing

The guides who stick around and build a reputation here end up with the best schedules and the most trips. It's straightforward: be good, be reliable, be someone guests want to paddle with.
