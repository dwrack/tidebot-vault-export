# Post-Tour Closeout
**Tour's not done when guests get off the water. Close it out right.**

1. Help guests out of kayaks/SUPs at the landing area
2. Collect all paddles and PFDs from guests
3. Thank each guest personally
4. Ask if they'd be willing to leave a review: "If you had a great time, a Google review really helps us out"
5. Hand out or mention any business cards, sticker giveaways, or referral info
6. Rinse and inspect each kayak/SUP for new damage
7. Rack or secure all boats
8. Rinse paddles and store upright or hanging
9. Rinse PFDs with fresh water if used in Lady Bird Lake (algae builds up fast)
10. Clean up launch site: pick up any trash, move cones/signage
11. Log the trip in FareHarbor: note actual headcount, no-shows, any incidents

> [!tip]
> The review ask works best right after a genuine "thank you" and while the experience is still fresh. Don't make it weird, just a quick mention.

> [!warning]
> Algae on PFDs is a health issue and a bad look. Rinse them every time, no exceptions.
