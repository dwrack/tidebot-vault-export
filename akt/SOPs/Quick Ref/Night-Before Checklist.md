# Night-Before Checklist
**Confirm tomorrow's trips are locked in before you leave for the night.**

1. Open FareHarbor and review all bookings for tomorrow
2. Confirm headcount per trip and note any special requests or accessibility needs
3. Check weather forecast for wind speed, lightning risk, and temperature
4. If wind >15 mph or storms forecast, flag for potential Weather Hold (see Deep Read: Weather Hold Protocol)
5. Confirm guide(s) assigned to each trip and text to verify availability
6. If a guide is unavailable, contact backup guides immediately
7. Verify equipment count matches tomorrow's bookings: kayaks, SUPs, PFDs, paddles
8. Confirm launch site at 200 E Riverside Dr is clear, or if using secondary meeting point at 7 East Ave, note which trips go where
9. Check that bat tour bookings (if scheduled) have correct sunset-adjusted start times
10. Charge any electronics (radios, phone batteries, lights for bat tours)

> [!warning]
> Never assume a guide confirmed just because they were scheduled. Get a text-back confirmation every time.

> [!tip]
> Bat tours are weather-sensitive and time-sensitive. Double-check sunset time and adjust departure if needed.
