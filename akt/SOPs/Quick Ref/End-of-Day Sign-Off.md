# End-of-Day Sign-Off
**Last person out confirms everything's buttoned up.**

1. Count all kayaks and SUPs against inventory list
2. Count all paddles against inventory list
3. Count all PFDs and confirm they're hung or laid flat to dry (never stuffed wet into a bin)
4. Confirm first aid kit is restocked if anything was used
5. Check that all gear storage is locked/secured
6. Walk the launch site: no gear left behind, no trash, signage pulled if needed
7. Report any equipment damage or loss to management via text or Slack
8. Report any guest incidents, complaints, or notable interactions
9. Confirm no upcoming bat tours missed or double-booked for tomorrow
10. Lock up trailer/storage

> [!warning]
> PFDs stored wet will mildew fast in Austin heat. Hang them or lay flat, every time.

> [!tip]
> A quick photo of your gear count at end of day takes 10 seconds and saves arguments later.
