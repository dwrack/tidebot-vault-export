# Contractor Onboarding Checklist
**Manager-facing. Every step completed before a new guide or rental agent touches a guest.**

1. Send culture doc and have contractor confirm they've read it
2. Collect signed W-9 (both Kayak/SUP Guides and Rental Agents are 1099)
3. Verify CPR/First Aid certification is current (copy on file)
4. Run background check and confirm clear
5. Confirm swim proficiency (verbal confirmation or observed assessment)
6. Note any paddle experience (preferred but not required)
7. Clarify role and pay structure:
   - Kayak/SUP Guide: per-trip, 1099
   - Rental Agent: $15/hr, hourly 1099
8. Schedule orientation session covering:
   - Equipment overview (kayaks, SUPs, PFDs, paddles)
   - Launch site walkthrough at 200 E Riverside Dr and secondary point at 7 East Ave
   - FareHarbor booking system basics
   - Weather Hold Protocol
   - Safety Briefing Script (must be able to deliver from memory)
   - Bat tour specifics if they'll guide evening trips
9. Schedule shadow trip: new contractor observes experienced guide
10. Schedule co-guide trip: new contractor leads with experienced guide present
11. Debrief after co-guide trip and address any gaps
12. If ready, schedule solo sign-off trip with manager or senior guide observing
13. Add to scheduling rotation in FareHarbor
14. Add to group text/Slack channel

> [!warning]
> No solo trips until the sign-off trip is completed and approved. No exceptions.

> [!tip]
> TPWD requires all commercial vessels to be registered. Make sure new guides know which kayaks/SUPs carry registration and where the stickers are.
