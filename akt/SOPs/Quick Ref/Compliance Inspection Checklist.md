# Compliance Inspection Checklist
**Texas Parks & Wildlife Department (TPWD) compliance. Stay ahead of inspections.**

1. Confirm all commercial kayaks and SUPs have current TPWD vessel registration
2. Verify registration stickers are visible and not peeled/faded
3. Confirm commercial outfitter permit is current (if required for operation area)
4. Check that every vessel on the water has a USCG-approved Type III PFD for each occupant
5. Verify PFDs are readily accessible (not buried under gear)
6. Confirm a throwable flotation device (Type IV) is available at the launch site
7. Check that a sound-producing device (whistle) is on each guide
8. Verify fire extinguisher availability if any motorized support boats are in use
9. Confirm all guides have CPR/First Aid certification on file
10. Check that the business liability insurance policy is current
11. Verify waivers are being collected for every guest via FareHarbor
12. Confirm no expired or recalled PFDs are in rotation (check manufacture date)
13. Review incident log for any unreported events

> [!warning]
> TPWD can conduct on-water inspections without notice. Every vessel must be compliant every day, not just when you expect a check.

> [!tip]
> PFDs have a usable life. If the foam feels crunchy or the fabric is degrading, pull it regardless of what the manufacture date says.
