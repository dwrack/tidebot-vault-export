## Season rundown

### What went well
- **Guests loved the guides.**
  - Two-thirds of 2026 reviews name someone by name.
  - Safety came up again and again: "made sure we stayed safe in the choppy waters" (River), "avoided choppy water around the caves" (Devin), "very reassuring for first time kayakers" (Lucas).
  - Torey got "Best guide at the company. Give him a bonus."
- **Tips were real money.**
  - $31.5k came through FareHarbor gratuity.
  - Top earners per tour were Lea ($84), Torey ($75), Ethan ($74) and Lucas ($65).
  - Lea's number gets a lift from e-bike tours, where she ran 14.
- **The core carried the load.**
  - Devin, Jackson, Lucas and Lea ran 286 of the 534 departures between them, a bit over half.
  - Jackson, Devin, Kasey and Lea were the ones still working in September.
- **Good habits stuck where someone owned them.**
  - Lea took over schedules and the office, and trained Kasey and Tristan.
  - Devin got the VHF radios working and wrote a channel guide.
  - Ethan and Jackson made the 6am water and conditions calls.

### What broke
1. **Leadership collapsed in late July.**
   - Bella's last shift was Jul 20, with no handoff in Slack that survives.
   - Vesper was out sick or in hospital on and off from Jul 14 to Jul 31. On Jul 30 a guide asked whether they still had a manager.
   - Lea filled the gap while still guiding.
   - The July and August revenue drop in the season review lines up with this window.
2. **Attrition between Aug 3 and Aug 17 gutted capacity.**
   - River quit on Aug 3, and Torey no-showed the same day.
   - Tristan's loan stint ended.
   - Ethan's last shift was Aug 16 and Lucas's was Aug 17.
   - Earlier exits: Anna (Jul 24) and Mckayla (Jun 12).
   - This is the same August walk-off as 2025.
3. **Payroll failures were the company's fault, not the guides'.**
   - Ethan went most of the season unpaid (resolved Oct 2).
   - Andrew's direct deposit questions went unanswered for weeks.
   - These happened under the manager gap, and they cost goodwill with exactly the guides you want back.
4. **Guide assignment in FareHarbor was sloppy.**
   - 47 departures with 315 guests had no guide assigned. September alone had 14.
   - Those departures carry $1,525 of gratuity that FareHarbor can't credit to anyone.
5. **Balances were left open.**
   - 30 past guided-tour bookings still show $6,644 due.
   - 15 more past bookings still have "Need to collect remaining balance" checked.
   - The Aug 28 cleanup ask went unanswered until the bot pushed it on Sep 19.
   - Balance collection is a check-in or office job, so the guide on the trip isn't necessarily the person who missed it. Per departure: Jackson's trips $1,151, River's $923, Lea's $806, Ethan's $678. None of this is a guide-performance strike on its own.
6. **The end of the season was a mess.**
   - On Oct 1 the shop and house were left trashed.
   - Vesper, Devin and Alexis left without notice.
   - Vesper was unreachable.
   - Kasey was the only one who responded.
7. **Security.**
   - Device and Apple ID passwords were posted in the Slack channel on Jul 11, Jul 23 and Aug 1. Rotate them.

### Staffing lessons for 2027
- **Hire for September, not July.** Make the October completion bonus (already designed in `HR/Retention Bonus Pool — Design.md`) the headline of every offer, and announce it in writing before the season starts.
- **Name a backup manager.** Pick someone the day the manager is hired. Lea effectively was the backup this year without the title or the pay.
- **Use Homebase scheduling, or accept that lateness can't be measured.**
  - Only 39 of 867 shifts were scheduled in Homebase, so late clock-ins and no-shows can't be measured from the data.
  - Slack is the only record of them.
- **Hard rule: nobody runs a FareHarbor departure with an empty crew slot.** That one rule fixes most of the orphaned tips and the attribution gaps.
- **Fix payroll setup before day one.** Clear duplicate Homebase profiles, confirm direct deposit is working, and get the employee agreement signed by everyone. Only 6 people signed the training-camp waiver in 2026, and no returning guide did.
