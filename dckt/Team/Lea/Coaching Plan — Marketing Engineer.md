# Lea — Marketing Engineer Coaching Plan (Summer 2026)

## The Vision

You're not just "the social media person." You're DCKT's marketing engineer: someone who builds content systems, uses AI as a co-pilot, and owns the engine that keeps our brand visible and authentic all summer. Think less "post 3x a week" and more "here's my system for turning one kayak tour into five pieces of content in under an hour."

This plan is designed to start with your strengths, stack some early wins, and build from there. No fire hose of information week one.

---

## Phase 1: Discovery + Easy Wins (Week 1-2, late April)

**Goal:** Figure out what Lea's already good at, build confidence, get her creating immediately.

### Kickoff Call (this week)
- What platforms do you already use and love?
- What kind of content do you naturally gravitate toward? (video, photo, writing, design)
- Show me an account you think does social really well. Why?
- What feels intimidating or unclear about this role?
- Any AI tools you've played with? (ChatGPT, Canva AI, CapCut, etc.)
- What's your relationship with Door County? Been here before, know the vibe?

### First Assignments (low pressure, high reward)
- [ ] Follow 20 Door County accounts (@doorcountykayaktours competitors, local businesses, tourism boards). Screenshot 5 posts you think are great and tell me why.
- [ ] Write 3 practice captions for old DCKT photos (we'll provide). No rules, just your voice.
- [ ] Film a 30-second "day in the life" style clip of literally anything. Just get comfortable with the camera.
- [ ] Browse our Instagram (@doorcountykayaktours) and note what you'd do differently. Be honest.

**What we're learning:** Is she a natural writer? Visual thinker? Video-first? Analytical? This shapes everything after.

## Phase 2: Brand Voice + Content Systems (Weeks 3-4, early May)

**Goal:** Lock in the DCKT voice and teach her the workflow, not just the tasks.

### Brand Voice Download
- Review the [[DCKT Social Engagement Voice]] guide
- Core tone: warm, adventurous, "your friend who lives in Door County." Never corporate, never salesy.
- The test: "Would David actually say this?" If it sounds like a brand account, rewrite it.
- Practice: comment on 5 DC business posts as @doorcountykayaktours. We review together.

### Content System Training
- **The One-Tour Engine:** Film one tour → edit into a Reel + carousel + 2 stories + a caption that could be a blog excerpt. Target: 45 minutes start to finish by end of Phase 2.
- **AI Co-Pilot Setup:** Get her comfortable using ChatGPT/Claude for brainstorming captions, repurposing content, generating hashtag sets, drafting engagement comments. She edits and decides, AI drafts.
- **Batch Days:** Teach her to batch-shoot content (take 2 hours on a tour day, get a week of content). Not reactive posting.
- **Canva templates:** Set up 3-4 reusable templates she can swap photos into for stories and carousels.

### Guardrails (autonomy framework)
- She can post without approval after Phase 2, as long as it passes the voice guide
- Hard stops: no pricing/discount posts without David/Bella approval, no responding to negative reviews solo, no paid promotion without sign-off
- Everything else: run with it

## Phase 3: Ownership + Experimentation (Weeks 5-8, May-June)

**Goal:** She's running the content engine independently. Start experimenting.

### Weekly Rhythm
- **Monday:** Plan the week's content (3-5 posts, daily stories on tour days)
- **Tour days:** Batch-capture content on water
- **Wednesday or Thursday:** Engagement session (comment on 10-15 local accounts, respond to DMs/comments)
- **Friday:** Quick review with Bella or David. What worked? What flopped? 10 minutes max.

### Experiments to Try
- [ ] Reels vs. carousels: which gets more saves and shares?
- [ ] Behind-the-scenes guide content vs. scenic/guest-facing content
- [ ] "Ask a local" Q&A in stories
- [ ] Collaborating with local businesses on cross-posts
- [ ] UGC repost pipeline (guest photos/videos with permission)

### Stretch Goals (only if she's ready)
- TikTok content (repurposed from Reels)
- Blog post drafts from tour content
- Email newsletter content blocks
- Engagement with @midsommargirls and other woman-led marketing teams for cross-pollination and inspo

## Phase 4: Full Speed (July-August)

**Goal:** She's the marketing engineer. Systems are running, she's optimizing.

- Content calendar owned entirely by Lea
- Monthly analytics review: what's growing, what's not, adjust
- Pitch one new content idea per month (a series, a collab, a campaign)
- Mentor newer guides on capturing good content on their tours
- Document her systems so they're repeatable next season

## How to Coach Her (Notes for David + Bella)

- **Start with strengths.** If she's a natural on camera, lean into Reels first. If she writes well, start with captions and stories. Don't force the thing she's weakest at first.
- **Celebrate early wins publicly.** Repost her best content, shout her out in GroupMe, tell guests "our marketing engineer Lea shot this."
- **Feedback as questions, not corrections.** "What were you going for with this one?" works better than "this isn't on brand."
- **Give her real responsibility early.** The fastest way to build confidence is trusting her with something that matters and watching her nail it.
- **Keep it fun.** This is a summer in Door County making content about kayaking. If it starts feeling like a corporate marketing job, something's wrong.
- **Connect her with peers.** The Midsommar Girls IG collab is a great example. She shouldn't feel like she's figuring this out alone.
