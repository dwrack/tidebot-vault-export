# Paddler's Circle — Closing Ritual Scripts
*Beat 11 of the 11-beat pathway. ~60 sec at the end of every tour. Visit-count-aware.*

---

## What this is

The Paddler's Circle is DCKT's returning-guest program, defined in `Brand Story & Values.md` (lines 70-93). For first-timers, this closing ritual is their **induction** into the Circle — the moment that gives them a reason to come back. For returning guests, it's the **milestone recognition** that says we know how many times they've been with us and we care.

This is one of three peak moments in the 2-hour Cave Point tour (Beats 1, 7, and 11). It's the last thing guests experience before they walk back to their car, which means it's the moment they'll narrate to whoever they go home with.

Visit count comes from FareHarbor customer history. Guide checks before tour starts (Beat 5). Bella or Jessica can flag visit count in the day's Slack thread to make sure the guide doesn't miss it.

---

## When and where it happens

After Beat 10 (return & take-out). Group has stepped out of kayaks. PFDs are coming off. Gear is being stowed but the moment hasn't shifted to "walking to the cars" yet. Guide pauses the group near the dock, not in the parking lot.

If multiple guests have different visit counts in the same group: do the **first-timer induction for the whole group**, then pull the returning guest aside privately for the milestone moment. Don't make the first-timers feel left out.

---

## First-Timer Induction (most common script)

Delivered to the whole group. ~60 seconds.

> *"Before everyone heads out — one more thing. You just did Cave Point. Most people in Wisconsin will never see those caves. The ones that do mostly drive past Hwy 57 and don't know what's down here."*
>
> *(pause, eye contact, scan the group)*
>
> *"From today on, you're part of what we call the Paddler's Circle. That just means: you're not a stranger to us anymore. Next time you book — and we hope you do — you'll get access to a route we don't put on the public calendar. Skill upgrades, real cave entry technique, things we don't teach first-timers. And we'll know who you are when you walk back in."*
>
> *(hand each guest a signed Paddler's Circle postcard)*
>
> *"This is yours. The back has my name, the date, the tour. Keep it. When you book your next trip, mention it and we'll know."*
>
> *(beat — let them look at it)*
>
> *"Best moment from today? I want to know what stuck."*
>
> *(listen to their answers — this becomes the review ask and the favorite-moment log per the customer journey playbook)*
>
> *"Thanks for paddling with me. Drive safe getting home."*

**Token:** Paddler's Circle postcard. Front = DCKT branded illustration (caves, kayak, simple). Back = pre-printed fields the guide fills in:

- Guest's first name (or party name)
- Tour date
- Guide's first name
- One handwritten word in the lower corner that captures something specific to this group ("strong paddlers" / "cave whisperers" / "rookie squad")

Postcards are stocked at the launch in a waterproof case in the gear box. Guide signs them in the parking lot before tour or during gear stow.

## 3rd Visit — "Welcome back to the Circle"

Pull the returning guest aside after the first-timer induction is done. ~30 seconds.

> *"[Name] — this is your third time out with us. That means something. Most people who try this once don't come back. Three times means you're a paddler now."*
>
> *(hand them the 3-visit postcard — different design from the first-timer one)*
>
> *"This one's different from the one new folks get today. We're paying attention. Next time you book, we're going to take you on the locals' route — it's not on the website. See you then."*

**Token:** Paddler's Circle 3-visit postcard. Different design — same brand family but visibly different. Mentions visit count.

---

## 5th Visit — Physical token

Same private moment as 3-visit. ~45 seconds.

> *"Five paddles. That's not a tourist anymore — that's part of this place. We made these for people who've been out with us five times. There aren't many."*
>
> *(hand them the 5-visit physical token)*
>
> *"Wear it, throw it on your kayak, whatever. It's yours. And next time you book — we've got something you haven't done. Sunset paddle, ice cave in March, late-fall colors run. Tell me what you want and we'll make it happen."*

**Token (TBD by David / Bella):**
- Enameled pin or patch with DCKT Paddler's Circle 5 design
- Or: branded carabiner that clips to a PFD
- Or: small Lautenbach's cherry juice bottle (ties to the partnership) with a custom label
- Whatever it is, must be something the guest would actually keep/use, not landfill swag

5-visit guests should also get a hand-written personal note from David in their next post-tour follow-up email, separate from the standard GHL automation.

## 10th Visit — Name on the wall

This is rare and major. ~60 seconds.

> *"[Name] — ten paddles. I've been thinking about how to do this right."*
>
> *(pause)*
>
> *"There's a wall at the shop with names on it. Ten paddlers in DCKT history. You're one of them now. Your name goes up next week. When you come back next time, walk in and look for it."*
>
> *(handshake or hug — read the moment)*
>
> *"Thanks for what this has meant. Genuinely."*

**Token:** Their name physically added to a designated wall at the shop. Permanent. Likely a small brass plate or hand-painted board, formatted consistently. David maintains the wall; new names go up within 7 days of qualifying tour.

Optional: David sends a personal handwritten letter to 10-visit Circle members. Not from a template. Not from GHL. Actual paper.

## What NOT to do

- **Don't manufacture the moment.** If a guest is rushing back to their car for a flight, give them the postcard and a real thank-you, but don't force the script. The moment is the gift, not a transaction.
- **Don't recite it word-for-word.** These are anchor phrases, not lines. The brand doc explicitly prohibits scripted monologues. Know what to say, then be a human.
- **Don't skip it because you're tired.** This is the highest-leverage 60 seconds of the day for retention. Skipping it on a busy day kills the program.
- **Don't say "Welcome to the family."** Off-brand, generic, and corporate.
- **Don't reveal the higher-tier tokens to first-timers.** Part of the magic is that they discover the next level on their next visit. Tease that "we have things for returning guests" without enumerating the milestones.
- **Don't email the script to the team.** Walk every guide through it in person during training. The ritual loses energy if it's circulated as a doc only.
