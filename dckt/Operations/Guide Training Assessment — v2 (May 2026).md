# Guide Training Assessment — v2

*Sign-off sheet for clearing a new guide to run solo. Maps to the 2-hour Cave Point Operations Playbook, the Cold Water Go-No-Go Protocol, and the Paddler's Circle closing.*

---

## How to use this sheet

Work top to bottom. Mark each line **No / Yes / Needs work** and put a note when it's anything other than a clean Yes.

**★ = hard dealbreaker.** A guide cannot run solo with any ★ item still at No or Needs work, full stop. These are the lines where a miss puts a guest in the water, in the cold, or in the path of a trailer. Everything else is coachable on the job.

A guide is cleared to run **solo** only when:
- Every ★ item is a Yes, and
- No more than **3** non-★ items are at Needs work (each with a follow-up date below), and
- The assessor signs off at the bottom.

A guide can run **as a sweep / second guide** under a cleared lead before all of the above is true, at the assessor's discretion.

*From the Cold Water Go-No-Go Protocol. This is DCKT's highest-likelihood serious-injury risk. A guide who can paddle but can't make this call is not cleared.*

*The whole point of the tour. A guide can pass every prep line above and still deliver a flat paddle. This section is what earns the 5-star review. Maps to the 11-beat pathway.*

## What changed from v1 (the original PDF)

- **Added Section 2 (Cold Water & Conditions Judgment)** — the original sheet never tested the GREEN/YELLOW/RED call, our single highest-likelihood serious-injury risk.
- **Added Section 10 (On-Water Tour Delivery)** — the original was all prep, rescue, and breakdown. Nothing tested actually running the tour: the dock briefing, group management, the cave peak moment, the Paddler's Circle.
- **Added Section 11 (Emergency Response)** — scenario-based, including the radio protocol the gear list assumes but never checked.
- **Added a scoring rubric and ★ dealbreakers** so "cleared to run solo" means something specific.
- **Added a guide/assessor header and a sign-off block** for the paper trail.
- **Added a first aid / CPR cert line** (with the explicit no-ACA-claim note).
- **Fixed the cut-off "NRS straps... with 4 knot" line** (flagged for David to confirm the standard).
- **Added the one-waiver-per-activity / no-transfer** nuance to guest check-in for combo days.
