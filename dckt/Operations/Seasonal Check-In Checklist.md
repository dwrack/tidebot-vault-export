# DCKT Seasonal Check-In Checklist

Recurring ops check-in run 3x per season: **July 1, August 1, end of season (week after Labor Day — season effectively winds down at Labor Day, this round is the cleanup/winterize pass while some staff are still around)**. Owner: Bella (Business Manager), confirmed via #dckt-tour-logs. David gets an emailed summary each round.

## 1. Brochures & Rack Cards
- [ ] All open brochure/rack card requests from hotels, visitor centers, and chambers fulfilled within the standard turnaround
- [ ] Current stock count at the shop — reorder if running low
- [ ] Any partner location reporting they're out and haven't reordered

## 2. Life Jackets (PFDs)
- [ ] Full fleet washed (freshwater rinse, no mildew smell)
- [ ] Buckles, zippers, and D-rings functioning on each unit
- [ ] Damaged/worn units pulled from rotation and flagged for replacement
- [ ] Correct size spread still matches guest mix (kids/adult sizing)

## 3. Fleet — Kayaks & Paddles
- [ ] Hulls checked for cracks, gouges, or soft spots
- [ ] Footpegs, seats, and hatches functioning
- [ ] Paddle blades/shafts checked for cracks or loose ferrules
- [ ] Dry bag zippers/seals still watertight

## 4. E-Bike Fleet
- [ ] Tire pressure checked on all units
- [ ] Battery health / charge cycles checked, charging routine confirmed with staff
- [ ] Brake pad wear checked
- [ ] Locks functioning (self-checkout model has no gate — this is the one mechanical backstop)

## 5. First Aid & Safety Gear
- [ ] First aid kit contents checked, expired items replaced
- [ ] Fire extinguishers (shop + vans) in date, gauge in green
- [ ] Throw ropes / rescue gear present and in good condition
- [ ] Radios and phone chargers present and charged

## 6. Vans & Trailers
- [ ] Oil change due/current on each van
- [ ] Tire tread and pressure checked
- [ ] Lights (brake, turn, trailer) functioning
- [ ] Tie-down straps in good condition, no fraying
- [ ] Registration and insurance cards current and in each vehicle
- [ ] **End-of-season round only:** battery tenders installed on all vehicles before winter storage so batteries don't die

## 7. Signage & Shop Exterior
- [ ] Check and report back — fading, damage, or anything that hurts first impression (no fix required at check-in time, just flag it)
