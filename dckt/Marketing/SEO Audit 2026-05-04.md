**Date:** 2026-05-04

## Executive Summary

DCKT runs 4 GSC properties, but only 2 of them are doing real work. The main site (`doorcountykayaktours.com`) is the brand authority and gets 1,500+ clicks. `cavepointpp.com` is quietly outranking the main site for the most valuable cluster you own (Cave Point sea cave terms). `doorcountykayak.com` is a near-duplicate ghost site stealing scraps. `dcdetours.com` has no GSC data at all (either no traffic or not verified properly).

The portfolio's #1 problem is not content quality. It's **self-cannibalization on the Cave Point cluster**. You have 3 sites competing for the same 8-10 commercial keywords, splitting authority three ways. The Cave Point SEO PERT in your Planning folder is building a 16-page cluster on the wrong domain to win this back, because the alt domain is the one currently winning.

Top 3 priorities: (1) Pick a winner for Cave Point and 301-redirect the losers, (2) Fix or kill the two underperforming domains, (3) Push the 4 page-2 town pillars (Baileys Harbor, Fish Creek, Ephraim) to page 1 with title rewrites.

## CRITICAL: Cross-domain cannibalization

This is the single biggest finding in the audit. Same queries are appearing across multiple properties, splitting clicks and confusing Google about which page to rank.

### Confirmed cannibalized queries (3+ properties competing)

| Query | DCKT-main pos | cavepointpp pos | doorcountykayak pos | Google's pick (winner) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| **cave point kayak tour** | 4.6 | **3.8** | not ranking | cavepointpp.com |
| **cave point county park kayaking** | 3.7 | **3.6** | not ranking | cavepointpp.com (tie) |
| **door county kayaking** | **9.8** | 9.3 | 12.8 | cavepointpp slightly ahead |
| **kayaking door county** | **10.3** | 9.3 | not ranking | cavepointpp |
| **kayak door county** | **3.8** | 6.1 | 8.8 | DCKT-main |
| **door county kayak tours** | **5.5** | 12.0 | 9.4 | DCKT-main |
| **kayaking in door county** | 14.5 | 12.4 | 17.9 | cavepointpp (page 2) |
| **cave point county park** | 16.6 | **18.0** | not ranking | nobody — both stuck on page 2 |

### What this means

For the **Cave Point cluster** (the most valuable commercial cluster you own), Google currently prefers `cavepointpp.com`. This contradicts the assumption baked into your Cave Point SEO PERT, which is building 16 new pages under `doorcountykayaktours.com/cave-point/...` to "reclaim" the cluster.

You're not reclaiming. You're competing with yourself.

For the **generic Door County terms** ("door county kayak tours", "kayak door county"), DCKT-main wins clearly. That part is healthy.

The third site, `doorcountykayak.com`, doesn't win anything. It's pure tax — it eats some impressions, depresses CTR on the queries it shows up for, and offers no upside.

### Cannibalization severity ranking

1. **Cave Point cluster** (severe) — 3 sites compete; cavepointpp wins; DCKT-main is building new content that will compete with the winner.
2. **"door county kayak tours" branded query** (moderate) — DCKT-main wins, but doorcountykayak.com is splitting ~462 impressions of search demand.
3. **"cave point county park" informational** (moderate) — both DCKT and cavepointpp have a `/7-reasons-why-cave-point-county-park-is-a-must-visit/` blog post. Same title, two domains. This is the textbook case for consolidation.

## Internal linking & cross-linking strategy

### Should the sub-brands link back to DCKT-main?

**doorcountykayak.com → DCKT-main:** Yes, aggressively. This site doesn't deserve its own ranking strategy. Every page should canonical to or 301-redirect to its DCKT-main equivalent. If you can't kill it (FareHarbor multisite constraints, partner agreements, etc.), then at minimum every page should have a clear sitewide link to the main DCKT site as the "official" booking destination.

**cavepointpp.com → DCKT-main:** Selectively. CPPP earns its keep on Cave Point queries. Don't dilute that. But for non-Cave-Point queries (general Door County kayaking, town queries, sauna, e-bike), CPPP should link out to DCKT-main as the authority. Specifically:
- CPPP's "things to do in Door County" content → link to DCKT town pillars when published.
- CPPP's broader kayak how-to content (kayak sizing, calories burned) → keep on CPPP, but add an outbound link to DCKT-main's Cave Point tour booking page since that's the commercial intent.

**dcdetours.com → DCKT-main:** Diagnose first. Until we know if the site is live and what it ranks for, no recommendation.

## Top 3 critical issues across the portfolio

### 1. Cave Point cluster split across 2 domains (severe)

DCKT-main and cavepointpp.com both rank top 5 for the highest-value commercial Cave Point queries. CPPP wins by a hair. DCKT-main is building 16 new pages that will compete with the winner. Doing nothing means: in 60 days you'll have 32+ Cave Point URLs across 2 domains all chasing the same 8 queries. Pick a winner, 301 the loser.

### 2. doorcountykayak.com is a near-zombie alt domain

54 clicks in 90 days from a property that exclusively competes with your main domain on brand and category terms. Every query it ranks for is already covered better by DCKT-main. This is pure SEO tax. Either redirect it to DCKT-main (recommended) or kill it entirely.

### 3. Three town pillars stuck at page 2 with thousands of impressions

Baileys Harbor, Fish Creek, Ephraim — all sitting at position 11 with 1,800-2,700 impressions per quarter each. Combined that's ~7,000 impressions you're not converting because you're below the fold. The Towns keyword map already has the rewrite plan. Nothing's blocking execution.

## Action plan

### Quick wins (this week, ≤1 day each)

1. **Title and meta rewrite on the 3 town pillars.** Rewrite titles to match modern town-guide intent ("Things to do in Baileys Harbor: a kayaker's guide") and update meta descriptions. Target moving each from pos 11 to pos 7-8.
2. **CTR rewrite on `/kayak-tours/cave-point-county-park/`.** Page is at pos 4.6 with only 6.6% CTR for "cave point kayak tour." Title is likely too plain. Test "Cave Point Kayak Tour: Sea Caves & Cliffs (4.9★, 240+ Reviews)" or similar with social-proof.
3. **Add internal link from pregnancy-kayaking blog to Cave Point tour.** 127 clicks/qtr of pre-qualified low-impact paddlers, currently a dead-end content page.
4. **Add internal link from `/blog/door-county-bike-routes/` to `/e-bike-tours/`.** 90 clicks/qtr informational post that should be funneling to the e-bike commercial page.
5. **Diagnose dcdetours.com.** Visit the site, check it's serving 200 + indexable. Either fix or formally retire the GSC property.

### Strategic plays (this quarter, 1-4 weeks each)

1. **Pick a winner for Cave Point. Probably consolidate cavepointpp.com → DCKT-main.** Reasoning: DCKT is the brand authority customers know, has the better review profile (4.9★ on 240+ reviews), and the Cave Point SEO PERT is already building on DCKT. To "win" CPPP's current rankings, 301-redirect the top 5 CPPP Cave Point pages to their DCKT-main equivalents (or to the new pages from the PERT once published). Keep CPPP alive only if there's a non-SEO business reason (separate FareHarbor company, separate insurance, etc.); otherwise the cleaner play is full migration. Expected outcome: DCKT-main inherits CPPP's Cave Point rankings, the 16-page Cave Point cluster wins on a domain Google already trusts.
2. **Sunset doorcountykayak.com via 301 redirects.** Map every URL to its DCKT-main equivalent, set up server-level 301s, decommission the WordPress install. Expected outcome: small ranking lift on brand queries (no more split signals), one less property to maintain.
3. **Execute the Towns keyword map.** Build `/baileys-harbor-door-county/`, `/fish-creek-door-county/`, `/ephraim-door-county/` as proper visitor-guide pillar pages per the map. 301 the old `/blog/7-reasons-why-...` URLs to the new pillars to consolidate the existing rankings. Expected outcome: 3 page-1 rankings on town queries with combined ~7k impressions/qtr.

### Consolidate vs differentiate verdict

**Consolidate.** The 4-domain portfolio is a legacy of FareHarbor multisite spinups, not a deliberate brand strategy. None of the sub-brands has enough SEO equity or audience differentiation to justify the split, and the cannibalization is provably hurting the main brand. Recommendation: 1 main brand (DCKT), retire `doorcountykayak.com`, migrate `cavepointpp.com` into DCKT under a `/cave-point/` cluster, decide whether `dcdetours.com` is a real business or a parked domain. The only argument for keeping CPPP separate is if it's a legally distinct LLC or operates from a different launch site under different permits — and even then, the SEO play is to have one of them noindexed.
