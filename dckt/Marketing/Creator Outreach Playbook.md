# Creator Outreach Playbook: Door County Kayak Tours

**Owner:** Bella + Lea
**Business Instagram:** @doorcountykayaktours (or current handle)

---

## What This Is

Proactive outreach to content creators, offering them a free sea cave kayaking tour in exchange for nothing. No contract, no posting requirements, no talking points. If they make something cool, great. If they just enjoy the paddle, that's fine too.

This is different from the UGC Repost Playbook (which is reactive, finding content that already exists). This is about building relationships with creators before they've ever been in your kayak.

## Why This Works for DCKT

We sell the experience creators want to film. Kayaking through sea caves on Lake Michigan, towering limestone cliffs, crystal clear water, cave exploration from the water. That's content. The visuals are absurd: turquoise water, rock formations, the scale of the cliffs against a single kayak. It films itself.

Door County also has a concentrated tourist season, so creator content that goes viral in spring/early summer drives bookings all season long. Timing matters here more than most businesses.

The cost is nearly zero. An empty seat on a tour that's going out anyway costs us nothing. The content a creator produces from that seat would cost $500-2,000 to produce ourselves.

## Who to Target

### The Sweet Spot: Micro-Influencers (2K-50K followers)

Don't chase big accounts. You want creators who:
- Live in or frequently visit Wisconsin/Midwest
- Post outdoor, adventure, travel, or family activity content
- Have real engagement (comments from real people, not bot spam)
- Show original storytelling, not just lip-syncs and trending audio reposts
- Would genuinely enjoy a sea cave kayak tour

### Creator Types to Prioritize

| Type | Why | Example |

| Outdoor/adventure creators | Direct alignment with the product | Hikers, paddlers, nature photographers |

| Travel creators passing through | One viral post can outperform a month of local content | "Door County weekend guide" format |

## How to Reach Out

### DM from the Business Instagram Account

Not your personal account. Not email. A direct message from the business page.

### First DM: Cold Outreach

Keep it short. No pitch deck energy.

**Template A: Simple invite:**

> Hey [name]! Love your content, especially [reference a specific recent post]. We run sea cave kayak tours on Lake Michigan in Door County. Paddling through actual caves, cliff faces, turquoise water. We'd love to have you out on a tour. No strings, no posting requirements, just come enjoy it. Want us to set something up?

**Template B: For travel creators:**

> Hey [name]! Saw you're heading to Door County (or Wisconsin). We run kayak tours through the sea caves on Lake Michigan. It's one of those things that looks unreal on camera because it kind of is. We'd love to have you out while you're here. Free, no catch. Let us know if you want us to hold a spot.

**Template C: For creators who posted about a competitor or similar experience:**

> Hey [name]! Saw your [post about Cave Point/similar activity]. We run guided kayak tours right through those caves. Totally different perspective from the shore. Would love to have you out to see it from the water. On us, no strings. Interested?

### Rules for Outreach
- **Reference something specific** about their content. "Love your content" alone sounds automated.
- **Don't ask them to post.** The moment you say "in exchange for a post," it becomes transactional and the content suffers.
- **One follow-up max.** If they don't reply after 3-5 days, send one casual follow-up. After that, move on.

**Follow-up DM (if no reply after 5 days):**

> Hey just bumping this in case it got buried. No pressure either way!

### If They Say Yes

> Awesome! When works for you? We run tours [days/times]. Pick whatever works and I'll hold a spot. You can bring a friend too if you want.

Then:
- Hold the spot, confirm the day before
- Let the guide know a creator is coming (just so they're aware)
- After the tour, send a quick thank you DM: "Hope you had a good time! No ask from us, just glad you came out."

### If They Post Something

- Like it immediately
- Leave a genuine comment (something specific about their content, not "Thanks for sharing!")
- Repost using the UGC Playbook process (ask permission, download, repost with tag)
- Flag top performers for ads per the UGC playbook
- Add them to your "relationship" tier for future invites

### If They Don't Post

That's fine. Don't follow up asking where their post is. The relationship still exists. They had a good experience, they'll mention you organically at some point, and you can invite them back next season.

## The "Access as Currency" Playbook

| What you have | What creators want | How to offer it |
|---------------|-------------------|-----------------|
| Sea caves (limestone, turquoise water) | Backdrops nobody else has | "Want to come out on an early morning paddle before tours start?" |
| Sunrise/sunset on Lake Michigan | Golden hour light in the caves is unreal | Recommend the right tour time for their content style |
| Behind-the-scenes access | Content that feels exclusive | "Want to ride along on a guide training day?" |
| Group experiences | Content with friends performs better | "Bring 3-4 friends, we'll set you up" |
| Seasonal changes (fall colors, calm water days) | Unique seasonal content opportunities | Flag perfect-condition days for creators |

### Relationship Tiers

- **One-timers**: Came, enjoyed it, maybe posted, no ongoing relationship. That's fine.
- **Repeat creators**: Loved it, posted, want to come back. Invite them for seasonal openings, perfect weather days.
- **Brand friends**: Genuinely love what you do, mention you unprompted, bring friends. These are gold. Give them first access to everything.

## Frequency & Time Commitment

| Task | How often | Time |
|------|-----------|------|
| Search for new creators to invite | Twice a month | 20-30 min |
| Send outreach DMs | As you find them | 5 min each |
| Coordinate tour dates for interested creators | As replies come in | 5 min each |
| Post-tour thank you DM | Day after their tour | 2 min |
| Review if they posted + respond | Week after their tour | 5 min |

**Total: ~1-2 hours per month.** This is not a heavy lift.

## Seasonal Note

Door County has a compressed season. Front-load creator outreach in May/June so content is circulating when people are booking July/August tours. A Reel that goes up in June drives bookings for the rest of summer. Don't wait until peak season to start.

## TikTok Creator Outreach (added June 2026)

TikTok is its own channel now (@doorcountykayaktour). Our own analytics say the account lives almost entirely on Search, not For You, so creators with real Door County / Wisconsin reach are how we borrow distribution we can't get organically yet. Same philosophy as the rest of this doc: invite, don't transact. We never make a post a condition.

**Who to target on TikTok:**
- Wisconsin and Midwest outdoor/travel creators, 1K to 50K followers (micro beats mega, their audiences actually drive to Door County).
- Door County travel and "things to do" accounts, plus Green Bay / Milwaukee / Chicago day-trip creators.
- Niche fits: dog-travel creators (we allow dogs on tours), van/RV life, "hidden gem" and bucket-list accounts.

**Where to find them on TikTok:**
- Search: "door county," "things to do in door county," "wisconsin road trip," "midwest travel."
- Hashtags: #doorcounty, #wisconsinadventure, #cavepoint, #travelwisconsin, #midwestisbest, #doorcountytiktok.

We'd love to have you out on a tour this season, on us, no strings and no posting requirement. Just come paddle. Want us to hold a spot?
