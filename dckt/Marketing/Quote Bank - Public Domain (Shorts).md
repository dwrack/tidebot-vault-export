# Quote Bank: Public Domain (Shorts)

For DCKT YouTube Shorts and IG Reels, 15-25s, warm female narrator, captions a phrase at a time. Built 2026-10-08.

**Rules:** authors died before 1955, works published before 1929. Every quote was checked word for word against the Project Gutenberg plain-text file linked under it. Ellipses mark trims, always at a phrase boundary. Poem line breaks are flattened for narration (line-initial capitals lowercased); otherwise punctuation follows the source unless a note says so.

**Captions:** each quote is split into 2-4 verbatim cards that cover the whole narration. Cards over 34 characters are shown wrapped onto 2 lines (the ` / ` marks the line break).

**Already used, skip:** Chopin: The voice of the sea is seductive (already used); Thoreau: A lake is the landscape's most beautiful (already used); Thoreau: We need the tonic of wildness (already used).

## Rejected

| Quote | Why |
|---|---|
| Adopt the pace of nature: her secret is patience. (Emerson) | Paraphrase of Emerson; not in his text verbatim (per brief). |
| The mountains are calling and I must go. (John Muir) | From a private 1873 letter; could not verify exact wording against a primary PD text, so left out. |
| Roll on, thou deep and dark blue Ocean, roll! (Byron, Childe Harold IV) | Verified, but the stanza goes straight to shipwrecks and ruin (grim). |
| The tide rises, the tide falls (Longfellow) | Poem is about a traveller who never returns (death theme). |
| It is a beauteous evening, calm and free (Wordsworth) | Second line is 'The holy time is quiet as a Nun' (religious). |
| The moving waters at their priestlike task (Keats, Bright Star) | Religious imagery (priestlike, ablution). |
| Muir: 'Nature has always something rare to show us' (Wind-Storm) (John Muir) | Verified, but the same sentence talks about 'danger to life and limb'; wrong message for a kayak brand. |
| Stopping by Woods on a Snowy Evening, last stanza (Robert Frost (1923)) | Verified (Gutenberg #58611) and PD, but snow imagery clashes with summer footage. Bench option for evening/e-bike if wanted. |
