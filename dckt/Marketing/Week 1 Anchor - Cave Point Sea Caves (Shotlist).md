## Voiceover spine (rough, rewrite in your voice)

> Everyone thinks you have to fly somewhere for water like this. You don't.
> This is Cave Point, in Door County, ninety minutes from Green Bay.
> You don't need to know how to kayak. That's our job. You just show up.
> We paddle the shoreline, into the sea caves, past water so clear you can count the rocks ten feet down.
> Some people jump. Some people just float.
> Either way, you leave different than you got here.
> No experience needed. We've got you. Come see it for yourself.

No em dashes. Keep it spoken, not written.
