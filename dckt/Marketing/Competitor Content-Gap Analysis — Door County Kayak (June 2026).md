# Competitor Content-Gap Analysis — Door County Kayak (June 2026)

Ran the content-gap technique against the strongest organic competitor. Goal: find the questions their audience searches that the competitor barely answers, then own those pages.

## The competitor: doorcountykayak.com

Strongest organic rival for "Door County kayak tours" terms. Owns the near-exact brand name (minus "tours"), ranks #1 on core head terms, widest product menu (kayak + e-bike + walking food tour). Separate operator from our doorcountykayaktours.com.

## The gap

Their tour pages are thin booking pages and their FAQ is only seven questions. Three open lanes, consistent across every page:

1. **Zero educational depth.** They name-drop the Fleetwing shipwreck, Niagara Escarpment, Death's Door, and pictographs, then explain none of it. Real search demand for "Fleetwing shipwreck" and "Death's Door history" goes unanswered. Biggest open lane.
2. **Anxiety questions unanswered.** Minimum age, do I have to swim, will I get seasick, what if I tip over, never kayaked and scared. High-intent pre-booking searches the market leader ignores.
3. **No seasonality/timing content.** Best month, water temp by month, sunset timing, cave conditions. People plan Door County months out and search exactly this.

The pattern: everyone treats their site as a booking shortcut, not a resource. Whoever builds the answer library wins top-of-funnel and feeds it into bookings.

## 10 content ideas to own

1. **The Fleetwing Shipwreck: The True Story Behind Door County's Most Famous Schooner Wreck** — "Fleetwing shipwreck Door County history." They name it, explain nothing.
2. **Why It's Called Death's Door: The History That Made This the Most Dangerous Strait on the Great Lakes** — "Death's Door Wisconsin / Porte des Morts history." Used as flavor text everywhere, explained nowhere.
3. **Can You Kayak at Cave Point If You've Never Kayaked Before? An Honest Beginner's Guide** — "Cave Point kayak for beginners." Most common pre-booking worry, unaddressed.
4. **Do You Need to Know How to Swim to Kayak in Door County? (And What Happens If You Tip Over)** — top fear search, missing from every competitor FAQ.
5. **Kayaking Door County With Kids: Age Limits, Tandem Options, Which Tour Is Family-Friendly** — competitor lists weight limits but no age or family suitability.
6. **Best Time of Year to Kayak Door County: Month-by-Month Water Temp, Crowds, Cave Conditions** — seasonality completely absent from their site.
7. **How Cold Is Lake Michigan for Kayaking? Water Temps by Month and What to Wear** — they give one-line "wear layers," skip the cold-water reality.
8. **What Are the Cave Point Caves, Really? Geology of the Niagara Escarpment for Visitors** — one sentence on the escarpment, no explainer.
9. **Best Sunset Kayak Spots in Door County (and How to Photograph the Bluffs Without Dropping Your Phone)** — sunset/photography appear nowhere.
10. **Will I Get Seasick Kayaking on Lake Michigan? What the Water Is Actually Like at Cave Point and Death's Door** — seasickness/roughness never addressed.

## Build first

Start with #1 (Fleetwing) and #2 (Death's Door): high curiosity, low competition, link-worthy, distinctly ours. Then the fear/logistics set (#3, #4, #6). Link each answer page to the matching tour page to capture the click before they reach doorcountykayak.com.
