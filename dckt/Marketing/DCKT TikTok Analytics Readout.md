## The one-paragraph verdict

This account is not really running. It has 489 followers and 16 posts going back to 2023, but it has been dark since November 30, 2025, which means zero posts during the entire 2026 peak season so far. The bigger problem is distribution: over the last 365 days, 100% of views came from Search and 0% came from For You. TikTok is not pushing this content to anyone. The only reason it gets any views at all is that people actively search "kayaking door county" and similar terms and stumble onto it. So today the account behaves like a small SEO listing, not a social channel. The good news hiding underneath: the 489 followers we already have are almost a perfect match for the DCKT customer avatar (women 35-54, United States), and the two best videos we ever posted both did the same simple thing, paired Cave Point with a claim or a question hook. We have a clear template. We just stopped using it.

## Posting cadence and timing

The posting history, not a steady channel:

- **2023:** 5 posts (Mar to Dec), including both all-time winners.
- **2024:** 1 post (a January hiring post).
- **2025:** 9 posts, but 8 of them crammed into a single ~2.5-week burst (June 17 to July 4), then one gift card photo in November.
- **2026:** nothing.

So the real history is two short bursts and a lot of silence. The June 2025 burst is the only time the account looked alive, and it happened to produce the 1,701-view post. Bursts get punished by the algorithm less than long silence, but neither builds momentum. Consistency is the entire game on TikTok and we have never given it one.

(Note on "best time to post": TikTok Studio says followers were most active around 7am and viewers around 8-9am, but that is computed off 23 weekly viewers, so it is noise. Ignore it until we have real volume. Default to the Social Media Plan's guidance and post late afternoon/evening Central for the Midwest audience, then let real data correct us.)

## Recommendations (next 30 days)

1. **Just start posting again.** The single biggest lever is that we are dark in peak season. 3 to 5 posts a week per the Social Media Plan. Anything consistent beats the current zero.

2. **Run the template we already proved.** Lead with Cave Point and a claim or question hook in the first 3 words of on-screen text. Our winners: "#1 Door County Activity" and "Caribbean or Door County?". Reuse that energy: "Most Door County tourists never see this," "Caribbean? No, that's Cave Point," "Ever paddled into a sea cave?"

3. **Stop posting captionless montages.** The "pretty water + trending song, no hook" format is the bottom of our own catalog. Every post needs a reason to stop scrolling in the first second.

4. **Feed the search intent on purpose.** Since 100% of traffic is search, write captions and on-screen text around the exact terms already finding us: "things to do in Door County," "kayaking in Door County," "Sturgeon Bay kayaking." Keep one evergreen gift-card post pinned, people are literally searching "kayaker gift ideas."

5. **Re-warm the 489 we have.** They are our exact customer and they are asleep. A few posts that ask a question or invite a comment ("first-timer or done it before?") will tell us fast whether the following is salvageable or vanity.

6. **Don't chase FYP yet.** It will not turn on from a standing start. Post consistently for 3 to 4 weeks first, then re-pull this readout and watch whether the For You share moves off 0%. That is the number that tells us the algorithm woke up.
