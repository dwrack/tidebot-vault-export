# The Quiet Bottom

## Door County Kayak Tours Content Universe, Content Series Bible 02

This file is the production engine that sits on top of the World Bible. The World Bible (01) is the canon: who Ansel is, what the season means, which devices pay off where. This file is the slate of recurring formats we shoot inside that canon. Think of it as a set of cameras pointed at the same world. The narrator's eye. The dating profile. The fake reviews. Each one is a different lens on one place and one moral axis, quiet versus loud, and each one has a job in the larger story.

If anything here contradicts the World Bible, the World Bible wins. Read 01 first.

House style is strict and non-negotiable. No em dashes anywhere, use commas or colons. No double dashes. Plain ASCII in every caption. Short direct sentences. Every spoken word is captioned on screen. Hook on frame 1. Vertical 9:16. Loop ending. A save trigger or a send trigger near the close. The primary search term (Cave Point, Door County sea caves, Death's Door) is in the on-screen text inside the first 2 seconds and in the first line of the caption. We take positions. We do not hedge.

## How To Read This File

Eleven recurring series. Each one has the same header block so a writer can grab any series and shoot it without re-reading the whole document.

- **The Camera.** What lens this series points at the world. One sentence.
- **The Job.** What this series does for the larger story and the funnel. This is the most important line. A series with no job gets cut.
- **Cinematography Mode.** Which of the five visual languages this series lives in (see the mode key below). Models, not imitation.
- **Real vs AI.** What we shoot live (real guide and tour footage) versus what we generate (talking animals, impossible shots). Default bias: real footage wherever a human can physically hold a camera, AI only for Ansel, the talking cast, and shots no GoPro can get.
- **Awareness Stage + Blair Warren Lever.** The Schwartz awareness stage the series is written for, and the one Blair Warren lever it pulls (encourage dreams, justify failures, allay fears, confirm suspicions, throw rocks at enemies). Named per series, then named again per episode where it shifts.
- **Episodes.** 5 to 8 ideas each, with the hook on frame 1 written out, the search term placement, and the device or canon hook it ties to.

### The Five Cinematography Modes (the mode key)

1. **BEAUTY.** Malick and Lubezki for natural light and reverence, Deakins for fog and scale. The lake as a cathedral. Slow, wide, patient. This is the mode that earns the send-to-someone-you-love clip.
2. **DEADPAN COMEDY.** Wes Anderson symmetry and chapter cards. The Life Aquatic is literally nautical, so the whole nautical-research-vessel look is on the table: centered frames, flat affect, hard cuts, label cards. This is the mode for the comedy formats.
3. **MARITIME GOTHIC.** Robert Eggers (The Lighthouse) and Spielberg's Jaws inverted. The menace is the cold water and Death's Door, not a shark. Dread, fog, the thing under the surface. This is the lore mode.
4. **MOCKUMENTARY / DRY REALISM.** Nathan Fielder and Taika Waititi. Guides talking flat to camera, real and a little awkward, the comedy of someone explaining a serious thing earnestly. This is the guide-to-camera mode.
5. **THE STANDOFF / REVEAL.** Sergio Leone. Extreme close on the eye, the slow zoom, the held beat before the reveal. Reserved for Ansel and for Birgit's payoffs. Use sparingly, it is the most expensive look we have.

### AI Prompt Block Convention

Every AI beat gets a paste-ready prompt block for Seedance 2.0 / Higgsfield, formatted like this so the editor can drop it straight in:

```
SEEDANCE PROMPT
Shot: [framing + subject]
Camera: [move]
Light: [source + quality]
Detail: [the one thing that sells it]
Sound: [ambient + any VO cue]
Duration: [seconds], 9:16 vertical
```

Real beats are marked **[REAL]**. AI beats are marked **[AI]**. Mixed beats say which part is which.

# SERIES 1: Bottom of the Lake

**The Camera.** Ansel's eye, looking up from the silt at the surface world. The narrator's POV. This is the only series where Ansel frames the whole video start to finish, and it is the spine the other ten hang off of.

**The Job.** Hold the universe together. **Cinematography Mode.** Standoff / Reveal for Ansel himself (extreme close on the old eye, the slow Leone zoom, never the full body). Beauty for everything he looks at. The contrast is the show: a weary eye in the dark, a cathedral of light above it.

**Real vs AI.** Ansel is always **[AI]**, he has to be, no sturgeon will hold a monologue. We cut between the AI eye below and the real world above. That cut is the whole grammar of the series.

**Awareness Stage + Blair Warren Lever.** Mostly Product Aware and Most Aware, this series talks to people who already get the brand. Lever: confirm suspicions (you already suspected the loud weekend was missing the good part, Ansel says it out loud) and, in the tender episodes, encourage dreams.

# SERIES 2: Expectation vs Reality

**The Camera.** Two pictures side by side or hard-cut: the thing people brace for, then the thing that actually happens. Honesty as a hook.

**The Job.** Kill the wrong mental picture before it kills a booking. The biggest objections to a cold-water Great Lakes kayak tour are "I will be freezing and miserable" and "I am not athletic enough." This series shows the rocky launch and then the Caribbean-blue payoff, the scary cold and then the warm laughing reality. Funnel job: pure objection removal, mid-funnel, the clip that turns a maybe into a booking. This format also doubles as a quiet safety and credibility signal, calm morning versus choppy afternoon proves we know the water.

**Cinematography Mode.** Deadpan for the setup frame (flat, almost ugly on purpose), Beauty for the reality frame. The whiplash from drab to gorgeous is the mechanism.

**Real vs AI.** Almost entirely **[REAL]**. This series only works if both halves are real, the second a viewer suspects the gorgeous half is AI, the proof value dies. Ansel can voice it but the pictures must be true tour footage. The one allowed **[AI]** beat is Ansel's eye as a transition wipe between the two halves.

**Awareness Stage + Blair Warren Lever.** Solution Aware and Product Aware. Lever: allay fears, this is the fear-killer series. The "Caribbean or Door County" beat also pulls confirm suspicions (you suspected this water was special, here is proof).

## Hard Canon Reminders For Every Series (do not break)

These override any clever idea a writer has.

- Ansel is folklore. Never promise a sturgeon sighting on the tour. Real wildlife only on screen: bald eagles, herons, cormorants, sandhill cranes, painted turtles, swans, the Eco Estuary otters and beavers. Ansel and his eye are always AI.
- The villain stays faceless. No named competitor, ever. No real brand on a boat or jet ski. The Wake is a force, not a business. This binds Series 6, 7, and 11 especially.
- No founding year on camera. The vault contradicts itself (company entity older, current guided operation newer), so we name neither number on screen.
- No ACA certification claim. The only safe guide language is local, naturalist-led, CPR and first-aid current. This binds Series 6 and 9.
- No specific review count on camera until reconciled. Series 7 gets its proof power from satire, not from a number.
- Real footage wherever a human can hold a camera. AI only for Ansel, the talking cast inner-voices, and shots no GoPro can physically get (the 1888 storm, the depth pullback, the underwater eye). Mark every beat REAL or AI.
- The primary search term goes in the on-screen text inside the first 2 seconds and in the first caption line. Cave Point, Door County sea caves, Death's Door, Eco Estuary, depending on the episode.
- House style: no em dashes, no double dashes, plain ASCII, captions on every spoken word, hook on frame 1, vertical 9:16, loop ending, save or send trigger near the close. Length: 8 to 19 seconds for scenery and POV, 21 to 34 for lore. Never near 60.
- Run every line through the 3-test filter before it ships: can I visualize it, can I falsify it, could nobody else say it. Purge the kill-list (unlock, unleash, seamless, elevate, leverage, robust, holistic, utilize, world-class, unforgettable).
