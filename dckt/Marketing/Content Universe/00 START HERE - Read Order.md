This is the front door to the Door County Kayak Tours content universe. Read in the order below. The World Bible is canon; everything downstream obeys it.

## The Universe in 4 Lines

- **Name:** The Quiet Bottom. A short-form video world for @admirenola, set on the water off Door County.
- **Narrator:** Ansel the Sturgeon (AN-sel), a roughly 100-year-old lake sturgeon on the lake bottom. Dry, weary, secretly soft about the place. He watches the tours, he never takes them.
- **Moral axis:** Quiet versus loud. The kayak way versus the engine way. Slowness is the price of admission to everything worth seeing.
- **Read first:** 01 World Bible. It is the canon. If a script breaks a rule in there, the script is wrong, not the rule.
