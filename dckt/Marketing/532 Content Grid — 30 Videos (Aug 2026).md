## The 5 topics

1. **The sea caves** — Cave Point limestone, 420 million years old, the thing nobody drives past and knows about
2. **Planning a Door County trip that isn't wineries** — the itinerary gap
3. **Water, weather, and when to go** — conditions, season, best light
4. **Beginner fear** — flipping, cold, fitness, kids, "am I going to embarrass myself"
5. **The ebike side** — routes, who it's for, kayak day vs bike day

## The 3 formats

- **Breakdown** (easy) — "3 things nobody tells you about X"
- **Myth-bust** (easy) — take the thing guests believe and kill it
- **POV** (ambitious) — put the viewer in the seat

## The 2 layouts

- **A — Talking head on location.** Guide on the limestone or at the launch. Wind noise is fine, it's proof.
- **B — Faceless voiceover over GoPro/drone b-roll.** For guides who won't go on camera. Batch the drone in one flight.

## Cover type

Heavy condensed sans, all caps, cream on deep water blue. 3-6 words. Legible at 1 inch.
