# DCKT YouTube Channel Playbook

Built June 15 2026. This is the operating system for the channel: the reason people watch, the schedule, the title and hook formulas, the series, and the shoot list. Pair it with `IG Video Content Catalog.md` (the 188-video library) and `project_youtube_channel_state` (the 319K flagship and playlists).

---

## 1. The North Star: "Wait, that's Wisconsin?"

Nobody stops scrolling for a kayak clip. They stop for water that looks fake and a feeling they're missing something a 90-minute drive away, not a flight to Florida.

That's the whole channel. **Wonder is the hook. Calm is the payoff. Permission is the close.**

The footage already proves it. The captions that hit hardest are the disbelief ones: "the side of Wisconsin you just have to see for yourself," "Googled my symptoms, turns out all I needed was a weekend on Lake Michigan." We're not selling kayaking. We're selling the moment a tired Midwest mom realizes the most beautiful water she's ever seen is two hours away and she's allowed to go.

**Who's watching:** 35-55, Wisconsin or Chicago suburbs, never kayaked, a little nervous. She doesn't want extreme. She wants beautiful, close, and safe. Every choice on this channel serves her.

---

## 2. The three pillars

Every video does at least one. The best do all three.

1. **The view that doesn't compute.** Caves, gin-clear water, cliffs. The shot that makes someone say "that's not Wisconsin." Always in the first second.
2. **The reset.** Slow down, get on the water, the lake fixes your week. The feeling, not the activity.
3. **You're in good hands.** Beginner-friendly, guides handle everything, dogs welcome, families. The nervous first-timer's permission to book.

If a video hits none of these, it doesn't go up. That's the test the old kiteboarding and travel clips failed.

## 3. Two phases

**Phase 1, Backfill (running now, ~27 days).** The drip is pushing all 164 on-brand videos onto the channel as Shorts, 6 a day, correctly titled. This builds the searchable library: someone googling "Cave Point kayak" or "Door County sea cave" starts finding us. Hands-off. Don't touch it.

**Phase 2, The Engine (starts now, runs forever).** Once the channel has a library, the growth comes from a steady curated layer on top: one marquee Short a day pinned to a series, plus one long-form anchor a week. This is where the wonder thesis gets sharp and the packaging gets tight.

## 4. The weekly cadence

| Slot | What | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Daily | 1 marquee Short (from the library or fresh), tied to a series | Volume + search coverage, feeds the algorithm |
| Weekly (Thu) | 1 long-form anchor (3 to 8 min) | Builds watch time, subscribers, and the "real channel" signal |
| Weekly (Sun) | 1 community post or poll | Keeps subscribers warm between drops |
| Monthly | 1 "big swing" (the shipwreck film, the first-timer doc) | The video that can break out |

Thursday for long-form because trip-planning research spikes Thursday into the weekend. Sunday community post because that's when people plan the week.

## 7. Hook formulas (first 1 second)

You win or lose in the first second. No logo intro. No slow build. The wow shot or the wow line, instantly.

**Visual cold opens:**
- The cave reveal: paddle straight into the dark mouth, light on the water.
- The clarity drop: camera dips to show rock 10 feet down through the water.
- The cliff jump: body inverted mid-air over turquoise.
- The pan that pays off: trees, then BAM, the impossible water.

**Text hooks (top of frame, second 0):**
- "No one believes this is Wisconsin."
- "Save this for your summer trip."
- "Things to do in Door County that aren't fudge."
- "She'd never kayaked before this."
- "This water is 90 minutes from Green Bay."

Pair a text hook with a visual cold open and you've got a scroll-stopper.

## 8. The five recurring series

Series beat one-offs. They train the audience to come back and they make planning easy.

1. **Wait, That's Wisconsin?** Short, wonder-led. The clear water, the caves, the cliffs. Your scroll-stoppers. (Daily-able.)
2. **First Timers.** A nervous beginner's arc from "I might tip" to glowing on the water. Pure permission content. Your highest-converting format for the avatar.
3. **Guide Life.** Day in the life, the team, the goofy shop culture, hiring. Humanizes the brand and recruits guides at the same time.
4. **Door County 101.** Trivia, weather literacy, Death's Door history, local color, "is it worth it." Positions you as the local expert, not just a vendor.
5. **Conditions Report / Cave of the Week.** Short, current, "it's glass today, come paddle." Drives same-day and near-term bookings. Real-time relevance the algorithm loves.

## 10. Packaging rules

- **Thumbnail:** the clarity or cave shot + a human face + 3 to 4 words max. Consistent style so the channel reads as one brand.
- **First second:** wow shot, no intro. Always.
- **Length:** Shorts under 30 seconds for retention. Long-form 3 to 8 min, get to the payoff inside 15 seconds.
- **End screen:** soft CTA, "come see it for yourself," link and the next video. Never a hard sell.
- **Pinned video:** the Cave Point sea cave film. First thing a new visitor sees.
- **Playlists:** Cave Point, Death's Door, Eco Estuary, E-Bike, First Timers, Guide Life. Make the library navigable.

---

## 11. The CTA ladder

Don't ask for the sale in a Short. Walk them up.

1. **Watch** (great footage earns the next second)
2. **Comment a keyword** ("comment PADDLE and I'll send the link") to trigger a DM and train the algorithm
3. **Link in bio / description** to the tour page
4. **Book** at doorcountykayaktours.com

The hard sell lives on the website. The channel's job is to make her want it.

---

## 12. What to measure

Ignore vanity views. Watch these:
- **First 3 seconds retention / swipe-away rate** on Shorts. If they leave fast, the hook's wrong.
- **"Where is this?" comments.** That's the wonder thesis working. Pin a reply with the location and the link.
- **Click-through on the pinned and the link.**
- **Saves and shares** over likes. A save means "I'm planning a trip."
- **Subscriber-per-video** on long-form. That's the channel actually compounding.
