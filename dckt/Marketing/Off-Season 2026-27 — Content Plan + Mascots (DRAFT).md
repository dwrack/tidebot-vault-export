## The idea in one line
Three Door County locals keep the lake company all winter: a loon, a gull and a sturgeon. They react to our summer footage, hype the businesses that stay open, and remind everyone their $20 is melting before Dec 31.

Rules for the characters:
- **Captions carry every joke**, so the post works with the sound off. ElevenLabs voices go on top, one fixed voice per character, so they stay consistent.
- These are the only synthetic voices allowed. The no-AI-voice rule still applies to ads and to anything presented as a real person.
- Characters never make safety claims and never promise anything a guide will do.
- **Overlays on other businesses' content:** only with that business's OK. Use IG Collab posts or their clip plus a tag. Reposting without permission hurts reach (Instagram's originality scoring) and borrows content we don't own.

## $20 credit campaign
- **Who gets it:** past guests. Exact list TBD (2026 guests at minimum).
- **What it's good for:** a gift card purchase or an e-bike rental. Expires 11:59pm CT Dec 31, 2026.
- **Emails, sent through SES with a hosted one-click unsubscribe:**
  - Nov 3: "We got you something."
  - Dec 1: "Still yours."
  - Dec 15: "Two weeks."
  - Dec 29: "Last call."
- **Posts:** Gary brings it up in every December Gull Report. Lou folds it into the gift guide.
- **Compliance wording is required on every email and code.** See the Research doc. Call it a "promotional credit," never a "gift card." A gift card bought with the credit must not expire.
- **Check in FareHarbor before building:** can the promo code apply to gift card purchases? If not, the credit is e-bike and tour only, and the gift angle becomes "use your $20 on a tour, gift the rest."
