# Door County Lodging — Link-Building Master Target List

Built June 2026. Goal: get DCKT linked/recommended on lodging "things to do" and blog
pages across Door County. No paid backlink tool was used; link status was checked
directly per site (Google `site:` + fetching each property's activities page).

## Headline finding

**Essentially zero Door County lodging sites currently link to doorcountykayaktours.com.**
A web-wide search for the domain surfaced only DCKT's own blog, Tripadvisor, travel guides,
and the Destination Door County directory. No resort, inn, or rental manager was found
linking out to us. The backlink field is wide open.

Second finding: **almost every property runs a blog and/or a "things to do / area activities /
links" page, names local restaurants and shops, but never names a kayak or e-bike outfitter.**
That is the gap the outreach fills. The free-blog-post offer lands because so many of them
publish content already.

Third: DCKT's own blog already recommends Gordon Lodge and White Gull Inn, so a reciprocal
"we already send guests your way" angle is genuine for those two.

## Legend
- **Things-to-do page**: has an area activities / things-to-do / links / explore page.
- **Blog**: runs a blog or news page (strongest fit for the free-post offer).
- **Onsite kayaks**: keeps kayaks for guests (still a fine target — a guided sea-cave/e-bike
  tour is a different product than beach kayaks — but lead with the blog/SEO angle, not "send guests").
- **Tier 1** = real email + things-to-do or blog page, no competitor entanglement → draft staged.
- **Tier 2** = strong fit but only a phone/contact form (no public email) → David emails via form or we call.
- **Tier 3** = breadth (chains, group rentals, competitor-tied, no activities page) → low priority.
