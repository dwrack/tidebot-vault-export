# SEO Overview — Why Lodging Links, and the Blog-for-Link Play

## Why lodging "things to do" links are worth chasing

Most DCKT customers book a place to stay first, then look for things to do once the trip is
locked in. The page where that second decision happens is the lodging site's "things to do /
area activities" page or its blog. A link from there does three things at once:

1. **Sends warm referral traffic.** A guest reading the resort's activities page is already
   in Door County with money set aside for fun. That is a higher-intent visitor than most ad clicks.
2. **Passes local relevance for SEO.** Door County lodging domains are topically and
   geographically perfect linking sources. These are exactly the links that move rankings for
   "things to do in Door County," "Cave Point kayak," town queries, etc.
3. **Locks out competitors.** Right now most of these pages name no kayak outfitter at all.
   Whoever they add first owns that referral path. Door County Kayak (doorcountykayak.com),
   kayakdoorcounty.com, and Lakeshore Adventures are the rivals who could grab it instead.

Audit result: **no lodging site we checked currently links to DCKT.** Wide open.

## The hook: a free custom blog post, written by us, for their site

Asking for a link cold is weak. Giving them content is strong. The offer: DCKT writes a
genuinely useful blog post for the property's own blog, about kayak + e-bike tours near *that*
property and one unique nearby spot. The post naturally links back to DCKT (that is our payment),
but it is real, publishable content their guests want.

Why this works for them:
- Lodging blogs are usually thin and rarely updated. Free, well-written, locally specific content
  is a gift, not a favor.
- A post titled around their town ("Best Half-Day Adventures Near Fish Creek," etc.) helps THEM
  rank for their own local queries and feeds their guest emails and socials.
- It costs us little: we already have the source angles drafted in `SEO Content/` (Cave Point,
  Death's Door, half-day upsell, e-bike). We reskin per property and location.

Stacked with: a **reciprocal link** from a DCKT "Where to Stay" recommendation, and a soft
offer of a **printable guest packet / QR card** for the check-in desk. The guest discount code
is held for round 2, after a property engages, so round 1 stays value-first.

## Per-property blog angle (the "unique location" each post features)

Assign by proximity so each post feels written for that property's guests:

- **Egg Harbor** (Ashbrooke, Newport, Shallows, Landmark, Landing): central, ~20 min to Cave Point.
  Angle: Cave Point sea caves day trip + Peninsula State Park e-bike loop.
- **Fish Creek** (Thorp House, White Gull, Settlement, Homestead): next to Peninsula State Park.
  Angle: Peninsula State Park e-bike + Nicolet Bay / Green Bay paddle, with a Cave Point upgrade.
- **Ephraim** (Eagle Harbor Inn, Ephraim Shores, Bay Breeze, Pine Grove): calm harbor town.
  Angle: beginner-friendly Eagle Harbor paddle + Peninsula State Park e-bike. Great for our
  first-timer avatar.
- **Baileys Harbor** (Gordon Lodge, Blacksmith, Beachfront): Lake Michigan side.
  Angle: Cana Island + Cave Point day trip; e-bike the Ridges / Mud Lake.
- **Sister Bay / Ellison Bay** (Open Hearth, Inn at Little Sister Hill, Coachlite, Sister Bay Inn):
  north end. Angle: Death's Door story + Europe Bay (Newport State Park) paddle + e-bike the tip.
- **Sturgeon Bay / Whitefish Dunes** (Glidden Lodge, Bay Shore): closest to our Cave Point flagship.
  Angle: Cave Point sea caves (this is our signature tour, and Glidden Lodge is the nearest lodging
  to it — highest-value single link on the whole list once we have their email).

## Always-on rules for the posts (when we write them)

- Lead with reassurance for first-timers (WI / Chicago-suburb mom avatar), not extreme adventure.
- Upsell the half-day ($145) over the 2-hour ($69) above the fold, per the half-day-upsell rule.
- Sit-on-top safety angle; clear-bottom only where shipwreck content fits.
- One link back to the matching DCKT tour page, plus a soft link to the property (reciprocity).
