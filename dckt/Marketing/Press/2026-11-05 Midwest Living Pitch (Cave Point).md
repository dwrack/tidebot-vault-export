## Body

Hi Taryn,

For your 2027 Midwest destinations list: Cave Point County Park in Door County, Wisconsin.

What: Sea caves on Lake Michigan. Waves have been cutting into this limestone shoreline since the glaciers pulled back about 10,000 years ago, and the rock itself is Niagara Escarpment dolomite laid down more than 400 million years ago, when this part of Wisconsin sat under a tropical sea. The cliffs stand 40 to 60 feet tall. On a sunny day the water under them turns a turquoise most people don't expect from a Great Lake, because the light bounces off the pale rock bottom.

Where: The Lake Michigan side of the Door County peninsula, near Jacksonport, about an hour northeast of Green Bay. It is a county park and sits right next to Whitefish Dunes State Park, so the caves, a sand beach and wooded trails are all in one stop.

Why: Most people in the Midwest don't know there are sea caves here that you can paddle into. From the cliff trail you hear the water booming inside the rock. From a kayak you go in. The largest cave fits four kayaks side by side and runs about 40 feet back into the cliff. No experience is needed and kids 8 and up can do it, which makes it a rare trip that works for grandparents, parents and kids in the same boat lineup. The quiet side of the peninsula also gets a fraction of the traffic the bay side towns do, and in fall the same shoreline is a good e-bike ride when the colors turn. In winter the cliffs ice over and it becomes a different place entirely.

About us: Door County Kayak Tours has guided paddlers along this shoreline since 2002, with more than 2,000 five-star reviews. We run guided kayak tours and e-bike tours at Cave Point from late spring into fall.
