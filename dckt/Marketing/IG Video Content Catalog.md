## What this tells us (baseline for new video)

- **Cave Point is the engine.** The overwhelming majority of tour and scenery footage is Cave Point: sea caves, cliff jumping at Devil's Bathtub, clear turquoise water. It is the strongest, most repeatable asset. Keep shooting it, but you are heavily concentrated there.
- **Door Bluff and Death's Door are underweight.** Only a handful of clips (the shipwreck underwater footage #98, the cliff-strata Door Bluff tour #126, open-water crossings) cover the higher-margin half-day Death's Door product. This is the biggest content gap to fill in 2026: paddle the Fleetwing wreck and the cathedral bluffs on purpose.
- **Eco Estuary is thin but distinct.** A few strong lily-pad / pelican / Mud Lake clips (#38, #45, #109, #159, #107). The calm, beginner, wildlife angle is a clean contrast to the Cave Point thrill content and deserves more dedicated shoots.
- **E-bike content is solid and year-round.** Strong winter fat-tire footage (#30, #66), Peninsula State Park itinerary tours (#116, #117), and POV rides. A real differentiator most kayak competitors do not have.
- **Cana Island half-day (#138) barely exists on film.** One good lighthouse aerial. Worth a fresh shoot to support that premium tour.
- **Reusable formats that already work:** WEEKEND RECAP, fake-DM booking nudge, on-screen FAQ ("most popular tour?"), "Sorry I have plans," guide day-in-the-life, Door County Trivia, testimonial-over-B-roll. These are templates, not one-offs.
- **Highest-value hero clips to cut for the website and ads:** #43 and #143 (clear-water clarity), #8/#156 (cliff jump/backflip), #4/#132 (paddling inside the sea cave), #74/#169 (aerial clear-water shoreline), #98 (underwater shipwreck).
- **Beginner reassurance is a recurring, on-brand theme** (#53 "we'll take care of you," #147 calm-water, #42 cloudy-day still gorgeous). This matches the avatar: nervous first-timer. Make more of it.
