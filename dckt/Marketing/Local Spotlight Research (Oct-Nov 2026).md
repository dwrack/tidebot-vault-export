## A. 2026 Wisconsin gun deer season

- **Gun deer: Nov. 21 - Nov. 29, 2026** (9 days; Saturday before Thanksgiving through Sunday after).
- Other dates: Youth/disabled gun hunt Oct. 10-11; muzzleloader Nov. 30 - Dec. 9; December antlerless-only Dec. 10-13; archery/crossbow Sept. 12 - Jan. 3, 2027.
- DNR note: "All dates are subject to change through rulemaking or a legislative process."
- Source: https://dnr.wisconsin.gov/topic/hunt/dates.html (pulled 2026-10-13)
- Practical: bow hunting is already open now, so orange is smart all fall.

## B. Hunting at Whitefish Dunes and Cave Point; blaze orange

- **Whitefish Dunes State Park: hunting is allowed in open areas of the park.** DNR: "Hunting and trapping are allowed in the open areas of the park" (https://dnr.wisconsin.gov/topic/parks/whitefish/recreation/hunting). Statewide state-park hunting window: firearm, bow, and trapping **Nov. 15 - Dec. 15**; bow continues through the Sunday nearest Jan. 6 (https://dnr.wisconsin.gov/topic/parks/hunt). The park publishes a hunting map showing closed areas. So the whole gun deer week, Nov. 21-29, falls inside the window.
- **Cave Point County Park: unconfirmed.** Door County says "Gun and/or Bow hunting is permitted in certain county parks from November 15th thru April 1st during legal DNR hunting seasons" with a permit, and does not name which parks (https://www.co.door.wi.gov/476/Park-Permits-Information and https://www.co.door.wi.gov/267/County-Parks). Cave Point is on the county park list but I could not confirm whether it is a hunting park. Call Door County Facilities & Parks, 920-493-7133 (number from a county search result), before saying it is closed to hunting. Cave Point sits next to Whitefish Dunes, so assume hunters are nearby.
- **Blaze orange:** I found no DNR text requiring or advising it for non-hunters at these parks. The Door County Land Trust recommends hikers wear blaze orange (vest, hat, or pack cover) October through April (https://doorcountylandtrust.org/stories/hiking-during-hunting-season). Recommendation for DCKT posts: say "wear blaze orange Nov. 15 onward, especially Nov. 21-29" as safety advice, not as a legal requirement.
