# Glidden Drive + Fall Lunch Stops Research

Researched 2026-10-14 (a Wednesday). All hours and facts below were checked on that date; anything I could not confirm is marked UNVERIFIED. "MAP-DERIVED" means computed with OSRM bike routing (routing.openstreetmap.de, routed-bike profile) on OpenStreetMap data. OSRM bike speed works out to about 9.5 mph, so e-bike times with photo stops will differ a bit.

Goal: "perfect fall afternoon" e-bike loop. Shop (6329 State Hwy 57, Jacksonport) to Cave Point County Park to Whitefish Dunes State Park, then on to Glidden Drive / the Whitefish Bay community, lunch, ride back.

## 4. Return route and loop mileage (MAP-DERIVED, OSRM bike profile)

Key finding: the DNR allows bikes on the **Red Trail from the Whitefish Dunes office to S. Cave Point Drive**, plus Third Beach access to Clark Lake Rd and office to Schauer Rd. Bikes are NOT allowed on the beach, dunes or hiking trails. Source: https://dnr.wisconsin.gov/topic/parks/whitefish/recreation/biking. The page gives no surface description and no bike fee info. Whether a state park vehicle admission pass applies to bikes is UNVERIFIED. The OSRM route uses an unnamed 1.25 mi segment that I believe is this Red Trail connector (UNVERIFIED). Have a staff member ride it once on an e-bike before selling it.

**Out (scenic):** shop to Cave Point, Schauer Rd to the Whitefish Dunes office, Red Trail to S. Cave Point Dr, Cave Point Dr, Whitefish Bay Rd to the Hitching Post.

**Sensible return, Hitching Post to shop: 6.5 mi, about 40 min OSRM.** Whitefish Bay Rd, Nelson Lane, Clark Lake Rd (cross), Nelson Lane, East Town Line Rd, Loritz Rd, then State Highway 57 north about 2.7 mi into Jacksonport. Only the last 2.7 mi is on a state highway. Hwy 57 shoulder width and speed limit: UNVERIFIED.

**Alternate return (scenic, longer):** back the way you came via Nelson Ln, Clark Lake Rd, Schauer Rd, Cave Point Dr, Lake Shore Rd. About 8.7 mi, 54 min OSRM. Avoids Hwy 57 except the last 0.5 mi.

## Best itinerary

**"Glidden Drive Fall Lunch Loop": about 14 miles, about 1.5 hours riding, 3.5 to 4 hours total with stops. Best on a Saturday or Sunday.**

| Time | Stop | Cumulative miles |
|---|---|---|
| 10:30 | Leave the shop (6329 Hwy 57): Hwy 57, Lake Shore Rd, Cave Point Dr | 0 |
| 11:00 | Cave Point County Park: lake and cave viewpoints, about 20 min | 4.1 |
| 11:30 | Schauer Rd to the Whitefish Dunes office; walk to the beach and dunes (no bikes there) | 5.0 |
| 12:00 | Red Trail connector to S. Cave Point Dr, Cave Point Dr, Whitefish Bay Rd | 7.8 |
| 12:15 | Lunch at the Hitching Post, Whitefish Bay (Sat and Sun noon-8) | 7.8 |
| 1:30 | Optional 20 min Glidden Drive (Rustic Road 9) out-and-back toward Donny's for lake views | +1.7 |
| 2:00 | Return: Nelson Ln, East Town Line Rd, Loritz Rd, Hwy 57 north | 14.3 |
| 2:45 | Back at the shop; optional drink or dessert at Island Fever, 0.15 mi away | 14.3 (16.0 with spur) |

- Total about 14.3 mi (16.0 with the Glidden spur). About 96 min at OSRM bike pace, so plan 1.5 to 1.75 hours of e-bike moving time.
- Backup if the Hitching Post is closed (Tue, Wed) or the Red Trail is a mess: ride the road-only route (15.2 mi) and have lunch at Island Fever next to the shop, after a phone call to check hours.
- Weekday option: Mon, Thu, Fri the Hitching Post opens at 4 pm, so it only works as an early dinner, and November light is short after DST ends Nov 1.
