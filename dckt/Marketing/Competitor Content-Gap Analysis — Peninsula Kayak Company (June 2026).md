# Competitor Content-Gap Analysis — Peninsula Kayak Company (June 2026)

Ran the content-gap technique against Peninsula Kayak Company (David's chosen competitor for this pass).

## The competitor: Peninsula Kayak Company (peninsulakayakcompany.com)

Door County, WI. Based on Logan Creek near Cave Point / Whitefish Dunes, phone (920) 918-4314. Well-built site, thin on depth. Tours (Cave Point, Eagle Bluff, Wetland, Sunset, Fireworks, Ephraim Historical, plus SUP and e-bikes) are short sales blurbs. They have a blog, FAQ, and Meet the Team. They name-drop geology ("Niagara Escarpment," "425 million years," "dolostone") but never explain it. FAQ covers operational basics: age 4+, 40-250 lb weight limits, "no experience needed," weather refunds.

## The gap

1. **Fear and safety dodged entirely.** Nothing on swimming ability, what happens if you tip over, or how cold the water is. They run sit-on-top tandems (the most beginner-proof boat) but never explain why that matters to a nervous first-timer. Biggest avoided angle: the "should I even book this" anxiety search.
2. **Cave Point geology named but never taught.** They say "dolostone" and "karst" and move on. Nobody owns the actual explainer.
3. **Shipwrecks and Death's Door history completely absent.** This is DCKT's home-field advantage (the Fleetwing tour) and Peninsula doesn't touch maritime/wreck content. Their "Ephraim Historical Tour" is village heritage, not shipwrecks.
4. **Water temp and seasonality** get one vague line ("conditions checked daily"). No month-by-month reality, no "is Lake Michigan too cold in June" answer.
5. **Photography** is implied by the Sunset tour but never coached.

## 10 content ideas to own

1. **Do I Need to Know How to Swim to Kayak in Door County?** — nervous-beginner gut-check. Their FAQ skips swimming and capsize entirely.
2. **What Actually Happens If Your Kayak Tips Over (And Why It Rarely Does on a Guided Tour)** — "what if I flip kayak Lake Michigan." They avoid capsize language; DCKT can demystify it honestly.
3. **How Cold Is Lake Michigan? Door County Kayak Water Temps by Month (May-October)** — "Lake Michigan water temperature June Door County." They give one vague line, no numbers.
4. **The Fleetwing Shipwreck: Kayaking Over a 19th-Century Schooner in Death's Door** — "Fleetwing shipwreck / kayak over shipwreck." Zero shipwreck content on their site; DCKT's signature tour, uncontested topic.
5. **Why Is It Called Death's Door? The Real History Behind Porte des Morts** — "why is it called Death's Door Wisconsin." They never engage the name or its shipwreck-graveyard history.
6. **How the Cave Point Caves Were Made: A Plain-English Guide to Dolostone and the Niagara Escarpment** — "how were Cave Point caves formed." They name-drop the terms without explaining them.
7. **Can Kids Kayak at Cave Point? An Honest Age and Readiness Guide for Door County** — "kayak tour with kids / minimum age." They list "ages 4+" with no guidance on whether a young kid will actually be okay.
8. **First-Time Kayaker's Survival Guide to a Door County Tour (What Nobody Tells You)** — "first time kayaking what to expect." Their experience-level answer is literally one word: "NONE."
9. **Best Time of Year to Kayak Door County: A Month-by-Month Honest Breakdown** — "best month to kayak Door County." They only note "closed in winter," no seasonal nuance.
10. **How to Photograph the Cave Point Caves From a Kayak (Without Dropping Your Phone)** — "Cave Point photography / kayak photo tips." Sunset tour leans on golden-hour imagery, zero photo coaching.

## Build first

Ideas 4-6 (shipwreck / Death's Door / geology) are DCKT's moat: real expertise the Fleetwing tour already delivers, and Peninsula structurally won't follow there. Ideas 1-3 and 7-8 (fear, water temp, beginners, kids) are the bigger traffic play: high-intent, high-anxiety searches every Door County operator avoids because they're harder to talk about than a sunset photo. Link each answer page to the matching tour.
