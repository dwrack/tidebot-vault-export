## Trends to use (2026)
- **IG reach:** the strongest signal for reaching non-followers is DM sends. Instagram now scores originality, so reposted clips lose reach. Trial Reels can be scheduled. Carousels now have the highest engagement rate.
- **Shorts:** watch time per impression drives ranking. Original voiceover is reportedly favored for small channels. Searchable how-to Shorts surface even for tiny channels.
- **Oct to Feb is the "dreaming window," Mar to Aug is the booking window.** Inspire in winter, then switch to availability and urgency in March.
- **Door County hooks:**
  - Destination Door County's Winter Guide lists businesses open Nov to Mar; pitch them our Cave Point ice footage.
  - Holiday events: Sturgeon Bay tree lighting Nov 20, Holly Days and Capture the Spirit Nov 27-28, and the parade Dec 5.
  - Winterfest Feb 6-7, 2027, the Fish Creek polar plunge, and Fire & Ice in mid Feb.

## $20 credit: compliance
- **It must be a promotional credit, never called a "gift card" and never sold.**
- **Required wording:** "$20 Promotional Credit, a free gift from Door County Kayak Tours. Promotional value expires 11:59pm CT Dec 31, 2026. No cash value, non-transferable, one per guest, no fees. Questions: [phone] / doorcountykayaktours.com."
- **In FareHarbor:** issue it as an expiring promo code or gift certificate, not a FareHarbor gift card, because gift cards can't carry an expiration date.
- **If the credit is used to buy a gift card,** the gift card that's bought must not expire. Only the $20 promotional part can expire.
- **Still open:** whether FareHarbor lets a promo code apply to a gift card purchase. Check this in the dashboard.
- **Deadline rules:** a real, enforced Dec 31 deadline is fine. Don't quietly extend it and then send another "last chance" email.
- **Email:** a hosted one-click unsubscribe is required (standing rule).
