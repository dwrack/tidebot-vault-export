## Picks

**Best e-bike: Da75xh0AwLS (Jul 18).** It has 8 seconds of unbroken POV down a sunlit gravel forest trail at 1080x1920. The text is one thin line up in the trees, so cropping the lower 65% gives you clean footage. DbG4sRmJSQg has better scenery (Cave Point cliffs, the dunes boardwalk) but captions run through every shot.

**Best rough-water: DaA0DOaxQnr (Jun 25).** Whitecaps rolling past the buoys at dawn under a heavy sky. It's the right mood for a "the lake decides" kind of quote. The problem is the text sits dead center for the whole clip, so the quote card has to go right over it. The backup is the first 2.7s of Da75xh0AwLS (waves on a rocky ledge, crop-clean), but it's short.

**Cleanest overall:** Db_c7PDpKnm from 5.0 to 11.5s has no text, a cinematic grade, clear water, kayakers, and cliffs. It's letterboxed 16:9, so it works best letterboxed under a quote. DcyuA46JSFn is the best long Cave Point pan and crops easily.

Locations marked "launch dock" or "likely" aren't verified, so confirm them before naming a spot in a caption.
