# Guide Log Template — 2-Hour Cave Point
*Slack-thread structure. Three posts per tour: briefing (thread starter), mid-trip check (reply), closeout (reply). No paper forms.*

---

## How this works

Per the 11-beat operations playbook, each 2-hour Cave Point tour is one thread in `#dckt-tour-logs`. The guide owns three posts in that thread:

1. **Thread starter** — Beat 7 dock briefing video + conditions + group photo
2. **Reply 1** — Beat 9 mid-trip check (~15 min before return)
3. **Reply 2** — Beat 11 closeout (after gear stow)

A separate pre-tour shop check post lives in the channel as its own message (see `Operations/Pre-Tour Shop Check Slack Template.md`). Cross-link the briefing thread back to the pre-tour post.

This file is the copy-paste reference. Guides keep it bookmarked on their phone or save it as a Slack snippet.

---

## Post 1 — Thread starter (Beat 7, dock briefing)

Guide records the briefing video on their phone, takes the dock group photo, then posts:

```
🛶 2hr Cave Point | [date] [launch time] | guide [name]
Group: [size] | Pre-tour check: [link to pre-tour shop check post]
Conditions: [GREEN/YELLOW] | Air [X]°F / Water [X]°F / Wind [X] mph from [dir]
Forecast next 2 hrs: [forecast]

[briefing video attached]
[group photo attached]

Verbal acknowledgments captured: ✅ all [X] guests
```

**What's attached:**
- Briefing video file (mp4 or m4a — phone native format is fine)
- Group photo (jpg)

**Caption rules:**
- One-line summary at top so the channel is scannable
- Pre-tour post link is the cross-reference back to allergy/health/visit-count flags
- Conditions in the post body, NOT only in the video — searchable in Slack
- Verbal acknowledgments confirmation is a single ✅, not a list of names (PII in the channel)

**If anything's flagged on the pre-tour post:**

Add a one-line note below the conditions block:

```
Special handling: Mrs. Lawson asthma flagged, inhaler confirmed at briefing. Karim 3rd visit — postcard ready.
```

## Post 2 — Mid-trip check (Beat 9, reply in thread)

~15 min before return. Guide posts from a sheltered cove or wherever cell signal allows. Short.

```
Mid-trip check
Quality: [1-5]
Mechanical: NO | [or YES: detail]
Incident: NO | [or YES: detail]
Reviews asked: YES | [or NO: why]
```

If YES on mechanical or incident, `@manager @owner` in the same post.

**Examples:**

```
Mid-trip check
Quality: 5
Mechanical: NO
Incident: NO
Reviews asked: YES
```

```
Mid-trip check @bella @david
Quality: 3
Mechanical: YES — paddle splintered on guest 4's right blade, swapped at cave 2
Incident: NO
Reviews asked: not yet
```

## Post 3 — Closeout (Beat 11, reply in thread)

After Paddler's Circle ritual and gear stow. Before guide leaves the launch.

```
Closeout | return [time]
Incidents: NONE | [or detail]
Gear: OK | [or damage flagged]
Paddler's Circle: [first-timer induction (X guests) / 3rd visit (name) / 5th visit (name) / 10th visit (name) / none]
Reviews asked: YES | NO
Photos filed: YES | NO
Notable: [optional one-line for team]
```

**Examples:**

```
Closeout | return 11:55am
Incidents: NONE
Gear: OK
Paddler's Circle: first-timer induction (6 guests), 3rd visit (Karim)
Reviews asked: YES
Photos filed: YES
Notable: Lawson family — really emotional moment at cave entry, mom thanked me by name. That family is coming back.
```

```
Closeout | return 12:10pm @bella
Incidents: YES — guest 2 (Reyes) tipped at cave 1 launch, in water ~30 seconds, fully recovered, no injury, declined to continue tour, completed and got refund offer
Gear: OK
Paddler's Circle: none (tour cut short)
Reviews asked: NO (guest left immediately)
Photos filed: YES
Notable: Reyes was sober but spooked. I'd call her this afternoon if you can.
```

## Incident addendum (only if needed)

If an incident occurred during the tour, post a fourth message in the thread within 1 hour of return. This is the **detailed incident record** that supplements the brief "Incidents: YES" line in the closeout. Include:

```
Incident addendum
What happened: [chronological narrative]
Guests involved: [first names or party name]
Injury: [type, severity, body part — or NONE]
First aid administered: [YES/NO, what]
911 called: [YES/NO]
Witnesses: [other guests, sweep guide, third parties]
Followup needed: [calls to make, refunds, doctor recommendation]
@bella @david
```

If the incident is serious (transport, hospitalization, suspected liability concern), guide also records a 60-90 second audio addendum on their phone and posts it in the same thread. **Do not text or email about it. Slack thread only — single record location.**

---

## Photo handling

- **Dock group photo** posts in the thread starter.
- **On-water photos taken by guide** post in the closeout or as a separate reply with caption "On-water photos — [tour date]." These can be used for marketing only if the guest gave verbal consent (default opt-in unless they said otherwise during pre-brief).
- **Incident photos** (if any — e.g., gear damage, dock condition) post in the incident addendum.

Do NOT post photos of minors in any post that could be used for marketing without separate parent consent. For safety/incident records only.

---

## Channel etiquette

- Don't post in `#dckt-tour-logs` unless it's tour-related. Side chatter goes to main DCKT channel.
- React with ✅ as a reader if you've reviewed a tour's thread. Bella/David use this as a "seen" signal during coaching review.
- Don't edit posts after the fact. If something was wrong, reply with a correction. Edit history matters for the record.

---

## What's NOT in this template (and why)

This template intentionally does NOT include:

- A paper safety checklist with checkboxes. The audio/video briefing covers the items; the verbal acknowledgments capture confirmation; the post structure captures the record. Paper checkboxes are redundant and slow.
- Individual guest names on every post. Names are in the pre-tour shop check post and in FareHarbor; we don't need to re-list them in the briefing thread.
- A signature line. The video IS the signature.
- A "Tour SOP" with step-by-step pre-launch checks. Those live in `Operations/2-Hour Cave Point — Operations Playbook.md`. This template is for the record, not the procedure.
