# Employee Offboarding Checklist
**Clean exits protect the business. Complete every step regardless of circumstances.**

> [!tip]
> DCKT is a seasonal operation. End-of-season offboarding should follow the same steps, with a note about whether the employee is expected to return next season.

1. Notify employee of end date in writing (text or email)
2. Remove from FareHarbor scheduling rotation
3. Deactivate in Homebase (or set to inactive for seasonal return)
4. Remove from group text/Slack channel
5. Collect any company property: radios, dry bags, branded gear, keys
6. Revoke access to launch site storage if keyed
7. Confirm final hours in Homebase for payroll
8. Issue final paycheck per Wisconsin labor law requirements
9. Remove from any shared accounts or logins
10. Document reason for separation in employee file
11. If involuntary, note any incidents or performance issues in file
12. If seasonal, note whether employee is invited back for next season
13. Update equipment access list

> [!warning]
> Wisconsin law has specific requirements for final paycheck timing. Confirm compliance with state labor law before processing.

> [!warning]
> Collect keys and revoke access on or before the last day. Don't let it slide, especially at end of season when you might not be on-site daily.
