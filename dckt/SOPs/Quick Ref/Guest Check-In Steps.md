# Guest Check-In Steps
**First impression sets the tone for the whole tour. Make it count.**

1. Greet guests by name when possible (pull from FareHarbor booking)
2. Verify headcount against the booking in FareHarbor
3. Check waiver status in FareHarbor for each guest on that specific booking
4. If a guest hasn't signed, have them complete it on-site using the booking-specific waiver link (do NOT use a generic QR code)
5. Fit each guest with a Type III PFD, adjust straps, confirm snug fit
6. Ask about swimming ability and comfort level on water
7. Deliver safety briefing (see Deep Read: Safety Briefing Script)
8. Cover paddle technique basics: grip, forward stroke, turning
9. Confirm any physical limitations, injuries, or concerns
10. Mention water temperature if it's cold: explain what to expect if they get wet
11. Point out restroom location before launch
12. Assign kayaks and help guests into boats at water's edge

> [!warning]
> Never skip the waiver check. Every person on the water must have a signed waiver tied to their specific booking.

> [!tip]
> Door County attracts a lot of first-time kayakers on vacation. Budget an extra minute for the paddle technique demo. It'll save you time on the water.
