# Compliance Inspection Checklist
**Wisconsin DNR compliance. Stay ahead of inspections.**

1. Confirm all commercial kayaks meet Wisconsin DNR requirements for commercial watercraft
2. Verify any required DNR commercial operation permits are current
3. Check that every vessel on the water has a USCG-approved Type III PFD for each occupant
4. Verify PFDs are readily accessible (not buried under gear)
5. Confirm a throwable flotation device (Type IV) is available at the launch site
6. Check that a sound-producing device (whistle) is on each guide
7. Confirm all guides have CPR/First Aid certification on file
8. Check that the business liability insurance policy is current
9. Verify waivers are being collected for every guest via FareHarbor
10. Confirm no expired or recalled PFDs are in rotation (check manufacture date)
11. Verify all W-2 employment paperwork is current in Homebase (W-4, I-9)
12. Review incident log for any unreported events
13. Confirm seasonal operation dates align with any DNR permit conditions

> [!warning]
> Wisconsin DNR can conduct inspections at any time. Every vessel and guide must be compliant every day of the season.

> [!tip]
> Start-of-season and end-of-season are the most common times to have paperwork gaps. Do a full compliance review before the first trip of each season.
