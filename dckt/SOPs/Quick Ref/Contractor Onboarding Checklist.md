# Employee Onboarding Checklist
**Manager-facing. Every step completed before a new guide touches a guest.**

> [!warning]
> DCKT guides are W-2 employees through Homebase, NOT 1099 contractors. Use W-4 and I-9 forms, NOT W-9.

1. Send culture doc and have employee confirm they've read it
2. Complete Homebase onboarding: add employee profile, set pay rate, assign schedule
3. Collect W-4 (federal tax withholding)
4. Complete I-9 (employment eligibility verification) with valid documentation
5. Verify CPR/First Aid certification is current (copy on file)
6. Note kayak/paddle instructor certification (preferred, not required)
7. Note swiftwater/open water rescue training (preferred, not required)
8. Run background check and confirm clear
9. Confirm swim proficiency (observed assessment)
10. Schedule orientation session covering:
    - Equipment overview (kayaks, PFDs, paddles)
    - Launch site walkthrough
    - FareHarbor booking system basics
    - Weather Hold Protocol (Door County-specific conditions)
    - Safety Briefing Script (must be able to deliver from memory)
    - Seasonal operation expectations (start date, end date, peak weeks)
    - Wisconsin DNR regulations overview
11. Schedule shadow trip: new employee observes experienced guide
12. Schedule co-guide trip: new employee leads with experienced guide present
13. Debrief after co-guide trip and address any gaps
14. If ready, schedule solo sign-off trip with manager or senior guide observing
15. Add to scheduling rotation in FareHarbor and Homebase
16. Add to group text/Slack channel

> [!warning]
> No solo trips until the sign-off trip is completed and approved. No exceptions.

> [!tip]
> Seasonal hires need to ramp up fast. Try to compress the shadow/co-guide/sign-off into the first week if trip volume allows.
