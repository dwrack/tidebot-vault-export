# Door County Kayak Tours — SOP: Guide Offboarding
*Updated 2026-05-20 | W-2 employees*

> **Purpose:** When an employee leaves (voluntarily or otherwise), this is the complete checklist. Nothing gets missed, nothing lingers.
>
> DCKT guides, captains, and managers are **W-2 employees** paid through Homebase. This SOP uses employment offboarding steps — final hours in Homebase, final pay under Wisconsin labor law. There are no contractor commissions, 1099s, or ICAs to settle.

---

## When to Use This

- Guide, captain, or manager resigns
- Employee is terminated or not renewed
- Seasonal team member is not returning for the next season

---

## Step 1: Communication (Day of Decision)

- [ ] Verbal or written confirmation that employment is ending
- [ ] If termination: brief, direct reason documented (internal notes only)
- [ ] Final shift date confirmed
- [ ] Collect any company property: keys, radios, PFDs, dry bags, branded gear

---

## Step 2: System Access Removal (Within 24 Hours)

### FareHarbor
- [ ] Remove from guide/staff scheduling
- [ ] Remove dashboard access

### Homebase
- [ ] Deactivate the employee profile (or set to **inactive** if a seasonal return is expected)

### Communication Channels
- [ ] Remove from Slack workspace (or relevant channels)
- [ ] Remove from group texts / WhatsApp groups
- [ ] Remove from any shared email aliases

### Other Platforms
- [ ] Remove from Google Drive shared folders
- [ ] Remove from GHL sub-account if they had access
- [ ] Remove from DocuSeal (revoke access, retain signed documents)

---

## Step 3: Regulatory & Permits (Within 7 Days)

- [ ] Update any state/local permits if the departing person was listed
- [ ] Update Crew Roster & Credentials Tracker (mark as departed + date)
- [ ] Return or note expiration of any permit cards

---

## Step 4: Financial Closeout (Within Wisconsin Labor-Law Timeline)

- [ ] Confirm final recorded hours in Homebase for all completed shifts
- [ ] Process the final paycheck through Homebase payroll
- [ ] Issue final pay within the timeline required by Wisconsin labor law — confirm current requirements before processing
- [ ] Document final payment date and amount

---

## Step 5: Documentation & Vault Cleanup

- [ ] Update this vault: remove from any "active" lists or rosters
- [ ] Add to offboarding log below
- [ ] Archive (don't delete) their signed employment agreement, W-4, I-9, and credentials

## Notes

- Never delete an employee's signed employment agreement, W-4, or I-9. Archive them — retention is required.
- If someone might come back seasonally, set them to **INACTIVE** in Homebase rather than fully offboarding.
- Wisconsin has specific rules for final-paycheck timing. Confirm compliance before processing the last payment.
