# Wind Cancellation — Rebooking Texts

Use these copy-paste SMS templates when wind conditions force us to cancel a kayak tour. Pick the option that fits the situation, fill in the bracketed fields, and send.

---

## Quick decision guide

- **Same-day e-bike swap available?** → Use Template A (push e-bike at the same time).
- **No same-day swap, but Cave Point slots open tomorrow?** → Use Template B (reschedule to Cave Point).
- **Want to offer both at once?** → Use Template C (combo deal — 20% off e-bike today + full-price kayak tomorrow).
- **Guest can't do any alternative?** → Use Template D (flexible rebook / refund).

---

## Template A — Same-time e-bike swap

> Hi [Name], this is Door County Kayak Tours. Due to high winds on the water, we have to reschedule today's [TIME] kayak tour for safety. Good news — we can switch you over to our [TIME] e-bike tour today instead, same time, same group. Want us to move you over? Just reply YES and you're all set.

---

## Template B — Cave Point reschedule (tomorrow)

> Hi [Name], this is Door County Kayak Tours. We have to reschedule today's [TIME] kayak tour due to high winds on the water. We'd love to get you back on the schedule — we have Cave Point kayak tours open tomorrow at **9:00 AM** or **12:00 PM**. Reply with the time that works and we'll rebook you at no extra cost.

---

## Template C — Combo deal (e-bike today + kayak tomorrow)

> Hi [Name], this is Door County Kayak Tours. We have to reschedule today's kayak tour due to high winds. We've got two ways to make it up to you:
>
> **Option 1:** Cave Point kayak tour tomorrow — 9:00 AM or 12:00 PM.
>
> **Option 2:** Combo deal — 20% off our e-bike tour today, plus your full kayak tour tomorrow at regular price.
>
> Just reply with the option you'd like and we'll get you booked. Thanks!

---

## Template D — Flexible rebook / refund fallback

> Hi [Name], this is Door County Kayak Tours. Unfortunately we have to reschedule your [TIME] kayak tour today due to unsafe wind conditions on the water. We can rebook you any day this week, swap you to an e-bike tour, or issue a full refund — whichever you prefer. Just let us know how you'd like to proceed. Thanks for understanding!

---

## Notes for the sender

- Always lead with the safety reason — guests respond better when the "why" is clear.
- Default first offer is the **same-time swap** (Template A) — it keeps the day intact for the guest.
- The **combo deal (Template C)** is our strongest save when the guest seems hesitant to fully cancel.
- If the guest had a Cave Point booking originally, prioritize Template B so they still get the experience they signed up for.
- Always confirm the rebook in FareHarbor before replying "you're all set."
