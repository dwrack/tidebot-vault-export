# Standard Operating Procedures (SOP)
**Door County Kayak Tours**

**Effective Date:** May 20, 2026

This SOP is the master policy document for all employees of Door County Kayak Tours. It outlines employee expectations, communication standards, scheduling procedures, safety requirements, payroll procedures, and operational responsibilities. All employees are required to read, understand, and acknowledge these procedures as a condition of employment.

> [!info] How this document fits
> This master SOP is the policy spine. Each section links to the detailed operational SOP that governs the day-to-day "how." Where this document and a detailed SOP differ, **the detailed SOP is the source of truth.** New hires should also read the culture doc, [[How We Run Things — DCKT]], before their first day.

## 1. Employee Account Creation

All employees are required to create and maintain active accounts with Homebase and Slack.

Employees must provide the following information prior to their first scheduled shift:

- Full legal name
- Current phone number
- Valid email address
- Emergency contact name and phone number
- Weekly availability

Employees are responsible for keeping their personal information updated at all times. The full new-hire process is documented in [[Door County Kayak Tours — SOP Guide Onboarding]].

## 2. Scheduling Procedures with Homebase

All scheduling will be managed through Homebase.

- Employees must enter and maintain accurate availability in Homebase.
- Time-off requests must be submitted in advance through Homebase.
- Employees are responsible for reviewing schedules regularly and confirming assigned shifts.
- Employees must clock in and clock out through Homebase at the beginning and end of each shift.
- Failure to properly clock in or out may delay payroll processing.

## 3. Communication Standards

Slack is the primary communication platform for Door County Kayak Tours.

- Employees are expected to check Slack regularly before, during, and after scheduled shifts.
- Professional communication is required at all times.
- Employees should respond to work-related communication in a timely and respectful manner.

Staff must communicate clearly and promptly regarding tour readiness, including:

- Confirmation that all safety checks have been completed
- Confirmation that all guests have signed waivers
- Notification that tours are departing
- Any delays, emergencies, or changes in weather conditions

## 4. Tour Guide Expectations

The detailed, step-by-step checklist for every tour — day-before through post-tour — lives in [[Door County Kayak Tours — SOP Pre-Tour Preparation]]. Guides follow that SOP on every shift. The summary below is the policy expectation.

### Pre-Shift Responsibilities

- Arrive on time and prepared for the shift
- Inspect kayaks, paddles, life jackets, and safety equipment
- Review weather conditions and water conditions
- Ensure waivers are signed before guests enter the water
- Conduct guest check-in and safety briefing

### During the Tour

- Maintain professionalism and positive guest interaction
- Prioritize guest safety at all times
- Monitor group pace and environmental conditions
- Communicate clearly with guests and staff
- Follow all kayaking safety procedures and company guidelines

### Post-Shift Responsibilities

- Return equipment to proper storage areas
- Report damaged equipment immediately
- Clean and organize tour areas
- Submit incident reports when necessary
- Clock out accurately in Homebase

### Certifications

Guides are required to hold and maintain current CPR and First Aid certification as a condition of guiding. Employees are responsible for renewing certifications before they lapse and providing updated copies to management. Certification requirements are detailed in [[Door County Kayak Tours — SOP Guide Onboarding]].

## 5. Guest Eligibility — Age & Weight

Guides confirm guest eligibility at check-in. Booking and front-of-house communications should set these expectations before guests arrive.

**Age**

- Minimum age to participate is **5 years old**. Guests under 5 cannot be accommodated.
- Children aged 5 and older ride in a **tandem kayak with a participating adult**. Whether an older minor may paddle their own single kayak is at the **guide's discretion** at check-in, based on the child's size, paddling ability, and the day's conditions.
- A **parent or legal guardian must sign the waiver** for any guest under 18.

**Weight**

- Kayak capacity sets the limits: **single kayak — 275 lbs per paddler; tandem kayak — 450 lbs total combined.**
- Guests at or near these limits are placed in the appropriate kayak; when in doubt, the guide makes the call at check-in. Guests who ask about weight before booking should be helped to choose the right setup.

## 6. Weather and Emergency Procedures

Safety is the highest priority during all kayak tours.

Tours may be delayed, modified, or canceled due to unsafe weather conditions, including lightning, strong winds, severe storms, or unsafe water conditions. **The specific weather-hold decision thresholds** (wind speed, lightning distance, visibility, water temperature) are defined in the Weather Hold Protocol in [[Door County Kayak Tours — SOP Pre-Tour Preparation]]. Employees must immediately notify management of changing weather conditions or safety concerns.

In the event of an emergency:

- Remain calm and prioritize guest safety
- Follow emergency response procedures
- Contact emergency services when necessary
- Notify management immediately
- Complete an incident report after the situation is resolved
- Guides should never continue a tour if conditions are considered unsafe.

The full emergency response sequence and the incident report template are documented in [[Door County Kayak Tours — SOP Coast Guard & Compliance]]. Any injury, near-miss, or property damage must be reported within 24 hours.

## 7. Payroll Procedures

Employees of Door County Kayak Tours are W-2 employees. Payroll is processed through Homebase.

- Employees are responsible for accurately clocking in and out for every shift.
- Employees should review their recorded hours regularly and report discrepancies immediately.
- Unauthorized overtime is prohibited unless approved by management.
- Failure to accurately record hours may result in payroll delays or disciplinary action.

## 8. Employee Conduct and Accountability

- Employees are expected to conduct themselves professionally at all times.
- Harassment, discrimination, unsafe behavior, substance use during work hours, or disrespectful conduct will not be tolerated.
- Employees are accountable for following company procedures, maintaining safety standards, and representing Door County Kayak Tours professionally.
- Failure to follow SOP guidelines may result in disciplinary action, including termination.
