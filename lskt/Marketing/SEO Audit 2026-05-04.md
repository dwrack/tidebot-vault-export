## Executive Summary

Three things matter:

1. **The site is functionally an Austin site, not a Texas-wide brand site.** Despite the "Lone Star" name and the LLC umbrella covering Houston (Buffalo Bayou Kayak, Houston Pedal Barge) and Austin, every single ranking query is Austin-specific. There is one orphaned Houston blog post pulling 1 click. Houston traffic for this domain is essentially zero.
2. **Massive striking-distance opportunity.** Page-2 keywords are pulling 11,630 impressions but converting almost none of them. The biggest single opportunity, "best place to kayak in austin" at position 13.7, is sitting on **3,475 impressions and 0 clicks**.
3. **The homepage is doing 95% of the lifting** with no real category pages competing for non-brand terms. Tour pages exist but aren't ranking.

Top priority: decide if lonestarkayaktours.com stays Austin-focused or becomes a true Texas hub. Given austinkayaktours.com exists as a separate property, recommendation below is to keep LSKT as the Austin commercial site and **not** geo-segment Houston into it (Houston gets its own domain).

## City Segmentation Verdict: Austin-Only

**Houston-specific impressions in top 50 queries:** 38 (just `austin kayak houston` and `kemah kayak rental` and a few stragglers).
**Austin-specific impressions:** ~10,500.
**Ratio:** ~99% Austin / 1% Houston.

The site already ranks for tons of "austin" modifier queries. It does not rank for `houston kayak`, `buffalo bayou kayak`, `kayak houston tx`, etc. There's a single Houston blog post (`/blog/buffalo-bayou-kayak-tours-in-houston-texas/`) sitting at position 22.8 with 1 click.

**Recommendation:**
- Keep lonestarkayaktours.com Austin-focused. It's already winning that battle.
- Don't dilute it by adding Houston tour pages. Houston traffic should funnel to a dedicated Houston property (buffalobayoukayak.com or similar).
- Use lonestarkayaktours.com as the operational/commercial Austin site. Treat austinkayaktours.com (currently undocumented per the AKT vault) as either redundant or a redirect target.
- The "Lone Star" brand naming is misleading SEO-wise but irrelevant to ranking. Don't rebrand the URL, just stop pretending it's a Texas-wide site.

One caveat: if the long-term plan is to consolidate all Texas kayak ops under one domain, then yes, geo-segment with `/houston/` and `/austin/` subfolders. But that's a 6-month project and would tank the existing Austin rankings during migration. Don't recommend it unless there's a strategic reason.

## Content Gaps (5 missing pieces)

1. **`/kayak-rental-austin/` (or `/austin-kayak-rentals/`)**
   Rental keywords pull 700+ impressions and the homepage is the only competitor. A purpose-built page should rank top 5 within 60 days. Highest commercial value gap.

2. **`/best-places-to-kayak-in-austin/` (guide)**
   3,475 impressions for "best place to kayak in austin" with 0 clicks. This is the largest single keyword opportunity. Build a long-form guide listing 8-10 spots (Lady Bird Lake, Town Lake, Barton Creek, Lake Austin, McKinney Falls, Lake Travis, etc.) and internally link every spot to the relevant tour page. Free content marketing play with massive upside.

3. **`/austin-kayak-bat-tour/` consolidation page**
   Right now bat tour traffic is split across `/kayak/sunset-bat-bridge-kayak-tour/` and `/kayak/paddle-board-bat-tour/`. Multiple page-2 keywords (`austin bat kayak tour`, `bat kayak tour austin`, `austin kayak bat tour`) at positions 8-12 with 350+ combined impressions. A consolidated landing page would clean this up.

4. **`/things-to-do-in-austin/` or `/austin-itinerary/` (top of funnel)**
   The vacation-planning searcher who hasn't decided on kayaking yet. Existing blogs (breakfast, dog-friendly, skyline) hint at this audience. A real "Things to Do in Austin" hub linking to your tours would capture massive top-of-funnel intent.

5. **`/lady-bird-lake-kayaking/` (geo-specific)**
   "kayaking lady bird lake" / "kayak lady bird lake" / "lady bird lake kayaking" pull 500+ combined impressions, all positions 8-11. A dedicated lake page would consolidate. Bonus: links from this page to bat tour, skyline tour, sunrise tour all make sense.

## Top 3 Critical Issues

1. **`/tours/` is a dead category page (position 35.9, 1,419 impressions, 2 clicks).**
   This should be your second-most powerful page after the homepage. Either fix it (make it a real category landing page with tour cards, descriptions, internal links) or kill it and consolidate. Right now it's bleeding crawl budget.

2. **`/blog/skyline/` is sitting on 9,196 impressions of unused equity.**
   Position 8.6 with 6 clicks (CTR 0.07%). Either the title is wrong, the search intent is wrong, or the page is too thin. This is the single most underperforming page on the site. Likely searchers are looking for skyline photos / building names, and the page isn't delivering. Audit it: either rewrite for the audience landing on it, or add prominent CTAs to the skyline tour for the people who DO care about kayaking.

3. **No dedicated landing pages for high-intent commercial keywords.**
   The homepage is ranking for everything, which means it ranks for nothing well. "kayak rental austin," "best place to kayak in austin," "guided kayak tours" all need their own pages. Trying to win commercial keywords from the homepage caps you at position 8-12 forever.
