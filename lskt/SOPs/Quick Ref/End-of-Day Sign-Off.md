# End-of-Day Sign-Off
**Lock it down before you leave.**

1. Confirm all equipment is stored and secured (kayaks, paddles, PFDs)
2. Verify all PFDs are hanging or laid out to dry (Houston humidity breeds mildew fast)
3. Check that first aid kit is restocked for tomorrow
4. Review tomorrow's FareHarbor bookings and note headcount
5. Confirm guide assignments for tomorrow's tours
6. Send any outstanding incident or near-miss reports to management
7. Empty trash and clean up staging area
8. Lock storage if applicable
9. Text management confirming close-out is complete

> [!warning]
> PFDs stored wet in a closed space will mildew within 24 hours in Houston's climate. Always air dry before storing.

> [!tip]
> A 30-second text to management at end of day ("closed out, all good, 14 guests tomorrow") keeps everyone aligned with zero effort.
