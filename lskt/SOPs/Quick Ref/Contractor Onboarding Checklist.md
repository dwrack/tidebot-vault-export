# Contractor Onboarding Checklist
**Manager-facing. Complete all steps before a new guide takes their first tour.**

1. Collect signed 1099 contractor agreement
2. Collect W-9 form (required for tax reporting)
3. Verify CPR/First Aid certification is current (not expired)
4. Verify swim proficiency (in-person demonstration or documented proof)
5. Run background check and confirm clearance
6. Collect copies of any kayak/paddle instructor certifications (preferred, not required)
7. Collect copies of any swiftwater or open water rescue training (preferred, not required)
8. Set up contractor profile in payment system
9. Schedule shadow tours (minimum 2) with an experienced guide
10. After shadow phase, schedule co-guide tours (minimum 3) for evaluation
11. Provide access to all SOPs in the Lone Star Kayak Tours vault
12. Walk through FareHarbor: how to view bookings, check waivers, note headcount changes
13. Review weather thresholds and Weather Hold Protocol
14. Confirm contractor understands TPWD compliance requirements
15. Get management sign-off on solo clearance after co-guide evaluation

> [!warning]
> No guide takes a solo tour without management sign-off. Shadow and co-guide phases are not optional regardless of experience level.

> [!tip]
> 1099 contractors set their own availability, but they still follow our safety protocols and SOPs on every tour. Make sure that distinction is clear during onboarding.
