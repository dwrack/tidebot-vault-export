# Night-Before Checklist
**Confirm tomorrow's trips are locked in before you leave for the night.**

1. Open FareHarbor and review all bookings for tomorrow
2. Confirm headcount per trip and note any special requests or accessibility needs
3. Check weather forecast for wind speed, lightning risk, visibility, and temperature
4. If wind >15 mph or storms forecast, flag for potential Weather Hold (see Deep Read: Weather Hold Protocol)
5. Confirm guide(s) assigned to each trip and text to verify availability
6. If a guide is unavailable, contact backup guides immediately
7. Verify equipment count matches tomorrow's bookings: kayaks, PFDs, paddles
8. Confirm launch site is clear and accessible
9. Check water temperature if early season or cold front has moved through
10. Charge any electronics (radios, phone batteries)

> [!warning]
> Never assume a guide confirmed just because they were scheduled. Get a text-back confirmation every time. Guides are 1099 contractors and aren't obligated to be available.

> [!tip]
> Houston-area weather can shift fast with coastal fronts. Check the marine forecast, not just the general weather app.
