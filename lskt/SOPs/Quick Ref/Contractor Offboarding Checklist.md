# Contractor Offboarding Checklist
**Manager-facing. Complete when a guide exits the contractor relationship.**

1. Confirm final tour date and that all scheduled tours are covered or reassigned
2. Collect any company equipment: radios, dry bags, first aid kits, branded gear
3. Revoke access to FareHarbor
4. Revoke access to any shared communication channels (Slack, group texts)
5. Remove from scheduling system
6. Issue final payment for any outstanding tours worked
7. Confirm W-9 is on file for year-end 1099 filing
8. Collect feedback: ask what worked and what didn't (optional but valuable)
9. Update contractor roster and notify active guides of the change
10. If departure was performance-related, document the reason for records

> [!warning]
> Revoking FareHarbor and communication access should happen on the last day, not weeks later. Former contractors should not have access to guest data or booking info.

> [!tip]
> Good offboarding keeps the door open for contractors who might come back next season. Even if someone leaves mid-season, handle it professionally.
