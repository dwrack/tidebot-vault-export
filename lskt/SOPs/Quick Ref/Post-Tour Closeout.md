# Post-Tour Closeout
**Tour isn't over until gear is accounted for and guests are off-site.**

1. Help all guests out of kayaks at the takeout point
2. Collect all PFDs and do a count against what went out
3. Collect all paddles and check for new damage
4. Rinse kayaks if saltwater or heavy mud exposure
5. Visually inspect each kayak hull for new damage from the tour
6. Secure all kayaks in the storage area or on trailer
7. Ask guests how the experience was (genuine, not scripted)
8. Mention reviews: "If you had a good time, we'd love a Google review"
9. Check the launch and takeout area for left-behind items
10. Log any equipment issues, guest incidents, or near-misses
11. Update FareHarbor if there were any no-shows or headcount changes

> [!tip]
> The review ask works best when it's casual and earned. If you had a good connection with the group, a simple mention goes further than a card or QR code.

> [!warning]
> Check the water's edge for gear that may have drifted. Paddles left at the shoreline disappear fast.
