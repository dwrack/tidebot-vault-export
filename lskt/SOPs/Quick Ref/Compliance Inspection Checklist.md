# Compliance Inspection Checklist
**TPWD regulatory readiness. Review monthly and before any known inspection.**

1. Verify all kayaks have current Texas registration
2. Confirm Type III PFDs are available for every guest plus spares
3. Inspect all PFDs: buckles functional, no rips, straps intact, Coast Guard approved labels visible
4. Verify throw bags are stocked and accessible for lead guides
5. Confirm first aid kits are complete and sealed
6. Verify guide certifications are current: CPR/First Aid not expired
7. Check that waiver process is active in FareHarbor and waivers are being collected for every booking
8. Confirm insurance documentation is current and accessible
9. Verify emergency action plan is posted or accessible at launch site
10. Check that all required safety equipment is on-site, not in storage or transit
11. Review incident log for any unreported events
12. Confirm fire extinguisher on transport vehicle (if applicable, for trailer tow vehicles)

> [!warning]
> TPWD can conduct unannounced inspections. If an inspector arrives, cooperate fully, be polite, and call management immediately. Do not argue or delay.

> [!tip]
> The best time to prepare for an inspection is every day, not the day you hear one is coming. Running compliant daily means an inspection is just a normal Tuesday.
