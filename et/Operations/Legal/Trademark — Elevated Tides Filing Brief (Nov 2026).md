## Cost
- USPTO: $350 per class = $1,400 for 4 classes (using ID Manual descriptions; free-text descriptions add $200/class).
- Statement of use later for each ITU class: $150 each ($450). Extensions if a class is not in use 6 months after allowance: $125 per class per 6 months, up to 36 months.
- Comprehensive search: $300-600.
- Attorney: typically $1,000-2,500 flat for a multi-class filing.
- Total year one: roughly $3,000-4,500.

## Timeline
- Filing to examiner: 6-8 months.
- Registration (use-based class): 12-14 months if no office action.
- ITU classes register only after the statement of use is accepted.
- Priority date is the filing date, which is the reason to file now rather than after the dock rebuild.
