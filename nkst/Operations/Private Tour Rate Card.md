## What this is

One per-person private rate card, used two ways:

1. **Published product.** Groups that want the swamp to themselves — bachelorette, corporate, family reunion, birthday. Connor quotes straight off this card, no escalation to David.
2. **Salvage offer.** When a departure is under the 4-guest minimum, the same numbers are the "keep your slot, make it private" offer.

Private is an **upsell**, not a discount. Every tier sits above the $65 public seat.

## Rules of the card

- **No inversions.** Every boundary is checked: a group never pays less by adding a person. 3 people at $450, 4 at $480. 6 at $720, 7 at $770. 12 at $1,320, 13 at $1,365.
- **Over 20 people:** stop and call David. Multiple vans, multiple guide crews, different math.
- **Public tours cannot be bought out.** Public caps at 12 with two guides and stays public. A group that wants exclusivity books private.
- **Quote the total, not the per-head**, on parties of 4 and up. "$1,320 for your group of 12" closes better than "$110 each."

---

## Using it as the salvage offer

When FareHarbor shows a standard departure under 4 paying guests, 48-72 hours out, by text or email — never day-of, never at pickup:

1. **Offer the free move first.** Nearby departure with a fuller group, no extra cost.
2. **Then offer private.** Their exact slot, whole boat to themselves, at the rate above.

Script:

> "Looks like it's just [you / the two of you] on this one. Happy to move you to [nearby departure] where there's already a full group, no extra cost. Or if this time works best for you, we can run it as your own private tour, just your group and the guide, for $[X] total."

A 2-person booking that converts to private is $350 instead of $130 on a slot that was going to lose money. A 2-person booking that reschedules costs nothing and keeps the guest.
