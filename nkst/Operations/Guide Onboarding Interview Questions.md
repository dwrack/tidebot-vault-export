# Guide Onboarding Interview Questions

Send these via Slack during onboarding. Not all at once. Space them out over the first week, 3-4 questions per day. Frame it as "we're building out guide profiles so guests can get to know the team." Keep it casual. Tell them there are no wrong answers and they can skip anything they're not into.

These answers feed directly into the sales agent's follow-up messages. The more specific and personal, the better. Generic answers don't work. Push gently for real stories, real opinions, real details.

---

## About You

1. Where are you from originally? How'd you end up in New Orleans?
2. What were you doing before you started guiding?
3. What's something most people wouldn't guess about you?
4. If you weren't doing this, what would you be doing?
5. What's the most interesting place you've traveled to? What made it interesting?

## About the Swamp

6. What's your favorite tour we offer and why? (Be specific, not "they're all great.")
7. What's the single best moment you've had on the water with a group?
8. Is there a spot on the tour route that you look forward to every single time? What is it about that spot?
9. What's the most surprising thing you've learned about the swamp ecosystem since you started?
10. What wildlife sighting still gets you excited, even after hundreds of tours?
11. What do you tell nervous first-timers right before they get in the kayak?
12. What's something about the swamp that most people don't know and you wish they did?
13. How does the swamp change across seasons? What's your favorite time of year out there?

## About New Orleans

14. What's your go-to restaurant in New Orleans? The one you actually eat at, not the one you'd tell a tourist.
15. What's a spot in the city that most tourists miss but shouldn't?
16. If a guest asked you "what should I do tonight?", what would you tell them?
17. What neighborhood do you live in? What do you love about it?
18. Best coffee in the city?
19. If someone only had 48 hours in New Orleans, what would you tell them to skip?
20. What's the most overrated thing about New Orleans? What's the most underrated?

## About Guiding

21. What's the thing you're most proud of as a guide?
22. Has a guest ever said something to you after a tour that really stuck with you? What was it?
23. What's the hardest part of the job that guests never see?
24. How do you handle a guest who's scared of alligators?
25. What do you want people to feel when they leave your tour?
26. What makes our tours different from every other swamp tour in Louisiana? (In your own words, not the marketing version.)

---

## How to Use These Answers

Once you have responses, build a guide profile using this format and add it to the agent's knowledge base:

**[Guide Name]**
- Background: [1-2 sentences, where they're from, how they got here]
- Swamp credentials: [how long guiding, tours led, any unique qualifications]
- Favorite tour: [which one and why, in their words]
- Best moment on the water: [specific story]
- Opening line with guests: [what they actually say]
- New Orleans rec: [restaurant, bar, or activity, with the reason]
- Hidden gem: [something tourists don't find]
- Fun fact: [the unexpected thing]
- In their own words: [a direct quote that captures who they are]

These profiles get referenced by the sales agent (Magnolia) in follow-up messages. The more specific and human the answers, the better the follow-ups perform. "I like nature" doesn't work. "The first time I saw a barred owl swoop down and grab a fish 6 feet from my kayak, I knew I was never leaving this job" works.
