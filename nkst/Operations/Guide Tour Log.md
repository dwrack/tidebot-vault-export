# Guide Tour Log (Audio + Photos/Video)

Required for every tour. Two voice recordings in the Slack channel (one before you leave, one when you're back) plus 2 short videos and 2 photos when you're done. The whole thing takes about 3 minutes total. This is an insurance requirement and it helps us all get better.

---

## Recording 1: Pre-Tour Check-In

Record this in the Slack channel before you pull out of Piety St. Just talk through it naturally, like you're leaving a voicemail for your contractor.

**Cover these (in any order):**

- Your name, today's date, which tour and location
- How many guests
- Odometer reading and gas level
- Van condition: clean, lights clear, trailer locked and strapped
- PFD count and condition, paddles counted
- First aid kit, tow rope, phone charged
- Weather: what it looks like, any alerts, water level if you know it
- Anything you want to flag before you head out

**Takes about 45 seconds.** You don't have to hit every bullet word-for-word. Just make sure the vehicle, gear, safety, and weather stuff is in there. That's what insurance needs on record.

---

## Recording 2: Guest Question (Way Out)

On the drive to the launch or while everyone's getting settled at the put-in, pick one of these and pass the phone around. Let people riff. Record it.

1. "What's the most adventurous thing you've done this year?"
2. "Has anyone here been in a kayak before? What happened?"
3. "What brought y'all to New Orleans?"
4. "If you could have any animal you saw today as a pet, what would you pick?"
5. "What's one thing on your bucket list you haven't done yet?"

Drop the recording in the Slack channel. Don't overthink it. Some groups will give you gold, some won't. Both are fine.

---

## Recording 3: Post-Tour Check-In

Record this when you're back at Piety St and everything's put away.

**Cover these:**

- Your name, date, tour just finished
- Odometer reading and miles driven
- Gas topped off at Shell (code 6006) or not, and why
- Van clean, no guest stuff left behind
- PFDs hung and sprayed with MiraZyme
- All gear accounted for, any damage
- Van, container, and gate locked
- Anything to flag: vehicle issues, gear problems, guest situations

**Then add your take:**

- How'd the tour go? Anything stand out?
- Anything you'd change for next time?
- Any guest moments worth mentioning?

---

## The Proof Set: 2 Videos + 2 Photos (With Your Post-Tour Check-In)

Drop these in the same Slack thread as Recording 3, before you leave Piety St. About 90 seconds with your phone.

1. **Video, 10-15 seconds:** pan the van interior from the rear or side door, floors and seats visible. Shows it's clean and nothing got left behind.
2. **Photo: PFDs on the rack**, hung to dry. Get the MiraZyme bottle in the shot.
3. **Video, 5-10 seconds:** the dashboard, showing the gas gauge and that no warning lights are on (check engine, oil, tire pressure). Say the odometer reading out loud while you film if you want to knock it out in one go.
4. **Photo: locked gate or container** on your way out.

Why video for 1 and 3: a still can't really prove the whole van is clean or that the dash is clear of warning lights. A quick pan can.

Your tour log isn't complete without these, and payment for the tour processes off the complete log. Not trying to be a pain about it: the footage settles every "was the van clean" question before it ever becomes a question.

**One more rule that protects YOU:** if you show up and the van or gear is NOT the way the last guide's proof says they left it, film it and drop it in Slack before you touch anything. That puts it on the right person. If you don't flag it at pickup, the condition at drop-off is yours.

---

## Recording 4: Guest Question (Way Back)

Different vibe now. They've had the experience. On the paddle back or the drive home, pick one and pass the phone around again.

1. "What moment out there are you going to remember?"
2. "What's something you're grateful for right now?"
3. "If you could bring one person back here, who would it be?"
4. "What surprised you most about today?"
5. "What's one thing you want to do before the end of this year?"

Drop it in the channel. These responses are the ones that end up on our website, in our marketing, in our story. When guests say it in their own voice, that's real.

---

## What Happens With These

**Insurance:** Recordings 1 and 3 are your timestamped safety and vehicle documentation. Date, conditions, gear, mileage, all on record.

**Contractor:** Reviews weekly. Catches gear issues early, tracks mileage for oil changes (every 3,500 mi), follows up on anything flagged.

**Marketing:** Guest recordings get pulled for social content, review prompts, website quotes, brand story. Guides who consistently surface great guest moments get recognized for it.

**Culture:** Your post-tour take matters. If something needs to change, say it. If something went great, say that too. Every recording gets listened to.
