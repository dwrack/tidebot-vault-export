### Tue 7/28 — Tips: printer or iPhone screen

> Kicking around two options for tips and add-ons in the vans. Option one, a small receipt printer: more prep on our end (stocked paper, harder to fix after it prints), but the receipt gets a checkbox for a % tip. Option two, an iPhone screen guests can tap, like when a server hands you the screen, diverts their eyes, and asks you to "just answer a few more questions." I'm asking FareHarbor if they have a customer-facing screen for exactly this.
>
> Either way, the upside for you: a % tip coming off a bigger bill. If the guest added sunscreen, a hat, a phone case, that math works in your favor. Push the add-ons, keep them stocked.
>
> Two questions: would you actually use either one, or is it one more thing to babysit? Be straight with me. And what's the service signal like at the launch? I like the tap screen but if signal's dead it's useless, and I'm not sure I'm ready for a Starlink on the van (ugh).
