# Combo Tour Van Reset SOP

**Who:** the guide on any combo tour (kayak + plantation).
**When:** while guests are inside the Whitney Plantation, about 90 minutes of downtime.
**Why:** combo tours are where newer guides earn their way onto more 2-hour and Extended tours. This is real work, and doing it well every time is what moves you up in Crew Points.

---

## Before you leave the marina

Check the van is stocked. Fix anything missing BEFORE guests load.

- [ ] Sunscreen (at least 2 bottles)
- [ ] Waterproof phone cases (1 per guest on the manifest, plus 2 spares)
- [ ] Interior cleaning kit: microfiber cloths, interior wipes or spray, trash bags, a small towel for wet seats
- [ ] Water for guests

**Post in your Pre-Tour log:** photo of the stocked bin (sunscreen + phone cases visible).

If something's out, tell Jeff in Slack right away so it gets restocked. Don't leave it for the next guide.

---

## During the plantation stop

1. Drop guests at Whitney and confirm the pickup time with them.
2. Drive to the van wash station around the corner from Whitney *(exact name and address: TBD, Jeff to confirm)*.
3. **Exterior:** wash off the mud and road grime, wheels included.
4. **Interior:**
   - Pull all trash, bottles and wrappers
   - Wipe down seats, armrests, door handles and the dash
   - Dry any wet seats or floor mats from the kayak half
   - Shake out the floor mats
5. Restock anything guests used (sunscreen, phone cases) from the backup supply if it's on board.
6. Be back at Whitney **10 minutes before pickup**. The van is cleaned on guest downtime only, never on guest time.

---

## Proof (this is the accountability part)

Post in your **Post-Tour log** in Slack:
- [ ] 1 photo: van exterior after the wash
- [ ] 1 photo: interior, back seats
- [ ] 1 photo: interior, front and dash
- [ ] 1 line: "Stock check: sunscreen ✓ cases ✓" or what's running low

No photos = the reset didn't happen. That counts against reliability, same as a missing tour log.

---

## How it's checked

- TideBot looks for the 3 van photos on every combo tour's Post-Tour log and flags any that are missing to David in #helm-nkst.
- The next guide to take that van does a quick look at loading. If it's dirty or empty, they post a photo and it goes back to the last combo guide.
- Clean resets plus on-time pickups count as reliability points. That's the fastest way to more 2-hour and Extended tours.
