## Screening call, 15 minutes

Ask these in order. The first three are disqualifiers, so get them out of the way before spending time.

1. Which specific day of the week can you commit to, every week, through at least spring? (If the answer is vague, this is not the hire. Vague is the exact problem we are solving.)
2. Do you have a valid driver's license, and are you comfortable driving a 15-passenger van pulling a trailer?
3. Do you have CPR certification, or can you get it in the next two weeks?
4. Walk me through your week. What else are you doing? (We want to hear a real structure that has a hole in it, not someone with nothing going on.)
5. Have you guided, taught, bartended, or done anything else where you were responsible for a group of strangers having a good time?
6. It is 96 degrees, you are two hours in, and one of your guests is struggling and wants to turn back. What do you do?
7. What do you know about the Manchac swamp or Louisiana wetlands? (No wrong answer. We are listening for curiosity, not knowledge.)

Question 6 is the one that separates people. Listen for whether they think about the group, not just the one guest.
