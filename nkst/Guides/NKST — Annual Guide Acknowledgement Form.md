# NKST Annual Guide Acknowledgement Form
*Master version | Last updated April 2026 | Renewed annually each January 1*

This is the single signature record that confirms each guide has read, understood, and agreed to operate under NKST's full body of policies for the year. Signed once at onboarding, then renewed every January 1 alongside CPR, MVR, and insurance refreshes.

> **For setup:** This file is the markdown source for a Google Form. Each section becomes a required checkbox in the Form. The form captures typed full legal name, date, IP, timestamp. Federal E-SIGN Act and Louisiana UETA accept this as a valid electronic signature. Form responses route to `NKST Guide Acknowledgements YYYY` Sheet, and a copy lives in `Guides/[Name]/acknowledgement_[YYYY].pdf`.

## Contractor Operating Notes

### Initial Onboarding

1. Contractor creates a Guide folder: `Guides/[FirstName_LastName]/`
2. Guide receives:
   - Link to the Google Form acknowledgement
   - Link to the Google Form quiz
   - PDF copy of every document referenced above
3. Guide reads everything, takes the quiz (must pass 80%), then submits the acknowledgement form.
4. Contractor downloads the acknowledgement response (PDF export from Forms or print to PDF) and saves to the Guide folder as `acknowledgement_[YYYY].pdf`.
5. Contractor updates the Guide Roster & Compliance Tracker.

### Annual Renewal (every January)

1. GHL workflow triggers on January 1 (or first business day):
   - Email + SMS to all active guides: "Annual acknowledgement and quiz due by January 31. [Link]"
2. Reminders go out weekly through the month.
3. Any guide who hasn't completed by January 31 is paused from scheduling until done.
4. New year's acknowledgement is filed in the Guide folder; old year's stays in the folder forever (retain 7 years minimum).

### Document Update Triggers

If a major policy changes mid-year (Handbook revision, new SOP, new harassment policy version), trigger an out-of-cycle re-acknowledgement specifically for that document. Don't redo the whole annual unless multiple things change.

---

## Why Annual

- LDWF guide permits expire December 31, so the renewal cycle naturally aligns
- CPR certs are typically 2-year, MVR re-checks fit easily
- Insurance COIs are usually annual
- Any policy changes from the past year get re-acknowledged
- Plaintiff's lawyer asking "what did the guide sign that's relevant to this incident on June 14, 2026?" gets pointed at the January 2026 signed acknowledgement

---

## Audit Trail

For each signed acknowledgement, retain the following for 7 years:
- Google Form response (PDF export)
- IP and timestamp from Forms metadata
- Email confirmation receipt
- The full set of policy documents as they existed at the time of signing (date-stamped versions in `Archive/Policies/[YYYY]/`)

The last point is critical: if a policy is updated mid-year, archive the version that was in effect at signing, so we can prove what the guide actually agreed to.
