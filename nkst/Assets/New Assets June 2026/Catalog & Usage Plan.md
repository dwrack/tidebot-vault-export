## YouTube plan

NKST's channel leans on Shorts for discovery (search skews swamp / bridge / fishing). These three clips are all Shorts format already.

**Ship as Shorts (fastest win):**
1. **Gator Short** — IMG_9898. Hook text on frame 1, gator as payoff. This is the highest-pull of the three. We already have a cold-reach concept (`01-gator-surfaces-3-feet-from-kayak`) this raw clip feeds directly.

3. **"To love a swamp" Short** — e08. Already captioned. Pin it as the brand statement piece.

One catch worth saying out loud: at 5-11s these are short even for Shorts. They'll perform better stitched. My recommendation is to build **one 25-40s Short** that opens on the gator reveal (IMG_9898), cuts to the anhinga + cypress (IMG_4466), and closes on the quote (e08) with a "book a Manchac tour" end card. Punchy hook, scenery middle, brand close. Then also post the gator clip standalone since it stands on its own.

**Long-form:** Honestly, not enough footage here for a real long-form video yet. These are three short vertical clips, wrong orientation and length for a 5-10 min horizontal piece. If we want long-form, we need a guide to shoot 20-30 min of horizontal B-roll on a tour (paddling, gator, guide narration, launch + rope swing). The photos can fill a long-form as Ken-Burns stills, but they can't carry it alone. I'd hold long-form until we have purpose-shot footage.

**Photo-based Shorts:** The 5 photos can also become a fast slideshow Short ("5 things you'll see on a NOLA swamp tour") with the gator photo (#5) as the thumbnail. Cheap to make, good for filling the calendar.
