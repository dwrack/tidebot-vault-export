## The idea in one line
The neighbors who actually live in Manchac have opinions about you: a heron, two gators, a crawfish, an old snapping turtle and an owl. They react to our swamp footage, hype the New Orleans spots worth the drive, and keep reminding everyone the swamp is wild, never baited, never staged.

**Voices**
- **Poppy picks her character first.** Junior, Lil Pinch or Cookie fit a kid's voice best, but it's her call. The other kids can pick next.
- **ElevenLabs** voices whoever the kids don't take, one fixed voice per character. It's allowed only for these cartoon characters, never for ads or anything posing as a real person.
- **Kids' recordings:** a quiet room, a phone mic and 5 minutes per episode, recorded in batches of 6 to 8 lines. No faces, no names, and no cloning of the kids' voices. The raw files stay local.

**Character rules**
- **Captions carry every joke,** so the post works with the sound off.
- The characters **never assure safety.** They say "wild, never baited, never staged." Wildlife is "common, not guaranteed," and Earl can just not show up.
- Airboats are fair game as a *category* (noise, wake). Never name a company.
- **No bats** (they belong to Austin) and **no party content**. NPB crossovers are two brands visiting each other, not one brand.
- Size claim (David, 9/26): Manchac is "the second-largest bald cypress swamp in the US." Never "largest in the world."
- **Overlays on other businesses' content:** only with that business's OK, done as an IG Collab or their clip plus a tag. Reposts without permission hurt reach and borrow content we don't own.

## Where it posts
