## The idea in one line
The neighbors who actually live in Manchac have opinions about you: a heron, two gators, a crawfish, an old snapping turtle and an owl. They react to our swamp footage, hype the New Orleans spots worth the drive, and keep reminding everyone the swamp is wild, never baited, never staged.

## Cross-brand storyline: the snowbird heron (DCKT x NKST)
*From the DCKT mascot session, David's decision 9/26. Both names are still his to confirm.*

- **The story:** DCKT has its own great blue heron, a snowbird. Every fall it leaves Door County (real behavior: great blue herons do migrate toward the Gulf Coast), flies to New Orleans, and meets Beaux Bleu, who shows it the swamp. In March it flies home and kicks off DCKT's season countdown. In summer, Beaux visits Door County to return the favor.
- **The pairing (David, 9/27): zen vs flashy.** "Doris," the DCKT heron (name PROPOSED by the DCKT thread, David hasn't confirmed it), is a calm, zen Midwesterner who stands still for an hour and calls that a good day. Beaux Bleu is the flashy New Orleanian who can't stand still, plays sax, and wants to show her every second line. She slows him down; he gets her dancing.
- **Joint posts** (IG Collabs between the NKST side account and DCKT):

| When | Post |
|---|---|
| Late Oct / early Nov | **Arrival:** the DCKT heron lands at Manchac. Beaux: "Doris! You're early, baby, the band's not ready." Cast-reveal adjacent. |
| Nov | First paddle: the visitor sees Spanish moss and Grandpa Earl for the first time (Earl doesn't move). |
| Dec | **Holiday visit:** the levee bonfires, and the visitor tries gumbo with Lil Pinch. |
| Feb 9 | Mardi Gras: the visitor in beads, overwhelmed. Can fold into the Junior x Alfred crossover. |
| Early Mar | **Send-off:** Beaux plays her a slow sax solo off the dock, pretending he's not sad. This hands off to DCKT's countdown. |
| Jul | **Beaux visits Door County,** overdressed and freezing in the cold water. It's DCKT's post, with NKST as the Collab. |

- **Rule:** each brand only describes its own tours. The DCKT heron posts show NKST tours as NKST's, and Beaux's Door County trip shows DCKT tours as DCKT's. No bundled offers, and no bats.

**Voices**
- **Poppy picks her character first.** Junior or Cookie fit a kid's voice best, but it's her call. **Not Lil Pinch:** he's shared with NPB's Swamp Judges page (21+ party clips), where kids are off the page, so Lil Pinch is ElevenLabs on both pages. The other kids can pick next.
- **ElevenLabs** voices whoever the kids don't take, one fixed voice per character. It's allowed only for these cartoon characters, never for ads or anything posing as a real person.
- **Kids' recordings:** a quiet room, a phone mic and 5 minutes per episode, recorded in batches of 6 to 8 lines. No faces, no names, and no cloning of the kids' voices. The raw files stay local.

**Character rules**
- **Captions carry every joke,** so the post works with the sound off.
- The characters **never assure safety.** They say "wild, never baited, never staged." Wildlife is "common, not guaranteed," and Earl can just not show up.
- Airboats are fair game as a *category* (noise, wake). Never name a company.
- **No bats** (they belong to Austin) and **no party content**. NPB crossovers are two brands visiting each other, not one brand.
- Size claim (David, 9/26): Manchac is "the second-largest bald cypress swamp in the US." Never "largest in the world."
- **Overlays on other businesses' content:** only with that business's OK, done as an IG Collab or their clip plus a tag. Reposts without permission hurt reach and borrow content we don't own.
