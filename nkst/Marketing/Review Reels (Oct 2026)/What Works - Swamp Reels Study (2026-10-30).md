# What Works: Swamp Reels Study (2026-10-30)

Research only. Nothing posted, no queue edits, no launchd changes. Data pulled 2026-10-30 with the NKST Apify account (3 Actor runs, est. under $0.70, see Caveats). "Measured" = number or caption text pulled from Instagram. "Inferred" = my read of what the video shows, based on caption, length and audio flag. I did not watch the videos.

## 1. Bottom line

- **Personality beats proof, by a lot.** Our three review-quote reels got 249, 182 and 93 plays on 5,314 followers (0.05x to 0.01x followers). Our own best reels (4,744 to 11,331 plays) are a joke or an identity line with no CTA.
- **The winners in this category are a human or an animal doing something, with a caption of 3 to 12 words.** Moondance Kayak's top reel (78,104 plays on 2,882 followers, 27x) is a 14s clip captioned "Don't ask us about Chuckanut sandstone." Cajun Encounters' all-time hit (2.3M plays) is "They'll learn one day!" over an 11s raccoon clip (inferred gag).
- **Promo and "book now" posts sit at the bottom everywhere.** Cajun Encounters bus-tour and "Book your swamp tour today" posts: 541 to 1,000 plays on 12.7k followers. Pictured Rocks "filling fast" and 20% off posts: 535 to 818 plays. Louisiana Tour Company merch and family-pitch posts: 150 to 300 on 123 followers.
- **Local swamp competition is weak, so the gap is ours to take.** Cajun Encounters median is 1,920 plays on 12.7k followers (0.15x). Cajun Pride has not posted a reel since 2020. Louisiana Tour Company is dormant since Jul 2025. Zip NOLA (zipline, not a swamp tour) is the only other local account winning, and it wins on guides being goofy.
- **Review content is not dead, the format is.** Moondance's review post "She still had to clean boats all morning but nothing beats the feeling of a 5 star review" did 10,252 plays (371 likes, 17 comments). It is a person-first story about the review, not a quote card with a stock track.

**Length:** Winners cluster at 8 to 15s (Moondance, Zip NOLA, Cajun Encounters). **Caption:** Humor winners use 3 to 12 words, 0 to 5 hashtags. Information winners (our 3/21) can run long if the first line is the identity statement. Nobody's top reel opens with "Book your...".

## 5. Learning-loop spec

**Log weekly, per post** (Graph API insights, 48h+ old posts only): reach, views (plays), likes, comments, saves, shares, profile visits, website taps, avg watch time. Derived: plays-to-reach ratio (plays / reach; above 1.3 means people are rewatching), share+save rate = (shares + saves) / reach, reach vs baseline = reach / account median reach.

**Baseline:** median reach of the last 10 posts before the test (currently 1,892 per Performance.md). Re-compute every 4 weeks.

**Promote a format** (give it 2 weekly slots) when, after 3 posts: median reach >= 1.5x baseline AND share+save rate >= 1.5%, or any single post >= 3x baseline.
**Hold** (keep 1 slot, change hook) when median reach is 0.75x to 1.5x baseline.
**Retire** when, after 4 posts, median reach < 0.75x baseline AND share+save rate < 0.5%. Keep the concept in the idea bank, drop it from the calendar.
**Always:** no format is judged on one post; no format is retired in its first 3 posts. Anything >= 3x baseline goes on the boost list (paid + organic single loop).
**Second-order check:** a post that gets reach but 0 saves and 0 shares (Admire NOLA "log" reel pattern) counts as a hook win and a conversion loss, so tag it "reach only".

**Performance.md table columns (exact):**

`Post date | Reel ID | Format | Hook type | Subject | Length s | Audio (original/library/trending) | Caption words | CTA (none/soft/book) | Posted time CT | Reach | Views | Plays-to-reach | Likes | Comments | Saves | Shares | Share+save % | Reach vs baseline | Avg watch s | Web taps | Verdict (promote/hold/retire/reach-only)`

## 6. Caveats and unverified

- **I did not watch any video.** Hook, subject and "gag" descriptions are inferred from captions, duration and the audio flag. Quote any of them as "likely" until someone watches the top 5.
- **Plays are not reach.** Competitor numbers are plays (include replays). Ours in Performance.md are Graph API reach. For 3/21 the scraper shows 11,331 plays while the brief you gave me says "400 views-metric", so the two sources disagree by a lot for that post. Treat cross-account ratios as rough.
- **Shares and saves are not available** for the account pulls (shares add-on was off to save cost; saves are never public). Only the hashtag pull exposed shares.
- **The hashtag pull is not a random sample.** It returned 60 reels across 2022 to 2026 that skew toward big winners, and mixes swamp with general kayak content. Use it for patterns, not benchmarks.
- **Small samples.** 6 to 23 reels per account, one 90-day to 12-month window each. Medians have wide error bars.
- **Apify cost not read from billing.** Est.: reel scraper 149 items at about $0.0026 plus start fee = about $0.39; profile scraper 7 profiles about $0.02; hashtag scraper 60 items, price not checked, probably under $0.30. Total under $0.70, 3 of 4 allowed runs used.
- **Zip NOLA and Moondance are not swamp-tour peers.** They are used for format lessons (guide humor, tourist POV), not as local competitors.
- **Posting-time advice rests on about 10 data points.** Test it, do not trust it.
- Handles `wildlouisiana`, `honeyislandswamptour`, `swampadventuretours` returned not_found; Manchac and Honey Island operators other than Cajun Encounters are not covered.
