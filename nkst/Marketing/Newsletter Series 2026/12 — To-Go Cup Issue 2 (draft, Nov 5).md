## 1. The pour

November is the month the city stops sweating and starts eating. Every weekend from here to Thanksgiving has a free festival downtown, the light goes gold by 4, and out in the swamp the birds take over from the gators. If you were waiting for the right week, you can stop waiting.

## 4. The thing only locals know

**The Christmas Eve bonfires are closer than you think.** On Dec 24 the River Parishes light log pyramids along the Mississippi levee in Gramercy, Lutcher and Paulina, about 40 miles upriver, to guide Papa Noel. Our Manchac launch is in LaPlace, one parish downriver of there, so if you are driving yourself to a December paddle you are already most of the way. Plan it now; the levee road fills by late afternoon on the 24th. [VERIFY 2026 parish details and any ticketed bonfire events, e.g. the Dec 12 Cajun bonfire dinner]

## 5. Swamp report

[FILL from guide Pre/Post-Tour Slack logs, week of Oct 26.]

What changes in November: once mornings drop under about 65 F the alligators go quiet, so a 9 am paddle is bird season and a warm 2 pm is still gator season. The hyacinth thins out, which opens channels we cannot get into in summer. The Extended 4-hour is the trip for this; the bare cypress and the migratory birds are the reason winter paddlers come back.
