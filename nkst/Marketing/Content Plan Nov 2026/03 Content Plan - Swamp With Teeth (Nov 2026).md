Inputs: 00 Asset Inventory, 01 Hot Topics Research, 02 Format Research (this folder) and Review Reels (Oct 2026)/What Works study + Performance.md.

## Why we are changing course
- Review-quote reels reached 76 to 161 accounts each. Our personality reels reached 1,900 to 8,900. The snake reel alone did 10,000 plays. Proof posts do not travel; a person, an animal or an opinion does.
- Local swamp accounts are weak (the biggest one posts at 0.15x its follower count). Nobody in the space is saying anything with a point of view. That is the open lane.
- We have 6,300 photos and 580 videos but almost nothing people argue about. The argument is the missing ingredient, not the footage.

## The idea in one line
A swamp tour company that talks like a local who actually lives out there: funny, specific, and willing to say the quiet part about canals, trash, beads, airboats and marshmallows.

## Four lanes, one post each per week (4 posts a week)

| Lane | Day | Job | Comment engine |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Pick a side | Mon | A defensible hot take on a local issue, over our own footage | People arguing with us and each other |
| 2. Settle it | Wed | Local identity bait: swamp vs bayou, thin vs thick catfish, estuary vs lake | Locals correcting each other |
| 3. Resident of the week | Fri | Deadpan wildlife intro, size guess, name-this-bird, frog audio | Guesses, IDs, "I saw one bigger" |
| 4. Guest and guide | Sat/Sun | Guest photo reshare with credit, guide one-liner, caption-this, comment-reply reel | Tags, friends, reposts |

Lane 1 is the edge. Lanes 2 to 4 keep it from reading as a cause account. If a Lane 1 post blows up, the next three posts are Lanes 2 to 4, never two fights in a row.

## Lane 1: Pick a side (the edgy lane)
Rules: public facts with a source in the caption or first comment, no company or person named, no "the swamp is doomed", always end with a question. We reply to every comment in the first hour and pin the best disagreement. Each take below has a sourced number behind it in 01 Hot Topics Research.

Ranked by how likely locals are to argue:
1. Canals. "Every straight line out here was cut by people. Nature does not draw with a ruler." 35,163 canals counted across 14 parishes. Asset: 2018 drone top-down (DJI_0117/0131) over a straight canal, slow push.
2. Trash from trucks. "The trash out here did not float in from tourists. It flew out a window on I-55." Asset (checked 2026-10-31): two 2025 cleanup-crew photos, `2025 Wildlife & Guests/1000015392.jpg` (group with orange bags, ~20 faces) and `1000015395.jpg` (guide selfie, crew hauling bags behind, marina). Both are "we pick it up" proof, not trash-in-the-swamp; shot at a marina cleanup, not our launch. Faces are volunteers, so clear rights before posting. `Photos/cleanupphoto.jpg` is a 2019 promo graphic (800px, old phone number), not usable. No kayak-level litter shot exists. Needs a 20-second guide phone clip of trash at the launch (see Shoot list).
3. Mardi Gras beads. "46 tons of beads came out of five blocks of St. Charles. Some of it ends up here." 93,000 lb pulled from catch basins (2018). Asset: cypress-knee water-line shot, text card.
4. The diversion that got cancelled. "Cancelling the big diversion was the right call, and the swamp you are paddling still needs a river." Mid-Barataria terminated July 17, 2025. Asset: ghost cypress stills.
5. Marshmallows. "If a gator comes to your boat for a marshmallow, a tour taught it to. We do not." Asset: 01-gator-surfaces-3-feet-from-kayak.mp4 (7s, ready).
6. Airboats. "You cannot hear a swamp from an airboat. You can only hear the airboat." Asset: silent paddling clip with the only sound being paddle drips, then Frogs at Night audio.
7. Second-growth trees. "Nearly every tree you paddle past is a replacement. The originals built the country." Last virgin cypress cut 1957. Asset: old-stump still next to a 2026 cypress, then-and-now wipe.
8. Nutria. "Nutria are not cute. A $6 tail bounty is fair." 235,220 taken this season. Asset: none; needs a guide nutria clip.
9. Storm memory. "Which storm do you still measure everything by?" Ike put 10 ft of surge through Pass Manchac. Asset: overcast winter stills, water-line shots.
10. Ghost forest. "A ghost forest is the best tour stop we have. Ugly truth sells." Asset: dead-cypress stills from the 2018 drone set.

Do not touch (from the research): naming any operator, restaurant, owner or politician; saying a named competitor feeds gators; Julia Brown / Frenier as a joke; I-55 fog crashes; the Chevron verdict as settled (it is contested after the April 2026 Supreme Court ruling); doom with no hope.

## Lane 2: Settle it (local identity)
- "It is a swamp, not a bayou, and the people correcting you are also wrong." (Explain all three in the comments.)
- "Lake Pontchartrain is not a lake. It is a salty estuary with a good publicist."
- "Thin or thick catfish at the old Manchac spot, and why are you right?" (Do not name the restaurant in the post; everyone will name it in the comments, which is the point.)
- "Tell me you are from Tangipahoa without telling me you are from Tangipahoa."
- "Rougarou, swamp monster, or a very big gator. Which are you betting on?"
Format: 3 to 5 second photo flip with the line as big on-screen text, trending low-key sound. Lowest effort in the plan; cut these first.

## Gaps and risks
- No oil or industry footage, no night, no snake video, no nutria, no 2025-26 drone. Lane 1 starts on canals, beads, marshmallows and trees, which we can cut today.
- Several facts carry open verification flags in 01 Hot Topics Research (litter cost figure, airboat dB from a Virginia study, Chevron verdict status). Captions use only the numbers marked verified or cite the source in the first comment.
- Edgy posts pull in people who disagree. Tone rule: we state it plainly and let them argue; we never pile on a commenter. Pin the best disagreement, not the dumbest.
- One Slack-pull photo (kayak-guide-with-anhinga-bird) looks AI-generated or filtered. Do not post it.
