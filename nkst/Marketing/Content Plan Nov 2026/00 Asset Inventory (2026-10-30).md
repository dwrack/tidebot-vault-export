- Vault root for every path below: `~/Library/Mobile Documents/iCloud~md~obsidian/Documents/`. Paths start at the brand folder.

- Dates: for GBP and Slack files the date is the real post/upload date. For vault files it is the file modified date, which is often an import date (2015-12-31 is a camera with a dead clock; 2026-xx on old footage is the day it was filed). Treat vault dates as "when it landed", not "when shot", unless the filename says otherwise.
- Descriptions are from filename, folder, existing catalogs in the vault, or (15 images marked VIEWED) looking at the image. Videos were NOT viewed. "(unverified)" means filename-only guess.
- Tags used: gator, bird, snake, cypress, moss, sunset, guide-on-camera, guests, drone, dock/launch, night, frog-audio, trash/litter, industry/oil, water-level, weather.
- Rights flags: **GBP-customer** = uploaded by customers or reviewers to the Google listing, so ask permission before reposting (house rule: photo permission). **Social-pull** = downloaded from TikTok/IG/FB, rights unverified, do not post.
