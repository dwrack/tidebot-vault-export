## The 5 topics

1. **Kayak vs airboat** — the whole positioning, and the single highest-intent comparison search we own
2. **The alligators** — what's real, what's baited, what we won't promise
3. **The swamp itself** — cypress, ecology, why it's disappearing, why silence is the product
4. **Trip logistics from the Quarter** — 30 minutes out, what to wear, what to expect
5. **The eerie angle** — night, fog, cypress at dusk, the ghost-story-adjacent search demand

## The 3 formats

- **Comparison** (easy) — us vs the airboat, directly
- **Myth-bust** (easy) — the honesty position is a content engine
- **Cinematic, no talking** (ambitious) — silence is literally the product, so sell it that way

## The 2 layouts

- **A — Talking head, guide on the water.** Guides are the brand. Their face is the asset.
- **B — Silent, text on screen over cypress b-roll.** Assume sound off. Big weathered type.

## Cover type

Weathered serif, slightly gothic, moss green + bone. 3-6 words.

## Notes

- The honesty position ("common, not guaranteed") is the strongest content asset this brand has and it's currently invisible on social. Rows 9 and 10 should go out first.
- The eerie angle is deliberate SEO, per the NOLA haunted/ghost search demand. Row 27 keeps it honest: lean into the atmosphere, don't claim a ghost tour we don't run.
- Row 8 names a competitor practice. Keep it factual and unnamed. Describe the behavior, never the business.
- Boost whatever gets the most **saves** as a social-proof ad.
