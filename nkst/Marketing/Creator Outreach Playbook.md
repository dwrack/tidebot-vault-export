# Creator Outreach Playbook: New Orleans Kayak Swamp Tours

**Owner:** Dylan LP
**Business Instagram:** @neworleanskayakswamptours (or current handle)

---

## What This Is

Proactive outreach to local content creators, offering them a free kayak swamp tour in exchange for nothing. No contract, no posting requirements, no talking points. If they make something cool, great. If they just enjoy the paddle, that's fine too.

This is different from the UGC Repost Playbook (which is reactive, finding content that already exists). This is about building relationships with creators before they've ever been in your kayak.

## Why This Works for NKST

We sell the experience creators want to film. A kayak tour through Manchac Swamp, no motors, real wildlife, cypress canopy, silence broken only by birds and paddles hitting water. That's content. We're not asking anyone to hold up a product and smile. We're inviting them into something genuinely worth capturing.

The anti-motorboat positioning matters here: our tours feel different from the airboat crowd, and that difference is immediately visible on camera. Quiet, intimate, natural. Creators who care about aesthetics gravitate toward this.

The cost is nearly zero. An empty seat on a tour that's going out anyway costs us nothing. The content a creator produces from that seat would cost $500-2,000 to produce ourselves.

## Who to Target

### The Sweet Spot: Micro-Influencers (2K-50K followers)

Don't chase big accounts. You want local creators who:
- Live in or frequently visit New Orleans
- Post outdoor, food, culture, travel, or nature content
- Have real engagement (comments from real people, not bot spam)
- Show original storytelling, not just lip-syncs and trending audio reposts
- Would genuinely enjoy a quiet morning on the swamp

### Creator Types to Prioritize

| Type | Why | Example |
|------|-----|---------|
| Outdoor/nature creators | Direct alignment with our product | Hikers, paddlers, nature photographers, birders |
| Food/culture creators | Their audience is already visiting NOLA | Restaurant reviewers, "hidden gems" accounts |

| Photography/videography creators | The swamp is visually stunning, they'll capture it well | Landscape, wildlife, drone content |

## How to Reach Out

### DM from the Business Instagram Account

Not your personal account. Not email. A direct message from the business page.

### First DM: Cold Outreach

Keep it short. No pitch deck energy.

**Template A: Simple invite:**

> Hey [name]! Love your content, especially [reference a specific recent post]. We run kayak tours through Manchac Swamp here in New Orleans, small groups, no motors, real wildlife. We'd love to have you out on a tour. No strings, no posting requirements, just come enjoy it. Want us to set something up?

**Template B: For travel creators:**

> Hey [name]! Saw you're hitting New Orleans soon. We run kayak tours through the swamp outside the city, totally different from the airboat tourist stuff. Small groups, quiet water, alligators and herons. We'd love to have you out while you're here. Free, no catch. Let us know if you want us to hold a spot.

**Template C: For creators who posted about a competitor or similar activity:**

> Hey [name]! Saw your [post about airboat tour/similar activity]. We do something pretty different here: kayak tours, no motors, small groups, real wildlife up close without the engine noise. Would love to have you out to see the difference. On us, no strings. Interested?

### Rules for Outreach
- **Reference something specific** about their content. "Love your content" alone sounds automated.
- **Don't ask them to post.** The moment you say "in exchange for a post," it becomes transactional and the content suffers.
- **One follow-up max.** If they don't reply after 3-5 days, send one casual follow-up. After that, move on.

**Follow-up DM (if no reply after 5 days):**

> Hey just bumping this in case it got buried. No pressure either way!

### If They Say Yes

> Awesome! When works for you? We run tours [days/times]. Pick whatever works and I'll hold a spot. You can bring a friend too if you want. Morning tours have the best wildlife if you care about that.

Then:
- Hold the spot, confirm the day before
- Let the guide know a creator is coming (not to treat them differently, just so they're aware)
- After the experience, send a quick thank you DM: "Hope you had a good time! No ask from us, just glad you came out."

### If They Post Something

- Like it immediately
- Leave a genuine comment (something specific about their content, not "Thanks for sharing!")
- Repost using the UGC Playbook process (ask permission, download, repost with tag)
- Flag top performers for ads per the UGC playbook
- Add them to your "relationship" tier for future invites

### If They Don't Post

That's fine. Don't follow up asking where their post is. The relationship still exists. They had a good experience, they'll mention you organically at some point, and you can invite them back next season.

## The "Access as Currency" Playbook

| What you have | What creators want | How to offer it |
|---------------|-------------------|-----------------|
| The swamp itself (cypress, wildlife, silence) | Backdrops nobody else has | "Want to come out on a sunrise paddle before tours start?" |
| Wildlife encounters (gators, herons, owls) | Unscripted moments that go viral | Morning tours have the best wildlife, steer them there |
| Behind-the-scenes access | Content that feels exclusive | "Want to ride along on a guide training day?" |
| Golden hour timing | Beautiful light for content | Recommend the right tour time for their content style |
| Group experiences | Content with friends performs better | "Bring 3-4 friends, we'll set you up" |

### Relationship Tiers

- **One-timers**: Came, enjoyed it, maybe posted, no ongoing relationship. That's fine.
- **Repeat creators**: Loved it, posted, want to come back. Invite them quarterly or for special events (new tour launch, seasonal kickoff).
- **Brand friends**: Genuinely love what you do, mention you unprompted, bring friends. These are gold. Give them first access to everything.
