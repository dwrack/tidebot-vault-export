## FAQ SCHEMA

**Q1:**
```
How much does a swamp tour cost in New Orleans?
```
**A1:**
```
Most guided swamp tours near New Orleans run $50 to $90 per person for a 1 to 2.5 hour trip. A 2-hour guided kayak tour with New Orleans Kayak Swamp Tours is $65 per person, with a 4-hour extended version at $130. Add $25 per person round trip if you need pickup from the city instead of driving to the launch site yourself.
```

**Q2:**
```
Is transportation included in the price of a swamp tour?
```
**A2:**
```
Usually not, and this is the most common price surprise. Most operators, including us, charge the tour price separately from the shuttle. Expect to add roughly $25 to $30 per person round trip if you're being picked up from a hotel or the French Quarter instead of driving yourself to the launch site.
```

**Q3:**
```
Why do some swamp tours cost twice as much as others?
```
**A3:**
```
Group size, boat type, and what's included. A 30-person airboat running a 45-minute loop can undercut a small-group kayak tour on price per head because it moves more people per trip. Combo tours that add a plantation stop, private tours, and longer trips (3 to 4 hours) all cost more because you're paying for more guide time and a smaller group, not just a longer route.
```

**Q4:**
```
Are New Orleans swamp tours worth the money?
```
**A4:**
```
For a guided, small-group tour with a trained naturalist, most guests say yes based on the reviews we see, particularly compared to a rushed 45-minute loop through the same water. Free self-guided options exist, but you won't get a guide, transportation, or gear included.
```

## CONTENT (Markdown body)

```markdown
We get this question in about half our phone calls: "so what does this actually cost?" Fair question. Swamp tour pricing around New Orleans is all over the place, $35 quickie loops up to $200+ private trips, and the website menus don't always make it obvious why. New Orleans Kayak Swamp Tours quotes this all day, so here's the real breakdown.

## The Short Answer

Expect **$50 to $90 per person** for a standard guided swamp tour near New Orleans, kayak or motorized, running 1 to 2.5 hours. Our 2-hour Manchac Swamp Wildlife Tour is **$65 per person**. If you want more time on the water, the Extended Manchac Tour runs 4 hours at **$130 per person**. That's the range. Everything below explains why a given tour lands where it does in it.

## What Drives the Price Up or Down

Three things move the number more than anything else.

**Group size.** A 25-person airboat running a fast loop can charge less per head because it's spreading the cost of the boat, fuel, and captain across more people. Our kayak tours cap at 12 guests, which means the guide's time is split fewer ways and priced accordingly.

**Trip length.** A 45-minute airboat loop and a 4-hour kayak tour aren't the same product even if they're both called "swamp tour." More time on the water means more guide time, more distance covered, and usually more wildlife and ecology covered too.

**What's included.** Life jackets, kayaks, safety briefing, and a trained naturalist guide are baked into our price. Some cheaper tours charge extra for gear or run with less-trained staff. Read the fine print before you book on price alone.

## Our Actual Pricing (No Surprises)

Here's exactly what we charge, current as of this season:

| Tour | Price | Length |
|---|---|---|
| Manchac Swamp Wildlife Tour | $65/person | 2 hours |
| Extended Manchac Tour | $130/person | 4 hours |
| Whitney Plantation + Swamp Combo | $195/person | Full day |
| Honey Island Tour (summer only) | $65/person | 2 hours |

Add $25 per person round trip if you want pickup from a hotel or the French Quarter. Drive yourself to the launch site in LaPlace and you save that $25.

## The Transportation Add-On Nobody Mentions Upfront

This is the number one thing that surprises guests, so we're putting it front and center instead of burying it. **Transportation is not included in the base tour price**, on our tours or most others in the area. It's a separate $25 per person round trip.

Why? Because the launch site is about 35 minutes outside the French Quarter, out in the Maurepas Swamp Wildlife Management Area, and running a shuttle van costs money on top of the guide and the boat. If you've got a rental car and don't mind the drive, skip the add-on entirely, drive yourself, and pocket the difference.

**One exception:** TripAdvisor bookings include transportation in the listed price already. Worth knowing before you comparison shop across platforms.

## Combo Tours and Why They Cost More

Our Whitney Plantation + Swamp Combo runs $195 per person, more than double a standard tour. That's not a markup for the sake of it. You're getting a guided plantation tour, transportation both ways, and the full swamp kayak trip, all in one day, with a guide dedicated to walking you through some genuinely heavy Louisiana history at Whitney before the swamp portion. Book each piece separately and you'd likely spend more, not less.

## Is It Worth It, or Should You Do It Free

You can absolutely see a Louisiana swamp for free. We wrote [internal link: an entire guide to doing exactly that → /3-free-swamp-walks-in-and-around-new-orleans/], and we stand behind it, boardwalks and public land access are real and worth doing.

What you don't get for free: a guide who can tell a cypress knee from a tupelo stump at a glance, someone trained in CPR and swamp navigation who knows where the gators actually hang out that week, and the safety net of a professional operation if something goes sideways. A guest last spring told our guide she'd tried the self-guided boardwalk route the day before and "saw a sign about herons, not a single heron." That's the gap a guide closes.

## What to Budget for a Group

Quick math for planning purposes, using our own pricing as the example:

- **Couple, standard tour, no transportation:** $130
- **Couple, standard tour, with pickup:** $180
- **Family of 4, standard tour, with pickup:** $360
- **Group of 6, extended 4-hour tour, with pickup:** $930
- **Solo traveler, standard tour, driving self:** $65

We could run larger groups through faster and cheaper. We don't, because 12 guests is the cap that keeps the tour actually feeling like a tour and not a shuttle bus with paddles.
