## The legend (told straight, played light)
Since the 1960s, people in the Honey Island swamp have reported a creature, seven feet tall, gray hair, yellow eyes, walks on two legs, smells like death. Two trappers even claimed a plaster cast of a strange three-toed track. Locals call it the Honey Island Swamp Monster, or the Louisiana Bigfoot. Honey Island is one of the least-altered river swamps in the country, thousands of acres most people never set foot in. Which is exactly why the story has legs.

## The recurring bit
A short, tongue-in-cheek but grounded segment on Honey Island tour footage:
- The legend told honestly as legend.
- The "evidence" played for fun (a weird sound at dusk, a shape in the trees, a track in the mud that is definitely a wild boar... probably).
- The real wildlife doing the actual spooking, wild hogs crashing through, barred owls calling, the dark coming down early under the canopy.
- Hank the Heron as the deadpan skeptic: "It's a boar. It's always a boar. ...probably a boar."
