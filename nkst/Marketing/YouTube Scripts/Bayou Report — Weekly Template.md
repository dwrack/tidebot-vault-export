# The Bayou Report — Weekly Template

*The recurring host segment (Weatherman archetype, naturalist version). 60 to 90 seconds, one guide, one phone, shot at the launch or from a kayak. Drives this-weekend bookings. Ship one every week.*

**Booking link:** /tours/swamp-kayak-tours/?utm_source=youtube&utm_medium=video&utm_campaign=bayou-report
**Cadence:** weekly, ideally Wednesday or Thursday so it lands before the weekend.

---

### The fixed structure (so it feels like a show)

**Open (same every week):**
> Hey, it's [Guide] with New Orleans Kayak Swamp Tours, and this is your Bayou Report.

**1. Conditions (10–15 sec).** Water level, water temp, weather for the weekend. Plain and honest.
> Water's up a few inches after the rain, temp's sitting around [X], and the weekend's looking [clear / warm / breezy].

**2. What's moving (20–30 sec).** The wildlife update. This is the heart of it.
> Gators are [out sunning on the warm afternoons / still slow, it's early]. We've been seeing [herons nesting / a lot of turtles / an osprey working the channel]. The [cypress is greening up / spider lilies are blooming / migration's bringing new birds through].

**3. Swamp fact of the week (10–15 sec).** One small piece of real natural history. Hank the Heron can deliver this as a voice-over bit on alternating weeks.
> Quick one: [a cypress knee isn't a root, nobody's totally sure what it's for / tannins from the leaves are what make the water look like tea / a great blue heron will stand still for twenty minutes waiting on one fish].

**4. The nudge (10 sec).** Tie conditions to the booking.
> So if you want to get out here, this weekend's a good one. We paddle small groups, no motors, 30 minutes from the Quarter. Link's in the description.

**Close (same every week):**
> That's the Bayou Report. See you on the water.

---

### Rules
- Real conditions only. If the gators are slow, say the gators are slow. "Common, not guaranteed" is the brand, and people trust it.
- Keep it to one guide on camera so the audience builds a relationship. Rotate guides across a month if you want a bench, but each report is one face.
- No hype words, no exclamation-mark energy. Warm, dry, local.
- Hashtags: #SwampTour #NewOrleans #BayouReport
- Title format: `Bayou Report: [Month] [Week] — [the one most interesting thing]` e.g. "Bayou Report: Late June — Gators Are Out and the Lilies Are Blooming"

### Why it works
Every episode names the place and the season (search), gives a reason to come back (subscribe), and points at this weekend (booking). It is the cheapest piece of the whole cast and the one that compounds fastest.
