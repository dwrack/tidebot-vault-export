# Hank the Heron — Character Bible & Voice-Over Bits

*The NKST mascot. A great blue heron with a dry, patient, watchful personality. The naturalist counterweight to NPB's Alfred the gator. Hank is the channel's narrator and recurring wink. Use him as VO on existing Shorts before he ever gets his own episode.*

---

## Who Hank is
- A great blue heron who has lived on this bayou his whole life and has seen everything.
- Deadpan. Patient. Mildly unimpressed by humans, fully unbothered by gators.
- Prefers the silent kayaks because they do not scare off lunch. Has strong opinions about airboats (negative).
- Not a party animal. He is the quiet one. That is the whole point, he matches NKST's tone, not NPB's.
- Catchphrase energy, never forced: "...anyway." / "I've been standing here since Tuesday." / "Mm." (the heron equivalent of a shrug.)

## How he talks (sample VO lines)
On a guest's paddling:
> Look at this. Both hands. Splashing. Every fish in the parish knows you're coming. ...I respect the effort.

On a gator sighting:
> Everybody's screaming about the gator. The gator could not care less about you. The gator is thinking about a fish. Same as me.

On the silence:
> No motor. No speaker. Just water. This is the good stuff. You people pay extra for a quiet room, I get it for free.

On the cypress:
> These trees were here before the city. They'll be here after. Probably. If we're careful. Anyway.

On winter:
> Everybody thinks summer's the season. Amateurs. Cool morning, low sun, fish moving, nobody out here but me and the brave ones. Come in February. Trust the bird.

## How to deploy him
1. **VO on existing Shorts** (cheapest): drop Hank's voice over wildlife and paddle clips already on the channel. No new footage needed.
2. **"Hank rates your paddle form"** recurring Short: guest footage + Hank's deadpan score.
3. **Swamp fact of the week**: Hank delivers it on alternating Bayou Reports.
4. **Hank vs. Alfred** (crossover with NPB): the heron who wants peace vs. the party gator one swamp over. Recurring, links the two channels, both ride each other's search.

## Hard rules
- Hank is dry, not mean. He likes the guests, he just won't admit it.
- He never oversells the tour. If anything he undersells it, which is funnier and on-brand.
- Keep him accurate to a real great blue heron (stands still, patient hunter, eats fish, common on every Manchac tour). The comedy comes from real heron behavior, not cartoon nonsense.
- He is the differentiator from NPB. Do not let him drift into Alfred's party-gator voice.
