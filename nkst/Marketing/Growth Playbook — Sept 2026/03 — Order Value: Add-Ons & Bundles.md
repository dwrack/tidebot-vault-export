## How to read the numbers

**What we actually know (FareHarbor, last 30 days to 2026-05-09):**
- 167 bookings, 392 guests, **2.3 guests per booking**, **$77.66 average per guest**.
- Manchac: 322 guests, $24,739, which is **$76.83 per guest**. At a $65 seat, that gap implies roughly **47% shuttle attach** ($11.83 ÷ $25). At a $69 seat (open question 1), it implies about **31%**. So the real shuttle attach rate sits somewhere between 30% and 50%.
- Processing fees ran **1.8%** of gross ($542 on $30,442). Use 1.8% as the fee drag on every add-on below.
- Whitney combo: **1 booking in 30 days.** The flagship bundle is barely selling. That's the biggest opportunity in this file.
- The 90-day forecast (2026-09-24) shows October at about 100 bookings, roughly 230 guests.

**Two illustrative scenarios** are used throughout. Neither is a forecast.
- **Scenario A, "a real spring month":** 400 guests, 175 bookings.
- **Scenario B, "the 1,000-guest month":** 1,000 guests, 435 bookings (at 2.3 guests per booking).

Attach rates are **assumptions**, shown as low / base / high. Replace them with real FareHarbor numbers after 60 days live.
