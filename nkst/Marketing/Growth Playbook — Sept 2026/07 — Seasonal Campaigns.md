## Ground rules for every campaign

- **Margin-safe offers only.** Use value-adds (a printed photo, a bird ID card, a cypress certificate, hand warmers, a schedule perk), not percentage discounts. The one exception is a small referral credit that's already specced ($10/$10, not yet built).
- **Honesty about wildlife, season by season.** Alligators are common at Manchac from spring through fall and go quiet below 65°F. Winter is a bird season. Honey Island is summer only, alligators are **rare** there, and water levels make it less consistent. Say "common, not guaranteed" every time wildlife appears in copy.
- **Voice.** No "guaranteed," "customers," "purchase," "extreme," or "adventure of a lifetime." Don't name competitors. Keep exclamation marks to a minimum. Contact persona in guest-facing copy: **Ray Fontaine**, (504) 571-9975.
- **Why fall matters most this year.** The 2026-09-24 FareHarbor forecast shows Oct–Dec at **~$47K, down 37% from the same window last year.** Fall wildlife, Halloween and holiday gift cards carry Q4. Put the most budget there.
- **Existing pages to use** (from the staged blog drafts' internal links): Manchac tour `/tours/swamp-kayak-tours/manchac-mystic-kayak-tour/`, Extended `/tours/swamp-kayak-tours/extended-manchac-kayak-tour/`, Whitney combo `/tours/swamp-kayak-plantation-tour/`, gift certificates `/gift-certificates/`, birding `/new-orleans-bird-watching-tours/`.
