## 2. Commission structure (ALL PROPOSED pending David)

### 2a. Standard: referral commission

| Who | Rate | Base | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Everyone (default) | **10%** | Tour price, after any discount, before taxes and fees | No commission on the $25 shuttle, gratuities, or booking fees. |
| Private tours | **10%** | Private total from the [[Operations/Private Tour Rate Card]] | Same base rules. A 12-person private at $1,320 pays $132. |

### 2b. Volume tiers: hotel desks, concierge desks, travel agencies only

Tiers are counted in **paid, completed guests per calendar quarter**. Once a partner reaches a tier, the higher rate applies from the next booking onward, not retroactively. That keeps the monthly math simple.

| Tier | Paid guests / quarter | Rate |
|---|---|---|
| Standard | 0–24 | 10% |
| Preferred | 25–74 | 12% |
| Premier | 75+ | 15% |

Individual affiliates (the ~100 GHL signups: baristas, drivers, lobby staff) stay on a flat 10%. The affiliate doc found that tiers confuse individuals, so they don't get them.

### 2c. Net-rate option: tour operators and travel agents

The partner books the guest at our net rate and resells at **no less than the public price**. That's rate parity: nobody undercuts our own website. The partner keeps the difference.

| Product | Public (per guest) | Proposed net | Partner margin at public price |
|---|---|---|---|
| Manchac Swamp Wildlife Tour (2 hr) | $65 | **$52** (−20%) | $13 |
| Extended Manchac (4 hr) | $130 | **$104** (−20%) | $26 |
| Whitney Plantation + Swamp Combo (~8 hr) | $195 | **$170** (fixed) | $25 |
| Honey Island (2 hr, **summer only**) | $65 | **$52** (−20%) | $13 |
| Private tours (any size on the card) | Rate card | **Card −10%** | 10% of card |
| Round-trip shuttle | $25 | $25 (no discount) | $0 |

*Why the Combo gets a fixed net:* $25 of every Combo ticket is Whitney admission, a hard cost (per the rate card). A flat 20% off would cut into admission, not margin.

*Before approving:* check these nets against NKST's actual per-seat cost and against what Viator and GetYourGuide already take on the bookings they send. FareHarbor data in the vault shows both channels booking NKST. If an OTA already takes about 20% or more, a 20% net for a local agent who also sends repeat groups is no worse.

### 2d. Optional guest-perk split *(PROPOSED, decide later)*

Some hotels would rather give their guests a perk than take a fee. The partner code can be set to **5% off for the guest + 5% commission to the partner**. The total cost to NKST is the same as the 10% default. Offer it only if a partner asks.

### 2e. Who can't take a commission

Some hotels bar front-desk staff from taking personal referral fees. Ask. We can pay **the property**, **the desk's staff fund**, or **the individual**, whichever the employer allows. Never pay an individual behind their employer's back.

## 4. Payout terms (PROPOSED)

- **Earned when the tour runs.** Commission is earned on completed tours, not on the booking date.
- **Paid monthly, by the 15th,** for all tours completed the month before. Fast payment matters. The affiliate doc notes that "word spreads in hospitality circles when you actually pay out."
- **Minimum payout $25.** Anything under $25 rolls to the next month. Balances are paid out at least once a year no matter what.
- **Method:** ACH or PayPal (preferred), or a check on request.
- **No commission** on cancelled bookings, bookings refunded for weather, no-shows refunded as a courtesy, or chargebacks. If a paid-out booking is later refunded, the commission comes off the next payout.
- **Net-rate partners** are invoiced by FareHarbor or NKST on the partner's terms (for example net-15 after the tour date). Confirm how FareHarbor handles `invoiceable_to_affiliate`.
- **Tax paperwork:** a W-9 from every partner before the first payout. Issue 1099s at whatever the current IRS threshold is. Confirm with NKST's accountant, because the threshold has recently been in flux.

## 5. Partner FAQ

**What does the guest actually do?**
They paddle a tandem or solo sit-on-top kayak for about 2 hours through cypress swamp with a naturalist guide. The water is calm and has no current. Every tour starts with a safety briefing and everyone wears a USCG Type III life jacket.

**Do they need experience?** No. The tour is built for first-timers.

**Age and size limits?** The minimum age is 8. Anyone under 16 paddles tandem with an adult. Tandem kayaks hold 400 lbs combined, and solo paddlers are most comfortable under 250 lbs. If a guest is unsure, they can call us and we'll give them a straight answer.

**Will they see alligators?** From spring through fall, alligators are common at Manchac. We never bait them and we never promise them. Below about 65°F the reptiles go quiet, and winter brings migratory birds instead. Please don't promise gators to your guests either.

**Is this an airboat tour?** No. We use silent kayaks, not airboats. There's no engine noise, and you're right at water level.

**How do guests get there?** Two ways. The round-trip shuttle leaves 740 N Rampart St, at the edge of the French Quarter, 1 hour 15 minutes before tour time and costs $25 per guest. Or they can drive to the launch on Old US Hwy 51 in LaPlace (Plus Code 5H64+W3), about 35 minutes from the Quarter. **Please tell guests not to take an Uber or Lyft to the launch. There's no ride back.**

**What if it rains?** We paddle in light rain. We cancel only for lightning or severe weather, and if we cancel, the guest gets a full refund.

**Cancellation?** Free cancellation up to 48 hours before the tour.

**Can a group book the whole tour?** Yes, as a private tour, for 1 to 20 guests at the rates in the product sheet. For more than 20, contact us. A public departure can't be bought out. It stays open to the public, 12 guests max.

**Can my guests use a gift certificate?** Yes. Gift certificates are sold on our site.

**How do I know I got credit?** You'll get a monthly report listing every booking made with your link, code, or agent account.

**Can I advertise you online?** Yes, with our approved copy and photos, and with a note saying you may earn a referral fee (see the term sheet). Please don't bid on our brand name in paid search.

**Who do I call?** Ray Fontaine · (504) 571-9975 · info@neworleanskayakswamptours.com

## 11. Partner agreement term-sheet outline

*Not legal advice. This is an outline for NKST's attorney to turn into a one-page agreement. Keep it short, because partners won't sign a 10-page contract to hand out cards.*

1. **Parties and relationship.** NKST and [Partner]. Independent contractors, non-exclusive. The partner isn't NKST's agent and can't make promises for NKST (especially about wildlife or safety).
2. **Term.** 12 months, auto-renews. Either side can end it with 30 days' written notice. Commission earned before termination is still paid.
3. **Compensation.** Commission rate, tier, or net rate as chosen from §2. Base excludes shuttle, taxes, fees and gratuities. NKST can change rates with 30 days' notice. Bookings already made keep the old rate.
4. **Attribution.** Credit goes only to bookings made with the partner's link, code, or agent account. If a booking carries two partner codes, the code on the booking wins. No retroactive claims after 60 days.
5. **Rate parity (net-rate partners).** The partner doesn't sell below NKST's public price, and doesn't advertise a discount without written OK.
6. **Payment.** Monthly, by the 15th, for tours completed the month before. $25 minimum with rollover. Refunds and chargebacks are clawed back. W-9 required.
7. **Marketing use.** Use only NKST-approved copy, photos and logos. No bidding on NKST brand terms in paid search. Never describe the tour as an airboat tour. Never promise wildlife. Never name or disparage another operator in materials that mention NKST.
8. **Disclosure.** Online recommendations disclose the referral relationship (FTC endorsement guidelines), for example "may earn a referral fee."
9. **Guest data and privacy.** NKST shares only booking-level data needed for payout. Neither side sells guest data. The partner doesn't collect guest payment details for NKST (except net-rate partners booking through FareHarbor).
10. **Safety, waiver and insurance.** Every guest signs NKST's waiver. NKST carries general liability and provides a current COI on request, naming the partner as certificate holder. Mutual indemnity for each party's own negligence (attorney to draft).
11. **Cancellations and weather.** Guests follow NKST's published policy (free cancellation up to 48 hours out; full refund if NKST cancels for weather). The partner repeats that policy and nothing else.
12. **Confidentiality.** Net rates and commission terms stay confidential.
13. **Governing law.** Louisiana.
