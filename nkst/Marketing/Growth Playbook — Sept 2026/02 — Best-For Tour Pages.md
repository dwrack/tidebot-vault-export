### Wildlife calendar at Manchac
*Everything below is common, not guaranteed. Wild animals keep their own schedule.*

| Season | What's out | Our tip |
|---|---|---|

| **Winter (Dec–Feb)** | Below 65°F, reptiles go quiet. Migratory birds arrive, bald eagles are more common, and the bare canopy opens up the sky. | Great for birders. Try the Extended tour on a warm day. |

### Wildlife FAQ

Close enough for good photos, never close enough to bother them. Guides set the distance.

**Is it safe to kayak near alligators?**

**Can you promise I'll see a gator?**
No, and be careful with anyone who does. What we can say: sightings at Manchac are common spring through fall.

**Should I bring binoculars?**
Yes, if you have them. A waterproof phone case helps too, and we sell them at the launch for $10.
