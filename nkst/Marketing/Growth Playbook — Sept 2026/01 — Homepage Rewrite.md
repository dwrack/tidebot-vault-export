## 4. Why silent kayaks, not airboats

**H2:** Why a kayak changes the swamp

You sit low, right at water level. There's no engine, so you hear what's actually out there: herons lifting off, a woodpecker working a cypress, water dripping off your paddle. Wildlife stays put and behaves naturally, because nothing is chasing it.

- **You hear the swamp.** Silence is the whole point. For a lot of guests, that quiet is the moment the tour really starts.
- **You get closer.** Kayaks slip down narrow channels between cypress trunks where bigger boats can't go.
- **You learn the real story.** Our guides are trained naturalists, not script readers. Cypress, tannins, coastal land loss, Choctaw history, how to spot a gator's eyes at the waterline.
- **You leave it better.** Silent kayaks, not airboats, means no wake and no noise. We also help restore wetlands at Bayou Bienvenue with Sankofa CDC.

## 9. FAQ

**H2:** Questions guests ask before they book

**Do I need kayaking experience?**
No. The water has no current, and our sit-on-top kayaks are wide and stable. Your guide gives a full safety briefing before launch, and plenty of our guests have never paddled before.

**Are alligators dangerous? Will I see one?**
Alligators at Manchac are common from spring through fall, but they're wild animals, so it's common, not guaranteed. They're shy of people and keep their distance. Your guide knows how to watch them safely, and we never bait or feed wildlife. In cooler months (below 65°F) gators go quiet, and migratory birds take over.

**Can kids come?**
Yes, from age 8. Anyone under 16 paddles tandem with an adult. Have a younger child? Call us at (504) 571-9975 and we'll talk through options, including our self-guided Bayou Bienvenue rental. More on our [family swamp tours page](/family-swamp-tours).

**What if it rains?**
We paddle in light rain. It's honestly one of the prettiest times to be out there. We cancel only for lightning or severe weather, and if we cancel, you get a full refund.

**What should I wear and bring?**
Quick-dry clothes (no cotton, no jeans), shoes that strap on, sunscreen for your legs, a hat, water, and a waterproof phone case. You might get a little wet.

**Is there a weight limit?**
Tandem kayaks hold 400 lbs combined. Solo paddlers are most comfortable under 250 lbs. Text us before booking if you have questions and we'll set you up right.

**How does pickup work?**
Round-trip pickup is $25 per person from 740 N Rampart St, on the edge of the French Quarter. The van leaves 1 hour 15 minutes before your tour. Driving yourself? Meet us at the launch in LaPlace, about 35 minutes away. Please don't take a rideshare out there, because there's no reliable ride back.

**What's your cancellation policy?**
Free cancellation up to 48 hours before your tour.
