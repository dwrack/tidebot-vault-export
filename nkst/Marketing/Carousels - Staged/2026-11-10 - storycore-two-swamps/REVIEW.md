## Caption

Everyone photographs the pretty swamp.

We do too. Manchac is the second-largest bald cypress swamp in the US, about 30 minutes from the French Quarter, and it's our most popular tour. It earns that. Moss, reflections, wildlife that's common but never guaranteed.

But tourism has a habit. It goes where things are already beautiful and leaves the rest alone.

Bayou Bienvenue, in the Lower 9th Ward, used to be a cypress-tupelo swamp. It was logged out in the early 1900s, and that wood helped build this city. After Katrina, a destroyed wetland meant less storm surge protection for the neighborhood beside it. Now Common Ground Relief is putting cypress back in the ground. It's slow, muddy, unfinished work.

We'll be honest, we sell the pretty one too. It pays the bills. But every group that paddles Manchac hears about the swamp that isn't there anymore, because that's the part of the story worth carrying home.

So go see Manchac. We'll gladly take you. Just don't stop there.

What's a place you keep showing up for because it needs you, not because it photographs well?
