The pattern is simple. Every post that traveled was about the viewer: their fear, their city fatigue, their kind of trip. Every post that died was about us: our reviews, our facts. Same B-roll in both piles, so the footage is not the problem. The first line is.

Two other things I think are hurting us. The auto-poster publishes through the API, which can't attach Instagram's trending audio, and the winners above were posted by hand in the app. And we've been posting 3 to 7 times a week of one format, so one bad format sinks the whole week.

## The 10 formats

Each one is a 7 to 12 second reel: one B-roll shot or two, the hook as on-screen text in the first second, one line of caption, no logo intro.

1. **Nature is closer than you think.** For locals. "You live 45 minutes from this and you've never been." Proven: 8,896 reach, 400 shares.
2. **Fear flip.** "Me, scared of gators, booking a kayak tour anyway." Cut to a calm gator sliding by. Proven: 7,430. Never says safe, just shows calm.
3. **Some people want X, we want Y.** "Some people want rooftop bars. We want cypress knees and a heron that judges you." Proven: 3,840.
4. **The other New Orleans.** Split second of Bourbon Street noise, then 9 AM on the Manchac, silent. "Same city. Day two." Proven: 2,085.
5. **Send this to.** "Send this to the friend who would scream at the first gator." Built for shares, which is what moved the top post.
6. **One perfect shot.** Seven seconds, the single best clip we have, one line: "This is a Tuesday." Loops clean. Cheapest to make, best test of raw footage.
7. **Things my guide said.** One real guide line over paddling footage. "That's not a log. Keep paddling." Guides send the lines in Slack, no filming needed.
8. **Day-trip math.** For visitors planning. "Hotel 8:15, swamp 9:00, back for lunch by 12:30." Made to be saved, and saves count.
9. **Log or gator.** Freeze frame, 3 second pause, reveal. Already in the queue as L3, keep it as the one game format.
10. **The swamp right now.** Seasonal and dated. "November: no bugs, no heat, gold cypress." This one also sells the empty seats we have this month.

## How we run it

- One format per day, never the same format twice in a week.
- Each format gets 3 posts, then a verdict against the usual 488 reach. Under half: drop it. Over 1.5x: it gets two slots a week.
- Formats 1 to 4 are already proven here, so they go first.
- Two posts a week go up by hand in the app with trending audio (formats 3 and 5 need it most). Somebody has to own that, and right now nobody does. The rest stay on the auto-poster.
- The judge is shares and reach, not likes. The dashboard's Instagram section shows the verdict per post.
