# Competitor Content-Gap Analysis — Wild Louisiana (June 2026)

Ran the content-gap technique against Wild Louisiana Tours (David's chosen competitor for this pass).

## The competitor: Wild Louisiana Tours (wildlouisianatours.com)

Manchac-based, family-run, positions as a serious eco/nature/photography outfitter (also surfaces as Crescent City Kayak / crescentcitykayak.com). Tours: Manchac Magic kayak ($65-79), private mud-boat, cypress photography, birding, kayak fishing, multi-day photo workshops. They cover photography (their obsession), birding, wildlife, guide credentials ("Louisiana Master Naturalists"), eco-education, seasonal nature content, beginner-friendly framing.

## The gap

In one line: Wild Louisiana writes for the **nature enthusiast who already wants to go.** They have almost nothing for the **nervous first-time tourist** deciding whether to go, and they completely ignore the **Manchac haunted legend.** Their About page explicitly says "education and safety, not folklore", a door left wide open. The average New Orleans visitor is Googling "is it scary," "will I see a gator," "Julia Brown swamp," "what do I wear." Wild Louisiana answers almost none of it.

Two structural soft spots: (1) the haunted angle they refuse to touch because it's off-brand for a naturalist outfitter, and (2) the practical-anxiety questions they treat as too basic to write about. Both are exactly where the volume is.

## 10 content ideas to own

1. **The Legend of Julia Brown: The True Story Behind the Manchac Haunted Swamp** — "julia brown manchac / voodoo woman manchac." Their About page openly rejects folklore for "ecological stewardship."
2. **Is the Manchac Swamp Really Haunted? What Locals Actually Believe** — "is manchac swamp haunted / haunted swamp new orleans real." They never mention "haunted" anywhere.
3. **Will I See an Alligator? Gator Season, Sightings, and Safety on a Manchac Kayak Tour** — the #1 fear/hope question. Alligators only appear in a passing customer review; no safety/season content.
4. **Kayak vs Airboat Swamp Tour: Which Is Right for You? (An Honest Comparison)** — "kayak vs airboat swamp tour." They sell kayaks and avoid naming the airboat alternative, so they never capture the comparison searcher.
5. **What to Wear on a Louisiana Swamp Kayak Tour (Every Season)** — logistics searchers who already plan to book. Confirmed absent across homepage, tours, and blog.
6. **Mosquitoes, Heat, and Humidity: How to Actually Enjoy a Summer Swamp Tour** — a real objection killing summer bookings. No mention of bugs or heat anywhere.
7. **First Time Kayaking? A Nervous Beginner's Guide to the Manchac Swamp Tour** — they slap a "beginner-friendly" label but never address the actual fear (tipping, gators, getting lost).
8. **Sunset and Night Swamp Tours: What You See After Dark in Manchac** — pairs with the haunted angle. They only offer sunrise/sunset for photographers, no after-dark content for general visitors.
9. **Best Spots to Photograph the Manchac Swamp (From Your Kayak)** — beating them at their own game. Their photo content is gear/trip-report focused and gated toward $350+ pro workshops, not free how-to for the casual phone shooter.
10. **Bringing Kids on a Swamp Kayak Tour: Age Limits, Safety, and What to Expect** — families, a huge segment. They only say "open to all ages" in passing.

## Build first

Cluster into two pillars: a **Manchac Haunted Swamp** hub (#1, #2, #8) that Wild Louisiana has structurally disqualified itself from, and a **Plan Your Swamp Tour** hub (#3-7, #10) of practical-anxiety answers they consider too basic. The haunted pillar is the bigger moat: real searched demand, fits NKST's existing angle, and the competitor's brand identity actively prevents them from chasing it. Aligns with the standing NOLA haunted/ghost SEO strategy.
