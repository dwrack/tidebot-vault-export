# NKST SEO Audit, 2026-05-04

**Window:** 2026-02-03 to 2026-05-04 (last 90 days)
**Properties pulled:** `https://neworleanskayakswamptours.com/` (primary) and `sc-domain:crescentcitykayak.com` (secondary funnel brand)
**Source data:** Google Search Console MCP (top queries, keyword opportunities, top pages, index status). Vault docs: Brand Story & Values, Brand Voice — Bayou Connection, Tourpreneur Marketing Action Plan, master vault SEO Strategy notes.

---

## Executive Summary

NKST has a solid base of brand and informational traffic but is leaking commercial-intent demand. The "alligators vs crocodiles" blog is a content monster (310k impressions, 586 clicks) that drives almost zero booking value because it sits in an info silo with no internal links to the money pages. Meanwhile money-keyword variants like `swamp tours new orleans`, `swamp tour new orleans`, `airboat tours new orleans`, and `new orleans swamp tours` are all stuck on page 2 with combined ~5,141 impressions and only 7 clicks. Page-1 push on those four alone is the single biggest lever. Crescent City Kayak (CCK) is currently a duplicate-intent property hurting more than helping: it ranks for the same kayak/swamp queries as NKST but at worse positions, and its top page (`is-it-safe-to-kayak-with-alligators`, 46k impressions) has no obvious bridge to NKST bookings.

**Top 3 priorities by impact:**
1. Push the four page-2 commercial keywords to page 1 via internal links from the alligator blog and other high-traffic info pages.
2. Decide CCK's role: either redirect to NKST (consolidate authority) or rebuild it as a true funnel page that links into NKST.
3. Fix CTR on the high-impression info posts (alligators-vs-crocodiles, top-5-classic-new-orleans-style-homes) — title and meta rewrites with a clear "book a tour" hook.

**Diagnosis:** CCK is mild cannibalization, not catastrophic, but it's also not pulling its weight. Its only standout asset is `/blog/is-it-safe-to-kayak-with-alligators/` (356 clicks, 46k impressions, position 3.3) — which has zero discernible link back to NKST tour pages.

**Recommendation:** Two paths.
- **Path A (preferred):** 301 redirect CCK property to NKST. Map `/blog/is-it-safe-to-kayak-with-alligators/` → a new NKST page on the same topic (or to `/alligators-vs-crocodiles/` as a fallback). Capture all the link equity.
- **Path B:** Keep CCK alive as a top-of-funnel content brand, but rewrite the alligator safety post to include a prominent "Book a guided kayak tour with NKST" CTA above the fold and contextual links to specific NKST tour products. Currently CCK is doing SEO charity for nobody.

## 6. Top 3 Critical Issues

1. **Money keywords stuck on page 2.** `swamp tours new orleans`, `swamp tour new orleans`, `new orleans swamp tours`, `airboat tours new orleans` are all positions 11-15 with combined 5,141 impressions and 7 clicks. These are the highest commercial-intent keywords NKST could possibly rank for. The homepage is the target page for all four, which means it's not getting enough internal links with the right anchor text.

2. **Cross-domain dilution between NKST and CCK.** CCK has 46k impressions on a single alligator safety post that doesn't drive bookings. NKST and CCK compete for the same kayak/swamp queries with NKST winning every head-to-head. CCK in its current form is a leaky bucket. Decide: redirect or rebuild as a real funnel.

3. **Massive impression-to-click gap on the alligator content cluster.** `/alligators-vs-crocodiles/` gets 310,731 impressions and 586 clicks (0.19% CTR). Even improving CTR to 1% would more than double clicks to that page. Every one of those visitors is one internal link away from a tour booking page, and right now there's no clear path.
