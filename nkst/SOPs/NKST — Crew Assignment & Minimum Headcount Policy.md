# NKST — Crew Assignment & Minimum Headcount Policy

*New Orleans Kayak Swamp Tours | Added 2026-07-18*

---

## A Note on This Document

This is new as of July 2026. Before this, tour dispatch and crew scheduling ran informally. Two problems prompted it: (1) standard 2-hour tours were sometimes running with 1-2 paying guests at a near-zero or negative margin, and (2) with only 4 active guides and up to 6 tour slots a day, there was no written logic for who runs what.

This policy covers the standard 2-hour Manchac/Honey Island tour. Extended (4hr) and Whitney Plantation Combo (8hr) tours are **exempt from the minimum-headcount rule below** — see 10.1/10.2 in the Guide SOP Master for their own economics.

---

## 1. The 4-Guest Minimum (Standard 2hr Tour)

A standard 2-hour tour should not depart with fewer than **4 paying guests** at the standard per-seat rate ($65/person). Below that, the guide flat fee alone (~$100-125, not yet formally standardized — see Part 9 of the Guide SOP Master) exceeds or roughly matches revenue:

| Pax | Revenue | Guide pay (est.) | Margin |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | $65 | $110 | **-$62** |
| 2 | $130 | $110 | **~$0** |
| 3 | $195 | $110 | $64 |
| 4 | $260 | $110 | $127 |

This is not a hard "never run it" rule — it's a trigger for the offer sequence in Section 2 below.

## 2. What To Do With an Under-Minimum Booking

**Always offer in this order — free option first, paid option second:**

1. **Reschedule (no cost to the guest).** Check FareHarbor for a nearby departure that already has a fuller group and offer to move them there. This is the default, trust-preserving option — lead with it.
2. **Private/guaranteed departure (if they want to keep their original time).** Offer to run their exact slot as a private tour:
   - **All private rates now live on [[Operations/Private Tour Rate Card]] (set 2026-08-21).** Standard 2-hour: $250 / $350 / $450 flat for 1 / 2 / 3 guests, then $120pp (4-6), $110pp (7-12), $105pp (13-20).
   - The old $450 flat 1-3 item and the shared $400 / $600 / $900 Extended/Combo ladder are **both retired.** The Combo ladder was underpriced because it ignored the $25/person Whitney admission; the new card bakes admission into every Combo number.

**Timing:** Raise this 48-72 hours before departure once FareHarbor shows the booking is under threshold — by text or email, not a phone call, and never at pickup. Raising it day-of reads as a bait-and-switch; the guest has already mentally locked in the price they paid.

**Script:**
> "Looks like it's just [you / the two of you] on this one — happy to move you to [nearby departure] where there's already a full group, no extra cost. Or if this time works best, we can make it your own private tour for $[X] more per person — whole boat to yourselves."

## 3. Crew Rotation (4 Guides, Up to 6 Daily Slots)

With 4 guides and a schedule that can run 8am/9am/9:30-10am/11:30am/2pm/4:30pm, no single guide covers every slot every day. Source of truth for who's eligible for what is the **Guide Roster & Capability Tracker** (separate doc/sheet) — this policy describes the logic, the tracker holds the actual data.

- **Standard 2hr tours:** any active guide can run these. Rotate for fairness (spread tour count roughly evenly) and reliability (guides with recent no-shows or compliance gaps get fewer slots until resolved — see Guide Roster tracker compliance column).
- **Extended/Combo tours:** route only to guides marked Combo/Extended-qualified in the tracker. As of this writing, qualification status for the current 4 guides is unknown and needs to be captured — this was flagged explicitly as an open item, not assumed.
- **Minimum coverage:** at least one guide should be reachable/on-call for every scheduled slot, even if not physically assigned, given the small crew size — no slot should have zero backup.
