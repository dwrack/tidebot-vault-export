# Morning Inspection Checklist

Catch problems at Piety St, not at the launch with 12 guests watching.

---

## Van

1. Walk around the van. Visual tire check, all four.
2. Check tire pressure: 60 psi front, 75 psi rear.
3. Check fuel level. If not full, fill at Shell only. Card code: 6006.

> [!warning]
> Shell stations only. Code 6006. Non-negotiable. Other stations will decline the card.

4. Check interior is clean. No trash, no gear from a previous tour left behind.
5. Check coolant level.

## PFDs

6. Count PFDs. Minimum 14 on the trailer/van.
7. Inspect each PFD: buckles work, straps adjust freely, no tears.
8. Smell check. Foul odor = flag to management immediately.

> [!tip]
> Spray MiraZyme on every PFD after every tour. This is how you prevent the odor problem in the first place.

## Paddles

9. Count paddles. Match to expected guest count + spares.
10. Inspect for cracks, loose blades, damaged shafts.

## Guide Bag

11. Check guide bag is fully stocked:

- [ ] First aid kit
- [ ] Cold pack
- [ ] Sting relief
- [ ] Tweezers
- [ ] Cortaid
- [ ] Advil
- [ ] Extra sunscreen
- [ ] Extra sunglasses x2
- [ ] 12 ponchos
- [ ] 2 fleece sweaters
- [ ] 2 towels
- [ ] Granola bars
- [ ] Extra water
- [ ] Tow rope + carabiner
- [ ] Dry bag
- [ ] Charged waterproof phone
- [ ] Dramamine / ginger chews

## Merchandise

12. Check merchandise stock: t-shirts, phone cases, sunglass straps.
