# Night-Before Checklist

Confirm everything the night before so the morning is execution, not discovery.

---

1. Open FareHarbor. Confirm tour time, guest count, and full manifest.
2. Note special circumstances: large groups, minors, guests with physical limitations, third-party bookings (Viator, Expedia, TripAdvisor).

> [!tip]
> All third-party bookings include shuttle pickup at 740 N. Rampart. No exceptions.

3. Check weather forecast for tour window. Flag wind, rain, or lightning concerns to management immediately.
4. If running Honey Island: check water gauge at wx.aerisweather.com/rivers/gauge/PERL1.html

> [!warning]
> Honey Island safe range: 5-12 ft. Under 5 ft or over 12 ft = move to Manchac. Notify guests AND management of the route change.

5. Text every guest using the night-before template. Include tour time, pickup location, and what to bring.
6. Confirm van is available and not scheduled for another use.
7. Set alarm. You need to arrive at 1407 Piety St **1 hour 25 minutes** before tour time.
