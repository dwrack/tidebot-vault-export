# End-of-Day Sign-Off

Final check before you're done for the day. Run through this list, confirm each item, move on with your life.

---

1. All equipment returned and accounted for (kayaks, paddles, PFDs, guide bag).
2. Van cleaned inside. No trash, no guest belongings left behind.
3. Van fueled at Shell (code 6006).
4. All PFDs hung to dry at Piety base.
5. PFDs sprayed with MiraZyme.
6. Piety base locked.
7. Keys returned under metal bucket on glass table.
8. Any incidents or equipment issues reported to management (text or Slack, same day).
9. Social media post made (photo from today's tour, tag @nolakayaktours).
10. Post-tour texts sent to all guests.
