# Post-Tour Closeout

The tour isn't over when guests get out of the water. It's over when PFDs are hung, van is fueled, and Piety is locked.

---

1. Collect all paddles and PFDs at the launch. Count them before loading.
2. Thank guests genuinely. Mention something specific from the tour ("that alligator sighting was unreal" or "you guys crushed it for first-timers").
3. Ask for a review. Be direct but not pushy: "If you had a great time, a Google or TripAdvisor review means more to us than you'd think."
4. Ask about tips. Keep it natural. Don't dwell on it.
5. Load all kayaks onto the trailer. Secure every strap. Check the hitch.
6. Load guests into van.
7. Drive to 740 N. Rampart St. Drop off guests.
8. Fill gas tank at Shell. Card code: 6006.

> [!warning]
> Shell only. Fill the tank every time, even if it's three-quarters full. The next guide shouldn't have to worry about fuel.

9. Clean van interior. Remove all trash, lost items, sand, water.
10. Return to 1407 Piety St.
11. Hang ALL PFDs to dry. Every single one.
12. Spray every PFD with MiraZyme.

> [!tip]
> This is the single most important equipment maintenance task. PFDs that smell bad ruin the guest experience before the tour even starts.

13. Lock Piety base.
14. Return keys under the metal bucket on the glass table.
15. Post on social media or Google. Use a photo from the tour, tag @nolakayaktours.
16. Send post-tour SMS to all guests.
