# Guest Check-In Steps

This is your first impression. Guests decide in the first 90 seconds if they trust you with their day.

---

1. Arrive at 740 N. Rampart St (Rampart Treehouse) 5-10 minutes before pickup time.
2. Park safely and visibly. Guests are looking for you.
3. Before guests arrive, review the manifest. Know their names.
4. Greet each guest by name as they arrive.
5. Open FareHarbor on your phone. Begin checking in guests.

> [!warning]
> REFRESH the manifest before you start. Last-minute additions happen constantly. If you're working off a stale list, you'll miss someone.

6. Check waiver status for every single guest.

> [!warning]
> **Unsigned waiver = guest does not get in the van.**
>
> For unsigned waivers: pull up that guest's **booking-specific** waiver link on your phone and hand it to them to sign. Do NOT use a generic QR code. Every waiver is tied to a specific booking. This matters for liability.
>
> No signed waiver, no ride. No exceptions.

7. Confirm third-party bookings (Viator, Expedia, TripAdvisor) are accounted for. These guests always get shuttle service.
8. If any guests haven't arrived 5 minutes after scheduled pickup, text them.
9. Wait a maximum of 15 minutes past pickup time. Contact management before leaving without anyone.

> [!tip]
> Don't just drive off. Management may have context you don't, like a guest who called in running late.

10. Remind all guests: leave valuables out of the van. We're not responsible for items left inside.
11. Load guests and depart.
