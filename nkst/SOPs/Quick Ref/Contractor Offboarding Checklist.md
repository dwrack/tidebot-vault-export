# Contractor Offboarding Checklist

Clean break, no loose ends. Whether the guide is leaving voluntarily or being let go, every step here matters.

---

## Immediate

1. Verbal or written confirmation that the relationship is ending.
2. If termination: document the reason internally. Be specific.
3. Confirm the final shift date with the guide.
4. Collect all company property: keys, PFDs, branded gear, anything else issued.

## Within 24 Hours

5. Remove from FareHarbor scheduling.
6. Deactivate ASN affiliate link.
7. Remove dashboard access.
8. Remove from Slack.
9. Remove from group texts.
10. Remove from Google Drive.
11. Remove from DocuSeal.

> [!warning]
> Don't delay on access removal. 24 hours max. A departed contractor with live system access is a liability.

- [ ] FareHarbor removed
- [ ] ASN affiliate link deactivated
- [ ] Dashboard access revoked
- [ ] Slack removed
- [ ] Group texts removed
- [ ] Google Drive removed
- [ ] DocuSeal removed

## Within 7 Days

12. Update permits if the departing guide was listed on any.
13. Update Crew Roster & Credentials Tracker: mark as departed, add departure date.

- [ ] Permits updated
- [ ] Roster updated

## Within 30 Days

14. Calculate any outstanding pay.
15. Calculate pending affiliate commissions.
16. Issue final payment.
17. Flag for 1099 reporting if the guide earned over $600 YTD. Keep the W-9 on file.
18. Document final payment date and amount.

- [ ] Outstanding pay calculated
- [ ] Affiliate commissions calculated
- [ ] Final payment issued
- [ ] 1099 flag set (if applicable)
- [ ] Final payment documented

## Vault Cleanup

19. Remove the guide from all active lists in the vault.
20. Add entry to offboarding log with date, reason, and any notes.

- [ ] Active lists cleaned
- [ ] Offboarding log updated
