# Contractor Onboarding Checklist

Contractor-facing. Use this to bring a new guide from first contact to first solo tour.

---

## Step 0: Culture

1. Have the new guide read the "How We Run Things" culture doc in full before anything else.

## Pre-Hire Documents

2. Collect valid Louisiana driver's license (verify clean driving record).
3. Collect CPR certification, or confirm willingness to obtain before solo tours.
4. Send and collect signed Independent Contractor Agreement (ICA) via DocuSeal.
5. Collect W-9.
6. Collect photo ID.
7. Collect direct deposit or PayPal info for payment.

> [!warning]
> No guide leads a solo tour without a signed ICA and current CPR cert. No exceptions.

- [ ] Driver's license verified
- [ ] CPR cert collected (or timeline agreed)
- [ ] ICA signed via DocuSeal
- [ ] W-9 collected
- [ ] Photo ID on file
- [ ] Payment info collected

## Slack & Accountability

7a. Invite the new guide to `#new-orleans-kayak-swamp-tours` before the first shadow shift — this is where guide logs, incident flags, and day-to-day coordination actually happen, not a side channel.
7b. Walk through the pre/post-tour guide log with TideBot: **no pre-tour log + no post-tour log = no pay for that tour.** This is already true for active guides; new guides should hear it on day one, not find out the hard way.
7c. Walk through shift-coverage expectations: if a guide can't make a scheduled tour, finding a replacement from the active roster and updating the crew assignment in FareHarbor is the guide's job, not management's. Management does not broker swaps.

- [ ] Added to guide Slack channel
- [ ] Pre/post-tour log requirement explained (tied to pay)
- [ ] Shift-swap responsibility explained

## Orientation

8. Walk the new guide through 1407 Piety St base: equipment storage, van, trailer, PFDs, paddles, guide bag.
9. Show the van and trailer setup. Demonstrate hitch, strap system, backing procedure.
10. Walk through the full equipment inventory and where everything lives.

## Shadow Shifts (2-3 tours minimum)

11. New guide rides along with an experienced guide. Observes the full cycle: check-in, drive, launch, tour, closeout.
12. During shadow shifts, practice loading and unloading kayaks.
13. Practice backing the trailer at the launch site.
14. Debrief after each shadow shift. Note what clicked and what needs work.

## Co-Guide Shifts (1-2 tours)

15. New guide leads specific segments: the safety briefing, the PFD fitting, the FareHarbor check-in.
16. Experienced guide observes and fills gaps.
17. Debrief after each co-guide shift.

## Pre-Solo Verification

18. Confirm ICA is signed and on file.
19. Confirm FareHarbor login has been issued and guide can navigate it.
20. Confirm CPR cert is current.

- [ ] ICA signed
- [ ] FareHarbor access granted
- [ ] CPR current

## First Solo

21. Management is on call throughout the first solo tour. Guide has contractor's cell number.
22. Debrief after the solo tour, same day.
23. Log the solo sign-off in the Solo Sign-Off table.

- [ ] First solo completed
- [ ] Debrief done
- [ ] Logged in Solo Sign-Off table
