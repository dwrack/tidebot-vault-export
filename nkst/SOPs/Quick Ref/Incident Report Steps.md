# Incident Report Steps

When something goes wrong, this is the order. Safety first, documentation second, everything else after.

---

## Immediate Response

1. Ensure the safety of the injured/affected guest and all other guests.
2. If there are injuries or immediate danger: call 911.
3. Contact management with your GPS coordinates or Plus Code (5H64+W3 for Maurepas).
4. If someone is unresponsive and you are CPR-certified: begin CPR.
5. Keep other guests calm and positioned away from the incident.

> [!warning]
> Do NOT move anyone with a potential spinal injury. Stabilize and wait for EMS.

## Capsizing

6. Call out: "Stay with your boat, I'm coming."
7. Paddle directly to them. Signal other guests to stop and hold position.
8. Help the guest re-enter the kayak: guide positions at the back deck, guest kicks and pulls up from the side.
9. If the guest cannot re-enter: tow them to shore using the tow rope.
10. Once on shore, assess for injuries, cold, and shock. Wrap in fleece sweater or towel from the guide bag.

## Lightning

11. Head to shore immediately. No discussion.
12. Get all guests out of kayaks and away from the water.
13. Seek shelter in the van. Avoid tall isolated trees.
14. Contact management. Do not return to the water until 30 minutes after the last thunder.

## Vehicle Incident

15. Ensure guest safety first.
16. Call 911 if there are injuries.
17. Contact management.
18. Do not leave guests unattended for any reason.

> [!warning]
> Never leave guests alone at the launch site, on the water, or in the van to deal with a problem somewhere else. Guests stay with you.

## After Immediate Response

19. Fill out an incident report. Include:
    - What happened
    - When it happened (exact time)
    - Who was involved (guests and crew)
    - What actions were taken
    - Names of witnesses
20. Photograph relevant conditions: water level, weather, equipment state, injuries (with permission).
21. File the incident report with management the same day. Do not wait.
