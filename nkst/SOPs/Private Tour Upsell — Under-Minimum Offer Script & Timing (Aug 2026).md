## The rule

Lead with the free option, not the upsell. It protects trust and reviews.

Script shape: "Looks like it's just the two of you on this one. Happy to move you to [nearby departure] where there's already a full group, no extra cost. Or if this time works best, we can make it your own private tour for $X more per person, whole boat to yourselves."

Timing: reach out 48-72 hours before departure, once FareHarbor shows the booking is under threshold. Text or email, not a phone call. Raising it at pickup is too late; it reads as a bait-and-switch. For direct or phone bookings, raise it at time of booking while dates are still flexible.

Booking-flow idea from the note: ask at checkout with two opt-in lines, "I would be willing to switch to a private tour if the tour minimum isn't met (+how much)" and "I would be willing to switch days if the tour minimum isn't met."
