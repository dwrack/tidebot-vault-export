# New Orleans Kayak Swamp Tours — SOP: Contractor & Guide Offboarding
*2026-04-20*

> **Purpose:** When someone leaves (voluntarily or otherwise), this is the complete checklist. Nothing gets missed, nothing lingers.

---

## When to Use This

- Guide or contractor resigns
- Contractor is terminated or not renewed
- Affiliate becomes inactive and you're formally removing them
- Seasonal guide not returning

---

## Step 1: Communication (Day of Decision)

- [ ] Verbal or written confirmation that the working relationship is ending
- [ ] If termination: brief, direct reason documented (internal notes only)
- [ ] Final shift date confirmed
- [ ] Collect any company property: keys, radios, PFDs, dry bags, branded gear

---

## Step 2: System Access Removal (Within 24 Hours)

### FareHarbor
- [ ] Remove from guide/staff scheduling
- [ ] Deactivate ASN affiliate link (if applicable)
- [ ] Remove dashboard access
- [ ] Note in Affiliate Master Doc (Google Sheet): mark row as INACTIVE with date

### Communication Channels
- [ ] Remove from Slack workspace (or relevant channels)
- [ ] Remove from group texts / WhatsApp groups
- [ ] Remove from any shared email aliases

### Other Platforms
- [ ] Remove from Google Drive shared folders
- [ ] Remove from GHL (GoHighLevel) sub-account if they had access
- [ ] Remove from scheduling tool (FareHarbor calendar, Google Calendar, etc.)
- [ ] Remove from DocuSeal (revoke access, retain signed docs)

---

## Step 3: Regulatory & Permits (Within 7 Days)

- [ ] Notify LDWF if guide had a permit card issued under our license
- [ ] Confirm their guide permit card is returned or noted as expired
- [ ] Update Crew Roster & Credentials Tracker (mark as departed + date)
- [ ] If they were listed on the SUP (Special Use Permit), contact Forest Burks to remove

---

## Step 4: Financial Closeout (Within 30 Days)

- [ ] Calculate any outstanding pay for completed shifts
- [ ] Calculate any pending affiliate commissions (only for completed, non-refunded tours)
- [ ] Issue final payment via Venmo/Zelle/ACH
- [ ] If they earned over $600 YTD: flag for 1099 at year-end (keep W-9 on file)
- [ ] Document final payment date and amount

---

## Step 5: Documentation & Vault Cleanup

- [ ] Update this vault: remove from any "active guides" lists or rosters
- [ ] Add to offboarding log below
- [ ] Archive (don't delete) their ICA and credentials in Drive
- [ ] If they were a hurricane plan designee or emergency contact: assign replacement

## Notes

- Never delete someone's signed ICA or W-9. Archive them in Drive under a "Former Contractors" folder.
- If someone might come back seasonally, mark them as INACTIVE rather than fully offboarding. Skip the permit/regulatory steps until you're sure they're done.
- Affiliate offboarding is lighter: just deactivate the ASN link, mark the row in the spreadsheet, and stop payouts. No regulatory steps needed unless they were also a guide.
