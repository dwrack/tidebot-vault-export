## Trip Summary

> The tour navigated the full loop from Buzzards Bayou woods to Old Duck Camp/Lillys Woods, Bayou, lasting 5 hours. The guide noted significant spider presence and some hyacinth crossings. The woods on Lilly's Bayou were open. Wildlife observed included gators, red-shouldered hawks, owls, egrets, heron, swamp raccoon, and rabbits. This was the first time the full loop had been done since 2024.
