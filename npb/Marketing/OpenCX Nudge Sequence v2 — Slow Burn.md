## Step 1: kill the zero

Deactivate `93f61197-288f-4d92-aa34-07d3f0147bb0` before anything else. It went live Aug 10 and dumped all 1,703 messages between Aug 11 and Aug 17, about 340 a day: a backlog of ~344 contacts hit with five messages each in six days. Zero replies. That is not a drip, that is a burst, and it is actively training people that our DMs are noise.

## Step 2: the new cadence

Five touches over eleven days instead of six over two. Every touch must be worth opening on its own, and every touch has a job.

| When | Job | Copy |
|---|---|---|
| **T+45 min** (was 3 min) | Stay useful, don't beg | "Forgot to mention: the sunset slot is the one people actually remember. Golden hour, gators cruising, the whole thing. Worth knowing before you pick a time." |
| **T+20 hrs** (before the Meta window shuts) | The durable-channel ask | "Before this thread goes quiet on me: what's a good cell? If your date opens up or the weather turns I'd rather text you than let you find out the hard way. Or tap below and I'll only ping you when we've actually got open seats." |
| **T+3 days** | Give something, ask nothing | "No pitch, just a thought. If you're still figuring out the New Orleans plan, the boat is 15 minutes from the Quarter, which means you can do it and still make your dinner reservation. Most people assume it eats a whole day. It doesn't." |
| **T+7 days** | The group chat unlock | "Genuine question, is it you deciding or is it a group chat? If it's a group chat I can write the whole thing up in one paste-able message so you're not the one defending it." |
| **T+11 days** | Warm exit, one last door | "Last one from me, promise. If the trip's still happening, just reply with a date and I'll handle the rest. If plans changed, no hard feelings, Bourbon Street will still be there. Have a good one out there." |

Rules that apply to all of them:
- **Stop the sequence dead the moment they reply.** Non-negotiable.
- **Never announce a session closure.** 632 close-out messages earned 16 replies. Just close it silently.
- Signed **Nolan**, never "Team."
- One message per touch. No stacked follow-ups.

## Step 3: the T+45 replacement is the one to watch

The 3-minute "are you still there?" is the only nudge currently earning anything (8.7%), so don't be reckless with it. It works because they're still holding the phone, not because the copy is good. The v2 version keeps that window (roughly, moved out to 45 minutes so it isn't needy) and swaps a needy question for something useful.

**If the T+45 version drops below 6%, move it back to 15 minutes before you change the copy again.** Timing and copy are two variables. Change one.

## Step 4: measurement

- One OpenCX tag per touch, so replies attribute cleanly.
- Weekly: pull tagged sessions, match names against FareHarbor bookings.
- Scoreboard is **replies per 100 sent** and **bookings per 100 sent**, per touch.
- Kill any touch sitting at zero after three weeks, and cap any sequence at 50 sends/day so a backlog can never get blasted like this again. That cap is the rule that would have caught the 1,703 on day one.
