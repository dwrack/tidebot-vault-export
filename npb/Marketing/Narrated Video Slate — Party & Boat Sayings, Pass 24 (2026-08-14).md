## What this is

12 new narrated short-form pieces (19.0-22.5 seconds each) built around real party-boat and eco-tour sayings and small on-water rituals: the things guests actually say, ask for, or start on their own, plus two genuine, externally verifiable words/terms (Louisiana Creole "lagniappe," and the birding-community term "lifer") used the same way Pass 23 used "where y'at" and "throw me something," as real regional/subcultural language, not invented company slang. Format is modeled exactly on the Admire NOLA narrated series (`Admire NOLA/Content/Narrated/01-poetic-NOTES.txt` and `Admire NOLA/Content/Script & Caption Bank.md`): timestamped VO lines with a bracketed visual on every line, a named voice sample, a music bed, and a shot list.

Where Pass 23 deliberately underweighted toasts and captain-scripted lines, Pass 24 continues that shift and goes further: every piece below is driven by a guest, a genuine unscripted crew behavior, or a real piece of outside language, never a rehearsed company catchphrase. Two pieces (05 and 08) are genuinely new territory for this slate: 05 is the first corporate-specific piece built without a toast at its center, and 08 is the first piece centered on a quiet or reserved guest rather than the loudest moment on the boat.

Split evenly across NPB's two on-water products, a deliberate change from the roughly 7/5 party-fleet-heavy ratio most prior passes used, because eco-tour premises were getting thinner while party-fleet premises stayed easy to find. An even split forced sharper eco-tour ideas instead of stretching thin ones:
- **Private BYOB party fleet** — pieces 01-06
- **Naturalist eco swamp tour** — pieces 07-12

Every spoken line was drafted, then routed through `/copy-mentors` (routed to whichever master fit the piece's shape) and then `/copywriting-2.0` (the 3-test filter: can I visualize it, can I falsify it, can nobody else say it). What got cut or rewritten is noted per piece below. The recurring pattern this pass: soft, unfalsifiable claims ("that's the best photo op on the boat," "it's a magical moment," "you'll thank us for it") kept surfacing in first drafts and kept getting cut for something specific and checkable instead.
