## Part 1. One-page sponsor sheet

### Headline

**The only Tales venue that floats.**

### Subhead

300+ brand events happen during Tales week. Every one of them is in a hotel ballroom or a bar. Yours could be on a bayou, at sunset, with a band on the sand and your bottles behind every bar.

### The venue (left column)

**NOLA Party Barge, Bayou Bienvenue.** A private marina 7 miles from the French Quarter. 15 to 20 minutes in an Uber, not an hour in a van.

- Four covered party boats: Freaky Tiki (25), Twerkin Tiki (25), Party Queen (22), Bayou Boogie (18). 90 guests on the water at once.
- Indoor room, sand beach with fire pits, space for a live act. **[confirm indoor room capacity]**
- Real bayou. Cypress, egrets, gators. Not a set.
- 1,000+ guests come through the marina on a normal Friday-Saturday. Your brand is in front of them all week, not just at your event.
- Licensed captains, crew, production and guest list handled by us. You bring the product and the ambassador.

### What you get (right column)

**Title Partner. $50,000. One brand.**
- Category exclusive across the whole week (only one whiskey, one rum, one tequila)
- "[Brand] presents NOLA Party Barge at Tales" on every boat, the marina, crew shirts and all socials
- Two nights: one industry-only (bartenders, media, Spirited Awards crowd), one ticketed public night
- Your ambassador runs the boat bar. Tales guest-shift format, on the water.
- Walkthrough video, boat cam, recap reel and crew content all week

**Presenting Partner. $20,000. One brand per category.**
- Category exclusive in your category
- Named on one signature night, logo on all boats
- One guest shift, co-hosted night, recap reel and boat cam

### Where the money goes

40% act and production. 30% promotion (Meta, local press, the bartender invite list). 30% to us. We'd rather you see the budget than guess at it.

### The ask

Pick a night. We'll build the room around your brand. Reply to this sheet or call (504) 264-1056. nolapartybarges.com

### Notes on building it

- **Voice:** Nolan. Contractions, short sentences, a little swagger. No "leverage," no "activation" unless the brand says it first.
- **Photos:** none of the current IG feed shows the marina well (JT's read). The deck waits on the Track D walkthrough shoot. Shoot at 7am or golden hour until October.
- **Mock sponsor banner:** make one fake "[Brand] presents" boat mock-up in Canva. Brands need to see their name on it to picture it.
- **Licensing line:** if the ATC/TTB license isn't in hand by spring 2027, slide 7 and 8 say "sponsor supplies product, poured by our licensed bar partner" instead of "we sell it." Don't promise retail margin we can't legally take yet.
- **Dates:** Tales 2027 isn't announced. Say "July 2027" until it is.
- **No pricing on the one-pager for the local tier.** Seven Three, Roulaison, Happy Raptor etc. get the Presenting tier at a discount. Quote that in person, not on paper.
