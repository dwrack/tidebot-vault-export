# nolapartybarges.com: new page titles and descriptions (2026-09-24)

- Titles are 60 characters or less, descriptions 160 or less, so Google shows them in full.
- Paste into each page's SEO title / meta description field in wp-admin. If the suffix comes from a site-wide setting, change it once there instead of per page.

- "(keep)" means the current description is fine and has no Pedal in it.
- Old blog URLs with "nola-pedal-barge" in the slug are left alone. Changing a URL needs a 301 redirect and isn't worth it for these.
- Pedal Bike Bar and Airboat pages: the audit recommends hiding them while the tours have no dates. Titles are here in case they stay up.
