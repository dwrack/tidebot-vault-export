# OpenCX Re-Engagement Strategies — Ghosted Conversations

*Built 2026-08-27 from live OpenCX data.*

## The problem, quantified (last 60 days)

| Channel | Sessions | Ended with us talking (ghosted) |
|---|---|---|
| Messenger | 580 | 565 (97%) |
| Email | 92 | 60 |
| Web chat | 50 | 32 |
| Instagram | 39 | 35 |

- We're paying ~$2.83 per Messenger conversation started (~205/mo from the TOF campaign), and almost all of them end with our message hanging.
- **The killer stat: of everyone who's ever messaged, only 125 contacts have a phone number and 85 have an email.** Once the Meta 24-hour messaging window closes, we legally cannot re-open the conversation with a promo. No phone, no email, no opt-in = that lead is gone forever.
- So the strategies split into two jobs: (A) convert more people *inside* the 24h window, and (B) capture a durable channel (SMS opt-in, Messenger marketing opt-in, email) so we can come back *after* it.

## The 10 strategies

### Inside the 24-hour window (free, policy-safe)

**1. Timed double-nudge (T+2h / T+20h).** OpenCX workflow: if the contact hasn't replied 2 hours after our last answer, send a short human-sounding bump ("Still thinking it over? Happy to check another date."). A second one at hour 20, right before the window closes, with a concrete next step. This alone typically recovers 15-25% of ghosts.

**2. Quote-hold urgency for price/date askers.** Anyone who asked pricing or availability and went quiet gets a specific hold offer: "I can pencil you in for Saturday 6pm and hold it til tomorrow, want it?" Concrete beats generic. Tag these sessions so we can measure.

**3. Phone capture as a default step (David's idea).** Bot asks for a number mid-conversation with a reason attached: "What's a good cell? If a slot opens up or the weather shifts I'll text you." That converts a dead-end DM into an SMS lead. Route sends through the toll-free 888-618-9606 once verification clears (10DLC memory: the old number is carrier-filtered).

**4. Email capture on every group quote.** Groups of 8+ get "I'll email you the quote + booking link so you have it." Same-day email with photos and the FareHarbor link, automatic +3 day bump if unopened/unbooked.

### Re-opening the conversation after 24h

**5. Messenger Marketing Messages opt-in.** Meta's official one-tap "get updates from us" opt-in inside the convo. Once tapped, we can message them promos indefinitely without paying for ads. This is the single highest-leverage fix because it works on the 97% who ghost on Messenger.

**6. Weekly "this week's open seats" locals blast (David's idea).** Every Tuesday, send opted-in Messenger + SMS lists the actual unsold inventory for the coming week, framed as a locals deal. Moves last-minute seats (matches the booking-lead-time shift to in-week) without discounting the brand publicly.

**7. Click-to-Messenger retargeting audience.** Build a Meta custom audience of "messaged the page in last 90d, no purchase" and run a cheap always-on adset ($5-10/day) that re-opens the conversation. Paid, but it targets exactly the pool we already paid $2.83/head to start.

**8. Comment-to-DM re-trigger on the FB auto-poster.** The daily reels already reach our page audience. Anyone who comments or reacts re-opens their 24h window; OpenCX auto-DMs them ("Good to see you back, still thinking about a cruise?"). Turns the existing organic machine into a re-engagement net.

### Smarter targeting

**9. Intent-tagged re-engagement.** Mine ghosted transcripts and tag the drop reason: price shock, date unavailable, group logistics, just browsing. Each gets a different play — date-unavailable ghosts get an availability alert when that date opens; price ghosts get the locals deal; group ghosts get the human touch (#10).

**10. Human text for high-value ghosts.** Any conversation mentioning 10+ people gets flagged to Jeffrey with a one-line personal text from the business line (not a call: Google Voice history shows 68.3% text reply rate vs ~29-85% call miss rates). One booked tiki pays for a month of everything else here.

## How to test between them

- Tag every session touched by a strategy in OpenCX (one tag per strategy).
- Weekly: match tagged contacts against FareHarbor bookings by name.
- Scoreboard: recovered bookings per strategy per week. Kill anything at zero after 3 weeks, double down on the top 2.
- Sequence: ship #1, #3, #5 first (they're pure OpenCX workflow config), then #6 once opt-in lists exist, then the paid/organic nets (#7, #8).
