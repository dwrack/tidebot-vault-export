## BODY

### How Far Is the Swamp From New Orleans?

Seven miles. About 15 minutes from the French Quarter, and you never cross the river.

That's the short version. The longer version is that most swamp tours sold in New Orleans aren't in New Orleans. They're in Slidell, or Marrero, or out past LaPlace toward Manchac. The tour is two hours. The driving is another ninety minutes round trip. Nobody puts that on the booking page.

We're on Bayou Bienvenue at 2101 Paris Road, still inside Orleans Parish. You get in your car or your Uber, you drive fifteen minutes, you get on a boat.

### Can you do a swamp tour without a long drive?

Yes, and you've got two versions of it.

Drive yourself. Fifteen minutes out Chef Menteur or I-10, free parking at the marina, check in inside where there's air conditioning and a real restroom. Rideshare runs about the same and you skip the parking entirely.

Either way you're back in the Quarter for dinner. A 1.5 to 2 hour tour that starts at 3 doesn't eat your whole evening, which is the actual complaint about the Slidell trips.

### What you're not giving up

The nearest swamp tour is usually the worst one. Not here, and the reason is geography rather than anything we did.

Bayou Bienvenue is a cypress swamp on the edge of the Central Wetlands Unit. Alligators, herons, egrets, the occasional wild boar on the bank. It's the same wildlife the 45-minute-away tours are selling, because it's the same wetland system. The water just happens to reach the city limits.

You get: a covered boat with music and a restroom onboard, a captain who grew up here, BYOB, 1.5 to 2 hours, from $59.99.

You don't get: a fan boat that's too loud to hear the guide, or a van.

### Getting here

2101 Paris Road, New Orleans, LA 70129.

From the French Quarter, take Chef Menteur Highway east or I-10 east to Paris Road. Fifteen minutes without traffic, twenty with. Free parking on site. Check in inside the marina building.

Rideshare works fine. Coming back, request it from the covered area by the dock.

### Questions people actually ask

**What's the closest swamp tour to New Orleans?**
Ours, at 2101 Paris Road. Seven miles from the French Quarter and inside Orleans Parish. Every other operator is in Jefferson, St. Tammany, or St. John Parish.

**How far is Honey Island Swamp from New Orleans?**
About 45 minutes to Slidell, closer to an hour if you hit traffic on the Twin Span. It's a good swamp. It's just not a short trip.

**Do I need a car?**
No. Rideshare from the Quarter runs about 15 minutes.

**Is there a bathroom?**
On the boat. Which matters more than people think, because the alternative is a two-hour tour after a 45-minute drive.

**Are there really alligators this close to the city?**
Yes. Bayou Bienvenue is a working cypress swamp, not a detour on the way to one. Gators are most active March through October when the water's warm.

**How long does the whole thing take, door to door?**
Two and a half to three hours including travel. The Slidell and Manchac tours are closer to four and a half.
