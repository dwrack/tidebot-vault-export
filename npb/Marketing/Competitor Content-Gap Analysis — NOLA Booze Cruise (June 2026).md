# Competitor Content-Gap Analysis — NOLA Booze Cruise (June 2026)

Ran the content-gap technique against the strongest New Orleans party-boat competitor. Pedal products on both sites were deliberately ignored (no-pedal rule).

## The competitor: NOLA Booze Cruise (nolaboozecruise.com)

Cleanest exact-match domain for the money keyword, ranks #1 for "New Orleans Booze Cruise" and "Party Boats in New Orleans." Captained pontoon fleet (Party Queen ~22, Freaky Tiki ~25-26 with full restroom + tiki bar, Bentley ~6), all BYOB/BYOF with Bluetooth, thin eco-tour section. Public ~$63/person; private Freaky Tiki $1,200 for up to 25. (Tipsy Gator / boattoursatthewharf.com is the other contender but its site throws a TLS cert error, an organic trust liability.)

## The gap

They nail the vibe ("PARTY HARD. MAKE MISTAKES.") and list boats and prices, but answer almost none of the planning questions a group organizer asks before dropping $1,200. No FAQ, no cancellation/weather policy, no "what to bring," no dock/parking detail, no bachelorette guide, no sober/family framing, and the eco tour is a stranded page with zero link to the party fleet. NPB runs both a private party fleet AND a social eco swamp tour, so it can own the combo angle they completely ignore.

## 10 content ideas to own

1. **New Orleans BYOB Boat Rules: What You Can (and Can't) Bring on the Water** — they say "BYOB, BYOF" with zero detail; glass vs cans, coolers, open-container.
2. **The Real New Orleans Bachelorette Boat Party Planning Guide (Headcount, Timeline, Budget)** — they tag boats "good for bachelorettes" but offer no planning content or pricing math.
3. **Is There a Bathroom on the Boat? New Orleans Party Cruise Restroom Guide** — top unspoken anxiety for 2+ hour BYOB cruises; their restroom info is buried in one boat's feature list.
4. **What to Bring on a New Orleans Booze Cruise (Sun, Shade, and the August Heat)** — zero "what to bring" or weather-prep content despite Louisiana summer being the #1 comfort issue.
5. **Where Do New Orleans Booze Cruises Launch From? Dock, Parking & Rideshare Guide** — no departure-location or parking detail anywhere; causes day-of stress and lost bookings.
6. **Rained Out? New Orleans Party Boat Weather & Cancellation Policy Explained** — they publish no cancellation/weather policy at all.
7. **Private Boat Charter vs Joining a Public Cruise in New Orleans: Which Is Worth It?** — they list both, never help the buyer choose; no break-even headcount math.
8. **The Booze Cruise + Swamp Tour Combo: See Gators and Still Have a Party** — their eco tour and party boat live on separate pages with no combo. NPB runs both and can own this hybrid intent outright.
9. **Sober-Friendly and Group-Friendly: New Orleans Boat Outings Beyond the Booze** — their "make mistakes" brand repels sober groups, families, and corporate planners searching for a calmer option.
10. **New Orleans Party Boat for 10, 15, or 25 People: Pricing and Boat Size by Group** — pricing is scattered one boat at a time, no group-size matching for the most common buyer question.

## Build first

Quick-win logistics/FAQ pages (#1, #3, #5, #6) capture high-intent traffic they ignore. Planning content (#2, #7, #10) drives the private-charter buyer. #8 (swamp/eco combo) and #9 (sober/family) are NPB's structural moats: things NOLA Booze Cruise's brand voice and site structure can't easily copy.
