# Content engine: 3 options to hit 3+ posts a week

**Goal:** at least 3 feed posts a week on IG + TikTok, every week, without it depending on one person remembering. 2026 is at 8 a month. 2023 and 2024 ran 10.4 and 13.8.

**Where we are (2026-10-31):** Jeff has IG and TikTok in good shape. The strategy (Value First, 5 pillars) is written. 30 reels are cut, captioned, and sitting in the queue as `staged`. The auto-poster on the always-on Mac posts any row flipped to `queued` at 11am. Nothing has been flipped, so the Oct 17 slate never went out. The machine is built. What's missing is a weekly owner and a steady supply of fresh footage.

**The real bottleneck is footage, not editing.** The 30-reel slate mostly uses the IG archive and old raw masters. Once that's used up, there's nothing new unless someone films on the boats every week.

---

## Option 1: Claude runs the engine, crew feeds it footage (recommended to start)

**How it works**
- Crew films. Every captain or deckhand gets a quota: 3 clips per trip, 5 to 15 seconds each, dropped into Crew Clips the same night. Shot list taped in the boat: a guest moment, a crew bit, one bayou or wildlife shot.
- Every Monday, Claude builds the week's slate from the newest Crew Clips plus whatever's left in the archive: cuts, cover lines, captions, a pillar for each post. It goes on the Review Board.
- Jeff (or David) spends 10 minutes on Monday flipping the week to `queued` or killing posts. The poster does the rest.
- Jeff stays on Stories, comments, DMs, and trends. Those need a human in the moment.

**Cost:** about $0 extra. Maybe $50 to $100 a month in crew capture bonuses (for example $5 per clip that gets posted).
**Time:** about 10 min a week for the approver, plus the crew's 2 minutes per trip.
**Risk:** the crew doesn't film, and the queue runs dry in about 4 weeks. Fix: tie the clip quota to the end-of-shift checklist, and post a clip count in the 8am digest so misses get noticed.
**First week:** re-date the staged slate to start Wed Nov 4, Jeff approves week 1 on Tuesday, crew shot list goes out Tuesday.

## Option 2: Option 1 plus a local creator 2 days a month

**How it works**
- Everything in Option 1, plus a New Orleans freelance videographer/creator who shoots 2 busy trips a month (a Saturday bachelorette and a sunset run) and hands over 20 to 30 raw clips each time.
- Better footage than phone clips from the crew: gimbal, good audio, planned bits like the Crew characters and Bayou Files explainers.
- Claude still cuts, captions, and queues. The creator doesn't post or run the account.

**Cost:** ballpark $300 to $600 per shoot day in New Orleans, so $600 to $1,200 a month (unverified, get 2 quotes).
**Time:** Jeff books the creator and gets them on board, about 1 hour a month.
**Risk:** paying for footage that's prettier but less real. Brief them on the Value First rules (people in frame in the first 2 seconds, no glossy promo).
**When to choose it:** if crew footage is thin or flat after 4 weeks of Option 1.

## Option 3: Hire a part-time social media manager

**How it works**
- A part-time contractor (local or remote plus a local shooter) owns IG + TikTok end to end: plans, shoots or sources, edits, posts, reports monthly.
- Jeff moves off social completely and onto ops.

**Cost:** ballpark $1,500 to $3,000 a month for a real one. Cheaper ones tend to post generic stock-style content (unverified, depends on market).
**Time:** 4 to 6 weeks to hire and train. Someone has to manage them.
**Risk:** highest cost, slowest start, and they'll rebuild what Claude already does. It also puts the brand voice in a stranger's hands. Worth it only if Jeff needs his social hours back for ops.

---

## My pick

**Start with Option 1 this week. Check the numbers on Dec 1. Add Option 2 only if fresh footage is the problem.** We already paid for the hard part (strategy, slate, auto-poster). The cheapest fix is a weekly owner and a crew clip habit. Option 3 makes sense only if we're taking social off Jeff's plate for ops reasons, not for content reasons.

**Scorecard for Dec 1:** posts per week (target 3+, stretch 5), number of fresh crew clips per week (target 15+), and reach per post compared with October.
