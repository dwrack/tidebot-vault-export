## Platform rules (research 2026-09-27)
**TikTok alcohol**
- Minors near alcohol get the post removed.
- Excessive drinking, chugging, shotgunning and drinking games get restricted to 18+ and pulled from For You.
- Paid alcohol promotion by creators is restricted and 21+.
- **Rule for us:** judge outfits, dancing, decor, the playlist and the chaos. Never judge how drunk anyone is.
- Sources: https://www.tiktok.com/community-guidelines/en/regulated-commercial-activities and https://www.tiktok.com/community-guidelines/en/fyf-standards

**TikTok Stitch/Duet and music**
- A Stitch or Duet needs real added commentary, or it gets flagged as unoriginal.
- They only work where the creator has enabled them.
- Re-posting a Stitch to IG needs that creator's permission.
- Business accounts are limited to the Commercial Music Library, with tighter enforcement since 2025-07-25. https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/commercial-music-library

**AI labels**
- **TikTok:** a label is required for realistic AI or for AI voices that imitate a real person. Obvious cartoons generally don't need one, but TikTok auto-detects C2PA metadata anyway.
- **Meta:** the "AI info" label is required for realistic audio or video, and ElevenLabs voices arguably count.
- **Rule for us:** turn the label on for every Judges post on both platforms. It costs nothing.

**Instagram originality: the biggest risk**
- Since 2026-04-30, accounts that mostly repost content they didn't create aren't recommended to non-followers. https://www.tubefilter.com/2026/04/30/instagram-removes-algorithm-recommendations-repost-content-aggregator/
- **Rule for us:**
  - The judges, scorecards and voiceover must be the content, and other people's clips are only the prompt.
  - Use Remix, never download and re-upload.
  - Mix in original skits (Chambers) and our own footage every week.

**Instagram reach**
- Sends per reach, watch time and likes per reach are the top signals. Sends matter most for reaching non-followers.
- Carousels built to be DM'd to the group chat ("send this to your MOH") fit.
- Collab posts allow up to 5 co-authors, each account must accept, and engagement is pooled.
- Trial Reels show only to non-followers for 72 hours, which is good for testing new bits.

**Format references**
- WeRateDogs: over-the-top scores, and the joke is that they go past 10.
- Keith Lee: a steady 1-10 formula.
- Portnoy's One Bite: he has already reviewed Big Easy Daiquiris, so there's an easy callback.
- **Duolingo's Duo in other brands' comments:** the closest model for our Comment Section.
