# Competitor Sales Channels + Commission Research (Oct 31 2026)

Desk research from public web pages only. Nobody was contacted. Commission rates are industry benchmarks; no competitor publishes its agent terms. Linked to in-flight item f103.

## Recommendation
- 25% to French Quarter tour desks and hotel concierges (the volume channel; they steer walk-ins)
- 20% to OTAs (Viator, GetYourGuide, Tripshock, Tripster, Greetwell)
- 15% on FareHarbor Distribution Network (confirm fee stacking with Kevin Schmitt at FareHarbor)
- 10-20% to travel agents
- Optional bonus up to 5%, taken from the base rate rather than added on top
- Verify before launch: have someone on the team call or walk into 3-4 desks and ask what they're paid per swamp-tour seat

## Regulation
- Anyone marketing, selling or arranging tours on the public right-of-way needs a city Tour Company license: Category 2, $1,000/yr, expires Sept 30 (nola.gov/tour-planner-license/)
- The licensee is responsible for tours sold on its behalf
- A street-solicitation ban by tour companies is reported, but the current code text is unverified (check City Code Chapter 30)
- Fixed storefront and hotel desks are the safe route. Don't pay street hawkers until the license question is confirmed.
