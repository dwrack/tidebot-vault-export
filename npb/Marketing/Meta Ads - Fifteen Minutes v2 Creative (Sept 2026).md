**Why it exists:** on 2026-09-29 Meta flagged creative fatigue on three of the four ad
sets, wording it as "cost per result for these ad sets may be at least twice as much as
ads you ran in the past because their image or video has been shown to parts of your

2026-09-22. This is the structural cost of holding creative constant for a clean copy
test, and the fix is a second video, not a change to the copy.

## How it differs from v1

v1 ("Gator Collision") opened on a gator close-up with a shock line, THERE'S A GATOR 20
FEET AWAY, then cut to party footage. v2 inverts the shape: it opens wide and empty on the
bayou and uses the proximity contrast as the hook, then reveals the boat, then the gator.

Fresh footage throughout. v1 used IMG_1500, IMG_5791, IMG_5792, IMG_5794, IMG_5882 and the
two-alligators drone clip. v2 shares none of those except the gator close-up source, and
uses a different moment of it (4.5s rather than 3.0s and 8.0s).

## Deliberate choices worth knowing

**No price burned into the video.** v1's ad copy quotes $63 a seat and $1,250 private, but
`Marketing/Site Audit 2026-09-24.md` found FareHarbor actually charges $59 and $1,200. Until
that is reconciled, price stays in the ad copy where it can be edited without a re-render.

**No private-boat claim on screen.** $63 is the public shared seat; $1,250 is the private
charter. Text like "your whole group" was cut for that reason. Every line in v2 is true of
both products.

**Footage that was rejected.** A lot of the dump is twerking and swimwear footage and
carries real Meta adult-content risk: `NPB video dump/225613746708835408.mp4`,
`682554809377183693.mp4`, `Assets/Videos/nola freaky tiki video.mp4`, and roughly the first
13 seconds of `7529836440114d8896fe11856fb50eb2.mov`. Only the clean stretch of that last
clip, about 16 to 20 seconds, is used.

**Two different boats appear.** Beats 4, 6 and 7 are the thatched NOLA Freaky Tiki. Beats 2
and 5 are the teal covered party barge. The endcard is the parent NOLA Party Barge logo,
which is what ties them together. Both boats run the same bayou cruise.

## How to deploy it without wrecking the test

The four ad sets are a copy test with creative held constant. **v2 has to go into all four
ad sets, not one.** Putting it in a single ad set confounds creative with copy and throws
away the week already spent.

Add it as a second ad inside each existing ad set. Do not create new ad sets, do not touch
budgets, and do not edit the existing copy while the test is running.
