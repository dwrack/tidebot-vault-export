PIECE 04 — Funny / toast lane — "Cheers to Whoever's Paying"
Product: Private BYOB Party Fleet
Voice: EL-George (ElevenLabs)   Music: Hot 8 Brass Band-style bounce bed (clearance unconfirmed)   Length: 21.5s

SCRIPT (timed to narration):
 0.0s  Someone always yells it before the first toast even starts.       [group gathered, cups raised, energy building]
 3.0s  "Cheers to whoever's paying!"                                     [guest shouting the line, group laughing]
 5.8s  Nobody checks their phone to find out. Everybody already knows.   [group turning, laughing, looking at one guest]
 9.5s  It's always the same friend. The one who actually books things.   [organizer laughing, slightly embarrassed, cup raised]
13.5s  So this toast is actually for them. They ran the group chat. They earned it.  [group toasting the organizer directly, genuine]
18.0s  Somebody always makes it happen. Buy them a drink.                [closing wide shot, group cheering, sunset]

ON-SCREEN TEXT TREATMENT:
Quote-caption pill on the shouted line only ("CHEERS TO WHOEVER'S PAYING!"), high-energy bounce-in animation matching the group's shout. No text anywhere else in the piece — the redirect to the organizer plays better without a caption explaining the joke.

SHOT LIST:
- Group gathering, cups raised, pre-toast energy (Shot #2 variant)
- The shouted line, group laughing (candid, not staged-to-camera)
- Group turning and laughing toward one specific guest (whip pan or quick cut)
- Organizer's embarrassed laugh, cup raised (Shot #5/#4 blend — a genuine reaction, not posed)
- Group toasting the organizer directly — this should read as sincere, not just a punchline
- Closing wide shot: group cheering, sunset (Shot #6)

SOURCE GROUNDING: Directly adjacent to Unreasonable Hospitality idea #61 ("The 'your admin is a legend' move" — publicly thanking the person who organized), adapted from the corporate-outing context in idea #61 to the general party-fleet context, since idea #61 is written specifically for a corporate booking and this piece is written to work for any group type (bachelorette, birthday, friend group) without requiring a corporate setting. Distinct from Pass 17 Piece 03 ("The Corporate Toast Nobody Expected," a corporate executive-acknowledgment toast) because this piece is generic to any group and specifically about the person who did the *booking and planning*, not a boss/leadership acknowledgment.

COPY PASSES: Drafted, then routed through /copy-mentors (Ogilvy — state the real, observable behavior plainly, let the truth of it do the selling rather than exaggerating). Then /copywriting-2.0 (3-test filter): an early draft assumed the organizer was "she" by default, written with a bachelorette-party lens carried over from brainstorming. Caught on the "can nobody else say it" test in a broader sense — nothing about this script requires the organizer to be any particular gender, and defaulting to one without any actual person specified isn't earned by the content. Rewritten throughout to "they/them."
