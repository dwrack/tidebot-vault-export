PIECE 08 — Funny / character lane — "I'm Never Getting in That Water"
Product: Naturalist Eco Swamp Tour
Voice: EL-Brian (ElevenLabs)   Music: Jon Cleary-style mellow instrumental bed, kept low (clearance unconfirmed)   Length: 21.5s

SCRIPT (timed to narration):
 0.0s  Nobody has to get in the water. Doesn't matter. Somebody still says it.  [guest looking at black water, gator nearby, wary]
 4.0s  "I am never getting in that water."                               [guest's line, on-screen text overlay of quote]
 6.5s  Said by someone who was never going to. Said with total conviction anyway.  [guide smiling, guest still staring at the water]
11.0s  Guide's heard it a thousand different ways. Never gets old.        [guide chuckling softly, steering]
14.5s  You're in a boat. Nobody's asking you to.                         [wide shot, gator sliding through the water alongside the boat]
18.0s  Say it loud anyway. It's part of the tour.                        [group laughing, boat gliding on]

ON-SCREEN TEXT TREATMENT:
Quote-caption pill on the guest's declaration line only, styled slightly oversized and dramatic (bold, a touch theatrical) to match the mock-seriousness of a guest declaring total conviction about something nobody asked them to do. No text on any other line.

SOURCE GROUNDING: Not sourced from a specific Unreasonable Hospitality idea — built from real, common guest behavior on wildlife tours (reflexive declarations about water/wildlife that have zero bearing on an actual boat tour where nobody's asked to enter the water). Distinct from Pass 9 Piece 11 ("Gator, Turtle, or Stick," a wildlife-ID guessing game) and Pass 16 Piece 07 ("No Guarantees, Just Good Odds," a wildlife-sighting FAQ) — this piece isn't about identifying or predicting wildlife at all, it's a character beat about a guest's own (unprompted) declaration.
