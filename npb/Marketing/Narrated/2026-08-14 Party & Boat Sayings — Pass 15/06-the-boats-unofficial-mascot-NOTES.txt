PIECE 06 — Funny / prop-bit lane — "The Boat's Unofficial Mascot"
Voice: EL-Brian (ElevenLabs)   Music: Hot 8 Brass Band-style bounce, quirky and fun (clearance unconfirmed)   Length: 19.5s

SCRIPT (timed to narration):
 0.0s  Nobody remembers whose flamingo this actually was.                      [close-up: worn inflatable flamingo tied to the rail]
 2.8s  It's been on every cruise since. Nobody's claimed it.                    [quick cuts: flamingo in different group photos, different occasions]
 6.0s  Brides hold it for the toast.                                           [bride holding the flamingo up mid-toast, laughing]
 8.4s  Birthdays put a party hat on it.                                        [flamingo wearing a small party hat, guests laughing]
10.8s  It's got more group photos than most of the crew.                       [montage: flamingo in dozens of different guest photos]
13.8s  Somebody tries to take it home every single trip.                       [guest mock-hiding the flamingo in a bag, deckhand shaking head, laughing]
16.8s  It always makes it back to the rail.                                    [flamingo back on the rail, boat pulling away, sunset]

Feedback wanted: voice? pacing? text style/placement? footage choices? music level?

PRODUCTION FLAG: this script assumes a mascot prop already exists and already rides along on cruises. Confirm that before shooting. If no such prop exists yet, this piece is also the pitch to start the tradition (same move as Pass 9's "Ring the Bell," which pitched installing a bell that wasn't there yet) — buy a cheap inflatable flamingo, put it on one boat for two weeks, see if crew and guests actually pick it up before promising "every cruise" on camera.
