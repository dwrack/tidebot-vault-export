Product: Naturalist eco swamp tour
Voice: EL-George (ElevenLabs)   Music: quiet, minimal, no percussion (mood only, no artist cited)   Length: 15.5s

SCRIPT (timed to narration):
 0.0s  The first time the guide asks for quiet, it's a request. Voices down, paddles up.   [wide shot: kayaks gliding, guide holding up a hand, group quieting]
 3.5s  Twenty minutes later, an egret lands close, and the guide doesn't say a word.   [medium shot: egret landing near the kayaks]

10.0s  Nobody decided to get quiet. It just happened.   [medium shot: guide glancing back, mildly surprised, pleased]
12.5s  By the second time, the water taught them faster than the guide did.   [closing wide shot: kayaks drifting silently past the egret]
