PIECE 01 — Heartfelt / poetic, practical lane — "Sixty Seconds, Music Off"
Product: Private BYOB party fleet
Voice: EL-Eric (ElevenLabs)   Music: warm, unhurried, near-silent under the pause (mood only, no artist cited)   Length: 17.0s

SCRIPT (timed to narration):
 0.0s  Forty minutes into a party cruise, right at the loudest point, the music just stops.   [wide shot: boat mid-party, music audibly cutting, guests turning to look]
 3.5s  No countdown. No announcement first. Just off.   [close-up: deckhand's hand lifting off the speaker]
 6.0s  For about a minute, all anybody hears is the water against the hull and the city somewhere behind them.   [medium shot: guests going quiet, looking out at the water]
10.5s  Nobody checks their phone. Nobody asks when it's coming back.   [close-up: guests just looking, still, drinks forgotten in hand]
13.5s  Then it comes back on, and the party picks up exactly where it left off.   [closing wide shot: music resuming, guests cheering, dancing again]

None. The piece's own hush would be undercut by text on screen; matches the Admire NOLA poetic lane's convention of letting VO and visual carry a quiet beat.
