Product: Private BYOB Party Fleet
Voice: EL-Eric (ElevenLabs)   Music: playful funk, New Orleans Suspects reference (clearance unconfirmed)   Length: 15.0s

SCRIPT (timed to narration):
 0.0s  Everybody waiting to board calls it something different.   [wide shot: guests gathered near the container bar before boarding]
 3.0s  One guy calls it "the shipping container bar." His girlfriend calls it "the blue thing."   [close-up: guest gesturing, girlfriend correcting him, laughing]
 6.5s  A bachelorette group's already renamed it "the pregame box." No vote was taken.   [close-up: group laughing, one guest pointing it out to the others]
 9.5s  Nobody's right, nobody's wrong. It's just where the party quietly starts.   [wide shot: guests mingling, drinks in hand, waiting to board]
12.5s  By the time the boat pulls out, nobody remembers needing a name for it at all.   [closing wide shot: boat departing, the container visible on the dock behind]
