PIECE 01 — Heartfelt / corporate lane — "Your Team Showed Up for You"
Product: Private BYOB Party Fleet
Voice: EL-Lily (ElevenLabs)   Music: warm soul guitar, Deacon John reference (clearance unconfirmed)   Length: 15.0s

SCRIPT (timed to narration):
 0.0s  A corporate group boards like corporate groups always do. A little stiff, half of them still on their phones.   [wide shot: group boarding in business-casual, several checking phones]
 3.0s  Then somebody quietly points out who actually planned the whole thing.   [close-up: guests glancing toward one person, an admin or manager]
 5.5s  The captain doesn't make a speech. Just leans in and says it plain: "Your team showed up for you. That's not nothing."   [close-up: captain speaking to a manager, no fanfare]
10.0s  Nobody claps. The boss just looks around the boat a second too long.   [wide shot: boss glancing at the team, quiet]
13.0s  Ten minutes later, phones are away and somebody's already dancing.   [closing wide shot: group loosened up, dancing, phones down]

ON-SCREEN TEXT TREATMENT:
None. The captain's line is the whole piece; text on top of it would undercut the delivery.

SHOT LIST:
- Wide shot, group boarding, phones out (opener)
- Close-up: guests glancing toward the organizer
- Close-up: captain speaking to the manager, no fanfare
- Wide shot: boss looking around the boat
- Closing wide shot: group dancing, phones away
