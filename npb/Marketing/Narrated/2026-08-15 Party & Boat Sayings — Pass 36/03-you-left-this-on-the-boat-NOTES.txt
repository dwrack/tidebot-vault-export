PIECE 03 — Heartfelt / practical lane — "You Left This on the Boat"
Product: Private BYOB Party Fleet
Voice: EL-Lily (ElevenLabs)   Music: warm, understated soul vocal feel, Charmaine Neville reference (clearance unconfirmed)   Length: 16.0s

SCRIPT (timed to narration):
 0.0s  Every so often, somebody leaves something behind on this boat.   [wide shot: empty boat after guests disembark, deckhand tidying up]
 3.0s  A phone charger. A pair of sunglasses. Once, an entire birthday cake.  [close-up: items found on a seat]
 6.5s  Somebody has to notice, then somebody has to text.   [crew member texting on a phone]
 9.5s  "Hey, you left your sunglasses. We'll hold them at the dock."   [text bubble overlay: the actual message, shown sending]
12.5s  Small thing. Doesn't take long. Still means somebody was paying attention after you'd already gone.  [closing wide shot: boat idling at dock, quiet evening]
