Product: Private BYOB party fleet
Voice: EL-Daniel (ElevenLabs)   Music: funky brass, The Stooges Brass Band reference (clearance unconfirmed)   Length: 17.5s

SCRIPT (timed to narration):
 0.0s  Every crew huddle before a shift eventually turns into the same argument.   [wide shot: crew gathered near the container bar before a shift, relaxed]
 3.0s  One deckhand pitches "Whatever happens on the barge stays on the barge." Somebody groans.   [close-up: deckhand pitching, gesturing, second deckhand groaning]
 6.5s  Another tries "See you on the water." Too plain, somebody says.   [close-up: second crew member pitching, unimpressed reactions]
10.0s  A third line gets floated, then walked back before it even finishes.   [medium shot: crew member starting to speak, stopping, laughing at themselves]
13.0s  Nobody's landed on the one the boat says every single time. Not yet.   [wide shot: crew shrugging, boat visible behind them]
15.5s  Ask again next season.   [closing shot: crew dispersing to start the shift, still half-arguing]
