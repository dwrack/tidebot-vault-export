PIECE 08 — Heartfelt lane — "The One Who's Hanging Back"
Product: Naturalist Eco Swamp Tour
Voice: EL-Lily (ElevenLabs)   Music: Jon Cleary-style solo piano, warm and understated (clearance unconfirmed)   Length: 22.5s

SCRIPT (timed to narration):
 0.0s  Most of the group is up front, pointing at everything.              [wide shot: group at the bow, engaged, pointing at wildlife]
 3.5s  One person's usually a little further back, a little quieter.       [guest sitting slightly apart, watching, not saying much]
 7.0s  Nobody makes a thing of it. A guide just drifts back and sits with them for a minute.  [guide moving back, sitting near the quiet guest]
12.0s  Doesn't ask why. Just points out a heron, asks where they're from.   [guide pointing gently, guest starting to relax]
16.5s  Ten minutes later, they're the one pointing something out to somebody else.  [same guest now leaning forward, pointing, smiling]
21.0s  Nobody's left at the back of this boat for long.                    [wide closing shot, whole group together, engaged]

ON-SCREEN TEXT TREATMENT:
None. This is the one piece built to let a quiet moment breathe without a caption competing with it, same instinct as prior passes' unscripted-moment pieces (e.g. Pass 23's "Give It Up for the Guide" and "This Reminds Me of My Grandpa's Camp").

SHOT LIST:
- Wide shot: group engaged at the bow, pointing at wildlife (opener)
- One guest sitting slightly apart, quieter, watching
- Guide drifting back, sitting with the quieter guest
- Guide pointing out a heron, asking where they're from, gently
- Same guest now leaning forward, pointing something out to someone else
- Closing: wide shot, whole group together, engaged

PRODUCTION / CONSENT FLAG: Standard Social Capture Playbook filming ask applies. Keep the quieter guest's shots general (over-the-shoulder, wide, not a tight identifying close-up) unless they're visibly comfortable and consent is confirmed. This is lower-stakes than prior consent-flagged pieces (no grief, no medical disclosure, just a guest who's a little more reserved than the group), but the same care applies since it involves reading a guest's mood.

SOURCE GROUNDING: This is a genuinely new premise, the first of nine passes centered on a quieter or more reserved guest rather than the loudest moment on the boat. Loosely sourced from `Ideas/Unreasonable Hospitality — 100 Ideas for NPB.md` idea #29, "The 'I see you' move. If a crew member notices someone hanging back, not fully in the party, bring them in gently." Adapted here from the original party-fleet framing (a big group, a wallflower) to the naturalist tour's smaller, quieter group dynamic, where "hanging back" reads as contemplative rather than left out. Not confirmed as a formal guide practice, flagged accordingly, not stated as documented company policy.
