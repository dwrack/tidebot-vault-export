PIECE 02 — Funny / running-joke — "Man Overboard (Nothing's Actually Overboard)"
Product: Private BYOB Party Fleet
Voice: EL-Eric (ElevenLabs)   Music: bouncy, comedic timing (mood only)   Length: 16.0s

SCRIPT (timed to narration):
 0.0s  Someone yells "man overboard!" and half the boat actually turns to look.   [wide shot: guests spinning around, half-panicked, half-laughing]
 3.0s  It's not a person. It's someone's hat, doing slow backstroke away from the boat.   [close-up: hat floating on the water, drifting]
 6.5s  The deckhand's already got the net out before anyone finishes the sentence.   [medium shot: deckhand scooping with a long-handled net, practiced motion]
10.0s  Nobody explains why there's a net just for this. There's always a net just for this.   [close-up: net swinging back aboard, hat dripping, guest reaching for it, relieved]
13.0s  By the third cruise of the day, "man overboard" doesn't mean what it used to.   [closing wide shot: boat cruising on, guest wringing out the hat, laughing]

None. The mismatch between the alarm and the actual stakes is the joke; captioning it would explain the punchline before the visual lands.
