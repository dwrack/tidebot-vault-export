PIECE 02 — Practical, captain line lane — "Two Minutes, Port Side"
Product: Private BYOB party fleet
Voice: EL-Daniel (ElevenLabs)   Music: bright, urgent-but-easy, quick swell (mood only, no artist cited)   Length: 16.0s

SCRIPT (timed to narration):
 0.0s  Every golden-hour cruise hits the same sixty seconds where the light goes from good to unbelievable.   [wide shot: boat moving, golden light breaking across the water]
 3.0s  The captain doesn't announce it early. There's no warning built in ahead of time.   [close-up: captain glancing at the horizon, hand on the wheel]
 5.5s  Then, flat and quick: "Two minutes, port side. Get your photos now."   [close-up: captain speaking into the mic, no ceremony about it]
 9.0s  Fourteen phones come out at once. Nobody's arguing about who's blocking whose shot.   [medium shot: guests scrambling to the port rail, phones up]
12.5s  By the time the color's gone, every single one of them already has the picture.   [closing wide shot: sunset fading fast, guests lowering phones, satisfied]

ON-SCREEN TEXT TREATMENT:
"TWO MINUTES, PORT SIDE" pops as a quote card the instant the captain says it at 5.5s, clears fast. No other text.
