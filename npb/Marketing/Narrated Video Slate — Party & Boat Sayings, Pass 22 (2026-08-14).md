## What this is

12 new narrated short-form pieces (19.0–23.5 seconds each) built around real party-boat and eco-tour sayings — toasts, captain lines, bachelorette/birthday-group lines, and running jokes that happen out loud on the water. Format is modeled exactly on the Admire NOLA narrated series (`Admire NOLA/Content/Narrated/01-poetic-NOTES.txt` and `Admire NOLA/Content/Script & Caption Bank.md`): timestamped VO lines with a bracketed visual on every line, a named voice sample, a music bed, and a shot list.

Split 7/5 across NPB's two on-water products, matching the ratio used in four of the five prior passes:
- **Private BYOB party fleet** — pieces 01–07
- **Naturalist eco swamp tour** (per-seat, wildlife-and-naturalist focus) — pieces 08–12

Every spoken line was drafted, then run through `/copy-mentors` (routed to whichever master fit the piece's shape — Halbert's one-person specificity for the overheard-line and character pieces, Sugarman's slippery-slide curiosity-build for the humor-into-payoff pieces, Bencivenga's tension-and-reveal structure for the anticipation-build pieces, Ogilvy's restraint and product-as-character warmth for the brand-personality pieces, Schwartz's reframe for the objection/expectation pieces) and then `/copywriting-2.0` (the 3-test filter: can I visualize it, can I falsify it, can nobody else say it). What got cut or rewritten is noted per piece below — mostly unfalsifiable absolutes ("nobody's ever not laughed," "nobody's ever mad about it"), one line that implied an unconfirmed on-water activity outside what this fleet actually offers, and one assumed pronoun rewritten to stay identity-neutral for a hypothetical guest.

This pass leans on three real, previously-unused ideas already sitting in this vault instead of inventing every hook from nothing: the captain's stock safety-briefing joke as its own bit (adjacent to `Ideas/Unreasonable Hospitality — 100 Ideas for NPB.md` idea #18's "give them a character, not a job title" principle, applied to the life-jacket talk → Piece 02), the boat-with-personality concept already established in `Brand Story & Values.md` Section 3 ("Fleet with personality") pushed into an actual toast-to-the-boat bit (→ Piece 07), and the real sourced nutria fact from `Admire NOLA/Content/Script & Caption Bank.md` Lane 9 (wildlife spotlight) → Piece 11.

```
 0.0s  Nobody assigns this toast. It just happens.                       [wide deck shot, group mid-conversation]

Copywriting-2.0 kept the line as drafted; it passed the 3-test clean (visualizable, falsifiable against real footage, and specific enough that no competitor's generic "cheers" reel could say it).
