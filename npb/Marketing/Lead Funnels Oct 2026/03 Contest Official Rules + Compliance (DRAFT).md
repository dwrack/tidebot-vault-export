## 6. Open questions for counsel (6)

1. **Monthly drawings and the $5,000 line.** If each month is its own sweepstakes with its own rules, dates, and entry pool, and one $1,200 prize, do New York (GBL 369-e) and Florida (849.094) treat them separately? Or do they add up to a registered and bonded promotion? If they add up, the fallback is to exclude New York and Florida residents or to register and bond. Also confirm Rhode Island 11-50-1 doesn't reach an online promotion with no Rhode Island store.
2. **Referrals under Meta's current policy.** The draft has no referral entries. Is there any referral reward that survives the ban on incentivizing people to publicize a promotion, for example a reward tied to sharing the free trip guide instead of the sweepstakes? And is making marketing email part of entry acceptable, with one-click unsubscribe that doesn't affect the entry?
3. **Louisiana R.S. 51:1721 to 51:1723.** Does a written prize confirmation within 10 days satisfy the delivery rule for an experience that gets scheduled later? Is the $50 code to non-winners clear of the "you have won" rules as worded? Please supply or approve the Affidavit of Eligibility and the Liability and Publicity Release.
4. **Liability on the water.** Is the release in rules section 13 plus the standard rider waiver enough for a prize charter, given maritime law? Does anything change because the boat is BYOB and guests as young as 6 may ride on a private charter? Does any state alcohol permit, current or applied for, bring alcohol promotion rules into play?
5. **Sponsor identity and boilerplate.** Which legal entity is the Sponsor? Are the dispute terms in rules section 17 (Louisiana law, Orleans Parish venue, no class actions, damages limit) enforceable as written? Does the privacy policy need more than the one added line?
6. **Mail-in timing, the confirm step, re-entry, and unsubscribed entrants (added 2026-10-06).** (a) A mail-in card must be received by the close date, not postmarked by it, so the drawing can run the next morning. Is receipt-by-close acceptable for the free entry method, given the close is a Sunday? If not, the fallback is postmark-by-close plus a grace period, with the close moved up a week. (b) An online entry only counts after the entrant taps a confirm link in our email (rules 4a). Any issue with that for the free entry method or equal odds? (c) A non-winner can enter the next month's sweepstakes by tapping one button in the result email (rules 4c), carrying over the 21+ and US residency checkbox and the email consent from the earlier entry. Is that carry-over acceptable, or does the tap need to land on a page that shows the checkbox again? (d) An entrant who unsubscribes stays in the drawing and, if drawn, gets one winner notice by email (rules 16). Is that one email fine under CAN-SPAM after an unsubscribe?
