# Funnel Variations, Ranked (NOLA Party Barge)

*Drafted 2026-10-03. Internal planning doc, draft only. Nothing here is live, sent, or posted. It fits inside the Sept 30 lead plan [P]: one CRM (fh-lead-engine), one main drip (The NOLA Countdown), many front doors. Magnet details live in file 05 (Lead Magnet Plan). Contest details live in the contest files.*

**The position.** The survey contest is the new top of the funnel. But the cheapest leads are people already touching us: people who quit checkout, people asking what it costs, riders on the boat, people who DM. Build those four doors right behind the contest. Everything else waits its turn.

**How to judge all of them.** By bookings. Not entries, not clicks. The alligator video in the spring test campaign (Apr to May 2026) pulled 21,500 link clicks and 3 purchases on $3,199 of spend [M]. Each funnel below gets one number.

Codes like [G] point to the source list at the bottom. Anything older than 14 days is marked "as of." Volume numbers are guesses with the math shown, not forecasts.

## 2. Top 5 specs

### #1 Survey contest (short form; the contest files hold the real spec)

- **Entry:** an organic post asking "What's the best thing about New Orleans?", links `/win` and `/win-ig`, and the "Win a Trip!" nav item.
- **Steps:** post, 5-tap survey, email and a 21+ box, confirm tap, warm-up emails built from their answers, the draw, $50 code to non-winners, hand-off to the Countdown or the Open Boat List.
- **First 3 emails:**
  1. Instantly: "You're in the {month} drawing." Entry confirmed, draw date named, one tap for a bonus entry.
  2. About day 2: their favorite thing about New Orleans, done like a local, with one pick from the Spots research file [K].
  3. About day 4: bonus entries from the follow-up questions, one tap each. Referrals, tags and shares never count.
- **Build:** survey and result pages, entry ledger, answer links, tracked links, rules page, draw script. All of it needs build. Today the engine runs one fixed step list per lead [X].
- **Seed it:** 3,749 people entered the old MailerLite contest and are due into the CRM on Oct 27 [P]. Invite them through the weekly reel email, not a cold send from the new engine. [VERIFY: how old that list is and how it opted in]
- **The number:** private boats booked by entrants, per round. Bar: 2. That covers the prize at retail about twice.

### #2 Abandoned booking recovery

- **Entry:** a booking gets started and not finished.
- **Steps:**
  1. FareHarbor's native recovery email at +2 hours. Asked twice, no reply logged [A][W]. The third ask goes to the FareHarbor account manager (the escalation path, as of 2026-08-27), not the support queue.
  2. Booked signal. The FareHarbor webhook has been posting new, changed, and cancelled bookings to the always-on Mac since 2026-09-29 [m4]. Forward each one to the engine's `/booked` so booked leads leave every selling email. Small, needs build.
  3. Rescue emails for CRM leads tagged hot (2 boat clicks, a planned feature [P]) with no booking after 3 hours.
  4. Retargeting stays on. It's the best thing in the ad account: 12.9x over the 30 days to 2026-09-21 [M].
- **First 3 emails (Rescue):**
  1. +3 hours: "Your boat on {trip_date}." What a crew of {group_size} gets, and one booking link.
  2. +24 hours: "Paste this in the group chat." The plan in four lines and the split per person.
  3. +48 hours: "Still on for {trip_date}?" Three taps: yes, different date, plans changed. A yes pings the manager. No holds, since dates aren't held without payment [A].
- **Build:** the FareHarbor follow-up, the webhook forwarder, a Rescue step list, `?ref=rescue` on every link. Tracked links and the hot flag need build.
- **The number:** bookings a month carrying `ref=rescue`, plus FareHarbor's own recovered count once that email is on. Bar: 10.

### #3 Price-question capture (full spec in file 05, section 2)

- **Entry:** /boat-rental/, /our-boats/, each boat page, the budget post, and Nolan's reply to "how much."
- **Steps:** 3 taps. The boat and the per-person number show right away, no email wall. The email unlocks the group chat message and the code. If it's 13+ guests with a date inside 30 days, a person gets pinged.
- **First 3 emails:**
  1. Instantly: "Your split: {boat}, {per_person} each." The group chat message, ready to paste, and the $50 code with its end date.
  2. +2 days: "How many days is enough? (and what it costs)". Countdown chapter 2.
  3. +5 days: "Where to crash with a crew". Countdown chapter 3.
- **Build:** one small page that posts to `/lead` with `source=split`. It works on the engine as it is today. The math comes from the verified price table: a $1,200 tiki for 25 is $48 each, an $800 Bayou Boogie for 18 is about $44 each.
- **The number:** splitter leads who book within 90 days. Bar: 5%, same as the Countdown target [P].

### #4 Rider capture (The Crew Photo)

- **Entry:** a QR sign at the bar on the four party boats, and the captain's send-off line.
- **Steps:** the crew takes one group shot. Riders scan and give a first name, an email, and two taps (live here or visiting, what are you celebrating). The photo goes in that boat's album before the shift ends. The link goes out the next morning. Adults only on the form, since private charters carry ages 6 and up.
- **First 3 emails (Rider):**
  1. Next morning: "Your crew photo from {boat}." The photo link and nothing else.
  2. +2 days: "Your turn to plan one." The $50 code and the per-person math.
  3. +7 days: one question. Visitors: "When's your next trip?" Locals: "Want the Open Boat List?"
- **Build:** 4 QR signs, a `/crew` page posting `source=crewphoto` with the boat and ride date, a Rider step list (needs build), a 15-second crew script, a shared album per boat per day. No review ask in these emails. FareHarbor's email and the review text already do that job [W][m4].
- **Check first:** [VERIFY: does the FareHarbor waiver collect each signer's email with a marketing opt-in? If it does, most of this list already exists and the QR is a bonus.] [VERIFY: photo release wording in the waiver.]
- **If the photo habit doesn't stick:** the same QR becomes a contest entry ("win this boat for your crew"). Same page, no crew work.
- **The number:** new emails per private boat. Bar: 4.

### #5 Chat and DM email ask

- **Entry:** every Nolan conversation on site chat, Messenger, and Instagram.
- **Steps:** a price question gets one line about the boat day and the per-person split (the splitter link, once #3 exists). Then one ask: "Want the NOLA Countdown? It's a local's guide to your trip, and there's $50 off a private boat in it." Email, date, size, and occasion go to the engine. Nudge v2 (45 minutes, then 20 hours) replaces the 3-minute "still there?" (as of 2026-08-28) [N]. After the Meta window closes, email takes over.
- **First 3 emails:**
  1. Instantly: "The boat for your crew of {group_size}." A recap of what they asked, the boat that fits, the split.
  2. +1 day: "Paste this in the group chat."
  3. +2 days: "How many days is enough? (and what it costs)". Then the Countdown runs as normal.
- **Build:** a prompt edit (a rule for the bare "How much" opener), an OpenCX action that posts to `/lead` with `source=chat` (needs build), approval of Nudge v2, and a check that the old 5-step DM drip is off [VERIFY].
- **Heads up:** the OpenCX connector for this brand failed to start in this session. Its config points at a path on another Mac. Fix that before anyone edits the bot.
- **The number:** share of non-email chats that leave an email. About 3% now (24 of 787). Bar: 12%.

## 4. What not to do

1. **No "tag a friend" or "share to enter."** It made the biggest post in the page's history (59,108 shares in 2021, as of 2026-06-20) [F]. Meta's promotion rules don't allow it as a way to enter [X]. The same readout says giveaways pull deal hunters when they run more than twice a month. The comment keyword does that job inside the rules.
2. **No texting in any of these, and leave the review text alone.** June's text blast: about 2,360 sent, 887 blocked by carriers, 22 STOPs (as of 2026-06-19) [m9]. Nothing texts until the toll-free or A2P verification clears [X]. The review text is the one text that lands (19% tap rate, with an A/B test running to mid-October) [m4]. A promo line in it puts that at risk.
3. **No "just checking in" messages, and no extra blasts to the past-guest list.** The 5-step DM drip sent 1,703 messages and got 0 replies (as of 2026-08-28) [G]. About 25,500 past guests got the same email twice in September [m8]. Every new funnel rides a scheduled send or builds its own small list.
