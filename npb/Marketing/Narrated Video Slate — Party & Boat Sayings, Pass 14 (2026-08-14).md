## What this is

12 new narrated short-form pieces (15–24 seconds each) built around real party-boat and eco-tour sayings — toasts, captain lines, bachelorette/birthday-group lines, and running jokes that happen out loud on the water. Format is modeled exactly on the Admire NOLA narrated series (`Admire NOLA/Content/Narrated/01-poetic-NOTES.txt` and `Admire NOLA/Content/Script & Caption Bank.md`): timestamped VO lines with a bracketed visual on every line, a named voice sample, a music bed, and a shot list.

Split 6/6 across NPB's two on-water products per the brief:
- **Private BYOB party fleet** — pieces 01–06
- **Naturalist eco swamp tour** (per-seat, wildlife-and-naturalist focus) — pieces 07–12

Every spoken line in every piece was drafted, then run through `/copy-mentors` (routed to whichever master fit the register — Sugarman's slippery-slide for the scenario/running-joke pieces, Halbert's one-person conversational voice for the dialogue-driven ones, Ogilvy's specific-over-clever for the poetic eco piece, Schwartz's reversal structure for the myth-vs-reality piece) and then `/copywriting-2.0` (the 3-test filter: can I visualize it, can I falsify it, can nobody else say it). What got cut is noted per piece below — mostly unfalsifiable superlatives ("best day they've had all year"), redundant scaffolding lines that explained a joke instead of landing it, and one line that invented a specific tour duration nobody confirmed.

This pass also spreads the crew roster out on purpose: pass 9 leaned on Captain Ben twice and didn't feature the deckhands by name. This pass gives Captain Eddie, Captain "Poppa" Charles, and deckhands Shannon, Darius, and Calvin each a piece built around their own moment, with Ben getting one appearance instead of two.

## Piece 01 — "Whose Toast Is It Anyway"
**Lane:** Funny / ritual · **Product:** Private BYOB party fleet
**Voice:** EL-Sarah (ElevenLabs) · **Music:** brass/bounce party bed, comedic pacing (Hot 8 Brass Band feel — clearance unconfirmed, commercial-library fallback for launch) · **Target:** 23.4s

```
 0.0s  Somebody raises a toast.   [wide shot: guest standing up on the bow, cup raised, group cheering]
 1.6s  Bad move.   [reaction shot: guests grinning, already reaching for their own cups]
 3.4s  Now everybody wants one.   [second guest hops up onto a cooler, mock-serious pose]
 5.6s  "Toast number four!" somebody yells.   [close-up: guest counting on fingers, cracking up]
 8.2s  Nobody remembers who started this.   [wide shot, whole group laughing, cups everywhere]
10.6s  Captain Eddie hasn't moved the boat in ten minutes.   [Eddie at the helm, arms crossed, deadpan]
13.4s  He's not stalling.   [close on Eddie's face, the corner of his mouth twitching]
15.2s  He's counting.   [Eddie's eyes flick toward the group]
17.0s  Toast number nine, and he still hasn't budged.   [wide shot: boat idle at the dock, skyline behind, group still going]
19.8s  Some captains run a schedule.   [Eddie checking his watch]
21.6s  Eddie runs a scoreboard.   [Eddie grinning straight at camera, cut to logo]
```

**On-screen text:** None over the narration — keep it clean and let the joke land in the read. "Toast number four!" and "Toast number nine" could get a quick bold caption pop for the punch, optional.
**Shot list:** Guest #1 toasting on bow → group reaction → guest #2 up on cooler → counting close-up → chaos wide → Eddie deadpan at helm → Eddie's smirk → Eddie's eyes flick → idle boat wide vs. skyline → watch check → Eddie grin/logo card.
**Casting/production note:** Needs a real Captain Eddie on camera for the deadpan reaction beats — five separate Eddie shots, so this piece lives or dies on his face.
**Copy passes:** copy-mentors (Sugarman slippery-slide + Halbert dryness) cut the original longer, literal lines ("Captain Eddie just wants to hit the water," "he never says stop") down to punchier fragments, since short beats read funnier aloud. Copywriting-2.0 kept every remaining line — the named specifics (Eddie, "toast number four/nine," "ten minutes") all visualize and can't be said about anyone else's boat.
