## 4. Content Gaps (5)

1. **`/swamp-tours/` pillar hub.** Doesn't exist. You rank pos 7 on the head term with the homepage. A dedicated 2,000+ word pillar page with H2s for "BYOB swamp tour," "closest to French Quarter," "with onboard bathrooms," "alligator sightings," "best time of year," "what to bring," "FAQ" would consolidate the swamp keyword cluster (728 + 733 + 435 + 33 + 33 = 1,962 monthly impressions across head + variants) into one ranking asset.

2. **`/bachelorette-party/` pillar hub.** Three thin pages compete with each other. None ranks better than position 11.8. Build one canonical pillar (2,500 words, FAQ schema, internal links to all boats), then 301 the weakest siblings into it.

3. **Sunset cruise comparison content.** `/sunset-cocktail-cruise-seafood-boil/` ranks pos 17 with only 531 imp. The Growth Plan's call for vs-Steamboat-Natchez and vs-Creole-Queen comparison pages is supported by the absence of competing comparison content in the SERP. Comparison search is high-intent and low-competition for a brand willing to write the actual comparison.

4. **Booze cruise hub.** `/new-orleans-booze-cruise/` ranks pos 7.1 with 1,481 impressions and 8 clicks. Page is underbuilt for the demand. Rewrite as a real hub for the BYOB/cocktail cruise category, link to Freaky Tiki, Twerkin Tiki, and the seafood boil cruise as the three product options.

5. **"Boat ride / boat rides New Orleans" content.** Five separate query variants (boat ride, boat rides, boat ride in NOLA, boat rides in NOLA, NOLA boat ride) pulling 1,500+ combined impressions, all positioning the homepage at pos 7-11. No dedicated page exists. A `/boat-rides-new-orleans/` page targeting "boat ride" intent specifically (vs "boat rental" which is a different page) would capture this cluster cleanly.

## 5. Top 3 Critical Issues

1. **Title tag rendering bug.** Documented in Action Plan section 2.1. GSC quantifies the cost: ~95,000 high-intent impressions per quarter ranking in positions 2-7 with sub-1% CTR. At normal CTR for those positions, that's 5,000-8,000 missed clicks per quarter. Single biggest fix on the site.

2. **www vs non-www canonical split.** Homepage is ranking as TWO separate URLs. www variant has 1,332 clicks at pos 5.6, non-www has 505 clicks at pos 11.4. Same page. Equity is splitting and Google isn't sure which to feature. This was flagged in Action Plan 1.1.4 as "high"; based on GSC data it should be "critical." A correctly canonicalized homepage could consolidate to a single page-1 result.

3. **Three commercial pages are orphans.** `/swamp-tours-with-onboard-restrooms/` (308 imp, the moat), `/sunset-cocktail-cruise-seafood-boil/` (531 imp, the premium product), and `/the-cajun-queen/` (686 imp, on page 2) all rank but get almost no internal links. Money pages with no inbound link equity will not move up. This is invisible in any single page audit but obvious in the GSC top-pages roster.
