# NOLA Party Barge — SEO Audit (Aug 15, 2026)

**Live-site state verified today** (title tags, schema, robots, og tags fetched from production). This is a delta on top of the Aug 11 audit — what got fixed, what's still bleeding, and the current 90-day GSC read.

---

## Executive Summary

The site is in a strong organic position and ranking despite two structural leaks. Since May, the big win landed: **the homepage `<title>` bug is fixed** — it now renders `Swamp Tour Party Boat in New Orleans | Nola Party Barges`, and meta descriptions are populated on the pages I checked. That was the single highest-ROI fix from spring, and it's done.

Three things are still costing real clicks right now:

1. **~82,000 impressions a quarter are wasted on two blog posts at <1% CTR.** The 2024-vacation-budget post (39,165 impressions, 0.92%) and the December-weather post (40,667 impressions, 0.39%) rank positions 4-5 and get almost no clicks. A title + meta rewrite on these two is the biggest near-term win on the site — normal CTR here is roughly +1,500 clicks/quarter for a couple hours of work.
2. **The "New Orleans Pedal Barge" brand ghost is still live at the template level.** Every product page title still ends in `| New Orleans Pedal Barge`, and `og:site_name` is `New Orleans Pedal Barge` sitewide. The homepage title got fixed but the product template didn't. This splits the entity signal and violates the no-pedal rule in the SERP itself.
3. **The money product pages carry no Product schema.** The Freaky Tiki page — the site's breakout hit — has only VideoObject + BreadcrumbList markup. No price, no rating, no offer. That's why resellers with 7 reviews get star + price rich snippets on the "party boat new orleans" SERP and NPB gets a plain blue link.

Underneath all that: the homepage is doing 100% of the commercial ranking. Fifteen-plus money keywords rank on `/`; the product pages rank for nothing but brand terms. And the bachelorette pages — highest commercial value on the site — are stranded at positions 21-29 because nothing links to them.
