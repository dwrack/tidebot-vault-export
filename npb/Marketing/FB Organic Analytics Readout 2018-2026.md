## Winning angles, ranked by what the data actually shows

1. **Weather / climate comfort** — "always cooler on the bayou," covered + heated boats, "75° inside." Most durable evergreen, works every season. (#2, #9, #16, #19, #20)
2. **Party / dance energy reels** — high-share video. (#6 2026, bride flip, twerk sunset)
3. **Proximity** — "15 minutes from the French Quarter." Recurs through every strong year.
4. **Giveaways / tag-a-friend** — the all-time peak reach mechanic, but juices vanity metrics and pulls deal-hunters. Use 1–2x/month, never daily. (#1, plus 2019 "WIN 6 SEATS" posts)
5. **POV / relatable** — "Another day at the office," "POV you're on a boat."
6. **Bachelorette / group**, **sunset**, **gators**.

## Format takeaway

Every recent top post is a **video reel**. Photos and link posts underperform video badly in the modern algorithm. The daily queue should be video-first.
