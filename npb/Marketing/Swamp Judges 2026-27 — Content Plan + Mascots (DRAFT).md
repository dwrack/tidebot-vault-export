## The idea in one line
Three swamp critters sit on a log in Bayou Bienvenue and score every boat party in America, 1 to 10. Ours included. Especially ours.

## Why a separate account
- Keeps the edgier party stuff off @nolapartybarge, so a strike or restriction can't touch the main account or the ads that run through it.
- It's a comedy page first. Anyone in the country can laugh at a gator giving a bachelorette a 4, so it can travel way past people planning a NOLA trip.
- The main account coasts at roughly 20 likes a post on 22k followers (Aug 19 readout). Personality posts beat party footage 10 to 1 there. This page is 100% personality.
- **The moat:** nobody else can put a real gator and a dance floor in the same frame. The judges live where our boats run.

Character rules:
- **The scorecards and captions carry every joke,** so every post works with the sound off. ElevenLabs voices go on top, one saved voice per judge, never changed.
- These are the only synthetic voices allowed. They're never used for ads, and never for anything that sounds like a real person. David's kids don't voice anyone on this page, since it carries 21+ party footage.
- **Label them as AI.** Turn on TikTok's AI-generated content label and Meta's "AI info" label on every Judges post. Meta counts realistic AI audio, so the ElevenLabs voices qualify.
- **Instagram originality is the biggest risk.** Since Apr 30, 2026, repost-heavy accounts stop getting recommended to non-followers. The judges, scorecards and voices are the content, and someone else's clip is only the prompt. Use Remix, never re-upload, and post an original Chambers skit or our own footage every week.
- **Punch up, never down.** Judges roast outfits, choices, the city, the heat, and each other. Never bodies, weight, race, or anyone who didn't choose to be on camera. They never score anyone under 21 or anyone who looks it.
- **No drinking scores.** Nobody gets points for how much they drank, for chugging, or for games. Drinks can sit in the frame, but they never become the joke.
- No safety claims, no promises about what the crew will do, and never "the captain will wait."
- Alfred lives in **Bayou Bienvenue**, not Bayou St. John. Fix the old YouTube Ep 1 script to match.
- Crossover with NKST: Beaux Bleu (the heron, a sax player) vs Alfred is a natural rivalry. Junior (Alfred's baby cousin) shows up once a month, but only on clean gator-only clips, never on party footage.

## How it feeds bookings
- **Collab posts** put our-boat Scorecards in front of @nolapartybarge's 22k followers and vice versa.
- The **bio link** and a pinned comment on our-boat posts ("the boat in this one: NOLA Party Barge, Bayou Bienvenue") send traffic with UTMs we can read in GA4 as `swampjudges`.
- **Retargeting:** once a Scorecard pulls views, its video viewers can join the existing "RT | Video Viewers 75%" pool that feeds the "Still Thinking" ad. That's a paid-side decision for later, and it only works if the post is on or Collab'd with a page in the ad account.
- The page never hard-sells: no prices in captions and no "book now." That lives on the main account and in the ads.

## Production
1. **Character art:** Higgsfield, one reference sheet per judge so they stay consistent. The Higgsfield connector needs authorization in claude.ai connector settings first. Otherwise, David makes the art in Higgsfield directly. Alfred has to look clearly different from NKST's Grandpa Earl (old and still) and Junior (baby).
2. **Voices:** ElevenLabs, one saved voice per judge, from David's account. The API key goes in through a hidden-prompt setup script on the Desktop, never pasted in chat.
3. **Overlays:** judge cutouts, scorecard pop-ups and caption bubbles on the clips, assembled with ffmpeg or David's CapCut.
4. **Audio:** if the account is set up as a business account, only "Approved for Business Use" sounds from TikTok's Commercial Music Library, plus the judges' own voices.
5. **Scheduling:** TikTok's built-in scheduler and Meta Business Suite. Each weekly batch waits for David's OK before anything is scheduled.
6. **Tracking:** add a `Swamp Judges Tracker.csv` next to the old Freaky Tiki tracker (views at 48h, shares, saves, profile visits, link clicks).
