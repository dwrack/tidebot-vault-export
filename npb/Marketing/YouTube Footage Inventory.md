## The honest read

Most of the vault library is low-res social re-downloads (640x360 and 720x406, Snaptik/FDownloader rips). The energy is great, the pixels are not. These work for Shorts with the blur-pad treatment, but none of it carries a long-form video. The real HD material is a short list, and the Drive archive has the best stuff: drone footage, swamp.mp4 at 101 MB, and a 2025 media folder full of 20-130 MB phone originals.

Rule of thumb going forward: always pull the ORIGINAL from the phone or Drive, never the version that already went through TikTok/Facebook compression.
