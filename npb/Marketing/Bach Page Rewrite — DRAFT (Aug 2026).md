## Page body (HTML-ready)

<p>Planning a New Orleans bachelorette party? Skip the hour-long van ride to a crowded swamp boat full of strangers. Book a private party barge for your crew instead: your playlist, your drinks, your people, and actual bayou with actual gators, 15 minutes from the French Quarter.</p>

<h2>The New Orleans Bachelorette Party Boat</h2>

<p>Here's how it works. You book one of our party boats for your whole group. We provide the captain, deckhand, sound system, and coolers. You bring the drinks, the snacks, and the playlist. It's BYOB for real: no $14 margaritas, no drink minimums, no upsell. The bride runs the show and we drive the boat.</p>

<p>You'll cruise the bayou for an hour and 45 minutes. Cypress trees, egrets, turtles, and yes, alligators sliding past while someone's popping champagne. This isn't a theme park loop. It's the real swamp, and it happens to be gorgeous in photos.</p>

<h2>Pick Your Boat</h2>

<p>Every boat in the fleet is private. When your group books it, nobody else gets on. No awkward small talk with a couple from Ohio while you're trying to celebrate your best friend.</p>

<li><strong><a href="https://nolapartybarges.com/the-twerkin-tiki/">The Twerkin' Tiki</a></strong> — up to 25 guests, same energy, its own personality.</li>

<li><strong>The Bayou Boogie</strong> — up to 18 guests, right-sized for a smaller crew.</li>
</ul>

<p>Only got a bride and three friends? You don't have to charter a whole boat. Grab per-seat tickets on our social party boat and join the fun with other travelers.</p>

<h2>Why Bachelorette Groups Pick Us</h2>

<p><strong>You're 15-20 minutes from your hotel.</strong> We're 7 miles from the French Quarter. The other swamp and party boats are out in Slidell or Lafitte, 45 minutes to an hour each way. When your group is already running behind schedule (they always are), that's the difference between making the dinner reservation and missing it.</p>

<p><strong>Bathrooms onboard.</strong> Ask anyone who's done a boat day: this matters more than anything else on this page.</p>

<p><strong>It photographs like a dream.</strong> Golden hour on the bayou, drinks up, bride in front. You've seen the pictures.</p>

<p><strong>Every captain is licensed, every boat is USCG certified.</strong> We keep everyone safe so nobody has to think about it.</p>

<h2>How to Plan It</h2>

<p>Most groups book the boat as the daytime anchor and build the night around it. Morning brunch, afternoon on the water, back at the hotel to freshen up by 6. If you want the full playbook, steal our <a href="https://nolapartybarges.com/a-locals-itinerary-for-a-new-orleans-bachelorette-party/">local's itinerary for a New Orleans bachelorette party</a>.</p>

<p>Bring: drinks, snacks, sunscreen, a speaker-ready playlist. We handle everything else.</p>

<h2>FAQs</h2>

<p><strong>Is it really BYOB?</strong> Yes. Bring whatever your group drinks. We provide the coolers.</p>

<p><strong>Will there be strangers on the boat?</strong> Not on a private charter. Your group only.</p>

<p><strong>How long is the cruise?</strong> 1 hour 45 minutes on the water.</p>

<p><strong>Will we see alligators?</strong> Usually, yes. It's the real bayou and they live here. Warm months are best.</p>

<p><strong>What if it rains?</strong> The boats are covered, and our captains make the weather call. If we cancel, you don't pay.</p>
