# Local Partner Outreach List

Non-competing New Orleans operators for NOLA Party Barge cross-referral partnerships. The play: mutual referrals + featuring them in a "NOLA group weekend" blog post that sends them traffic first, so the ask is a gift, not a favor. Referral kickback ~12% (half of Viator's cut) via a unique FareHarbor link/code per partner.

**No swamp tours, no boat tours, no on-the-water operators** (those compete). Walking tours, food tours, cooking classes, and low-competition group experiences only.

Contacts verified against each operator's own site or a reputable listing as of June 2026. `not found` = couldn't verify, don't guess. *(unverified)* = third-party listing only, confirm before use.

**Status key:** Not contacted / Contacted / Replied / Live partner / Pass
