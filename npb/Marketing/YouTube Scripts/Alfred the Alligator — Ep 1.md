## The character
Alfred is a grumpy old bull gator who's lived in Bayou St. John for 80 years and has watched every party boat float past his log. Dry, deadpan, secretly loves the chaos. Cajun drawl. Think Sam Elliott crossed with a swamp uncle. He narrates; he never moves his mouth, the captions and VO carry it.
