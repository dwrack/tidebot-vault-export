# Meta Ads: "Gator Collision" Creative (Sept 2026)

Built from a live Meta Ad Library pull on 2026-09-22 (the gap the July ad-research pass
couldn't fill), plus `Meta Ads -- Brand Constants.md`, the IG analytics readout, and the
historical ROAS benchmarks.

## What competitors are actually running right now

Live, active ads pulled 2026-09-22. Advertisers: Pontoon Saloon (Nashville), Cajun
Encounters, BrewBoat Cleveland, WakeLine Austin, Lake Travis Yacht Rentals, Pittsburgh
Party Boat Co, Tiki Pub Cincinnati, Cruisin' Tikis (Long Island + Holden Beach),
St Pete Coastal Cruises, Punta Gorda Adventures, Nashville Party Barge.

**1. BYOB is table stakes, not a differentiator.** Nine of eleven operators lead with it.
Pittsburgh, Cleveland, Austin, Cincinnati, Holden Beach, Punta Gorda, Long Island all
say "BYOB" in the first three lines. If our ad's main claim is BYOB, we're saying what
everyone says. Our moat is BYOB *plus gators* plus the 7-mile number.

**2. The emoji checklist format is saturated.** "✅ Private boat ✅ BYOB ✅ Bluetooth
speakers" appears near-identically across five operators. The two most sophisticated
advertisers in the set, Pontoon Saloon and Cajun Encounters, do the opposite: plain prose,
concrete nouns, zero emoji. That's the move.

**3. Everyone prices in the ad. We don't.** "From $35." "Starting at $399." "$25 with code
HAPPYHOUR." "From $99.99." "Save an average of $150." Price is also one of the most
repeated unanswered questions in our own IG comments. Putting a number in the ad is the
single cheapest fix available.

**4. Proximity contrast is the hook the good ones use.** Pontoon Saloon: "You can be ten
minutes from Broadway and feel nowhere near it." Cajun: "The big boats can't go where we
go." Cajun again: "be back in time for dinner." We own the strongest version of this claim
in the entire New Orleans market and barely use it.

**5. Objection-relief is live and working.** Cajun's whole summer flight is heat relief
("climate-controlled," "Without the Sweat"). Pontoon Saloon's is crowd relief. Our vault
research flagged objection-relief as the missing emotional lane. Confirmed: it's the lane
the best-funded competitor is spending in.

**6. Review counts get used as raw numbers.** "100,000+ five-star reviews." "Rated 5-stars
by 91,000+ travelers." We have 3,800+ at 4.9 and it appears in almost no current ad copy.

**7. Anti-pedal is a stated selling point.** BrewBoat Cleveland writes "(No pedaling
required on any boats)" directly in ad copy. Confirms the no-pedal rule and suggests
there's an active differentiator in it.

**8. The wedge: nobody can say both.** Cajun Encounters owns gators in this market and
structurally cannot market drinking (alcohol is not permitted on their boats). Every party
boat operator nationwide owns BYOB and has no wildlife to show. We are the only operator
who can put an alligator and a dance floor in the same frame.

**9. The lane is empty.** An Ad Library search for New Orleans + swamp + boat +
bachelorette returns "No ads match." Nobody is running paid creative against the exact
intersection we sell.

## Hero ad: "Twenty Feet From The Dance Floor"

Format: vertical video, 9:16. Objective: Sales / Purchase. Never Traffic (0.18x ROAS).

### Primary text

> There's an alligator about twenty feet from the dance floor right now.
>
> That's the ad. That's the whole thing.
>
> Most swamp tours out here are a two hour round trip and you need a rental car to get
> there. We're seven miles from the French Quarter, off Paris Road. Fifteen minute Uber,
> and you're back before dinner.
>
> Bring whatever you're drinking. Plug in your own playlist. The captain finds the gators,
> you handle everything else.
>
> An hour and forty five minutes on the bayou. Covered, so a rainy forecast doesn't cancel
> your afternoon. Bathroom on board, which no other party boat in New Orleans can say.
>
> 4.9 stars, 3,800+ reviews, and the line that keeps showing up is "best part of our trip."
>
> $63 a seat, or take the whole boat for your group at $1,250. Pick a date and we'll see
> you at the dock.

### Headline
`Gators. Your playlist. 15 minutes.`

### Description
`$63 a seat. BYOB on the bayou. 4.9 stars, 3,800+ reviews.`

### CTA button
`Book Now`

> **Pricing confirmed 2026-09-22** from the live Freaky Tiki page: **$63 per person**
> (public cruise, ages 21+) and **$1,250 private charter** up to 25 passengers, ages 6+.
> Note the accuracy trap: $63 is the *public* seat, where you share the boat with other
> groups. "Private boat, your group only" is the $1,250 product. Don't pair the private
> claim with the $63 number, the ad has to carry both or it misrepresents the booking.

## Video script (0:00 to 0:22)

Cut every 0.8 to 1.5 seconds. Burned-in text, Alegreya Sans SC 700, white with dark drop
shadow, minimum 48px equivalent. Sound on optional, the text carries it.

| Time | Shot | Burned-in text |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00-0:03 | Gator sliding through water, close, real, handheld | THERE'S A GATOR 20 FEET AWAY |
| 0:03-0:05 | Hard whip-pan to the boat, group mid-dance, drinks up | FROM THIS |
| 0:05-0:08 | Wide of the barge on the bayou, cypress, gold light | 7 miles from the French Quarter |
| 0:08-0:11 | Phone plugging into the aux, someone reacting to the song | Your playlist |
| 0:11-0:14 | Cooler opening, cans, someone handing a drink over | Your drinks |
| 0:14-0:17 | Captain pointing at the bank, everyone turning to look | Your captain finds the gators |
| 0:17-0:20 | Group shot, everyone laughing, covered roof visible | 1 hr 45 min. Rain or shine. |
| 0:20-0:22 | Logo on coral gradient, pill CTA | 4.9 stars. Book it. |

**Footage sources:** `NPB video dump/`, `Assets/Videos/`, `Assets/Shorts/`. The gator
footage from the YouTube "Actual Alligators" cut is the highest-value asset we own and it
is the open frame.

**Do not use:** marshmallow or hand-feeding footage (eco-conscious buyers actively avoid
feeding operators, and it clashes with the naturalist product). Champagne guns as the hero
moment (zero supporting review evidence, keep it as a VIP-tier detail).

> 4.9 stars, 3,800+ reviews.
>
> Headline: `The part of NOLA that isn't Bourbon St`

## Targeting

Put the hero into `NPB | Purchase | TOF Prospecting` (`6976231037096`).

- **Nightlife & Bars** first. This is the proven winner at 5.74x ROAS from the Party Bike
  Bar campaign. Parties, Bars, Nightclubs, Nightlife, Happy hour, Upcoming birthday.
  Age 24-45, New Orleans 50mi.
- **Bachelorette & Weddings** second. Age 24-38, female, NOLA 50mi plus feeder cities
  (Dallas, Houston, Atlanta, Nashville, Birmingham, Memphis).
- Feed the retargeting campaign (`6976231067696`) with the video viewers. Retargeting has
  run 30-63x and it starves without top-of-funnel video.

**Do not** run this as a carousel. Carousel-C01 did 2.4x against video's 4.5%+ CTR.
