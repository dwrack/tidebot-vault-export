## The 5 topics

1. **Seven miles from the Quarter** — proximity is the reason half of guests book us
2. **BYOB and you run the show** — control of the vibe, the playlist, the drinks
3. **Which boat did you book** — private fleet vs social per-seat vs eco tour. Three products, constantly confused.
4. **Group planning pain** — bachelorettes, birthdays, the person stuck organizing it
5. **The bayou itself** — alligators sliding past while someone pops champagne

## The 3 formats

- **Comparison** (easy) — three products, and us vs the hour-drive swamp tour
- **Scenario / skit** (easy for this brand, the energy is already there) — the group chat, the planner, the guy who brings one seltzer
- **Guest reaction** (ambitious) — needs real captures, highest converting

## The 2 layouts

- **A — On-boat, handheld, loud.** Match the product. Polish would be wrong here.
- **B — Text-on-screen over guest UGC / b-roll.** Sound off, high contrast.

## Cover type

Loud script or bold neon, high saturation. Competing against nightlife content, so it has to be brighter than the feed.
