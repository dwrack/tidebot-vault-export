## What this is

12 new narrated short-form pieces (22.5–27.5 seconds each) built around real party-boat and eco-tour sayings — toasts, captain lines, bachelor/bachelorette and birthday-group lines, and running jokes that happen out loud on the water. Format is modeled exactly on the Admire NOLA narrated series (`Admire NOLA/Content/Narrated/01-poetic-NOTES.txt` and `Admire NOLA/Content/Script & Caption Bank.md`): timestamped VO lines with a bracketed visual on every line, a named voice sample, a music bed, and a shot list.

Split 6/6 across NPB's two on-water products per the brief:
- **Private BYOB party fleet** — pieces 01–06
- **Naturalist eco swamp tour** (per-seat, wildlife-and-naturalist focus) — pieces 07–12

Every spoken line was drafted, then run through `/copy-mentors` (routed to whichever master fit the piece's shape — Ogilvy's restraint for the sensitive/plain-fact pieces, Sugarman's slippery-slide for the story-build and running-joke pieces, Halbert's one-person conversational specificity for the character-and-dialogue pieces, Bencivenga's structural reveal for the twist pieces, Schwartz's reversal for the expectation-vs-reality pieces) and then `/copywriting-2.0` (the 3-test filter: can I visualize it, can I falsify it, can nobody else say it). What got cut or rewritten is noted per piece below — mostly unfalsifiable therapy-speak and corporate-adjacent lines ("healing takes many forms," "it's the little things that matter most," "there's no wrong answer"), one closer that drifted into the poetic lane's territory when this pass was deliberately keeping that lane light, and — twice — a specific elapsed-time or duration claim that isn't actually confirmed for the eco tour and got replaced with something vaguer but still true.

This pass leans on three real, previously-unused ideas already sitting in this vault instead of inventing new rituals from nothing: the maid-of-honor intel call (`Ideas/Unreasonable Hospitality — 100 Ideas for NPB.md`, idea #44), the bachelorette survival kit (idea #42), and the bride-goes-last dock ritual (idea #49). None of the three have been built into a content piece across any prior pass. It also deliberately introduces two occasion types the first three passes never touched — a divorce party and a bachelor party — both explicitly valid per Brand Story & Values Section 2 ("Every occasion is valid... bachelor parties, family reunions, divorce parties, 'we just felt like it' Saturdays").

## Piece 01 — "The Divorce Party Toast"
**Lane:** Funny / heartfelt, ritual · **Product:** Private BYOB party fleet
**Voice:** EL-Sarah (ElevenLabs) · **Music:** brass/bounce party bed, warm not sarcastic (clearance unconfirmed) · **Target:** 25.0s

```
 0.0s  A group books "just us girls." Captain doesn't ask why.                  [Captain Ben helping four women board, easy welcome, no forced small talk]
 3.0s  Turns out it's Maria's divorce being finalized Monday.                   [close-up: Maria laughing, friends around her, sunglasses on]
 6.2s  She wanted a boat, not a bar.                                            [wide shot: group settling onto deck, drinks poured]
 8.8s  Captain pours the first round himself.                                   [Ben pouring drinks, handing them out]
11.2s  "To signing the papers and not the check."                               [Ben raising a cup, group laughing, on-screen text overlay of quote]
14.6s  Nobody flinches. Nobody asks for the story.                              [group toasting together, cups raised, genuine smiles]
17.6s  Some parties celebrate what's starting. This one celebrates what's over. [wide shot: sunset, group dancing, cups still raised]
21.4s  Either way, we've got the boat.                                          [closing shot: boat cruising, golden light]
```

**On-screen text:** quote card on "To signing the papers and not the check" only, nothing else — let the rest read plain.
**Shot list:** boarding (no ring, no dwelling on it) → Maria close-up laughing → wide group settling in → Ben pouring → toast quote → group toast reaction → sunset dancing → closing cruise shot.
**Casting/production note:** this business explicitly lists divorce parties among the occasions it's proud to host (Brand Story & Values, Section 2), so this isn't a stretch — it's an underused true thing about the brand.
**Copy passes:** copy-mentors (Ogilvy — restraint, don't oversell the emotion) kept the joke plain and refused to make the divorce itself the punchline. Copywriting-2.0 cut a line reading "healing takes many forms" (unfalsifiable therapy-speak) in favor of "Some parties celebrate what's starting. This one celebrates what's over," which says the same thing without editorializing.
**PRODUCTION FLAG:** this uses a real guest's real occasion — a finalized divorce is genuinely sensitive personal information. Do not shoot this unless a specific real guest has given explicit, enthusiastic consent to being filmed and referenced (even by first name) in connection with their divorce, separate from the standard booking-waiver media consent. If no willing guest exists when this gets greenlit, this ships as a general illustration with no specific name or reveal, not a claim about one real trip.
