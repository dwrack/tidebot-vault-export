## What else belongs on it (my recommendations, in order of payoff)

1. **Link-back requirement with copy-paste credit + HTML embed.** This is the real SEO play. One tap copies a credit line already containing the link. Most people won't hand-type it.
2. **Boilerplate paragraphs** (50 and 150 words) writers can paste verbatim. Cuts wrong facts in other people's articles.
3. **Pre-written captions per photo.** Gets our keywords ("party boat New Orleans," "swamp tour") into their alt text and captions.
4. **A short video reel** (b-roll, 30 to 60 sec). Local TV and bloggers ask for this first. We have footage in Assets/Videos and Raw Footage.
5. **Quotable stats and angles:** a few "story hooks" (bachelorette capital of the South, only BYOB party boats near the swamps, etc). Only with facts we can back up.
6. **Awards / reviews / "as seen in" strip** once there are 3+ real mentions. Social proof for the next writer.
7. **Press-release archive.** Even one release (Climate Culture tie-in is queued) makes the page look alive.
8. **Seasonal story ideas:** Halloween, Mardi Gras, Saints game days, holiday parties, Tales of the Cocktail 2027.
9. **Partner / event-organizer section.** Krewes, festivals, hotels asking to use our photos is the same use case. Link to a "request a custom shot" form.
10. **Mention tracking:** page URL tagged ?utm_source=press so GA4 shows what the page drives, and a simple "tell us when you publish" line so we can reshare.
