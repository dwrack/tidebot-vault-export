# Newsletter relaunch: a New Orleans email people want

**The idea (David, 10/15):** run it like City Cast. Each email is a short, fun guide to what's good in New Orleans right now. The boat shows up naturally as one of the good things, not as the whole pitch.

**Why:** w10 got 372 clicks from 42,849 delivered (0.87%) and no sales we can see. The old format was 3 lines plus a Book button, which gives people nothing to read.

## Format (every issue)

| Section | What | Length |
|---|---|---|
| Subject | Specific and a little funny, names real things happening | 6-9 words |
| Opener | 2 lines in the crew's voice, what's going on out here this week | 2 sentences |
| **This week in New Orleans** | 4-5 real events with day, date, place and a one-line take | 1 line each |
| **Crew pick** | One spot a captain or deckhand actually goes to, and why | 2-3 sentences |
| **Out on the bayou** | What the water's doing this season: birds, gators, sunsets, weather | 2 sentences |
| **Plan it around the boat** | How a boat fits with one of the events above. The one booking link. | 2-3 sentences |
| Sign-off | The crew, no owner names | 1 line |

**Rules**
- Every event is checked against its own site the week it sends, with dates written out. We don't run anything we haven't verified. The closed-places list in `NOLA Spots + Mardi Gras 2027 Krewes — Research (2026-09-30).md` is the "don't promote" list.
- At most one booking push per issue. The rest is just good info.
- Use the full business name, never "NPB." Don't mention pedaling.
- Crew picks come from Jeff, JT and the captains: one question a week, answered in a text or voice note.
- Fold in a reel or guest photo only when it fits the issue.

**Name (pick one):** The Bayou Dispatch / This Week on the Water / Fifteen Minutes from the Quarter

**Cadence:** keep the existing Tuesday slots (10/20, 10/27, 11/17), then every other Tuesday through Mardi Gras. The same sections feed the new-lead drip once it's built.

**Subject:** A parade for ghosts, a film about Irma Thomas, and your boat
**Preview:** What's good in New Orleans the next two weeks, from the crew on the bayou.

Hey {first},

The heat finally broke out here. Mornings on the bayou are cool enough for a hoodie, and the sunsets have gone orange and early. This is the good stretch.

Here's what's worth your time in New Orleans over the next couple weeks.

**THIS WEEK IN NEW ORLEANS**

- **New Orleans Film Festival, Thu Oct 22 to Tue Oct 27.** Screenings at the Contemporary Arts Center, the Prytania and The Broad. It opens with *Victory*, a documentary about Irma Thomas, the Soul Queen of New Orleans. Go to that one.
- **Krewe of BOO!, Sat Oct 24, around 6:30pm.** The city's official Halloween parade, with floats built by the same shop that builds the big Mardi Gras ones. It runs from the Marigny through the Quarter and it's free. Bring a bag for throws.
- **Zombie Run, Sat Oct 24, 9am.** Two miles from Lucy's in the Warehouse District, in costume. Gets your steps in before the parade.
- **Oktoberfest at Deutsches Haus, Fri Oct 23 and Sat Oct 24.** Brats, oompah and steins, New Orleans style.
- **Halloween on a Saturday, Oct 31.** The best possible night of the week for it. Frenchmen Street turns into one big costume party with brass bands on the corners.
- **Coming up:** Beignet Fest Sat Nov 7, then the Oak Street Po-Boy Festival Sun Nov 8. Pace yourself.
