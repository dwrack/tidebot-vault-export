# Gator Collision: test variants B, C, D

Three copy variants to run against the hero ad. Same video on all four, different copy.
That is deliberate: if the creative changes too, you cannot tell whether the copy or the
footage moved the number.

Ad names, so the reporting reads cleanly:

- `NPB | Planner's Win | 9x16 video`
- `NPB | Weather Kill | 9x16 video`
- `NPB | Anti-Bourbon | 9x16 video`

All three use the same settings as the hero: Sales objective, Purchase event, GT Pixel,
New Orleans Party Barge / @nolapartybarge, landing page
`nolapartybarges.com/the-freaky-tiki/`, CTA **Book Now**.

---

## B. The Planner's Win

Organizer status. The brief flagged this as a lane the Creative Matrix has never tested:
the existing bachelorette angle sells what the bride remembers, not what the person who
books it gets out of it.

**Primary text**

```
You're the one who booked it, so you're the one who gets the credit.

Private boat on the bayou, seven miles from the French Quarter. Everybody brings what they want to drink, somebody hooks up the playlist, and the captain goes and finds you an alligator. An hour and forty five minutes, and you're back before dinner.

Nobody's going to remember which bar you went to. They'll remember this.

Whole boat for your group, $1,250 for up to 25. Or $63 a seat if it's a smaller crew.
```

**Headline**

```
You'll look like a genius for this
```

**Description**

```
Private BYOB boat on the bayou. 4.9 stars.
```

## C. The Weather Kill

Objection relief. There is no weather angle anywhere in the Creative Matrix, and weather is
the third most repeated pre-booking hesitation in the research, with a ready-made reversal
sitting in the review bank.

**Primary text**

```
"We were nervous with the rain but the boat was fully covered and we stayed dry and dancing the whole time."

That's Sara C., and she's the reason we don't cancel on a forecast.

Covered roof. Heated in winter. Bathroom on board, which no other party boat in New Orleans can say. And we're seven miles from the French Quarter, so you're not burning a rental car and half a day getting out to the swamp.

Bring your own drinks, plug in your own playlist, let the captain find the gators.

$63 a seat, or $1,250 for the whole boat. Book the date and let the weather do what it does.
```

**Headline**

```
Rain doesn't cancel this
```

**Description**

```
Covered, heated, BYOB. 4.9 stars, 3,800+ reviews.
```

> Sara C.'s quote is real, from the Batch review bank in `Meta Ads -- Brand Constants.md`.
> Do not edit the wording. If you want it shorter, cut the whole quote rather than
> paraphrasing it into something she did not say.

## D. The Anti-Bourbon

Identity play. This is the lane that matches what actually performs organically: the humor
and POV posts beat party footage roughly 10x on Instagram, and the account's all-time
monster is a pure personality reel with zero sell.

**Primary text**

```
You've done Bourbon Street. You did it hard. You have the photos and you have the regret.

Here's the other thing.

Private boat on the bayou, fifteen minutes out. Your group only. Your drinks, your music, and actual alligators about twenty feet off the rail. Nobody hands you a plastic cup of anything.

An hour and forty five minutes, then you're back in time for dinner.

4.9 stars, 3,800+ reviews. $63 a seat, $1,250 for the whole boat.
```

**Headline**

```
The NOLA that isn't Bourbon St
```

**Description**

```
Private BYOB boat, 15 min from the Quarter.
```

## Reading the results

Four ads in one ad set is not a clean test. Meta will find one ad early and push most of
the delivery into it, so the other three may never collect enough impressions to judge.
Two ways to handle that:

- **Accept it.** Treat it as "let Meta pick the winner." Fine if you mostly want the best
  performer rather than knowing why it won.
- **Or split them.** One ad per ad set, same targeting, and let each get its own budget.
  That costs more per read but gives four comparable numbers.

Either way, give it seven days before you judge anything. At $40/day and a ~$140-155
average booking value, a few days is noise.
