## What this is

12 new narrated short-form pieces (19.0-23.5 seconds each) built around real party-boat and eco-tour sayings: the things guests actually say out loud on the water, plus a couple of genuine New Orleans phrases guests pick up along the way. Format is modeled exactly on the Admire NOLA narrated series (`Admire NOLA/Content/Narrated/01-poetic-NOTES.txt` and `Admire NOLA/Content/Script & Caption Bank.md`): timestamped VO lines with a bracketed visual on every line, a named voice sample, a music bed, and a shot list.

This pass leans away from crew-scripted one-liners and toward things guests themselves say, notice, or start. Where the brief asked for "party sayings, boat sayings, toasts, captain lines," this pass deliberately underweights toasts and captain lines specifically (both are saturated after six passes, see Status flags above) and leans into running jokes, adopted phrases, and the small verbal rituals guests build without any crew prompting.

Split 7/5 across NPB's two on-water products, matching the ratio used in most prior passes:
- **Private BYOB party fleet** — pieces 01-07
- **Naturalist eco swamp tour** — pieces 08-12

Every spoken line was drafted, then routed through `/copy-mentors` (routed to whichever master fit the piece's shape) and then `/copywriting-2.0` (the 3-test filter: can I visualize it, can I falsify it, can nobody else say it). What got cut or rewritten is noted per piece below. The recurring pattern this pass: unfalsifiable absolutes ("nobody's ever not clapped," "that's the whole trick") got cut and replaced with something specific and checkable, and one early draft implied guests physically throwing items between boats, cut for the same unconfirmed-activity reason Pass 22 cut its cannonball line.
