## What this is

12 new narrated short-form pieces (19.5–25.5 seconds each) built around real party-boat and eco-tour sayings — toasts, captain lines, bachelorette/birthday/corporate/family-reunion-group lines, and running jokes that happen out loud on the water. Format is modeled exactly on the Admire NOLA narrated series (`Admire NOLA/Content/Narrated/01-poetic-NOTES.txt` and `Admire NOLA/Content/Script & Caption Bank.md`): timestamped VO lines with a bracketed visual on every line, a named voice sample, a music bed, and a shot list.

Split 6/6 across NPB's two on-water products per the brief:
- **Private BYOB party fleet** — pieces 01–06
- **Naturalist eco swamp tour** (per-seat, wildlife-and-naturalist focus) — pieces 07–12

Every spoken line was drafted, then run through `/copy-mentors` (routed to whichever master fit the piece's shape — Ogilvy's restraint for the plain-fact and ritual pieces, Sugarman's slippery-slide for the story-build and running-joke pieces, Halbert's one-person specificity for the overheard-line pieces, Bencivenga's structural twist/parallel-repetition for the reveal pieces, Schwartz's reversal for the expectation-vs-reality pieces) and then `/copywriting-2.0` (the 3-test filter: can I visualize it, can I falsify it, can nobody else say it). What got cut or rewritten is noted per piece below — mostly unfalsifiable absolutes ("works every time," "they'll actually remember this"), one unconfirmed historical claim about the age of a phrase, one line drifting into the poetic lane's territory (this pass deliberately carries no poetic entry, same call Pass 16 made), and lines that over-explained a joke the visual already landed.

This pass leans on three real, previously-unused ideas already sitting in this vault instead of inventing every hook from nothing: the crew intro-monologue device (`Ideas/Unreasonable Hospitality — 100 Ideas for NPB.md`, idea #18 → Piece 05), the executive/team acknowledgment toast (idea #58 → Piece 03), and the plain, non-pushy review ask (idea #36 → Piece 06). None of the three had been built into a content piece across any prior pass.
