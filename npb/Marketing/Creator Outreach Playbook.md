# Creator Outreach Playbook: NOLA Party Barge

**Owner:** Jeffrey Chitek
**Business Instagram:** @nolapartybarge (or current handle)

---

## What This Is

Proactive outreach to local content creators, offering them a free cruise in exchange for nothing. No contract, no posting requirements, no talking points. If they make something cool, great. If they just have a good time on the water, that's fine too.

This is different from the UGC Repost Playbook (which is reactive, finding content that already exists). This is about building relationships with creators before they've ever been on your boat.

## Why This Works for NOLA Party Barge

We sell the experience creators want to film. A BYOB party boat with live music on the water in New Orleans. That's content. The energy, the music, the skyline, the group vibe: it all films itself. We're not asking anyone to stage something. We're inviting them into a party that's already happening.

The live music angle is unique. Most party boats play a Bluetooth speaker. We have actual musicians on board. That's a differentiator creators notice immediately.

The cost is nearly zero. An empty spot on a cruise that's going out anyway costs us nothing. The content a creator produces from that spot would cost $500-2,000 to produce ourselves.

## Who to Target

### The Sweet Spot: Micro-Influencers (2K-50K followers)

Don't chase big accounts. You want local creators who:
- Live in or frequently visit New Orleans
- Post nightlife, party, bachelorette, couples, or city-life content
- Have real engagement (comments from real people, not bot spam)
- Show original storytelling, not just lip-syncs and trending audio reposts
- Would genuinely enjoy a party boat

## How to Reach Out

### DM from the Business Instagram Account

Not your personal account. Not email. A direct message from the business page.

### First DM: Cold Outreach

Keep it short. No pitch deck energy.

**Template A: Simple invite:**

> Hey [name]! Love your content, especially [reference a specific recent post]. We run party boats with live music here in New Orleans, BYOB, great group vibe on the water. We'd love to have you out on a cruise. No strings, no posting requirements, just come have a good time. Want us to set something up?

**Template B: For travel creators:**

> Hey [name]! Saw you're hitting New Orleans soon. We run BYOB party boats with live musicians on the water. It's basically a floating party with a great view. We'd love to have you out while you're here. Free, no catch. Let us know if you want us to hold a spot.

**Template C: For creators who posted about a competitor or similar experience:**

> Hey [name]! Saw your [post about competitor/similar activity]. We do something a little different here: live music on the boat, BYOB, smaller crowd, actual vibe. Would love to have you out to see the difference. On us, no strings. Interested?

### Rules for Outreach
- **Reference something specific** about their content. "Love your content" alone sounds automated.
- **Don't ask them to post.** The moment you say "in exchange for a post," it becomes transactional and the content suffers.
- **One follow-up max.** If they don't reply after 3-5 days, send one casual follow-up. After that, move on.

**Follow-up DM (if no reply after 5 days):**

> Hey just bumping this in case it got buried. No pressure either way!

### If They Say Yes

> Awesome! When works for you? We run cruises [days/times]. Pick whatever works and I'll hold a spot. You can bring a friend or two if you want.

Then:
- Hold the spot, confirm the day before
- Let the captain/musician know a creator is coming (just so they're aware)
- After the cruise, send a quick thank you DM: "Hope you had a good time! No ask from us, just glad you came out."

### If They Post Something

- Like it immediately
- Leave a genuine comment (something specific about their content, not "Thanks for sharing!")
- Repost using the UGC Playbook process (ask permission, download, repost with tag)
- Flag top performers for ads per the UGC playbook
- Add them to your "relationship" tier for future invites

### If They Don't Post

That's fine. Don't follow up asking where their post is. The relationship still exists. They had a good time, they'll mention you organically at some point, and you can invite them back for a special event later.

### Relationship Tiers

- **One-timers**: Came, enjoyed it, maybe posted, no ongoing relationship. That's fine.
- **Repeat creators**: Loved it, posted, want to come back. Invite them for special cruises, holiday events, live music showcases.
- **Brand friends**: Genuinely love what you do, mention you unprompted, bring friends. These are gold. Give them first access to everything.
