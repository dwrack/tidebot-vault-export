# NOLA Party Barge: SEO Audit (2026-10-31)

Read-only audit. Nothing on the site, GBP or ads was changed. Live site fetched 2026-10-31 (all 135 URLs in the three sitemaps: 31 pages, 13 activities, 91 posts). GSC pulled 2026-10-31 for the 90 days ending 2026-10-29.

## Executive summary

Brand and "Freaky Tiki / Twerkin Tiki" queries are won (position 1 to 3). Two things from the Aug 15 audit are now FIXED: product page titles end in "NOLA Party Barge" (no more "| New Orleans Pedal Barge") and `og:site_name` is "New Orleans Party Barge" on all 135 URLs. Three leaks remain:

1. **Generic boat queries sit at positions 7 to 9 with sub-1% CTR.** Seven "boat ride / boat tour new orleans" variants add up to about 10,400 impressions and 67 clicks (0.6%). No page is built for that intent; the homepage is titled "Swamp Tour Party Boat" and has no "boat tour" or "boat ride" language in the title.
2. **No Product / Offer / AggregateRating / FAQ schema on any URL.** Money pages carry only BreadcrumbList + VideoObject (homepage has LocalBusiness). GBP shows 3,700 reviews at 4.9; none of it is machine-readable on the product pages.
3. **72 of 91 blog posts have zero internal links in from any page (nav, grid or body), and the product pages only get links from the site-wide nav and a template "activity grid".** `/new-orleans-booze-cruise/` and `/pedal-bike-bar/` have 0 links in from any page. The December-weather post alone drew 76,272 impressions for 201 clicks and passes almost nothing on.
