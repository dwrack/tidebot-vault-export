## 2. Formats that fit a party boat

### Hot right now (Aug 2026)

| Format | Mechanic | Sound | Freaky Tiki version |
|---|---|---|---|
| Netflix Documentary | Confessional chair, text "Me when Netflix makes a documentary about [niche]" | Duomo "On a Mission" | "...about the captain of a tiki boat full of bachelorettes" |

| Paparazzi ugly-to-hot | Worst angle, hard cut on drop | Gaga "Paparazzi" dubstep | Dock pre-drink vs hour 2 |
| "It's a clock" | Lip-sync calling out the obvious | Hunger Games audio | "Why is every bach group at the same 3 bars"... cut to boat |
| Repeat twice, reveal third | Same failed action twice, third swap | timed audio | Two tries at champagne gun, third the gator "reacts" |

Most trends peak in 7-10 days; format trends outlast sound trends. Business accounts must use "Approved for Business Use" audio. "Mind Blank" (OTE feat. Zorro) is a trending sound brands can use.

### Evergreen travel formats
- POV / "When you..." text over footage
- "Here's what no one tells you about visiting New Orleans"
- "I almost didn't book this..."
- Expectation vs reality, GRWM, day-in-the-destination, "what I spent in NOLA"
- "Tell me you're in NOLA without telling me" (no dominant NOLA version exists, it's open)
- Format plus personal asset: tiki boat, champagne gun, live gators, captain character, 25 screaming bachelorettes. No production value needed; small accounts broke out with static phone shots.
- Proof in frame: gator / champagne spray / crowd in the FIRST frame.

## 3. Comparable accounts

Brand videos get 60-100 likes. The viral stuff is GUEST UGC: "WE WENT ON A PARTY PONTOON IN NASHVILLE, byob, best energy to start a night out #nashvillepartyboat." Lesson: make guests post, hand them the hashtag, make crew the recurring characters.

**Batch (@letsbatch):** "Bachelorette Playlist on a Pontoon" ~5.7K likes. Batch already lists "The Freaky Tiki Boat Party Fiesta (BYOB)" as a NOLA experience. Distribution partner.

**Miami:** unlimited drinks + DJ hype reels. #bachelorettepartymiami averages 90K views/post on 66 posts.

**Lake Havasu / Travis:** what blew up was a boat crash POV. "Gone wrong" out-travels "went right" (#boatdaygonewrong 91K avg vs #boatday 11.8K).

**Cruisin' Tikis (Tampa):** the direct product comp. Caption formula: "Explore Tampa Bay on a floating tiki bar. Call (727) 477-TIKI to book! #tampa #tikiboat". Phone in every caption.

**Louisiana swamp tours (the gator pillar):** every viral hit is a gator doing something unexpected: climbing onto the airboat for marshmallows, guide jumping in to feed gators, "They said it's just a baby. Baby WHERE?!" Named guides (Cajun Dan, Captain Bebop) drive it. #alligatorsoftiktok averages 94K views/post.

**Own account:** @nolapedalbarge posts like "Would you rather see an alligator or Dance? On the Freaky Tiki you can do both #thingstodoinneworleans 504-264-1056". Right instinct, wrong handle.

## 4. Positioning

### Core line
**"The swamp tour that turns into a party."** Swamp tour (20k/mo Google, 8B gator views) x bachelorette (3.5B views). Nashville has no gators, Miami has no swamp.

### 5 pillars
1. **Gator reactions** (reach engine). Guest-side screams, champagne gun mid-spray when the gator surfaces. #alligatorsoftiktok #gator #swamp.
2. **Bachelorette chaos** (conversion engine). Boarding, first champagne blast, dance floor at hour 2, bride moments. Plus "bachelorette tips" posts (#bachelorettetips 121K avg on 483 posts).
3. **Captain / crew character** (retention engine). Named captain + deckhand, recurring bits.
4. **NOLA not-Bourbon** (local SEO). "Things to do in New Orleans," "Bourbon at 2pm vs this."
5. **Meme remix** (1/week max). Whatever format is peaking, shot on the boat.

### 10 hooks
1. "POV: your bachelorette booked the swamp tour that turns into a party"
2. "Tell me you're in New Orleans without telling me"
3. "Bourbon Street at 2pm vs. this"
4. "They said it's just a baby. Baby WHERE?!"
5. "Things the captain has to say every single Saturday"
6. "Me when Netflix makes a documentary about driving a tiki boat full of bachelorettes"
7. "Expectation: a peaceful swamp tour. Reality:"
8. "3 things every New Orleans bachelorette group gets wrong"
9. "Seeing how our captain responds when someone asks if we'll see a gator"
10. "We let the bride fire the champagne gun. We regret nothing."
Bonus: "Would you rather see an alligator or dance? Here you do both."

### Hashtags (3-5 per post: 1-2 broad, 1-2 niche, 1 own)
- Broad: #neworleans #nola #louisiana #bachelorette #girlstrip #alligator #swamp
- Niche: #thingstodoinneworleans #swamptour #tikiboat #boozecruise #boatparty #bacheloretteparty #bachelorettetrip #bachelorettetips #bachelorettepartyideas #gator #alligatorsoftiktok #birthdaytrip #cajun
- Own (claim now, empty): #nolabachelorette #neworleansbachelorette #nolapartyboat #tikiboatneworleans #freakytiki
- Skip: #fyp #foryou #viral

Per pillar:
- Gator: #alligatorsoftiktok #swamp #neworleans #swamptour #freakytiki
- Bach: #nolabachelorette #bachelorette #bachelorettepartyideas #neworleans #freakytiki
- Local: #thingstodoinneworleans #nola #neworleanshiddengems #tikiboat #freakytiki

### Cadence
- 4-5 posts/week: 1 gator, 2 bach/hype, 1 crew, 1 trend remix when one fits.
- Mon-Thu 4-6pm CT, plus one Sat 11am-1pm clip shot live that morning.
- Bach-heavy Dec-Mar. Gator/summer-heavy Jun-Sep. Push hard through Labor Day.
- Batch film: one Saturday = 10+ clips. Consent at boarding (sign at dock + verbal).
- UGC loop: hashtag + phone printed on the tiki bar; repost guest clips.

### Caption CTA
- Number in every caption, one line: "Private BYOB tiki boat, 25 people, gators included. Call or text 504-264-1056 to grab your Saturday."
- Say it once at the end of the video too (verbal + on-screen 3-5s).
- True urgency: "Saturdays in October are almost gone."
- Comment bait: "Tag the bride who needs this."
- Pin a comment with the number. Bio link to the Freaky Tiki booking page, not the homepage.

### Two structural notes
- Handle: @nolapedalbarge buries the product. Launch @nolafreakytiki as the dedicated channel; pedal barge account cross-posts.
- The gator is the unfair asset. Nobody in Miami, Nashville, Havasu or Tampa can film an alligator next to a champagne gun. Lead with it in the first frame of at least 1/3 of posts.
