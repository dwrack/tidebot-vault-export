## What this is

12 new narrated short-form pieces (18-26 seconds each) built around real party-boat and eco-tour sayings: toasts, captain and crew lines, bachelorette/birthday-group lines, and running jokes, per the brief. Format is modeled exactly on the Admire NOLA narrated series (`Admire NOLA/Content/Narrated/01-poetic-NOTES.txt` and `Admire NOLA/Content/Script & Caption Bank.md`): timestamped VO lines with a bracketed visual on every line, a named voice sample, a music bed, on-screen text treatment, and a shot list.

Split across NPB's two on-water products, per the brief, consistent with every prior pass's two-product framing (the vault's third, per-seat social boat has never been part of this slate series and isn't introduced here either):
- **Private BYOB party fleet** — pieces 01-07
- **Naturalist eco swamp tour** — pieces 08-12

Every spoken line was drafted with `/copy-mentors` (routed to whichever master's framework fit the piece — noted per piece) and then run through `/copywriting-2.0`'s 3-test filter (can I visualize it, can I falsify it, can nobody else say it). One documented cut or rewrite is logged per piece below and in its NOTES file. The recurring first-draft failure this pass: unfalsifiable superlatives ("a moment they'll never forget," "the best way to start the year," "such a unique bird") — every one got cut for something specific and checkable.
