## What this is

12 new narrated short-form pieces (20.5-23.0 seconds each) built around real party-boat and eco-tour sayings, toasts, and small on-water rituals: the things guests actually say, coin, or turn into a running bit, plus real regional language guests pick up along the way. Format is modeled exactly on the Admire NOLA narrated series (`Admire NOLA/Content/Narrated/01-poetic-NOTES.txt` and `Admire NOLA/Content/Script & Caption Bank.md`): timestamped VO lines with a bracketed visual on every line, a named voice sample, a music bed, and a shot list.

Split evenly across NPB's two on-water products, consistent with the "private BYOB party fleet and social eco swamp tour" framing this pass was built to, and matching the two-product split all eight prior passes have used (the vault's third, per-seat social boat has never been part of this slate series and isn't introduced here either, for consistency with the existing 96-piece library):
- **Private BYOB party fleet** — pieces 01-06
- **Naturalist eco swamp tour** — pieces 07-12

Every spoken line was drafted using `/copy-mentors` (routed to whichever master's framework fit the piece, noted per piece below) and then run through `/copywriting-2.0`'s 3-test filter (can I visualize it, can I falsify it, can nobody else say it). What got cut or rewritten is documented per piece. The recurring pattern this pass: soft, unfalsifiable superlatives ("the best part of the day," "one of the coolest facts," "it's honestly hilarious") kept surfacing in first drafts and kept getting cut for something specific, checkable, and scene-grounded instead.
