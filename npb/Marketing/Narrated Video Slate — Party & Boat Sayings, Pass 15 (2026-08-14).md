## What this is

12 new narrated short-form pieces (17.5–22.5 seconds each) built around real party-boat and eco-tour sayings — toasts, captain lines, bachelorette/birthday-group lines, and running jokes that happen out loud on the water. Format is modeled exactly on the Admire NOLA narrated series (`Admire NOLA/Content/Narrated/01-poetic-NOTES.txt` and `Admire NOLA/Content/Script & Caption Bank.md`): timestamped VO lines with a bracketed visual on every line, a named voice sample, a music bed, and a shot list.

Split 6/6 across NPB's two on-water products per the brief:
- **Private BYOB party fleet** — pieces 01–06
- **Naturalist eco swamp tour** (per-seat, wildlife-and-naturalist focus) — pieces 07–12

Every spoken line was drafted, then run through `/copy-mentors` (routed to whichever master fit the piece's shape — Ogilvy's specific-over-clever for the ritual and poetic pieces, Sugarman's slippery-slide for the story-build pieces, Halbert's one-person specificity for the character pieces, Bencivenga's structural-twist for the reveal pieces, Schwartz's reversal for the myth-vs-reality piece) and then `/copywriting-2.0` (the 3-test filter: can I visualize it, can I falsify it, can nobody else say it). What got cut or rewritten is noted per piece below — mostly generic "every group's got one" openers that had already been used three times across the two prior passes, one unfalsifiable "they'll actually remember this" closer, and a couple of specific-sounding claims that needed replacing with something safer once they didn't survive the falsifiability test.

This pass leans on two real, previously-unused ideas already sitting in this vault instead of inventing new rituals from nothing: the dockside welcome chalkboard (`Ideas/Unreasonable Hospitality — 100 Ideas for NPB.md`, idea #9) and the surprise-song hospitality move (same doc, idea #20). Both were written up as ideas, never built into content. This pass also deliberately keeps crew load light — only pieces 04 (Poppa Charles) and 05 (Shannon) need a specific named crew member to perform a bit, versus 5 pieces in Pass 14 that all depended on a specific captain or deckhand being available and on camera.

## Piece 01 — "The Welcome Board"
**Lane:** Funny / ritual · **Product:** Private BYOB party fleet
**Voice:** EL-George (ElevenLabs) · **Music:** Kermit Ruffins-style trad-jazz, warm and low under narration (clearance unconfirmed) · **Target:** 20.5s

```
 0.0s  Every dock, same chalkboard.                                              [deckhand crouched at the dock, chalk in hand, board blank]
 2.0s  Ten seconds. Then your group name goes up.                                [close-up: chalk touching board, first letter forming]
 4.8s  Nobody asked for it. Everybody photographs it.                            [guests arriving, phones out, snapping the board]
 8.0s  Bachelorette weekends. Fortieth birthdays. A softball team that just won a game nobody expected.  [quick cuts: different board messages, different groups]
13.0s  By the time you're back on land, that board's already erased.             [wide shot: board being wiped clean for the next group]
16.5s  Yours was up long enough.                                                 [group photo in front of the board, big smiles]
18.5s  Welcome aboard, [group name].                                             [board close-up, chalk finishing the last letter]
```

**On-screen text:** None over the narration itself — the board is the on-screen text. Optional: let the actual chalk handwriting be legible in-frame rather than adding a caption layer over it.
**Shot list:** deckhand crouched, board blank → chalk detail, first letter → guests arriving, photographing → quick-cut montage of past board messages (needs a backlog of real photos) → board being wiped → group photo at the board → chalk finishing the final letter.
**Casting/production note:** Low-lift. This is exactly what deckhands already do at check-in (per Unreasonable Hospitality idea #9) — start collecting real board photos in #crew-content now so piece 01's montage beat (the "quick cuts" line) has real material instead of one shoot day's worth.
**Copy passes:** copy-mentors (Ogilvy — the plain fact sells itself) kept this almost entirely factual, no exaggeration needed. Copywriting-2.0 tightened "Ten seconds before your group name goes up" into the punchier fragment "Ten seconds. Then your group name goes up" — shorter beats read better spoken aloud, matching the rhythm precedent set by Pass 14's "Whose Toast Is It Anyway."

**Lane:** Funny / heartfelt, storyline · **Product:** Private BYOB party fleet

**On-screen text:** The chat-bubble graphic at 0.0s can carry a stylized song-title placeholder; nothing else needs text, let the reaction shot at 12.2s land on its own.
