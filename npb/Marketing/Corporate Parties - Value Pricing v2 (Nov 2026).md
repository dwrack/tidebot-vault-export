# Corporate Parties: Value Pricing v2 (Nov 2026)

Drafted 2026-11-06 from the David + Jeff call. DRAFT, not live. This replaces the three fixed packages in `Corporate Party Packages - Per Person (Nov 2026).md` with one per-person base price plus levers. That file stays as history and keeps the comps and vendor list.

## The idea
- Lead with photos and the story, not the price. The email sends people to a corporate page where they build their own quote.
- Every group gets the same base price per person, with a few extras thrown in that have a clear value.
- When someone pushes on price, we take something out (less time on the water, a shorter party, a lighter food tier). We don't discount.
- Food and bar go through partners (Bar Beau Q, Greta's Sushi, Barbo's), with our margin built into the per-person price.

## Base: $59 per person (Mon-Thu)
- 1 hour private cruise on the bayou, with the skyline view
- 2 hours at the marina: party room, tiki village, palapas
- Covered boats, heated when it's cold, bathroom on board
- Parking, tables, chairs and a sound system
- Minimum 40 guests ($2,360). Fri-Sun is +$20 per person, minimum 50.

## Levers down (instead of discounts)
- 1 hour on the water instead of the full cruise
- 2 hours at the marina instead of 3
- Lighter food tier, or bring your own snacks
- BYOB with no bartender
- A Monday to Thursday date
