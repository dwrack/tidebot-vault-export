## Coaching: 6 habits
1. **Face in the first second.** Crew, captain or a guest. No empty boat, no sunset-only openers.
2. **Caption = one line + a tag ask.** "Tag the friend who..." is our best performer. Under 12 words. No links, no "book online" (the bio does that).
3. **Shares beat likes.** The Tesla reel got 233 shares on 2.5k reach. Make things people send to the group chat: roasts, fake facts, "this is so you."
4. **Film every trip, post 4 a week.** Keep a running camera roll: crew bits, guest reactions, gators, the dock band. Batch-edit Monday.
5. **Name the crew.** Recurring characters win. Pick 2 to 3 deckhands who are game and make them regulars (the "He's seen things" captain, the deadpan rules reader).
6. **TikTok is a search engine for us.** 90% of TikTok views come from search. Post the same reels there with titles like "things to do in New Orleans with friends" or "alligator boat ride New Orleans." It's been quiet since Sep 4, so restart it with the reels that already worked on IG.

Stop: carousels, flyer-style promo reels, posting the same promo twice.
