## Mardi Gras 2027 (Mardi Gras Day = Tue Feb 9, 2027)
The dates come from neworleans.com's 2027 list. Every time and route is **TBC** until the krewes publish them.

**Top 6 for party crews**
1. **Muses**, Thu Feb 4, Uptown. All-women and satirical, and the shoe is the throw everyone wants. The bachelorette parade.
2. **Endymion**, Sat Feb 6, ~4pm, Mid-City. Biggest parade, and the neutral ground is an all-day tailgate. It's back on the full route (Canal → St. Charles → Superdome) after the 2023-24 detour.
3. **Tucks**, Sat Feb 6, noon, Uptown. Toilet humor, rowdy daytime. Do it before Endymion.
4. **Bacchus**, Sun Feb 7, 5:15pm, Uptown. Celebrity king, Bacchagator float.
5. **Orpheus**, Mon Feb 8, 6pm, Uptown. Anyone can buy tickets to its Orpheuscapade after-party.
6. **Krewe du Vieux**, Sat Jan 23, Marigny → Quarter. Bawdy adult satire, the only krewe that goes through the Quarter. The pick for early-season trips.

**Also good:** Chewbacchus (Jan 16, date conflicts with another source's Jan 31), Krewe Bohème (Jan 22), Freret (Jan 30), Babylon (Feb 4, before Muses), Hermes + d'Etat (Feb 5), Iris (Feb 6), Zulu (Feb 9, 8am) and Rex (Feb 9) for Mardi Gras Day.
**Skip for party groups:** Druids, Thoth. Nyx isn't rolling.

Sources are in the subagent report (neworleans.com, mardigrasneworleans.com, Yelp, The Infatuation, krewe and restaurant sites, Axios, WWL).
