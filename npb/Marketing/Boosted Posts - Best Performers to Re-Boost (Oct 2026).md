# Boosted posts, last 12 months: what to boost again

Pulled 2026-10-02 from Meta Business Suite > Ad Center > All ads, Page 195245397959049,
date range Oct 3 2025 to Oct 2 2026. Roughly 70 boosts and ads in the window.

**Where this data is NOT:** none of these boosts appear in ad account act_87863118. That
account holds only 9 structured campaigns for the year. Boosts are billed separately, which
is exactly why `Boosted Post Tracking Links.md` says Ads Manager shows no conversions for
them. So the numbers below are reach and clicks only. **No boost in this list has booking or
revenue attribution.** Ranking is by cost per click, not by money made.

**Two result types, not comparable.** Meta recorded some boosts as "Link clicks" and others
as "Landing Page Views" depending on the objective picked at the time. A link click is
cheaper and shallower than a landing page view. Compare within a type, not across.

## Before re-boosting anything, two blockers

**1. Most of the older winners say "Pedal".** The January, February, March, May and most 2025
posts send people to **www.NolaPedalBarge.com** and several say "Nola Pedal Barge" in the
caption. That breaks the no-pedal rule and points at the old domain. Those posts cannot be
re-boosted as they are; the caption needs editing first, or a fresh post with the same hook.

Clean on this already: Lake Borgne, "Get on a boat and do whatever ya like", "Party Boat
with Alligators", "World class boat party with a view".

**2. Nothing here is tracked to bookings.** Use the tracking links from
`Boosted Post Tracking Links.md` on any re-boost (`/boost` for Facebook, `/boost-ig` for
Instagram) so GA4 can finally attribute. Better still, run them from act_87863118 so the
pixel reports purchases, which is the only way to know whether cheap clicks are worth
anything.

## One number to treat with suspicion

The 2025-11-13 Instagram reel "Lovely boating weather" shows **4,979 link clicks from 7,141
views**, a 70 percent click rate, for $139.98. That is not credible as real link clicks and
is more likely counting profile or sticker taps. Do not use it as a benchmark without
checking the breakdown.

## Worst of the year, for contrast

- 2026-04-29 "Paradise is a place": 83 clicks for $157.57, **$1.90 per click**
- 2025-10-26 "Lovely boating weather" on Facebook: 79 clicks for $97.77, $1.24 per click
- 2025-10-26 "Captain Barry": 5 clicks for $13.97

Same account, same kind of creative, 50 to 80x worse than the Lake Borgne reel. The spread
between best and worst is the real argument for re-boosting proven posts instead of new ones.

## Recommendation

Re-boost in this order, all with tracking links attached:

1. **Lake Borgne Surge Barrier reel** (Facebook). Proven three times, cheapest clicks, clean copy.
2. **"Get on a boat and do whatever ya like"** (Instagram). Best IG result, most recent.
3. **"Party Boat with Alligators"** (Facebook). Matches the current gator campaign thesis.

Each has a "Boost again" button in Ad Center. Spend was $100 to $210 per run historically.

Do **not** re-boost Jingle on the Bayou (2025-12-16, 854 landing page views for $149.77);
per the standing decision that product is left alone.
