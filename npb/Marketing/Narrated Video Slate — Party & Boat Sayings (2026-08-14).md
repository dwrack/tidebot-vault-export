## What this is

12 narrated short-form pieces (15–27 seconds each) built around real party-boat and eco-tour sayings: toasts, captain lines, bachelorette/birthday-group lines, and running jokes. Format is modeled exactly on the Admire NOLA narrated series (`Admire NOLA/Content/Narrated/01-poetic-NOTES.txt` and `Admire NOLA/Content/Script & Caption Bank.md`) — timestamped VO lines with a bracketed visual on every line, a named voice sample, a music bed, and a shot list.

Split 7/5 across NPB's two on-water products per the brief:
- **Private BYOB party fleet** (Freaky Tiki, Twerkin Tiki, Bayou Boogie, Party Queen) — pieces 01–07
- **Naturalist eco swamp tour** (per-seat, wildlife-and-naturalist focus) — pieces 08–12

Every spoken line in every piece was drafted, then run through `/copy-mentors` (routed to Ogilvy, Schwartz, Halbert, Sugarman, or Bencivenga depending on the piece) and then `/copywriting-2.0` (the 3-test filter: can I visualize it, can I falsify it, can nobody else say it). Full transcripts of both passes are in this conversation's session; the locked lines are what's below and in each NOTES file. A handful of lines got cut for being unfalsifiable vibe-filler ("none of them subtle," "we've clocked it," a trailing "though") — noted per piece.

## Piece 01 — "The Toast Captain Ben Gives"
**Lane:** Funny / ritual · **Product:** Private BYOB party fleet
**Voice:** EL-Eric (ElevenLabs) · **Music:** brass/bounce party bed, Hot 8 Brass Band feel (clearance unconfirmed — see Music - Local Sound Bank.md) · **Target:** 23.0s

```
 0.0s  Every captain's got a toast. Ben's is the one people ask for by name.        [Captain Ben at the helm, raising a cup toward camera]
 3.8s  To the bride, who got twelve people on a boat by 11 AM. Nobody knows how.    [Bride and group cheering, toast raised]
 8.0s  To the guy who brought his own blender. Nobody asked him to. Everybody thanked him.  [Cutaway: portable blender wedged in the cooler]
12.5s  To the bayou, for putting up with all of us today.                          [Wide shot: cypress and open water passing]
16.0s  And to whoever's driving Ubers tonight. Godspeed, truly.                     [Group laughing, cups clinking]
19.5s  Whatever happens next is going on Instagram. Act accordingly.                [Closing shot: dock approach, group laughing, golden hour]
```

**On-screen text:** Bold script/neon caption pill, high saturation. Burn in only the toast subject per beat ("THE BRIDE" / "THE BLENDER GUY" / "THE UBER DRIVERS"), not the full line. No text on the closer.
**Shot list:** boarding shot (captain greeting group) → captain toast moment → blender detail shot → wide cypress/water → group cup-clink reaction → dock-approach closer.
