## What kind of post wins (copy these patterns)

1. **Dates + times + address in the first lines.** Every top event post is a mini calendar. People save them.
2. **"Coming soon" and "turning one."** Anticipation beats the thing itself. Bonchon got more likes for *not being open yet* than most open restaurants.
3. **"Sold out" honesty.** Pretty Funky Treats: "SO many sold out case days." Makes people plan.
4. **Short limited windows.** "He's only here Oct 28-30." Urgency without a discount.
5. **"Save this, tag your outing partner."** The free/cheap weekend lists (@thingstodonola, @locallynola) live on saves and shares.
6. **Top 5 lists with a local opinion.** @straightupnola's oyster list (Drago's, Felix's, Acme, Casamento's cash only, Neyow's). Simple, arguable, forwardable.
7. **One weird history fact.** Po-boys were born in the 1929 streetcar strike, the Martin brothers fed striking "poor boys." Muffuletta, 1906, Central Grocery. These get read.

## Newsletter: make it the email people wait for

**The move:** stop making the newsletter about the boat. Make it **"The Bayou Report: what's actually worth doing in New Orleans this week, from the crew."** The boat shows up once, at the bottom, as the obvious next plan. People open a cheat sheet. They skip an ad.
