# Cousin Trips — Research & Content Plan

JT spotted it: constant "cousin trip" matching-shirt groups at the marina, 6 different ones on a single riverboat cruise. This doc sizes the trend and turns it into content NPB can shoot now.

## TLDR

- It's real, but it lives on social and in Facebook groups, not in Google search. Direct "cousin trip" searches are small (~170/mo US), but the proxy behavior is big: "cousin crew shirts" pulls 3,600 searches/mo and "family reunion shirts" 4,400/mo, both trending up.
- The driver is durable, not a fad. Americans born ~1995 have ~4 first cousins vs ~24 for the 1960s generation ("the great cousin decline"), so cousin bonds are scarce and intentional. 47% of 2026 travelers are planning a multigenerational trip (+17% vs 2024); group trips of 6+ are up 67% YoY.
- The play: own an empty niche. Zero local operators rank for "cousin trip new orleans" (the SERP is an IG reel, a Facebook planning thread, and an Etsy shirt page). Average group: 8 adults, planned 6-12 months out by one "cruise director" cousin. Win the planner, win 12 tickets.

## The trend

- Adult cousins (25-55, often siblings-plus-cousins) taking a dedicated group trip; matching "Cousin Crew" / "Cousin Trip [Year]" shirts are the ritual, ordered 2-6 weeks pre-trip. Many make it annual and rotate cities.
- Why now: shrinking family size makes cousins scarce (RD's "great cousin decline"); families are geographically dispersed so seeing cousins requires a trip; multigen travel is the fastest-growing trip type; every posted trip recruits the next one on TikTok/IG (#cousins content ~34M posts).
- Group shape: avg family reunion trip = 8 adults + 4 kids over 5 days; adult cousin trips 6-15 people. One full tiki boat, sometimes two. 55% start planning 6-12 months out. They book one "anchor activity" per day that works ages 25-65, photographs well in matching shirts, and needs no athleticism. A 2-hour tiki cruise is a caricature of the brief.
- The shirts crossed into mainstream: Etsy has dedicated market pages (including a "New Orleans Cousin Trip" page), Walmart and Shein carry cousin-crew lines.
- Live example: a Facebook group thread titled "New Orleans, LA cousin trip planning and feedback needed" has 110+ comments with the planner posting activity options for a family vote. That's the exact conversation NPB needs to be findable inside.

## Where to reach them

- TikTok/IG: #cousintrip #cousinstrip #cousincrew #cousingoals #familyreunion2026 + destination stacks. TikTok discover pages for "cousin trip location ideas" mean TikTok search is the planning tool; a "Cousin trip to New Orleans: day one" video surfaces to planners for years.
- Facebook groups: where the booking vote actually happens (family-reunion planning groups, group-travel communities, NOLA travel-tips groups; planner demo skews 35-60). Content that members share travels there organically; don't spam.
- Pinterest: "cousin trip ideas," "family reunion ideas" boards; planners pin 6-12 months out. A few vertical pins are set-and-forget.
- Etsy sellers: the shirt order happens after the destination locks. Top "New Orleans cousin trip" shirt sellers have a pre-qualified, destination-confirmed customer list.
- Reunion planning sites: Reunions Magazine, reunion-planner tools, New Orleans CVB group pages.
