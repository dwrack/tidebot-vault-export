# NolaPartyBarges.com — new site, draft v2 (2026-11-10)

**Quick look:** open `Preview - desktop.png` or `Preview - phone.png`. They show the whole page top to bottom.
**Live look:** double-click `index.html` to open it in a browser. The booking buttons go to the real FareHarbor flow, so don't finish a checkout.

- One-page static site in the new logo colors (navy, yellow, cyan, pink, lavender).
- Headline: "BYOB on the bayou. / New Orleans style. / Party where the gators live."
- Every boat shows both prices. The public seat is $63 a person. The private boats are Party Queen $954 (up to 26), Freaky / Twerkin' Tiki $1,272 (up to 25), Bayou Boogie $848 (up to 18), Pontoon $371 (up to 6) and Eco Tour $53 a person (13+). All prices include fees and are shown before checkout.
- Every FareHarbor link is tagged `ref=site-v2-*`.
- The newsletter sign-up sits after the prices.
