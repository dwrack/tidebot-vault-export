# Crew & Captain Downtime Playbook
*What to do when no boat's going out, or there's a long gap between trips.*

For deckhands and captains both. If you're on the clock and not on the water, this is your guide.

---

## The Rule

If you're on shift and on the clock, you're working on something. Not standing around, not on your phone (unless you're filing a check-in, grabbing content for us, or pulling a task from the Slack list). The marina is small and guests can see you from the parking lot. What they see is the brand.

This is in priority order. Start at the top and work down. If you finish Tier 1, you move to Tier 2. If a guest walks up while you're on any tier, drop everything and go be a host.

---

## Tier 1 — Customer-Facing (Always First)

This is what guests see when they pull in. If any of this is off, fix it before doing anything else.

- [ ] Outdoor customer area: chairs straight, tables wiped, cushions placed, shade sails secured
- [ ] Both customer restrooms: clean toilet, clean sink, paper goods stocked, floor swept, no trash, mirror wiped
- [ ] Walk from the parking lot to the docks: any trash, debris, or unsightly items removed
- [ ] Trash cans: not overflowing, fresh liners
- [ ] Welcome signage clean and visible
- [ ] Your boat's exterior visible from the dock looks sharp (clean, no clutter, music ready if on prep window)

If you genuinely can't find anything to do here, take one slow lap through the customer area pretending you've never been here before. You'll find something.

---

## Tier 2 — The Live Slack Todo List (#crew-todo)

This is the new way. The deep-clean rotations and marina projects change week to week, so we don't keep them on a printed list. They live in Slack, in the **#crew-todo** channel.

**How it works:**

- Jeff, JT, and any crew member can post tasks into #crew-todo any time something needs doing.
- Tidebot keeps a **pinned message** in that channel called "🛠 Marina Todo — Live List." He keeps it organized and current.
- The list is grouped: **Today (priority)** / **This Week** / **When You Have Time**.
- When you have downtime, open #crew-todo, scroll to the pinned message, and grab a task that fits the time you've got.

**To claim a task:**

1. React to the task with 🙋 (raise hand emoji). That tells everyone "I've got this one."
2. Do the work.
3. Reply in the thread with "done" + a quick photo of the result.
4. Tidebot strikes the task from the pinned list.

If you grab a task that turns out to be bigger than expected, or you can't finish it, post in the thread: "ran out of time, got X% done." Honesty resets the clock — no penalty.

**To add a task:**

Anyone can. Format:

> [Boat or Marina] — [What needs doing] — [Rough time estimate]
>
> Example: "Bayou Boogie — front fender is fraying, replace before Saturday — 20 min"
> Example: "Marina — supply room is a mess, needs reorganizing — 1 hr"
> Example: "Office windows haven't been done in a month — 30 min"

Tidebot will tag it (boat / marina / supply / safety / cosmetic) and add it to the right section of the pinned list.

**If you don't see Tidebot or the channel:** Ping Jeff or JT and they'll add you.

**Captain note:** Captains, you're encouraged to drop tasks too. You see things on the boats that deckhands don't. If you spot a frayed line, a soft cushion, a flickering nav light, drop it in #crew-todo with the boat name. Don't sit on it.

---

## Tier 3 — Bigger Projects (Need Sign-Off)

Some tasks in #crew-todo will be marked **[Sign-off needed]** by Tidebot. These are bigger jobs (paint, anything involving boat systems, anything that costs money) that need Jeff or JT to greenlight before you start. React with 🙋 in the thread, wait for a 👍 from Jeff or JT, then go.

If a task isn't marked that way, you don't need permission. Just claim and do.

---

## Tier 4 — Content Capture for Marketing

This is paid time, but it's also kind of fun. See **Deckhand — Social Capture Playbook** for what to actually shoot. Quick version:

- Walk-around vertical video of your boat looking pristine (no people)
- Aerial-feel shot of the marina from the dock end
- Wide shot of the customer area set up perfectly
- Behind-the-scenes: prepping speakers, stocking the cooler, sweeping the dock
- Anything visually weird or beautiful (sunset light, a bird, a wake from a passing boat)

Drop everything to the crew Slack content channel. Best clips get used in ads. Crew who consistently feed the channel get first pick of shifts.

---

## Tier 5 — Personal / Skill Building

Use this only when 1-4 are genuinely done and there's no boat coming for at least an hour.

- Re-read **NOLA Party Barge — Crew & Captain Performance Guide** (the storytelling and questions sections especially)
- Practice your safety briefing out loud
- Build out your personal NOLA story bank (you should have 3-5 favorites you can tell cold)
- Refresh your Spotify playlist for the boat
- Review yesterday's manifest patterns: who's coming, what kind of group
- Review any new menu / pricing / FAQ updates

---

## What NOT to Do

- Don't sit in the office unless you're sheltering from rain or cleaning it.
- Don't work on personal projects that aren't visible (don't read on your phone, don't online-shop, don't watch shows). Even if you're "between things," guests don't know that.
- Don't leave the property without telling Jeff or JT.
- Don't let another deckhand take all the customer-facing work while you grab the easy invisible jobs. We see it.

---

## If You're Genuinely Stuck

Open #crew-todo first. There's always something on the live list. If somehow that's empty, message Tidebot in the channel: "What's the next thing?" He'll pull from the list or kick something to Jeff/JT to add.

Don't disappear. Don't sit silently. There is always something — guaranteed.

> "I can't find anything to do" usually means "I haven't actually walked the property yet." Walk it. You'll find it.
