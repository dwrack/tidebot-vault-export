## What Changed and Why

Crew used to get paid one flat number that covered the whole day. Opening the boat, running trips, cleaning between trips, closing up, all buried in one figure.

Now every piece of work is its own line item with its own rate.

- Open the boat, get paid to open the boat
- Run four trips, get paid four times
- Fuel run on a Tuesday, that's its own line

**Nothing is blank on purpose except the money.** Those are the numbers we need from Jeff.

### What each captain line requires

**1. Tour run (core)** — per tour
- Departure, full route, return to dock
- Pre-trip audio log filed
- Post-trip audio log filed

**2. Opening boat prep** — per day, first trip only
- USCG safety check: engine, fuel, bilge, batteries, navigation, comms, life safety, documentation
- Manifest and waivers verified against FareHarbor
- Maintenance issues noted before guests board

**3. Closing, shutdown, lockup** — per day, last trip only
- Vessel shut down, secured, locked
- All FareHarbor trips closed out
- End-of-day audio log filed

**4. Turnaround between trips** — per tour
- Trash out, ice topped, cooler restocked
- Deck wiped, boat ready for next boarding

**5. Deep clean** — per occurrence, pre-authorized
- Full interior and exterior, beyond a normal turnaround

**6. Boat move or relocation** — per occurrence, pre-authorized
- Between slips, between marinas, or to and from service

**7. Fuel or supply run** — per occurrence, pre-authorized
- Fuel, ice, or marina supplies

**8. Weekend lead / on-call** — per weekend
- First call for coverage gaps, walk-ups, day-of problems

**9. Content clip published** — per clip the Company actually uses
- Not paid per clip submitted

**10. Upsell commission** — % of sale
- Drinks, photo packages, ice and branded merch, recorded at point of sale

## Tips (both roles)

- Pooled per trip, split **70% captain / 30% deckhand**
- The Company takes **nothing** off the tip pool. Ever.
- Captain counts the pool at end of trip, splits same day, no IOUs
- Cash handed directly to one person still goes in the pool

## What Is Not Paid

- Onboarding paperwork
- Orientation
- Shadow trips
- Ride-along coaching
- The Monday weekly check-in (optional, and it's for you)
- Self-study, SOP reading, the Culture Quiz
- Clips submitted that don't get published

**Assisted trips ARE paid at full rates.** Once you're running the boat, you're working, no training discount.

Tips and commission are on top of all of it.
