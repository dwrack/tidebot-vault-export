## 1. The Deal

This agreement is between **NOLA Party Barge LLC** ("the Company") and **[CAPTAIN NAME]** ("you," "the Captain"). You are an independent contractor, not an employee. You're responsible for your own taxes, your own health insurance, and your own time outside of the trips you accept.

What you bring to the table: your USCG license, your skill on the water, and your ability to host a great experience.

What we bring: the boats, the bookings, the marina, the SOPs, the back-office support, and a coaching system designed to make you better at this job over time, not just keep you in line.

Your job, simply: run safe, legal, memorable trips that guests rave about and rebook.

## 3. Onboarding & Training Path

We don't drop you on a boat alone. The path is structured and the goal is for you to feel confident before you ever sign off.

### Phase 1: Paperwork (Before Anything Else)
- This ICA signed via DocuSeal
- W-9 on file
- Photo ID, USCG license, TWIC, CPR/First Aid, Maritime Consortium enrollment, drug test result, all on file
- Background check cleared
- Emergency contact and payment method submitted
- Crew Intake Form complete

### Phase 2: Orientation (½ day, paid as a shift)
- Walk through the **Crew & Captain Performance Guide** with Jeffrey or a senior captain
- Equipment tour, safety equipment locations, emergency procedures
- Read the culture doc **How We Run Things**, complete the **Culture Quiz**
- Review the relevant SOPs: Pre-Tour Preparation, The First 15 Minutes, Welcome Script, Wow Moment design, Tip Close, Coast Guard & Compliance

### Phase 3: Shadow Shift (1-2 trips, paid)
You ride along with an experienced captain. Observation only. You watch the full arc: arrival, build, peak, land, send-off. Debrief with the captain after each trip.

### Phase 4: Assisted Shifts (2-4 trips, paid at full per-tour rate)
You run the trip, the experienced captain is on board to step in if anything goes sideways. After each, a 10-minute debrief: what worked, what to adjust, what to try next time.

### Phase 5: Solo Sign-Off
Manager (Jeffrey or a designated lead captain) signs off when:
- Guest handling is solid
- Safety procedures are automatic
- Gear, motor, and FareHarbor competency confirmed
- You can run the emotional arc without a script

Sign-off is documented in the **Crew Roster & Credentials Tracker**. You're now in the regular rotation.

## 4. The Coaching System

This is the part that matters. We're not interested in catching you doing something wrong. We're interested in making you a better captain every month than you were last month.

### 4.1 Tidebot (Daily)
Tidebot is the AI assistant in the crew Slack. He nudges you on prep, answers logistics questions, and reminds you to log end-of-day. Use him. He's not a snitch, he's the friend who pokes you so you don't walk off the dock with something unfinished.

### 4.2 Captain Audio Log (After Every Shift)
At end of day, record a 60-second voice memo: how the day went, anything weird, anything broken, anything that worked surprisingly well. Drop it in the **Captain Audio Log** Slack channel. We process these per the **Captain Audio Log Processing SOP** and use them to improve the operation.

### 4.3 Weekly Check-In (15 min, optional but offered)
Every Monday, Jeffrey is available for a short 1:1 if you want one. Bring something you want to get better at, or a problem you keep running into, or just a vent. No agenda from us, you drive it.

### 4.4 Ride-Along Coaching (Quarterly)
Once a quarter, a senior captain or Jeffrey rides along on one of your trips and gives you a written debrief afterward. Three things you did well, one thing to work on. This is a development tool, not a performance review.

### 4.5 Performance Guide Updates
The **Crew & Captain Performance Guide** is a living document. If something in there doesn't match reality on the water, tell us, we'll change it. The standard you're held to is the one that's actually written down, not folklore.

## 7. When Things Go Wrong

The coaching system is designed to fix small things before they become problems. We expect you to use it. We expect Jeffrey to use it. If a coaching conversation has happened and the same issue keeps repeating, then we're in a different conversation.

### 7.1 Strike System (Documented Coaching Failure)
Strikes apply only after a documented coaching conversation has happened on the same issue and the issue continues. They are tracked in the coaching log.

A strike is one of:
1. **Repeating an issue** that's already been coached and acknowledged in writing
2. **Skipping or falsifying** a required check-in, log, or report
3. **Walking off-shift** without completing end-of-day signoff

Three documented strikes in a rolling 90-day window triggers a contract review with Jeffrey. Outcomes range from extra ride-along coaching to contract termination, depending on severity and trajectory.

**What does not count as a strike:**
- Asking for help
- Flagging a problem honestly
- A bumpy day where something didn't go to plan but you logged it
- A first-time mistake on something we hadn't coached yet

If you're honest, we'll figure it out together.

### 7.2 Immediate Termination (No Coaching, No Strikes)
The following are firing offenses with no progression. One time, contract ends:

- Operating a vessel under the influence of alcohol or drugs
- Allowing guests to operate the vessel
- Skipping the safety briefing
- Departing with insufficient PFDs
- Falsifying an incident report or failing to report an incident
- Harassment or discrimination toward guests or crew
- Operating outside approved routes or in restricted areas
- Ignoring a weather hold or USCG advisory
- Theft from the company, crew, or guests

These aren't coaching items. They're hard lines.

This agreement begins on the date you sign and continues until either party ends it.

- The Company can end this agreement immediately for cause (Section 7.2 items, or repeated strikes per Section 7.1).

## 10. Confidentiality, IP, Non-Solicit

### 10.1 Confidentiality
You will not share with outside parties: customer lists, SOPs, financials, internal Slack content, audio logs, or any business information that's not public.

### 10.2 IP
- Photos and videos you take of guests during a paid shift, while using Company branding or vessels, are owned by the Company. The Company grants you a limited license to use these for your own portfolio with credit.
- Captain audio logs and incident reports are Company property.
- Any SOPs or content you help author during a paid shift are Company property.

### 10.3 Non-Solicit (Customers Only, Not Employment)
For 12 months after this agreement ends, you won't solicit Company customers or marketing leads for competing tour services in the New Orleans market. You're free to take any other captain work, including for direct competitors. We don't restrict your career.
