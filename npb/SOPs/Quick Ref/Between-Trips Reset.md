# Between-Trips Reset

Fast turnaround between tours. The next group should never see evidence of the last one.

---

1. Clear all trash from guest areas, seats, cup holders, and floor
2. Wipe down all seating and surfaces
3. Check and restock ice in coolers
4. Empty and rinse any coolers that need it
5. Reset the music system: stop current playlist, queue up boarding music for next group
6. Check restroom (if applicable to your vessel), restock supplies
7. Sweep the deck
8. Pull up the next booking manifest in FareHarbor
9. Note headcount, special requests, group type (bachelorette, corporate, birthday, etc.)
10. Do a quick PFD count, confirm all are back in storage position
11. Walk the boarding area, pick up anything guests left behind or dropped
12. Stage any special setup needed for the incoming group

> [!tip] If the incoming group has a special request (decorations, specific music, birthday setup), this is when you handle it. Not after they arrive.

> [!warning] If anything is broken, missing, or looks off from the last trip, report it before the next group boards. Don't send a compromised vessel out.
