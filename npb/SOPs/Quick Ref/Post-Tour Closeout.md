# Post-Tour Closeout

Lock down the trip before moving on to the next one or ending the day.

---

1. Confirm all guests are off the vessel, do a headcount out
2. Walk the vessel for left-behind items (phones, sunglasses, bags)
3. Return all gear to storage: PFDs hung or stored properly, throwable PFD back in position
4. Rinse any gear that got wet or dirty
5. If any incident or injury occurred during the trip, begin the incident report immediately (see Incident Report Steps)
6. Note any equipment issues, damage, or maintenance needs
7. Trigger the review request via FareHarbor or your review link
8. Log any guest feedback worth noting for the coaching log (good or bad)

> [!tip] Don't wait until end of day to write down feedback. A quick note in Slack right after the trip is worth more than a vague memory at 7 PM.

> [!warning] If there was any incident, even minor (guest tripped, equipment malfunction, near-miss), document it now. Same-day reporting is required.
