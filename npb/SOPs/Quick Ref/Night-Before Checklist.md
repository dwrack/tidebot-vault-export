# Night-Before Checklist

Confirm tomorrow's operations are locked before you leave today.

---

1. Open FareHarbor and review every booking for tomorrow
2. Note headcount per trip, special requests, and any VIP/bachelorette flags
3. Check weather forecast: wind speed, precipitation %, wave height
4. If wind forecast >15 mph, flag for morning re-check with captain

> [!warning] If forecast shows >20 mph winds, lightning, or USCG advisory, begin pre-cancel communication. See Weather Hold Protocol.

5. Send 48-hour reminder to guests using Guest Comms template
6. Confirm all scheduled captains and crew have responded "confirmed" in Slack
7. If anyone is unconfirmed by 6 PM, start backup outreach immediately
8. Review float plan and route for any new conditions (construction, events, closures)
9. Verify all gear is staged, charged, and accounted for at the dock
10. Confirm cooler supplies, ice, and restocking needs for morning

> [!tip] If a large group (15+) is booked, double-check PFD count and cooler capacity the night before. Don't wait until morning.

- [ ] All bookings reviewed in FareHarbor
- [ ] Weather checked and flagged if borderline
- [ ] Guest reminders sent
- [ ] Staff confirmed
- [ ] Float plan reviewed
- [ ] Gear staged and accounted for
