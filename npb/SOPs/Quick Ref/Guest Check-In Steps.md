# Guest Check-In Steps

Get every guest checked in, safe, and excited before they step on the boat.

---

1. Greet guests by name when possible (check the manifest before they arrive)
2. Verify headcount against the FareHarbor manifest
3. If headcount doesn't match, confirm with the booking holder before proceeding
4. Check waiver status in FareHarbor for every guest on the manifest

> [!warning] Unsigned waivers: Pull up the booking-specific waiver link and hand the device to the guest. Do NOT use a generic QR code. No boarding without a signed waiver. No exceptions, no "they can sign after." Every guest, every time.

5. Ask about medical conditions, mobility limitations, or anything that might affect their experience
6. Fit PFDs on every guest, check the fit (snug, not loose)

> [!tip] Kids under 13 must wear PFDs for the entire trip. Adults should know where theirs is and how to put it on quickly.

7. Deliver the safety briefing (see Safety Briefing Script for the full version)
8. Confirm the group knows the trip duration, route overview, and what to expect
9. Offer a photo opportunity at the dock before boarding
10. Board guests, do a final headcount on the vessel before departure

- [ ] All guests greeted
- [ ] Headcount matches manifest
- [ ] All waivers signed
- [ ] Medical/mobility limitations noted
- [ ] PFDs fitted on every guest
