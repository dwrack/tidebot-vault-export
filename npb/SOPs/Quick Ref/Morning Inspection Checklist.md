# Morning Inspection Checklist

Every vessel inspected, every morning, no shortcuts.

---

## Boat-Specific Arrival Duties

1. **Freaky Tiki** captain/crew: Set up customer area and clean restrooms
2. **Twerkin Tiki** captain/crew: Sweep vessel and empty all trash
3. **Bayou Boogie** captain/crew: Clean the dock and boarding area
4. **Party Queen** captain/crew: Landscaping and grounds around the site

> [!tip] These duties rotate with the vessel assignment, not the person. Whoever is on Bayou Boogie that day cleans the dock.

## Vessel Inspection (Every Boat, Every Day)

### Engine & Systems
5. Check engine oil level
6. Check fuel level, confirm sufficient for the day's trips plus reserve
7. Inspect bilge for water or oil, pump if needed
8. Check battery charge and connections

### Navigation & Communication
9. Test navigation lights (bow, stern, masthead)
10. Test horn, confirm functional
11. Turn on VHF radio, confirm Ch 16 is active and clear

### Life-Saving Equipment
12. Count PFDs (Type I, II, or III), confirm sufficient for max vessel capacity
13. Confirm throwable PFD (Type IV) is on deck and accessible

> [!warning] If PFD count is short by even one, the vessel does not leave the dock. No exceptions.

14. Confirm first aid kit is stocked and sealed

### Fire Safety
15. Check fire extinguisher: charged, pin intact, inspection tag current
16. Check flares: present and within expiration date

### Documentation
17. Confirm vessel documentation is on board
18. Confirm COI (Certificate of Inspection) is posted visibly in passenger area
19. Confirm captain has USCG license on their person

### Final Walk-Through
20. Walk the boarding area: no tripping hazards, no loose lines, no debris
21. Check seating, railings, and any guest-contact surfaces for damage
