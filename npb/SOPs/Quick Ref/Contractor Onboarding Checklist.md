# Contractor Onboarding Checklist

Manager-facing. Every step completed before a new captain takes a solo trip.

---

## Step 0: Culture

1. New contractor reads the "How We Run Things" culture doc in full

> [!tip] If they skim it, they'll miss what makes NPB different from every other boat gig. This isn't optional reading.

## Document Collection

2. Collect USCG Captain's License (OUPV / 6-pak minimum)
3. Collect current CPR / First Aid certification
4. Collect TWIC Card (Transportation Worker Identification Credential)
5. Run background check
6. Run drug test
7. Collect signed W-9
8. Send and collect signed ICA (Independent Contractor Agreement) via DocuSeal
9. Collect photo ID (driver's license or passport)
10. Collect direct deposit info for payments

> [!warning] Do not schedule any shifts until all documents are collected and verified. No exceptions. USCG license, TWIC, drug test, and background check are non-negotiable before they touch a vessel.

- [ ] USCG License verified (OUPV/6-pak min)
- [ ] CPR/First Aid current
- [ ] TWIC Card collected
- [ ] Background check cleared
- [ ] Drug test passed
- [ ] W-9 signed
- [ ] ICA signed via DocuSeal
- [ ] Photo ID on file
- [ ] Direct deposit info collected

## Orientation Day

11. Equipment tour of all 4 vessels (Freaky Tiki, Twerkin Tiki, Bayou Boogie, Party Queen)
12. Walk through every piece of safety equipment: PFDs, fire extinguisher, flares, throwable PFD, horn, VHF radio
13. Show vessel documentation location and COI posting
14. Practice the safety briefing out loud (see Safety Briefing Script)
15. Walk the full route on water with an experienced captain
16. Review boat-specific arrival duties (which boat does what)
17. Review the emotional arc: ARRIVAL, BUILD, PEAK, LAND, SEND OFF
18. Review FareHarbor: how to pull manifests, check waivers, confirm headcounts

## Progression to Solo

19. Shadow shift: ride along on a full day of trips, observe everything
20. Assisted shift: run a trip with an experienced captain on board as backup
21. Solo sign-off: manager or senior captain signs off that they're ready

> [!warning] Solo sign-off requires the new contractor to demonstrate: morning inspection, guest check-in, safety briefing, full trip execution, and post-tour closeout. If any piece is weak, they do another assisted shift.

- [ ] Orientation day completed
- [ ] Shadow shift completed
- [ ] Assisted shift completed
- [ ] Solo sign-off granted
