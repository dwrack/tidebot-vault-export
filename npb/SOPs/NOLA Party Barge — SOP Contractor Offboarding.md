# NOLA Party Barge — SOP: Contractor & Crew Offboarding
*2026-04-20*

> **Purpose:** When someone leaves (voluntarily or otherwise), this is the complete checklist. Nothing gets missed, nothing lingers.

---

## When to Use This

- Captain or crew member resigns
- Contractor is terminated or not renewed
- Affiliate/outreach networker becomes inactive
- Seasonal crew not returning

---

## Step 1: Communication (Day of Decision)

- [ ] Verbal or written confirmation that the working relationship is ending
- [ ] If termination: brief, direct reason documented (internal notes only)
- [ ] Final shift date confirmed
- [ ] Collect any company property: keys, radios, uniforms, branded gear, boat keys

---

## Step 2: System Access Removal (Within 24 Hours)

### FareHarbor
- [ ] Remove from captain/crew scheduling
- [ ] Deactivate ASN affiliate link (if applicable)
- [ ] Remove dashboard access
- [ ] Note in Affiliate Master Doc (Google Sheet): mark row as INACTIVE with date

### Communication Channels
- [ ] Remove from Slack workspace (or relevant channels)
- [ ] Remove from group texts / WhatsApp groups
- [ ] Remove from any shared email aliases

### Other Platforms
- [ ] Remove from Google Drive shared folders
- [ ] Remove from GHL (GoHighLevel) sub-account if they had access
- [ ] Remove from scheduling tool (FareHarbor calendar, Google Calendar, etc.)
- [ ] Remove from DocuSeal (revoke access, retain signed docs)

---

## Step 3: Regulatory & Credentials (Within 7 Days)

- [ ] If USCG-licensed captain: note their license number as inactive in Crew Roster
- [ ] Update Crew Roster & Credentials Tracker (mark as departed + date)
- [ ] If they were a Drug Free Vessel consortium member under our company: notify Maritime Consortium to remove
- [ ] Confirm TWIC card was personal (it is), no action needed
- [ ] If they had signing authority on any marina/dock agreements: transfer to active crew

### Hurricane Plan
- [ ] If listed as Hurricane Plan designee: assign replacement immediately
- [ ] Update Hurricane Readiness Plan document with new designee name
- [ ] Confirm replacement knows where straps, anchors, and trailering supplies are

---

## Step 4: Physical Access & Security

- [ ] Recover keys to: dock gate, storage stalls, booth, shack
- [ ] Change codes/locks if they had unsupervised access to 6301 Paris Road
- [ ] Remove from marina/dock access list (if applicable)
- [ ] Remove from any shared vehicle insurance policies

---

## Step 5: Financial Closeout (Within 30 Days)

- [ ] Calculate any outstanding pay for completed shifts
- [ ] Calculate any pending affiliate commissions (only for completed, non-refunded tours)
- [ ] Issue final payment via Venmo/Zelle/ACH
- [ ] If they earned over $600 YTD: flag for 1099 at year-end (keep W-9 on file)
- [ ] Document final payment date and amount

---

## Step 6: Documentation & Vault Cleanup

- [ ] Update this vault: remove from any "active crew" lists or rosters
- [ ] Add to offboarding log below
- [ ] Archive (don't delete) their ICA, credentials, and drug test records in Drive
- [ ] If they were an emergency contact for any role: assign replacement

## Notes

- Never delete someone's signed ICA, W-9, drug test records, or USCG license copies. Archive in Drive under "Former Contractors" folder.
- If someone might come back seasonally, mark them as INACTIVE rather than fully offboarding. Skip the regulatory steps until you're sure they're done.
- Captains with active USCG licenses can work elsewhere, that's their personal credential. We just need to stop scheduling them and remove access.
- Affiliate-only offboarding is lighter: deactivate ASN link, mark the spreadsheet row, stop payouts. No regulatory or physical access steps needed.
