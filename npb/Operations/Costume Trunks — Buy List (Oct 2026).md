## Kit rules (every trunk)

- Everything wipeable or cheap enough to lose. Budget ~15% of kit cost a month for replacements.
- **No wigs.** You can't sanitize them between groups. Use headbands, hats and sunglasses instead.
- No glitter, nothing that sheds, no full-face masks.
- Each trunk has a laminated inventory card on the lid, 2 mesh laundry bags, and a can of disinfectant spray. That adds about $20 a trunk.
- Each trunk has one hero piece for the guest of honor, so the birthday person or the bride stands out in photos.

## Ops

- The trunks live at marina check-in. A deckhand opens the trunk on camera as the group walks up, and that clip goes to Jeff.
- The deckhand counts against the card at the dock in about 30 seconds and sprays everything down. Each trunk gets a full wash once a week.
- A lost-item fee goes on the waiver. No deposit.
- Bachelorette groups get the Mardi Gras trunk plus the existing bachelorette kit (#32) as a bundle.
