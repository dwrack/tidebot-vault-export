# Weather and cancellation policy (draft)

## Customer-facing wording (site FAQ + OpenCX)

**What happens if the weather is bad?**
We watch the forecast and make the call for you. If we cancel a trip for weather, you choose: move to another date or time, or get a full refund. No fees, no credit-only runaround.

We make storm calls 24 hours before departure. For wind on the day of, we make the call about 2 hours before, because the bayou can be fine when the forecast looks ugly. You'll get a text and an email either way.

**Can I cancel because the forecast looks bad?**
If we're still running and you cancel inside 7 days of your trip, you get a credit good for a year, not a refund. Most forecast-scares turn into fine afternoons on the water, so we'd rather hold your spot. Outside 7 days, our normal cancellation terms apply.

**What if it rains?**
Rain alone doesn't cancel a trip. The boats have cover and the party goes on. Lightning, high wind, or a storm warning does.
