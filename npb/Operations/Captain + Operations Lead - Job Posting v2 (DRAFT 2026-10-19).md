# Captain + Operations Lead, NOLA Party Barge (New Orleans, 3 to 4 days a week)

We run party boats and tiki boats out of a marina on the bayou, about 20 minutes from downtown, plus kayak swamp tours nearby. Business is good, the season never really stops, and we have bigger things coming: events at the marina, corporate parties, and a beer and wine license. We need someone to run the day so the rest of us can build.

**What the job is**
- Some days you captain. Some days you run the floor: crew, check-in, the schedule, the call when a guide is out or a fourth boat books.
- You own how the crew communicates. Pre-shift, weather calls, who's where. We use Slack, FareHarbor, and an AI assistant that does a lot of the busywork with you.
- You make the day predictable. Checklists, start times, no surprises.

**Who you are**
- You hold a USCG captain's license.
- You've run a schedule for a crew before. Bar, restaurant, events, dispatch, the military, a charter outfit, it all counts.
- You keep your word, and people follow you because of it.
- You're good on a computer and you like trying new tools. If you've ever used ChatGPT or Claude to fix a problem at work, tell us about it.
- You're calm with a loud, happy group three drinks in.
- Weekends are part of the job. We'll build the rest around you.

**Nice to have:** TIPS certification (or we'll pay for it), bar management, event sales, comfortable with a 15-passenger van.
