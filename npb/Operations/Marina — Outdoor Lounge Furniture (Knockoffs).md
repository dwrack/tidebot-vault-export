## 1. Plank Chair by ORCA (Claude Home)

- **Retail:** $1,700, cedar, made to order in LA, 5-7 week lead. https://claudehome.com/products/plank-chair-by-orca
- **Spec'd size:** 24" W x 30" D x 36" H, 75 lb. Natural cedar (left to silver) or "ebonized" (black semi-transparent stain).
- **Reference photo:** `Assets/Marina Lounge Furniture/orca-plank-chair-reference.jpg`
- **Why it's easy:** the whole chair is one board size (2x12) with square cuts and two angle cuts per side. No joinery, no curves, no slats. Six boards, screws, done.

### How it's built (read off the photo)

Each side is a triangle: a 2x12 base rail lying on edge on the ground, and a 2x12 rear strut rising from the back corner of the rail at roughly 65 degrees. The two back planks screw to the front face of the struts. The two seat planks sit on top of the base rails and overhang the front. A 2x12 cross-leg on edge sits under the front of the seat, and a 2x12 stretcher ties the two base rails together at the back. That's it.

### Cut list (all 2x12, actual 1.5" x 11.25")

| Part | Qty | Length | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Back planks | 2 | 42" | square ends, 1" gap between |
| Seat planks | 2 | 24" | square ends, 1" gap, front overhang ~3" |
| Side base rails | 2 | 30" | on edge, on the ground |
| Rear struts | 2 | 44" | ~25 deg off square both ends, screw to inside-rear of base rail |
| Front cross-leg | 1 | 21" | on edge, under seat front, between base rails |
| Rear stretcher | 1 | 21" | on edge, between base rails behind the seat |

~27 linear feet total, so **four 2x12 x 8' boards per chair** with offcuts. Seat height lands around 13" (low-slung lounge, that's the look). Add a 2x4 riser under the seat planks if you want 15".

Hardware: 3" exterior structural screws (GRK or Spax), countersunk. Carriage bolts at the strut-to-rail joint if you want the hardware to show as part of the look.

### Wood and cost (rough, NOLA, unverified until someone calls)

| Wood | ~Cost per chair | Call |
|---|---|---|
| **Cypress** | ~$200 | Do this. Local, rot and bug resistant, on-brand for a NOLA marina. Riverside Lumber (Metairie) or any local cypress mill. |
| Cedar | ~$300 | Matches the original, harder to find in 2x12 here. |
| Pressure-treated | ~$110 | Cheapest. Only if stained black (ebonized look hides the PT green). |

Plus ~$15 screws per chair. Labor: a carpenter who's done one should knock out a chair in about an hour. Material for a set of 6 in cypress ≈ $1,300, which is less than one retail chair.

### Finish

- Natural: nothing. Let it silver out like the original. Easiest, zero maintenance.
- Ebonized: one coat black semi-transparent exterior stain (Cabot or Behr semi-transparent, "black" or "ebony"). Re-coat every 2-3 years.
