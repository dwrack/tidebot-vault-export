# Indeed posting: Operations and Communications Manager

**Job title:** Operations and Communications Manager (Captain)
**Location:** New Orleans, LA 70129 (Paris Road marina, on the bayou)
**Job type:** Part-time, 4 days a week (about 32 hours), weekends included
**Pay:** $24 to $28 an hour, based on experience. Extra $100 to $150 on days you captain a boat. Tips on boat days. Quarterly bonus for hitting numbers we agree on up front.
**Apply:** info@nolapartybarges.com (Indeed apply is fine too)

## Description

We run party boats and tiki boats out of a marina on the bayou, about 20 minutes from downtown New Orleans, plus kayak swamp tours nearby. The season barely stops, and we have more coming: events at the marina, corporate parties, and a beer and wine license.

We need one person who keeps the whole operation moving four days a week so the owners can build. It's a real job with real responsibility, and the days are different every time.

**What you'll do**
- Run communications: answer the crew, guests and vendors fast, keep the Slack channel clean, post the daily plan and the weather calls.
- Run logistics: crew schedule, who's on which boat, backups when someone calls out, supply and fuel runs, errands.
- Help us grow the business: marketing, photos and short videos on the water, the daily Story, reviews and listings, and new ideas for events, corporate groups and repeat guests.
- Use AI every day. We work with Claude to draft messages, build checklists, track numbers and chase loose ends. You'll be expected to use it and to find better ways to use it.
- Captain a boat. Captain days pay extra.

**What we're looking for**
- Comfortable using Claude, ChatGPT or other AI tools for real work. Tell us how you've used them.
- A USCG captain's license, or a real plan to get one. We'll help you through it, and it should be done within your first few months.
- Focused on growing a business, not just clocking in. You think about what brings in more guests and what saves time, and you act on it.
- You've run a schedule or a crew before: bar, restaurant, events, dispatch, charters, military, anywhere people depended on you showing up.
- You write clearly and answer quickly.
- You stay calm around a loud, happy group.
- You can work weekends.

**Nice to have:** TIPS certification (we'll pay for it), TWIC card, First Aid/CPR, event or sales experience, comfortable driving a 15-passenger van.

## How to apply

Send a short note to info@nolapartybarges.com. Tell us about a day or a shift that went sideways and what you did. A resume helps but the story matters more.
