## The funnel fix that gates everything

We measured this and it is the reason more top-of-funnel budget is the wrong move right now:

- We pay **~$2.83** to open a Messenger conversation.
- **97% of 626 Messenger sessions ghosted** (610 of them). 59% of all sessions were one message and gone.
- Only **~6%** of non-email contacts leave a phone or an email. When Meta's 24-hour window shuts, they are unreachable, permanently.
- Asking for a phone works **6x better** than not asking (12% vs 2%) — and we ask one time in seven.

Every dollar of new TOF spend buys leads into that. Fix the capture first, then scale. The v2 prompt and slow-burn nudge sequence are written and waiting on David's approval — that approval is worth more than a budget increase.

**Highest-leverage single unlock in the whole plan:** Messenger Marketing Messages one-tap opt-in, enabled in Meta page settings. It is the only mechanism that reaches the 97% after the 24-hour window closes. Needs enabling in page settings and OpenCX workflow support confirmed. — **David**
