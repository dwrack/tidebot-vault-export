# First Mate / Utility Captain, New Orleans Party Barge (part-time, day rate)

We run tiki boats and party boats on the water out of a marina in New Orleans, plus kayak tours nearby. We need one more person who can step in wherever the day needs them.

**What the job actually is**
- Some days you're on a boat as crew. Some days you're the captain. Some days you're driving the van to pick up a kayak group, or answering the phone and moving a booking.
- You're the person we call when a fourth boat books, when a guide calls out, or when a storm means the boats need to be tied down right.
- Weekdays and weekends both. We'll build the schedule around your availability, but "flexible" is the whole point of the role.

**Who we're looking for**
- You have a USCG captain's license, or you're willing to get one. We'll pay for the course.
- You run on checklists. Pre-trip, post-trip, fuel, lines, cleanup. You don't need to be reminded.
- You're calm with guests who are three drinks in and loud.
- You notice what's about to go wrong and handle it before someone asks.
- Clean driving record. Comfortable with a 15-passenger van and a trailer is a plus.
- TIPS certification, or willing to get it (we'll pay).
