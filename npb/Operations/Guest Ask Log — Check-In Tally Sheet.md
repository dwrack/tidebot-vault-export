# Guest Ask Log — Check-In Tally

Print one per day. Tape it to the check-in counter. Every time a guest asks for
something, make one mark in the row, in the column for what happened.

Takes three seconds. Do not write sentences. Marks only.

**The three columns are the whole point:**

- **SOLD** — we had it, they bought it. (Are we surfacing it enough?)
- **OUT** — we sell it, we were out. (Par level is wrong. This is money on the floor.)
- **NOPE** — we don't carry it at all. (This is the new-product list.)

## Why we're doing this

We are guessing at what to stock. JT already knows the top two asks cold ("where is
the bathroom" and "where do I buy liquor, I thought this was a booze cruise") because
he hears them a thousand times a weekend. Nobody has ever written down number three
through number ten, so nobody can rank them or put a dollar figure on them.

A thousand people come through on a Friday and Saturday. If forty of them ask for ice
and we're out, that's not a vibe, that's a number, and it's the number that tells
Sophia what her par levels should be.

Ties to: Beverage Program — Sophia Bevens Plan (Phase 1 asks "what runs out"),
Deckhand — On-Boat Revenue & Tips Playbook (cooler upsell), Container Bar — Business
Plan Draft, tracker #16 (portapotty).
