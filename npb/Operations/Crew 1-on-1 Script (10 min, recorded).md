# Crew 1-on-1 Script (10 min, recorded)

Created 2026-09-09 for the Fri 9/11 round. First round was JT on 8/29. Cadence after this: quarterly, or 30 days after any new hire.

## Why we do these (for Jeffrey / Michael)

- Catch problems before they blow up on a boat in front of 100 customers (the Ben situation).
- Know who's a keeper, who's coasting, who wants to move up to captain. Pay and scheduling decisions get made off this.
- Makes it clear who runs the crew. The person asking the questions is the boss.
- Recording = David and Michael can watch remotely without flying in. Also proof of what was said if someone later gets let go.

## Rules

- 10 min each. Set a timer, say so up front.
- Ask permission to record. "This is so David and Michael can hear it straight from you, not filtered through me."
- Don't fill silence. Count to five. Deckhands talk when you shut up.
- If they give a one-word answer: "Give me an example." Every question has a follow-up for that.
- Manager records a 60-second take on each person AFTER they leave, same video. Strengths, weaknesses, keep/coach/cut.

## The 7 questions

1. **Tell me about one trip this week that went well. What did you do on it?**
   Follow-up: what did the guests say when they got off?

2. **What's the most annoying part of your shift?**
   Follow-up: if you could change one thing about how we run a trip, what?

3. **A guest is hammered and won't sit down while we're moving. What do you do, step by step?**
   (Swap in: someone goes in the water / two guests start fighting / motor dies mid-bayou.)
   This is the training check. Vague answer here = they need drilling, not a raise.

4. **Rate yourself 1 to 10 on: showing up on time, energy with guests, cleanup after. Why not a 10?**
   Three numbers. Fast, and the "why not" is where the honesty is.

5. **What do you need from me or Jeffrey that you're not getting?**
   Follow-up: gear, hours, training, a straight answer on something?

6. **Where do you want to be in six months? Still here, captain, somewhere else?**
   No judgment. Someone leaving in October is fine to know in September.

7. **Anything I didn't ask that I should know about?**
   Leave it open. This is where you hear about the coworker problem.

## Scoring after (manager only, 60 sec on camera)

- Keep / coach / cut
- Captain track: yes / maybe / no
- One thing to follow up on by next 1-on-1
