# Buffalo Bayou Kayak — SOP Contractor Onboarding

*Updated 2026-04-21*

---

All guides at Buffalo Bayou Kayak are independent contractors under an Independent Contractor Agreement (ICA). Not W-2 employees. This matters for how we onboard, pay, and work together.

---

## Step 0: Read How We Run Things

Before anything else, the new guide reads "How We Run Things — Buffalo Bayou Kayak" and completes the Culture Quiz. This isn't optional. If someone reads the five principles and it doesn't resonate, better to find out now.

---

## Pre-Hire Requirements

These must be verified before a guide leads any tour or rental session:

| Requirement | Detail |
|---|---|
| CPR / First Aid Certification | Current certification from ARC, AHA, or equivalent. Must be valid through at least 6 months from start date. |
| Background Check | Clear background check (we'll provide the link) |
| Swim Proficiency | Must be a competent swimmer. Self-attestation plus in-water assessment during orientation. |
| Age | 18+ |
| Physical Fitness | Able to paddle for 2-3 hours, assist guests in/out of kayaks, handle equipment |

## Onboarding Steps

### 1. Offer

- Verbal or written offer with compensation rate, expected availability, and tour types
- Send ICA via DocuSeal for e-signature
- Send link for background check
- Confirm CPR/First Aid cert is current (or schedule certification before start)

### 2. Orientation (Day 1)

- Tour of Allen's Landing site: launch area, equipment storage, parking, guest flow
- Equipment walkthrough: kayak types (single, tandem), SUPs, paddles, PFDs, first aid, communication devices
- FareHarbor training: how to read the manifest, check waiver status, find booking-specific waiver links
- Review all SOPs together (Pre-Tour, Coast Guard & Compliance, Guest Communication)
- Waiver procedure walkthrough: booking-specific links, no generic QR codes, no unsigned launches
- Weather hold/cancel protocol
- Incident report procedure
- Meet other guides if possible

### 3. Shadow Tours (2-3 tours)

New guide rides along on tours led by experienced guides. They observe:

- Guest check-in flow
- Waiver verification process
- Safety briefing delivery
- Route navigation and narration
- Guest management on the water
- Equipment handling and post-tour cleanup

After each shadow tour, debrief with the lead guide. What went well, what questions came up.

### 4. Assisted Tours (1-2 tours)

New guide leads the tour with an experienced guide present as backup. The experienced guide:

- Lets the new guide handle check-in, waivers, briefing, and tour
- Steps in only if needed for safety or major guest experience issues
- Provides detailed feedback after each tour

### 5. Solo Sign-Off

Management or lead guide confirms the new guide is ready to lead tours independently based on:

- Consistent execution of check-in and waiver procedures
- Solid safety briefing delivery
- Confident route navigation and narration
- Good guest management and communication
- Proper equipment handling and post-tour procedures
- Calm under pressure (weather changes, nervous guests, equipment issues)

## Ongoing

- Guides maintain current CPR/First Aid certification at all times. Lapsed cert = no tours until renewed.
- ICA is reviewed and re-signed annually or when terms change
- Periodic check-ins on tour quality (guest reviews, shadow observations)
- Any changes to SOPs communicated and acknowledged before next shift
