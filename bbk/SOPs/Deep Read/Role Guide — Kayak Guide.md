# Role Guide — Kayak Guide
## Buffalo Bayou Kayak

### What This Role Is

You're the face of Buffalo Bayou Kayak on the water. Guests remember their guide more than anything else about the experience. Your job is to keep people safe, show them a great time on Buffalo Bayou, and make them feel like they're in good hands from the first handshake to the last paddle stroke.

Guides at Buffalo Bayou Kayak are **1099 contractors** under Davey Jones' Locker.

### Requirements

- Current CPR/First Aid certification (must be on file before your first trip)
- Clear background check
- Age 18 or older
- Swim proficiency (assessed in-water during orientation, not verbal confirmation)
- Physical fitness sufficient for paddling in Houston heat and humidity
- Signed SOP acknowledgment via DocuSeal

### What Makes a Good Guide Here

The Bayou is a different animal than a lake. Water quality varies, levels change, and Houston weather is its own challenge. The guides who thrive here share a few things:

They know the Bayou. Water levels after rain, where the current picks up, which stretches have debris. You're not just leading a paddle, you're navigating a waterway that changes week to week.

They handle heat. Houston summers are brutal. You need to manage your own hydration and energy while keeping an eye on guests who might be underestimating the conditions. A guest showing signs of heat exhaustion is a medical situation, and you need to spot it early.

They read the group. A corporate team-building outing needs different energy than a couple on a date paddle. Adjust on the fly.

They stay calm when things go sideways. Someone capsizes, weather rolls in, a guest panics, water levels start rising. Your composure keeps it from escalating.

### Training Progression

1. **Orientation** — Equipment overview, launch site walkthrough on Buffalo Bayou, FareHarbor basics, Weather Hold Protocol, Safety Briefing Script memorization, membership program overview, in-water swim assessment
2. **Shadow trip** — You ride along and observe an experienced guide run a full tour
3. **Co-guide trip** — You lead the tour with an experienced guide there as backup
4. **Debrief** — Honest conversation about what went well and what needs work
5. **Solo sign-off** — Manager or senior guide observes you running a trip independently. If you pass, you're on the schedule.

This usually takes 1-2 weeks depending on trip availability and your schedule.

### Code of Conduct

- Show up 30 minutes before your trip start time. Every time.
- No alcohol or drugs before or during trips. Zero tolerance.
- Treat every guest like they chose your tour over dozens of other things they could've done in Houston. Because they did.
- Keep your phone in a dry bag and use it only for safety, navigation, or logging.
- No badmouthing other tour companies, guides, or businesses.
- If you can't make a trip, notify management as early as possible. Last-minute cancellations hurt the whole operation.

### Pay and Scheduling

Guides are paid per trip. Rate depends on tour type and group size. Details are covered during onboarding.

Scheduling happens through FareHarbor. You'll see your assigned trips there. If you want more trips, communicate that to management and stay reliable with the ones you've got.

### The Membership Program

Buffalo Bayou Kayak offers a $300/year membership for unlimited rentals. Members book through the same system and get the same check-in process. As a guide, you should know the membership exists and be ready to mention it to guests who seem like repeat paddlers. It's not a hard sell, just awareness.

### Ongoing Development

- Bayou water safety and awareness training (flooding, current changes)
- First responder upgrade courses (optional but encouraged)
- Seasonal check-ins with management on performance and feedback
- Guest review highlights shared with you so you know what's landing

The guides who stick around and build a reputation here end up with the best schedules and the most trips. Be good, be reliable, be someone guests want to paddle with.
