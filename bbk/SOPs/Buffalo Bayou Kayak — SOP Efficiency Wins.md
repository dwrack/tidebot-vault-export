## FAQ Topics

These are the questions that come in repeatedly. Use for chatbot training, auto-replies, and website FAQ.

1. **Age and weight restrictions** — Kayak weight limit 450 lbs combined (tandem). No age minimum for most tours, but some tours have minimums (Bat Bridge 7+, Kemah 8+, Pedal Barge 8+, Bar Tour 21+). Kids under 13 must wear PFDs per Texas law (we require everyone to).
2. **Location and parking** — 1019 Commerce St, Houston 77002 (Sunset Coffee Building at Allen's Landing). Park at Bayou Lot (901 Commerce) or Spaghetti Warehouse lot (915 Commerce).
3. **Cancellation and weather policy** — Full refund if cancelled 24+ hours in advance. Rain = 100% refund or reschedule, guest's choice.
4. **Experience level required** — No experience needed. We provide instruction and safety briefing. Sit-on-top kayaks are stable and beginner-friendly. SUPs come with a quick lesson.
5. **Pricing and tour options** — Tours from $50-$75 depending on type. Rentals from $20/hr (single) to $85 full day (tandem). Annual membership $300 for unlimited rentals.
6. **Hours of operation** — Open daily 10am-6pm. Sunset and specialty tours may start later.
7. **Private and group bookings** — Available. Contact us for groups of 6+ for custom pricing and scheduling.
8. **Membership info** — $300/year for unlimited kayak and SUP rentals. Pays for itself in 6 single kayak sessions.
9. **What to bring** — Water, sunscreen, shoes that can get wet, clothes you don't mind getting splashed. We provide all equipment and life jackets.
10. **Restrooms** — No restroom access during tours. Use the facilities at Allen's Landing before departure.
