# Buffalo Bayou Kayak — SOP Pre-Tour Preparation

*2026-04-21 | Vessel type: kayak/paddle vessel*

---

## Day-Before Checklist

- [ ] Check FareHarbor manifest for tomorrow's bookings (headcount, tour type, special requests, group size)
- [ ] Confirm weather forecast for tour window (wind speed, precipitation, lightning risk)
- [ ] Verify enough kayaks/SUPs are available and serviceable for booked headcount plus 1-2 spares
- [ ] Charge communication device (phone, radio, or VHF if applicable)
- [ ] Check for any guest messages or special requests in FareHarbor/email
- [ ] If weather looks marginal, prepare hold/cancellation messaging

## Morning-Of Checklist

- [ ] Arrive at Allen's Landing (1019 Commerce St) minimum 45 minutes before first tour/rental
- [ ] Inspect all kayaks and SUPs assigned for the day
  - Hull integrity: no cracks, punctures, or significant scratches
  - Seats and footpegs secure (kayaks)
  - Deck pads in good condition (SUPs)
  - Drain plugs in place
- [ ] Count and inspect paddles (correct count for manifest plus spares)
- [ ] Inspect PFDs (Type III)
  - All buckles and zippers functional
  - No tears or excessive wear
  - Enough sizes available for booked guests (S, M, L, XL)
- [ ] First aid kit: stocked and accessible
- [ ] Safety whistle: on your person
- [ ] Communication device: charged, functional, on your person
- [ ] Launch site clear of debris and hazards
- [ ] Check bayou water conditions: current speed, floating debris, water level
- [ ] Stage equipment at launch area in order needed

## Guest Check-In Procedure

**Arrive at the Sunset Coffee Building at Allen's Landing. Parking at Bayou Lot (901 Commerce St) or Spaghetti Warehouse lot (915 Commerce St).**

1. **Greet guests by name** when possible (check manifest)
2. **Verify headcount** against FareHarbor manifest
3. **Check waiver status in FareHarbor for every guest:**
   - Open the specific booking in FareHarbor
   - Confirm each participant has a signed waiver tied to that booking
   - If anyone is unsigned: pull up the booking-specific waiver link on your device, have them sign right there
   - Do NOT use a generic QR code or universal waiver link
   - Do NOT let anyone launch without a signed waiver. No exceptions.
4. **Fit PFDs** to each guest. Demonstrate proper adjustment. Confirm snug fit.
5. **Deliver safety briefing** (see below)
6. **Confirm any limitations:** "Anyone have shoulder injuries, back problems, or concerns about being on the water?"
7. **Quick photo op** at launch before departure (good for social, good for the guest)

## Safety Briefing Script

Deliver this before anyone gets in a kayak or on a SUP. Every time.

> "Welcome to Buffalo Bayou Kayak. A few things before we get on the water.
>
> **Life jackets stay on the entire time.** Even if you're a strong swimmer. It's the rule, no exceptions.
>
> **Stay with the group.** I'll be leading. Don't paddle ahead or fall too far behind. If you need to stop, wave or call out.
>
> **If you flip, stay calm.** Stay with your boat. Don't try to swim to shore. I'll come to you. Your PFD will keep you floating.
>
> **Whistle signals:** One blast means stop and look at me. Three blasts means emergency, come to me immediately.
>
> **No alcohol** on this tour. If you brought any, it stays on shore.
>
> **Stay hydrated.** Houston heat is real. Drink water before we launch and bring a water bottle if you have one.
>
> Any questions before we get started?"

Adjust for SUP tours: add standing balance tips, how to kneel if you feel unstable, keep paddle across the board if you need a break.

## Weather Hold Protocol

| Condition | Action |
|---|---|
| Wind sustained >15 mph | Hold launch. Reassess in 15 min. |
| Wind gusts >20 mph | Cancel. Offer reschedule or refund. |
| Lightning detected within 10 miles | Cancel immediately. Get off water if already launched. |
| Visibility <0.25 miles (fog, rain) | Hold. Reassess in 15 min. |
| Heavy rain, no lightning | Guide judgment. Light rain is fine, heavy downpour hold. |
| Bayou water level significantly elevated | Cancel. Post-storm debris and current make conditions unsafe. |
| Heat advisory (heat index >110F) | Shorten tour or offer early morning/evening alternative. |

You do not need manager approval to cancel for weather. Document the conditions, notify the guest, process through FareHarbor.

## Post-Tour Checklist

- [ ] All kayaks/SUPs accounted for and returned to staging area
- [ ] Rinse all equipment with fresh water (hulls, paddles, PFDs)
- [ ] Inspect for any damage sustained during tour
- [ ] Hang PFDs to dry properly
- [ ] Stow paddles
- [ ] Clear launch site of any guest belongings or trash
- [ ] Log any equipment issues (note which vessel, what's wrong)
- [ ] Log tour completion in system (headcount, conditions, any incidents)
- [ ] If last tour of day: secure all equipment, lock storage
