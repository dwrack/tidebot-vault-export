# Guest Check-In Steps
**First impression sets the tone for the whole tour. Make it count.**

1. Greet guests by name when possible (pull from FareHarbor booking)
2. Verify headcount against the booking in FareHarbor
3. Check waiver status in FareHarbor for each guest on that specific booking
4. If a guest hasn't signed, have them complete it on-site using the booking-specific waiver link (do NOT use a generic QR code)
5. For membership holders ($300/year), confirm active membership status
6. Fit each guest with a Type III PFD, adjust straps, confirm snug fit
7. Ask about swimming ability and comfort level on water
8. Deliver safety briefing (see Deep Read: Safety Briefing Script)
9. Cover paddle technique basics: grip, forward stroke, turning
10. Confirm any physical limitations, injuries, or concerns
11. Let guests know there is no restroom access during the tour, so use facilities before launch if available nearby
12. Assign kayaks and help guests into boats at water's edge

> [!warning]
> Never skip the waiver check. Every person on the water must have a signed waiver tied to their specific booking, including membership holders.

> [!tip]
> The "no restrooms on the water" heads-up prevents a lot of mid-tour distress. Mention it early and casually.
