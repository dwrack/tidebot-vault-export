# Compliance Inspection Checklist
**Texas Parks & Wildlife Department (TPWD) compliance. Stay ahead of inspections.**

1. Confirm all commercial kayaks have current TPWD vessel registration
2. Verify registration stickers are visible and not peeled/faded
3. Confirm commercial outfitter permit is current (if required for Buffalo Bayou operation area)
4. Check that every vessel on the water has a USCG-approved Type III PFD for each occupant
5. Verify PFDs are readily accessible (not buried under gear)
6. Confirm a throwable flotation device (Type IV) is available at the launch site
7. Check that a sound-producing device (whistle) is on each guide
8. Confirm all guides have CPR/First Aid certification on file
9. Check that the business liability insurance policy is current
10. Verify waivers are being collected for every guest via FareHarbor
11. Confirm no expired or recalled PFDs are in rotation (check manufacture date)
12. Verify all guides have signed SOP acknowledgment on file via DocuSeal
13. Review incident log for any unreported events

> [!warning]
> TPWD can conduct on-water inspections without notice. Every vessel must be compliant every day.

> [!tip]
> Buffalo Bayou water levels can change quickly after rain. If the water rises significantly, check whether current conditions are safe for commercial operations before launching.
