# Contractor Offboarding Checklist
**Clean exits protect the business. Complete every step regardless of circumstances.**

1. Notify contractor of end date in writing (text or email)
2. Remove from FareHarbor scheduling rotation
3. Remove from group text/Slack channel
4. Collect any company property: radios, dry bags, branded gear, keys
5. Revoke access to launch site storage if keyed
6. Confirm final trip count for pay reconciliation
7. Issue final payment per contractor agreement terms
8. Remove from any shared accounts or logins
9. Archive their signed DocuSeal SOP acknowledgment (keep on file, don't delete)
10. Document reason for separation in contractor file
11. If involuntary, note any incidents or performance issues in file
12. Update equipment access list

> [!tip]
> Keep it professional even if the split isn't friendly. A clean offboarding avoids drama and protects the business.

> [!warning]
> Collect keys and revoke access the same day as the final trip. Don't let it slide.
