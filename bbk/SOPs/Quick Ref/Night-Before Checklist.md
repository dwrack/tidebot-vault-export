# Night-Before Checklist
**Confirm tomorrow's trips are locked in before you leave for the night.**

1. Open FareHarbor and review all bookings for tomorrow
2. Confirm headcount per trip and note any special requests or accessibility needs
3. Check for any $300/year membership holders with reservations (they book like anyone else but their payment structure is different)
4. Check weather forecast for wind speed, lightning risk, and temperature
5. If wind >15 mph or storms forecast, flag for potential Weather Hold (see Deep Read: Weather Hold Protocol)
6. Confirm guide(s) assigned to each trip and text to verify availability
7. If a guide is unavailable, contact backup guides immediately
8. Verify equipment count matches tomorrow's bookings: kayaks, PFDs, paddles
9. Confirm launch site on Buffalo Bayou is clear and accessible
10. Charge any electronics (radios, phone batteries)

> [!warning]
> Never assume a guide confirmed just because they were scheduled. Get a text-back confirmation every time.

> [!tip]
> Membership holders sometimes book more casually since they have unlimited access. Still verify their waiver and check-in like any other guest.

- [ ] Membership bookings identified
- [ ] Weather checked and flagged if needed

- [ ] Equipment count verified
