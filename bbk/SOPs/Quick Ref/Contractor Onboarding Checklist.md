# Contractor Onboarding Checklist
**Manager-facing. Every step completed before a new guide touches a guest.**

1. Send culture doc and have contractor confirm they've read it
2. Collect signed W-9 (guides are 1099 contractors)
3. Verify CPR/First Aid certification is current (copy on file)
4. Run background check and confirm clear
5. Confirm age 18+ (required)
6. Conduct swim proficiency assessment (in-water during orientation, not just verbal)
7. Assess physical fitness for the role (paddling in Houston heat is demanding)
8. Send SOP documents for review and collect signed SOP acknowledgment via DocuSeal
9. Schedule orientation session covering:
   - Equipment overview (kayaks, PFDs, paddles)
   - Launch site walkthrough on Buffalo Bayou
   - FareHarbor booking system basics
   - Weather Hold Protocol
   - Safety Briefing Script (must be able to deliver from memory)
   - Membership program overview ($300/year, unlimited rentals)
   - No restroom access on tours (how to communicate this to guests)
10. Schedule shadow trip: new contractor observes experienced guide
11. Schedule co-guide trip: new contractor leads with experienced guide present
12. Debrief after co-guide trip and address any gaps
13. If ready, schedule solo sign-off trip with manager or senior guide observing
14. Add to scheduling rotation in FareHarbor
15. Add to group text/Slack channel

> [!warning]
> The swim proficiency check must be an actual in-water assessment during orientation. Verbal confirmation is not enough for Buffalo Bayou Kayak.

> [!warning]
> DocuSeal SOP acknowledgment is required before any on-water activity. No exceptions.

> [!tip]
> No solo trips until the sign-off trip is completed and approved.
