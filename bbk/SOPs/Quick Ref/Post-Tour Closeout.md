# Post-Tour Closeout
**Tour's not done when guests get off the water. Close it out right.**

1. Help guests out of kayaks at the landing area
2. Collect all paddles and PFDs from guests
3. Thank each guest personally
4. Ask if they'd be willing to leave a review: "If you had a great time, a Google review really helps us out"
5. For non-members, mention the $300/year membership for unlimited rentals if they seemed to enjoy themselves
6. Hand out or mention any business cards, sticker giveaways, or referral info
7. Rinse and inspect each kayak for new damage
8. Rack or secure all boats
9. Rinse paddles and store upright or hanging
10. Rinse PFDs with fresh water (Bayou water can carry sediment and bacteria)
11. Clean up launch site: pick up any trash, move cones/signage
12. Log the trip in FareHarbor: note actual headcount, no-shows, any incidents

> [!tip]
> The membership mention works best right after a great tour. "If you want to come back whenever, we have a $300 annual membership for unlimited rentals." Simple, no pressure.

> [!warning]
> Always rinse PFDs after Bayou use. The water quality varies and gear left dirty degrades fast in Houston heat.
