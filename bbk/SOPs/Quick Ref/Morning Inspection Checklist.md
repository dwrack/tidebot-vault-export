# Morning Inspection Checklist
**Every piece of gear gets eyes on it before guests arrive.**

1. Count all kayaks needed for today's trips and rentals
2. Inspect hulls for cracks, deep scratches, or soft spots
3. Check all paddles for damage (blade cracks, shaft bends, ferrule looseness on adjustables)
4. Count PFDs (Type III) and confirm enough for max headcount plus 2 spares
5. Inspect each PFD: buckles click, straps aren't frayed, no rips in fabric
6. Check first aid kit: stocked, dry, sealed
7. Inventory guide bag: whistle, phone in dry bag, sunscreen, bug spray, throw rope
8. Test radios if using for multi-guide trips
9. Check launch area on Buffalo Bayou for hazards, debris, or floating obstructions
10. Check water level and current speed (Bayou levels fluctuate, especially after rain)
11. Confirm any stored gear is dry and not mildewy from Houston humidity

> [!warning]
> A PFD with a broken buckle is not a PFD. Pull it from rotation immediately and mark it for repair or replacement.

> [!tip]
> Houston humidity destroys gear fast. Check PFD straps for mildew and foam degradation more often than you think you need to.
