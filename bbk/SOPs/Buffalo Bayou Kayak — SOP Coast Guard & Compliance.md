Kayaks and SUPs operated as commercial livery vessels are classified as uninspected vessels. They don't require USCG inspection, but they are still subject to federal and state safety equipment requirements.

## Required Safety Equipment (Per Vessel)

| Equipment | Requirement | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| PFD (Type III or higher) | One USCG-approved PFD per person | Must be worn at all times on our tours. Not just "accessible," worn. |
| Sound-Producing Device | Whistle or horn | Guide carries whistle. One per vessel recommended. |
| Visual Distress Signal | Not required on inland waters for vessels <16 ft | Keep a flashlight available for sunset/night tours |
| Navigation Lights | Required if operating between sunset and sunrise | Sunset tours, Full Moon tours, Bat Bridge tours, Fireworks tours all need lights. White light visible 360 degrees, visible at 2 miles. Attach to PFD or vessel. |
| Fire Extinguisher | Not required for open kayaks/SUPs | N/A |
| First Aid Kit | Not federally required, operationally required by BBK | Guide carries one for every tour |

**Navigation lights are critical for us.** Several of our tours operate at dusk or after dark (Sunset Skyline, Bat Bridge, Full Moon, Fireworks). Every vessel on the water after sunset must have a white light. This is federal law, not optional.

## Texas Parks & Wildlife Department Requirements

Texas has additional regulations for commercial paddle craft operations:

- **Livery operator requirements:** As a commercial livery (renting kayaks/SUPs to the public), we must provide a USCG-approved PFD for each person and give safety instructions before launch
- **Children under 13:** Must wear a PFD at all times when on any vessel (this is Texas state law regardless of our policy, which requires all ages to wear PFDs)
- **Registration:** Kayaks and SUPs propelled solely by paddle are exempt from Texas vessel registration
- **BUI (Boating Under the Influence):** Texas law. BAC 0.08% or above while operating a vessel is a criminal offense. Our policy is stricter: no alcohol on any kayak or SUP experience
- **Waste discharge:** No discharge of any waste into Buffalo Bayou. Pack out everything.
- **TPWD game wardens** have authority to conduct safety inspections on the water at any time. Comply fully and politely.

## Incident Report Procedure

Any of these require an incident report:

- Capsizing (even if no injury)
- Guest injury of any kind
- Equipment failure during a tour
- Near-miss with motorized vessel traffic
- Medical event (heat exhaustion, allergic reaction, panic attack)
- Property damage
- Guest complaint involving safety

### Steps

1. **Ensure everyone is safe first.** Render aid. Get off the water if needed.
2. **Call 911 if there's a medical emergency or someone is in danger.**
3. **Notify management immediately** by phone: (832) 305-5794
4. **Document within 2 hours** using the template below
5. **Preserve any evidence:** photos of equipment, conditions, injuries (with consent)
6. **Do not admit fault or speculate about cause** with guests or third parties
7. **File with USCG if required:** Incidents involving death, disappearance, injury requiring medical treatment beyond first aid, or property damage >$2,000 must be reported to the nearest USCG Sector (Sector Houston-Galveston)
