### Notes on Complaint Responses

- Respond within 4 hours during business hours, 12 hours max
- Acknowledge the specific issue. Don't use generic apology language.
- Propose a concrete resolution (refund, complimentary tour, partial credit)
- If the complaint involves safety, escalate to management immediately regardless of time of day
- If it's a public review, respond publicly with a short acknowledgment and invite them to continue the conversation privately
- Document every complaint in the incident/feedback log
