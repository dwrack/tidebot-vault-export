# Creator Outreach Playbook: Buffalo Bayou Kayak

**Owner:** Connor Champaign
**Business Instagram:** @buffalobayoukayak (or current handle)

---

## What This Is

Proactive outreach to local content creators, offering them a free kayak session in exchange for nothing. No contract, no posting requirements, no talking points. If they make something cool, great. If they just enjoy the paddle, that's fine too.

This is different from the UGC Repost Playbook (which is reactive, finding content that already exists). This is about building relationships with creators before they've ever been in your kayak.

## Why This Works for Buffalo Bayou Kayak

We sell the experience creators want to film. Urban kayaking through downtown Houston, paddling past skyscrapers, bridges, and wildlife in the middle of the fourth-largest city in America. That juxtaposition is the hook: herons and turtles against a skyline backdrop. People don't expect nature in the middle of Houston, and that surprise factor is what makes content from our tours shareable.

The "wildlife in the middle of Houston" angle is genuinely unique. Nobody else owns that positioning.

The cost is nearly zero. An empty seat on a tour that's going out anyway costs us nothing. The content a creator produces from that seat would cost $500-2,000 to produce ourselves.

## Who to Target

### The Sweet Spot: Micro-Influencers (2K-50K followers)

Don't chase big accounts. You want local creators who:
- Live in or frequently visit Houston
- Post outdoor, nature, urban exploration, or photography content
- Have real engagement (comments from real people, not bot spam)
- Show original storytelling, not just lip-syncs and trending audio reposts
- Would genuinely enjoy kayaking through downtown

### Creator Types to Prioritize

| Type | Why | Example |
|------|-----|---------|
| Houston outdoor/nature creators | Direct alignment with the product | Birders, hikers, nature photographers in HTX |
| Urban adventure/exploration creators | The "nature in the city" angle is their thing | Urban explorers, hidden gems accounts |
| Photography/videography creators | The skyline + water + wildlife combo is visually rich | Landscape, architecture, drone content |
| City/local life creators | Their audience IS your customer | "Things to do in Houston" accounts |

## How to Reach Out

### DM from the Business Instagram Account

Not your personal account. Not email. A direct message from the business page.

### First DM: Cold Outreach

Keep it short. No pitch deck energy.

**Template A: Simple invite:**

> Hey [name]! Love your content, especially [reference a specific recent post]. We run kayak tours on Buffalo Bayou, right through downtown Houston. Skyscrapers on one side, herons and turtles on the other. We'd love to have you out on a paddle. No strings, no posting requirements, just come enjoy it. Want us to set something up?

**Template B: For travel creators:**

> Hey [name]! Saw you're hitting Houston soon. We run kayak tours on Buffalo Bayou right through the middle of downtown. Wildlife in the middle of a major city, it's one of those things people don't expect. We'd love to have you out while you're here. Free, no catch. Let us know if you want us to hold a spot.

**Template C: For creators who posted about Buffalo Bayou or similar:**

> Hey [name]! Saw your [post about Buffalo Bayou Park/similar]. We run kayak tours on the bayou itself, totally different perspective from the trail. You paddle right through downtown, past the skyscrapers, tons of wildlife. Would love to have you out on the water. On us, no strings. Interested?

### Rules for Outreach
- **Reference something specific** about their content. "Love your content" alone sounds automated.
- **Don't ask them to post.** The moment you say "in exchange for a post," it becomes transactional and the content suffers.
- **One follow-up max.** If they don't reply after 3-5 days, send one casual follow-up. After that, move on.

**Follow-up DM (if no reply after 5 days):**

> Hey just bumping this in case it got buried. No pressure either way!

### If They Say Yes

> Awesome! When works for you? We run tours [days/times]. Morning has the best wildlife and light if you care about that. You can bring a friend too if you want.

Then:
- Hold the spot, confirm the day before
- Let the guide know a creator is coming (just so they're aware)
- After the tour, send a quick thank you DM: "Hope you had a good time! No ask from us, just glad you came out."

### If They Post Something

- Like it immediately
- Leave a genuine comment (something specific about their content, not "Thanks for sharing!")
- Repost using the UGC Playbook process (ask permission, download, repost with tag)
- Flag top performers for ads per the UGC playbook
- Add them to your "relationship" tier for future invites

### If They Don't Post

That's fine. Don't follow up asking where their post is. The relationship still exists. They had a good experience, they'll mention you organically at some point, and you can invite them back next season.

### Relationship Tiers

- **One-timers**: Came, enjoyed it, maybe posted, no ongoing relationship. That's fine.
- **Repeat creators**: Loved it, posted, want to come back. Invite them quarterly, or flag perfect weather days.
- **Brand friends**: Genuinely love what you do, mention you unprompted, bring friends. These are gold. Give them first access to everything.

| Task | How often | Time |

**Total: ~1-2 hours per month.** This is not a heavy lift.
