## What Happened to Bayou City Adventures

The original kayak rental company at 1520 Silver St / Lost Lake Visitor Center is gone. Their domains:

| Domain | Status |
|--------|--------|
| bayoucityadventures.org | Live BUT redirects to **www.kante.berlin** (a Berlin nightclub) |
| bayoucityadventures.com | 404 dead |
| www.bayoucityadventures.com | 404 dead |
| bayoucityadventure.us | DNS fail (dead) |
| bayoucityadventures.us | DNS fail (dead) |

The .org redirect to a German nightclub is the best pitch hook — editors hate finding out their "helpful resource" link is now pointing to a dance club in Berlin.

Note: **bcoadventures.com** ("Bayou City Outdoors") is a completely different organization — a Houston hiking/social club, still active. Do not confuse the two or target their links.
