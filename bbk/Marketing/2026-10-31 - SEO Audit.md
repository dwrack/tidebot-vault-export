## Executive summary
BBK owns the map pack: #1 for every money keyword from the dock and from Discovery Green, 4.8 stars on 596 reviews. The organic side is weaker than it looks. The page that ranks best on the money keywords is `/other-houston-experiences/`, a catch-all page titled "Other Houston Experiences" that mostly sells the sibling pedal barge and Kemah tour. The real tour pages sit lower. The blog is still dead: 11 posts from Nov 2023, 21 drafts staged in the vault, zero published, while "houston bat colony" (6,600/mo) and "waugh bridge bats" (880/mo) have no BBK page ranking. Top 3 priorities: (1) publish the bat guide, (2) fix the Other Houston Experiences page, (3) clean up the duplicate GBP listing.
