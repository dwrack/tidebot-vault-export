## Variant 1 — Static: "Four Hours, Not Whenever We Get To It"

**Angle:** Four Hours, Not Whenever We Get To It. First execution.
**Evidence:** "Respond within 4 hours during business hours, 12 hours max." (SOP Guest Communication Templates, "Notes on Complaint Responses," verbatim, owned.)
**Objection handled:** If something goes wrong with my booking, will anyone actually get back to me, or does it disappear into a small-business inbox.
**Hook (draft):** "Four hours. That's our max response window, business hours."
**Body framework:** Ogilvy factual-specific proof, a real SLA number stands in for the vague "we care about you" claim.
**Overlay text (draft):** "You message us. You hear back within 4 hours during business hours."
**Supporting line (draft):** "12 hours, max, even after hours."
**Primary text (draft):** If something comes up with your booking, you're not shouting into a void. We respond within 4 hours during business hours, 12 hours max outside of them. A real person answers, not a form letter. Houston Skyline Tour, $55/person, 2 hours, launching from Allen's Landing.
**Headline:** "We Respond Within 4 Hours. Every Time."
**CTA:** Book Your Tour. Sales/Purchase-objective campaign.
**Format spec:** Static. Text-overlay card, clock or phone-notification visual over launch-point or skyline photography; existing assets likely sufficient.
