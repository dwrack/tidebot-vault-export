## The 7 new angles

1. **You'll See Us Coming From Two Miles Out** (proven, owned). BBK's own compliance SOP requires a navigation light "visible 360 degrees, visible at 2 miles" on every after-sunset vessel. A concrete, visualizable safety fact for the night-tour line (Full Moon, sunset, Bat Bridge). Distinct from the already-scripted "It's Federal Law, and We Don't Skip It" (general compliance framing).
2. **Nobody Guides Solo Until They've Passed Three Real Tests** (proven, owned). The Role Guide documents a real vetting sequence: in-water swim test, shadow trip, co-guide trip, debrief, then a manager-observed solo sign-off. Process transparency, distinct from the existing in-the-moment "Your Guide Reads Water You Can't See" and the personality-focused "The Guide Has a Name."
3. **Broken Gear Fixed Within 48 Hours, Every Time** (proven, owned). The compliance SOP's maintenance calendar states damaged equipment is logged and repaired within 48 hours. Complements, doesn't repeat, "Everything Gets Checked Before You Ever See It" (that's the daily pre-tour check; this is the year-round repair SLA).
4. **Break Even in Six Trips** (proven, owned). The $300/year membership "pays for itself in 6 single kayak sessions" (SOP Efficiency Wins, verbatim) — real, checkable math, never scripted as its own hook before. This pass's strongest and most versatile angle; given two executions (static + carousel). Note: a companion sales-script doc rounds this to "pays for itself by month three" for a once-a-month paddler, a looser figure than the SOP's precise number — flagged for David to reconcile, the SOP's number is what's used below.
5. **The Only Thing We've Lost Out Here Is Phones** (proven, owned). A real, documented guide line: "the bayou has claimed more phones than I can count." Attributed guide voice, not a guest testimonial, labeled clearly as such. Fills this pass's review-overlay slot, which had been starved for three straight passes.
6. **Two Guests Get the Full Show** (proven, owned). A two-person tour still gets run "like it's a group of twenty," per the Role Guide's own stated culture. Reassurance for hesitant solo/couple bookers, distinct from "No-Flake Booking" and "The Buddy System."
7. **Can You Actually Paddle Back?** (emerging, web/youtube). A first-person Houston Press account of an unguided canoe trip asking exactly that question, plus a thinner unconfirmed logistics data point. Rated emerging, not proven, and routed to the lower-stakes UGC format rather than a hard-claim static/carousel execution.

**No competitor is named in any script below**, per standing brand voice (`Brand Story & Values.md`: "We never bash competitors by name, and we don't need to").

**Two operational flags for David, not scripted as ad copy:** a bat-count inconsistency across owned docs (300,000 vs. 250,000), and the Bat Bridge press release's claim that the colony "doesn't leave for the winter" — which directly contradicts the already-tested "The Bats Leave for the Winter" angle. Worth resolving which is correct before either recurs in a future pass.

## Guardrails carried into scripting

- **The Only Thing We've Lost Out Here Is Phones**: the caption/label must clearly identify this as a real guide's own words, not a guest review, to avoid any fabricated-testimonial risk. Same labeling discipline pass 40 used for its non-guest citation execution.
- **Can You Actually Paddle Back?**: does not name or disparage any self-guided/rental alternative. Frames BBK's guided structure (routed timing, a guide managing pace) as the direct answer to the fear, not a comparison against a named competitor or product.
- **You'll See Us Coming From Two Miles Out**: states the fact plainly, does not imply other operators skip this federal requirement.
- **No fabricated testimonials anywhere in this batch.** Every claim in every script below traces to a real owned-document fact, a real attributed guide line, or a real, sourced third-party quote.
