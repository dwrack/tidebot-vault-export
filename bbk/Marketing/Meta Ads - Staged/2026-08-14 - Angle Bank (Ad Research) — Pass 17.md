## Angles scripted this pass

1. **You're On It, Not In It:** proven, the strongest new angle this pass and the first in the whole research run built on an actual government source (Houston-Galveston Area Council / TCEQ) instead of a review or a blog. Distinct from and stronger than pass 16's "Say the Quiet Part About the Trash" (a single litter review) and "The Ick Factor" (a blogger's phobia about a different operator). This is the health/regulatory version of the water-quality objection, not the litter or disgust version. Carries a hard guardrail: the source is about swimming and wading, not boating, and copy must never claim the water is clean or that the finding doesn't apply.
2. **The View From the Water, Not the Sidewalk:** proven, the first angle to directly answer the real "why would I pay when I can watch for free from Waugh Drive" objection, instead of just selling the bat moment in isolation the way the pass 9 Bat Bridge Reveal angle does. Built on independently confirmed tourism-board sources (downtownhouston.org, buffalobayou.org, tpwd.texas.gov), all naming the same free, no-reservation viewing spot.
3. **Team Building That's Not a Trust Fall:** emerging, one clean first-party source (BBK's own corporate-events page, fetched live this pass). Pass 16 logged "corporate/team-building" as searched with no usable evidence; this pass found the gap was in the search terms, not the product. BBK has a real, live Corporate Events page with its own CTA and contact info that has never once been turned into an ad across 9 rounds of scripting. Rated emerging rather than proven because the evidence is owned copy, not yet a third-party buyer quote, the same evidentiary category "We Clean What We Paddle" started in before it graduated to proven.

## Not scripted this pass: operational flags, not ad angles

No new operational flags surfaced this pass. The three standing flags from passes 15 and 16 were not re-investigated in depth (no new evidence appeared in the sources checked this pass) and remain open:
- The no-show/last-minute-cancellation pattern, now with a roughly two-year tail across three dated instances.
- A TripAdvisor review (Karen M, Aug 2025) describing a kayak taking on significant water mid-tour, trainee guides, and inconsistent waiver checks.
- The Peek.com service-consistency complaint.

Still hold any reliability, safety-guarantee, or punctuality-claim angle until David and Connor confirm these are addressed operationally. None of this pass's 8 scripts make that kind of claim.

## A first-party source that was deliberately NOT used as a testimonial

The Agent Instructions doc's chatbot follow-up script bank includes an unattributed, unnamed line about a corporate team ("their manager told me it was the first team event where nobody checked their phone"). That's flavor text written for the AI concierge to say in conversation, not a named, dated, attributed guest review. Using it as if it were a real corporate testimonial would risk misattributing invented-sounding copy as a customer quote. Both corporate-events variants in this pass build strictly from BBK's own verbatim site copy and booking-script language instead.
