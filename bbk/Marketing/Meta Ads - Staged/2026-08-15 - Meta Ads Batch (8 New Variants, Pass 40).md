## Variant 1 — Reel: "The Version You're Picturing Isn't the Real One"

**Angle:** Not the Advanced Course. First execution.
**Evidence:** "No special fitness level is required. Kayaking on flat water is low-impact and accessible to most adults and older children." (buffalobayoukayak.com/faq/, verbatim, owned.)
**Objection handled:** Is Buffalo Bayou the advanced/riskier kayaking option compared to a calmer alternative I could pick instead, and should I just play it safe.
**Hook (draft):** "You're picturing whitewater. This is not that."
**Body framework:** Schwartz belief-correction. Meet the reader at the wrong mental picture they already have, then replace it with the real one.
**Shot list:**
1. Text overlay: "When people hear 'Buffalo Bayou,' some picture rapids."
2. Cut: flat, glassy water, skyline in background.
3. Text overlay: "This is what it actually looks like."
4. Cut: first-timer stepping into a kayak, guide steadying it.
5. Text overlay: the FAQ line.
6. Cut: relaxed paddling on calm water.
7. End card and CTA.
**Primary text (draft):** Some people hear "Buffalo Bayou" and picture something more advanced than a first kayak trip should be. It's not. No special fitness level is required, kayaking here is low-impact and accessible to most adults and older children. Flat water, guided, three miles from downtown. Houston Skyline Tour, $55 per person, 2 hours.
**Headline:** "Not the Advanced Option. Just the One With a Skyline."
**CTA overlay text:** "See What It Actually Looks Like"
**CTA:** Book Your Tour. Sales/Purchase-objective campaign.
**Format spec:** Reel. Needs a clean flat-water establishing shot up top; likely covered by existing footage.
