### The 4 new angles, detail

- **Four Hours, Not Whenever We Get To It** — SOP Guest Communication Templates, "Notes on Complaint Responses": *"Respond within 4 hours during business hours, 12 hours max."* A specific, checkable internal SLA never scripted as its own hook before. Rated proven on the same evidentiary bar the vault has used for prior single-owned-doc facts (e.g. pass 41's 48-hour gear-repair SLA).
- **Your Guide Already Scouted the Best Angle** — Culture Quiz's "The Skyline Selfie" training scenario: *"There's an incredible angle of the skyline from about 100 yards up. Let me get the group positioned there, it'll be way better than anything from here."* and *"How about we do a group photo at the turnaround point?"* Distinct from "Nobody Staged This. That's the Point." (raw-content trend execution, not guide behavior) and "Screenshot This for the Group Chat" (logistics, not photo composition). Rated emerging: the source is a documented correct answer to a training scenario, not a witnessed recurring guest account.
- **More Time Paddling, Less Time Waiting** — Culture Quiz's "The Better Way" scenario: *"Launching beginners before experienced paddlers and staggering by 2-3 minutes makes the whole group flow better. Less bunching, less waiting, more time on the water."* Distinct from the same-day-booking stat angles and from "Ten Minutes to 'I've Got This'" (learning curve, not launch logistics). Emerging: a described best practice, not a binding SOP threshold.
- **If One Person Struggles, the Trip Doesn't Stop** — a real, fresh Google review via Birdeye: *"We booked a tour with the bat flight included. Our guide was helpful and knowledgeable. Sadly, we had one in our group that struggled with the physical aspect of the trip. But, our guide was very professional and made arrangements for the others to continue the journey."* — Nicole Blanchard, posted approximately one week before this pass, 5 stars. **Transparency flag:** this same reviewer was already used in pass 10/38 for a different sentence in the same review (arm-fatigue corroboration for the already-scripted "Honest Effort" angle). This pass uses a different sentence for a materially different claim, guide accommodation when a guest can't continue. This is a real guest testimonial and must be cited as such, quoted verbatim, attributed to Nicole Blanchard / Google review via Birdeye, not relabeled as a guide quote and not fabricated.

## Guardrails carried into scripting

- **If One Person Struggles, the Trip Doesn't Stop**: the review-overlay execution (Variant 7) must quote Nicole Blanchard's real review verbatim with correct attribution. The static execution (Variant 8) must be a clearly paraphrased brand-promise fact card, NOT presented as a quote and NOT attributed to any reviewer, so the two executions stay visibly distinct.
- **Your Guide Already Scouted the Best Angle**: this is a service-behavior claim (what a guide actively does), not a guarantee of a specific photo outcome. Keep it to the guide's proactive behavior, not a promise about photo quality.
- **More Time Paddling, Less Time Waiting**: describes launch-flow logistics, not a stopwatch guarantee. No specific minute-count promise beyond the 2-3 minute staggering detail already in evidence.
- **No fabricated testimonials anywhere in this batch.** Every claim traces to a real owned-document fact or a real, sourced guest review.
- **No competitor named anywhere**, per standing brand voice (`Brand Story & Values.md`: "We never bash competitors by name, and we don't need to").
- Objective is always Purchase/Sales, never Traffic, per this vault's standing convention.
- Brand voice throughout: guests, paddle, the bayou, downtown, urban nature, wildlife (never oversold). No "customers," no "extreme," no "expedition."
