## Variant 1 — Reel: "You'll See Us Coming From Two Miles Out"

**Angle:** You'll See Us Coming From Two Miles Out.
**Evidence:** "Navigation lights are critical for us... Every vessel on the water after sunset must have a white light. This is federal law, not optional." / "White light visible 360 degrees, visible at 2 miles." (SOP Coast Guard & Compliance, verbatim, owned.)
**Objection handled:** Is a sunset/night tour actually safe and visible to other boat traffic.
**Hook (draft):** "It's dark on the water. Here's why that's not a problem."
**Body framework:** Ogilvy factual-specific proof, let the exact spec number do the reassuring instead of an adjective.
**Shot list:**
1. Text overlay: "Full Moon Tour. After dark, on the bayou."
2. Cut: kayak silhouette against the lit-up downtown skyline at dusk.
3. Text overlay: "Every kayak carries a light visible from two miles away."
4. Cut: close-up of the navigation light glowing against dark water.
5. Text overlay: "Federal requirement. We don't skip it."
6. Cut: full group paddling under moonlight, skyline reflected on the water.
7. End card and CTA.
**Primary text (draft):** Paddling the bayou after dark sounds like it should feel riskier. It doesn't have to. Every kayak on our night tours carries a navigation light visible from two miles away, it's federal law and we don't skip it. Full Moon Tour, $65 per person, 2 hours, under the skyline.
**Headline:** "Visible From Two Miles Out, Every Night Tour"
**CTA overlay text:** "See the Bayou After Dark"
**CTA:** Book Your Tour. Sales/Purchase-objective campaign.
**Format spec:** Reel. Needs a low-light shot of the navigation light itself, likely a new capture; skyline-at-dusk footage may already exist in the asset library.

## Variant 8 — UGC: "Can You Actually Paddle Back?"

**Angle:** Can You Actually Paddle Back? Rated emerging (one strong first-person source plus one unconfirmed data point), routed to the lower-stakes UGC format rather than a hard-claim static/carousel execution. Does not name or disparage any self-guided/rental alternative.
**Evidence:** "It was then that we began to wonder: Can we actually paddle back?" (Houston Press, first-person account of an unguided bayou canoe trip, verbatim, direct-fetch confirmed.) Supporting, unconfirmed: a WebSearch synthesis describing a 1.5-hour hard-paddle outbound leg against current, treated as color, not a confirmed quote.
**Objection handled:** If I go the DIY route, can I actually paddle back against the current.
**Hook (draft):** "I almost went out on my own. Then I thought about the way back."
**Body framework:** Sugarman first-person story, the exact worry a self-guided paddler would actually have, resolved by the guided structure rather than by comparison to any named option.
**Creator talking points (draft, for a real guest/creator to film in their own words, not a real captured quote):**
1. Open on: "I almost just rented a kayak and figured out the route myself."
2. "Then I thought: what if I can't paddle back? What if the current's worse than I think?"
3. "With the guided tour, that's not a thing I have to figure out. The guide handles the pacing, the whole way."
4. "I got to just paddle and look at the skyline instead of worrying about the way back."
**Primary text (draft):** The part nobody tells you about paddling the bayou on your own: the way back is the hard part, not the way out. On a guided tour, that's not something you have to figure out, your guide manages the pace and the route the whole way. Houston Skyline Tour, $55 per person, 2 hours, round trip handled.
**Headline:** "The Way Back Is Handled"
**CTA:** Book Your Tour. Sales/Purchase-objective campaign.
**Format spec:** UGC. Requires a real guest or creator willing to film this talking-points script in their own words; does not currently exist in the asset library, flagged for a creator brief and outreach.
