## Guardrails carried into scripting

- **Yes, There Are Alligators**: heaviest-care angle this pass. Copy must keep all three qualifiers from the source together (sightings not guaranteed / alligators generally avoid humans / no guest has ever had an unsafe encounter). Never "no alligators here" (false) and never "guaranteed sighting" or a blanket "totally safe from wildlife" claim (overpromise). This is a track-record statement, not a forever guarantee.
- **The Bats Leave for the Winter**: this is an annual migration cycle, not a permanent closing. Never "last chance ever." Copy should read as true for this year's window (roughly through November) and needs review or a pause once December hits.
- **Once a Month, Then It's Gone**: CTA must route to "check the calendar for the next date." Never imply the Full Moon Tour can be booked on any night.
- **Standing By Minute 15**: "most beginners," never "everyone" or "guaranteed." A single WebSearch-summarized review attributed a guide named "David" to the paddleboard tour; that name is unverified against raw review text and is not used anywhere in this batch. If a guide is named at all, only the two confirmed active guides (Dulce Delgado, Axel Johnson, per pass 27's roster read) are eligible.
- **Before Houston Wakes Up**: the 7am departure is real friction, not just a selling point. Scripts acknowledge the early wake-up rather than hide it.
