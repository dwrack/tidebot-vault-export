## The 8 new angles

1. **Rush Hour Reversal** — "So much more peaceful being down on the water than fighting all the traffic on the bridges and roadways above." (Dave H, TripAdvisor). A concrete, named local pain (the commute) versus calm, not a generic city/nature contrast.
2. **The Internet Lied to Me** — "Well, whoever that person is, they lie" (Houston Press, on bad online advice that the paddle is "easy"). Reframes the guided tour as the smart move for a first bayou trip, without disparaging BBK's own rental line.
3. **Honest Effort** — "Your arms feel like they will fall off from paddling" (3 independent reviewers). An honest expectation-reset in Rio's own voluntary-vulnerability style, not a hidden downside.
4. **We Clean What We Paddle** — BBK's real, existing monthly bayou clean-up, turned into proof against the "is the bayou dirty" objection instead of just a reassurance line.
5. **Beat the Heat** — "Unless you like being a self-basting human in an oven" (htownbest.com). Morning-slot framing, pulled straight from Rio's own sales script ("Houston heat is real... morning slots are the move").
6. **What's Actually Down There** — alligator snapping turtles, herons, egrets, 300,000 bats. A curiosity-driven wildlife angle for a different (non-family, non-cute) audience than the reserved Family Wildlife angle.
7. **Real Reviews, Real Guests: "Blake made me feel safe"** — a genuine, named-guide TripAdvisor quote replacing the kind of placeholder pass 9 had to flag.
8. **Real Reviews, Real Guests: "So much more peaceful than traffic"** — the Dave H quote used as its own standalone review-overlay ad (Rush Hour Reversal gets both a Reel and a review-overlay execution, since it's the strongest-evidenced angle this pass).
