Companion doc: [[2026-08-14 - Angle Bank (Ad Research)]] — read that first for where these angles came from and why they're prioritized.

Pipeline note: `/ad-research` and `/ad-scripter` are not real skills in this Claude setup (checked both the global skill library and this vault's local skills — neither exists). Every script below did go through the two skills that *do* exist and were explicitly requested: **`/copy-mentors`** (channeling Ogilvy, Schwartz, Halbert, Sugarman, Bencivenga) and **`/copywriting-2.0`** (the line-level 3-test filter: can I visualize it, can I falsify it, can nobody else say it). ⚠️ **One hard flag before anything here ships:** the two review-overlay ads (#5, #6) use illustrative quotes built from language already approved in the company's own sales-agent script — they are NOT real, sourced guest reviews. Running a fabricated quote as a "review" ad would be a deceptive ad, not a copy problem.

## Format 1: Reels

### Variant 1 — "The Bat Bridge Reveal"

**Angle:** #1 in the angle bank. Highest-revenue tour ($146 avg booking), highest urgency, most visually shareable.

**Hook (on-screen, 0-2 sec):** "300,000 bats. 3 miles from downtown."

**Shot list / visual beats:**
1. Drone/wide shot — downtown skyline at golden hour, kayaks small on the bayou below. Text: "300,000 bats. 3 miles from downtown."
2. Water-level shot — paddle strokes, hands, light catching the droplets.
3. Wide shot — kayaks gliding past Allen's Landing and the Sunset Coffee Building.
4. Light drops — gold shifts to blue dusk, skyline turning to silhouette.
5. Guests go still, looking up at the Waugh Drive Bridge, waiting.
6. The emergence — a dark ribbon of bats pouring out from under the bridge, swirling against the dusk sky.
7. Guest reaction close-ups — mouths open, pointing, grinning.
8. Final wide shot — kayaks silhouetted under the bat swarm and skyline. End card + CTA.

**Voiceover / caption track:**
- "300,000 bats live under a bridge three miles from downtown Houston."
- "We live here. We didn't know either."
- "We paddle out at golden hour, hold position under the bridge, and wait for dusk."
- "Then the sky moves."
- "Common, not guaranteed. Worth the wait anyway."

**CTA overlay text:** "See the Bats for Yourself — $60"

**Primary text (caption):** 300,000 bats live under a bridge three miles from downtown Houston. We live here and didn't know either. We paddle out at golden hour, hold position under Waugh Drive Bridge, and watch dusk break the colony loose. Sunset Skyline Bat Bridge Tour. $60, 2-3 hours, ages 7+.

**Headlines:** "300,000 Bats, 3 Miles Downtown" · "Watch 300K Bats From a Kayak"

*Copy-mentors + copywriting-2.0 both applied. Strongest surviving line: "Then the sky moves" — visualizable, falsifiable, and no competitor can borrow it.*
