## The 4 new angles

BBK's own sales script already gives its human agents a tool for this ("a quick summary you can screenshot for the group chat"); this angle turns that same tool into the ad creative itself.
2. **The Whisper** (emerging). An insider, off-the-record-sounding staff recommendation for the Bat Bridge tour ("between you and me... the best thing we do"). Heaviest-guardrail angle this pass, see below before scripting or approving.
3. **Eight to Seventy-Eight** (proven). A specific, checkable age-range claim from the GBP calendar: 8-year-olds and 78-year-olds on the same tour, both finish smiling, paced to the slowest paddler. Also supplies first-party proof for pass 28's weakly-sourced "No One Gets Left Behind" angle.
4. **Adventure Doesn't Have to Mean Roughing It** (emerging). This vault's first angle built on third-party, dated 2026 travel-trend data (KAYAK.com/TikTok's WTF Report) rather than an owned document: "one in four travelers now mix light adventure with relaxation."

## Guardrails carried into scripting

- **The Whisper**: must read as one guide's honest personal favorite, never an objective "best tour" claim. Do not pair with urgency or a discount. Do not let it imply the Skyline, Full Moon, Serenity, or Paddle Board tours are lesser products, none of which this vault has any evidence are actually worse.
- **The Buddy System**: the "over a thousand five-star reviews" figure is corroborated in a second location in the same source document (the sales-close bank's line 10 area: "We've had over a thousand five-star reviews from guests who came in skeptical and left wanting to come back"), so it's a consistent internal figure, not a one-off. Still single-document sourced overall; don't dress it up as an audited stat with a specific count.
- **Eight to Seventy-Eight**: keep "reasonable health, basic mobility" as the standing qualifier this vault has used since pass 24's "We'll Work With You." Never round up to "anyone can do this" or drop the age-range specificity into a vaguer "all ages welcome" claim, which would lose the actual evidence.
- **Adventure Doesn't Have to Mean Roughing It**: the source trend report names paddleboarding specifically, not kayaking, as an example "light adventure" activity. Copy should stay honest that this is a market-wide travel-behavior finding BBK's guided kayak (and paddleboard) tours fit, not a claim that the report is about BBK or kayaking by name.
