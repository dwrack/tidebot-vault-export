## Read this before the scripts

**Angle 2 carries this pass's clearest guardrail.** While researching the Annual Membership for third-party corroboration, `wildtexastours.com` surfaced with word-for-word identical membership copy, the same address (1019 Commerce St), and the same named tours as BBK. That is not independent demand validation, it is the same operation under a probable second brand name. None of the membership scripts below imply outside corroboration, "other places are doing this," or category-wide demand. The pitch stays entirely first-party: BBK's own product, BBK's own math. Worth an Entity Canon look outside this process; not acted on here.

**Angle 4 carries the same discipline as pass 24's sober-friendly guardrail.** The "not a comedy tour" line exists to set honest expectations for BBK's own tone. It never names, implies, or compares against the sibling Pedal Barge Party Cruise or Houston Booze Cruise products, both of which lean into a louder, party-boat energy on purpose and share the `buffalobayoukayak.com` domain. The review-overlay script below is scoped strictly to BBK's own guide experience.

**A factual correction made during scripting, not in the research doc:** the research brief for this pass described Irma's, Boomtown Coffee, and Tacos Tierra Caliente as "Montrose spots." Checking the source (`Agent Instructions (Copy-Paste).md`) directly: Irma's and Boomtown Coffee are filed under "Downtown / Allen's Landing area," a few blocks from the launch dock, and Tacos Tierra Caliente is an Airline Drive food truck recommended as an after-tour stop, not tied to a neighborhood. None of the three are in Montrose per the source doc. Montrose is a real, separately documented neighborhood in the same file (Menil Collection, Rothko Chapel, Underbelly/Georgia James Tavern, Hugo's), just not where these three specific spots are. The scripts below use the Brand Story's own "eat lunch in Montrose" line as an illustrative, verbatim quote (it's real, owned copy) but do not claim Irma's, Boomtown, or Tierra Caliente sit in Montrose, since the source doesn't support that. Flagging so pass 26 doesn't inherit the mislabel.

**Angle 5 needed the full rental pricing table, not just the sales-script quotes.** The Agent Instructions hard-rules table (not the guest-chat scripts) confirms: single kayak or paddleboard $20/hour, $40/2 hours, $55/full day; tandem kayak $35/hour, $65/2 hours, $85/full day. The sales script's "$20 for an hour" quote checks out against the hard-rules table exactly. Both static and carousel executions below use the full table, not just the single-kayak headline number.
