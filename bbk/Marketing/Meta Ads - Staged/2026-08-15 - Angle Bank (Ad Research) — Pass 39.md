## Guardrails carried into scripting

- **The Divorce Boat**: must land as warm, self-aware humor, never an actual warning. Every execution below pairs the joke with BBK's real differentiator, flat, calm, forgiving water, unlike the whitewater or open-water conditions where the trope usually bites, so the payoff is genuine reassurance, not just a laugh. No BBK-specific guest quote exists for this angle; it's borrowed cultural shorthand, labeled as such everywhere it appears. Never suggest real relationship risk.
- **The Screen Break That Doesn't Feel Like One**: do not claim "no cell service" or "no signal out here." BBK is 3 miles from downtown Houston and that claim is unverified and likely false. Every execution frames the mechanism honestly: hands are full for two hours, so the phone stays in the dry bag, not a connectivity claim.
- **No competitor callouts.** Nothing below references or gestures at any named competitor, per standing brand voice in `Brand Story & Values.md`.
- **No fabricated testimonials.** Where a script uses a first-person "guest" voice (Variant 8, UGC), it is written and labeled as a creator talking-points brief, not presented as a captured real quote.
