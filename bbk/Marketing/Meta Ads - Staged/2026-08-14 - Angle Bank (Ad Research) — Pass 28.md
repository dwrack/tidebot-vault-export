# Angle Bank (Ad Research) — Pass 28

Full research write-up: [[../../Ad Research/2026-08-14 ad-research buffalo-bayou-kayak — Pass 28|Ad Research/2026-08-14 ad-research buffalo-bayou-kayak — Pass 28]]. Machine artifact: [[../../Ad Research/ad-research-pass28.json|Ad Research/ad-research-pass28.json]].

Pass 28 closed out the last unread internal-document ground in the vault (the `SOPs/Quick Ref/` folder and `Role Guide — Kayak Guide.md`, both explicitly flagged unopened by pass 27) and mined BBK's own live public FAQ page directly for the first time in 28 passes. That produced 7 new angles, 3 proven (sourced from the FAQ, direct-fetched and quoted verbatim), 4 emerging (sourced from internal SOPs or a mix of owned language and unverified competitor-review signal).

## The 7 new angles

1. **Go Before You Go** (proven). BBK's own FAQ and Guest Check-In SOP both say the same thing: no restrooms during the tour, arrive 15 minutes early to use facilities and sign the waiver. First angle built from logistics candor rather than an emotional or competitive hook.
2. **You Can't Sink This Thing** (proven per research, rescoped for scripting). BBK's FAQ and internal Incident Report Steps both confirm sit-on-top kayaks float and a guide is on the water to assist anyone who capsizes. Flagged as the strongest new angle this pass in the research report itself. Scripted with extra care, see below.
3. **Change Your Mind, No Penalty** (proven). BBK's FAQ states the real cancellation policy: full refund up to 24 hours before the tour.
4. **Everything Gets Checked Before You Ever See It** (emerging, sourcing-only). The Morning Inspection Checklist SOP, an internal-only document never mined for ad copy before, describes a specific, real daily gear-inspection routine.
5. **Your Guide Reads Water You Can't See** (emerging, sourcing-only). The Role Guide document describes what makes a guide good at this specific waterway: tracking changing water levels, current, and debris. Distinct from a personality-focused guide angle.
6. **No One Gets Left Behind** (emerging). Partly built on an unverified competitor-review signal (WebSearch summary, not raw-verified text), but scripted this pass using BBK's own sales-script language instead ("your guide adjusts the pace to the group"), which is real, owned, and checkable.
7. **Older Kids, Slower Swimmers, Rusty Arms, All Fine** (emerging). BBK's own FAQ accessibility line, corroborated by the sales script's family and seniors guidance.

## Guardrails carried into scripting

- **You Can't Sink This Thing**: this pass's heaviest-care angle. The research angle's evidence ("sit-on-tops won't sink," from the internal capsize procedure) sits directly next to the still-open Karen M complaint (Aug 2025), which describes a kayak taking on water and nearly sinking mid-tour. Both scripted variants were deliberately rewritten away from the angle's literal working title to a narrower claim: if you flip, you float, because of life vests and guide presence, not a claim about equipment failure rates. See the full batch's pre-notes for the complete reasoning and a recommendation that Connor review both scripts before launch.
- **Everything Gets Checked Before You Ever See It**: answers the same complaint's "is the equipment actually checked" question honestly, using the real checklist, but only describes the documented process, not a record claim.
- **Your Guide Reads Water You Can't See**: stays scoped to local-knowledge, not a blanket "every guide is fully experienced" claim, given the same complaint's trainee-guide question.
- **No One Gets Left Behind**: scripted using BBK's own owned pacing language only. The competitor-review signal that partly inspired this angle in the research pass is not used, quoted, or alluded to anywhere in the script, and no competitor is named, per brand voice.

## The FAQ / "You're On It, Not In It" tension

Pass 28's research flagged that BBK's FAQ language on swimming ("bring a small towel... if you want to go for a swim, sometimes you get wet from paddling") could read as in tension with the already-scripted "You're On It, Not In It" angle (pass 17), which argues a guided tour isn't contact recreation under state bacteria-advisory standards. Read against pass 17's actual script text, that angle's claim is narrow (the tour itself isn't swimming or wading) and doesn't forbid a guest from choosing to get wet or swim. Resolved as compatible; the pass 17 script was left untouched. To keep it that way, this pass's new scripts, especially You Can't Sink This Thing, stay scoped to capsize/floatation, not to whether guests can voluntarily swim. Full reasoning in the finished batch's pre-notes.
