**Angle:** Your Dog Can Come Too. First execution.

**Evidence quote:** "My husband and I went Kayaking and we were able to bring our small dog with us. He had a blast!" — Mariah Reeves, reviews.birdeye.com, direct fetch via `?page=2`, pass 77.

**Body framework:** Ogilvy proof-through-fact. Let the guest's own quote carry the claim, frame the ask-first guardrail plainly.

#### Copy-mentors pass

**Channeling:** Ogilvy, alone. A guest's real quote is the strongest available proof for a claim this vault has never made before. Ogilvy's job here is framing it cleanly and not oversteering it into a policy statement the brand hasn't actually made.

**Framework applied:** Present the quote as the entire proof, add one line connecting it to what a guest considering the same thing should actually do (call ahead), close on the fact.

**Critique of the draft:** The draft primary text is close, but it opens with "A guest brought her small dog kayaking with us," a slightly flat restatement of the quote that immediately follows, redundant. Tightened to let the quote do more of the opening work itself.

**Reasoning:** Halbert's direct-address (naming the guest's exact hesitation, "can I even bring my dog") was considered, but that's closer to what Variant 2's Reel does with the same angle. Keeping Variant 1 in Ogilvy's quote-forward register gives the batch two different entry points into the same angle.

#### Copywriting-2.0 pass

**Verdict:** Works, opening line trimmed for redundancy, headline checked and kept.

**Line-by-line:**
- Draft hook, "One guest didn't want to leave her dog behind. So she didn't," passes all three tests (visualizable, falsifiable against the actual review, ownable to this specific guest's account), kept as the card's lead-in line rather than a separate on-image hook, since a review-overlay card's real hook is the quote itself.

- Headline, "A Guest Brought Her Dog. He Had a Blast," passes all three: visualizable, falsifiable (matches the quote), ownable (this specific review). Kept.

**Sharpened final:**
