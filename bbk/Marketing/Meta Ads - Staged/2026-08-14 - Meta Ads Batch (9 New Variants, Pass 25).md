**Read this before the scripts:**

1. **The membership angle (Variants 4 and 8) has this pass's clearest guardrail.** A page at `wildtexastours.com` was found with identical membership copy and the same address as BBK, almost certainly the same operation under a second brand name, not an independent business validating demand for the product. Neither script below claims or implies outside corroboration. The pitch is entirely first-party: BBK's own product, BBK's own math on when it pays off.
2. **The honest-tone angle (Variant 6) needs the same care as pass 24's sober-friendly guardrail.** It states BBK's own tone on its own terms and never names, implies, or compares against the sibling Pedal Barge Party Cruise or Houston Booze Cruise products, both of which share the `buffalobayoukayak.com` domain and lean into a louder energy on purpose. That comparison lives in an internal sales script for live conversations only.
3. **A factual correction made during scripting.** The research brief for this pass described Irma's, Boomtown Coffee, and Tacos Tierra Caliente as "Montrose spots." The source doc (`Agent Instructions (Copy-Paste).md`) actually files Irma's and Boomtown Coffee under "Downtown / Allen's Landing area," a few blocks from the launch dock, and Tacos Tierra Caliente as an Airline Drive food truck with no neighborhood tag. None of the three are in Montrose per the source. Variants 1 and 3 below use the Brand Story's own "eat lunch in Montrose" line as a verbatim illustrative quote (it's real, owned copy) but do not claim any of the three named spots sit in Montrose, since the source doesn't support that.
4. **The rental angle (Variants 5 and 9) uses the full pricing table, not just the sales-script headline number.** Single kayak or paddleboard: $20/1hr, $40/2hr, $55/full day. Tandem kayak: $35/1hr, $65/2hr, $85/full day. Cross-checked against the Agent Instructions hard-rules table, which the guest-chat script's "$20 for an hour" quote matches exactly.
5. **No group price, private-tour minimum, or gift card amount is invented anywhere.** Where a script references group booking, it points to the phone/email quote path, not a fabricated number.
6. **Standing operational flags, unchanged from passes 15 through 24:** the no-show/cancellation pattern (roughly 2.5-year tail across four dated instances, most recently January 2026), the Karen M kayak-took-on-water/trainee-guide safety complaint, the Peek.com service-consistency complaint, and the disputed lost-belongings complaint (Nov 2025). None of the 9 scripts below make a reliability, punctuality, or belongings-safekeeping claim.
7. **Fact-check pass:** every price and hour below was cross-checked against `Buffalo Bayou Kayak — Agent Instructions (Copy-Paste).md`. Houston Skyline Tour: $55/person, 2 hours, all ages. Annual Membership: $300/year, unlimited rentals (tandem, single, or paddleboard), priority booking, special event invitations. Operating hours: daily 10:00 AM to 6:00 PM. Location: 1019 Commerce Street, Houston, TX 77002, Allen's Landing. Still worth a final human check against live FareHarbor listings before spend.
8. **Style rule:** no em dashes, no double dashes, anywhere in any headline, hook, body line, or CTA. Commas and colons only. Checked line by line.

## Format 1: Reels

### Variant 1: "What I Did Today"

**Angle:** Right Next To The Rest Of Your Day. New this pass, proven, the strongest angle in the batch on evidence quality.

**Evidence:** "Right next to the rest of your day. Paddle in the morning, eat lunch in Montrose, see a show that night. We fit into a normal Houston day, we don't take it over." (Brand Story & Values.md, named differentiator, verbatim.) Operationalized with real detail from Agent Instructions: "After your paddle, Irma's is a few blocks away for some of the best Mexican food in the city. The lemonade is famous." / "If you're coming early, grab coffee at Boomtown Coffee on Main Street." / "Tacos Tierra Caliente on Airline (it's a truck, cash only, life-changing)."

**Hook options (this pass's "What I Did" three-panel Reel trend format, per the 2026 trend finding):**
1. Text overlay, no audio: "What I did today. All of it."
2. "You don't need to clear your calendar for this."
3. "Paddle in the morning. Tacos by dark. Still just a Tuesday."

**Body framework:** Three-beat day-in-the-life, matching the "What I Did" three-panel trend mechanic exactly to BBK's own named Brand Story differentiator: the screen splits into panels that fill in as the day happens, so the pitch is structural, not just spoken.

**Shot list / visual beats:**
1. Cold open, text overlay: "What I did today."
2. Screen splits into three stacked horizontal panels, each fills in as the video plays.
3. Panel 1: guest paddling, downtown skyline behind, morning light. Tag: "Morning: paddled through downtown."
4. Panel 2: a hand holding coffee outside Boomtown Coffee on Main Street, a short walk from the dock. Tag: "Before: coffee a few blocks from the water."
5. Panel 3: a plate of tacos at a food-truck window, cash in hand. Tag: "After: tacos on Airline. Cash only. Worth it."
6. All three panels hold on screen together for a beat, the whole day visible at once.
7. Guide or guest, to camera, real audio: "Paddle in the morning, eat where the locals eat, still make it to whatever's next. Right next to the rest of your day."
8. End card and CTA.

**Voiceover/caption track:** Minimal. The panel tags carry the open, one spoken line near the end.

**CTA overlay text:** "Fit It Into Your Day. $55"

**Primary text (caption):** You don't need a free Saturday for this. Paddle downtown in the morning, grab food or coffee a few blocks from the dock, and still make it to whatever you had planned tonight. Buffalo Bayou Kayak is built to fit next to the rest of your day, not replace it. Houston Skyline Tour, $55/person, 2 hours, all ages, Allen's Landing.

**Headlines:** "Right Next To The Rest Of Your Day" and "Paddle in the Morning. Still Have Your Whole Day."

**Objection handled:** I don't have time to build a whole outing around this, what do I even do before or after.

**Format spec:** Reel. Needs new B-roll pickups at Boomtown Coffee and a taco-truck shot (Tacos Tierra Caliente on Airline) if not already in the asset library; panel 1 can reuse existing skyline-paddle footage.

### Variant 2: "The One Who Has to Pick"

**Angle:** Don't Be The One Who Picked The Bad Activity. New this pass, emerging.

**Evidence:** "You're probably worried about being the person who picked the activity nobody wanted to do. That's the worst feeling. Here's why that doesn't happen with this one..." (Agent Instructions, pre-emptive accusation audit, verbatim.)

**Hook options:**
1. "You're the one who has to pick the activity this time."
2. "Nobody says it, but everyone's thinking it: please don't pick something lame."
3. "The fear isn't the activity. It's being the one who chose it."

**Body framework:** Pre-emptive accusation audit, borrowed directly from BBK's own sales framework: name the unspoken fear before the viewer has to say it, then resolve it visually instead of with more talking.

**Shot list / visual beats:**
1. Open: one person, phone in hand, scrolling group-activity options, visibly stuck. Text overlay: "When you're the one who has to pick."
2. Text overlay, second beat: "Everyone's thinking the same thing. Please don't pick something lame."
3. Guide or narrator, to camera, real audio: "You're probably worried about being the person who picked the activity nobody wanted to do. That's the worst feeling."
4. Cut: the same person now on the water with their group, paddling, visibly relaxed, skyline behind them.
5. Text overlay: "Here's why that doesn't happen with this one."
6. Cut: group laughing together mid-paddle, genuine, unposed.
7. Guide/narrator: "Nobody's ever mad they went kayaking through downtown Houston."
8. End card and CTA.

**Voiceover/caption track:** Two spoken lines pulled directly from BBK's own accusation-audit script.

**CTA overlay text:** "Make the Safe Pick. Reserve Now."

**Primary text (caption):** You know the feeling. You're the one who has to pick the activity, and if it flops, that's on you for the next six months. Nobody has ever regretted kayaking through downtown Houston. It's different enough to be memorable, easy enough that nobody's out of their depth, and the skyline photos do the rest. Houston Skyline Tour, $55/person, 2 hours, all ages.

**Headlines:** "You're the One Who Has to Pick" and "Nobody's Ever Mad They Went Kayaking"

**Objection handled:** what if my group hates what I picked and I hear about it for years.

**Format spec:** Reel. Needs one new staged-but-real cold open (a person alone, deciding); everything after it can reuse existing group-paddle B-roll from prior birthday/bachelorette Reels.
