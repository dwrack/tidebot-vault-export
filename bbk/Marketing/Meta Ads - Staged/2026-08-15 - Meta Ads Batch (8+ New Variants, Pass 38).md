## Variant 1 — UGC: "3 Spots Locals Actually Paddle To"

**Angle:** The Locals' List. First execution.
**Evidence:** Video hook pattern ("Paddling Houston's Hidden Buffalo Bayou Is An Amazing Adventure") + 2026 hyper-local-list Reels trend, both source-noted in the research doc.
**Objection handled:** I live in Houston, I'd already know if this were worth doing.
**Hook (draft):** "3 spots on Buffalo Bayou locals actually paddle to. Not the tourist stuff."
**Body framework:** Text-on-screen list over real B-roll, addressed directly to Houstonians, not visitors.
**Shot list:**
1. Text overlay: "You've lived here your whole life. Have you paddled this?"
2. Cut: launch point near downtown, skyline visible.
3. Text overlay: "1. The stretch under the Waugh Bridge."
4. Cut: bat emergence footage or the bridge itself if daytime.
5. Text overlay: "2. The bend where the herons hang out."
6. Cut: heron/wildlife footage.
7. Text overlay: "3. The quiet stretch that doesn't feel like Houston at all."
8. Cut: calm water, skyline receding.
9. End card and CTA.
**Primary text (draft):** You've driven past this bridge a hundred times. Never paddled under it. 3 spots on Buffalo Bayou that locals actually go, not the version tourists see from the sidewalk. 3 miles from downtown, an entirely different city. Houston Skyline Tour, $55/person, 2 hours.
**Headline:** "3 Spots on Buffalo Bayou Locals Actually Paddle To"
**CTA overlay text:** "Book the Local's Route"
**CTA:** Book Your Tour. Sales/Purchase-objective campaign.
**Format spec:** UGC-style vertical, lo-fi text-on-screen list, minimal production. Uses existing skyline, bridge, and wildlife footage; no new asset strictly required, though a fresher handheld clip would strengthen the "locals, not tourists" framing.

## Variant 4 — Reel: "The Story You'll Actually Tell"

**Angle:** The Story You'll Tell. First execution.
**Evidence:** "Managed to push off and flip our kayak over, landing in the Bayou... successful laughter and entertainment are provided for free." (Take Another Route travel blog, source-attributed, exact operator not confirmed as BBK specifically, used as illustrative pattern per research guardrail.)
**Objection handled:** Fear of looking foolish if something goes wrong, reframed rather than promised away.
**Hook (draft):** "Nobody remembers the trip that went perfectly."
**Body framework:** Reframe structure: name the fear, then flip it, then back it with a real safety fact so the humor doesn't stand alone.
**Shot list:**
1. Text overlay: "Nobody remembers the trip that went perfectly."
2. Cut: guests laughing on the water, mid-paddle.
3. Text overlay: "If you tip, you float. PFDs, always."
4. Cut: guide assisting a guest back into a kayak, everyone smiling.
5. Text overlay: "The story you'll actually tell? This one."
6. End card and CTA.
**Primary text (draft):** Nobody comes back talking about the trip that went exactly according to plan. If you flip, you float, life jackets are non-negotiable and your guide's right there. Most groups laugh about it for the rest of the day. This is the story you'll actually tell. Houston Skyline Tour, $55/person, 2 hours.
**Headline:** "The Trip You'll Actually Talk About"
**CTA overlay text:** "Book Your Tour"
**CTA:** Book Your Tour. Sales/Purchase-objective campaign.
**Format spec:** Reel. Needs a real clip of guests laughing after a minor mishap or a staged-but-honest re-creation; does not currently exist in the asset library, flagged for a dedicated shoot.
