## The 4 new angles

1. **Most Guests Don't Plan This** (proven). Real FareHarbor data: 51% of last month's bookings were made the same day as the tour, median lead time 0 days. First angle in the whole research history sourced from BBK's own transaction data instead of the sales script or a review.
2. **The Bestseller Isn't the One With Bats In It** (proven). Real FareHarbor data: One Hour Watersports Rentals had 52 bookings last month, more than the Bat Bridge Tour (21) and Houston Skyline Tour (21) combined... actually more than either alone, by count. Strengthens the already-scripted "Just Rent It" angle (pass 25) with hard proof.
3. **It's Federal Law, and We Don't Skip It** (proven). Real regulatory-compliance facts from the internal Coast Guard & Compliance SOP: PFDs must be worn, not just carried; every vessel on the water after sunset needs a navigation light, by federal law. Best fit for the after-dark tours (Bat Bridge, Full Moon, Fireworks), which had emotional angles but no trust/credibility angle.
4. **We Don't Cancel on a Feeling** (emerging). Real numeric weather-hold thresholds from the internal Weather Hold Protocol (wind >15 mph, lightning within 10 miles, etc.) plus the fact that guides have standing authority to call a hold without permission. Complements, doesn't duplicate, the already-scripted "Rain Doesn't Cancel This" (pass 23).

## Guardrails carried into scripting

- **Most Guests Don't Plan This**: never promise a guaranteed same-day slot (Agent Instructions rule #15). Describe guest behavior, not inventory. CTA is "check availability."
- **The Bestseller Isn't the One With Bats In It**: additive, not comparative-negative. It's a booking-count stat, not a revenue stat (Bat Bridge still earns more per month despite fewer bookings). Say "most booked," never "best" or "most popular tour."
- **It's Federal Law, and We Don't Skip It**: heaviest-care angle this pass. Stay narrowly scoped to the two checkable facts (PFDs worn, nav lights after dark). Never expand into "safest operator" or "100% safe." The open Karen M safety-complaint flag (Aug 2025) makes this discipline non-negotiable.
- **We Don't Cancel on a Feeling**: positions as the flip side of "Rain Doesn't Cancel This," not a contradiction. Never implies cancellations are always smooth or rare (standing no-show/cancellation flag, most recent Jan 2026, remains open).
