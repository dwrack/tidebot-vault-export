Pair with a real safety fact, don't let humor stand alone. The source quote is illustrative of a plausible experience pattern, not a verified BBK guest review, don't present it as one.
- **The Locals' List**: keep the register as insider-status FOMO and local pride, not a tourist pitch. This is the brand's own nemesis framing made explicit, stay honest to what `Brand Nemesis Framework.md` already says rather than inventing new claims.
- **The One Thing Nobody Tells You**: this is a footwear tip, not a safety warning. Don't inflate it into an alarmist claim; keep it light and specific.
- **No competitor callouts.** Two dropped candidates this pass traced back to a named-competitor (Bayou City Adventures) guest complaint thread. Neither survived into scripting, and nothing below references or gestures at any competitor by name, per standing brand voice.
