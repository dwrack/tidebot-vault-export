## Variant 1 — Carousel: "Go Before You Go"

**Angle:** Go Before You Go (logistics candor: no restrooms on the water).
**Evidence:** "Meet at least 15 minutes before your scheduled tour to use the bathroom and sign waivers." (buffalobayoukayak.com/faq/, direct-fetched verbatim.) "Let guests know there is no restroom access during the tour, so use facilities before launch if available nearby." (SOPs/Quick Ref/Guest Check-In Steps.md, verbatim.)
**Objection handled:** Wait, is there a bathroom out there?
**Hook (draft):** "Nobody tells you this part: there's no bathroom out on the bayou."
**Body framework:** Practical logistics candor as trust-building, small stakes, same honesty pattern as the higher-stakes transparency angles.
**Card table (draft):**

| 1 | Allen's Landing dock, morning light. | There's no bathroom on the water. | We'll just say it. Plan accordingly. |

| 4 | Clean text card, pricing. | Houston Skyline Tour, $55/person. | Now you know. Let's get you on the water. |
**CTA:** Check Availability. Sales/Purchase-objective campaign.

## Variant 2 — Reel: "If You Flip, You Float"

**Angle:** You Can't Sink This Thing (float/capsize reassurance). First execution.
**Evidence:** "All participants wear life vests, so you will float. Guides are on the water with every tour and will assist anyone who falls in." (buffalobayoukayak.com/faq/, direct-fetched verbatim.) "Guide the guest to hold onto the kayak (sit-on-tops won't sink)." (SOPs/Quick Ref/Incident Report Steps.md, Capsizing section, verbatim.)
**Objection handled:** What actually happens if I flip the kayak?
**Hook (draft):** "What actually happens if you flip? You float. Here's why."
**Body framework:** Proof-first reassurance, scoped narrowly to floatation and guide response, not a blanket safety guarantee.
**Shot list:**
1. Calm water, a sit-on-top kayak floating, guide nearby.
2. Text overlay: "Every guest wears a life vest. The whole tour. No exceptions."
3. Cut: guide demonstrating how a sit-on-top kayak sits above the water.
4. Text overlay: "If you go in, your guide is already on the water with you."
5. End card and CTA.
**Primary text (draft):** If you flip a sit-on-top kayak, you don't go under, you float. Every guest wears a life vest the entire tour and a guide is on the water with you the whole time to help if you go in. Houston Skyline Tour, $55/person, 2 hours.
**Headline:** "If You Flip, You Float"
**CTA overlay text:** "Book Your Tour"
**CTA:** Book Your Tour. Sales/Purchase-objective campaign.
**Format spec:** Reel. Needs a real, controlled demo shot showing a sit-on-top kayak's stability and a guide nearby, not a dramatized capsize.

## Variant 5 — Reel: "Every Kayak Gets Checked Before You Ever See It"

**Angle:** Everything Gets Checked Before You Ever See It (pre-trip gear diligence).
**Evidence:** "Inspect hulls for cracks, deep scratches, or soft spots. Inspect each PFD: buckles click, straps aren't frayed, no rips in fabric. Check water level and current speed (Bayou levels fluctuate, especially after rain)." (SOPs/Quick Ref/Morning Inspection Checklist.md, verbatim.)
**Objection handled:** Is this gear actually maintained, or just whatever's lying around?
**Hook (draft):** "Before you ever see your kayak, someone already checked it."
**Body framework:** Proof-first, morning-routine reveal.
**Shot list:**
1. Early morning, guide alone at the dock, running a hand along a kayak hull.
2. Text overlay: "Every kayak gets inspected before any guest touches it."
3. Cut: guide checking a PFD buckle.
4. Text overlay: "Every buckle. Every strap. Every morning."
5. Cut: guide looking at the water, checking current.
6. Text overlay: "The water gets checked too."
7. End card, guests arriving to gear that's ready.
8. CTA.
**Primary text (draft):** Every morning before the first tour, we inspect every hull for cracks or soft spots, check every PFD buckle and strap by hand, and check the water level and current before anyone launches. This is the actual checklist our guides run, every day. Houston Skyline Tour, $55/person, 2 hours.
**Headline:** "Every Kayak Gets Checked Before You Ever See It"
**CTA overlay text:** "Book Your Tour"
**CTA:** Book Your Tour. Sales/Purchase-objective campaign.
**Format spec:** Reel. Needs a real morning-inspection clip (hull check, PFD buckle check), not currently in the asset library.
