## Variant 1 — Reel: "Half Our Bookings Happen Today"

**Angle:** Most Guests Don't Plan This.
**Evidence:** "Same day: 46 (51%)... Median: 0d" (FareHarbor Reports, Last 30 Days, verbatim).
**Objection handled:** I'd have to plan this days in advance.
**Hook (draft):** "Half the people who book us decide the same day."
**Body framework:** Data-led permission-giving. State the number, then show it in action.
**Shot list:**
1. Someone scrolling their phone on a Saturday with nothing planned.
2. Text overlay: "51% of our bookings happen the same day."
3. Cut to that same energy, on the water within the hour, paddling past downtown.
4. Text overlay: "You don't need a plan. You need an afternoon."
5. End card and CTA.
**Primary text (draft):** We checked our own numbers. More than half our bookings happen the same day someone shows up. No big plan required, just an open afternoon and 10:00am to 6:00pm to work with. Houston Skyline Tour, $55/person, 2 hours.
**Headline:** "Half Our Bookings Happen the Same Day"
**CTA overlay text:** "Check Today's Availability"
**CTA:** Check Availability. Sales/Purchase-objective campaign.
**Format spec:** Reel. Needs a quick, casual, low-production "spur of the moment" opening shot plus existing skyline-paddle B-roll for the rest.

## Variant 5 — Reel: "The Law Says Every Boat Needs a Light After Dark"

**Angle:** It's Federal Law, and We Don't Skip It.
**Evidence:** "Every vessel on the water after sunset must have a white light. This is federal law, not optional." / "Must be worn at all times on our tours. Not just 'accessible,' worn." (SOP Coast Guard & Compliance, verbatim).
**Objection handled:** Is this actually a regulated, safety-compliant operation, especially after dark.
**Hook (draft):** "Federal law says every boat on the water after dark needs a light. We don't skip it."
**Body framework:** Proof-first credibility, best paired with the Bat Bridge / Full Moon night visuals.
**Shot list:**
1. Guide clipping a white navigation light to a PFD in the dusk light, matter-of-fact, not dramatized.
2. Text overlay: "Every vessel on the water after sunset needs a light. That's federal law, not our policy."
3. Cut to kayaks on the water at dusk, lights visible, bats starting to emerge overhead.
4. Text overlay: "Life vests stay on the whole time. Not just nearby, worn."
5. End card and CTA.
**Primary text (draft):** This isn't a slogan, it's federal regulation for any commercial vessel on the water after sunset: a visible white light, no exceptions. Same goes for life vests, worn the entire tour, not just packed along. We follow it on every Bat Bridge and Full Moon tour we run. Bat Bridge Tour, $60/person, 2-3 hours.
**Headline:** "Every Vessel Gets a Light After Dark. It's the Law."
**CTA overlay text:** "Book the Bat Bridge Tour"
**CTA:** Book Your Tour. Sales/Purchase-objective campaign.
**Format spec:** Reel. Needs a real dusk-light clip-on shot; existing Bat Bridge B-roll covers the rest.

## Variant 7 — Static: "We Don't Cancel on a Feeling"

**Angle:** We Don't Cancel on a Feeling.
**Evidence:** "Wind speed >15 mph sustained: Hold or reschedule. Lightning within 10 miles: Cancel immediately, shelter." / "Guides can make a hold call on their own. You don't need permission to keep people off the water." (Weather Hold Protocol, verbatim).
**Objection handled:** What's the actual criteria for cancelling, or is it someone's mood.
**Hook (draft):** "We don't cancel because it looks iffy. We cancel because a number tells us to."
**Body framework:** Procedural transparency as trust-building.
**Primary text (draft):** We don't hold or cancel a tour based on a feeling. We have real thresholds: sustained wind over 15 mph, lightning within 10 miles, low visibility, rising water. Any guide can make the call on their own, no waiting on a manager. If it's light rain, we still paddle, that alone has never been a reason to cancel. Houston Skyline Tour, $55/person, 2 hours.
**Headline:** "We Don't Cancel on a Feeling. We Cancel on a Number."
**Description line:** "Real thresholds. Any guide can call it, no waiting."
**CTA:** Reserve Your Spot. Sales/Purchase-objective campaign.
**Format spec:** Static hero. Existing skyline B-roll works.
