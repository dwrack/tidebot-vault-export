Pricing/product facts used below are pulled directly from `Buffalo Bayou Kayak — Agent Instructions (Copy-Paste).md`, an owned document: Sunset Skyline Tour and Houston Skyline Tour, both $55/person, 2 hours, all ages. Launch point: Sunset Coffee Building at Allen's Landing, 1019 Commerce Street, Houston TX 77002.

No guide is named in any variant below. Neither angle this pass involves a guide-specific claim.

**Timing flag caught during drafting:** the research's own Regatta quote is phrased in future tense against a March 21, 2026 date ("returns... on March 21, 2026"). Today's date is 2026-08-15, so that specific 2026 running has already happened. Every draft below uses the recurring/annual framing ("every year," "54 years running") instead of restating the already-elapsed March 2026 date as if it were upcoming.

**Review-overlay flag caught during drafting:** neither angle has an actual guest quote on file that speaks to its specific claim. Angle 1 has no guest review about the Regatta or the bayou's "legitimacy." Angle 2 has no guest review about the restoration history. Per the vault's no-fabricated-testimonial standard, both Variant 4 and Variant 8 below are drafted as source cards (attributed to the real institutional sources), not styled as guest reviews.

**Product-naming flag caught during drafting:** BBK's own price sheet lists a product called "Buffalo Bayou Regatta Rental" ($90/person, 8.5 hours). Research found no sponsorship or participation link between BBK and the Buffalo Bayou Partnership Regatta race. To avoid implying a partnership that doesn't exist, that rental product is not mentioned anywhere in this angle's copy; only the Sunset Skyline and Houston Skyline Tours are used as the CTA product.

## Variant 1 — Static: "Texas' Largest Canoe and Kayak Race Finishes at the Exact Dock You'll Launch From"

**Angle:** Texas' Largest Canoe and Kayak Race Finishes at the Exact Dock You'll Launch From. First execution.
**Evidence:** "The largest canoe and kayak race in Texas returns to Houston's most significant natural waterway..." (Buffalo Bayou Partnership, buffalobayou.org, direct fetch, describing the 54th Annual Regatta). Independently corroborated: "the largest canoe and kayak race in the state of Texas" (365thingsinhouston.com, direct fetch). Finish line: Allen's Landing (both sources) — the same landing where BBK meets guests to launch, re-confirmed via direct fetch of BBK's own site: "Meet next to Talento Bilingue 1019 Commerce St, Houston, TX 77002."
**Objection handled:** Is Buffalo Bayou actually a real, legitimate place to paddle, or is this just some sad urban drainage ditch dressed up for tourists.
**Hook (draft):** "Texas' largest canoe and kayak race finishes on the same block you'll launch from."
**Body framework:** Ogilvy factual-specific, a real, externally-run, 54-year event used as scale-proof instead of a vague "real nature" claim.
**Overlay text (draft):** "The largest kayak race in Texas ends right where your tour begins."
**Supporting line (draft):** "Two independent sources agree. Same landing, every year."
**Primary text (draft):** Every year, the Buffalo Bayou Partnership Regatta brings out what its own organizers call "the largest canoe and kayak race in Texas," now in its 54th year. An independent local outlet, 365thingsinhouston.com, backs up the same claim. It finishes at Allen's Landing, the same landing where Buffalo Bayou Kayak meets guests to launch. We're not part of the race. We're just neighbors with it. Sunset Skyline Tour and Houston Skyline Tour, $55/person, 2 hours, all ages.
**Headline:** "Same Water, Same Landing, Texas' Biggest Kayak Race"
**CTA:** Book Your Tour. Sales/Purchase-objective campaign.
