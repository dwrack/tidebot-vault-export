## Read this before the scripts

**Angle 3 has a hard guardrail.** A third-party accessibility listing found this pass states the BBK rental facility is not wheelchair accessible and the activity is advised against for guests with spinal injuries. Every execution of "We'll Work With You" sticks strictly to BBK's own FAQ language, "most adults," "contact us in advance to discuss accommodations," and never claims blanket accessibility or ADA compliance.

**Four things were found and deliberately not scripted this pass:**
1. A 2025 news cycle about deaths in Houston's bayous (general waterway-safety commentary, not BBK-specific) — too sensitive to reference even obliquely in paid creative.
2. "Rolling the dice" booking-consistency skepticism from a TripAdvisor reviewer who is also a source of the standing no-show/cancellation operational flag — building trust copy from those exact words while the underlying issue is unresolved would be misleading.
3. A single-source, uncorroborated alligator-encounter story — not built into a wildlife angle; BBK doesn't list alligators among its named sightings (herons, egrets, turtles), and one personal blog post isn't enough evidence to promise a sighting species.
4. A lost-belongings complaint (Nov 2025, disputed "fine print" response from management) — considered as a light "what you left on the dock" joke, rejected as tone-deaf while that complaint is open.

**Standing operational flags, unchanged and still open:** the no-show/cancellation pattern (now a roughly 2.5-year tail across four dated instances, including a new Jan 2026 one), the Karen M kayak-took-on-water/trainee-guide safety complaint, the Peek.com service-consistency complaint, and (new this pass) a disputed lost-belongings complaint.
