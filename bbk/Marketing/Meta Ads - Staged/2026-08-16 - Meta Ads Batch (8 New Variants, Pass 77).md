## Variant 1 — Review-Overlay: "Your Dog Can Come Too"

**Evidence:** "My husband and I went Kayaking and we were able to bring our small dog with us. He had a blast!" — Mariah Reeves, reviews.birdeye.com, direct fetch via `?page=2`, pass 77.

He had a blast!" If you're thinking about bringing a small dog along, give us a call first so we can make sure it works for your date and boat.
**Headline:** "A Guest Brought Her Dog. He Had a Blast."
**CTA:** Check Availability. Sales/Purchase-objective campaign.

## Variant 3 — Static: "Kayak or Paddleboard, the Price Doesn't Decide It For You"

**Angle:** Kayak or Paddleboard, the Price Doesn't Decide It For You. First execution.
**Evidence:** "This surprises people every time: kayak and paddleboard cost the same. Self-guided rentals are $20 for one hour or $40 for two, whether you're in a single kayak or on a paddleboard. Full-day rentals run $55 for either." — `Marketing/Blog Drafts - Staged/2026-08-15 - kayak-vs-paddleboard-buffalo-bayou.md`, owned, unpublished.
**Objection handled:** Isn't the paddleboard going to cost me more than just renting a kayak.
**Hook (draft):** "Kayak or paddleboard. Same price either way."
**Body framework:** Ogilvy factual-specific, state the surprising price fact plainly, let the numbers prove it.
**Overlay text (draft):** "$20 for an hour. $40 for two. $55 for the day. Either boat."
**Primary text (draft):** Most people assume the paddleboard costs more, it looks like the trendier option. It doesn't. A single kayak and a paddleboard rent for the exact same price: $20 for an hour, $40 for two hours, $55 for a full day. Pick whichever one you'd rather be on.
**Headline:** "The Paddleboard Doesn't Cost More"
**CTA:** Book Your Rental. Sales/Purchase-objective campaign.
**Format spec:** Static. Text-overlay card, existing rental/paddleboard photography.
