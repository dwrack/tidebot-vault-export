## Variant 1 — Static: "They End Up Coming Back More Than They Expected"

**Angle:** They End Up Coming Back More Than They Expected. New in pass 60, emerging, first execution.

**Hook (draft):** "Most members think they'll come 3 or 4 times a year. That's already enough to pay for itself."
**Body framework:** Bencivenga proof-tier observation, a guide's real pattern replaces a vague "great value" claim.
**Overlay text (draft):** "3 to 4 times a year pays for the membership. Most members do a lot more than that."
**Primary text (draft):** A membership pays for itself if you paddle 3 or 4 times a year. That's the math. What guides actually see: most members come back a lot more than that once they have it. $300/year. Unlimited kayak, tandem, or paddleboard rentals, any day we're open, no reservation required.
**Headline:** "Members Come Back More Than They Planned To"
**CTA:** Get the Membership. Sales/Purchase-objective campaign.
**Format spec:** Static. Fact card over on-water lifestyle or dock photography; existing assets likely sufficient.

## Variant 3 — Reel: "The Thing That Looks Like an Alligator (It's a Fish)"

**Angle:** The Thing That Looks Like an Alligator (It's a Fish). New in pass 30, never scripted before, proven, first execution.
**Evidence:** "Turtles, herons, egrets, the occasional alligator gar surfacing. Look, don't touch. Don't feed them." (SOPs/Deep Read/Safety Briefing Script.md, verbatim, owned.) / "Watch for beaver and prehistoric alligator gar [while paddling Buffalo Bayou]" (savebuffalobayou.org, direct-fetched, verbatim, third-party.) / "Buffalo Bayou gar average 20-40 lbs." (garfish-texas.com, direct-fetched, verbatim, third-party.)
**Objection handled:** Implicit startle moment, what's that huge thing breaking the surface, is that an alligator.
**Hook (draft):** "Something the size of your leg just broke the surface next to your kayak."
**Body framework:** Sugarman slippery slide, delay the reveal, let curiosity carry the viewer to the payoff.
**Shot list:**
1. Text overlay: "Something huge just broke the surface."
2. Cut: water ripple/wake shot, guide reacting calmly.
3. Text overlay: "Relax. That's not an alligator."
4. Cut: guide pointing, guests looking toward the water.
5. Text overlay: "It's an alligator gar. 20 to 40 pounds. Been here longer than the skyline."
6. Cut: guests laughing, relieved, paddling on.
7. End card and CTA.
**Primary text (draft):** The first time it happens, guests freeze. Something big breaks the surface a few feet from the kayak. It's not an alligator. It's an alligator gar, a fish that's been swimming this bayou since before Houston existed, 20 to 40 pounds, prehistoric-looking, completely harmless. Guides point it out now instead of letting you panic first. Houston Skyline Tour, $55/person, 2 hours.
**Headline:** "It's Not an Alligator"
**CTA:** Book Your Tour. Sales/Purchase-objective campaign.
**Format spec:** Reel. Needs a reveal/reaction shot; flag for a fresh capture of a guide pointing out wildlife on the water with a genuine guest reaction.
