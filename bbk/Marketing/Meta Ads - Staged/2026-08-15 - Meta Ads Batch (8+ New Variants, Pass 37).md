## Variant 1 — Static: "Screenshot This for the Group Chat"

**Angle:** The Buddy System. First execution.
**Evidence:** "Want me to put together a quick summary you can screenshot for the group chat? Over a thousand five-star reviews, that usually settles it." (Buffalo Bayou Kayak — Agent Instructions (Copy-Paste).md, Priority 6, verbatim.)
**Objection handled:** I want to do this but I have to convince my whole group first and don't want to be the one who pushed it.
**Hook (draft):** "Send this to the group chat. Let the reviews do the convincing."
**Body framework:** Direct-offer, the ad itself is designed to be forwarded.
**Primary text (draft):** You already know they'll say yes once they see it. Over a thousand five-star reviews, right in the middle of downtown Houston, guide included, all gear included. Screenshot this and send it to the group chat. Houston Skyline Tour, $55/person, 2 hours.
**Headline:** "Over 1,000 Five-Star Reviews. Send This to the Group."
**CTA:** Book Your Tour. Sales/Purchase-objective campaign.
**Format spec:** Static. Clean quote/proof-card design, built to look good as a screenshot (large text, minimal clutter). Existing skyline/downtown launch photography works.
