**Reasoning:** Bencivenga's step-sequence structure (used elsewhere in this vault for process claims) was considered, but this angle is one fact stated three ways across cards, not a multi-step process, so Ogilvy's single-fact repetition is the better fit.

#### Copywriting-2.0 pass

**Verdict:** Works, hook swapped to avoid restating pass 78's own wording, card 2 and card 4 tightened.
