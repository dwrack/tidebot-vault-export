Source: [[../../Ad Research/2026-08-15 ad-research buffalo-bayou-kayak — Pass 76|Ad Research/2026-08-15 ad-research buffalo-bayou-kayak — Pass 76]] plus `Ad Research/ad-research-pass76.json`. Do not pull copy from here for anything client-facing, use the final combined file.

2 angles this pass, both built on the Kemah Kayak Historical and Ecological Tour, a second BBK product on Clear Lake never before scripted in this vault's 74 prior ad-research/ad-scripter passes. Angle 1 ("There's a Second BBK Tour, and It's Not on the Bayou at All") sells the fact of discovery: BBK runs a tour most people who know the downtown bayou tours have never heard of. Angle 2 ("Your Guide Also Tells You Where to Eat in Kemah") sells what happens once someone's already decided to make the trip: the guide doubles as a local-knowledge resource for the rest of the day, not just the two hours on the water. Both angles are rated proven, sourced directly from BBK's own tour listing at `buffalobayoukayak.com/all-activities/`, not from a guest quote. Each angle gets four executions, 8 variants covering all 4 required formats twice over: Static x2, Carousel x2, Reel x2, Review-overlay x2 (styled as attributed source cards, since neither angle has a guest quote to draw on).

Pricing/product facts used below are pulled directly from `Buffalo Bayou Kayak — Agent Instructions (Copy-Paste).md`, an owned document: Kemah Kayak Historical and Ecological Tour, $50/person, 2 hours, ages 8+.

No guide is named in any variant below. Neither angle this pass involves a guide-specific claim. See the research file for the standing roster gap (only Dulce Delgado and Axel Johnson confirmed).

Note on scope: no variant below references the Buffalo Bayou Regatta Rental product or the Buffalo Bayou Partnership Regatta race. Both are different products/events already covered by pass 72's angles, and the research file specifically flagged the naming-collision risk of mixing them into Kemah copy.

## Variant 1 — Static: "There's a Second BBK Tour, and It's Not on the Bayou at All"

**Angle:** There's a Second BBK Tour, and It's Not on the Bayou at All. First execution.
**Evidence:** "Join us on a kayak (a tandem or single) with a local, well informed guide on a two hour unforgettable experience. Learn how the town of Kemah was founded, the impact of hurricanes, and the importance of Clear Lake." (buffalobayoukayak.com/all-activities/, direct fetch, pass 76.)
**Objection handled:** I already know Buffalo Bayou Kayak, I've seen the downtown tour, is there anything else.
**Hook (draft):** "You know the downtown tour. You don't know about this one."
**Body framework:** Ogilvy factual-specific, name the actual second product instead of a vague "more experiences" tease.
**Overlay text (draft):** "Not on the bayou. Not downtown. A whole different trip."
**Primary text (draft):** Most people who know Buffalo Bayou Kayak know the downtown tour, the skyline, the bats, the bayou. There's a second one. The Kemah Kayak Historical and Ecological Tour puts you on Clear Lake instead, with a guide who covers how Kemah was founded, what the hurricanes did to it, and why Clear Lake matters to the area. $50 per person, 2 hours, ages 8 and up.
**Headline:** "The Tour Most People Don't Know We Run"
**CTA:** Book the Kemah Tour. Sales/Purchase-objective campaign.
**Format spec:** Static. Text-overlay card, Clear Lake or Kemah waterfront photography if available in the asset library, otherwise a clean text/graphic card since dedicated Kemah tour photography may not yet exist.
