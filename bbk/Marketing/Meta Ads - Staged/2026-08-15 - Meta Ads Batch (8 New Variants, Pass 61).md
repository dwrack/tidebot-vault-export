## Variant 1 — Static: "Not a First-Date Kayak"

**Angle:** Not a First-Date Kayak. First execution.
**Evidence:** "It can be, with one caveat: it works best for a second or third date, not a first meeting with a stranger, since a two-hour guided tour is a bigger time commitment than coffee." (houston-date-ideas-on-the-water.md, FAQ A2, verbatim, owned.)
**Objection handled:** Will spending two hours alone with someone I barely know feel too intense or awkward for a first date.
**Hook (draft):** "This isn't a first-date activity. We'll tell you that upfront."
**Body framework:** Halbert plain-directness, meets the brand's own documented "vulnerability and honesty" pattern, a small unprompted downside stated flatly builds trust in everything that follows.
**Overlay text (draft):** "Not a first date. A second or third one."
**Supporting line (draft):** "Two hours together is a bigger ask than coffee."
**Primary text (draft):** We'll be straight with you: this isn't the move for a first meeting with someone you just matched with. Two hours in a kayak is a bigger ask than coffee. But once you've already met a few times, it's one of the better date ideas in Houston, doing something together instead of talking across a table cuts through the awkwardness fast. Sunset Skyline Tour, $55/person, 2 hours, launching from Allen's Landing.
**Headline:** "We'll Tell You When Not to Book This"
**CTA:** Book Your Tour. Sales/Purchase-objective campaign.
**Format spec:** Static. Text-overlay card, couple in a kayak at golden hour, downtown skyline behind; existing sunset-tour photography likely sufficient.
