## Read this before the scripts

**Angle 3 carries this pass's hardest guardrail.** The underlying fact is a safety policy, not a lifestyle claim. Every execution stays positive and describes what the kayak tour actually is (calm, clear-headed, present), never a dig at drinking, never a comparison to bars or booze cruises, and never a reference, named or implied, to the sibling Pedal Barge Party Cruise or Houston Booze Cruise products. That comparison exists in the internal sales upsell script for live conversations only and has no place in paid creative.

**Angle 2 carries a domain-collision guardrail.** `buffalobayoukayak.com` also hosts a page for the Pedal Barge Party Cruise, a motorized, BYOB sibling product that shares the domain. A research search for "birthday party" content surfaced that page first. Every Birthday Groups script below is scoped strictly to the paddled kayak tour and pulls zero language from the Pedal Barge page.

**Two things were found and deliberately not scripted this pass:**
1. A deeper mine of `Agent Instructions (Copy-Paste).md` is still open. This pass only pulled the high-value-lead triggers and the by-season talking points from an 80KB file. The objection-handling scripts, local recommendations, and guide spotlights likely hold more usable first-party language. Logged as a pass-25 candidate rather than rushed this pass.

The spring seasonal talking point ("Spring is the sweet spot in Houston... wildflowers are out") exists in the same source as the fall/winter material but was not built into its own angle this pass, since spring is closer to territory the existing Beat the Heat and general seasonal framing already imply. Worth a dedicated look in pass 25 if David wants a fourth season angle.
