## The 5 topics

1. **Urban nature, 3 miles from downtown** — the reframe is the entire product
2. **The wildlife** — herons, turtles, the "I had no idea this was here" moment
3. **"It's a drainage ditch"** — the belief we're fighting, head on
4. **Who can do it** — calm water, families, no experience, no fitness bar
5. **The skyline** — Houston from the water, a view most locals have never had

## The 3 formats

- **Myth-bust** (easy) — the bayou's reputation is the content
- **Breakdown** (easy) — what you'll see, what to bring, how long
- **Before/after** (ambitious) — the parking lot vs 200 yards downstream. This format IS the brand.

## The 2 layouts

- **A — Talking head at the launch, downtown visible behind.** The contrast has to be in frame.
- **B — Silent, text on screen over GoPro/drone.** Sound off, city signage type.

## Cover type

Clean modern sans, city-signage feel. The visual joke is nature type over a skyline, so keep the type urban and let the footage be the wild part.

## Notes

- **Before/after is the highest-leverage format this brand has** and it's basically free to shoot. Every row 5/6/17/18 style pairing is one clip: point the camera at the parking lot, then at the water. The surprise IS the product per the brand story.
- Row 15 answers the thing everyone secretly thinks. Answering it plainly builds more trust than avoiding it.
- BBK's only social presence is Instagram right now. This grid is enough to justify starting the TikTok too, since faceless/silent layouts port directly.
- Boost the highest-**save** post as a social-proof ad. Saves signal "I'll do this later," which is exactly the local-market behavior we want.
