# Virality Rubric

100 points across 9 factors, weighted by how hard each signal pushes distribution in 2026
(sends > saves > completion/replay > watch time > likes). Brand playbooks override any
number here when they are more specific.

## Factors

### 1. Hook, first second (25)
The swipe decision happens before second 1 (outer edge: 1.3s TikTok, 2.1s Reels).
- 22-25: Frame 1 is the payoff teaser or a pattern interrupt AND on-screen text opens with a claim or a question in the first 3 words. Curiosity gap is obvious ("Caribbean or Door County?", "#1 Door County Activity").
- 15-21: Strong visual on frame 1 but text is soft, or strong text over a so-so visual.
- 8-14: Hook lands at 1.5-3s, or it is a vibe, not a claim.
- 0-7: Logo, title card, fade-in, slow drone push, black frame, talking head saying "Hey guys".

### 2. Retention and length fit (15)
- Length inside the band for the format: scenery/POV/before-after 8-19s, story/lore/explainer 21-34s. Never near 60s.
- A visual or text change every 1-2s, no dead air, story moves by second 5.
- 13-15 in band and tight. 8-12 slightly long or one slow stretch. 0-7 over 45s, or a long static middle.

### 3. Loop and replay (10)
- 9-10: Ends on unfinished motion or cuts back to the opening shot so the replay feels seamless, or a "wait for it" payoff that rewards a rewatch.
- 5-8: Clean ending, some rewatch reason.
- 0-4: End card, logo outro, fade to black, "follow for more".

### 4. Send trigger / shareability (15)
Strongest Reels signal for non-followers (about 3-5x a like).
- 13-15: There is an obvious person to send it to ("send this to the person you'd paddle with"), a relatable or arguable take, a surprising fact, or a "we have to do this" moment.
- 7-12: Pleasant, someone might share it.
- 0-6: Nobody sends a generic montage.

### 5. Save value (10)
- 9-10: Planning value people come back to: list, how-to, best time, what to know, prices, tips.
- 5-8: Some useful info.
- 0-4: Pure mood.

### 6. Sound-off readability (10)
85% watch muted.
- Every spoken word captioned, 3-7 words per line, high contrast, inside safe zones (not under the IG UI at the bottom ~20% or the right-side buttons).
- Primary search term in on-screen text inside 2 seconds.
- Deduct for text that is too small, too fast to read, or covering the payoff.

### 7. Caption and search (5)
- Line 1 = primary search term + hook. Proof line with a real fact. One save OR send trigger. 3-5 specific hashtags on their own line.
- Deduct for 30 hashtags, keyword buried under hashtags, no caption.

### 8. Audio strategy (5)
- 5: Trending sound caught early (<48h rising, under ~5,000 Reels uses) on a scenery clip, OR strong voiceover with real ambient on a story clip.
- 3: Original or evergreen sound, fine.
- 0-2: Late/saturated trend, music fighting a voiceover, audio that only works with sound on but no captions.

### 9. Originality and production fit (5)
- Original footage, native 9:16, sharp, 1080x1920, not a recycled watermark, people/faces when it fits the brand, feels like a person not an ad.
- Deduct for letterboxed 16:9, TikTok watermark on Reels, blurry, stock-looking.

## Gates and penalties (apply after summing)

| Issue | Penalty |
|---|---|
| Repost of someone else's content | cap score at 40 |
| Over 60s | -10 |
| No hook text AND no voiceover (captionless montage) | -10 |
| Watermark from another platform | -5 |
| House-style violations in caption or text (brand playbook) | -2 each, max -6 |
| Brand banned claim on screen or in caption (e.g. DCKT: founding year, review count, ACA claim, sturgeon promise, named competitor) | must fix before posting, -5 |
| Posting into a burst (2+ same day) | prediction tier -1 |

## Tiers and view prediction

Multiply by the account's median views on that platform (7-day window). Use the most
recent baseline available. If the prediction log has calibration notes, apply them.

| Score | Tier | Multiplier of median | Typical read |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0-39 | Flop | 0.3-0.8x | Below the account's normal |
| 40-59 | Average | 0.7-1.5x | Blends into the catalog |
| 60-74 | Solid | 1.2-3x | Above normal, top third of the catalog |
| 75-89 | Breakout candidate | 2-6x | Could beat the account's best |
| 90-100 | Viral candidate | 4-15x+ | Needs FYP reach; long tail if it hits |

Account-state adjustments:
- Dormant account or For You share near 0%: cap at Solid until the log shows real FYP reach.
- Account under ~1,000 followers: the upside is driven by non-follower reach, so send and hook factors matter more than likes. Mention it.
- No baseline data at all: give tier only, not a view number, and set confidence to Low.

Confidence:
- High: video file + real analytics baseline + 5+ calibrated log rows.
- Medium: video or full script + a baseline.
- Low: idea or caption only, or no baseline.
