# Creator Outreach Playbook — Master Template

*Central reference copy. Business-specific versions live in each vault's Marketing/ folder.*

---

## What This Is

Proactive outreach to local content creators, offering them a free tour or experience in exchange for nothing. No contract, no posting requirements, no talking points. If they make something cool, great. If they just enjoy the ride, that's fine too.

This is different from the UGC Repost Playbook (which is reactive, finding content that already exists). This is about building relationships with creators before they've ever been on your boat or in your kayak.

## Why This Works for Us Specifically

Most brands have to manufacture reasons for creators to show up. We sell the experience they want to film. A sunset kayak through a swamp, a party barge with live music, a floating sauna on the Columbia River: that's content. We're not asking creators to hold up a product and smile. We're inviting them into something genuinely worth capturing.

The cost is nearly zero. An empty seat on a tour that's going out anyway costs us nothing. The content a creator produces from that seat would cost $500-2,000 to produce ourselves.

## Who to Target

### The Sweet Spot: Micro-Influencers (2K-50K followers)

Don't chase big accounts. You want local creators who:
- Live in or frequently visit your city
- Post outdoor, food, travel, nightlife, or city-life content
- Have real engagement (comments from real people, not bot spam)
- Show original storytelling, not just lip-syncs and trending audio reposts
- Would genuinely enjoy your experience

### Where to Find Them

**Instagram:**
- Search your city's location tags, filter to Reels
- Search hashtags: #[city]life, #[city]things, #thingstodoin[city], #[city]outdoors
- Look at who's tagging local restaurants, bars, and attractions you respect
- Check who's commenting on your competitors' posts

**TikTok:**
- Search "[city] things to do" and "[city] hidden gems"
- Look at creators doing "day in my life in [city]" content
- Check local hashtags: #[city]tok, #[city]local

**Facebook Groups:**
- Local photography groups
- "[City] bloggers" or "[City] content creators" groups
- Tourism and activity groups (people who post great content here often have IG followings)

### Types of Creators to Prioritize

| Type | Why | Example |
|------|-----|---------|
| City/local life creators | Their audience IS your customer | "Things to do in NOLA this weekend" accounts |
| Outdoor/adventure creators | Direct alignment with kayak + sauna | Hikers, paddlers, nature photographers |
| Food/nightlife creators | Great crossover for party barges + NOLA | Restaurant reviewers, bar hoppers |
| Travel creators passing through | One viral post from a traveler can outperform a month of local content | "48 hours in [city]" format |
| Couples/friend group content | Matches the booking unit (groups, bachelorettes, date nights) | Couples doing "[city] date ideas" |

### Who to Avoid
- Accounts that only post sponsored content (their audience has ad blindness)
- Anyone with fake engagement (10K followers, 12 likes per post)
- Creators whose vibe doesn't match yours (if they'd never book your tour on their own, skip it)

## How to Reach Out

### The Approach: DM from the Business Instagram Account

Not your personal account. Not email. Not GHL. A direct message from the business page. This matters because:
1. It's verifiable (they can see the real business account)
2. It starts a relationship with the brand, not just you
3. It feels casual, not corporate

### First DM — Cold Outreach

Keep it short. No pitch deck energy. You're one human inviting another human to do something fun.

**Template A — Simple invite:**

> Hey [name]! Love your content, especially [reference a specific recent post]. We run [business name] here in [city] and we'd love to have you out on a [tour/session/cruise]. No strings, no posting requirements, just come enjoy it. Want us to set something up?

**Template B — For travel creators:**

> Hey [name]! Saw you're hitting [city] soon. We run [kayak tours through the swamp / a floating sauna on the river / party boats on the water] and we'd love to have you out while you're here. Free, no catch. Let us know if you want us to hold a spot.

**Template C — For creators who posted about a competitor or similar experience:**

> Hey [name]! Saw your [post about competitor/similar activity]. We do something a little different here: [one sentence differentiator, e.g. "kayak tours, no motors, small groups, real wildlife"]. Would love to have you out to see the difference. On us, no strings. Interested?

### Rules for Outreach
- **Reference something specific** about their content. "Love your content" alone sounds automated. "Loved the Reel of you and your friend at [place]" sounds human.
- **Don't ask them to post.** This is the most important part. The moment you say "in exchange for a post," it becomes transactional and the content suffers. If the experience is good, they'll post. If it's not, you have a bigger problem than marketing.
- **One follow-up max.** If they don't reply after 3-5 days, send one casual follow-up. After that, move on. Don't be weird about it.

**Follow-up DM (if no reply after 5 days):**

> Hey just bumping this in case it got buried. No pressure either way!

### If They Say Yes

> Awesome! When works for you? We run [tours/sessions] [days/times]. Pick whatever works and I'll hold a spot. You can bring a friend too if you want.

Then:
- Hold the spot, confirm the day before
- Let the guide/captain know a creator is coming (not to treat them differently, just so they're aware)
- After the experience, send a quick thank you DM: "Hope you had a good time! No ask from us, just glad you came out."

### If They Post Something

- Like it immediately
- Leave a genuine comment (not "Thanks for sharing!" but something specific about their content)
- Repost using the UGC Playbook process (ask permission, download, repost with tag)
- Flag top performers for ads per the UGC playbook
- Add them to your "relationship" tier (see tracking below) for future invites, early access to new tours, etc.

### If They Don't Post

That's fine. Don't follow up asking where their post is. The relationship still exists. They had a good experience, they'll mention you organically at some point, and you can invite them back next season. Playing the long game here is what separates this from transactional influencer marketing.

## The "Access as Currency" Playbook

Beyond free tours, think about what else you can offer that creators value:

| What you have | What creators want | How to offer it |
|---------------|-------------------|-----------------|
| Unique locations (swamp, river, caves, marina) | Backdrops that nobody else has | "Want to film here before we open for the day?" |
| Live musicians (Party Barge) | Collab content with local artists | Connect creator with the musician |
| Behind-the-scenes access | Content that feels exclusive | "Want to ride along on a guide training day?" |
| Wildlife encounters | Unscripted moments that go viral | Morning tours have the best wildlife, steer them there |
| Sunset/golden hour timing | Beautiful light for content | Recommend the right tour time for their content style |
| Group experiences | Content with friends performs better | "Bring 3-4 friends, we'll set you up" |

### Relationship Tiers

As your creator network grows, you'll naturally sort them:

- **One-timers**: Came, enjoyed it, maybe posted, no ongoing relationship. That's fine.
- **Repeat creators**: Loved it, posted, want to come back. Invite them quarterly or for special events (new tour launch, seasonal kickoff, holiday events).
- **Brand friends**: Genuinely love what you do, mention you unprompted, bring friends. These are gold. Give them first access to everything. If you ever do paid partnerships, start here.

## Frequency & Time Commitment

| Task | How often | Time |
|------|-----------|------|
| Search for new creators to invite | Twice a month | 20-30 min |
| Send outreach DMs | As you find them | 5 min each |
| Coordinate tour dates for interested creators | As replies come in | 5 min each |
| Post-tour thank you DM | Day after their tour | 2 min |
| Review if they posted + respond | Week after their tour | 5 min |

**Total: ~1-2 hours per month per business.** This is not a heavy lift.

## What This Is NOT

- Not a formal influencer program with contracts and deliverables
- Not an affiliate program (that's separate, see NKST affiliate program)
- Not automated outreach through GHL or any tool
- Not a replacement for the UGC Repost Playbook (that handles existing content, this creates new relationships)

This is relationship-first marketing. You're inviting people into something real and trusting that the experience speaks for itself. If it doesn't, the problem isn't your marketing.
