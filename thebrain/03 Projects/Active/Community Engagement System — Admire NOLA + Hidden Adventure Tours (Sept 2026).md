## 1. What this is for (and the one thing it is not)

Commenting, liking, and sharing on other people's posts is not a growth hack. It's how a young account earns the right to be seen. Both brands are small: Admire NOLA YouTube has 18 subs and ~1,650 views a day when it uploads, its TikTok is new, HAT's IG and TikTok are weeks old at zero followers. At that size the algorithms decide whether you're a real participant or a posting robot by what you do *between* posts.

Three things engagement actually buys:
1. **Profile visits.** A good comment under a 200K-view NOLA video gets more eyeballs than our own post will. People tap the avatar.
2. **Signal to the algorithm** that the account is a person in the NOLA/travel/outdoors cluster, so our posts get tested against that audience instead of nobody.
3. **Relationships** with the 40 to 60 local creators and pages that eventually share, tag, duet, or come out on a boat.

What it is NOT: lead gen. No comment ever sells. The funnel is the bio and the content. A comment that mentions our tours is the one comment guaranteed to lose.

## 2. The rules that keep us out of jail

These are the spam-proof / penalty-proof lines. Break one and the whole month's work gets throttled.

**Never**
- Post the same comment twice. Not across posts, not across brands, not with one word changed.
- Put a link, a phone number, a price, an @ of our own brand, or "DM us" in any comment. Anywhere.
- Comment as Admire NOLA *and* HAT on the same post or thread. One brand per post, ever. Two sibling accounts on one post is a pod and Meta knows what a pod looks like.
- Comment on a direct competitor's post (airboat companies, other swamp tours, other party boats, other bike bars). It reads as poaching, gets reported, and reports are the fastest route to a restriction. Adjacent is fine. Competing is not.
- Use any auto-like, auto-follow, auto-comment tool, browser extension, or bot, including our own Playwright bridge. Reading is fine. Posting is by thumb, from a phone.
- Emoji-only comments at volume, "Great post!", "Love this!", "🔥🔥🔥". These are the literal training set for the spam classifier.
- Correct, argue with, or dunk on the poster. Even when they're wrong about gators.
- Pile into a mega account (500K+) in the first 60 seconds. Bots do that. We land on mid-size accounts early and big accounts late.
- Mass-follow. Follows are 2 to 5 a day, from the creator map, people we actually engaged with.
- Use David's personal Facebook profile for any of this. Page-to-Page only (see the global rule on David's name).

**Ceilings** (vendor consensus; the platforms publish behaviour rules, not numbers, and they react to patterns more than totals)
| Platform | Account age | Comments/day | Comments/hour | Other |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Instagram | under 3 months | 20 to 30 | under 8 | follows under 10/day |
| Instagram | over 3 months | 40 to 60 | under 12 | likes under 100/day |
| TikTok | any | under 40 | under 10 | under 150 total actions |
| Threads | any | 30 | under 10 | replies count, not posts |
| Facebook Page | any | 15 | under 5 | shares 1 to 2/day |
| YouTube | any | 15 | under 5 | no links, ever |

We will run at roughly half those numbers. The ceiling is the fence, not the target.

**Spacing.** Minimum 60 to 90 seconds between comments. Never 10 in a row. Scroll, watch something to the end, then comment. A session is 15 minutes of mostly watching and 4 to 8 actions.

**Warm-up for a new account** (HAT IG, HAT TikTok, Admire TikTok, any new Threads): days 1 to 3 watch only, days 4 to 7 like and follow only, comments from day 8, and no commenting on others until the account has 3 to 4 of its own posts up. A zero-post account commenting around is the bot profile exactly.

**If you get action-blocked:** stop everything on that account for 72 hours. Don't appeal three times, don't switch to a VPN, don't log in from a new device. Then come back at half volume for a week. Log it on the scoreboard.

## 3. The comment formula: value, humor, support

Every comment has to pass one test: **would a local friend who actually knows this stuff say this, out loud, to this person?** If it sounds like a brand, delete it.

Six comment types. Rotate them. A session should never be all one type.

| Type | What it does | Example shape |
|---|---|---|
| Insider add | Adds one specific fact the poster didn't have | "That's Bayou St. John, not the swamp. Which is why nobody's sunburned." |
| Deadpan observation | Names the funny thing in the clip, dry | "the heron in the back has seen this exact bachelorette 400 times" |
| Specific compliment | Praises a *specific* second, shot, or choice, not the post | "the cut at 0:04 where the tuba comes in is the whole video" |
| Real question | Asks something you actually want answered, invites a reply | "Which dock is that? The light there is unreal." |
| Support / defend | Answers a fear in the comments, backs the poster when a thread turns | "they don't come near you if nobody's feeding them, that's the whole trick" |
| Callback | Runs a running joke the niche already has | "that's not a log" / "swamp puppy" / "nope rope" |

**Humor rules.** Dry beats loud. One joke per comment. Never at the poster's expense, only at the situation's (the humidity, the heron, the one friend filming, Bourbon Street). Punch sideways, not down. If you have to explain it, cut it.

**Support rules.** Support is the highest-value comment on these platforms right now: reply to *other people's comments* in the thread, especially the scared ones ("is it safe", "big nope", "I'd turn around"). Those threads are where conversation velocity lives, and a calm local answer gets more likes than the top-level comment. Aim for 1 in 3 of our comments to be replies to comments, not to posts.

**Length.** 6 to 25 words. Vary it. One-liners and two-sentence takes, never paragraphs.

**Voice split.** Admire NOLA is the calm, deadpan, honest-operator, nature-first voice (the "we don't feed them" brand). HAT is the punk, hype, slightly rude, party-and-music voice. Same person can run both, but the comment should be obviously from a different character. Details and banks in the lane docs.

## 4. Lanes: who engages with what

Both brands want the same "things to do in New Orleans" audience. To avoid stepping on each other (and tripping the pod detector), lanes are split.

| Lane | Admire NOLA | Hidden Adventure Tours |
|---|---|---|

| Bachelorette, party boats, Bourbon, nightlife | stays out | **Owns it** |
| Brass bands, second lines, bounce, Frenchmen | occasional, respectful | **Owns it** |
| Local news, weather, City Park, neighborhoods | Mon / Wed / Fri | Tue / Thu / Sat |

Cross-brand liking is fine. Cross-brand commenting on the same post is not. NPB, NKST, and Bike Bar should not all show up under a HAT post either. One sibling comment per post, period.

## 5. Platform playbooks (what actually moves each one)

**Instagram.** Top three ranking signals are watch time, likes per reach, sends per reach. Sends is the one a small account can influence, so our weekly "share" action on IG is a DM-send of someone else's reel to a friend and a Story reshare of a creator's post with one line of our take (tag them). Comments on Reels get seen more than on photos. Reply to every comment on our own posts within the hour, different words every time. Threads replies show on IG profiles now, which is free reach.

**Threads.** Auto-created from each IG account; confirm both exist. This is the best platform for text humor and the only one where a zero-follower account can get real reach (8 to 12 percent reach rates under 10K, replies weighted roughly 10x a like). Replies are the product here, not posts. Reply in the first hour on NOLA, travel, and swamp posts. Quote-post with a take once or twice a week. **Do not** run "comment YES if" or "tag someone who" on Threads; Meta demotes that since late 2024 and it's gotten stricter. The share-first captions rule we use on IG does not transfer to Threads. Ask real questions instead.

**TikTok.** Comments carry more weight than likes. The big move is **Reply with Video**: when a comment on our post deserves a 7-second answer, answer with a clip. It counts as a new post linked to the old one and it's the one format where "answering a question" is the content. Do one a week. Trending sounds are still a by-hand thing from the phone. TikTok US pushes new posts to followers first now, which at zero followers is brutal, so warm the account with watching and commenting before expecting anything. Reply to comments on our own videos fast; 15 minutes before and after every upload.

**Facebook.** Comment as the Page on other Pages' posts and Reels (local news, tourism boards, venues, festivals). Groups are mostly closed to Pages and we won't use David's profile, so groups are out unless a group explicitly allows Pages. FB shares as the Page (share a WWL weather post with "morning tours are the move this week") are cheap and count. FB Reels comments land well with the 35+ crowd that actually books.

**YouTube.** Comment as the channel on local creators' videos and Shorts, and on the big "3 days in New Orleans" travel vlogs (the ones with 100K+ views and comment sections that are still moving). A pinned/hearted reply from the creator is a real traffic source; people click channel avatars on YouTube more than anywhere. No links, no repeated text, or the spam filter eats it silently. Heart and reply to every comment on our own Shorts (Admire has 8 comments a month; each one gets a human reply). Community tab once a week with a question, not a promo.

**Reddit.** Already handled by ReplyAgent for Admire NOLA (daily monitor on mbp-2, #helm-admire, third-party recommendation model, no URLs, no prices, no customer pose). The growth-plays bank adds: target threads that already rank for "new orleans swamp tour reddit" searches, not just fresh ones. HAT stays off Reddit for now; two DJL brands recommending in the same threads is the thing that gets both banned.

## 6. Cadence

**Daily, 20 minutes total, phone, thumbs.** Alternate which brand gets the "outbound" session; both brands do own-post replies every day.

| | Admire NOLA day (M/W/F) | HAT day (T/Th/Sa) |
|---|---|---|
| 0 to 5 min | Scroll the feed, watch 6 to 10 NOLA/swamp/travel clips to the end, save 1 | Scroll, watch 6 to 10 party/music/NOLA clips to the end, save 1 |
| 5 to 13 min | 4 to 6 comments from the day's sheet, 1 in 3 as a reply to someone's comment, 60+ seconds apart | Same, HAT lanes |
| 13 to 17 min | Reply to every comment on our posts, both brands | Same |
| 17 to 20 min | 1 share action (DM-send a reel, Story reshare, FB Page share, or Threads quote), follow 2 to 3 accounts | Same |

**Weekly**
- 1 TikTok Reply-with-Video per brand (when a comment earns it).
- 1 Threads quote-post with a take per brand.
- 1 YouTube Community post (Admire) with a real question.
- Sunday: replies only. Fill the scoreboard (section 8).

**Monthly**
- Refresh the creator map. First refresh is data-driven: run `/follower-personas` creator mode for Admire (about $8 for the graph plus $0.40 for the creator enrichment) and use it for both brands, since HAT's IG has no audience to sample yet.
- Retire any target account we've commented on 4+ times with no reply. Move on.

## 7. Tooling: what Claude does, what David does

The line is simple. **Claude finds and drafts. David posts.** Nothing outbound is automated, because auto-commenting is the single most reliable way to get a young account flagged.

**Claude, daily (build in week 2):** the Engagement Sheet. A short list, 8 to 10 targets per brand-day, delivered to Slack #helm-admire by 7am CT and saved to the vault:
- Fresh posts (last 24h) from the creator map, pulled through the Instagram Graph API's business discovery (sanctioned, no scraping; reads public business/creator accounts by username) and the YouTube Data API for new NOLA videos. TikTok and Threads targets stay by-hand from the phone.
- For each target: the post, why it's a fit, and 2 drafted comment options in the brand's voice, written to the actual content (a specific second or line), passed through the copywriting 3-test filter (can you picture it, could it be wrong, could anyone else have said it).
- David picks one, changes a word or two, and posts it from the phone. Or ignores it. Never posts one verbatim twice.

**Claude, weekly (Sunday):** pulls IG account insights (profile views, follows, accounts engaged), YouTube analytics, and the ReplyAgent monitor log, and writes the scoreboard row. Flags any action block or reach cliff.

**Claude, monthly:** follower-personas creator run, rebuilds the target list, appends new creators to `TheBrain/Creator Outreach/Creator Master List.xlsx` as proposed rows, drafts invite emails for the top 3 (comped outing, sign as the business, David approves).

**David, daily:** the 20 minutes above. That's the whole job. If it doesn't fit, cut to one brand per day and keep own-post replies for both. Skipping days is fine. Bursts are not.
