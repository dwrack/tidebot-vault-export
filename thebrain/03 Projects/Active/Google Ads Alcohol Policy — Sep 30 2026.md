# Google Ads Alcohol Policy: Sep 30 2026

**Verdict: Low impact. Nothing here blocks us. The US stays an approved country and BYOB copy stays legal. 31 live ads mention alcohol, 20 already carry Google's "Alcohol: Limited" label and keep running. Zero live alcohol disapprovals. About 30 minutes of copy cleanup before Oct 1 is the whole job.**

Checked 2026-09-19. Read-only, nothing changed in any account.

## What changed
- Google replaces the Alcohol policy article on Sep 30 with one global framework: a single country table, revised country rules, more allowed locations. [Announcement](https://support.google.com/adspolicy/answer/17452318), [new policy preview](https://support.google.com/adspolicy/answer/17404252), [current policy](https://support.google.com/adspolicy/answer/6012382).
- New certification applies only to 0% alcohol products in Egypt, India and Indonesia. Old permissions get revoked Sep 30. Not us.
- Mixers, home brew gear and recipes are now explicitly allowed. Landing pages must show ABV for drinks at 0.5%+. Not us, we sell no alcohol.
- The "Irresponsible alcohol advertising" section was rewritten. This is the only part that touches us. It bans: drinking "prior to or during activities requiring sobriety or a high degree of skill", claims of social or sexual success, stressing alcohol strength, and creative showing minors or pregnant people drinking.
- Alcohol-labeled ads still can't run as Dynamic Search Ads, Gmail ads or app install ads, and never serve to minors.
- Google warns ads may show multiple policy labels for a few weeks during the switch. Expect it, don't panic-edit.
- Neither version of the policy mentions BYOB venues, tours or bars. We get labeled "Alcohol information" on the words alone (booze, BYOB, drinks).
- Caveat: I read Google's pages through a fetch summary, not raw text. Skim the preview link once, 2 minutes.
