## 1. Chez Pierre Bakery Lakeview

**Preview:** `/chez-pierre-bakery-lakeview/`
**Address:** 141 Harrison Ave, New Orleans, LA 70124 (Lakeview)
**Phone:** (504) 354-9405
**Google:** 4.3 stars, 63 reviews (46 five-star) · Instagram: @chezpierrefrenchbakery
**Hours:** Mon 8am–2pm · Tue–Sat 8am–7pm · Sun closed
**Best time to walk in:** Tue–Fri, 2:00–4:00pm. After the lunch banh mi rush, before the pre-close pickup wave. No popular-times data on this one, so confirm with whoever is at the counter.
**Likely decision-maker:** Lindsay (named in reviews as the person at the counter; may be owner or manager). Ask for her by name.

**Why they need this:** Smallest review count of the seven, which is the point. They have an Instagram but no way for "French bakery Lakeview" or "banh mi Harrison Ave" to find them on Google outside the map pin. Custom cakes and catering are their highest-ticket items, and right now there is nowhere to send someone who wants to order one.

**The hook:** "A reviewer wrote that people are sleeping on this place. I built you the thing that wakes them up."

**Show them first:** the menu section (croissants, banh mi, custom cakes) and the tap-to-call button. Then the Instagram link in the footer, so she sees it plugs into what she already does.

**Watch for:** Owner may be a franchisee or second location of Chez Pierre French Bakery. If so, ask who owns the Lakeview shop specifically. Also, the preview's stats bar says 4.7 stars while the header says 4.3; fix before showing.

## 2. Lucky Jean Seafood

**Preview:** `/lucky-jean-seafood/`
**Address:** 6746 Chef Menteur Hwy, New Orleans, LA 70126 (New Orleans East)
**Phone:** (504) 245-7842
**Google:** 4.2 stars, 805 reviews
**Hours:** Mon–Fri 10am–8pm · Sat–Sun closed
**Best time to walk in:** Mon–Fri, 10:00–11:00am, right at open. Busyness is lowest at open and builds steadily to a 4–6pm peak. Do not go Friday afternoon.
**Likely decision-maker:** Unknown. Family-run counter operation, 20+ years. The person taking orders is very likely family. Ask "who handles the business side?"

**Why they need this:** 805 reviews and a counter that has fed the East since before Katrina, and the only place to find them online is a map pin. Catering for parties and second lines is on their profile; that is a phone call they are not getting from anyone who searched for it.

**The hook:** "Twenty years of the East trusting you, and Google is the only one telling the story. I built the page that does it properly."

**Show them first:** the hero photo and the "What to order" list (fried shrimp by the pound, shrimp po'boy, $0.99 egg rolls). Then the catering line under "Good to know."

**Watch for:** Cash-heavy, no-nonsense operation. Keep it under two minutes. The leave-behind matters here more than the pitch.

## 3. Banks Meat Market

**Address:** 325 S Broad St, New Orleans, LA 70119 (Mid-City)
**Phone:** (504) 301-9171
**Google:** 4.1 stars, 731 reviews
**Hours:** Open 24/7
**Best time to walk in:** Tue–Thu, 9:00–10:00am. Overnight is quietest but nobody who can say yes is there. Mid-morning weekday is the daytime low before the lunch po'boy line. Avoid 4–7pm every day.

**Watch for:** A grocery and check-cashing business may care more about the store side than the food side. Let them steer.

**Phone:** (504) 333-6917

**Hours:** Daily 11am–9:30pm

**Watch for:** Preview header says 852 reviews, stats bar says 837. Fix before showing. Also confirm they have no site of their own (a table-service CBD restaurant with a sushi bar sometimes does, under a different name).
