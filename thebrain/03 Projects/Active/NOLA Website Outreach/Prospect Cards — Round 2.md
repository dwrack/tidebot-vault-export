## 1. Markey's Bar

**Preview:** `/markey-s-bar/`
**Address:** 640 Louisa St, New Orleans, LA 70117 (Bywater)
**Phone:** (504) 943-0785
**Google:** 4.5 stars, 490 reviews (340 five-star) · Instagram: @markeysbar
**Hours:** Mon–Fri 2pm–2am · Sat–Sun 1pm–2am
**Best time to walk in:** Weekday, 2:00–3:30pm, right at open. A 2pm bar is empty at 2pm and whoever unlocks the door is almost always the one who can say yes.
**Likely decision-maker:** Unknown. Long-running Bywater corner bar. Ask the opening bartender who owns it; at a 2pm open that is often the answer.

**Why they need this:** 490 reviews and a New Orleans Shuffleboard League team playing out of the back room, and there is no page anywhere that says so. Someone searching "shuffleboard bar New Orleans" or "Bywater bar with darts" finds nothing but a map pin. The league angle alone is a booking channel they are not running.

**The hook:** "You have a shuffleboard league playing out of your back room and nowhere on the internet says so. I built the page that does."

**Show them first:** the hero, then scroll to the story paragraph about the NOSL plaques on the green wall. The shuffleboard detail is the proof that a person looked rather than a machine scraped.

**Watch for:** No menu is posted anywhere online, so the menu block is a highlights list with a "prices as posted" note. If they want real prices on it, that is a five-minute edit and a good reason for a second visit.

## 2. City Donuts & Café

**Preview:** `/city-donuts-caf/`
**Address:** 3301 S Claiborne Ave, New Orleans, LA 70125 (Uptown)
**Phone:** (504) 372-5811
**Google:** 4.0 stars, 1,034 reviews (566 five-star) · Instagram: @city.donuts
**Hours:** Open 24 hours, seven days
**Best time to walk in:** Weekday 2:00–4:00pm. Between the lunch push and the evening staff change, and a 24-hour operation always has somebody awake. Avoid 6–9am entirely.
**Likely decision-maker:** Unknown. 24-hour counter operation, so ask which shift the owner works rather than whether they are in.

**Why they need this:** 1,034 reviews, open every hour of every day, and no website. "Open now" is their single biggest advantage and Google is the only thing telling anyone. Also the highest one-star count of the Round 2 group at 115, which makes a page they control more valuable, not less.

**The hook:** "You are open at three in the morning and the only thing on the internet that says so is a map pin. This page says it in the header and updates itself."

**Show them first:** the live open-status pill in the header. It reads "Open 24 hours" right now and it will read that at 4am too. Then the glazed beignet card, because that is the thing reviewers separate them on.

**Watch for:** 4.0 stars with 115 one-stars means service complaints exist. Do not oversell the rating. Sell the hours and the beignet.

## 3. Pal's Lounge

**Preview:** `/pal-s-lounge/`
**Address:** 949 N Rendon St, New Orleans, LA 70119 (Bayou St. John)
**Phone:** (504) 488-7257
**Google:** 4.6 stars, 709 reviews (530 five-star) · Instagram: @palsloungenola
**Hours:** 3pm–3am, seven days
**Best time to walk in:** 3:00–4:30pm any day, right at open. By nine this is the busiest bar in Bayou St. John and nobody will talk to you.
**Likely decision-maker:** The Google profile says "identifies as women-owned," so there is a named owner to ask for. Get her name from the opening bartender before pitching.

**Why they need this:** 709 reviews, 530 of them five stars, a women-owned neighborhood bar with a named drink list, and no website. They have an Instagram doing the work a site should do. Nothing captures a search for "bar near Bayou St. John" or "dog friendly bar New Orleans," and the dog thing is in review after review.

**The hook:** "Five hundred and thirty people gave you five stars and there is still nowhere to send someone who asks what you're about."

**Show them first:** the "what to order" row, specifically the Pal's koozie photo. It is their own branded merch in a customer's photo, which makes the point that the whole page is built from what is already theirs.

**Watch for:** The food is a pop-up in the yard, not a house kitchen, and the site says so. If they have taken a kitchen in-house, that copy needs updating before it goes live.

## 4. Holy Crepes!

**Preview:** `/holy-crepes/`
**Address:** 1100 N Peters St, Stall 23, New Orleans, LA 70116 (French Market)
**Phone:** (504) 370-3790
**Google:** 4.7 stars, 295 reviews (255 five-star, 86%) · Instagram: none found
**Hours:** Mon–Thu 10am–4:30pm · Fri–Sun 10am–5pm
**Best time to walk in:** Weekday 10:00–11:30am, before the market fills. One griddle, so any line at all means they cannot talk.
**Likely decision-maker:** Al or Misho. Both are named repeatedly in reviews as the people making the crepes. Ask for them by name; that alone will land well.

**Why they need this:** Highest five-star share of the entire Round 2 group at 86%, a cart that has held Bay 23 since July 2017, and no website. They are also the best gluten-free and dairy-free option in the Quarter according to their own reviewers, and there is no page anywhere that a person with a wheat allergy can find.

**The hook:** "Al, your reviewers say you are the best gluten free option in the French Quarter. Right now somebody with celiac cannot find that out before they get here."

**Show them first:** the allergy line in the facts list, then the hero photo of their own cart with CIRCA JULY 2017 painted on the side. They will recognise it immediately.

**Watch for:** A French Market stall may be on a market lease with rules about outside advertising. Ask what the market allows before promising a domain.

## 5. The Upper Quarter

**Preview:** `/the-upper-quarter/`
**Address:** 1000 Bienville St, New Orleans, LA 70112 (French Quarter)
**Phone:** (504) 523-4111 — **not on the Google profile.** Pulled from third-party directories, so ring it before you rely on the Call button in front of the owner.
**Google:** 4.7 stars, 358 reviews (286 five-star) · Instagram: none found
**Hours:** 12pm–2am, seven days
**Best time to walk in:** 12:00–2:00pm, right at open, any day. A Quarter bar at noon is staff only.
**Likely decision-maker:** Reviews mention "ownership" as present and talking to people, so the owner is around. Ask at open.

**Why they need this:** Their own hanging sign says A NEIGHBORHOOD BAR, which in the French Quarter is a real differentiator, and nothing online carries it. 358 reviews, 286 five stars, no phone on their own Google profile, no website. They are invisible to anyone searching for a Quarter bar that is not a Bourbon Street bar.

**The hook:** "Your sign says neighborhood bar. That is the whole pitch and there is nowhere online saying it. Also, your Google listing has no phone number on it."

**Show them first:** the missing phone number. It is a concrete, free, thirty-second fix on their Google profile, and fixing it in front of them buys the rest of the conversation.

**Watch for:** Hours on third-party directories disagree with Google (some list 3am close). The site uses Google's, which is the one they control. Worth confirming which is right.
