Canonical host is the bare domain. **Host signal split:** GSC reports the `https://www.` homepage with 13.9k impressions at position 3.7, and the bare homepage with 19.9k impressions at position 8.0. The same queries rank noticeably better on the www URL (for example "party boat kemah": 2.8 on www vs 6.9 on bare; "kemah party boat rental": 1.5 vs 6.7). The GBP website field is `https://www.houstonpedalbarge.com/`, and the footer Facebook link goes through a redirect. Google appears to still hold www as the preferred indexed host for many queries. Not a defect on its own, but worth consolidating (see fixes).

## Affects all FH sites (fix once, in the shared theme; FareHarbor contacts Kevin Schmitt / Jasantha Mari)

These are template-level or shared-component issues. HPB is the evidence here, but the same pattern shows on the sibling sites.

- **Page weight and blocking time.** Theme JS bundle (`the-anchor` frontend), jQuery 3.6.0 from cdnjs, FareHarbor honeycomb SDK, gtag and a cookie banner all load on every page. 4.7 to 10 MB per page and TBT of 1.5 to 3.5 s on HPB, consistent with the 1.2 to 2.8 MB of unused JS flagged across sites on 10/30.
- **`/privacy-policy/` listed in the page sitemap and 301/canonicalising to `/privacy-statement-us/`.** Confirmed again on HPB. Also 301 on NPB, BBK, AKT (and 200 on NBB), so this is a template default.
- **Two-hop redirect on the non-canonical host** (`http://www` goes to https www then bare).
- **Default tagline as meta description** ("See Clear Lake Like You've Never Seen It Before") on utility and legal pages. Template fallback when no meta is set.
- **Terms and gift-card pages carry 2 H1s** and the 2023 blog posts carry 6 to 7. Check the template heading levels.
- **Thin utility pages indexable and in the sitemap** (terms, unsubscribe, opt-out, survey). Should be noindex by default.
- **OpenCX widget loaded from `unpkg.com/@opencx/widget@latest`** (HPB, NPB, BBK confirmed). An unpinned `@latest` script means a vendor release can change or break the site's behaviour without anyone touching it. Elfsight platform script is also loaded on HPB and NPB, NBB.
- **Social share links use `twitter.com/share`** (now a login wall).
- Everything above is measurement only.
