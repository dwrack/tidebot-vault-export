# Tech SEO: AKT (austinkayaktours.com), 2026-10-31

Staged report, nothing changed on the live site. Measured 2026-10-31: PageSpeed Insights v5 (with the PSI API key from ~/.claude/.env), curl redirect checks, full sitemap crawl (79 URLs, status + canonical + robots meta + title + meta + H1), robots and sitemap fetch, homepage and blog-body link extraction, script comparison against four other FareHarbor sites.

**Verdict: crawl health is clean, mobile speed is red.** All 79 sitemap URLs return 200, no noindex, no canonical mismatches, no duplicate titles. Mobile performance is 35 on the homepage and 46 on the top revenue page (bat tour). Most of the weight is third-party JavaScript.

Thresholds: LCP good under 2.5 s, poor over 4 s; CLS good under 0.1; perf green 90+, red under 50. CLS is fine. Mobile LCP on the homepage and TBT on both pages are red. CrUX field data came back empty for both URLs, so INP and field LCP are unverified. The 2026-10-01 monthly watch had AKT at 36 mobile; no real change.

Biggest lab causes:
- Elfsight "all-in-one reviews" widget: about 2.0 to 2.5 s of main-thread time per page (`universe-static.elfsightcdn.com`, 461 KB).
- OpenCX chat widget loaded from unpkg (`@opencx/widget`, 647 KB transferred, 0.8 to 1.0 s). Lighthouse saw both `@latest` and a pinned 5.7.0 build, so it may load twice.
- TikTok embed on the bat page: 1.15 s of boot time.
- FareHarbor theme bundles (`cloudfront.net/static/cache/js/output.*.js`, 468 and 514 KB): 0.7 s each.
- Total page weight 6.5 MB (home) and 11.9 MB (bat page). Unused JS 1.7 MB and 2.2 MB.
- Homepage hero image `austin-1402097_1920.jpg?w=1600` is 717 KB (preloaded at high priority, so it competes with the JS for bandwidth). Image delivery savings about 212 KiB. 15 images on the page; the lazy-load is a script class (`lazyload`), not native `loading="lazy"`.
- Instagram embed CSS 320 KB.

## robots.txt and sitemap

- robots.txt: User-agent * disallows /edit/, /fhbr-console/, /cdn-cgi/ only; a scraper-bot block list (MegaIndex, Baidu, Yandex, SeznamBot, etc.). AI crawlers are not blocked. Sitemap line present. /llms.txt returns 200 (not reviewed).
- sitemap.xml is an index: page-sitemap (9), activity-sitemap (13), post-sitemap (57) = 79 URLs. Post count unchanged since 2026-08-16.
- Sitemap hygiene: /covid-19-info/, /affiliates-influencers/ and /privacy-policy/ are listed; the first is stale and the second ranks for rental terms it should not.

## Structured data (crawl)

- Home: Product (with 4 hand-picked TripAdvisor reviews and `reviewCount: "4"`), Organization, LocalBusiness (empty `openingHoursSpecification`), WebSite, WebPage. No `sameAs` on either entity, no `@id` link between LocalBusiness and Organization. GBP shows 143 reviews at 4.8 (pulled today).
- Bat tour: Product, FAQPage, BreadcrumbList. Rental: FAQPage, BreadcrumbList.

## Prioritized fix list

**P1 (breaks or splits indexing / kills speed)**
1. 301 the three duplicate URLs above.
2. Mobile speed: remove or defer the Elfsight reviews widget and TikTok embed below the fold, load OpenCX on interaction and pin one version (not `@latest`), shrink the 717 KB hero (target about 200 KB) and stop preloading it at high priority. Expect homepage mobile perf to move from the 30s to the 50s or better; lab runs vary 5 to 10 points.
3. Publish the two pillar pages that are 404.

**P2 (meaningful)**
4. Schema: FAQPage on /faq/; real rating and count, sameAs and opening hours on the homepage; Product schema on skyline, paddle board bat, halloween, all-day tours.
5. Collapse the 2-hop http apex redirect.
6. Trim the bat page meta (179 -> 139 chars).
7. 301 /kayak-paddleboard-rental/ to /rental/.
8. Run a full internal-link HEAD crawl of the 57 posts and fix 404s.

**P3 (polish)**
9. Rewrite the auto-excerpt meta on the top 10 blog posts by impressions; fix the "WakingTours" H1 on /austin-walking-tours/; demote the second H1 on five posts.
10. Remove /covid-19-info/ from the sitemap.

## Affects all FH sites (fix once, in the shared theme, via FareHarbor: Kevin Schmitt / Jasantha Mari)

- Theme JS bundles (`cloudfront.net/static/cache/js/output.*.js`, about 470 to 510 KB each) plus gtag and the FareHarbor SDK: 1.2 to 2.8 MB of unused JavaScript on every page tested. The theme is `the-anchor` (jQuery 3.6.0 loaded from cdnjs).
- Third-party widgets in the shared site build: the Elfsight reviews script appears in the homepage HTML of AKT, NKST, DCKT and HPB (not BBK); the OpenCX widget loads from `unpkg.com/@opencx/widget@latest` on NKST, DCKT, HPB and BBK and in the AKT lab run. Loading a widget from an unpinned `@latest` URL is both a speed and a stability risk. These are the largest single cost in every PSI run I looked at.
- Homepage structured data in the theme omits `sameAs` (present on HPB only) and ships an empty `openingHoursSpecification`.
- Total page weight 4 to 12 MB; hero and media not right-sized; no native lazy-loading.
- Two-hop http redirect on the non-canonical host (AKT and DCKT both confirmed).
- Auto-excerpt meta descriptions on blog posts exceed 160 characters across the theme.
- `/privacy-policy/` has four H1s on AKT (template issue; DCKT's privacy page redirects).

Everything above is measurement only. No site, DNS, or theme was changed.
