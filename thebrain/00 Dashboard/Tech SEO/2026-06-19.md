## P1 — fix first

**Inconsistent www canonicalization across the portfolio.** Five sites resolve on the bare domain (1 hop), three (AKT, DCKT, GT) force `www` via a **two-hop chain** (`http://dom → https://dom → https://www.dom`). Two problems:
1. The extra hop on AKT/DCKT/GT wastes crawl budget and bleeds a little link equity on every inbound link. Ideally `http://` lands on the final host in **one** 301.
2. Portfolio-wide inconsistency means inbound links, citations, and internal references get split between `www` and non-`www` forms depending on which site. Pick one convention. Since the majority already sit on non-www, standardizing the three www sites to non-www would be the cleaner call, but either direction is fine as long as it's consistent and single-hop.

This is a Cloudflare/redirect-rule change, so it routes through FareHarbor (Kevin Schmitt / Jasantha Mari), not something David can edit directly.
