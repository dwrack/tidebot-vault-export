## Top 5

**1. Contractor estimate + invoice (23).** Incumbents: Invoice Fly (#8 Apple Business), Invoice Simple (#9, and #2 on Play Business), SimplyWise, Joist, Invoice2go. They make you fill in client, line items, tax, terms, then export a PDF. The AI-native version: the contractor says or texts "replace the water heater at the Hendersons, 2 days, parts at cost plus 20%" and gets a finished estimate that turns into an invoice. Six apps in the top 18 and none ran away, so the interface is the problem. Fit is 3 because the buyer is a small operator, which David understands, but he has no list of contractors. What kills it: Intuit ships this, or Solo/Jobber absorb the job.

**2. Camera roll cleaner (22).** Five cleaners in the Utilities top 18, Cleanup is #1. They make you swipe through thousands of photos by hand. The AI version decides what is junk, duplicates and screenshots, and shows you one confirm screen. Fit is 2: cold consumer App Store, paid acquisition against funded competitors. What kills it: Apple ships it in Photos, and CAC.

**3. GPS audio tour (20).** The only target where David owns the inputs: tour routes, guides, local knowledge in NOLA, Houston, Austin and Door County. Incumbents Parkwolf and GuideAlong make fixed pre-recorded park narration. The AI version generates a live narrated route from the rider's position and time. The problem is Demand 2. Nobody is grossing in this job on the chart right now. Season is part of it, but the honest read is that we have no proof of paid demand. The proof would have to come from David's own guests.

**4. Recipe capture + meal planner (20).** Eight apps, none won. They make you paste links and tag by hand. The AI version takes a screenshot or a TikTok and returns a plan and a grocery list. Fit 2. What kills it: crowded and churning, so you need a niche.

**5. Scanner that skips the PDF (20).** iScanner, Scanner App, Adobe Scan, Smart Printer. The AI version never shows a PDF. It reads the document and files the data. Already has AI entrants. Fit 2.

Write the PRD for the contractor invoice app, and test the GPS tour in parallel with David's own guest list before spending anything on it. The invoice app has the strongest chart and a Fit of 3, which is the best shot at a real product. The tour app is the only place David has an unfair advantage, but zero chart demand means you validate with a $10 route pass sold to past guests first, not an app build.
