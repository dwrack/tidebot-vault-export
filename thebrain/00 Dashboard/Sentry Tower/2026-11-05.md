The highest-merit target is still the contractor invoice app (23, Fit 3). The highest-Fit target is still the GPS audio tour (20, Fit 5). They're different apps again. The invoice gap to the field is closing from the side, and the tour app still has no chart demand.

**Claim finder (16).** Settlemate at Finance #13. The incumbent shows you a feed of settlements and has you fill out each claim form. The AI-native version reads your email receipts and purchase history, matches them to open settlements, and files everything in one tap. It's on the list because it climbed 5 spots in a week. It's below 20 because it's one app with no imitators yet, it asks for inbox access (a trust barrier), and it's cold consumer at Fit 2.

## Top 5

**1. Contractor estimate + invoice (23).** Incumbents: Invoice Fly (#8 Apple Business, #5 Play), Invoice Simple (#9 Apple, #2 Play), SimplyWise (#11), Joist (#15), Invoice2go (#16). They make you fill in client, line items, tax and terms, then export a PDF. The AI-native version takes "replace the water heater at the Hendersons, 2 days, parts at cost plus 20%" by voice or text and returns a finished estimate that converts to an invoice. Six apps in the top 18 and none ran away, which says the interface is the problem. Fit is 3: small operator buyer he understands, no list of contractors. What kills it: QuickBooks (now #3) ships it, or Solo, Jobber or an Empacer-style voice assistant absorbs the job before a focused app ships.

**2. Camera roll cleaner (22).** Five cleaners in the Utilities top 18, Cleanup at #1. You swipe through thousands of photos by hand. The AI version picks the junk, duplicates and screenshots and gives you one confirm screen. Fit 2: cold App Store, paid acquisition against funded apps. What kills it: Apple ships it in Photos, and CAC.

**3. GPS audio tour (20).** The only target where David owns the inputs: routes, guides, local knowledge in NOLA, Houston, Austin and Door County. Parkwolf (now #23) and GuideAlong play fixed recorded park narration. The AI version narrates live from your position, time of day, and what the camera sees. The geology Show HN proves that build is cheap. Demand is the problem: nothing in this job is grossing in the top 18, and it's been that way for two scans. The proof has to come from David's own guests.

**4. Recipe capture + meal planner (20).** Eight apps, no winner, and the order shuffles every week. You paste links and tag by hand. The AI version turns a screenshot or a TikTok into a plan and a grocery list. Fit 2. What kills it: crowded and churning, so you'd need a niche.

**5. Scanner that skips the PDF (20).** iScanner #4, Scanner App #5, Adobe Scan #12, Smart Printer #14, plus CamScanner #8 in Productivity. The AI version never shows a PDF. It reads the document and files the data. AI entrants are already here. Fit 2.
