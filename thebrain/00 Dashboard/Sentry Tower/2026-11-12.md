## Top 5
**1. Contractor estimate + invoice (23).** Incumbents make you fill a form per line item, pick a template, and chase a PDF. The AI-native version takes a voice note or photo of the job, returns a priced estimate, and converts it to an invoice on approval. Six clones grossing and no runaway winner. Kills it: Intuit absorbing it, and Jobber moving down-market (Jobber is rising, Apple #21).

**2. Camera roll cleaner (22).** Five clones, Cleanup #1 in Utilities. The AI version deletes nothing you have to review: it groups, picks keepers, and acts. Kills it: Apple shipping this in Photos, and paid UA. Fit 2.

**3. GPS audio tour (20).** The only target he can feed from owned inputs. Narration is cheap now (Show HN geology narrator, 262 pts last scan). Kills it: no paid-demand proof on the chart, Parkwolf at #22 and seasonal.

**3. Recipe capture + meal planner (20).** Eight clones plus three more at #20-22. Paste a link, photo, or video and it returns a clean recipe and a plan. Kills it: every clone already does some of this, and the category is crowded for a cold launch.

**3. Scanner that skips the PDF (20).** Four clones. Scan a document and get the structured data, not the file. Kills it: already crowded with AI entrants and Adobe.

## What I'd do
Write the PRD for contractor estimate + invoice and put a hard stop on it: if a cheap landing-page test to local trades does not get signups in two weeks, drop it. The score is the highest and flank pressure eased this week. Fit 3 is the honest weak spot. GPS audio tour is the one to build only if he wants to ride owned distribution instead of chasing the highest-merit target.
