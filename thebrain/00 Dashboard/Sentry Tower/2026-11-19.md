## Top 5
**1. Contractor estimate + invoice (23).** Incumbents make you pick a template, fill a line-item form, and chase a PDF. The AI-native version takes a voice note or a photo of the job, returns a priced estimate, and flips it to an invoice on approval. Six clones on both stores and no runaway winner for 8 straight scans. Kills it: Intuit (#5 Apple, #3 Play) bolting this onto QuickBooks, or Jobber moving down-market.

**2. Camera roll cleaner (22).** Five clones, Cleanup #1 in Utilities for weeks. The AI version groups, picks keepers, and acts, instead of making you swipe through 4,000 duplicates. Kills it: Apple shipping it inside Photos, and the paid UA these clones live on. Fit 2.

**3. Scanner that skips the PDF (20).** Four clones, iScanner #3, Scanner App #4. Scan a document, get the fields (vendor, total, date, due), not the file. Kills it: Adobe Scan and CamScanner already layering AI on, and the category runs on paid UA. Fit 2.

**4. Recipe capture + meal planner (19).** Nine clones now. Whipp's jump shows plan-first beats save-first, which is the AI-native thesis working, and also means the gap is closing. Paprika going free shows incumbents will fight on price. Kills it: Whipp running away with it. Fit 2.

**5. Claim finder (17).** Collect passed Settlemate in one week, so the job has proven demand and real competition. The AI version reads your purchase history and files the claim for you instead of showing a list. Kills it: needs inbox or purchase-data access, which is a trust and plumbing wall. Fit 2.
