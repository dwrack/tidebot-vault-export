## The top 5

**Contractor estimate + invoice (23, Fit 3).** Incumbents make a plumber fill in line items after the job. AI-native version listens to the walkthrough or reads job-site photos and outputs the estimate. Wins because the whole interface is a form. Kills it: Intuit (QuickBooks #5 in Business) ships it, and Jobber is now in Google Play's Business top 18 doing field-service software with AI bolted on. Biggest prize, hardest door.

**Camera roll cleaner (22, Fit 2).** Incumbents make you swipe through 10,000 photos and hit you with an auto-renewing trial. AI-native version just does the triage. Cleanup has held #1 in Utilities three scans running with four clones behind it, so nobody's out-built anybody. Kills it: Apple ships it into Photos, and winning means buying cold App Store installs against funded clones.

**GPS audio tour (22, Fit 5).** GuideAlong and Parkwolf sell a fixed narration track per park, triggered by GPS. AI-native version generates narration for any route, at whatever depth the guest asks, and answers questions. Wins because David already has routes, guides, local knowledge, past-guest lists, and 12 GBP listings in four markets. Kills it: it's a small market, two apps deep, and GuideAlong's 5-spot drop this week is a reminder that this demand is seasonal.

**Recipe capture + meal planner (20, Fit 2).** Incumbents make you retype recipes from screenshots. AI version reads the photo or video and builds the week. Eight apps grossing and the leaderboard reshuffles every scan. Kills it: no recipe audience, and volatile fields attract funded entrants.

**Scanner that skips the PDF (20, Fit 2).** Incumbents give you a PDF. AI version gives you the answer in the PDF. Kills it: Adobe Scan already brands itself "AI PDF Scanner" on Play, and the stack is thinning, which usually means consolidation is starting.
