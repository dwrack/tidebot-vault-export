1. **GuideAlong fell off the Travel grossing chart entirely.** It went #10 (Oct 10), then #15 (Oct 15), and now it isn't in the top 25 at all. Parkwolf slipped #13 to #14. Last scan I set a tripwire: if GuideAlong drops under #18, Demand goes 4 to 3. It tripped, so GPS audio tour drops **22 to 21**. My read is still that the park season is winding down, not that something structural happened. Parkwolf holding at #14 is the evidence. Either way the stack is now one app deep in the top 18, and this demand clearly turns off in October.
2. **The calorie stack grew from 4 to 5.** MyNetDiary climbed #20 to #18. Cal AI climbed #12 to #10, and Lose It! came back at #22. More money is flowing into a job the AI-native app already won. It's a good receipt for the thesis and a bad target.

There are 6 pure invoice/estimate apps in Play's top 18, plus Jobber (#15) and a new name, **Solo: Your Gig Business App** (#16 Play, and new at #25 in Apple Finance). 4. **NeatEat jumped #13 to #6 in Food & Drink.** The recipe stack is still 8, still churning, and nobody is consolidating.
5. **Pocket AI dropped out of the Productivity top 18** (#18 to #22). Voice-to-action holds at 18. There's also new free pressure on it: BetterWispr (open-source Mac dictation, 220 pts on HN) and Atticus (hold-to-talk assistant on `claude -p`, 117 pts).

Two settlement-finder apps (Collect #22, Settlemate down to #19) and a subscription canceller (Subpilot #23) showed up in Finance. That's a small "find money you're owed" pattern. It's a single-model-call job, but it's only 2 apps and they're below #18, so I'm not scoring it yet. Momo AI fell out of the Photo & Video top 18 and Runway climbed #14 to #12.

Capability unlocks: **Krea 3** (an image model built for editing at scale, 352 pts) puts more pressure on the Facetune/Picsart tier, but none of those are targets. **Qwen3-4B with the AI writing tics trained out** (158 pts) is a small on-device writing model that sounds human. ## The top 5

**Contractor estimate + invoice (23, Fit 3).** With the incumbents, a plumber types line items into a form after the job. The AI-native version listens to the walkthrough or reads the job-site photos and writes the estimate. It wins because the whole interface is a form, and the demand is now confirmed on both stores (Invoice Simple is #2 in Play Business). What kills it: Intuit (QuickBooks #5 Apple, #3 Play) ships it, or an all-in-one like Jobber or Solo absorbs the job. David has no contractor audience, so Fit stays at 3.

**Camera roll cleaner (22, Fit 2).** The incumbents make you swipe through 10,000 photos and push an auto-renewing trial. The AI-native version just does the triage. Cleanup has been #1 in Utilities for four scans, with four clones behind it and a fifth (CleanX) arriving. What kills it: Apple builds it into Photos, and winning means buying cold installs against funded clones.

**GPS audio tour (21, Fit 5).** GuideAlong and Parkwolf sell one fixed narration track per park, triggered by GPS. The AI-native version generates narration for any route, at whatever depth the guest wants, and answers their questions. It wins because David already owns the routes, the guides, the local knowledge, the past-guest lists, and 12 GBP listings across four markets. What kills it: the chart just showed how seasonal and thin this market is. One of two incumbents vanished from the top 25 in 12 days. A national-park-style consumer app is a summer business. The fall-proof version is a city one: NOLA haunted, Houston, Austin.

**Recipe capture + meal planner (20, Fit 2).** The incumbents make you retype recipes from screenshots. The AI version reads the photo or video and builds the week. Eight apps are grossing and the order shuffles every scan (NeatEat +7 this week). What kills it: David has no recipe audience, and a volatile field attracts funded entrants.

**Scanner that skips the PDF (20, Fit 2).** The incumbents hand you a PDF. The AI version hands you the answer inside it. The stack is 4 in the top 18 with four more crowding #20 to #25 (ScanGuru is new). Adobe already markets "AI PDF Scanner, OCR" on Play. What kills it: crowding plus Adobe.

## What I'd do

Build the GPS audio tour anyway, but **build the city version, not the park version.** The GuideAlong collapse is the useful news this scan. It shows that park-narration demand switches off in October, and that's exactly when David's NOLA haunted season peaks. So aim the product at year-round city walking routes in the markets he already owns: Door County in summer, NOLA, Houston, and Austin the rest of the year. Don't aim at Parkwolf's turf. Four scans have given the same split, so more scanning won't settle this. Say "/sentry-tower prd gps audio tour" and I'll write the spec with the city-first angle built in.
