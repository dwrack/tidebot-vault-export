## Sparks (run with these)

### 1. Forecast bookings like a company with a data team
- **What changed:** Mistral released Timestral, four open forecasting models (5M to 209M parameters, modified MIT license). With no training, it predicts any time series. A plain 8-thread CPU produces a forecast in 10 to 140 ms. You can feed it things known ahead of time, like the weather forecast and a holiday calendar. Mistral says it ranks #1 among zero-shot models on three benchmarks. Those are their numbers, not independent ones.
- **Who it's for:** Any operator whose demand swings with weather, holidays and events. That describes every one of your brands.
- **Why now:** Until this month, a weather-aware headcount forecast meant hiring someone to build a custom model. Now it's a pip install that runs on mbp-2.
- **Own-the-ground angle:** Open weights run locally on your own FareHarbor history. No vendor and no API bill, and nobody can take it away.
- **Cheap first test:** Pull two seasons of daily headcount for NPB and NKST from the FareHarbor Reports folders. Run the 28M model with the weather forecast and a NOLA event/holiday calendar. Then check its 4-week forecast against what actually books. If it beats your gut on even a few weeks, it becomes the staffing and overtime-preapproval input for every manager.
- **Fit:** You have years of clean booking data across 12 brands and you know which levers move it (Mardi Gras, bachelorette season, a rained-out Saturday). A software founder has neither. It also gives managers a "why we're staffed this way" number they can't argue with.
- Source: [Mistral](https://mistral.ai/news/timestral/) (HN 145 pts), 2026-10-15 · scores: newly-possible 5/5, momentum 3/5, own-ground 5/5, fit 5/5, run-with-it 4/5

### 2. Make every site the official source AI agents can read, before the Cloudflare move
- **What changed:** Three things in one week. The Verge reported sites increasingly blocking AI agents (HN 150 pts, 139 comments). On Oct 15 Google started testing an "official sites" section near the top of AI Mode answers and put inline links higher. On Oct 16 a "Things To Do API" launched to feed local events to agents over MCP; it has no tours yet. Cloudflare now treats "Agent" bots (ChatGPT etc.) as a separate category, blocked by default on some pages since Sept 15.
- **Who it's for:** Any business that wants an AI assistant to say "book it here" and link the business, not Viator.
- **Why now:** Your Oct 16 OTA audit found ChatGPT already names NKST, Freaky Tiki and Austin Kayak #1, and Door County is the gap. Agents read whatever is cleanest. Most tour sites are messy, so you can be the clean one.
- **Own-the-ground angle:** It's your own domains and your own data. Every listing an agent reads from your site is one it didn't read from an OTA that takes 20-30%.
- **Cheap first test:** Publish one machine-readable tours file on doorcountykayaktours.com: JSON plus an `llms.txt` with tour names, all-in prices, durations, meeting points and the FareHarbor booking link. Then ask ChatGPT and Gemini "book a kayak tour in Door County for 4" next Monday and see if they cite it. Separately, add "set Agent + Search bots to Allow on every zone" to the late-Nov Cloudflare migration checklist so the move doesn't block the agents you want.
- **Fit:** This builds on the GBP AI-calling and OTA audits you just ran. You already know the wrong-price and wrong-duration problems (NPB $400 vs $59, Manchac 6 hr vs 4-5 hr), and a single source-of-truth file fixes them in one place.
- Source: [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/1013195/ai-agents-blocked-web) via [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/), 2026-10-18; [Google](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/products/search/updates-ai-mode-overviews-explore-web/), 2026-10-15 · scores: newly-possible 3/5, momentum 4/5, own-ground 5/5, fit 5/5, run-with-it 4/5

### 3. A fast on-device agent model for the night shift
- **What changed:** Liquid shipped LFM2.5-24B-A2B: open weights, 24B total but only 2B active per token, 131K context, trained specifically for tool calling inside agent harnesses. It runs 184 tok/s on an M5 Max and works through llama.cpp/GGUF. It's also $0.10 per million input tokens through their API. Separately, Vercel's October index says open-weight models now carry 71.5% of its gateway tokens, up a lot since June.
- **Who it's for:** Anyone running overnight or background agent loops on their own hardware.
- **Why now:** With 2B active parameters, it should run far faster than the 27B Qwen in your night-shift queue. That means more queue items finished per night on the same Mac.
- **Own-the-ground angle:** Open weights on your own box. No usage limits and no per-token bill.
- **Cheap first test:** Pull the GGUF into Ollama on the night-shift machine and run it on half of one week's `_Ops/Night Shift/Queue.md` against Qwen3.8 27B. Count finished, correct tasks.
- **Fit:** Honestly partial. Any coder could do this, but you already have the rig, so the test costs one evening.
- Source: [Liquid AI](https://www.liquid.ai/blog/lfm2-5-24b-a2b) (HN 288 pts), 2026-10-13 · scores: newly-possible 4/5, momentum 4/5, own-ground 5/5, fit 3/5, run-with-it 4/5
