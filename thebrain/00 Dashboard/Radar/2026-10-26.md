### 1. Move the bulk loops to the cheap tier
- **What changed:** Per minimaxir's tally, GPT-5.6 Luna dropped about 80% (Jul 30). Opus 5.5 came in 40-60% cheaper (Sep 22). The GPT-6.1 family is about 75% cheaper cumulatively. On Oct 22 IBM's CEO told investors open-weight models cost 10-25% of frontier tokens and are "90% as effective" (his numbers, an earnings-call claim).
- **Who it's for:** Anyone running always-on loops, which is you: night shift, reply drafts, review replies, EOD, daily brief, the watchdog.
- **Why now:** The gap between the best model and a good-enough model is now a 4-10x price gap. Most of your loops are classify, summarize or draft jobs that never needed the top tier.
- **Own-the-ground angle:** Ollama already runs on your Macs. Route the easy jobs to a local or cheap model and keep the frontier model for judgment calls. No vendor can reprice you.
- **Cheap first test:** List every loop that calls a model. Tag each "needs frontier" or "draft/classify". Re-point the second group to the cheap tier or local Qwen for one week. Compare the bill and spot-check 20 outputs.
- **Fit:** You know which outputs a human reads closely and which nobody reads. A pure coder doesn't.
- Source: [minimaxir](https://minimaxir.com/2026/10/cheap-llms/), [The Register](https://www.theregister.com/systems/2026/10/22/ibm-ceo-says-open-weight-models-are-now-irresistibly-good-and-cheap-on-prem/5304640), 2026-10-25 · scores: newly-possible 4/5, momentum 5/5, own-ground 4/5, fit 3/5, run-with-it 4/5

### 2. Put one flaky loop on Omnara and approve from your phone
- **What changed:** Omnara is an open-source "managed agent platform". Every step is saved to Postgres, so an agent resumes after a crash or restart. Tools can run automatically, need approval, or be blocked. It connects to Slack, Discord and GitHub and has an MCP server. Self-host is `docker compose up`.
- **Who it's for:** People whose agent loops die quietly. Your own memory notes list several silent-failure modes (claude -p exiting 0 when logged out, launchd and iCloud access).
- **Why now:** Durable, resumable agents were a paid vendor feature. Here it is Apache 2.0 and self-hostable, and the Slack approval path means the phone side comes with it.
- **Own-the-ground angle:** It runs on mbp-2, which already has Docker. Your data stays in your own Postgres. If the project dies, you still hold the code.
- **Cheap first test:** Stand it up on mbp-2 and move ONE loop in. The loop watchdog is the safest pick. See whether a crash resumes and whether an approval request shows up in Slack. Skip anything that touches outbound sends until it has proven itself.
- **Fit:** Your approval-before-send rules map straight onto its approve/auto/block permissions. The page says nothing about a mobile app, so the phone angle is Slack only.
- **Age note:** The repo shows no date. The HN post is Oct 21, so it's inside the window. The README warns the local defaults are insecure, so read the self-hosting guide first.
- Source: [GitHub](https://github.com/omnara-ai/omnara) (HN 114 pts), 2026-10-21 · scores: newly-possible 4/5, momentum 3/5, own-ground 5/5, fit 4/5 (includes the desk-mobile bonus), run-with-it 4/5

### 3. Show the solo-operator stack to the long tail of tour operators
- **What changed:** Skift counts about 20 versions of the one-person travel business, from a Delhi guide with 259 reviews to a co-host running 20+ rentals. Software handles guest messages, pricing and dispatch. It names FareHarbor and Bokun as the booking plumbing. The same week, Skift reports travel VC funding fell for a second straight quarter, with AI rounds cut in half.
- **Who it's for:** Small tour operators who run on FareHarbor like you do but have no data person, no review SMS, no daily brief.
- **Why now:** Funded travel-AI startups are pulling back, so the operators who built their own stack have the field to themselves.
- **Own-the-ground angle:** You'd be selling your own playbook and skills, not renting someone's platform. An email list from Tourpreneur-type audiences would be yours.
- **Cheap first test:** Write a one-page "what runs my 12 brands" sheet (daily FH brief, review SMS, GBP poster, loop watchdog). Send it to 5 operators you know. Count who asks for more. If it's zero, you saved yourself a build.
- **Fit:** You operate 12 brands. Nobody selling AI tools to tours has that.
- **Caveat:** This one is the least proven of the three. It rests on one Skift article and a hunch about demand.
- Source: [Skift](https://skift.com/2026/10/26/the-rise-of-one-person-travel-businesses/), [Skift VC](https://skift.com/2026/10/23/travel-vc-funding-fell-for-the-second-straight-quarter-rounds-for-ai-companies-cut-in-half/), 2026-10-26 · scores: newly-possible 3/5, momentum 3/5, own-ground 5/5, fit 5/5, run-with-it 3/5
