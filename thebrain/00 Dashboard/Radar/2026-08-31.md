## Sparks (run with these)

### 1. "Accept: text/markdown" content-negotiation standard
- **What changed:** A spec is forming (176-point HN thread) for websites to serve a distilled Markdown version of any page when a client asks for `Accept: text/markdown` instead of HTML. Same URL, two representations.
- **Who it's for:** Any content-heavy site trying to get read cleanly by ChatGPT, Perplexity, and RAG-style crawlers instead of scraped-and-mangled.
- **Why now:** AI search traffic is now big enough that sites are starting to optimize for the agent as a first-class visitor, not an afterthought that chokes on nav bars and JS.
- **Own-the-ground angle:** Build it directly into the FareHarbor/WP-VIP theme (or drop a small Cloudflare Worker in front of one site) — nothing rented, nothing that can be revoked.
- **Cheap first test:** Wire this up on one site this week and check server logs / GSC for AI-crawler hits before and after.
- **Fit:** This is a direct extension of the AI-SEO/GEO work already in motion across the 12 businesses — an infrastructure move a marketer wouldn't think to make but an operator running his own WP theme can ship in an afternoon.
- Source: [Hacker News](https://acceptmarkdown.com/) · scores: newly-possible 4/5, momentum 3/5, own-ground 5/5, fit 5/5, run-with-it 5/5

### 2. StemDeck — free local stem separator
- **What changed:** An open-source desktop app (243 HN points) uses Meta's Demucs model to split any audio or video into vocals, drums, bass, guitar, piano, and other — entirely on-device, free, no account.
- **Who it's for:** Anyone editing raw video who needs to deal with background music, David's boat-cam and social-clip pipeline included.
- **Why now:** Stem separation used to mean a paid cloud tool (Moises, LALAL.AI) or a subscription. This is a free, private, local version good enough for practical cleanup work.
- **Own-the-ground angle:** Runs entirely on his own machine. No upload, no vendor, no rug-pull risk.
- **Cheap first test:** Run one clip from the boatcam backlog through it this week and see if it strips unlicensed background music cleanly enough to make a clip postable.
- **Fit:** boatcam is explicitly blocked on a photo/video release right now, but the copyright-music problem on raw boat footage is a separate, real blocker this could chip away at in parallel, at zero cost.
- Source: [GitHub](https://github.com/stemdeckapp/stemdeck) · scores: newly-possible 3/5, momentum 4/5, own-ground 5/5, fit 4/5, run-with-it 5/5

### 3. Sopro V2 Turbo — on-device voice cloning
- **What changed:** A 120M-parameter, Apache-2.0 voice-cloning TTS model clones a voice from a 3-12 second clip and streams live on a laptop CPU or in a browser tab. No GPU, no API bill, SOTA-level intelligibility for its size.
- **Who it's for:** Anyone who wants a consistent narrator/host voice across video, ads, or IVR without paying per-character cloud TTS fees or booking a voice actor.
- **Why now:** Voice cloning has existed for a while, but running it free, local, and in-browser at this quality is new — it moves voice cloning from "cloud service with a bill" to "thing you just run."
- **Own-the-ground angle:** Fully local, open license, nothing that can change pricing or get cut off.
- **Cheap first test:** Clone one host or captain's voice from an existing clip and generate a single test voiceover line for a social post this week.
- **Fit:** Solid but not unique to David's edge — any operator could plug this in. Still a real cost/consistency win across the YouTube channels and ad voiceovers he already runs.
- Source: [GitHub](https://github.com/samuel-vitorino/sopro) (via TLDR AI) · scores: newly-possible 4/5, momentum 3/5, own-ground 5/5, fit 4/5, run-with-it 4/5
