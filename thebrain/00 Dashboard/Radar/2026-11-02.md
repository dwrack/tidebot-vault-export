## Sparks (run with these)

### 1. Mine your own guest corpus into a site, in one evening
- **What changed:** Karpathy took a Hugging Face dump of Hacker News comments, had Claude Code (Opus 5.5) filter it with a Sonnet workflow, and shipped a YouTube-style site of 6,454 videos across 150 topics in about two hours on Saturday. His words: he only talked to Claude. 2,693 HN points. By Monday there were HackerBooks (1,630 pts), Wikihack and HackerHack. The pattern is now a template: take a corpus a community made, let agents sort it, publish it on your own domain.
- **Who it's for:** Anyone sitting on a corpus nobody else has. You have years of reviews across 12 brands, OpenCX sessions full of real guest questions, and guide audio logs.
- **Why now:** Same week, BrightEdge (via SEJ, Oct 31) found big brands and booking platforms own the booking-intent AI answers, but lose on subjective and inspiration questions where first-hand experience gets cited. And Reddit, the main UGC source those answers lean on, is going logged-in-only. First-hand guest content on an owned domain is the opening.
- **Own-the-ground angle:** Your reviews, your chats, your domain. Nothing rented.
- **Cheap first test:** Pull every question and place-name out of the NOLA brands' reviews and OpenCX sessions. One evening with Claude Code: an "answered by our guests" page set on admirenola.com (what guests actually ask about the swamp, the barge, the Quarter), each answer quoting real guests. Watch GSC impressions and AI citations for 30 days. If it works, repeat for Door County before the 2027 season.
- **Fit:** A software founder has to buy or scrape a corpus. You already own one, in the voice AI answers reward.
- Source: [karpathy.bearblog.dev](https://karpathy.bearblog.dev/hacker-tube/), [SEJ/BrightEdge](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/big-brands-dominate-ai-search-but-leave-openings-for-smaller-sites/595397/), 2026-10-31 · scores: newly-possible 4/5, momentum 5/5, own-ground 5/5, fit 4/5, run-with-it 5/5

### 2. Answer the agents that are starting to call your phone lines
- **What changed:** The SF Standard documented Voygr's agents making 848 calls to local businesses during Tech Week, asking prices and availability. About 1 in 3 people kept talking after learning it was an AI. Roughly 1 in 25 businesses already had their own agent pick up. Agents that try to book still get stuck at payment and book last-minute. Two days earlier OpenAI's Retell case study put agent call volume at 100M a month, up from 30M in early 2026.
- **Who it's for:** Any business whose phone is a sales channel. NPB's main line misses 63% of calls (your own memory note).
- **Why now:** The direction flipped. The voice-AI story used to be "a bot answers your customers." Now the customers' bots are the callers, and they hang up on voicemail just like humans do.
- **Own-the-ground angle:** OpenCX phone agents and Twilio numbers are already yours. The MCP can create a phone agent today.
- **Cheap first test:** Put the OpenCX phone agent on the NPB main line for after-hours and missed calls only. One added rule: if the caller says it is an AI agent, give hours, all-in prices and the booking link, then tag the session. Count agent-tagged calls for two weeks. Zero means it's early in New Orleans; a handful means build the answers out.
- **Fit:** You know what callers ask and what a wrong answer costs. Also the first Spark in weeks that reaches you away from the desk: the phone agent runs whether or not you're at a keyboard.
- Source: [SF Standard](https://sfstandard.com/2026/10/29/ai-agents-calling-local-businesses/), [OpenAI/Retell](https://openai.com/index/retell-ai), 2026-10-29 · scores: newly-possible 3/5, momentum 4/5, own-ground 4/5, fit 5/5 (includes the desk-mobile bonus), run-with-it 4/5

### 3. Get a brand into Google's Connected Apps while the travel shelf is empty
- **What changed:** On Oct 28 Google opened the Connected Apps public preview. Any developer can apply. An app is a remote MCP server; Google reviews and tests it, then it runs inside AI Mode in Search and the Gemini app. Over 50 apps are live (Instacart, Canva, Yahoo fantasy). Booking and ordering services can now apply. Viator has been the only travel-experiences app since July. Marie Haynes (Oct 29) told SEOs this matters more than chasing AI citations. John Mueller called it "still extremely early."
- **Who it's for:** Businesses with inventory a user would want to check inside a Google chat. Tours by date are exactly that.
- **Why now:** US English only, waitlist, no pricing announced. That is the window. Clarity's study the same week showed "book appointment" in the top ten actions of agent sessions and not in the human top ten.
- **Own-the-ground angle:** The MCP server is yours and reusable (the same server could serve Claude, ChatGPT apps, or your own site). Google's review and placement are rented. Own-ground scored 3, not 5, for that reason.
- **Cheap first test:** A read-only remote MCP server for NPB on mbp-2 behind Tailscale Funnel: availability by date, all-in price, booking link with a `ref=`. Register it in the Google Connected Console and join the waitlist. No booking writes until Google approves and you've seen real sessions.
- **Fit:** Partial. Any developer can build the server. Only an operator can hand it live inventory and honest prices across 12 brands.
- **Caveat:** Haynes and Mueller both say the barrier is high (cloud server, OAuth, review). If the waitlist sits for a month, park it.
- Source: [SEJ](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/google-connected-apps-preview-ai-mode-gemini/595268/), [Google](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/technology/developers-tools/connected-apps-one-place/), [ppc.land/Haynes](https://ppc.land/marie-haynes-tells-seos-to-build-google-search-apps-instead-of-chasing-geo/), 2026-10-28 · scores: newly-possible 4/5, momentum 4/5, own-ground 3/5, fit 4/5, run-with-it 3/5
