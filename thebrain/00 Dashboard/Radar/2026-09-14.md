### 1. GPT-Live-1 (OpenAI full-duplex voice API)
- **What changed:** Voice AI stops taking turns. GPT-Live-1 listens and talks at the same time, the way a real phone call works, cutting interruptions ~80% versus the old turn-based systems everyone's been shipping. It's live in the API today, not a waitlist: $0.05/min, 12 voices, native transcripts.
- **Who it's for:** Anyone trying to build a phone line that doesn't sound like a robot playing walkie-talkie. That's basically every tour business's after-hours line.
- **Why now:** The turn-taking lag is the single biggest reason voice bots feel fake. That line just got crossed.
- **Own-the-ground angle:** Runs on his own Twilio number, not a rented consumer app. Twilio is the spine he already owns; GPT-Live-1 just becomes the voice on the other end of it.
- **Cheap first test:** Pick one business, wire GPT-Live-1 to one Twilio number for one narrow job (after-hours booking FAQ), and see if a real caller can get an answer without a human picking up.
- **Fit:** This is the desk<->mobile gap made concrete. Right now every ounce of AI leverage he has lives behind a keyboard. A voice agent that can actually hold a call means the phone starts working for him while he's on a boat, not just Slack and email.
