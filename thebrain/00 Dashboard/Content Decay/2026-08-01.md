**Refresh angles:**

Six of these eight show position holding steady or *improving* while clicks collapse. That's the tell: it's not a ranking problem, it's AI Overviews and SERP features eating the click before the visitor ever hits your site. The refresh has to target getting cited/extracted, not just ranking higher. Two pages (kayak-truck, Fish Creek) show real position drops and need different treatment.

- **Cave Point County Park** (pos improved 14.2→9.2, clicks -69%): This query is catnip for AI Overviews (hours, parking, is it free, best time to go). Put a tight, extractable FAQ block at the top answering those exact questions in plain sentences, add FAQ schema, and add a current-year "parking fills up by X am" tip that only an operator on the ground would know. Google can lift a canned answer from a Wikipedia-style page; it can't fake "we watch the lot fill up every July Saturday."

- **Homepage** (pos improved 20.1→9.3, clicks -28%): Check GSC query-level data before touching copy, branded queries are likely being absorbed by your Google Business Profile knowledge panel now that you rank better. Retarget the homepage hard at non-branded commercial intent ("Door County kayak tours," "book a kayak tour Door County") in the title and H1, and check whether a specific tour page or the Cave Point page is now cannibalizing homepage clicks for the same terms, since both improved in position around the same window.

- **Ephraim blog post** (pos flat 10.5, clicks -93%): Flat position with a 93% click collapse means this generic listicle got replaced in the click stream by an AI Overview or a fresher competitor snippet, not a ranking loss. Rewrite it as a 2026-dated guide with specific, bookable recommendations (named restaurants, named lodging, actual walking distances to your launch point) instead of generic "reasons," it needs proprietary detail an LLM summary can't reproduce. Also check it isn't cannibalizing against the Fish Creek post for the same "best town to stay" query.

- **How far can a kayak hang out of a truck** (pos worsened 2.9→4.9, clicks -71%): This is a real ranking loss on a question-format query that's an AI Overview magnet. Add a state-by-state legal overhang table with current-year DOT citations, a direct one-sentence answer in the first 100 words, and a diagram or photo showing proper flag/tie-down setup. You want to be the source AIO cites, and beefing up the E-E-A-T signal (legal citations, your own photos) is what gets you re-cited even if you can't win back position 2.9.

- **Fish Creek blog post** (pos 7.9→8.8, clicks -40%): Same fix as Ephraim: update to 2026, differentiate it clearly from the Ephraim post so they're not competing against each other, and swap generic "reasons" framing for specific itinerary content tied to your actual tour departure point in Fish Creek.

Add Product schema with live pricing and fleet photos so you're eligible for a rich result (price/rating stars) in the SERP, and rewrite the title tag to lead with price or a differentiator ("guided vs. self-guided") since a plain "E-Bike Rental" title is easy to skip past now.
