# TLDR Watchdog: 2026-11-03

Read 6 editions: Marketing, General, Product, Founders, Design, Hardware. Two items cleared the bar.

## 1. ChatGPT ads earn about a third of Google's per-visit value
- **Finding:** Paid ChatGPT visitors earned $0.89 per session vs $2.45 from Google Search and converted at under a third of the rate. Ads also cut organic referrals 6 to 8%.
- **Source:** TLDR Marketing 11/03, https://ecommerceresearch.org/chatgpt-ads/
- **Why it matters:** The AEO plan treats AI-chat visibility as a booking lever. This is ad-side data, not organic citations, so it does not kill the plan, but it argues against shifting budget from Maps and search toward AI chat.
- **First move:** Keep the AEO page batch as planned. Do not move paid budget. Revisit when we have an organic-citation number.

## 2. Reddit referral traffic falling
- **Finding:** Reddit referrals are down more than 1M visits a week as AI Overviews cut clicks.
- **Source:** TLDR Marketing 11/03, https://www.marketingdive.com/news/what-reddits-q3-earnings-mean-for-marketers-amid-major-traffic-shift/873386/
- **Why it matters:** Any Reddit-led content plan can't assume referral traffic holds. NKST GA4 showed reddit.com at 2 sessions and 6 conversions on 11/02, so it converts even at low volume.
- **First move:** Pull Reddit referral sessions and conversions in GA4 for NKST over 30 days before adding Reddit work.
