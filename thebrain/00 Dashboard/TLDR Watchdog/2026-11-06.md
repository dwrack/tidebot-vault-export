## 1. Reddit is testing a logged-in-only experience (contradicts a plan)

- Source: Standing On Giants, via TLDR Marketing 11/06, https://www.standingongiants.com/blog/eddit-logged-in-only-discord-search/ (5 min). Discord is going the other way and making public communities Google-searchable.
- Numbers: Reddit revenue up 53%, US user growth slowing. The test is framed as cutting unmonetized traffic and AI scraping.
- What it contradicts: `AEO Action Plan - All Sites.md` keeps Reddit "for its Google footprint (65M page-one keywords)". A login wall shrinks that footprint for Google and for AI crawlers. The reddit-miner skill and the Reddit warming track (back on 11/06) both assume public, crawlable threads.
- Why it matters: if Google and the AI engines lose access to Reddit threads, Reddit mentions stop feeding search and AI answers. Wednesday's Ahrefs item (mentions correlate 0.75 with AI visibility for Reddit) was the case FOR Reddit; this is the case for not over-investing yet.
- First move: keep the warming track (no spend). Do not add posting hours until the test's reach is known. Watch GSC referrals from reddit.com monthly. A dated caveat was added to the AEO plan today.
- Caveat: TLDR Marketing also ran Reddit's own "2027 Moments" vendor guide today (3 in 4 users consider a new brand after a community recommendation). Vendor claims; treat as marketing.

## 2. Gmail spam-complaint threshold (cheap first move)

- Source: AudienceBridge, via TLDR Marketing 11/06, https://www.audiencebridge.io/p/we-pushed-acquisition-too-hard-complaints-followed (5 min).
- Numbers: complaint rate above Google's 0.30% line for 4 days, peaking at 0.49%. Halving new-subscriber volume dropped it to 0.06%.
- Why it matters: the NPB newsletter funnel (live since 10/22) and the NPB/HPB win-back sends go out in bulk from the business domains through SES. A complaint spike would hurt transactional mail on the same domain. No vault doc covers Postmaster Tools.
- First move: add nolapartybarges.com and houstonpedalbarge.com (and any other SES sending domain) to Google Postmaster Tools. Check the compliance dashboard before each bulk send; throttle if the rate nears 0.30%.

## 3. AI pre-flight audit of ad landing pages

- Source: Practical Ecommerce, via TLDR Marketing 11/06, https://www.practicalecommerce.com/find-site-errors-with-ai-before-ads-run (2 min).
- What: use an LLM plus Lighthouse and Screaming Frog to catch broken links, conflicting offers, wrong availability and mobile issues before a campaign runs.
- First move: run it on the NPB and DCKT FareHarbor landing pages before the Black Friday and holiday ad pushes. Pairs with the Website Standards doc (all-in prices, ref= on every FH link).
