## 1. AI search answers cite creators, not brand content

**Source:** TLDR Marketing, "How to Win AI Search With Creators" (6 min read) — https://later-com.s3.us-east-1.amazonaws.com/download/Marketer%27s+Guide-+How+to+Win+AI+Search+with+Creators.pdf

**The finding:** Less than 10% of what AI answers say about a brand comes from the brand's own content. 1 in 3 AI answers cite a creator directly. Follower count barely matters — 57% of cited YouTube videos come from channels under 100K subscribers. The playbook: combine SEO keyword data with real customer questions to build a shared prompt list, pick creators for authority and specificity rather than follower count, and repost content stuck behind Instagram/TikTok login walls to YouTube as transcripts so AI crawlers can actually reach it.

**Why it matters here:** This is a lever on search visibility, specifically the AEO/GEO angle the vault's AEO Action Plan already cares about. Right now the portfolio's video content lives almost entirely on Instagram and TikTok — both are behind login walls that AI crawlers generally can't index. None of it exists on YouTube as transcript-accessible content. HPB's TikTok content in particular (once the Playwright pull is working again) and NPB's top-performing Reels are candidates.

**First move:** Pick 2-3 of the highest-performing existing IG/TikTok clips and repost them to YouTube (even unlisted/low-production is fine — the goal is transcript accessibility, not view count) as a test. Check GSC/AI-referral traffic in a few weeks to see if it moves anything.

## 2. Google Veo turns static photos into video ads for PMax and Demand Gen

**Source:** TLDR Marketing, "How to Use Google Veo to Create Video Ads for PMax and Demand Gen" (3 min read) — https://searchengineland.com/google-veo-video-ads-pmax-demand-gen-490571

**The finding:** Google Veo animates two existing static images into a 5 or 10 second video ad for Performance Max and Demand Gen campaigns, supporting 16:9, 9:16, and 1:1 formats. High-resolution product images matter more than anything else. The two 30-character headlines should focus on message, not branding (the business name already appears first). Recommended as a low-cost way to refresh ad creative and test lift on clicks and engagement.

**Why it matters here:** Two of the Google Ads accounts flagged in today's brief are underperforming on creative fatigue signals — LSKT (CPA spiked 2.66x yesterday) and HPB (dropped to $0 spend, weakest account in the portfolio per the standing goals note). A cheap creative refresh is a reasonable first lever to test before assuming it's a targeting or budget problem.

**First move:** Pull 2-3 existing hero images from the LSKT or HPB asset folders, run them through Veo, and swap into the PMax campaign as a creative test. Low cost, quick to evaluate.
