# TLDR Watchdog — Wed Sep 2, 2026

Three editions landed this morning. Two things are worth a change of plan. The rest is noise for a tour operator.

---

## 1. ChatGPT and Google are cutting citations to listicle/comparison pages in half. Our AEO plan bets on exactly that format.

**Source:** TLDR Marketing, The State of Brand (3 min read) — https://www.thestateofbrand.com/news/chatgpt-google-listicle-citations-collapse
ChatGPT citations for listicles fell 50.5%, from 15.77% to 7.80%, after its August 6 update, while product pages rose to 16.39% of retrieved pages. AI visibility now favors pages with real specs and pricing on the vendor's own domain over pages built mainly to match "best," "top," or "vs" queries.

**Why it matters here:** [[AEO Action Plan - All Sites]] Priority 5 is built entirely on comparison content — "Comparison content gets cited ~33% of the time in AI answers, more than any other format" — and there's a standing open task, "Build 'Kayak vs Motorboat' comparison page on NKST." That's the exact page type (and the exact site) this data says is losing ground. It's also the same page the roster is already watching (`airboat-vs-kayak-swamp-tour-new-orleans`), which has moved from 0 clicks at baseline to 2 clicks / 33 impressions over the last 8 days — real but small, and now questionable as a scaling bet.

**My read:** don't tear down what's built — [[AI Overviews Stability Study — What It Means For Our AEO (June 2026)]] already argues (correctly) that the durable inputs are Reddit presence, named-guide bios, plain verifiable 101 content, and YouTube coverage, not the page format itself. This new data is a second, independent signal pointing the same direction: format matters less than source material. The comparison pages aren't wasted, but they shouldn't be where new effort goes next.

**First move:** hold the existing comparison pages as-is, but redirect the next round of AEO content work (including the queued NKST "Kayak vs Motorboat" page) toward beefing up the tour/product pages themselves — specs, pricing, named guides, FAQ schema — before building another comparison page. Add a line to the AEO Action Plan noting this data point next to the existing AIO-stability caveat.

## 2. Google is expanding AI Overviews with a default "Ask anything" box, pushing organic results further down.

**Source:** TLDR Marketing, Search Engine Roundtable (2 min read) — https://www.seroundtable.com/google-ai-overviews-push-ai-mode-responses-41974.html
Google is showing a fuller AI answer and the "Ask anything" box by default for some queries now, instead of requiring a "Show more" click. This pushes traditional organic results further down the page and steers more searches toward AI Mode.

**Why it matters here:** every GSC-tracked site (8 of them) lives or dies on organic clicks below that fold. This doesn't change the plan — the June AIO study already told us not to chase the citation itself — but it's one more data point that the organic real-estate squeeze is getting tighter, not looser, which raises the stakes on the inputs (Reddit, YouTube, named guides) actually landing.

**My read:** no plan change. Just context for why DCKT sitting at #8.7 on its own brand term, or NKST at #23.7, matters more each month, not less.

**First move:** none needed beyond what's already in motion. Worth a glance at GSC "Show more" click-through rates next month if Google keeps expanding this.
