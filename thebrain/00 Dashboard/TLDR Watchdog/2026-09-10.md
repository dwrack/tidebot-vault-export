## 1. Entities are the foundation of modern SEO

**Source:** leadershipinseo.com, 10 min read — https://www.leadershipinseo.com/p/entities-are-the-foundation-of-modern (via TLDR Marketing, 2026-09-10)

**What it says:** Entity SEO — making a brand's name, people, products, and relationships legible and consistent to both Google and AI systems — is now foundational rather than a nice-to-have. The recommended process: audit owned properties first so names, people, products, and structured data line up everywhere they appear, then build credibility through third-party mentions, expert commentary, and evidence that backs up what the brand claims to be known for. An entity map can surface the gap between how a brand thinks it's positioned and how search data and the wider web actually connect it to people, products, and topics.

**Why it matters here:** This is squarely AEO territory but it's a gap `AEO Action Plan - All Sites.md` doesn't currently cover — the plan focuses on FAQ schema, quotable content, and comparison pages, not entity/NAP consistency. Across 13 businesses sharing crew, boats, and sometimes marketing copy (Gravity Trails NOLA runs 4 of them under one ad account), there's real risk of exactly the kind of inconsistency this piece flags — a guide named one way on GBP and another on the site, a business description that drifts between the website and third-party directories. The businesses with the most organic weight to protect are DCKT, NPB, and NKST (today's top GSC performers by clicks).

**First move:** Run an entity consistency pass on DCKT, NPB, and NKST first — compare business name, address, phone, and key person names (guides quoted in reviews, like DCKT's Jackson or NKST's Alex) across GBP, the website's schema markup, and any third-party listings. Flag mismatches before expanding the audit to the other 10 businesses.

## 2. Google's "People Also Ask" answers are now nearly 100% AI-generated

**Source:** searchenginewatch.com, 2 min read — https://searchenginewatch.com/google-people-also-ask-answers-are-now-nearly-100-ai-generated/ (via TLDR Marketing, 2026-09-10)

**What it says:** The short text snippets that used to populate Google's "People Also Ask" boxes have been replaced by AI-written answers in 97% of cases — a jump from the old system of pulling a sentence out of an existing page.

**Why it matters here:** `AEO Action Plan - All Sites.md` already treats FAQ schema and quotable content as the top priority fix (per the Sept 9 workup, 7 sites have FAQ content but no FAQ schema). PAA boxes are one of the concrete surfaces that plan is trying to win, and this confirms the box is now entirely an AI synthesis job rather than a snippet-extraction job — meaning structured, clearly-attributable answer content matters more for winning that slot, not less. This doesn't contradict the plan; it raises the stakes on executing it.

**First move:** Of the 7 sites the AEO plan flagged as missing FAQ schema, prioritize DCKT and NKST first — they're this brief's top two organic earners (33 and multiple double-digit query clicks respectively) and have the most to gain if PAA-box visibility converts to clicks.

## 3. Is the like button dead?

**Source:** futuresocial.beehiiv.com, 3 min read — https://futuresocial.beehiiv.com/p/is-the-like-button-dead (via TLDR Marketing, 2026-09-10)

**What it says:** Likes and comments are dropping hard across social platforms — Instagram likes are down 73% on carousels and 81% on Reels. Platforms are leaning on watch time, completion rate, replays, and dwell time to rank content instead. Engagement metrics (likes/comments) are becoming noisy; retention and strong hooks matter more than ever.

**Why it matters here:** HPB's TikTok strategy (per the account context in this skill) is already built around 7-10 second hook-driven clips — the format this shift rewards. But if anyone on the team is still judging a post's success by like/comment counts, that read is now unreliable across the board, not just on TikTok. This is a measurement problem more than a content problem: the content formula doesn't need to change, but what "working" means does.

**First move:** Once Playwright/TikTok access is restored (blocked this run — see Coverage Gaps), re-baseline what counts as a "good" HPB post using completion rate and rewatches instead of likes/comments before drawing conclusions from any week-over-week comparison.
