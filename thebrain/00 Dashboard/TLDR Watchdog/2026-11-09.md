## 1. YouTube description rewrites tied to a 359% rise in AI-search retrievals

**Source:** Search Engine Land, "YouTube description updates linked to 359% rise in AI retrievals" (1 min read). https://searchengineland.com/youtube-description-updates-ai-retrievals-study-500845

**What it says:** Peec AI ran a test on 15 of about 60 videos for one client, rewriting descriptions to be more retrieval-friendly. AI Overviews retrievals rose 293%, AI Mode retrievals rose 129%, blended to a 359% rise across the test set.

**Why it matters here:** Every tour brand with a YouTube channel (DCKT, NPB, NKST, HPB, and sibling brands under those umbrellas, plus Ebb & Ember) has descriptions that were written for humans skimming, not for an LLM trying to extract what the video is about. This is a near-zero-cost lever directly on AI search visibility — the exact kind of "AI Overviews / AI Mode" surface the vault's AEO work already targets.

**First move:** Pick the top 5 videos by views on DCKT's and NPB's channels. Rewrite each description with a clear keyword-rich summary paragraph up top (what the tour is, where, who it's for) plus timestamps. Measure citation/retrieval change informally over the next few weeks — there's no clean "AI retrieval rate" metric available to us yet, so this is directional, not measured.

## 2. "AI knows what your brand does but not who it's for"

**Source:** Search Engine Land (15 min). https://searchengineland.com/ai-knows-what-your-brand-does-but-not-who-its-for-500556

**What it says:** Across the queries tested, AI named the ideal customer for a brand correctly only 60% of the time. 34% of AI answers about a brand contained an invented fact — most often a wrong price.

**Why it matters here:** This is a direct stress test of `project_ai_citation_loop.md`, which per memory has never actually produced a working check despite being a standing project. Wrong AI-stated prices are a real risk for David's brands — a prospective customer who asks ChatGPT "how much is a NOLA Party Barge tour" and gets a wrong number either books expecting the wrong price (support headache) or doesn't book at all (invented price too high).

**First move:** Manually run "who is [brand] for" and "how much does [brand] cost" through ChatGPT and Gemini for 2-3 flagship brands (NPB, DCKT, NKST) and log exactly what's wrong. This is a 15-minute manual check, not a new build — the AI-citation loop project has been blocked on tooling; this doesn't need tooling.

## 3. Reddit's organic influence keeps growing, not shrinking

**Source:** Siege Media, "Why Reddit's influence isn't going down" (8 min). https://www.siegemedia.com/strategy/reddits-influence

**What it says:** Reddit organic search visits went from 46M (Nov 2021) to 495M (Oct 2026) — roughly 10x. Across 58 clients studied, a median of 9% of LLM answers cited Reddit as a source.

**Why it matters here:** This directly reinforces the already-built `reddit-miner` skill (turns Reddit's search footprint into a content roadmap). The data says Reddit isn't a secondary or declining channel to mine for content gaps — it's getting more load-bearing for both traditional search and AI answers.

**First move:** Run `reddit-miner` on the next content-gap sweep for one of the New Orleans brands instead of treating it as optional. No new build needed — just prioritize using what already exists.
