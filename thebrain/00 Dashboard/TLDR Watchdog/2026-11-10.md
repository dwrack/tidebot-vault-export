## 1. "85% of AEO is won offsite" (contradicts part of a written plan)

- **Source:** GTM Strategist (Maja Voje, Josh Grant), 11/6, via TLDR Founders 11/9. https://knowledge.gtmstrategist.com/p/offsite-aeo-playbook
- **Finding:** citing AirOps' 2026 State of AI Search, only about 13% of AI-answer influence comes from on-site work (FAQ, H1s, schema). The rest happens off your own domain. Creators are called the underpriced asset. At Webflow, traffic from AI assistants converted at 24% vs 4% for non-brand Google.
- **Where it cuts against the plan:** `TheBrain/AEO Action Plan - All Sites.md`.
  - Lines 334-352 and 522-525 put Review and AggregateRating schema on every site as Priority 6.
  - Lines 483-488 define off-site as only Reddit and YouTube, and line 486 already downgraded Reddit on 8/28.
  - So the plan is on-site heavy, and its off-site section has no creator or third-party mention lever at all.
- **Caveat:** the data comes from B2B software. The creator point is directionally useful, not proven for kayak tours or party boats.
- **Brands:** NKST, DCKT, NPB.
- **First move:** add a list of 3 to 5 local creators and travel bloggers per city to the plan's off-site section (half an hour). Hold the schema priority until the baseline AI citation check on plan line 525 has actually run. It never has.

## 2. Yahoo's 25 ad experiments: most lift tests can't tell profit from loss

- **Source:** Karan Dhir, via TLDR Marketing 11/10. https://karandhir.substack.com/p/yahoos-economists-ran-25-ad-experiments
- **Finding:** across 25 controlled ad experiments the median ROI confidence interval was wider than 100 percentage points.
- **Why it matters:** the NPB brand-pause test (`03 Projects/Active/NOLA Brand-Pause Test - Interim Read (Sep 19 2026).md`) is the closest thing to a lift test in the vault. Not checked whether that read reports a confidence interval.
- **First move:** treat that test's result as directional, not proof. Before Michael runs the next holdout, set a minimum detectable effect and put the interval next to the lift number.
