## 1. Performance Max and Advantage+ "grade their own homework"

**Source:** "Performance Max and Advantage+ grade their own homework" (7 min read, Optmyzr via Karan Dhir's newsletter), via TLDR Marketing 2026-10-08. [Link](https://karandhir.substack.com/p/performance-max-and-advantage-grade?utm_source=tldrmarketing)

**The numbers:** Optmyzr studied 503 Google Ads accounts and found Performance Max campaigns overlapped with existing Search keywords in 91% of them. Because Meta Advantage+ and Google Performance Max control their own buying, attribution, and reporting, platform-reported ROAS can count conversions that would have happened anyway through other channels — brand searches, existing customers, retargeting that was already working. The recommendation: treat platform ROAS as an optimization signal, not proof of incremental impact, and validate with randomized incrementality tests, holdouts, or finance reconciliation.

**Why it matters here:** Every Google Ads account in the portfolio — DCKT (8672151991), LSKT/AKT shared (8497383499), HPB (2986009791), and the Gravity Trails NOLA umbrella (4378232023, covering NKST/Bike Bar/Admire NOLA/NPB) — gets graded in this brief purely on platform-reported CPA and ROAS. Action Item #2 in today's brief flags Gravity Trails' $464.86 spend yesterday against a 7-day CPA of $163.75, and that number has no independent check behind it. If any of these campaigns run as Performance Max rather than standard Search, the "bad days" (zero conversions) and "good days" (healthy ROAS) could both be noisier than they look, because PMax's own brand-term overlap can be masking the real incremental lift.

**First move:** Pull `ads_get_campaigns` for the Gravity Trails umbrella (4378232023) and check campaign type — if any are Performance Max, flag which ones before treating the daily CPA swings as a real performance signal rather than attribution noise. This doesn't require a new tool, just a type filter on a call the brief already has access to.

## 3. How to plan 2027 when traffic is down and AI won't attribute

**Source:** "How to plan 2027 when traffic is down and AI won't attribute" (5 min read, Growth Memo), via TLDR Marketing 2026-10-08. [Link](https://www.growth-memo.com/p/how-to-plan-2027-when-traffic-is?utm_source=tldrmarketing)

**The framework:** Build the 2027 marketing plan around AI visibility, brand mentions, community participation, and branded search — signals that happen before a website visit — rather than last-click attribution. Identify which AI engines, communities, and publications influence buyers in your category, budget specific hours and owners for off-site work, measure exposure/branded-search/outcomes together monthly since no single metric captures influence, and reallocate budget each quarter based on what the three-layer measurement shows.

**Why it matters here:** This doesn't contradict anything in the vault — it reinforces the AEO Action Plan's existing direction and echoes yesterday's "AI Overviews are measurably cutting organic clicks" item (also from TLDR Marketing). Two days running, the newsletter is converging on the same point: last-click GSC/GA4 numbers alone are an increasingly incomplete read on what's driving bookings.

**First move:** None beyond what the AEO plan already calls for. Flagging as a second independent confirmation that the plan's direction is right, not a reason to expand scope.
