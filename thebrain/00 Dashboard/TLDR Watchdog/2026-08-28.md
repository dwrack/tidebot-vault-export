# TLDR Watchdog — Fri Aug 28, 2026

Five editions landed this morning. Four things in here are worth money or a change of plan. The rest is noise for a tour operator.

---

## 1. Reddit's ChatGPT citations fell from 3.8% to 0.5%. Our AEO plan is built on Reddit.

**Source:** TLDR Marketing, Semrush (2 min read) — https://www.semrush.com/blog/reddits-citations-in-chatgpt-fall/
The collapse tracks an Aug 8 change to how ChatGPT fans out search queries. Same edition, separate piece: YouTube and Reddit still dominate AI citations overall, Instagram is "nearly invisible" to answer engines.

**Why it matters here:** [[AEO Action Plan - All Sites]] line 485 names Reddit presence as a core AEO input, and we have a live posting machine behind it (`Reddit Outreach/pending.md`, 131 lines queued, plus a warm/auto queue). If part of the case for that machine was "get cited in ChatGPT," that case just lost most of its value in three weeks.

**My read:** don't kill it. Reddit still ranks for 65M page-one Google keywords and feeds AI Overviews, and the warm-up replies build the account we need anyway. But stop scoring it as an AI-answer play and move that weight to YouTube, which held.

**First move:** reweight the AEO plan's channel table so YouTube outranks Reddit for AI-citation intent, and keep Reddit scored on Google rankings and referral bookings only.

## 2. Mine Reddit instead of only posting to it.

**Source:** TLDR Marketing, Search Engine Land (6 min read) — https://searchengineland.com/reddit-keyword-research-tool-485859
Reddit ranks for ~166M keywords, 65M on page one, and is cited in 15M+ AI Overview queries. The play is to treat Reddit's search footprint as a keyword dataset: pull its non-brand rankings, AI Overview rankings, and keyword gaps to find pages that need optimizing, topics with no page yet, and real customer questions for FAQ schema.

**Why it matters here:** this is the highest-ROI item on the page. We have 12 sites on the same FareHarbor theme, a Schema Markup field on every page, and a content-gap process that currently starts from competitors. Starting from Reddit's rankings instead gives us questions Google has already decided are valuable, in the exact wording travelers use. "Is Door County worth it in June," "bachelorette ideas New Orleans not a bar crawl," that kind of thing.

**First move:** run one Semrush keyword-gap pass of reddit.com against doorcountykayaktours.com and nolapartybarge.com, pull the top 50 non-brand queries where Reddit ranks page one and we don't rank at all, and feed them straight into /seo-audit and the FAQ schema fields.

## 3. YouTube awareness spend takes about two months to show up. We probably kill it too early.

**Source:** TLDR Marketing, Haus study on TextNow (4 min read) — https://www.haus.io/customer/textnow
26-week YouTube test. First five weeks showed almost no cumulative lift. 87% of the total measured effect accumulated after the first two months. Ended at 1.99% total lift, and including a six-week post-treatment window improved measured efficiency by about 8%.

**Why it matters here:** every one of these businesses is seasonal, and the instinct is to turn awareness on when the season opens. If 87% of the lift lands after month two, spending that starts in May for a Door County summer is spending we will read as a failure and shut off in June, right before it starts working.

**First move:** for the 2027 season, awareness budget starts 8 to 10 weeks before the booking window opens, and we agree up front not to judge it on a 30-day read. Write the evaluation window into the plan before the money goes out, otherwise we will flinch.

## 4. We have nine YouTube channels wired up and mostly idle.

Combine items 1 and 3 with what the AEO plan already specs: the 30-second guide intro videos (line 41, line 390) are currently aimed at Reels and TikTok first. Answer engines cannot read those. YouTube is where the citations are, and we already have MCP access to npb, nkst, dckt, akt, bbk, hpb, ebb, admirenola, and dcdetours.

**First move:** the guide intro videos ship to YouTube Shorts first, with real titles and descriptions written for search, then get cut down for IG. Same asset, better order of operations.

## 5. Cut the fake personalization from creator outreach.

**Source:** TLDR Marketing (2 min read) — https://www.linkedin.com/posts/phil-ledgerwood_marketing-share-7498020405207707648-PX5h
Scraped details, local references, and AI-generated compliments read worse than a straight pitch. If you cannot personalize well at scale, leave it out and send something relevant instead.

**Why it matters here:** the `Creator Outreach/` pipeline is exactly where that opener creeps in. Worth a pass on the templates.

## 6. Two signals that back the human-pass rule

- **10% of webpages now show signs of AI authorship**, over a third of pages published since ChatGPT launched, and the tells are getting easier to spot. Strongest on .com. (Pew — https://www.pewresearch.org/data-labs/2026/08/20/how-much-of-the-internet-is-written-with-ai/)
- **"Shallow intent"** (16 min, Tomas Pueyo — https://unchartedterritories.tomaspueyo.com/p/shallow-intent-why-people-dont-like): people read polish as a signal of effort, so polished-but-thoughtless content reads as a betrayal. The fix is not less AI, it is a deliberate human pass before shipping.

Nothing to change, this is just the argument for why /content-gate exists. Worth keeping in mind that 12 sites on one theme with heavy generated content is exactly the footprint a detector loves.

## 7. Worth reading, not acting on yet

- **Don't price per token, charge for outcomes** (a16z, 5 min — https://www.a16z.news/p/you-are-not-a-model-dont-price-per). Relevant only if the AI ops stack ever becomes the second product in [[ACQ $100M Scaling Roadmap — Stage 5 Productize]].
- **Audit your agent files** (Addy Osmani, 16 min — https://addyo.substack.com/p/audit-your-agent-files). Agent configs have a half-life. Our skill and CLAUDE.md stack is large and mostly append-only. Running /doctor and forcing each instruction to re-earn its spot is real maintenance we have never done.
- **Cohere Parse, $1.50 per 1,000 pages** (https://cohere.com/blog/parse). Document extraction at volume. Possible fit for waivers, invoices, and 12 separate QBO files, but not urgent.
- **Chinese models at $0.14/M tokens vs OpenAI's $5.00** (TLDR Founders). A real cost lever for high-volume low-stakes jobs like captions and first-pass drafts. I would not put customer data through them.
- **Gen Z killed millennial minimalism** (https://thebrandblueprint.substack.com/p/how-gen-z-killed-millennial-minimalism) plus the punk-rock animation piece in TLDR Design. Both point the same way: loud, rough, visibly handmade is winning against clean and polished, partly as a reaction to AI slickness. That is a creative direction note for Ebb & Ember packaging and for thumbnails across the board. Our stuff should look shot on a boat, not rendered.
