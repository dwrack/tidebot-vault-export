## 1. ChatGPT clicks now show up as Direct

**Finding.** Since GPT-6, ChatGPT opens outbound links with no UTM tag and no referrer. Analytics tools file those visits as direct traffic. Source: Search Engine Roundtable, https://www.seroundtable.com/utm-parameters-missing-chatgpt-42299.html (via TLDR Marketing 10/15).

**Why it matters.** The [[AEO Action Plan - All Sites]] measurement section already says to score on bookings and branded search, not on citations, so nothing in the plan breaks. What breaks is any attempt to answer "is AEO sending people?" from GA4. NKST, the only brand with GA4 in the roster, logged 38 direct sessions yesterday out of ~165. Some of that is now ChatGPT and there's no way to split it out. Agentic commerce stat in the same issue: AI is involved in 11.4% of US path-to-purchase volume, so this share isn't going to shrink.

**First move.** Add "ChatGPT / AI assistant" as an option on the FareHarbor "how did you hear about us" custom field for NKST and NPB. Self-reported, imperfect, but it's the only signal left. Revisit in 30 days.

## 2. Google AI Mode as a free content-gap list

**Finding.** Search a service plus a city in Google AI Mode and read the follow-up questions it suggests. Those are the questions Google thinks matter when matching customers to local businesses. Answer them on the service page with detail only the owner has (pricing logic, process, what actually happens). Source: Darren Shaw / Whitespark on LinkedIn, https://www.linkedin.com/posts/darrenshawwhitespark_this-ai-mode-tip-from-claudia-tomina-is-awesome-share-7515787729448636416-5ms3/

**Why it matters.** It's a cheaper version of the reddit-miner idea: the questions come straight from Google instead of being inferred. NKST's /faq/ got 50 views last week at ~16 minutes average time on page, so people do read it.

**First move.** Run "kayak swamp tour new orleans" and "party barge new orleans" in AI Mode. Log the follow-up questions. Diff against NKST /faq/ and the NPB tour page. Add answers for any gaps. About 20 minutes per brand.
