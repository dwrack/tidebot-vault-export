## 1. Question-query mining in Search Console
- Source: TLDR Marketing 11/12, https://www.linkedin.com/posts/oleksiisidorov_embarrassingly-simple-seo-hack-go-to-google-share-7525872829980893184-E4Et/
- Method: in GSC, filter queries by regex for 8+ words, sort by impressions, find high-impression low-click questions, answer each near the top of the page that ranks.
- Our data today (GSC 11/4 to 11/11): NKST "are there hippos in new orleans" 420 impressions, 2 clicks, position 8.2. NKST "new orleans kayak swamp tours" 76 impressions, 5 clicks, position 14.0.
- First move: add a two-sentence direct answer to the hippo question near the top of the ranking NKST page, then pull 8+ word queries for NPB and NKST and do the top 5 each.
