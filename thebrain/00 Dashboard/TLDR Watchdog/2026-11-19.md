## 1. AI recommendations mostly repeat "best of" lists (contradicts the AEO plan's weighting)

**Finding.** Supercite's study: 90% of brands an AI assistant recommended appeared on at least one cited "best/top X" list. Brands on 3+ cited lists got recommended 44% of the time, brands on none 2.5%. In some categories the top 10 list publishers took up to 90% of citations. 83% of Gemini's cited pages had "2026" in the title or URL. Source: https://supercite.ai/blog/ai-recommendations-come-from-lists

**Why it matters.** `AEO Action Plan - All Sites.md` names the inputs to optimize as Reddit presence, named-expert bios, 101 content with FAQ schema, and YouTube (2026-06-23 note). Third-party lists get one line at the bottom ("Pitch 'best of' roundups at local publications"). If this study holds, list placement is the biggest single input and the plan has it last. Single study from a vendor that sells list tracking, so treat the 44% vs 2.5% as directional.

**First move.** For NPB and NKST, Google "best party boat new orleans" / "best swamp tour new orleans" / "things to do new orleans 2026", list the top 10 listicles, and mark which ones mention us. Pitch the 3 that rank highest and don't. Then move "best of" roundups up into the plan's input list.

## 2. Booking page test: grouped, compact cards with price and duration lifted conversions 13%

**Finding.** Churnet Valley Railway grouped 8 dining experiences into categories, put the popular ones first, used compact cards showing price, time and duration, and changed the button from "Book Now" to "Check Running Times". Conversions rose 13%, more for first-time visitors. Source: https://conversion-rate-experts.com/churnet-valley-railway-experiences-redesign-win-report/

**Why it matters.** Same shape as our tour pages. It backs the Website Standards rules (best seller on top, visible all-in price) and adds two things we don't do: duration on the card, and a softer "check availability" button.

**First move.** Put it in the new NPB site brief (Jeff draft v2, already on the dashboard): compact cards with price, duration and group size, best seller first, and test "Check Dates" against "Book Now".

## 3. Meta: simple still images outlast creator video

**Finding.** Of the 34 longest-running Meta ads across 48 DTC brands, 24 were simple still images. Creator videos ran a median 38 days and were among the first switched off. Strong batches mix 4+ formats and 3+ angles, with stills built to run for months. Source: https://links.tldrnewsletter.com/ksrZI8

**Why it matters.** NKST has 7 static ads staged and waiting (dashboard item due today). NPB's Gator Collision video campaign is at ROAS 0.16. DTC, not tours, so it doesn't transfer one for one, but it points the same way.

**First move.** Approve the NKST static ads upload (they go in paused). When the NPB Gator money moves, test 2-3 stills next to Retargeting rather than another video.
