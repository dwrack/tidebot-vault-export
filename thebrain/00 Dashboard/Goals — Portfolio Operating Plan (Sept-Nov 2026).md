You have 13 brands and 4 captains, one of whom (Connor) is running three businesses at once. Two businesses have **no captain at all** (NKST, DCKT). Bella left Aug 5 and TideBot's roster still lists her.

## How you actually hit these with your teams

The mechanism already exists. It is just missing the goal layer.

### 1. Fill the seats (Sept 15 deadline)
| Seat | Status | Action |
|---|---|---|
| NKST captain | Open | Hire or promote. Highest priority. |
| DCKT captain | Open since Bella left Aug 5 | Hire for next season now, not in April. |
| BBK first mate | Open | Connor cannot cover three businesses alone. |
| AKT first mate | Open | Depends on the AKT live/front-door decision. |
| TideBot roster | Still lists Bella | Clean up with the rest of offboarding. |

Connor being captain of HPB, BBK, and AKT simultaneously is the quiet risk nobody has named. Either give him a first mate at BBK or accept that two of those three will drift.

### 2. Wire the goal into the Monday beat
TideBot already asks each captain on Monday 8:00 CT for "one number they're moving this week." Right now that number is invented fresh each week, so it moves nothing.

**Change: the Monday number must be a stated fraction of the 90-day number above.** The prompt should show the quarter goal, the pace against it, and then ask for the week's slice. Same script, one added line, and suddenly twelve weeks of Monday posts add up to something.

### 3. Put the goal in the tile so the brief grades it automatically
Each file in `00 Dashboard/Business Tiles/` already has YAML frontmatter the daily brief reads. Add two fields:

```yaml
goal_q4_metric: weekly_booked_revenue
goal_q4_target: 30000
```

Then the morning brief can grade every business against its own goal without anyone maintaining a spreadsheet. This is the highest-leverage automation on the list, because it makes the goals impossible to quietly forget.

### 4. Monthly 1-on-1, using what already exists
The Manager Development Program already specifies "their three 90-day goals" in the manager 1-on-1. Those three goals are now defined per business above: **one number, one fix, one decision.** Run it Oct 1 and Nov 1. Thirty minutes each.

### 5. The one rule that makes it stick
A captain reports the number **even when it is bad**, and nothing bad happens for reporting it. The moment a missed number costs someone a hard conversation they did not sign up for, the Monday posts turn into fiction and you lose the only instrument you have.
