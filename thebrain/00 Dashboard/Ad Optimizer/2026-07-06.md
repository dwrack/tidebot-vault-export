### `AO-3` Landing-page CRO fixes for https://www.doorcountykayaktours.com/ (apply in CMS)
_DCKT · Brand | Door County Kayak Tours_

- **Fix message match.** The H1 "Door County Kayak Tours" is just the brand name. It should mirror the ad's hook word-for-word (e.g., if the ad says "Kayak Cave Point's Sea Caves," the H1 should say that). A visitor who clicks and sees the brand name instead of the promise they were sold will bounce before reading anything.

- **Add a sticky above-fold CTA.** "Book Now & Search by Date" is buried in the nav. There should be a single high-contrast button in the hero viewport, with one action ("Book Your Tour") and no competing links around it.

- **Make the hero do a job.** "More than just kayaking!" is doing nothing. Replace it with a specific, scannable value prop: "Guided sea cave kayak tours, $69, 2 hours, all skill levels, daily departures." Every word earns its spot.

- **Quantify the trust signal.** "Top Rated" is a claim without proof. Show the number: "4.9 stars across 600+ reviews" with a TripAdvisor or Google logo. Visitors are spending $69-$139+ with a stranger in a kayak. They need a reason to trust fast.

- **Kill the nav for paid traffic.** The full site nav (Kayak, Bike, SUP, Surrey, About, More) gives a visitor 12+ exit paths before they hit the CTA. For this ad's destination, use a stripped landing page with no nav links, or at minimum a minimal header with just the logo and one "Book Now" button.
