### `AO-1` Landing-page CRO fixes for https://houstonpedalbarge.com/ (apply in CMS)
_HPB · Search - Houston Pedal Barge_

- **Fix the message mismatch first.** The ad says "Pedal Barge" but the page title and H1 say "Party Barge." Visitors landing from a pedal barge ad see "Houston Party Barge" and wonder if they're in the right place. Change the H1 to match the ad copy exactly: "Houston Pedal Barge Tour" or whatever the ad headline is. This is almost certainly the biggest conversion killer here.

- **Replace the H1 with a value prop, not just a name.** "Houston Party Barge" is a brand name, not a reason to book. Try: "Houston's Only Pedal Party Boat, Clear Lake" with a subhead that hits the emotional outcome ("BYOB, 8+ people, 1 hr 45 min on the water"). Give people a reason to stay on the page.

- **Kill or mute the autoplay video above the fold.** Autoplay video tanks mobile load time and forces users to fumble for a mute button before they've even read the offer. If video is essential, use a poster image with a play button instead.

- **Add a real trust signal in the hero.** "Top Rated" is vague and does nothing. Replace it with a specific social proof hook: "4.9 stars, 300+ reviews on Google" with a star graphic and a link. Put it directly under the headline, not buried below the fold.

- **Make the primary CTA one decision, not two.** "Book Now & Search by Date!" is two asks in one button. Pick one: "See Available Dates" (lower friction) or "Book Now." Then run a second CTA further down the page. Decision paralysis at the first touch kills mobile conversions especially.
