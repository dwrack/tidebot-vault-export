### `AO-4` Landing-page CRO fixes for https://houstonpedalbarge.com/ (apply in CMS)
_HPB · Search - Houston Pedal Barge_

- **Fix message match first.** The H1 is just a brand name ("Houston Party Barge") while the ad likely promises something like "pedal party boat" or "booze cruise on Clear Lake." The visitor lands and has to confirm they're in the right place themselves. Change the H1 to mirror the ad's exact hook, e.g., "Clear Lake Pedal Barge Booze Cruises" so the brain says "yes, this is it" in under a second.

- **Kill the video autoplay in the hero or lazy-load it.** A background video fires HTTP requests immediately on page load and can add 2-5 seconds on mobile. At $152 in ad spend you're almost certainly losing people before the page even renders. Replace with a high-quality static image as default, video optional.

- **One CTA, not three.** You're showing "Book Your Party Barge Tour," "Book Your Pedal Barge Tour," and "Christmas Themed Booze Cruise" all within the first scroll. Decision paralysis is real. Pick the single most-booked option, make that the primary CTA button above the fold, and push the others below.

- **Make "Top Rated" a real trust signal, not a claim.** "Top Rated Party Barge Tours!" with no star count, review count, or platform (TripAdvisor, Google, Yelp) reads as self-promotion. Swap it for something like "4.9 stars across 200+ Google reviews" with a direct link. Actual numbers convert; adjectives don't.

- **Add urgency that's real, not fake.** "Spaces fill up quickly" is generic. Pull the next 2-3 available dates from FareHarbor and show them directly on the page: "Next available: Sat June 13 (4 spots left)." That's the kind of specificity that tips someone who's on the fence into booking now rather than "checking back later."
