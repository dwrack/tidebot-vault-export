### `AO-5` Landing-page CRO fixes for https://houstonpedalbarge.com/ (apply in CMS)
_HPB · Search - Houston Pedal Barge_

- **Fix the message match gap first.** The ad says "Houston Pedal Barge" but the page title and H1 say "Houston Party Barge." Visitors arriving from that ad experience immediate disconnect and bounce. Change the H1 to "Houston Pedal Barge" or add a direct subhead like "Clear Lake's Only Pedal Party Boat" so it mirrors the ad's language exactly.

- **Replace the hero headline with a benefit, not a brand name.** "Houston Party Barge" tells nobody why they should care. Swap it for something specific: "Pedal, Drink, and Cruise Clear Lake with Your Crew" or "Houston's Only Pedal-Powered Booze Cruise, From $55." Lead with the experience and the price anchor.

- **Kill the competing CTAs in the hero.** "Book Now," "Learn More," and "Book Your Party Barge Tour" are all fighting for the same click. Pick one: "Book Now, Starting at $55" and remove or move the Learn More links below the fold. Every extra option bleeds conversion.

- **Add a trust signal directly in the hero, not buried below.** "Top Rated Party Barge Tours" is sitting below the fold where nobody sees it. Pull one specific data point into the hero section: a star rating, a review count, or a single punchy quote. "4.9 stars across 200+ cruises" outperforms a vague "Top Rated" badge every time.

- **Put a date picker or availability hook at the top.** "Spaces fill up quickly, Book Online Now!" is the only urgency signal and it's in a banner most visitors ignore. Replace or supplement it with an inline date selector or a line like "Next available: this Saturday" right under the hero CTA. Scarcity is only convincing when it's specific.

Notes on the choices:

**Paddle the Sea Caves Daily** and **Spot Wildlife From the Water** hit experiential specificity the current set lacks. People searching the brand name are still shopping; these answer "why now, why you."

**All Gear Included** reduces a real friction point. First-timers often wonder what they need to bring. Removing that uncertainty at the ad level lowers the decision cost before the landing page.

**Summer Dates Filling Fast** is the urgency play missing from the current set. The reviews and 2002 heritage build credibility; none of the existing headlines push someone toward clicking *right now*.

**Morning & Sunset Paddles** signals variety without adding more keywords to bid on, which matters for a brand campaign where you're already owning the query.

**Top-Rated Since 2002** recombines two existing signals (reviews, longevity) into something more punchy, and frees up the two original headlines to test new angles.

### `AO-9` Landing-page CRO fixes for https://www.doorcountykayaktours.com/ (apply in CMS)
_DCKT · Brand | Door County Kayak Tours_

- **Kill the nav.** A homepage with 6+ top-level nav items and nested dropdowns bleeds clicks. Build a dedicated landing page (or strip nav to logo-only) so the only exit is booking. Every menu link is a conversion leak.

- **Replace the H1.** "Door County Kayak Tours" is just the brand name. Rewrite to match the ad's hook and lead with the outcome: something like "Guided Kayak Tours at Cave Point, Wisconsin's #1 Sea Cave" puts the destination and credibility in the first five words a visitor reads.

- **Single above-fold CTA with price anchored.** "Book Now & Search by Date!" competes with itself (two actions, no urgency). Replace with one button: "Book the Cave Point Tour, From $69" and put it directly under the H1 with no scroll required on mobile. Remove the giveaway entry from above the fold entirely, it trains visitors to wait rather than pay.

- **Lead with social proof count, not just a badge.** "#1 Attraction" with no source or review count is easy to ignore. Add the TripAdvisor/Google rating and total review count inline: "4.9 stars, 400+ reviews" immediately under the H1. Specific numbers convert; generic superlatives do not.

- **Check mobile hero image load time.** The Cave Point aerial or scenic shot is almost certainly an unoptimized PNG or oversized JPG, which tanks Time to Interactive on mobile where most ad traffic lands. Run the hero through `sips -Z 1920 -s format jpeg -s formatOptions 78` and verify it's under 250KB. A 3-second delay on a paid click is a guaranteed bounce.
