### `AO-5` Landing-page CRO fixes for https://houstonpedalbarge.com/ (apply in CMS)
_HPB · Search - Houston Pedal Barge_

- **Brand mismatch is likely killing ROAS**: the domain is houstonpedalbarge.com but the H1, title tag, and both CTAs say "Party Barge," not "Pedal Barge." If the ad's headline/creative says "Pedal Barge" (the actual product, per the copy: "city's first ever pedal party boat tour"), visitors landing on a page that never says "pedal" in the H1 will bounce, thinking they hit the wrong site. Fix: H1 and title tag should lead with "Pedal Barge," matching the ad exactly. This is probably the single biggest lever here.

- **Duplicate, competing CTAs on one screen**: "BOOK YOUR PARTY BARGE TOUR" and "BOOK YOUR PEDAL BARGE TOUR" both appear near the top, plus a third "Book Now" on the product card. Different labels for what should be the same action reads as confusing/broken, not premium. Fix: one consistent CTA label everywhere above the fold, tied to the exact product name used in the ad.

- **No trust signal in the hero**: no rating, review count, or "as seen on" badge visible before the fold. At $55+/head with a stranger's credit card on the line, cold traffic from an ad needs social proof fast. Fix: add a star rating + review count (e.g. "4.8 stars, 600+ reviews") directly under the H1, not buried in the footer.

- **Autoplay video hero is a speed tax**: video-first heroes are notorious for slow LCP on mobile, and paid traffic is disproportionately mobile. If this hasn't been checked, run it through PageSpeed Insights, a 3+ second delay before the CTA is visible will bleed cold ad clicks before they ever see the offer. Fix: swap to a fast-loading static hero image with the video as a secondary, user-initiated element, or lazy-load the video after LCP.

- **Nav clutter competes with the ad's promise**: "Skip to primary navigation," a full menu, gift cards, blog, contact all sit above the offer. Someone clicking a specific pedal-barge/booze-cruise ad wants confirmation they're in the right place and a price, not a full site menu. Fix: for paid traffic specifically, consider a stripped-down landing template (logo, H1 matching ad, price, one CTA, reviews) with the full nav removed or collapsed.

### `AO-9` Landing-page CRO fixes for https://www.doorcountykayaktours.com/ (apply in CMS)
_DCKT · Brand | Door County Kayak Tours_

- **Kill message-match gap**: the H1 just restates the brand name ("Door County Kayak Tours") instead of mirroring whatever hook got the click. Whoever wrote the ad copy, that same phrase needs to be the first thing visitors read above the fold, word for word. If the ad promises "Cave Point Kayak Tour from $69," the hero should say that, not make people hunt through a mega-menu to find it.

- **Nav is eating the fold**: Kayak, Bike, SUP, About, More, each with sub-dropdowns, all before any offer or CTA. Paid traffic has zero patience for that. Collapse to a single sticky "Book Now" button plus a stripped mobile nav, and push the tour card (Cave Point, $69, 2 hrs) into the very first screen.

- **The popup is fighting your own CTA**: "Enter to Win a Free Tour" competing with "Book Now & Search by Date" in the same header sends mixed signals on a page that needs one job: book a tour. Kill the giveaway modal for paid traffic specifically, or at minimum delay it 15+ seconds so it doesn't interrupt the booking decision.

- **No trust signal in the first screen**: nothing in the excerpt shows review count, star rating, or "as seen in" before the fold. At $69/person with a stranger driving the boat, that's the first objection. Put a review badge (count + rating) directly under the price, not buried in an About page.

- **CTA buried and generic**: "Book Now & Search by Date" is a nav item, not a hero CTA. Add a large, contrasting button directly on the tour card itself ("Check Availability" or "Reserve Your Spot") that jumps straight to the FareHarbor widget, skip the extra click through the menu.

Given the ROAS math ($76 spend, 0.6x vs 2x floor), you need roughly 3x the current conversion rate to hit floor. That's not a copy tweak, it's a structural fix: fold content, CTA placement, and message match first, THEN worry about smaller polish.
