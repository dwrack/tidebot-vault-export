> 
> As for the negatives, a couple have already been mentioned by others. The launch area is a little sketchy. You walk behind an apartment complex and then down a fairly steep hill to the water. The water itself wasn’t anything unusual for a lake, but the path could definitely be difficult if you have any mobility or accessibility concerns. Parking was also a downside. The lot you have to use was pretty expensive. Considering the price of the tour, it would be nice if parking were included.
> 
> Lastly, it was extremely dark on the way back. We had to use our phone flashlights to find our way up the hill and back to the parking lot. Some lighting along the path would make a big difference.
> 
> Overall, though, we had a great, relaxing evening, and it honestly wouldn’t have been the same without Michael. He made the entire experience worth it.
