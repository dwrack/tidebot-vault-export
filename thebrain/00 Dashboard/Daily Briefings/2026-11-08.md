Hi Taryn,

Pitching Door County, Wisconsin for the 2027 list, and specifically the part of it most visitors never see: the Cave Point sea caves, from a kayak.

What: Cave Point County Park is a stretch of 420-million-year-old dolomite cliffs on the Lake Michigan side of the peninsula. The caves are only reachable from the water. On a calm morning you paddle inside them, and the water underneath is clear to 15 or 20 feet, which nobody expects in the Midwest. Door County has more shoreline than any county in the US, more than Hawaii, and almost all of it is seen from the road.

Where: Jacksonport, on the quiet east side of the peninsula, about 20 minutes from Sturgeon Bay. Most Door County trips stay on the Green Bay side (Fish Creek, Ephraim, Sister Bay). The lake side is the reason to come back.

Why 2027: Door County has spent years being the cherry pie and fish boil destination. What is changing is how people are doing it. The water side of the county, sea caves, the shipwrecks off Death's Door, the Niagara Escarpment bluffs, is where the growth is, and it is still uncrowded compared with the Michigan or Minnesota shore.
