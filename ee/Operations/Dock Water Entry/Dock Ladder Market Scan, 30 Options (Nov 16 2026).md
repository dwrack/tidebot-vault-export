# Dock Ladder Market Scan: 30 options (Nov 16 2026)

Companion to [[Dock Ladder Decision Table (Nov 16 2026)]]. Prices and specs come from product pages on 2026-11-16. "(snip)" means the number came from a search snippet, not the product page. "n/a" means the page doesn't say. Nothing here was invented.

Our site: floating dock, deck 16-19 in above the water, so "reach below deck" minus ~17 in = how far the bottom step sits under the surface. Pulled every 2-4 weeks for algae. The ladders bought for the sauna boat had a handle buckle within a month (not Wet Steps; model not on file).

**Reading the table:** "Out of water" = how it comes out (flip-up hinge, lift/slide up, lift-off, or fixed). "Floating OK" = the page says it works on a floating dock. Legs that rest on the lakebed don't work on a floating dock.

## What this says

- **Only #1 Platforms & Ladders and #2 Alumistair combine a true 45° stair, 7 in treads, two rails and a lift-off mount.** Neither lists a weight rating, and both are built as shore stairs, so the floating-dock bracket needs a call. #1's price showed $2,898 on this pass (the decision table had $2,229 earlier today); confirm by phone.
- Wet Steps is still in play. The buckled handle Davey reported was on the sauna boat ladders, not Wet Steps (corrected 11/16).
- Max Dock (#5) and both Extreme Max stairs (#6, #7) stand on legs, which a floating dock can't use.
- The floating-rated lift ladders (#21, #22, #24, #25) are strong (650-750 lb) and come out easily, but they are vertical with 2-4 in steps and no full rails, which fails the cold and larger guest test.
- Nothing off the shelf is angled, railed, rated 500+ lb, floating-rated and lift-off all at once. That gap is the case for the custom welder spec in the decision table, with #1 as the buy-it benchmark.

Not found or blocked: Vonder, Rhino, Eagle, Max-Grip, Candock, Inter-Fab, Marine Hardware Inc, Hewitt, Wahoo, Accudock, Royal Docks, Floe, Connect-A-Dock (no priced product page, or 403/429).
