# Dock Water Entry: ladder + two ramps

Status: concept v0.1, started 2026-11-06. Nothing ordered, nothing approved. Drawing: `Dock Water Entry — Concept v0.1 (Nov 6 2026).html` in this folder.

## What it is
One 30 ft run of dock. Swim ladder in the middle. A ramp on each side of the ladder, mounted along the dock edge (12 ft long, 3 ft out), sloping down and away from the ladder to about 30 in under the surface. Dock only, not the sauna float.

## Known facts (verified 2026-11-06 from vault + shared record)
- Dock is Elevated Tides' (Jonah). E&E is the tenant.
- Main dock walk is about 10 ft wide (Marina Site Inventory, not tape-measured).
- New-ladder measurements in ee-in-flight: 19 in near the private saunas, 16 in on the boat side. Treated here as deck height above water. [confirm that is what was measured]
- River: 25 to 40 ft deep at the plunge zone, 1 to 3 kn typical current, 4 to 5 kn at freshet, level swings 10+ ft over the year. The dock floats, so the ramp geometry stays constant.
- Two water ladders exist today. The east one is buckling. Cleaners can't remove the left one.
- Shared record already has "Dock ladder replacement for swim exit" (both, in progress, 5-step fits, flip-up vs fixed undecided). The ramps are new; nothing on file.
- Dock framing, float type and edge detail: NOT on file. Has to be looked at before any bracket is specified.

## Attachment points
| Tag | Where | What | Count |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | Top of each ramp at the deck edge | Hinge or landing bracket through-bolted into the edge framing | 2 |
| B | Dock face every 4 ft down each ramp | Stand-off bracket, two 3/8 in 316 through-bolts, 5 x 5 x 1/4 in backing plate behind each | 6 |
| C | Under each ramp, outboard edge | Diagonal struts back to the dock frame, or a float under the low end | 4 |
| D | Ladder, center | Quick-release deck mount, through-bolted with backing plates | 1 |
| E | Deck, each side of the ladder | 316 stainless grab bars | 2 to 4 |

The ramps hang 3 ft off one edge of the dock with people on them. That load on the edge framing is the thing a builder has to check first.

## Slope
Deck to low end is about 4 ft of drop over 12 ft, 1 in 3. That needs raised cleats or shallow steps and a handrail. An accessible 1 in 12 ramp would need a 48 ft run, so this is not an ADA ramp and should not be called one.
