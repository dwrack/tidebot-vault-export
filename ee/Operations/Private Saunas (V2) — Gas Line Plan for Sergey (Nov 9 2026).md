## Fire features (G-003)
- **Recommended at the two sauna pits: dial timer + electronic ignition.** Twist the dial, it lights itself, goes out when the dial runs down. Needs a wire into the S1/S2 empty conduit already in the electrical set. Timer: Firegear ESTOP1-0H (~$140 to $200) or Outdoor Plus OPT-ESTOPTM (~$288), both 165k BTU/hr, 1/2 in ports. Vessel: The Outdoor Plus Cazo 31 or 36 in GFRC bowl, ash/gray, NG; match-lit ~$2,100 to $2,630, electronic ignition ~$4,450. Premium alternative Paloform Bento 32 / Solus Hemi 36 (~$4k to $6k, unverified).
- **Lounge fire table: dial timer + push-button spark + thermocouple safety pilot** if no power is handy (twist, press and hold, let go). Rectangular GFRC table ~60 x 24 in (Outdoor Plus Pismo class or Paloform Bol), $2,500 to $4,500.
- Skip: match-lit only, and 120 V commercial timers (HPC 694 ~$650+, Grand Effects ~$1,100).
- Confirm with the vessel maker that the ignition module tolerates a timer cutting gas upstream. Order NG, cover, fill, timer and ignition from one maker. Listed (CSA/UL) only.
