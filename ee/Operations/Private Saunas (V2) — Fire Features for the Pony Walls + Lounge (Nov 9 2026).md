## Controls (same at all three)
- Mechanical 20 to 60 min dial timer + E-stop upstream of each: Firegear ESTOP1-0H (~$140 to $200) or Outdoor Plus OPT-ESTOPTM (~$288), 165k BTU/hr, 1/2 in ports. Every pick above is under 165k.
- Electronic ignition where power is easy (both pony walls via S1/S2 conduit; the lounge already has power): twist the timer, it lights itself. Confirm with the maker that the ignition module is fine with gas cut by a timer upstream (it just sees no flame and locks out).
- Sergey: gas drop to the appliance valve, connect, fire. Grant: pony-wall framing to the unit's clearances (non-combustible for the Kalea Bay / RTF boxes), tile. Electrician: wire into S1/S2.

## Order of operations
1. Davey picks A, B or C for the pony walls and the lounge table (Del Mar 72 recommended).
2. Give Grant the unit's framing drawing so the pony walls are built to it, and give Sergey the appliance BTU + inlet spot (the Rev AK drops don't move).
3. Order: 1 to 2 week lead on stock items; call the dealer for a contractor price and ask for the timer + ignition from the same maker.
