## Phase framework

### Phase 1: Activation pilot
- Tie-off sign-off from Topper or a licensed engineer
- One large sauna (~30 to 50 people) moored to the dock
- Storage container on the bluff with pop-up windows for concessions. Ask for temp power + internet
- Adirondack chairs on beach and bluff
- Seasonal lower freeboard / swim float (maybe; dock used to have one)
- Minor landscaping as desired
- Our own ops needs, handled quietly on our side: showers, changing, potable water, sewage, security/cameras. Working idea is a trailer in the gravel lot.

### Phase 2: Lease (summer 2027)
- 20 to 30 year lease on the adjacent gravel lot, direct or via the City
- Restrooms, changing facilities, semi-permanent metal building in a fenced yard
- Kayak and paddleboard rentals
- Dock improvements, bluff improvements

### Phase 3: Permanent
- Partner with the City, CEIC, and OMSI on a permanent structure inside the area Master Plan (design-competition / grant play)

## Fee estimate under existing PP&R commercial rates (2026 guidelines)
Book rates are written for park-land concessions, not a dock lease, so this is the "if we just followed the book" number for the LOI conversation. Source: `PP&R General Guidelines for Commercial Activity (2026).pdf`, pages 5 to 6.

| Line item | Rate in guidelines | Phase 1 estimate |
|---|---|---|
| Application processing (Tier 1, 45+ days) | $165 one-time | $165 |
| Insurance / AIE review | $55 one-time | $55 |
| Sauna sessions (closest analog: Education & Fitness, 21 to 40 participants) | $11.75/hr | ~56 hr/wk = ~$2,850/mo |
| Sauna sessions if 41 to 60 participants | $23.50/hr | ~$5,700/mo |
| Recreational piers (dock use) | $230.75/mo per park | $231/mo |
| Concession container, "All Other Parks" small unit | $303/mo | $303/mo |
| Concession container if treated as a large unit (6x6 base + $10/sq ft over) | $886 + $10/sq ft | a 20 ft container is ~$2,100/mo |
| Electricity (110v/20A) | $36.50/mo | $37/mo |

Rough Phase 1 range: about $3,400/mo (~$41K/yr) on the low read, up to ~$8,000/mo if the City bills a 41 to 60 headcount tier and a large container. 10% discount for bookings of 4+ months. Recommend proposing the low read and letting the City counter.

## Background notes (ours only, not for the City)
Things we solve on our side so they never become the City's problem:
- Parks guidelines exclude fuel-based equipment. Our LOI should simply describe the sauna as a self-contained floating vessel with its own heating system, same as any moored boat.
- Improvements default to PP&R property. Describe everything in Phase 1 as temporary/seasonal.
- Insurance: $2M per person / $1M property (or $2M single limit), City as additional insured (CG 20 10 04 13 or CG 20 26 04 13), workers comp, 30-day cancellation notice. Get the cert lined up before it's asked for.
- Food/bev needs Multnomah County health + Fire Marshal sign-off; alcohol needs PP&R written approval + OLCC. Keep concessions simple in Phase 1.
- Live Nation venue (fall 2027) uses the adjacent lots on event evenings. Plan session hours around it; no need to surface it in the LOI.
- Gravel lot was slated for re-fencing as parking in June. Confirm availability quietly before Phase 2 planning assumes it.
- Headcount: guidelines treat 150+ as a Special Use event. A 50-person sauna plus beach guests stays well under.
