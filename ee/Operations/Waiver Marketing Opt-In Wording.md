# Waiver marketing opt-in wording (texts + email)

Goal: one friendly, optional checkbox on the Periode waiver that gives real consent for marketing texts and emails and gives Twilio the opt-in evidence it asks for.

## What Twilio A2P reviewers look for
- Checkbox is unchecked by default and separate from the waiver signature or terms acceptance. Consent cannot be a condition of the visit.
- Names the brand, says what the texts are, gives a frequency, "Msg & data rates may apply", "Reply STOP to opt out, HELP for help", and links to the privacy policy.
- Privacy policy states mobile numbers and opt-in data are not shared with or sold to third parties for marketing.
- Campaign form: paste the checkbox text as the opt-in description, attach a screenshot of the waiver page showing the box, and make sure the first text sent contains brand name, STOP and HELP.

## FINAL (11/15): one-sentence checkbox label, Periode allows a single line

[ ] Yes, text and email me about Ebb & Ember events, last-minute openings and discounts (about 2 to 4 texts a month, msg & data rates may apply, reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help, privacy policy at ebbandember.com/privacy).

Shorter if the field has a character limit:

## Notes
- Transactional texts (booking confirmation, 2-hour reminder, door code) do not need this box. Keep those separate so a STOP on marketing does not kill reminders.
- Capture the timestamp and the checkbox state in Periode or the export, and keep them with the signed waiver.
