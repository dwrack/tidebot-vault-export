# The Rose: Plaque Program Names (round 1)

_2026-10-17. For Davey to walk Hannah and Jess through. Private; Holman is off the record. Page to share (private until Davey shares it): https://claude.ai/artifact/NY2A1AnbAFy9d1SRTX9G6b_

## Talk track (5 minutes)
1. The model is settled: give $X, your name goes on a plaque by the seat, stone, plank or fire pit. Pioneer Courthouse Square bricks. No ownership.
2. Naming it is your lane. Here are four tests: reads on a plaque, community first, gift not ownership, no collisions.
3. Shortlist: The Trellis (recommended), Grafted, Raised ("Raised by Portland"), Names on the Water. Long list behind it.
4. Gift levels: bridges you can see from the Rose (Tilikum, Morrison, Burnside, Hawthorne, Steel), river words, or rose growth stages.
5. Ask for three calls: 1-2 names to take further, a ladder, and whether the name doubles as the campaign line.
