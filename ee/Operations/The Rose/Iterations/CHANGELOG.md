# The Rose: iteration log

Every round of the design is kept exactly as it was, so the evolution can be cut into a video later. Each version folder has the standalone 3D model (open the `.html` in a browser, needs internet for the 3D library) and screenshots. The published page has a version strip that replays all of them: https://claude.ai/artifact/VPwztPdkTQfPb23vPh2yzz

All on 2026-09-26, one working session. Davey's directions are in his words, lightly cleaned up from dictation.

---

## v0.1: First bloom
**Direction:** "PDX is Rose City... a rose sauna that was a circular design with petals that had flat parts on the roof for 8-12 people to sit on, with sunken sections surrounding fires... stadium seating, 3-4 door entrances... insulated drapes that lower from the ceiling... another thorn sauna for smaller sessions and a green stemmed waterslide as the stem into the water."
**What changed:** Round sauna on a 46 ft float. 3 tiers down into a sunken pit, 4 doors, glass petal toward the bridge. Flat roof terraces with glass rails. Center stone heater. Radial and ring drapes. The Thorn sauna, the Stem slide, one leaf float.
**Screenshots:** `v0.1/`

## v0.2: Petals to the ground
**Direction:** "I want the rose petals to fan out more so that they are the top area and they span down to the ground." Then: "Do just 3 door openings, make the swim-out leafs (add 2 more) near the waterslide so it looks more like a stem and leaves. We just need to get the space certified with the stoves in them, not the stove itself. The rocks need to curve outward like pistils." Heat: "EOS natural gas heaters they can custom create."
**What changed:** Flared outer petals from the deck up past the terraces, overlapping in a spiral. Doors moved into the slits between petals and cut to 3. Pistil stone cage around one gas altar, with the flue out the oculus. Three leaf floats on the stem. Pit widened, tiers 45 cm, lower ceiling, float to 49 ft.

## v0.3: More petals, less dock
**Direction:** "More space in between them so there can be hangout areas between each rose petal on the ground floor, and some steps up to the top of the petals, so you can jump off the top of the petals into the water. I don't want much dock visible, I want more petals. Make the pistils a wire cage with the heating pipe travelling through them and then back down, with rocks surrounding them." **What changed:** Inner petals spaced apart, with warm-wall lounges in the gaps. A second ring of 8 lower petals covering the float to the edge. Stairs up 3 petals to jump platforms. ## v0.4: The petals are the decks
**Direction:** "The actual area where people are standing on the roof should be the rose petals, so they open and eventually are horizontal for people to hang out on. I don't want a weird standing area on the top of the rose." Then: "The fires should be somewhat protected and inside each of the rose petals, not outside." And: "Each of the rose petals is an area below and above to hang out. I love the idea of one of the sides being glass so you can see the bridge."
**What changed:** Flat roof terrace gone. Five petals rise from the deck, bend outward and open flat into decks at ~2.9 m. The hot room roof became a closed bud of layered petals. A cupped outer petal under each open petal shelters a fire nook with a curved bench, so every petal is a deck above and a fire nook below. The bridge petal is glass, with a glass windbreak on its nook. Spiral stairs in the two gaps beside the glass petal.
