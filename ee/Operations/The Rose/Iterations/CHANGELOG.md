# The Rose: iteration log

Every round of the design is kept exactly as it was, so the evolution can be cut into a video later. Each version folder has the standalone 3D model (open the `.html` in a browser, needs internet for the 3D library) and screenshots. The published page has a version strip that replays all of them: https://claude.ai/artifact/VPwztPdkTQfPb23vPh2yzz

All on 2026-09-26, one working session. Davey's directions are in his words, lightly cleaned up from dictation.

---

## v0.1: First bloom
**Direction:** "PDX is Rose City... a rose sauna that was a circular design with petals that had flat parts on the roof for 8-12 people to sit on, with sunken sections surrounding fires... stadium seating, 3-4 door entrances... insulated drapes that lower from the ceiling... another thorn sauna for smaller sessions and a green stemmed waterslide as the stem into the water."
**What changed:** Round sauna on a 46 ft float. 3 tiers down into a sunken pit, 4 doors, glass petal toward the bridge. Flat roof terraces with glass rails. Center stone heater. Radial and ring drapes. The Thorn sauna, the Stem slide, one leaf float.
**Screenshots:** `v0.1/`

## v0.2: Petals to the ground
**Direction:** "I want the rose petals to fan out more so that they are the top area and they span down to the ground." Then: "Do just 3 door openings, make the swim-out leafs (add 2 more) near the waterslide so it looks more like a stem and leaves. We just need to get the space certified with the stoves in them, not the stove itself. The rocks need to curve outward like pistils." Heat: "EOS natural gas heaters they can custom create."
**What changed:** Flared outer petals from the deck up past the terraces, overlapping in a spiral. Doors moved into the slits between petals and cut to 3. Pistil stone cage around one gas altar, with the flue out the oculus. Three leaf floats on the stem. Pit widened, tiers 45 cm, lower ceiling, float to 49 ft.

## v0.3: More petals, less dock
**Direction:** "More space in between them so there can be hangout areas between each rose petal on the ground floor, and some steps up to the top of the petals, so you can jump off the top of the petals into the water. I don't want much dock visible, I want more petals. Make the pistils a wire cage with the heating pipe travelling through them and then back down, with rocks surrounding them." Then: "Remove the thorn sauna."
**What changed:** Inner petals spaced apart, with warm-wall lounges in the gaps. A second ring of 8 lower petals covering the float to the edge. Stairs up 3 petals to jump platforms. Pistils rebuilt as wire cages with a glowing heating pipe looping up and back down through each, packed with stones. Thorn removed.

## v0.4: The petals are the decks
**Direction:** "The actual area where people are standing on the roof should be the rose petals, so they open and eventually are horizontal for people to hang out on. I don't want a weird standing area on the top of the rose." Then: "The fires should be somewhat protected and inside each of the rose petals, not outside." And: "Each of the rose petals is an area below and above to hang out. I love the idea of one of the sides being glass so you can see the bridge."
**What changed:** Flat roof terrace gone. Five petals rise from the deck, bend outward and open flat into decks at ~2.9 m. The hot room roof became a closed bud of layered petals. A cupped outer petal under each open petal shelters a fire nook with a curved bench, so every petal is a deck above and a fire nook below. The bridge petal is glass, with a glass windbreak on its nook. Spiral stairs in the two gaps beside the glass petal.

## v0.5: Wild and free
**Direction:** "I'm not sure if I like the symmetry or want it more wild. Show me 3 variations of the petals being more wild and not so perfect. PDX is wild and free and built around love of the rose and love of that spirit." Then: "I also like one of the rose petals being a giant group waterslide that slides into the water."
**What changed:** One petal generator, four characters: Classic, Wind-swept (all petals leaning and twisting one way), Wild bloom (irregular sizes, one petal drooping to the water), Unfurling (petals at different stages of opening). One petal became a wide group waterslide.

## v0.6: It moves
**Direction:** "Could there be some immersive movement with the rose, maybe it can open and close? Interesting ways that a closed rose and an open rose could offer different experiences, but also that could be a whole other sauna on the other side of the river." Then: "That waterslide doesn't look like a petal. It should probably be 2 petals doing that."
**What changed:** Bloom control: the petals animate from open decks to a closed bud and back. Two petal-shaped slide petals droop into the water side by side. The Bud, a permanently closed smaller sauna, sits on the west bank facing the Rose.

## v0.7: Fitted to the dock
**Direction:** "I don't want the wind screen on the top of the rose, that looks weird. Why are the petals all crinkled?" Then: "Keep the two petal slides inside the stem. Look at the dock on Google Maps and see how we need to keep people inside the swimming area." And: "Remove the circle drape, just keep the partition drapes." "It's the Kerr Public Dock." "Give me a photo overlay of how large it will be on that map."
**What changed:** Glass guards removed from the open petals. Smooth shading and no ruffles, so the petals read as clean curves. Circle drape removed. The dock is modeled: the Rose moors off its outer tip, reached by a short gangway. The slide petals face the shore and land in a swim cove. The stem is now a floating boom around the downstream side of the cove, with the leaves inside it and a lane line upstream. To-scale site overlay: `../The Rose - Site Overlay (Holman-Kerr Dock).png`.

## v0.8: Fixed petals, easy water entry, all options side by side
**Direction:** "Drop the moving petals. The occupied decks are meant for jumping off of, so I'm not sure I want guards on them. We're a grey area of building as it's a boat or a water structure. Let me see all options for wild and symmetrical next to each other in another tab. I want petals under the slide petals that allow really easy access into the water, stepped down."
**What changed:** Bloom/Bud movement removed; petals are fixed. No guards on the jump decks. Four petal-shaped steps under each slide petal, from deck level to below the water. Two more wild seeds (Wild B, Wild C). New page, the petal lineup, with all six characters side by side and linked cameras: https://claude.ai/artifact/T99tEHYyXiAS9PSg8cT4uA

## v0.9: Amphitheater petals
**Direction:** "Make the other side, opposite the petal slides, have their petals counter-spaced so you can't see the door. A spiral ramp up the petals, or the petals as big stadium seating stairs above the fire pit, as easier access to the petal roofs. Getting that many people up to jump off, I want easier access. Multiple different heights of petals so people can jump off at different heights, but inside the swimmable area." Then: "I'm OK with glass on the outside petals where you can't jump off, as a barrier railing... maybe some chill lounge seating to watch the bridge and chat." "Maybe some cool bugs that are art lounge chairs." "I don't like the outside ramp or stairs you just made, remove them." "The lounge chairs are way too bulky and horribly designed." "Have the actual insides of the flower petals be stadium seating and steps that arch up, with the fire pit at the bottom of them."
**What changed:** Tried and removed in the same round: an outside spiral of stadium steps on a 59 ft float, and ladybug/caterpillar/bee lounge chairs. Kept: the three non-slide petals are now amphitheaters, six stadium steps arching up from a fire pit at the bottom. The rim is a saddle, low in the middle and high at the horns, so it offers jump heights from about 1 m to 2.9 m and keeps the bridge view from the hot room window. The two dock-side petals are lounges with a glass rail. Offset screen petals hide the dock-side doors. The stem boom now starts on the north-west side, so the water north of the flower is inside the swim cove. Float back to 49 ft.

## v0.10: Jump petal between the slides, big leaves
**Direction:** "The petal that you can jump off at different heights should be in between the two petal slides." Then: "Bigger leaf on the stem too, with a few people lounging on them."
**What changed:** Petal roles swapped. The jump amphitheater sits dead center in the swim cove with a slide petal on each side. The north slide petal keeps a glass base, so the hot room still sees the bridge. Leaf floats roughly doubled in size, with people lying out on them. Site overlay updated to match.

## v0.11: Rooftop lounges, 35 ft jump, swim reach to the bridge
**Direction:** "I like the unfurling so far the best. Also maybe there is a way to stand on the top of the sauna too, as a roof. More lounge space." "This should stretch all the way over to the other dock under the bridge." "Give me a few different versions of the roof lounge area, maybe another sunken fire pit with a circular lounge space." "The jump petal should be a petal that comes from the ground on the left to the highest point on the right." "Jonah probably wants the highest point higher... max out at 35 feet, or whatever height would allow for a double back flip."
**What changed:** Unfurling is the default petal character. The sauna's top is now a crown lounge ringed by upright petals, in three versions: flat crown deck, sunken fire circle, terraced bowl. The jump petal rises from deck level on the south side to a launch platform 35 ft above the water on the north side. The stem boom now runs ~150 m north, past Audrey McCall Beach and under the Hawthorne, to the fire station pier. Site overlay redrawn for the full reach.

## v0.12: Jump petal in proportion
**Direction:** "The jump petal is way too tall. Make it more proportional to the rose."
**What changed:** Peak dropped from 35 ft to about 20 ft above the water (5.6 m above the deck), just above the roof rail. Same sweep from the deck on the left up to the launch on the right, with fewer, gentler steps.

## v0.13: No launch platform
**Direction:** "Remove the weird platform on top of the jump petal and update our main model."
**What changed:** The flat launch platform at the top of the jump petal is gone. The petal's own tip is the high jump point.

## v0.14: Acoustics in the model
**Direction:** "Think about how we can make sure the interior acoustics aren't too loud or reflective." Then: "Yes" (show it in the model).
**What changed:** Hot-room lobe walls are now faceted into flat ~1.2 m panels instead of smooth arcs. Irregularly spaced timber ribs run across the ceiling over a dark slatted stone-wool absorber. Bench risers are slotted, with wool (and the speakers) behind. Estimated reverb goes from ~1.3 s empty to ~0.7 s empty and ~0.5 s full. Full plan in the brief under "Interior acoustics plan".

## v0.15: Jump petal unfurls, climbing wall
**Direction:** "I also want the petal that is the jump petal to unfurl outwards like a petal leaf, not just straight up. With the back side of it a gentle outsloped climbing wall, that if you fell off, you would fall off in the water."
**What changed:** The jump petal now curls outward as it rises (about 2 m of outward flare at the top), so it reads like an opening petal and its tip hangs over the water. Its back is a gently overhanging climbing wall with holds, starting below the waterline. Falls land in the river, deep-water-soloing style.

## v0.16: Green calyx
**Direction:** "Isn't there a green leafy surround on a rose at the base of it? Should we make the yellow base more of that green color."
**What changed:** The float deck and float edge are now sepal green (a rose's calyx). Four pointed sepals reach out between the petals and curl down to the water. The gap at the gangway stays clear.

## v0.17: One slide, a petal about to fall
**Direction:** "I only want 1 petal slide. Make the petal slide look more like an unfurled petal. Use an actual rose that has opened up and the petals have started to almost fall off as a physical example." And: "The climbing jump petal looks like it is over the base somewhat. If you fell off, would you only fall in the water?"
**What changed:** One slide petal. It's built from the shape of a blown rose's outer petal: narrow at the attachment, rising to a lip, then flopping outward and down as a broad rounded blade with a spoon-shaped middle (the channel), edges rolling back under, a shallow notch at the tip, and faint veins. The north petal is an amphitheater again. On the jump petal, climbing holds now only exist where the fall line is over open water, at least 4 ft past the float edge; the sepals under it are removed.

## v0.18: Stem, dock to dock
**Direction:** "If you look at the map, it should actually have the stem connecting the end of the rose to the other dock under the bridge."
**What changed:** The stem is one continuous boom from the north edge of the Rose to the tip of the fire station pier under the Hawthorne, about 150 m, with the leaf floats along it. Site overlay redrawn to match, with the v0.17 petal roles (one slide, jump petal between, north petal glass).

## v0.19: Botany pass
**Direction:** "The green leaves at the base should be more symmetrical, I see only 2. Keep the look of a rose. Are there actual leaves on a rose stem, or just thorns? Make it more realistic. What could the thorns be, any use? Leaves just on the inside for now." Then: "What could the feathered edges provide? Maybe one sepal is a gentle slide into the water for kids too scared to do the big petal." And: "The stem, you should be able to walk to the leaves on."
**What changed:** Five symmetric sepals (reach shortened so none sits under the climbing wall's fall line). One sepal is a kids' slide: a shallow 1 m drop over 5.6 m with feathered side lobes as climb-out steps, placed at the low end of the jump petal; climbing holds moved to the tall half only. Stem leaves are now botanically right: four compound leaves on the swim side, each a stalk with five leaflet floats. Hooked thorns along the stem alternate sides, each with a solar nav light at the tip; they double as anchor cleats and swimmer grab handles. The stem carries a 1.5 m walkway with a rail on the river side. Reference sketch: `The Rose - Botany Reference.png`.

## v0.20: DJ, sound layer, and the weight question
**Direction:** "Let's start thinking of speaker and acoustic placement. Maybe the middle of the top of the flower is where the DJ booth is? Make a version of that, a version where the DJ is on a floating petal, and a version where they are more hidden." "Remove the weird square things under the climbing wall, and make the green sepal slide a similar size as the other ones." "I have to think about this weight and what the current dock can support. I won't be able to put any more pilings in. This might have to be a lot smaller as version 1.0, and we might have to think about somewhere bigger." Decision: build both, side by side.
**What changed:** DJ control with three placements: on the crown (booth around the flue, fire circle cleared), on a floating petal moored in the cove, or hidden in the service ring behind the north petal. A Sound layer toggle shows every speaker and transducer: 18 seat transducers, 10 hot-room zone speakers, subs, and the outdoor petal, crown, jump and kids' slide speakers with coverage cones. The flat plates under the slide are gone; the kids' sepal is the same reach as the other sepals with a scalloped feathered edge. New page: Rose full vs Rose 1.0 (62% scale stand-in): https://claude.ai/artifact/W9RF3nKMB3fnWVJ4Bh1PhY

## v0.21: The real site, and a nested DJ
**Direction:** "You still have the layout incorrect in relation to the bridges. Add to the mockup of the area so it's more representative of what it really looks like." And on the DJ: "It needs a more permanent solution. What would inspire the artist, a view of the bridge, or seeing the people to understand their vibe? The top of the rose, but not so pronounced that people are worshipping them. This is where controls for fire poofers, the fires, sound, smoke machines go."
**What changed:** Site rebuilt from the map with the Rose at the origin: riprap bank, Eastbank Esplanade, Audrey McCall Beach north of the dock, the two stacked I-5 decks right behind the bank, Central Eastside blocks, the Steelhead shed, the Hawthorne at 110 m with its east approach over I-5, Fire Station 21 and its pier, the Marquam 470 m south, a 300 m river, the seawall, Waterfront Park, Naito and downtown, RiverPlace marina. The stem now really runs Rose to fire pier. Fourth DJ option, 'nested': half-sunk in the crown's south rim, facing north over the lounge, cove and bridge, with the show desk. Two new cameras: Whole site, Under the bridge.
