## 4. The Jordan pack (what goes on the table)

Keep it to five pieces. She reads fast and she'll ask for depth where she wants it.

1. **One page: the idea and the ask.** The Rose in a paragraph, Elevated Tides as the brand, "sell time and belonging, never dates," the raise target, and the three questions we want her to answer.
2. **The ladder.** Seed, Petal (sliding), Bloom Card, First Bloom, Thorn Circle, Rose Patron. Prices, units, the four rules, the backstop.
3. **The stack.** The eight-line table from the Parts Catalog, target column, with the honest note that the round and the partners depend on the engineered number.
4. **The Parts Board concept.** Ten example parts across the price range (a stone, a seat, a plank, a fire rose, the slide, the stove) with their credit language, so she can feel how it reads to the public.
5. **The stage plan.** Tease, waitlist, reveal, build in public, with what's public at each stage and what triggers the next.

Plus the renders and the model link on a laptop, not printed.

**What we ask her (in this order):**
1. Does the ladder price right? Where would she raise or lower, and which product would she cut?
2. What's the press story: the first floating sauna shaped like a rose, the community-built one, the wedding venue on the water, or the Rose City finally getting its rose? Pick one lead.
3. How would she sequence the reveal so the count is big before the press runs, and what's the exclusive?
4. Which presenting partners would she call first, and would she make the intros?
5. What's the over-promise risk she sees (Plus Pool comparisons, the date question, the site), and what language defuses it?
6. What would a project scope for the stage 3 reveal cost, and would she want it? (Not the full retainer.)

**What we do not ask her:** to start anything, to call anyone, to post anything. This meeting ends with notes, not assignments.
