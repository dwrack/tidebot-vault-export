## The order things have to happen in
1. **Davey:** budget ceiling, petal character, roof lounge pick, go/no-go on the NA study.
2. **Team:** approve the Holman strategy so the site work can leave the vault.
3. **Site data:** soundings, current, sightlines, water quality. Cheap, and everything downstream needs it.
4. **Insurance broker call** on jumps, slide, climbing wall, no rails. Do this before engineering so we don't design what can't be insured.
5. **Naval architect feasibility study** (with the weights sheet and the model).
6. **BDS pre-application conference** and the DSL lease conversation, in parallel with the LOI.
7. **Engineer of record + architect**, real drawings, real quotes.
8. **Funding**: presale tease once the name is cleared and the LOI is signed; community round and bank on the engineered budget.
9. **Permits** (12-24 months), fabrication in parallel where it doesn't depend on piles.
10. **Build, launch, inclining test, open.**
