# The Rose: Parts Catalog and Raise Stack

_2026-09-29. Every piece of the Rose someone can fund, name, make or claim, priced in bands, and the stack that gets those pieces plus the founding ladder to a **$2.5-3.5M raise**. Companion to the Community Funding Plan (products, contingency, brand) and the Design Brief (section 11 budget, section 18 commissions). Bands are pre-engineering estimates, not bids. Confidential while Holman is off the record._

**The rule that makes this work:** the Rose has one author and many hands. Brands buy the functional parts (stove, slide, wall, gangway). People buy the intimate parts (a seat, a stone, a plank, a rib). Artists make the beautiful parts, and patrons pay for them to be made. Nothing carries a brand on the flower itself. Every part gets a credit, a place on the Parts Board, and the same refund-or-roll protection as every founder.

---

## 1. How to price a part

| Kind of part | Price rule | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Functional element (a brand sponsor) | Hard cost x 1.5 to 2. The sponsor covers the thing and a share of the room around it | Stove Crown, slide, gangway |
| Art commission (a patron funds an artist) | Artist fee + materials + 30%. The patron's money pays the artist; both get the credit | A fire rose: patron $15K, artist $10K |
| Intimate part (a person's name on it) | Priced on feeling, not cost. What a person will pay to be part of it forever | A seat $2,500, a stone $250, a plank $250 |
| In-kind (a trade supplies material or labor) | Credited at invoice value against the budget line. No cash, but it lowers what we have to raise | Timber, rubber, marine work |

Every part comes with: the credit (on the part, on the Founders' Wall, in the diary, in the opening press), founder benefits scaled to the price (Petal at $2,500+, Thorn Circle at $10K+, a First Bloom block at $25K+), and content: a diary chapter when it's made and installed.

## 4. The raise stack: $2.5-3.5M

Build estimate from the brief is $2.0-4.3M all in. The community raise is aimed at $2.5-3.5M across every strategy, with debt only for whatever is left after the engineered budget lands.

| Community investment round (Reg CF, revenue share) | $500K | **$700K** | $1.0M | Grotto did $1.3M; Allas Sea Pool EUR 0.8-1.1M. Runs after the engineered budget, on the presale book as proof |
| Grants | $25K | **$50K** | $75K | Travel Portland, RACC (window to Oct 28), others as found |
| **Total** | **$2.2M** | **$3.2M** | **$4.4M** | |

Seed subscriptions ($25-50/month) sit outside the table on purpose: they are cash flow now, and most of it converts into ladder purchases later, so counting both double-counts. Call it $200-400K of early cash in year one that reappears above as Petals and Bloom Cards.

Debt (SBA 7(a)/504) and the City's in-kind lease credits are also outside the table. They close the gap between the raise and the final engineered budget.

**Reading the stack honestly:** the low case at $2.2M leaves a real gap against a $3M+ build. What moves it most is the community investment round and the presenting partners, and both depend on having an engineered number and a signed site. So the order is: founding ladder and intimate parts first (they need only the story), functional elements and trades second (they need the design to lock), the Reg CF round and partners last (they need the budget and the LOI).

## 5. The Parts Board

One public page that lists every part, its band, and whether it is open, reserved or claimed, with the credit as it will read. The Adopt-a-Seat page at a theater, the brick wall at a Habitat build, the lanterns at the Japanese Garden: people know how this works. Rules:
- Parts go live in waves that match the stage plan (Funding Plan section 8). Intimate parts and commissions at the reveal; functional elements once the design locks; trades as we bid them.
- Every claimed part gets a diary chapter when it's made and another when it's installed. The board is also the content calendar.
- The credit language is fixed per part type: a name or a rose variety for intimate parts; "made possible by ___" for elements; "by ___ (artist), made possible by ___ (patron)" for commissions.
- A part can be shared: two friends split a seat; a family buys a leaf.
- No brand mark on the Rose's body. Brand credit lives on the Founders' Wall, the gangway, the trailer and the press.
