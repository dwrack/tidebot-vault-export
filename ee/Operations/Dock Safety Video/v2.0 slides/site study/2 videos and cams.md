# E&E site: what video + cameras actually show (study 2026-11-13)
Sources: 94 vault videos (4 frames each, contact sheets in sheets/), 3 Blink cams on mbp-2 (~/ee-occupancy/snaps, 900 stills 11/11-11/13, 1280x720 fisheye). Only 3 cameras exist in the occupancy set: "Lounge", "Ebb boat", "Wired Floodlight - 23BJ". (blink-clips/bike holds older "Ebb boat" corner sheets, not other networks.) Marked [seen] or [inferred].

## 1. Site order
- [seen] Lounge deck (cedar-decked dock with curved cedar wall/"hull" on the left, bench, rubber stair-mats, string lights, red bistro table, loungers) is attached directly to the sauna boat's bow/corner. Cam "Lounge" sits on the boat looking out along that deck.
- [seen] Sauna boat: black hull/wall, cedar door, cable-rail roof deck, gold/yellow-framed stair on its side, outboard at stern, sits at the END of the lounge deck, water side to its right (cam Ebb boat). Boat is tied up beside a dock walkway; bay is calm marina (no visible current in any shot).
- [seen] Across a water gap (~ one boat-width of open water) is a SEPARATE detached floating dock/float, grey weathered planks, with a steel ladder, orange stuff/tarps on it, and the private sauna(s) building behind it (cedar+dark roof, flat dark roof, chimney) in the cam stills 11/11. In the 11/11 frames it is under construction/demolition (lumber, tarp, workers) = the V2 area. 
- [seen] Beyond: moored sailboats, other houseboats, pilings (large dark creosote pilings stand up at the dock edges), a blue-tarped boat.
- Walkway/pier: [inferred] arrival is along the dock at the lounge end (far left of cam Lounge: wall w/ hooks, towels, jackets on the left curved wall). Never seen a gate/gangway/keypad in the stills; the dark door on the boat (right edge of Lounge frame) has a keypad/lock [seen in IMG_8707 close-up: black keypad lock on cedar/glass door].
- Upstream vs downstream: UNKNOWN. No flow visible; water is still. Cannot assign.

## 3. Sauna boat
- Hull: matte black vertical-clad wall/hull [seen], lower hull edge dark; low freeboard with a grey/white swim platform at stern [seen].
- Cladding: BLACK boards on the walls and hull; cedar used for door frame, deck, trim and the curved lounge wall. Gold/yellow powder-coat railing frame on roof-deck stair side + LED glow strip (members video, IMG_8752).
- Windows: large black-framed picture windows on water side + corner glass (interior shots); frosted glass in the door.
- Door: cedar frame, frosted/obscured glass panel, black keypad lock, faces the lounge deck [seen].
- Roof deck: flat, cable railing with cedar cap rail, stainless posts; stainless chimney flue pipes poke up through it (2 flues in IMG_8428). Sun loungers/chairs on roof [seen: ebb_roofdeck_zoom].
- Route up: open-tread timber stair (thick live-edge treads on stainless stringer, cable side rail) on the end of the boat; also a diagonal stair rail with gold frame seen from the water (vid_members_boat_wide).
- Motor: large outboard on the stern, between the boat and the shell float [seen vid_members_boat_wide].
- Signage: "EBB & EMBER FLOATING SAUNAS" white vinyl text + flame/drop logo on the black hull, lower side near roof deck (vid_members_hull_sign_ebbember). Older footage (Sep) of the same hull style carried "Elevated Tides" mountain logo plate on the roof-deck rail (IMG_8698/8737). [inferred] rename.
- Outdoor shower: on the boat's side deck, brass shower head, LED strip, cedar floor (IMG_8817). Cold bucket/tipping shower: cedar-box bucket on a post with pull rope on the lounge deck (cold-bucket-shower, portland-downpour).
- Orange life ring on the boat rail; sign "RING BUOY IS FOR EMERGENCY USE ONLY / NO LIFEGUARD" at the far float post [seen ebb_right_zoom].
