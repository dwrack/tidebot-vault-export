# Dock Safety Video — Storyboard v1 (2026-11-13)

Animated stick-figure safety explainer, same idea as the Ben's Bites "morning in my life" cutaway GIF but cleaner: one wide line-art cutaway of the sauna float, dock, ladders and river, camera pans scene to scene.

- Live artifact: https://claude.ai/artifact/8UNQzCW78WDitv6VnXQ7CH
- Source: `dock-safety-storyboard-v1.html` (this folder). Opens in any browser; space pauses, arrow keys step scenes.
- Status: DRAFT, waiting on Davey's scene cuts. Not shown to guests or team yet.

## Scenes (13)
1. Title: Before You Get In
2. Watch your step (wet deck, walk, hand free)
3. The river moves (Columbia always moving, downstream direction)
4. Never jump upstream of the float or dock (current pushes you under; downstream side next to a ladder)
5. Know your ladders (find it before you get in)
6. Cold shock (ladder first time, hold on, breathe out slow, 10 s)
7. Use a partner (one in, one watching)
8. Rescue pole (reach, don't jump; lie flat; pull to ladder)
9. Throw ring (throw past them, pull the line, weight back)
10. Don't stay in too long (1 to 3 min; shivering hard or numb hands = out)
11. Steam burns (arm's length, step back, never touch stove or rocks)
12. Clear head, no diving (added by Claude, not in Davey's list; cut if unwanted)
13. End checklist
