## Stovepipe: what to order (Torch TS-80F manual, pages 5 and 7, per stove)

- **6" flue collar**, centered on the stove's width, **10.58" from the finished front wall** (6.58" from the stove's back/intake face; corrected 2026-10-15).
- **Class A insulated chimney, 6" ID, rated to at least 800°F**, vertical termination. Standard UL103 HT Class A (DuraTech / Selkirk / Metal-Fab type) exceeds that.
- Terminate **at least 3 ft above the roof at the exit point, and at least 2 ft above any structure within 10 ft**. Watch this one: the two saunas sit about 4 ft apart and the privacy wall is drawn at 8 ft, so each flue has to clear the other sauna's roof and the wall by 2 ft, not just its own roof by 3 ft.
- **Minimum draft height 6 ft** from the top of the stove to the cap.
- Any horizontal run has to be double-wall or insulated pipe, no longer than half the vertical height, and each 90 costs 5 ft of height. Run it straight up.
- Flue and controls go in by a licensed pro (manual, page 3).

**Where the penetration lands (Rev C stove orientation, stove back 4" off the front wall, 12" off the wood side wall):** flue centerline about **10⅝" from the finished face of the front wall and 22⅝" from the finished face of the side wall** (corrected 2026-10-15: the manual's 10.58" is measured from the clearance line, i.e. the finished wall, not from the stove back; an earlier version of this note said 14⅝". Scan-based placement + rafter conflict in [[Private Saunas (V2) — Stove + Flue Placement from LiDAR (Oct 15 2026)]]), projected straight up through the ceiling and roof. Confirm with the stove sitting in its final spot before anyone cuts.

**Pipe length, per sauna:** `(ceiling height at the flue − flue collar height off the floor) + ceiling-to-roof-deck cavity + 36" above the roof + cap`, then round up to the next stock lengths and check the 2-ft-over-neighbors rule. Two numbers Grant has to tape on site: the collar height on the stove (not in the manual) and the as-built ceiling at the flue (drawings say 7'4½" / 8'2½", the 9/18 thread said 7'6" / 8'4" minus 4"; Rev K issue 12).

Kit per stove: stove-to-pipe adapter, double-wall connector from the collar to the ceiling (6" to combustibles), round ceiling support box (Class A starts here), Class A lengths, pitch-matched adjustable roof flashing for a shallow shed roof, storm collar, rain cap, roof brace if more than 5 ft shows above the roof, and an attic insulation shield if there's a cavity between the ceiling and the roof deck.

## Tile

Grant's inside dimension: **89" × 113"** per sauna (floor only; no tile outside the door, finish to a transition plate at the threshold).

Not in that number: tile up the cement-board stove wall (not decided), and extra if you pick a pattern with more cut waste.

**Slope:** flat under the stove, then fall from the benches forward to a tile line / drain. Davey's worry is the spigot side: a dumped bucket must not pool. Grant could build the fall with ripped tapered strips under the subfloor, but a real tile setter is the better answer. Two things that decide tile size: a point drain needs a compound slope, which wants small-format tile or mosaic; a linear drain lets you keep a single plane and bigger tile. Pick the drain before the tile.

## Benches (Thermory, from Grant's call with the rep)

- **1x4 thermo-aspen, not T&G**, rounded edges. Rep: 1x4 drains better than T&G, and thermo holds up commercially where plain aspen "doesn't wear as well."
- Board math: 6 boards at 3½" + ⅛" gaps = **21⅝"** (fits the 21½" lower bench). 7 boards = 25¼" (the 24" upper bench lands between 6 and 7 boards; pick one).
- Stock comes in 6 to 10 ft lengths by the foot, so little waste.
- Davey: a little darker than hoped, but fine.
- **Removable in sections**, tight to the wall but liftable, interlocking "fingers" so it reads as one solid bench when it's in. Goal: lift out and pressure-wash the room (tile floor makes that possible), reach every nook.
- Lead: ~2 weeks from the East Coast. Still to ask the rep: sanding (needed? how often?), cleaning schedule, and the black-wax questions from the 10/15 Apple note ([[Bench Sauna Material & Sauna Scouting — Apple Note (Oct 15 2026)]]).
- Not ordered yet. Price-compare against cedar at bench time; the cedar on site wasn't ordered with benches in mind. Grant wants a sit-down before bench build ("time to chat"): half-dowel edge, backrest profile, the oval backrest at the round window.

## Interior cladding

Davey wants the boards laid out in a **gradient**: center outward, lighter in front, darker toward the back, so the room reads deeper (Grant did a left-to-right gradient on the exterior). Davey will be on site to sort boards when the inside goes up; Grant doesn't want to do the layout alone.

## Lighting

- Inside: **LED strip in channel**, two runs on separate dimmable switches: one around the upper perimeter, one behind the backrest. Proper sauna-rated LED driver + strip (matches E2 to E4 in the Rev K electrical schedule).
- Dimmers: push-knob style like Davey's Airstream, not toggle switches.
- Outside: a couple of small art spotlights on a hung piece, nothing more. **No can lights, ever.**
