## 1. Full job description (website, Indeed, LinkedIn, Poached)

**Operations Assistant**
Ebb & Ember, Portland, OR
Type: Part-time to start (20-30 hrs/week), with a path to full-time. Contract through Jan 1, then a W-2 role if it's a fit.
Pay: $25-35/hr, plus an Ember 2 membership
Schedule: Flexible. Some weekdays remote, some weekends in person on the river, shifting with our events, parties, experiences and aufguss rituals.

**Your office is a floating sauna on the Columbia River, with a view of Mt. Hood.**

Ebb & Ember is Portland's floating sauna. Guests get hot, climb down into Cascade snowmelt, then sit on the deck doing nothing for a while. We opened January 1, 2026. Two new private saunas go on the water this fall.

Guests see the calm. You'd run the part they don't see.

**What you'll do**

*Be the face of Ebb & Ember*
- Appear in our social videos: first-timer guides, the safety video, member night highlights
- Work with our social media manager, who plans and edits. You host.

*Events and experiences*
- Help plan and run member nights, themed nights (like Women's Wednesday) and new experiences
- Own the checklist from invite to cleanup
- Host sauna sessions on some weekends: greet guests, collect waivers, walk first-timers through the ritual

*Partnerships*
- Reach out to hotels, local brands and creators we'd love to have out on the dock
- Keep outreach organized and follow up

*Guests and members*
- Answer guest and member emails, fast and warm
- Keep bookings, waivers and member records clean
- Read reviews and feedback, and share what guests are saying

*Keep things moving*
- Chase vendors and contractors until jobs are actually done
- Keep our project lists and Slack updates current
- Turn meetings into clear notes and next steps

**You're probably the person who**
- Has 1-3 years in front desk, studio or gym ops, hospitality, events, or as an assistant
- Reorganized the front desk at your last job without being asked
- Is an excellent communicator, and keeps what gets said out loud in sync with what's written in Slack
- Is comfortable learning, using and updating our shared business brain: the docs, lists and systems that run the place
- Doesn't mind a camera, and maybe secretly likes it
- Lives in Portland and can get to our dock on NE Bridgeton Rd on short notice

No sauna experience needed.

**Nice to have:** a wellness or hospitality background, a Portland network, basic video editing.

**What you get**
- $25-35/hr plus an Ember 2 membership
- A flexible schedule
- A path up (see below). We're growing, and we'd rather promote the person who already knows how it runs than hire from outside
- A job where "go take a sauna" counts as a break

**Where this goes (1, 3, 5, 10 years)**

| When | Role | What you'd own |
|---|---|---|
| Months 1-12 | **Operations Assistant** | A tour of everything: a few months each on guests + hosting, events + experiences, brand + growth, and ops. We find out what you're great at, and you pick the lane you'll own next |
| Year 1 | **Operations Coordinator** | Your own lane (events + experiences, or guest ops), a small budget you can spend without asking, and training our weekend hosts |
| Year 3 | **Operations Manager** | Day-to-day for Ebb & Ember: the host team, vendors, the event calendar, and a seat in planning meetings |
| Year 5 | **General Manager** | A full site, start to finish: team, budget, guest experience, and helping open new saunas and experiences |
| Year 10 | **Director of Operations** | Every site we run, and the people who run them |

Each step is earned, not scheduled. Show you can own the last one and the next one opens up.

**How to apply**
Email davey@ebbandember.com with your resume and a few lines about a time you took something messy and got it under control. Skip the cover letter. If it's a fit, we'll ask for a short phone video next, since being on camera is part of the job.

*Ebb & Ember is an equal opportunity employer. We welcome applicants of every background, age, gender identity, ability and body. If you need an accommodation at any step, tell us and we'll make it work.*

## 3. Where to post (goal: 100-200 applications)

Social alone won't get you there. Your followers are guests, not job seekers. The volume comes from job boards; social brings the best-fit people.

| Channel | Why | Rough expected applicants |
|---|---|---|
| **Website /careers page** (Jess builds on Squarespace) | Home base. Every other post links here. Adds credibility | Source of truth, not a volume driver |
| **Indeed** (free post, then sponsor ~$10/day for 2 weeks) | Biggest volume for part-time Portland admin/hospitality roles | 60-120 |
| **LinkedIn** (free job post) | Assistant + ops candidates, some quality | 20-40 |
| **Poached** | Portland-born hospitality job board, right crowd for hosting + events | 10-30 |
| **Craigslist Portland** (jobs, small fee) | Still pulls local part-time applicants | 10-25 |
| **IG post + stories, Reel with the hook line** | Best-fit people who already love the place | 10-30 |
| **Member email** (ActiveCampaign) | Members and their friends make great hires | 5-15 |
| **PSU / University of Portland career boards (Handshake)** | Grad students and recent grads looking for flexible hours | 10-20 |
| **Referrals:** Hannah, Jess, L, Kimberlynn, Linnea, Danesh | Warmest leads | 3-10 |

Rough total: 130-290 over 2 weeks. Numbers are estimates, not benchmarks.

**Order:** careers page live first, then Indeed + LinkedIn + Poached day 1, social + member email day 2, Craigslist + Handshake day 3. Close applications after 14 days.

**Heads-up:** 150 emails landing in davey@ gets messy fast. Use a subject line rule ("Operations Assistant - [Name]") and a Gmail filter/label, or a simple form on the careers page that feeds a sheet Claude can pre-sort.
