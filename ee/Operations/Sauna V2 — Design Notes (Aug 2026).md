## Guest Experience

### Sauna 1 — "Sunrise"

- Faces **east toward the mountain**. All seating oriented that direction.
- Positioning: sunrise moment, or daytime hang / sun sauna.
- More exposed, more social. Faces the swimming pool and the community sauna.
- Headline feature: massive east facade exposure to the mountain.
- **Builder's idea: a big round window.** He has done several. Method is order a square insulated pane, frame in an octagon, trim out to round. Photos attached of one finished on the coast last week.
- Also floated: a **porthole window in the sauna door**. Keeps privacy, matches the round picture window, plays to the "floating" concept. He thinks there is real PR in it.

### Sauna 2 — "Sunset"

- Faces **west**. (Note: the summary says "toward the ocean." We are on the Columbia. Correct this before it reaches marketing copy.)
- Positioning: date night, more private, moodier.
- Headline feature: **floor-to-ceiling windows on two adjacent sides** so guests feel like they are on top of the water.
- Private hang area with much less exposure to the larger sauna community.

## Differentiation Ideas (builder's, cheapest to most expensive)

1. **Inverse color theme.** Brand colors are raw cedar and black. Swap the accents: one sauna gets vertical raw cedar siding with black window trim and accents, the other gets black siding with raw cedar accents.
2. **Different interior woods.** His suggestion: clear Western Red Cedar for one, clear Port Orford for the other.
3. **Different roof styles.** Gabled on one (interior ceiling can still be flat for steam and heat dispersion), shed roof on the other.
4. **Cedar shingle exterior siding.** His words: most expensive in materials and labor, but damn it looks good.

## Conflict to Resolve: Interior Wood

The builder is proposing **clear Western Red Cedar and clear Port Orford** for the interiors. Our own materials research (see the Sauna V2 Build Plan memory) landed the other way for a commercial daily-use floating sauna:

- WRC aromatic oils off-gas at heat, and a meaningful share of guests find it irritating. Commercial liability.
- WRC warps and cups under daily commercial use, with gaps showing at 18 to 24 months.
- Prior recommendation was **exterior cladding only** for cedar, with Thermory Thermo-Aspen benches and Thermo-Alder or Thermo-Spruce walls from Disdero Lumber in Clackamas (503-239-8888).

Raise this with him before wood gets ordered. His experience may override the research, but it should be a decision, not a default.
