## What is edgy right now (research 10/19, mostly retailer and design-press trend pieces, so weight accordingly)

- **Finger / kit-kat tile** in a vertical stack is the designer format of the moment; glossy or reactive glazes make it move with the light.
- **Glazed lava stone** (Ranieri, Mistral, Made a Mano) is the premium "I've never seen that" material: volcanic stone with a fired enamel glaze, custom color, inherently heat-proof. TileBar's Lava Art line is the stock US version, including a fluted metallic copper.
- **3D / fluted relief tile** was the lead story at Coverings and Cersaie 2026 (Gardenia Righe, Daltile Contempee, Marazzi Terramater). Works because our cove light rakes down the wall.
- **Tile drenching** (one tile across floor and walls) and **oversized tonal checkerboard** are the two layout moves designers are pushing instead of subway.
- **Color**: warm and saturated beat grey; moss/fern green, terracotta, oxblood, and glazes with visible variation (zellige, crackle, reactive).
- **Scallop / fish-scale** and **ombre runs** are the "statement" shapes showing up in 2026 collections.

Sources: Bedrosians 2026 trends, Clé bathroom tile trends 2026, Interior Design "Coverings 2026", Freeyork Cersaie 2026, Livingetc 2026 bathroom tile, Homes & Gardens 2026, MaterialDistrict glazed lava tiles, Ranieri Lava Stone, TileBar Lava Art, Archello kit-kat mosaic, Remodelista Cowley Manor lava bath.

## Rules that don't move

- Everything in the corner sits inside the Torch clearance zone: ventilated non-combustible shield (cement board on 1″ spacers over the studs), tile on that, never on wood. Tile does not reduce the clearance by itself.
- Torch manual p.7: TS-80F = 4″ intake side, **12″ on the other three**. Rev AD draws 6″ per Davey; Paul at Torch has to put 6″ in writing for the 80F or the installer/warranty hold to 12″.
- Fresh air for the burner (manual p.9): vent of at least **7″ × 5″** to the space the intake box sits in, screened and rain-protected, plus 2½″ open under the whole box, flashing on wood below. The outdoor weather cover's louver is that vent.
- Floors: porcelain mosaic, wet DCOF ≥ 0.60, 2″ hex or 2×2 so the setter can slope to the point drain. Lava stone, zellige and real terracotta are wall-only here.
- Ask every wall-tile vendor for a fireplace-surround / heat rating in writing before ordering.

Concept, not a built place (a commenter asked). **Basalt slip:** finish-dependent. Flamed or bush-hammered basalt is R11-R12 / wet DCOF above 0.60 and does not wear smooth; honed/polished is slick wet. Tumbled 2x2 basalt mosaic grips via grout lines. Dense, heat-proof, dark. Costs: ~13 lb/sq ft at 3/4" (about 1,000 lb per floor), cold underfoot until warm, seal yearly. Get the tested wet DCOF for the exact finish (Crossville's manufactured basalt line lists 0.50-0.60, natural stone varies). Sources: flooringclarity basalt guide, Stone & Tile Shoppe flamed basalt, Crossville wet DCOF chart.
