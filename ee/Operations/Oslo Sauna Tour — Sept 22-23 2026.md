## Optional / drive-by

- **SALT (Langkaia)** — land-based arctic-pyramid sauna village with bar + concerts. Reported temporarily closed; worth a walk-past for the event/venue model. Next door to #1 and #2.
- **Kongen Marina sauna (Frognerkilen, 2 mi)** — quieter marina-side sauna, good example of a low-key location.
- **Nesodden Folkebadstu (ferry, ~5 mi)** — community wood-fired saunas incl. a mobile trailer sauna that rotates locations. Not floating, but the volunteer model is interesting.
- Out of range (>20 mi): Drøbak, Moss, Son.

## Suggested shape of the two days

- **Tue Sept 22 (downtown day):** Morning session at Oslo Badstuforening (Sukkerbiten), walk Langkaia to see the OBF village + SALT, afternoon/evening KOK session at Aker Brygge. Tjuvholmen (Mad Goats) is a 5-min walk from KOK Aker Brygge.
- **Wed Sept 23 (west day):** Fjordtokt at Lysaker in the morning (try to get the Caldarium boat), train to Sandvika for Pust Kadettangen afternoon. If KOKCRUISE has a Sept 22-23 departure, it's probably the single highest-value booking of the trip.

## What to study on board

Stove placement + rock mass, changing-room flow, cold-plunge access (ladder vs. pool), ventilation intake/exhaust routing, mooring + gangway, ticketing/self-service access codes, capacity per m2, how they handle towels/shoes/wet decking.
