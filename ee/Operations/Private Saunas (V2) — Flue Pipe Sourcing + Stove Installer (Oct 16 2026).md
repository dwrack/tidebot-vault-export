## 3. WiFi thermostats

The social sauna runs an **Inkbird ITC-308-WIFI** (Tuya-based; that's the unit the stove watchdog talks to, per [[Stove Watchdog (Inkbird Auto-Relight)]]). About $50 list on Amazon, $30 to $35 when it's on a coupon. Order two.

One catch before buying: the Torch ships with its own control box and a copper-bulb thermostat on a 5 ft lead, with a 20 ft wire to the valve box. The Inkbird works on the social sauna because of how it's wired into the Scandia. Ask Paul whether an external controller can sit in the Torch thermostat circuit (and how), or the Inkbirds end up as monitors only. Same call as the installer question above.
