### F. Thermopane (insulated) vs single pane for the DOOR

**My call: single 8-10mm tempered on the doors, insulated glass on the windows only.**
- Door is ~12 sq ft of a room with ~45-76 sq ft of glass already. The heat savings is small.
- IGU edge seals are the weak point at 194°F (same worry flagged on the windows 9/17). A fogged door is a replacement, and a swinging, slamming door works the seal harder than a fixed window.
- IGU is roughly double the weight on the hinges, which already sag in heat.
- Counterpoint: Finlandia (Portland) and a few brands (Etna, Scandia, Lux Thermo) sell double-pane sauna doors, so it's proven. Only worth it if we want less condensation on the door.
