# Private Saunas (V2) — Exterior Layout Review (Nov 10 2026)

Source: Jonah's two PDFs in #private-saunas-build 11/10 (saved in `Assets/V2 Build Drawings/2026-11-10 Jonah exterior sketches/`). Drawing with Option C: `Assets/V2 Build Drawings/2026-11-10 Exterior layout options (Jonah 1, Jonah 2, Option C).html`.

Davey's brief (11/10): openings are water views, west, north and east matter most. South faces the plunge basin and the houses, so it gets fenced.

## Jonah sketch 1 (closets as the divider, east entry)
- Works: no divider fence needed between sides; both fire spots sit on the north water edge.
- Against the brief: the entry door and public walkway land on the EAST edge, one of the three view edges. The east deck's table faces the walkway.
- Closets sit mid-float (x 13 to 18.5), so the south edge still needs a full fence and the middle of the deck is lost.
- West side's only fire is the pocket between the saunas, a 7.5 ft slot. The sunset edge gets two chairs and no fire.
- The pocket between the saunas is where the Rinnai heater (ordered 11/10, 24 in clear in front) and the manifolds go per Rev AO. A fire pit + loveseat there conflicts.
- Round pits are drawn; Rev AI/AJ/AK already moved to 42 in linear gas fire walls with glass guards. Guests at a round pit on the north edge means guests at the fast-water edge again (the thing Davey asked to stop in Rev AI).

## Jonah sketch 2 (doors moved south, dock loop)
- Fixes the east-edge entry. South edge becomes the service edge: 2 doors + 2 jump gates in the fence line. Matches the brief.
- The loop (south strip + diagonal arm + long east arm) is a lot of dock. The south strip is a shared walkway between the two sides, so swimmers from one side step past the other side's gate. Split it at the center wall into two private landings.
- The arms only earn their keep if the gangway has to land there; otherwise the buoy line (Operations/Swim + No-Wake Buoy plan) marks the basin.
- Check the east arm against the pilings and existing walkway flagged in Rev K (#18).

## Option C (recommended): south service edge
- Four 3 × 3 closets at the south edge straddling the center wall (W change + shower west of the wall, E change + shower east). They are the privacy divider and 6 ft of the south fence.
- Entry door per side on the south edge next to the closets. Guest flow: door, change, shower, sauna door, fire + view edge, jump gate onto own landing.
- West fire wall on the sunset edge of the West deck (Rev AK as drawn). East fire wall across the east strip's north end (Rev AJ), loungers facing east, 42 in rail on the east edge. Nothing solid on W, N or E.
- Pocket between the saunas = utility core (heater, manifolds, slat screens), with a rail at the north edge. If Davey wants it for guests instead, the heater has to move and the pocket needs a rail or fire wall.
- South: 6 ft cedar slat fence, broken only by the 2 doors and 2 jump gates. Table for 4 on the east deck against the fence.
- Dock: one strip along the south edge split at the center wall into two private landings, each with its own ramp off the walkway. No loop unless the gangway needs it.
