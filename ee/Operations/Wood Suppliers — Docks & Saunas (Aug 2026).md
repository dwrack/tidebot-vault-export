# Wood Suppliers — Docks & Saunas (Aug 2026)

Researched 2026-08-27 for dock lumber (V2 platform work) and sauna-grade wood (Sunrise/Sunset interiors). All Portland-area or Oregon except one just across the river in Vancouver.

## Top picks

### 1. Sustainable Northwest Wood — SE Portland (docks, best fit)
- 2701 SE 14th Ave, Portland, OR 97202 · (503) 239-9663 · snwwood.com
- **Restoration juniper decking**: Eastern Oregon juniper from watershed restoration projects. Naturally rot-resistant with NO chemical treatment, outlasts cedar/redwood outdoors. That matters for us: untreated wood over the Columbia means no CCA/ACQ leaching into the river, and the restoration-sourcing story fits the brand perfectly.
- Also stocks FSC cedar, PNW hardwoods, decking, siding.
- Purpose-driven lumberyard, sources from a dozen family sawmills in Eastern Oregon. Easy first call.

### 2. Zip-O-Log Mills — Eugene (structural dock timbers)
- 2235 W 6th Ave, Eugene, OR 97402 · (541) 343-7758 · zipolog.com
- Family mill since 1944. 100% Douglas fir timbers, posts, joists, stringers, custom cuts up to 52 ft. This is the call for platform framing / structural members.

### 3. NW Immersion — Vancouver, WA (sauna wood, closest sauna-specific mill)
- 5305 NE 121st Ave, Vancouver, WA · call/text (360) 360-6968 · nwimmersion.com
- Mills clear-grade Western Red Cedar T&G for saunas, kiln-dried, ~$3.50/linear ft (3" reveal). They also build saunas, so they know heat-stable stock. 20 min from the marina.
- One consideration: they're technically a competitor-adjacent builder (PNW sauna market). Fine as a lumber source, just don't overshare V2 design details.

### 4. Nova USA Wood — Portland (marine timbers + thermally modified)
- novausawood.com, Portland-based importer/distributor
- Marine lumber and timbers for docks/piers/marinas, hardwood decking, and thermally modified wood (Ambara, a cedar alternative). Good backup if juniper or fir pricing doesn't work.

### 5. Conrad Lumber Co — Portland metro (pressure-treated Doug fir)
- conradlumberco.com · sister: Conrad Forest Products (conradfp.com), 3 PNW facilities
- Factory-direct pressure-treated Doug fir decking and timbers. Cheapest structural route, BUT treated lumber over the river is a chemical-leach and brand question. Use for anything not in/over water; prefer juniper or naturally durable species for wet-contact decking.

## Sauna wood notes
- Interior benches/walls: clear (knot-free) Western Red Cedar is the default; thermally modified aspen/alder is the low-sap, splinter-free alternative (Arbor Wood Co., Thermory USA, ProSaunas — national, ship in).
- The V2 interior-wood conflict (see [[project_sauna_v2]] notes) still needs a decision; NW Immersion or Sustainable NW Wood can quote both directions.

## Suggested first moves
1. Call Sustainable NW Wood re: juniper decking stock + truckload pricing.
2. Call Zip-O-Log re: Doug fir structural timbers, delivery to Portland.
3. Call/text NW Immersion re: clear WRC T&G availability for a sauna build this fall.

## Second tier — worth talking to (added after social check)
- **Salvage Works** — 2024 N Argyle St, Portland · salvageworkspdx.com · IG @salvageworkspdx ~13K. Reclaimed PNW barnwood/lumber, open 7 days. Not structural dock wood, but perfect for interior accents: changing room, lounge, signage, retail counter. Portland design audience, strong collab partner.
- **Goby Walnut** — Aurora, OR · gobywalnut.com · IG @gobywalnut ~57.6K. Oregon black walnut slabs/live edge. One statement piece (lounge table, check-in counter) + a build reel tagging them reaches a 57K maker/design audience. Not for sauna interiors (walnut doesn't like sauna heat).
- **In the Sticks Juniper Sawmill** — Fossil, OR (orforestdirectory.com listing). One of the actual mills behind SNW Wood's juniper. Direct call worth it if V2 needs truckload volume; retail through SNW otherwise.
- **JNB Inc.** — Eastern Oregon juniper logger/mill, jnbjuniper.com. Backup direct juniper source.
- **TerraMai** — White City, OR · terramai.com · IG ~4.2K. Reclaimed architectural wood, B2B/spec-driven. Only if a designer spec calls for it.
