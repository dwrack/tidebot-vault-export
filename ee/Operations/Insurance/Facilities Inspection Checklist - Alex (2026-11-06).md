# Facilities Inspection Checklist (Alex)

DRAFT 2026-11-06 for Davey/Jonah to approve before Alex starts using it. Written for the main floating sauna as it is today. Add the two private saunas when they open.

**Rule:** do the weekly check every week, no exceptions. That frequency is what the safety plan promises Capitol, so a missed week is a gap a lawyer will find. Log every check (date, initials, anything failed, what was done). A photo of anything that failed goes in #maintenance with the job ID.

**Anything that fails on fire, gas, alarms, rescue gear or the ladders = close the sauna until fixed.** Text Davey and Jonah right away.

---

## Weekly walk-through (every week, about 20 to 30 minutes)

### Sauna and stove
- [ ] Stove lights and heats normally; flame looks steady, no unusual sound or smell
- [ ] Fresh-air vent at the base of the stove and the exhaust vent near the stove are both clear (nothing stored or stacked in front of them)
- [ ] No gas smell anywhere on the boat (if any smell: shut the gas off, close the sauna, call the gas company)
- [ ] Nothing combustible near the stove (towels, bags, wood)
- [ ] Temperature sensor uncovered and reading normally
- [ ] Benches, backrests and floor: no loose boards, splinters or raised screws
- [ ] Sauna door opens freely from inside and outside, no sticking

### Alarms
- [ ] Gas and carbon monoxide alarm in the sauna: press test, it sounds

### Fire extinguishers (2)
- [ ] Sauna, just inside the first entry door: in place, unblocked, gauge in the green, pin and tag on
- [ ] Lounge: in place, unblocked, gauge in the green, pin and tag on

### Rescue gear
- [ ] Throw ring at the fore of the boat, next to the ladder: present, rope attached and not tangled
- [ ] Throw ring at the aft of the boat, next to the ladder: present, rope attached and not tangled
- [ ] Extendable rescue pole on each side of the boat: present, extends and locks
- [ ] First aid kit: present and stocked

### Ladders, decks and docks
- [ ] Boat ladders (fore and aft): solid, bolts tight, rungs not slick
- [ ] Downriver dock ladder: solid, bolts tight, rungs not slick
- [ ] Decks, roof deck and railings: no loose boards, rails solid, no trip hazards
- [ ] Dock walkway to the sauna: boards solid, lights working at night

### Water and electrical
- [ ] WaveLink Secure: app shows monitoring and no alerts
- [ ] Cold shower tower: runs, drains, no leaks
- [ ] No loose cords or exposed wiring

### Signs
- [ ] All safety signs in place and readable (no lifeguard, strong current, deep water, house rules, emergency 911 address)

### Cameras
- [ ] Dock cameras online in the Blink app

---

## Monthly (first week of each month, on top of the weekly)
- [ ] Look over the stove, vent and gas line connection; soap-test the gas connection at the stove
- [ ] Throw ring ropes: no fraying, full length
- [ ] Ladder mounts: check underneath for corrosion or loose hardware
- [ ] Pilings and dock connections at the sauna: secure
- [ ] First aid kit: check expiry dates, restock
- [ ] Alarm batteries: replace if the unit shows low or it has been 12 months

## Yearly (put dates in the calendar)
- [ ] Fire extinguisher(s) professionally inspected and tagged
- [ ] Gas stove serviced by a licensed gas technician
- [ ] Replace alarms at the age the maker says (usually 7 to 10 years)
