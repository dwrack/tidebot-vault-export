EBB & EMBER FLOATING SAUNAS: GUEST SAFETY PLAN
Elevated Tides Experiences LLC, DBA Ebb & Ember Floating Saunas
Elevated Tides Marina, 173 NE Bridgeton Rd, Portland, OR 97211, on the Columbia River
Prepared for Capitol Specialty Insurance Corporation (policy effective 11/6/2026)

This plan describes our guest rules, the equipment on site and our emergency procedure as of November 2026. Sauna and open-water activities carry inherent risks that guests accept in the signed release. This plan does not guarantee safety.

1. HOW WE OPERATE
- A floating sauna and lounge moored at Elevated Tides Marina, heated by a natural gas stove (Scandia DX6KN). Two small private saunas are under construction on the same docks and are not open.
- Open 6 AM to 10 PM. Sessions are booked online in advance.
- Social sessions are capped at 8 guests. Private buyouts are capped at [10 or 12, VERIFY with Jonah; shared record says 10].
- The sauna is unstaffed and there is no lifeguard. Guests use a door code for their session.
- Hosted events follow these same rules.

2. BEFORE THE VISIT
- Each guest is required to sign our waiver in their own name before their session. The waiver link is in every booking confirmation and reminder message.
- Whoever books for a group, and any event host, is responsible for every guest in the group signing their own waiver.
- The day before each session, every booking gets a reminder with the waiver link, the address and what to bring.
- What to bring: a swimsuit, two towels (one to sit on, one to dry off) and a full water bottle.
- No alcohol before or during a session. No animals.
- [VERIFY it is on the waiver or site, else cut] Ages 13 and up, private bookings only, with a parent or guardian present who has signed the waiver. Children must be supervised at all times.
- Guests who are pregnant, have a heart or blood pressure condition, or take medication that affects heat tolerance should check with a doctor first.

3. HOW TO USE THE SAUNA
- Enter calm and hydrated. The sauna typically runs at 175 to 190 F.
- Heat for up to about 15 minutes. Pour water on the stones one scoop at a time, after checking with others. Fresh water only.
- Cool off slowly, then rest 5 to 10 minutes. Repeat 1 to 3 rounds.
- Leave the heat right away if dizzy, sick or light-headed.
- Do not touch the heater controls or cover the temperature sensor.

4. COLD PLUNGE AND RIVER RULES
- No lifeguard. Guests enter the water at their own risk.
- Enter by the ladders only, feet first, one at a time. No diving.
- Never plunge alone.
- Never swim under the sauna, floats or docks.
- Watch for boats. Stay close to the sauna. The current is strong and the water is deep.
- Docks may be slippery when wet.
- If in trouble: lean back, float, breathe, call for help.
- Guests who prefer not to enter the river can use the cold shower tower.

6. VENTILATION
- Fresh air enters through a vent at the base of the gas stove.
- Air leaves the sauna through a vent near the stove.
- The gas stove draws its combustion air from outside the sauna.

7. MAINTENANCE
- A facilities manager maintains the site through a shared work list. [VERIFY, else cut:] The gas stove is serviced by a licensed gas technician.
- The sauna is closed when fire, gas, alarm, rescue gear or ladder equipment is out of service.
- Guests can reach us by text or call at 503-308-1293.

8. EMERGENCIES
- Call 911: Ebb & Ember floating sauna, Elevated Tides Marina, 173 NE Bridgeton Rd, Portland, on the Columbia River.
- Person in trouble in the water: throw a ring or reach with a pole, do not go in after them, call 911.
- Medical issue: call 911, use the first aid kit, move the person out of the heat.
- Fire or alarm: everyone out, call 911.
- Report any incident to management the same day.

9. ENFORCEMENT
- Guests who break these rules may have their session ended and be refused future bookings.
