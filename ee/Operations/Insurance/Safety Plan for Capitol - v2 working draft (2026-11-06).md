EBB & EMBER FLOATING SAUNAS: GUEST SAFETY PLAN
Elevated Tides Experiences LLC, DBA Ebb & Ember Floating Saunas
Elevated Tides Marina, 173 NE Bridgeton Rd, Portland, OR 97211, on the Columbia River
Prepared for Capitol Specialty Insurance Corporation (policy renewal effective 11/6/2026)

1. HOW WE OPERATE
- A floating sauna and lounge moored at Elevated Tides Marina, heated by a natural gas stove (Scandia DX6KN). Two small private saunas are under construction on the same docks.
- Open 6 AM to 10 PM. Sessions are booked online in advance.
- Social sessions are capped at 8 guests. Private sessions are capped at 12.
- The sauna is unstaffed and there is no lifeguard. Guests get a door code for their session.
- Hosted events follow these same rules.

2. BEFORE THE VISIT
- Each guest must sign our waiver in their own name before their session. The door code is sent only after the waiver is signed.
- Whoever books for a group, and any event host, must make sure every guest in the group signs their own waiver.
- The day before each session, every booking gets a reminder email with the waiver link, health warnings, river rules, the 911 address and what to bring.
- What to bring: a swimsuit, two towels (one to sit on, one to dry off) and a full water bottle.
- No alcohol before or during a session. No animals.
- Ages 13 and up, private bookings only, with a parent or guardian present who has signed the waiver. Children must be supervised at all times.
- Guests who are pregnant, have a heart or blood pressure condition, or take medication that affects heat tolerance should check with a doctor first.

3. HOW TO USE THE SAUNA
- Enter calm and hydrated. The sauna typically runs at 175 to 190 F.
- Heat for up to about 15 minutes. Pour water on the stones one scoop at a time, after checking with others.
- Cool off slowly, then rest 5 to 10 minutes. Repeat 1 to 3 rounds.
- Leave the heat right away if dizzy, sick or light-headed.
- Never use the sauna or the cold water alone.
- Do not touch the heater controls or cover the temperature sensor.

4. COLD PLUNGE AND RIVER RULES
- No lifeguard. Guests enter the water at their own risk.
- Enter by the ladders only, feet first, one at a time. No diving.
- Never swim under the sauna, floats or docks.
- Watch for boats. Stay in the plunge zone. The current is strong and the water is deep.
- Docks may be slippery when wet.
- If in trouble: lean back, float, breathe, call for help.
- Guests who prefer not to enter the river can use the cold shower tower.

5. SAFETY EQUIPMENT ON SITE
- First aid kit.
- Throw rings at the fore and aft of the sauna boat, next to the ladders.
- An extendable rescue pole on each side of the boat.
- Ladders on the boat, plus a ladder on the dock downriver of the sauna boat.
- Combination gas and carbon monoxide alarm in the sauna.
- Two fire extinguishers: one just inside the first entry door of the sauna, and one in the lounge.
- Cold shower tower on the boat.
- WaveLink Secure in-water electrical monitoring.
- Security cameras on the docks.

5A. SAFETY SIGNAGE (new sign package, being produced for installation within the next few weeks)
- Sauna and River Safety Guide at the entry: cold, fast river, no staff or lifeguard on site; health warnings; no alcohol; enter slowly by the ladder, never plunge alone; no diving; never swim under the sauna, floats or docks; watch for boats; stay in the plunge zone; children supervised; what to do in trouble in the water; how to call 911 with the full address.
- At the water entry: WARNING, strong current, cold water, swim at your own risk, no lifeguard on duty. NO DIVING signs, plus a NO DIVING marking on the deck at the plunge edge.
- DEEP WATER, 25 TO 40 FEET, depth changes with the river.
- THROW RING marker above each ladder.
- CAUTION: Docks may be slippery when wet, at the bottom of the gangway and at the entrance.
- Steam sign in each sauna: check with others first, start with one scoop, refill with fresh water only.
- Experience Guide outside each sauna door: heat 5 to 15 minutes, cool 1 to 3 minutes, rest 5 to 10 minutes and drink water.
- Security cameras in use.
- Safety signs use the ANSI Z535 WARNING format and are lit or reflective where they are read after dark.

6. VENTILATION
- Fresh air enters through a vent at the base of the gas stove.
- Air leaves the sauna through a vent near the stove.
- The gas stove draws its combustion air from outside the sauna.
- The vents are checked weekly to make sure nothing blocks them.

7. CHECKS AND MAINTENANCE
- Our facilities manager inspects the sauna, stove, alarms, rescue gear, ladders, decks and signs at least once a week using a written checklist, and logs each inspection. Anything that fails on fire, gas, alarms, rescue gear or ladders closes the sauna until it is fixed.
- Fire extinguishers are inspected weekly and professionally serviced yearly.
- Our maintenance technician cleans and services the gas stove at least once a month.
- Guests can reach us by text or call at 503-308-1293.

8. EMERGENCIES
- Call 911: Ebb & Ember floating sauna, Elevated Tides Marina, 173 NE Bridgeton Rd, Portland, on the Columbia River.
- Person in trouble in the water: throw a ring or reach with a pole, do not go in after them, call 911.
- Medical issue: call 911, use the first aid kit, move the person out of the heat.
- Fire or alarm: everyone out, call 911.
- Report any incident to management the same day.

9. ENFORCEMENT
- Guests who break these rules can be asked to leave.
