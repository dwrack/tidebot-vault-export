# Pricing matrix, live from Mon 11/23/2026

Refreshed 2026-11-14 from Jonah's Slack posts in #general: "New pricing is set" (thread 1793339884), the 11/6 gift card update, and "a few pricing changes since the review doc" (11/14). Latest decision wins. Today's Periode prices stay until 11/23.

## Single sessions (start time sets the price)
| | 1 hour | 2 hours |
|---|---|---|
| Weekday Days (Mon to Fri, starting before 5 PM) | $35 | $50 |
| Evenings & Weekends (weekdays from 5 PM, Sat, Sun, holidays) | $45 | $60 |

- "1 hour" is 45 minutes on the deck plus 15 to change; "2 hours" is 105 plus 15.
- Holidays: New Year's Day, MLK Day, Memorial Day, July 4, Labor Day, Thanksgiving and the day after, Christmas Eve, Christmas, New Year's Eve.
- Social Sauna: up to 8 guests, 18+, bookable 21 days out.

## Specialty
- Guided Banya, Mondays 6 to 8 PM: $65 (Anytime members $10, Daytime members $60). Self-guided Banya 8 to 10 PM: $60. 8 seats. Partner-hosted.
- Friday Sunrise Swim with WaterStrong, 6 to 9 AM: $25. Members get in on their membership.
- No discount codes on specialty sessions.

## Private rentals (1 hour / 2 hours)
| | Weekday Days | Evenings & Weekends |
|---|---|---|
| Group Session (whole floating sauna, up to 12) | $350 / $500 | $450 / $600 |
| East or West Sauna (up to 4, opening December) | $125 / $200 | $175 / $250 |

- Booking windows: public 60 days, members 90 (members book privates 30 days before everyone else).
- Cancel privates up to 48 hours before for a gift card; move up to 3 hours before.

## Passes and gift cards
- First Plunge: 3 visits in 45 days for $99, two people $180. Any Social Sauna, 1 or 2 hours, plus the Friday swim. Not Banya or privates. Join within 45 days and the $99 comes off the membership.
- Gift card: any amount, face value, never expires, works on any visit or private. Replaces punch passes and Sauna Credit (11/6). Bonuses run as short promotions only.
- Old punch passes stay good: one punch is one visit of any length.

## Memberships (one visit a day)
| | 3-month plan, then monthly | 12-month plan, billed monthly |
|---|---|---|
| Daytime (Weekday Days) | $125/mo | $100/mo |
| Anytime (any time) | $175/mo | $150/mo |

- Guest passes by plan: Daytime 1 a month (Weekday Days), Anytime 2 a month (any time). Unused passes roll over on every plan; up to 3 guests a visit.
- Friends beyond passes: $10 off Social Sauna: $25 / $40 Weekday Days, $35 / $50 evenings and weekends. Not on guided Banya, the Friday swim or privates.
- Daytime members pay $35 / $50 for evening and weekend visits.
- Every member: 10% off any private; book privates 30 days before the public.
- Tenant codes on Daytime 3-month: $93.75 floating homes, $112.50 slips.
- Current members: Ember 1 moves to Daytime at $100, no commitment, can upgrade to Anytime at $150 with no commitment until March. Ember 2 moves to Anytime at $150, no commitment. Old Ember plans end with each member's February payment.

## Discounts (single seats and 3-month plans only; never stack)
- Floating home neighbors (26 homes): 25%. Boat slip tenants: 10%. Zip codes 97217 and 97211: 10%. Partner of a member: 25% (3-month only).
- Never on privates, specialty sessions, gift cards, First Plunge or 12-month plans.
