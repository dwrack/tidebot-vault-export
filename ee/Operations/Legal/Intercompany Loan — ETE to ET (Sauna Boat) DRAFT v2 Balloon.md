# INTERCOMPANY LOAN AGREEMENT AND SECURED PROMISSORY NOTE (BALLOON)

**Principal Amount: $250,940.00**
**Effective Date: March 31, 2026**
**Maturity Date: 5 years from the first payment date (balloon)**

This Intercompany Loan Agreement and Secured Promissory Note (this "Note") is made as of the Effective Date stated above by and between:

**Lender:** **Elevated Tides LLC**, an Oregon limited liability company ("Lender" or "ET"); and

**Borrower:** **Elevated Tides Experiences LLC**, an Oregon limited liability company doing business as Ebb & Ember ("Borrower" or "ETE").

Lender and Borrower are each a "Party" and together the "Parties."

## Recitals

A. Borrower operates a floating sauna and lounge experience on the Columbia River near Portland, Oregon, under the brand Ebb & Ember.

B. During 2025 and the first quarter of 2026, Lender advanced funds to or for the benefit of Borrower to fund the purchase, construction, and build-out of Borrower's floating sauna vessel and associated lounge structure (collectively, the "Sauna Project").

C. As reflected on Borrower's balance sheet dated March 31, 2026, the aggregate outstanding amount so advanced and not repaid is **Two Hundred Fifty Thousand Nine Hundred Forty and 00/100 Dollars ($250,940.00)** (the "Principal Amount"), recorded as an intercompany loan payable by Borrower to Lender.

D. The Parties enter into this Note to memorialize the existing indebtedness described above, to set commercially reasonable repayment terms with a reduced periodic payment and a balloon at maturity, and to grant Lender a security interest in the vessel financed by the advances.

NOW, THEREFORE, for value already received, the Parties agree as follows.

## 1. Promise to Pay

Borrower promises to pay to the order of Lender the Principal Amount of **$250,940.00**, together with interest accrued on the unpaid balance as provided below, on the terms set out in this Note.

## 2. Interest

Interest accrues on the unpaid Principal Amount from the Effective Date at a fixed annual rate of **[4.50%]** per year **[PLACEHOLDER — insert the Applicable Federal Rate (mid-term, the rate category for loans over 3 years and up to 9 years) in effect for the month of the Effective Date, as published by the IRS; confirm compounding convention with the CPA]**. Interest is computed on the basis of a 360-day year of twelve 30-day months. The rate stated in this Section is intended to equal or exceed the applicable AFR so that no interest is imputed to either Party under Section 7872 of the Internal Revenue Code.

## 3. Repayment (Balloon)

3.1 **Reduced monthly payments.** Borrower shall pay **fifty-nine (59)** equal consecutive monthly installments of **$1,587.57** each. This installment is calculated as if the Principal Amount were fully amortized over a twenty (20) year term at the rate stated in Section 2, which produces a lower periodic payment than full amortization over the actual term.

3.2 **Balloon payment at maturity.** On the **Maturity Date** (the date of the 60th scheduled payment, being five (5) years after the first payment date), Borrower shall pay a single balloon payment equal to the entire remaining unpaid Principal Amount plus all accrued and unpaid interest. Based on the rate stated in Section 2, that balloon payment is approximately **$209,114.93** (approximately **$208,333.67** of remaining principal plus the final month's interest). The exact balloon equals the actual outstanding balance on the Maturity Date.

3.3 **Payment dates.** The first installment is due on **[first payment date — e.g., the first day of the month following execution]**, and each subsequent installment is due on the same day of each following month. The balloon is due on the Maturity Date. The complete payment schedule is attached as **Exhibit A**. (If the AFR confirmed under Section 2 differs from the placeholder, Exhibit A, the monthly installment, and the balloon will be recalculated.)

3.4 **Application of payments.** Each payment is applied first to accrued interest and then to principal.

3.5 **Prepayment.** Borrower may prepay all or any part of the Principal Amount at any time without penalty or premium, including paying the balloon early. Partial prepayments are applied to principal and do not reduce or postpone the scheduled monthly installments, but reduce the balloon due at maturity.

3.6 **No obligation to refinance.** Borrower acknowledges that a substantial portion of the Principal Amount remains due at maturity and that Lender has no obligation to extend, renew, or refinance the Note. **[Counsel: consider whether the Parties want an option to renew or convert at maturity given the related-party context.]**

3.7 **Form of payment.** Payments are made in lawful U.S. currency to Lender at the address Lender designates in writing.

## 4. Security Interest

4.1 **Grant.** To secure Borrower's obligations under this Note, Borrower grants to Lender a security interest in the floating sauna vessel financed by the advances described in the Recitals, together with all engines, machinery, equipment, fixtures, appurtenances, and proceeds thereof (the "Collateral"). The Collateral is described in **Exhibit B**.

4.2 **Perfection.** Borrower shall cooperate in the filing of a UCC-1 financing statement and, to the extent the vessel is or becomes a documented or state-registered vessel, in any preferred ship mortgage or state lien recording necessary to perfect Lender's security interest. **[Confirm with maritime/UCC counsel which perfection method applies based on the vessel's documentation or Oregon registration status.]**

4.3 **Covenants regarding Collateral.** Until this Note is paid in full, Borrower shall keep the Collateral insured against customary risks, shall not sell or further encumber the Collateral without Lender's prior written consent, and shall maintain the Collateral in good operating condition, ordinary wear and tear excepted.

## 5. Events of Default

Each of the following is an "Event of Default": (a) Borrower fails to pay any amount, including the balloon, within fifteen (15) days after it is due; (b) Borrower breaches any other covenant in this Note and does not cure within thirty (30) days after written notice; (c) Borrower becomes insolvent, makes an assignment for the benefit of creditors, or becomes the subject of a bankruptcy proceeding; or (d) the Collateral is sold, transferred, or further encumbered in violation of Section 4.

## 6. Remedies

On an Event of Default, Lender may, by written notice, declare the entire unpaid balance of principal and accrued interest immediately due and payable, and may exercise all rights and remedies of a secured party under the Oregon Uniform Commercial Code and applicable maritime law. Borrower shall pay Lender's reasonable costs of collection, including reasonable attorney fees, whether or not suit is filed.

## 7. Authorization and Related-Party Acknowledgment

7.1 The Parties acknowledge that Lender and Borrower are under common ownership and that this Note is a related-party transaction. The Parties intend that its terms are commercially reasonable and consistent with terms an independent lender would offer.

7.2 Borrower represents that the execution, delivery, and performance of this Note, including the incurrence of the indebtedness, the capital expenditure it financed, the grant of the security interest, and the resolution of the conflict of interest inherent in a related-party loan, have been authorized by the Consent of a Supermajority of Units of Borrower in accordance with §4.5(k), §4.5(l), §4.5(m), and §4.5(u) of Borrower's Operating Agreement, and approved in advance as required by §4.9. Evidence of that authorization is attached as **Exhibit C**.

7.3 Lender represents that the execution, delivery, and performance of this Note have been authorized by its Manager in accordance with its Operating Agreement.

8.1 **Governing law.** This Note is governed by the laws of the State of Oregon, without regard to conflict-of-laws principles.

8.4 **Amendment.** This Note may be amended only in a writing signed by both Parties and, to the extent required, authorized under Borrower's Operating Agreement.

8.5 **Waiver.** No delay or omission by Lender in exercising any right is a waiver of that right.

8.6 **Severability.** If any provision is unenforceable, the remaining provisions stay in effect.
