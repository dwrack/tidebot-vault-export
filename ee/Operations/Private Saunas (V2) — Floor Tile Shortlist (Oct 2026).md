# Private Saunas (V2): Floor Tile Shortlist (Oct 2026)

Compiled 2026-10-16 from web research only; nothing has been seen in person or priced. Two floors, Grant's inside dimension 89" × 113" each, order ≈155 sq ft plus the stove-zone wall shield if that goes tile (see [[Private Saunas (V2) — Stove + Flue Placement from LiDAR (Oct 15 2026)]], sheet 5/7). Point drain is the current plan, which means mosaic (2×2 or 2" hex) so the setter can build a compound slope.

## The spec to hand the tile setter

| Item | Ask for | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Material | Glazed or through-body **porcelain**, water absorption ≤0.5% | heat, wet/dry cycling, cleanable |
| Slip | **Wet DCOF ≥ 0.60** (ANSI A326.3) or **R11 / barefoot A+B+C** (DIN 51097). 0.42 is the code minimum for shoes on a level floor; this is bare feet on a sloped wet floor | commercial, barefoot, soapy water |
| Format | 2×2 or 2" hex mosaic on mesh, matte or textured | grout lines = grip, slope to a point drain |
| Color | mid-to-dark warm neutral: charcoal / basalt, warm grey, sand, or a muted moss green. Nothing white, nothing polished, nothing with a strong printed pattern | cedar reads warm; dark floors make it glow and hide water spotting |
| Grout | epoxy or high-performance cement grout, dark, sealed | pressure washing, no staining |
| Setting | modified thinset rated for heated floors; sheet-membrane waterproofing under it; schedule the setter before the door install (site walk sequence) | |

Skipped: natural stone (spalls and stains at sauna temps, hard to keep slip-safe), anything polished or lappato, anything white or light grey (shows every footprint and body-oil mark in a commercial sauna).

## What I'd do

1. Take the spec table to Bedrosians and CFM in one afternoon, pull True Black/Grey, IMPACT, and one StepWise greige. Get wet DCOF sheets for each.
2. Order a Fireclay sample box in a moss green, a warm charcoal and a terracotta. Hold them against the cedar in the room at dusk, which is when the saunas sell.
3. Let the tile setter kill anything that doesn't suit a point drain, then pick.

Lead times: local stock is days; Fireclay 6 to 8 weeks. Tile has to be on site before the door hangs (sequence in the 10/15 site walk), so decide by end of October either way.


## 2026-10-16 update: photos pulled, two findings

Sheets in `Assets/V2 Build Drawings/2026-10-16 Tile candidates - real product photos.png` (vendor photos) and `... - pattern + color studies.png` (12 to-scale pattern/color studies against a cedar band). AI renders were not possible: the Gemini image models are paid-only (every free key returns quota 0) and the Higgsfield account has no credits. The 12 render prompts are written (`scratchpad tile/ai/gen.py`) if either gets funded.

- **Emser IMPACT is a WALL tile** per Emser's own page title and usage text (no floor rating shown). Drop it for the floor unless Emser confirms in writing.
- **Bedrosians True** shows no results on bedrosians.com under the series filter; it may be a stock-only or discontinued line. Call the SE 3rd store before driving over.
- **Fireclay 2" hex matte**: $53/sq ft, ships 6 to 8 weeks, $3 samples. Colors seen: Rosemary (moss), Evergreen, Cardamom (sand/olive), Tempest (charcoal), Sweet Pea, Feldspar (warm grey). Price puts two floors at ~$8K before waste; only worth it if the look matters enough.
- **TileBar Stoneside Charcoal 2x2**: shower-floor rated, DCOF >0.42, ~$5/sq ft online. Get the wet number; it is the cheap charcoal option.
- **Daltile Industrial Park (StepWise, DCOF ≥0.60)**: Charcoal Gray IP09 and Light Gray IP07 only. Confirm a 2x2 or 3x3 mosaic exists in the line; otherwise it is a 24x24 + linear drain floor.
