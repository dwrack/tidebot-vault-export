## SLACK POST TEXT

*PRIVATE SAUNAS (V2) — BUILD OVERVIEW*
_Pinned. Edited in place as things move. Last updated Aug 20._

*Who's who*
• Kyle Bruser — Zen Sauna — design + renderings + material spec
• Grant McElroy — Kerf Builds — construction
• Hannah + Jess (Ours Here) — project management
• Jonah + Davey — decisions, budget

*What's locked*
• Two saunas on ONE platform, moved west of the extension dock, between that dock and slip 32
• That move creates a small "pool" between the main dock and the platform — that's the future private cold plunge. Day-two problem, but the lockable gate goes in NOW so we don't retrofit later
• 3.5' front roof overhang over the door on both
• Inverse exterior theme: raw cedar siding + black trim on one, black siding + raw cedar accents on the other. Stays on brand, reads as two different buildings

*East Sauna ("Sunrise")* — 8' x 8', roof pitch down to the west
• Faces east toward the mountain, all seating that direction
• Massive round window. Kyle's method: square insulated pane, framed in an octagon, trimmed out to round. He's done several
• Secondary slatted window rendered
• Vertical interior siding
• More exposed, more social — faces the pool and the community sauna. Daytime hang / sunrise moment

*West Sauna ("Sunset")* — 9' x 8', roof pitch down to the east
• Faces west over the water. Date night, moodier, private
• Windows close to floor-to-ceiling, 40" wide on two adjacent sides
• Cantilever 1' out over the floating foundation is doable (the AI render just couldn't show it)
• Two things to solve: the heater currently lands on a run of the proposed glass floor strip, and we lose some top bench space to the door/view placement
• Kyle's fix on 8/18: shorten the top bench extension, cheat the door toward center, use the corner nook option

*Heater*
• Kyle ran the math on the glass area — *minimum 60,000 BTU*, and his line was "you can always turn it down, you can't turn it past max"
• We're on Nippa G-Series gas. G-75 and G-90 share the same body, so the custom rock cage drawing is identical either way. Only dropping to a G-60 changes it
• Proposed heater locations came over 8/18, marked on the floor plans

*Rock cage (Nippa)*
• Custom 316L stainless cage speced: tall basket on top + free-standing 3-sided rock skirt, open front. Adds roughly +345 lbs per stove, +690 lbs across both — that number has to land in Grant's float load calc before the platform is ordered
• Email went to Nippa 8/14 with 12 questions. Asked for the top-plate drawing and one answer by *Fri Aug 21*: what is the 40" minimum ceiling clearance measured FROM — top of body, top of rock, or top of the diverter? That drives Grant's ceiling height and he's framing now
• *No reply from Nippa yet*

*Open questions on the table*
1. Floor construction — Grant wants direction, says being on the water adds a dimension. Still unanswered
2. Window header — Grant's flag: a window that big needs header space above it to carry roof load to the corner studs, and the rendering doesn't show much room there. Has Kyle framed one this large before? Still unanswered
3. True floor-to-ceiling glass — Kyle's comfort zone is ~6" up from the floor and 6" down from the ceiling. True FTC on two walls needs an engineer's framing plan. Cost driver, our call
4. Mirrored glass on Sunset — Kyle recommends against it. Too private means cleaning the sauna out multiple times a day. Agree or overrule
5. Interior wood — Kyle proposes clear Western Red Cedar and clear Port Orford. Our own research pushed the other way for daily commercial use (WRC off-gasses at heat, and cups/gaps at 18-24 months). Needs to be a decision, not a default, before wood is ordered
6. Differentiation tier — how far do we go? Inverse colors (cheap) → different interior woods → different roof styles (gable vs shed) → cedar shingle siding (best looking, most expensive)
7. Naming — Kyle's drawings say East/West, our narrative says Sunrise/Sunset. Pick one before it hits marketing
8. Copy fix: the original summary says Sunset faces "the ocean." We're on the Columbia. Fix before it reaches any public copy
