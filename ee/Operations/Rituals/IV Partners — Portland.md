# IV Partners: Portland / Vancouver WA

*Web research, Oct 7 2026. Nobody has been contacted. Prices are what each site listed that day; confirm before quoting a guest.*

**What we need from a partner:** comes to the float (173 NE Bridgeton Rd) on Sat/Sun mornings, handles groups of 2-6, runs their own intake + prescriber screen + charting, carries their own malpractice insurance, bills guests directly, and takes their sharps with them.

**One licensing catch:** E&E is in Oregon. Any nurse who starts an IV on our float needs an **Oregon** RN license. Oregon is not in the Nurse Licensure Compact, so a Washington multistate license doesn't count ([nursinglicensure.org](https://www.nursinglicensure.org/nursing-paths/rn-oregon)). And the order has to come from a prescriber licensed in Oregon. Vancouver-based companies must clear this; don't assume.

## Top 3

### 1. Good IV (Lake Oswego): best fit
- **Site:** [goodivpdx.com](https://goodivpdx.com) / [menu](https://goodivpdx.com/menu)
- **Who:** Founded by a physician (Dr. Richard Kozak) and an RN; drips given by licensed medical staff. Local, independent, been around since ~2015.
- **Pricing:** Easy Does It (1L fluids + electrolytes) $135. **Good God! hangover drip** (fluids, anti-nausea, pain relief, antacid, glutathione) $285. Myers $220. Zen $200. Add-ons $10-75.
- **On-site/group:** Yes. Their site says mobile IV is currently **only for groups and special circumstances**, which is exactly us. Past press mentions group discounts at 5-10, 10-20 and 20+.
- **Contact:** 503-880-0607, goodivpdx@gmail.com. Clinic: 425 2nd St #110, Lake Oswego.
- **Why #1:** Physician-led (cleanest on the Oregon rules), cheapest base drip in town, a named hangover drip, and they actively want group mobile work.

### 2. Boost IV & Wellness (NW Portland)
- **Site:** [boost-iv.com](https://boost-iv.com)
- **Who:** RNs and **nurse practitioners**. An NP on staff means a prescriber can be on-site or close, which smooths the per-guest order requirement.
- **Pricing:** Hydration Boost $140, Myers / Immune-C $220, Summit $280, NAD+ $280-800.
- **On-site/group:** Yes, mobile for events, weddings, parties.
- **Contact:** 971-533-1700, hello@boost-iv.com. Clinic: 2342 NW Thurman St, Portland.
- **Why #2:** Local Portland brand, NP-staffed, priced right. 20 min from the float.

### 3. Mobile IV 2U (Portland + Vancouver WA)
- **Site:** [mobileiv2u.com](https://mobileiv2u.com)
- **Who:** "Licensed nurses"; owner credentials not listed on the site.
- **Pricing:** Basic Hydration $250, Myers $275, Recovery/Hangover $295. **Travel fee included.**
- **On-site/group:** Yes, group IV for events and corporate wellness, discounts for large parties.
- **Contact:** Call/text 503-855-9079, book at mobileiv2u.com/book. Claims same-day and replies within the hour.
- **Why #3:** Mobile-first and covers both sides of the river. Priciest of the three, and less transparent about who's behind it, so ask for their medical director's name and their COI.

## What to ask when David's ready to reach out
1. Can you do Sat/Sun 9-11am on a floating dock in North Portland? Any minimum number of drips?
2. Who's your medical director, and how does the per-guest prescriber screen work (telehealth before arrival, or on-site NP)?
3. Is the nurse Oregon-licensed?
4. Send a **certificate of insurance** (malpractice + general liability) naming Elevated Tides LLC / Ebb & Ember as additional insured. File it in `Operations/Vendor Insurance Certs/`.
5. Group rate for 3-6 drips per morning?
6. You book and bill guests directly, you take your sharps and waste. OK?
7. Would you do one free trial morning for staff so we can see the setup before guests do?

**Money rule:** we don't take a percentage of their IV sales. If anything changes hands, it's a flat, fair-market venue fee agreed in writing. See the in-house plan for why.
