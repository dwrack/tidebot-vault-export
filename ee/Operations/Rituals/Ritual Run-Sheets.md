## Shared safety rules (read before every ritual)

These apply to all three. They're the floor, not the ceiling.

**Heat**
- Sauna runs 170-190°F. On ritual days, hold it at **175°F** max. Lower bench is the default seat; upper bench is earned, not assigned.
- Rounds capped at **12 min**. Anyone can leave anytime, no explaining needed.
- Water before the first round, every guest, every time. Staff watches it happen.

**Cold (the river)**
- The Columbia here is 40+ ft deep with real current. Ladder entry only, always holding the rail. No jumping, no swimming away from the ladder.
- **Buddy system in the water, no exceptions.** One guest in, one watching from the deck, plus staff eyes on the ladder.
- Plunge time: 15-60 sec is plenty. In winter water (42°F) keep it under a minute, full stop.
- Cold plunge is always optional. Sitting on the ladder counts. So does skipping it.
- **Do not plunge** (staff says this at the briefing, plainly): heart conditions or arrhythmia, uncontrolled high blood pressure, pregnancy, seizure history, Raynaud's, recent concussion, anyone on blood pressure or heart meds who hasn't cleared it with a doctor, anyone feeling dizzy, nauseous or lightheaded in the moment, and **anyone who's had alcohol in the last 8 hours or seems impaired**.

**Breathwork**
- Only calming breathwork: slow exhales, box breathing, physiological sigh. **No hyperventilation or breath-hold styles (Wim Hof-type rounds) anywhere near the water.** Breath holds after over-breathing cause blackouts, and a blackout at the ladder is a drowning.
- Breathwork happens seated, on the deck or in the lounge, never in the water and never right before a plunge.

**Staff authority**
- Any staff member can sit someone out of heat or cold, no debate. Say it kindly, say it once: "Not this round. Let's get you some water and a seat."
- Throw ring and life jackets staged and visible before guests arrive. Phone charged and on staff.

## (a) Hangover Ritual ("The Morning After")

*Internal name: Hangover Killer. Public name is softer because we don't promise outcomes. See offerings doc.*

**When:** Sat + Sun, 9:00-10:45am
**Capacity:** 8 guests
**Staff:** 2 (Ritual Lead runs aufguss + breathwork; Deck/Water Lead runs plunge safety + drinks). If an IV partner is booked, their nurse is a third person but is **not E&E staff** and we don't direct their clinical work.

### The house drink: "Low Tide"
Non-alcoholic, salty-sour, built to replace what a hangover takes. One 12 oz serving:

| Ingredient | Amount | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Unsweetened coconut water | 6 oz | Potassium |
| Cold water | 4 oz | Volume |
| Fresh lime juice | 1 oz | Brightness, cuts the salt |
| Honey (or maple) | 0.5 oz | Sugar helps the body take up sodium and water |
| Fresh ginger juice (or 3 thin slices, muddled) | 1 tsp | Settles a queasy stomach |
| Fine sea salt | 1/8 tsp (~290 mg sodium) | The point of the drink |
| Fresh mint | 4-5 leaves, slapped | Ties it to the mint theme |

Shake with ice, serve in a tall cup, mint sprig on top. Batch version: 1 gallon = ~10 servings. Make it day-of; keep it cold. Backup for anyone who wants it plainer: an LMNT-type sachet in 16 oz water.

Supplies cost: about **$1.50 per drink**.

### Door check script
> "Quick check before we start. When was your last drink? Anyone feeling dizzy, throwing up, or still a little buzzy?"

- Last drink under 8 hours ago, or they seem impaired: they do the **heat-lite version**, lower bench only, no plunge, extra water. If they're clearly drunk, they don't do the session; we issue a gift card per our policy and say it warmly.
- Vomiting in the last couple hours: sauna is a no today. Tea, drink, deck, gift card for the rest.

### Breathwork script (12 min, calming only)
1. Sit tall, feet flat. Eyes closed or soft on the river. (1 min)
2. **Physiological sigh** x 5: two short inhales through the nose, one long slow exhale through the mouth. (2 min)
3. **Box breathing**, 4 in, 4 hold, 4 out, 4 hold. Ritual Lead counts out loud quietly. (5 min)
4. **Long exhale**: in for 4, out for 8. No counting, just the pace. (3 min)
5. Open eyes. Notice the river. Done. (1 min)

Never cue breath holds after fast breathing. Never run this on the ladder.

### Hangover-specific safety notes
- A hangover is mild dehydration plus low blood sugar plus a stressed heart. Heat adds more dehydration and a faster heart rate. That's why rounds are short, the sauna runs cooler, and water is enforced up front.
- Watch for: pale, clammy, sudden quiet, sitting with head in hands, swaying on stand-up. Any of these = out of the sauna, seated, feet up if they feel faint, water, and they're done with heat.
- Stand up slowly after every round. Staff cues this out loud. Most fainting happens on the way out of the sauna, not in it.
- Anyone who faints, even briefly, does not go back in and does not plunge. Call 911 if they don't come around fast, hit their head, or have chest pain.

### IV add-on notes (partner-run)
- The partner's nurse runs the IV start to finish. E&E staff never touch the line, the bag, or the decision.
- **Default order: IV comes last.** Guests getting a drip do Rounds 1-2 and their plunges, then start the IV at 1:05 in the lounge. No heat and no plunge after the line goes in (the cannula site can't go in river water, and fluid shifts plus heat raise fainting risk). The partner's prescriber has final say on timing.
- Partner handles their own intake, good-faith exam (telehealth or in person), consent, and charting. We just give them a quiet, warm corner of the lounge with a chair that reclines.
- Sharps and medical waste leave with the partner. Nothing goes in our trash.

## (b) Silent Night

**When:** One weeknight to start (suggest Wednesday), 7:15-9:00pm
**Capacity:** 6 guests (fewer bodies = actually quiet)
**Staff:** 2 (Ritual Lead runs heat + the room; Deck/Water Lead runs plunge safety in the dark)

### The feel
Quiet, gentle, soft, calming. No talking from the first bell to the last bell. The river is the soundtrack.

### Scent: "Douglas Fir & Cedar, Lavender to close"
- Rounds 1 and 2: **Douglas fir needle oil + cedarwood** (about 2:1). Smells like the Gorge after rain. It's Northwest, it's on-brand, and it's calm without being sleepy-spa.
- Final round: a few drops of **lavender** added to the same bucket. That's the signal it's ending.
- Tea: **chamomile + lemon balm**. No caffeine.
- Skip mint here. Mint wakes people up, which is right for the morning ritual and wrong for this one.

### Light
- Sauna: one warm amber light, ~2200K, dimmed low. Red light is fine if we already have one.
- Lounge + deck: flameless candles only (it's a wooden float, no open flame). Warm string light on the path.
- **The plunge ladder stays well lit.** Calm doesn't mean dark at the water. Ladder light + a glow marker on the throw ring.

### Sound
- No music. River, wind, stove.
- One **singing bowl or soft chime** marks every transition. That's the only "talking" staff does.
- Foam earplugs in a bowl at check-in for anyone who wants them.

### Run-sheet (minute by minute)

| Time | What happens | Who |
|---|---|---|
| -0:30 | Heat to 180°F. Dim lights, set flameless candles, light the ladder. Brew chamomile/lemon balm. Mix fir/cedar bucket. Lay out eye pillows and blankets in lounge. | Both |
| 0:00-0:10 | Arrival, spoken in a low voice. Phones go in the cubby (we hold them). Waiver, tea. | Deck/Water |
| 0:10-0:15 | **The only spoken briefing.** Plunge rules, the "no" list, hand signals (thumbs up = I'm good, flat hand = I need help), buddy pairs. "After the bell, we're quiet until the last bell." | Ritual Lead |
| 0:15 | **First bell.** Silence starts. | Ritual Lead |
| 0:15-0:27 | **Round 1 sauna (12 min).** One slow ladle of fir/cedar water. No towel work. | Ritual Lead |
| 0:27-0:37 | Optional plunge (buddy, lit ladder) or deck rest under a blanket. | Deck/Water |
| 0:37-0:47 | Sit outside. Tea. Nothing to do. | Both |
| 0:47-0:59 | **Round 2: Soft Aufguss (12 min).** Two ladles, one slow towel pass. Bowl rings once at the start and once at the end. | Ritual Lead |
| 0:59-1:09 | Optional plunge or rest. | Deck/Water |
| 1:09-1:21 | **Lounge stillness (12 min).** Recline, eye pillows, blankets. Bowl at start and end. No guided talk. | Ritual Lead |
| 1:21-1:33 | **Round 3 (optional, 10 min).** Lavender added. Lights at their lowest. | Ritual Lead |
| 1:33-1:40 | Slow cool-down on the deck. No plunge after Round 3 at night (people are tired and it's dark). | Deck/Water |
| 1:40 | **Last bell.** Talking allowed. Staff says one line: "Thanks for being quiet with us. Take your time." | Ritual Lead |
| 1:40-1:45 | Tea, phones back, quiet goodbyes. | Both |
| 1:45-2:15 | Reset and close. | Both |

### Supplies (per 6-guest session)
- Douglas fir needle oil, cedarwood oil, lavender oil
- Chamomile + lemon balm tea
- Singing bowl or chime
- Flameless candles, amber bulb, ladder light, glow marker for throw ring
- Eye pillows (washable covers), wool or fleece blankets
- Foam earplugs, phone cubby

One-time setup (bowl, eye pillows, blankets, candles, ladder light): roughly **$350-450**.

### Silent Night safety notes
- Silence is for guests, not for safety. Staff speaks immediately if anything looks off.
- Night plunge: ladder lit, staff physically at the ladder for every entry, buddy watching. If the river's running high, debris is moving, or wind is up, staff calls the plunge off for the night. "When in doubt, don't go out."
- Hand signals at the briefing are not optional. A flat hand from any guest = staff goes to them right away.
- Watch for people falling asleep in the sauna. Lounge stillness invites dozing, and that habit can carry into Round 3. Ritual Lead keeps eyes on the room the whole round.
- Same plunge "no" list as above.

## (c) Focaccia + Whipped Butters

**What it is:** A warm focaccia board with three whipped butters, served in the lounge after the last round. An add-on to any session and a natural fit for privates.
**Staff:** Whoever's on. Adds ~10 min of prep per board.

### The board
- **Focaccia:** rosemary + flaky salt, cut into 2-inch squares. Warm it on a sheet pan 5 min before serving (needs a toaster oven or warming drawer in the lounge; we don't have one yet).
- **Three whipped butters** (1/2 oz each per guest):
  1. **Caraway + honey + flaky salt** (the one from the note; toasted caraway, it's the Nordic nod)
  2. **Roasted garlic + chive**
  3. **Smoked salt + black pepper**, or **brown-butter maple** in fall/winter
- Small bowl of good olive oil for anyone avoiding dairy.

### Whipped butter base (makes ~30 portions)
1 lb unsalted butter, softened + 2 tbsp heavy cream, whipped 5-6 min until pale. Split in three, fold in flavors:
- Caraway: 2 tsp caraway seed, toasted and lightly crushed + 1 tbsp honey + pinch salt
- Garlic-chive: 1 head roasted garlic + 2 tbsp minced chive + 1/2 tsp salt
- Smoked salt: 1 tsp smoked flaky salt + 1/2 tsp cracked pepper

Keeps 1 week refrigerated in sealed tubs, labeled and dated.

### Timing inside a session
| Time | Step |
|---|---|
| 1:15 | Pull butters from fridge (they spread better at cool room temp, but don't leave out more than 2 hours). |
| 1:25 | Warm focaccia 5 min. |
| 1:30 | Plate the board, set it in the lounge. |
| 1:45 | Clear leftovers. Butter that sat out gets tossed, not returned to the tub. |

### Cost per guest

| Item | Per guest |
|---|---|
| Focaccia (bakery sheet, ~$22 for 12 portions) | $1.85 |
| Butter + cream + flavorings (1.5 oz) | $0.75 |
| Olive oil, napkin, board share | $0.30 |
| **Total** | **~$2.90** |

Baking in-house would drop the bread to ~$0.40/guest, but we don't have a kitchen and it's not worth building one for this.

### Food safety + licensing (needs an answer before launch)
- Serving food to the public in Portland needs a license from **Multnomah County Environmental Health**, and whoever handles food needs an **Oregon food handler card** (~$10, online). Butter that sits out is the kind of thing an inspector cares about.
- **Easiest legal path:** buy the focaccia and butters from a licensed bakery or caterer that delivers them ready to serve, and keep everything pre-portioned. Confirm with the county whether that still needs our own license. Don't launch the add-on until we've asked.
- Ask about allergies at booking (dairy, gluten, sesame if a bakery's bread has it). Label the butters.
