# Contractor Onboarding Checklist
**Everything a new host needs to complete before they run a session solo.**

---

## Step 0: Culture Doc

1. New hire reads [[How We Run Things — Ebb and Ember]] before anything else.
2. Manager walks through any questions or scenarios that come up.

- [ ] Culture doc read
- [ ] Manager conversation completed

---

## Pre-Hire Requirements

- [ ] CPR/First Aid certification (current)
- [ ] Background check completed
- [ ] 2 professional references checked

---

## Documents to Collect Before First Shift

- [ ] W-9 (required for all 1099 contractors)
- [ ] Signed Independent Contractor Agreement (ICA) via DocuSeal
- [ ] Photo ID (driver's license or passport copy)
- [ ] Copy of CPR/First Aid certification
- [ ] Direct deposit / payment info (Venmo, Zelle, or bank ACH)
- [ ] Emergency contact information

---

## Insurance Requirements

> [!warning] All contractors must provide a Certificate of Liability Insurance before their first shift. This is non-negotiable.

- [ ] Certificate of Liability Insurance received
- [ ] Commercial General Liability confirmed on cert
- [ ] Professional Liability (Errors & Omissions) confirmed on cert
- [ ] **Ebb & Ember Floating Saunas** named as Additional Insured
- [ ] **Elevated Tides, LLC** named as Additional Insured
- [ ] Both AI addresses correct: 10350 N. Vancouver Way #5411, Portland, OR 97217
- [ ] ICA signed (activates Additional Insured coverage, cert alone is not enough)

**If contractor uses subcontractors:**
- [ ] Each subcontractor has their own cert, OR
- [ ] Written confirmation from contractor's insurer that subcontractors are covered under their policy

## Orientation (In-Person)

3. Facility walkthrough: sauna, dock, lounge area, storage, emergency exits, equipment locations.
4. Sauna operation: heater startup/shutdown, temperature monitoring, propane/fuel management.
5. Safety procedures: Morning Inspection Checklist, Guest Check-In Steps (including health screening), Incident Report Steps.
6. Guest health screening practice: walk through the contraindication questions, practice handling a guest who discloses a condition.
7. Safety briefing practice: new hire delivers it, manager gives feedback.
8. FareHarbor walkthrough: checking bookings, waiver status, manifest.
9. Fire safety: extinguisher location and use, carbon monoxide detector, evacuation procedure.
