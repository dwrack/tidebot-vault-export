# Safety & Equipment Checklist
**Monthly and seasonal deep inspection. Goes beyond the daily morning check.**

---

**Perform monthly or at the start of each season, whichever comes first.**

### Sauna Equipment
- [ ] Heater/stove: full operational test, no unusual sounds, proper heating cycle
- [ ] Temperature controls: accurate, responsive
- [ ] Ventilation: adequate airflow, vents unobstructed
- [ ] Sauna interior: benches structurally sound, no cracks, no splinters, hardware tight
- [ ] Door/entry: opens and closes properly, seals without sticking, can always be opened from inside
- [ ] Thermometer/hygrometer: calibrated and readable

### Fire Safety
- [ ] Fire extinguisher: charged, inspection tag current, accessible
- [ ] Carbon monoxide detector: functioning, battery current
- [ ] Fire alarm: functioning, battery current
- [ ] Propane/fuel lines: no leaks, connections tight (use soap test if gas)
- [ ] Propane tank: condition good, no rust, valve functional
- [ ] Clear zone around heater: no combustibles within required distance

### Dock and Water Access
- [ ] Dock structure: no rot, no loose boards, no protruding hardware
- [ ] Dock connections/pilings: secure
- [ ] Walking surfaces: no slippery spots, non-slip surfaces intact
- [ ] Cleats and lines: condition good, properly secured
- [ ] Entry/exit ladder: structurally sound, grip surfaces intact, secure mounting
- [ ] Lighting: adequate for evening sessions, all fixtures working

### Safety Equipment
- [ ] First aid kit: fully stocked, nothing expired
- [ ] Life ring / throwable flotation device: accessible (if required)
- [ ] Emergency contact numbers posted and visible

### Documentation
- [ ] Insurance current (expiration date: ___)
- [ ] All contractor certs on file and current
- [ ] Permits current (expiration date: ___)
