# Morning Inspection Checklist
**Everything checked and warming before the first guest arrives.**

---

1. Sauna stove/heater: power on and begin pre-heating to target temperature. Confirm it's reaching temp properly, no error lights, no unusual sounds or smells.
2. Thermometer/temperature display: working and readable.
3. Towels: stocked and staged in the guest area. Count matches expected guests plus extras.
4. Water bottles: stocked and cold. Positioned where guests can grab them easily.
5. Cooling buckets: clean, filled with fresh water. Positioned safely.
6. Entry/exit ladder: secure, no wobble, no slippery residue. Grip surfaces intact.
7. Dock/boarding area: clean, no trip hazards, no standing water on walking surfaces, lines secured.
8. First aid kit: stocked (bandages, gauze, antiseptic, gloves, instant cold packs, electrolyte packets). Replace anything used.
9. Fire extinguisher: accessible, charged, inspection tag current.
10. Carbon monoxide detector: functioning (test button).
11. Fire alarm: functioning (test button).
12. Emergency exit: clearly marked, unobstructed.
13. Sauna interior: clean, no debris, benches wiped, no damage.

> [!warning] If the heater isn't reaching temperature or shows any malfunction, do not attempt to troubleshoot beyond basic checks. Report to management and cancel/delay the session.
