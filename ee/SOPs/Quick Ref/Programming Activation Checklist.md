# Programming Activation Checklist
**Manager-facing checklist for bringing on any external programming: fitness, meditation, workshops, events, pop-ups.**

---

## Stage 1: Intake & Concept Review

1. Receive proposal from instructor/partner.
2. Review for brand fit, schedule availability, target audience, and session format.
3. Confirm space and time slot availability.

- [ ] Proposal reviewed
- [ ] Initial approval from David + Jonah (must have both before proceeding)

---

## Stage 2: Insurance & Legal

> [!warning] All of the following must be in hand before any dates are locked or promotion begins. No exceptions.

**Lead Instructor / Partner:**
- [ ] Certificate of Liability Insurance received
- [ ] Commercial General Liability confirmed on cert
- [ ] Professional Liability (Errors & Omissions) confirmed on cert
- [ ] **Ebb & Ember Floating Saunas** named as Additional Insured
- [ ] **Elevated Tides, LLC** named as Additional Insured
- [ ] Both AI addresses correct: 10350 N. Vancouver Way #5411, Portland, OR 97217

**Subcontractors (if any):**
- [ ] Each subcontractor has their own cert, OR
- [ ] Written confirmation from lead's insurer that subcontractors are covered under lead's policy

**Written Agreement:**
- [ ] Signed agreement in place (activates Additional Insured status on cert)
- [ ] Agreement covers: financial split, schedule, session format, cancellation terms, liability

> [!tip] A cert without a signed written agreement does not activate Additional Insured coverage. Both are required.

---

## Stage 3: Financial Agreement

- [ ] Revenue split agreed and documented in writing
- [ ] Payment terms confirmed (per session, per series, per participant, etc.)
- [ ] Instructor compensation confirmed
- [ ] E&E share confirmed
- [ ] Platform/processing fees accounted for

---

## Stage 4: Walkthrough Session

4. Schedule a private walkthrough for David, Jonah, and E&E partners.
5. Weekdays only.
6. Instructor delivers the session as they would for the public.
7. Team evaluates: quality, tone, safety, brand fit, use of space.

- [ ] Walkthrough scheduled and completed
- [ ] Go / No-Go decision documented

---

## Stage 5: Promotional Materials

8. Receive written session description from instructor.
9. Review and approve description for website and social.
10. Pass to social media manager.
11. Confirm dates, times, pricing for listing.

- [ ] Booking/registration link set up in FareHarbor
- [ ] Promotional materials approved and live

---

## Stage 6: Launch

12. Lock dates on E&E calendar.
13. Confirm instructor is briefed on day-of logistics (arrival time, setup, guest flow).
14. Guest communication template prepared.

- [ ] Promotion live (social, email, website)
- [ ] Calendar locked
- [ ] Post-series debrief scheduled

---

## Stage 7: Post-Activation Review

15. Reconcile attendance and revenue.
16. Collect guest feedback.
17. Conduct instructor debrief.
18. Make the call: continue, adjust, or sunset.
19. Document learnings for future activations.

- [ ] Revenue reconciled
- [ ] Guest feedback reviewed
- [ ] Instructor debrief completed
- [ ] Decision documented: continue / adjust / sunset
