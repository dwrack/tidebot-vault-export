# Guest Check-In Steps
**Every guest gets a real welcome, a health screening, and a safety orientation before entering the sauna.**

---

1. Greet guests as they arrive. Warm, calm energy. This is a wellness experience, not a theme park.
2. Verify headcount against the FareHarbor manifest.
3. Check waiver status in FareHarbor. The manifest shows who signed and who hasn't.
   - Most guests sign via the automated email link before arrival.
   - Anyone unsigned: pull up their booking-specific waiver on your phone or tablet and have them sign on the spot.
   - No generic QR codes. Every signature tied to a specific booking.

> [!warning] No one enters the sauna without a signed waiver. Zero exceptions.

4. Health screening: ask every guest about the following contraindications:
   - Heart conditions or cardiovascular issues
   - Pregnancy
   - Recent surgery (within the last 6 weeks)
   - Blood pressure medications
   - History of fainting or seizures
   - Any condition that could be affected by extreme heat

> [!warning] If a guest discloses a contraindication, they cannot use the sauna. Offer them the lounge/deck area, a partial refund, or reschedule. Do not let them "try it and see." This is a liability and safety issue.

5. Safety orientation for all guests:
   - Hydrate before, during, and after. Water is available, use it.
   - If you feel dizzy, nauseous, or lightheaded, exit the sauna immediately. This is not a test of endurance.
   - Recommended time limits for heat exposure (provide specifics based on sauna temp).
   - How to use the cooling buckets safely.
   - Entry/exit ladder procedure: one person at a time, hold the rails, watch your step.
   - No diving or jumping into the water.
   - Point out the first aid kit and fire extinguisher.
6. Photo opportunity if the guest is interested. Keep it optional, some people here want privacy.
7. Confirm everyone understands the exit procedure and where to find you if they need help.
