# End-of-Day Sign-Off
**Sauna shut down safely, space clean, everything accounted for.**

---

- [ ] Sauna fully powered down: heater off, temperature dropping, no residual heat risk
- [ ] Propane/fuel supply valve closed (if applicable)
- [ ] Carbon monoxide detector confirmed still functioning
- [ ] All towels collected and staged for laundry (or laundered)
- [ ] Cooling buckets emptied and stored
- [ ] Lounge/deck area cleaned: no trash, no personal items, furniture reset
- [ ] Dock area clear of hazards: lines secured, no loose items
- [ ] Entry/exit ladder secure and stowed (if removable)
- [ ] First aid kit checked and restocked if anything was used
- [ ] Fire extinguisher in place
- [ ] All guest personal items returned or documented for follow-up
- [ ] Any equipment issues or maintenance needs reported to management (same day, in writing)
- [ ] Any incidents documented via Incident Report Steps

> [!warning] The sauna heater must be fully powered down and verified before you leave. An unattended heater is a fire risk.
