# Night-Before Checklist
**Confirm tomorrow's sessions are set and nothing catches you off guard at the dock.**

---

1. Open FareHarbor and review every booking for tomorrow: headcount, session times, any notes or special requests.
2. Check weather forecast for thunderstorms, lightning, high winds (>25mph), or anything that might trigger a cancellation.
3. Confirm host availability for each scheduled session. If someone's out, find coverage tonight.
4. Verify towel inventory: enough clean towels for all booked guests plus a few extra.
5. Confirm water supply: bottled water stocked and cold.
6. Check cooling bucket status: clean and ready to fill.
7. Verify propane/fuel level for sauna heater (if applicable). Don't discover an empty tank at 7am.

> [!tip] If weather looks borderline for morning sessions, make the call by 7am and communicate to guests immediately. Lightning or high wind forecasts for the session window = cancel the night before if the forecast is confident.
