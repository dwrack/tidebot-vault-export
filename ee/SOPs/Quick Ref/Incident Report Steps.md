# Incident Report Steps
**Something went wrong. Handle it in this order.**

---

1. **Ensure guest safety first.** If someone is experiencing heat-related illness, get them out of the sauna immediately.

> [!warning] Heat stroke is a medical emergency. Signs: confusion, slurred speech, loss of consciousness, hot/dry skin, extremely high body temperature. Call 911 immediately. Move the person to a cool area, apply cool water to their skin, fan them. Do not give fluids if they're unconscious.

2. For heat exhaustion (heavy sweating, weakness, nausea, dizziness, headache): move to a cool area, give water, apply cool cloths, monitor closely. If symptoms don't improve within 15 minutes, call 911.
3. For any other medical emergency (fall, drowning risk, allergic reaction, cardiac event): call 911 immediately.
4. Contact management. Call, don't text. If no answer, call the backup contact.
5. If the incident involves water (fall from dock, submersion): account for all guests immediately. Ensure no one is in the water unassisted.
6. Administer first aid as trained while waiting for emergency services.
7. Clear other guests from the immediate area if needed. Keep the space calm.
8. Take photos if appropriate: scene, conditions, equipment involved.
9. Get contact info from any witnesses.
10. File an internal incident report the same day. Include:
    - Date, time, location
    - Host name
    - Number of guests present
    - What happened (factual, not opinion)
    - Injuries or illness (if any)
    - Actions taken
    - 911 called? (yes/no, response time)
    - Photos attached
    - Witness contact info
11. Do not post about the incident on social media or discuss details with anyone outside the company.

> [!tip] Even minor incidents get documented. A guest feels dizzy and sits down for 5 minutes, someone stubs their toe on the ladder, someone slips on the dock. Write it up. Undocumented incidents become problems later.
