# Cold Plunge Dealer Outreach — Strategy & Offer (Jun 2026)

Status: internal playbook. Nothing sends until Davey approves the ColdTub sample. Sample-first rule applies.

## The idea in one line

Turn Ebb & Ember into a live, on-water showroom for a cold plunge tub brand: their tub sits where high-intent wellness guests actually use it, and we get paid either by a monthly placement fee or by an affiliate cut when a guest buys one.

## Why a dealer says yes

Their hardest marketing problem is getting a $5k-15k tub *experienced* by a buyer before purchase. We solve that. A guest finishes a hot round, steps into their tub on a dock over the Columbia, and feels it. That's the highest-converting demo a plunge brand can buy, and they don't have to staff or build it.

**The seasonal hook is the strongest card, lead with it.** In winter the plunge is the river itself: Cascade snowmelt, around 42°F. In summer the river runs 60-65°F, too warm to be a real plunge. So from roughly June through September a chilled tub isn't a display, it's the actual cold source our guests use. The brand gets a working demo in front of every summer guest, and we get the cold we need. Genuine two-way value, not a favor.

## The offer (dealer picks one)

**Option A — Placement fee.** A monthly dollar amount for the showcase spot. Their tub stays on-site, branded, in use. **Don't name a number up front.** Lead with affiliate; if a brand bites on paying for the spot, that's a good sign, and we work out the figure then based on how much they want it and what else is in the deal. Keep it open.

**Option B — Affiliate / referral.** No fee. We earn a commission (or store credit) on every guest who buys a tub through our link or code, tracked via their existing program. Several brands already run these ($100-250/sale at Plunge, Ice Barrel, Pod Company), so this is often a same-day yes with no new paperwork on their side.

Let them choose. Most DTC brands will reach for B because it costs them nothing up front; the local makers and commercial-placement brands (ColdTub, Renu, BlueCube) are the ones more likely to entertain A or a hybrid.

## Currencies we can trade (mix to close the deal)

- Monthly placement fee (Option A)
- Affiliate % or per-sale bounty (Option B)
- Loaner/demo unit vs. a unit we buy at dealer cost
- Co-marketing: feature their tub to our list (~924 contacts), tag them on Instagram, on-site signage with a QR/code
- Comped sessions for their team and reps
- Their reciprocal push: a mention to their audience that the place to *feel* their tub is Ebb & Ember

## Guardrails (from Ebb's partnership playbook)

- Start small, pilot first. Propose a defined summer pilot, not an open-ended commitment. Expand if it converts.
- Two-way street, always. We give reach and comps, they give referral economics and co-marketing.
- Won't oversell. Cedar voice: grounded, direct.
- **Cold plunge stays optional for guests.** Always. The tub is one of several ways to get cold (river, Portland Downpour, the tub), never a forced step.
- One brand at a time on-site. This is a single showcase spot, so it's effectively an exclusive while it runs. That exclusivity is part of what the placement is worth, use it in the negotiation.

## Confirmed facts (use exactly, never improvise)

- Location: **at Elevated Tides on the Columbia** (173 NE Bridgeton Rd, Portland OR). Not "Bridgeton marina."
- **Portland's only floating sauna, and one of only a few in the entire country.** (Use the national framing for the DTC brand pitches, it's a bigger credential for them.)
- Sessions: up to **8 guests** social, **10** private, **1h45** each, open **7am-9pm daily**.
- River temp: ~42°F winter (snowmelt), 60-65°F summer (too warm to plunge).
- The sauna is gas-fired, but leave fuel unsaid in copy. Never say wood-fired.
- **Exposure numbers for the pitch (real, sourced):**
  - **~2,400 past customers** through the sauna to date (Davey).
  - **~200-300 booked sessions a month** (GA4 tracked, property 524300531): Mar 297, Apr 268, May 210, Jun ~200 run-rate. ~$11-17k/month in tracked session revenue.
  - **18 active members** (10 Ember 1, 8 Ember 2) on recurring monthly plans.
  - ~924 email contacts (Mar 2026); broader master list ~2,300.
  - **Press to name (published, verified):** Time Out USA, Portland Monthly (two pieces), KGW (NBC Portland), Travel Oregon, The Oregonian, Yahoo Lifestyle. Use a short subset in the pitch (Time Out / Portland Monthly / KGW / Travel Oregon reads clean). Do NOT claim KOIN, AFAR, or VIA, those are pending or journalist interviews, not confirmed published coverage.
  - Caveat: GA4 counts tracked online bookings (single-session sauna), so true guest count is at least this and likely higher. Quote as "around 200-300 a month" and "2,400+ so far," don't overstate to a precise figure.

## Conflict note (Plunge, Sun Home)

Both sell saunas too. Don't avoid them, reframe: we showcase your **cold plunge only**, we're an experiential venue not a retailer, and we send you plunge buyers. If you want, we become the place your sauna-curious leads go to *feel it* before they buy. Turns overlap into a bigger co-marketing partnership. Still, anchor wave one with the zero-conflict pure-plunge brands (ColdTub, Renu, BlueCube) regardless.
