- [ ] **1. Tue Oct 13: Rain with new eyes**
Image: 01-floating-sauna-portland-interior-river-window.jpg
> Rain season is back in Portland, and from inside the sauna it looks different. You watch it hit the Columbia through the window while the cedar holds the heat. Then you step out into it on purpose: cold air, rain on warm skin, steam coming off your shoulders. The weather stops being something to get through and becomes part of the session. Open daily, rain or shine.

- [ ] **2. Fri Oct 16: Gray days on the river**
Image: 14-gray-day-floating-sauna-columbia-river.jpg
> Gray sky, flat water, nobody else on the river. This is the Columbia most people never see, and it's one of our favorite days to be out here. Heat up in cedar, step out into the cool air, and watch the clouds sit low over the water. Gray days are good sauna days.

- [ ] **3. Tue Oct 20: Private buyouts for holiday groups**
Image: 07-private-sauna-party-deck-member-night.jpg
> Planning a holiday get-together, a team outing, or a Friendsgiving that isn't another dinner? Book the whole floating sauna for up to 10 people. Sauna, cold plunge, and the deck are all yours. December weekends go first, so lock in your date now.

- [ ] **4. Fri Oct 23: Member Night**
Image: 15-member-night-group-in-sauna.jpg
> Last month we threw our first Member Night on the Columbia: a full sauna, oysters on the dock, and a lot of familiar faces. That's what membership here looks like. Regular heat, regular people, and a place on the river that starts to feel like yours. Ask us about memberships next time you're out.

- [ ] **5. Tue Oct 27: Back from Oslo**
Image: 02-oslo-sauna-congress-sauna-hat.jpg

We came home with a notebook full of ideas, a lot of respect for old traditions, and a new favorite hat. One of those ideas is already in the works. Let's just say it might remind you of a waterfall. 😉 More soon.

- [ ] **6. Fri Oct 30: Local partners on the dock**
Image: 16-member-night-oysters-local-partners.jpg

- [ ] **7. Tue Nov 3: Evenings on the river**
Image: 03-evening-sauna-deck-lights-columbia-river.jpg
> The sun sets early now, and that's when the deck lights come on. Evening sessions on the Columbia mean heat, cold water, and the city lights across the river. Moonlight Sauna sessions are on the calendar, so book one in and trade a night on the couch for this.

- [ ] **8. Fri Nov 6: 6am sessions**
Image: 17-early-morning-floating-sauna-dock.jpg
> First session is at 6am. The river is quiet, the air is cold, and the sauna is already hot. It's a different way to start a weekday, before the inbox and the commute. Early sessions are the calmest of the day.

- [ ] **9. Tue Nov 10: Banya**
Image: 04-sauna-ladle-steam-rocks-banya.jpg
> Banya is a centuries-old steam ritual: water on hot rocks, a wave of heat, then cold. It's slower and more hands-on than a regular session, and people come back for it. Banya sessions run on the floating sauna in Portland. Spots are small, so book ahead.

- [ ] **10. Fri Nov 13: First time? Here's how it works**
Image: 18-first-timer-deck-lounge-river-view.jpg
> Never done a floating sauna? It's simple. Bring a swimsuit, a towel, and a water bottle. Heat up in the cedar sauna, cool down in the river or under The Downpour, rest on the deck, repeat. The plunge is always optional, and you'll get everything you need to know before you arrive.

- [ ] **11. Tue Nov 17: Social sessions**
Image: 05-cedar-sauna-interior-warm-light.jpg
> New to sauna? Social sessions are the easy way in. You share the cedar sauna, cold plunge, and deck with a small group, from $49 a person. Come solo or bring a friend, and leave your phone in the car. Portland's floating sauna on the Columbia River.

- [ ] **12. Fri Nov 20: River respect**
Image: 19-river-safety-dock-life-ring.jpg
> The Columbia is real water. It's cold this time of year and it has current. Life jackets are on deck, throw rings are close, and we use the buddy system for every river plunge. If you'd rather skip the river, The Downpour gives you the cold without it. Some people love the plunge and some don't go in, and both are fine.
