# Website Shot List (Oct 2026)

For the new ebbandember.com. What we have: Tyler's Member Night 4K video (social, oysters, river swim, Downpour, sauna interior), IG pulls, older library, Airstream interiors (800px only). What's missing is below, ranked.

**Rules for every shoot**
- Shoot vertical 4K (iPhone works) AND a horizontal pass of the hero shots. The site is phone-first but desktop needs wide.
- 10-second minimum holds, no zooms, no text burned in, no music. We edit for the site.
- Consent: anyone identifiable signs off before it goes on the site.
- Deliver raw files to the sauna@ Drive, not IG exports.

## 1. The big one: dawn-to-moonrise time-lapse
One locked-off camera on the dock, sauna + river + sky in frame, running from first light to after moonrise. Pick a clear-ish day near a full moon so the moon rises right after sunset.
- **Why:** the site can play the frame that matches the actual time of day. Someone opening it at 6:40 PM sees golden hour on our dock. It's the living-sky idea, but with real footage.
- Second pass in fall rain and one in winter fog, so the hero changes with the weather too (v2.0).
- Gear: a phone on a weighted tripod with a power bank works. A GoPro or mirrorless camera with an intervalometer is better. 1 frame every 5-10 sec.

## 2. Seasons and weather (what makes it Portland)
- Rain on the sauna glass from inside, steam rolling out the door into cold rain.
- Fog on the river at Sunrise, dock lights on.
- Frost on the deck and benches on a cold morning.
- Steam coming off bodies after the plunge in winter.
- Summer river swim (we have some from Member Night).

## 3. Sunrise and Moonlight sessions
Neither one has real footage yet, and they're our $39 hooks.
- Sunrise: first light through the sauna window, a solo guest with coffee on the deck.
- Moonlight: the full moon over the river from inside the sauna, dock string lights, night plunge.

## 4. The ritual, step by step (Othership does this well)
Short clips that teach a first-timer what happens, in order:
1. Arriving, the dock walk, where to check in
2. The changing area, towels, where your stuff goes
3. Entering the heat: door opening, first breath, sitting down
4. Ladle on the rocks: steam burst
5. Pulling the Downpour cord (have it), ladder into the river
6. Resting on the deck wrapped in a towel, tea, the view
7. Going back in
This set doubles as the first-timer guide and FAQ video.

## 5. Faces of the place
Othership puts guides and regulars front and center. We show almost no one who works here.
- Host or guide portraits, at work and relaxed.
- 4-6 regulars (with consent): portrait plus one line ("Tuesdays since March, for the quiet"). Othership's "visit count" cards work because they're real people.
- Banya Monday with Danesh: venik, steam, the leaves. It's our closest thing to a guided class.

## 6. Private and celebration shots
Privates are our highest-ticket item, and the site has nothing that sells them.
- A birthday or girls' group, about 8-10 people, toasting on the deck.
- A couple on the private deck at sunset.
- Setup details: the table, string lights, pillows.

## 7. Details and texture (filler that makes it feel premium)
Cedar grain close-ups, ladle and bucket, the thermometer, water drops on skin, towels stacked, river surface, a heron or geese flying past, a boat wake, the gas flame NOT shown (we leave fuel unsaid).

## 8. Context and arrival
- Drone or high-angle shot: marina, river, city in the distance. Shows where we are.
- Parking to the dock in one walking shot (cuts down "where do I go" messages).
