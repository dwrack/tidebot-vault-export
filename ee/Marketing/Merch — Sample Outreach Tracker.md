## Research Additions to the Shelf

Things the vendor pass surfaced that were not in the original curation and are worth a slot:

- **Löyly scent, Finnish-made.** Hetkinen and Löy both make essential-oil sauna scents. Guests ask what the steam smells like every session. $20 to $25.
- **Changing robe.** The river plunge in January makes this a real need, not a novelty. Dryrobe is the leader, VOITED and NIXY are cheaper and VOITED has an ambassador program. $80 to $150, low volume, high ticket.
- **Venik whisks.** Holy Banya, North Shore Sauna, and Wild Oak Trail all ship them. Pairs with Banya Mondays. $15 to $25, seasonal.
- **Sauna hygrometer or sand timer.** Scandia makes them. The gift for the guest building a backyard sauna. $25 to $40.
- **Body care from another bathhouse.** New York's Bathhouse and Inoki are the same concept as us in other cities. Salt scrub or peat soap at $12 to $20 is a natural post-plunge buy.
- **Electrolytes.** Cure and BUBS both run small-business retail programs. Single-serve packets at $3 in the hair-tie bowl.
