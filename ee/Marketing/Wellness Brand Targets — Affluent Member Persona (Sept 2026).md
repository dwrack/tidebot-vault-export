# Wellness Brand Targets — Affluent Member Persona

*Sept 14, 2026. Companion to `Event Brand Partnership Framework.md` and `Event Partner Target List (Aug 2026).md`.*
*That list is Portland/outdoor-lifestyle heavy. This one is the wellness-product cut, filtered on one question: does this brand need to reach a 30-50 year old professional with real money, and would they pay to do it?*

---

## The filter that actually matters

Not "is this wellness." It's **AOV and who signs the check.**

A $4 kombucha brand does not need our 60 people. They need shelf velocity. They'll gift product all day and never write a check, which makes them a Tier C product partner and nothing more.

A brand selling a $12,000 home sauna, a $399 ring, or a $5,000 mattress needs exactly 60 affluent people who already believe in the category. That's the whole pitch. Our room is small, which kills us with mass-market brands and is irrelevant to considered-purchase brands.

**So: the higher the price of the thing, the more our member list is worth to them.** Pitch high-AOV first. Everyone else is gift-bag filler.

## Category 1 — High-ticket home wellness hardware (best fit, pitch first)

AOV $3,000 to $20,000. Their buyer is a homeowner with disposable income who already sauna-ed somewhere and liked it. That is literally our member list. We're the top of their funnel and they know it.

| Brand | Category | Why them |
|---|---|---|
| **Redwood Outdoors** | Barrel saunas, cold plunges | Portland-area (verify HQ). Local, same category, their customer walks our dock |
| **Plunge** | Cold plunge tubs | Category leader, heavy influencer/event spend already, $5k–$12k units |
| **Sun Home Saunas** | Home saunas + plunges | Aggressive partnerships program, high AOV |
| **Clearlight / Sunlighten** | Infrared cabins | $6k–$15k, sells through experience-first marketing |
| **Blue Cube / Renu Therapy** | Premium plunges | Smaller, faster yes |
| **Gerhardt / Lumi / local sauna builders** | Custom builds | Mutual referral more than cash |

**Objection they'll raise:** "Aren't you competing with us?" Answer: no. Nobody buys a $12k cabin without trying it first, and we're where Portland tries it. We send them buyers, they fund the event.

## Category 2 — Wearables and sleep (the exact demographic, real budgets)

This is the "optimizing professional" category. Their marketing spend is enormous and their target is a 35-year-old with a good job who tracks things.

| Brand | AOV | Note |
|---|---|---|
| **Oura** | $399 + $6/mo | Best fit on this list. Aesthetic matches ours, buyer matches ours, they do experiential |
| **Whoop** | $239/yr | Runs recovery-themed activations constantly. Sauna is on-message for them |
| **Eight Sleep** | $2,500–$5,000 | Their entire customer is a high-income professional. Founder-led, moves fast |
| **Hatch** | $130–$500 | Sleep, lower AOV, softer fit |

**The catch, and it's real:** these brands live on HRV and recovery-score language. Our framework has a hard no on health claims. Pre-negotiate the language before you take the check, not after. What works: they show data, we never promise an outcome. Put it in the agreement.

## What to say no to

- **Supplements making claims.** Auto-no in the framework and it's the right call
- **Peptides, IV drips, hormone clinics, biohacking clinics.** Money's there, brand damage isn't worth it
- **Anything requiring a health promise to make the partnership work**
- **Mass-market beverage and snack.** Gift bag only. They don't value our audience because our audience is small

1. **Plunge** or **Redwood Outdoors** — Presenting. High AOV, category-native, we are their funnel
2. **Oura** — Featured or Presenting. Best demographic match on the entire list
3. **Vuori** — Featured. Real experiential budget, right buyer
4. **Aesop** — Supporting or premium Product Partner. Aesthetic halo worth more than the cash
5. **Ghia** or **De Soi** — Supporting. Funds the bar, looks great on camera

## The other reading of "business wellness"

If the ask was corporate wellness rather than consumer products, that's a separate and probably bigger revenue line: selling the sauna as a team offsite to Portland employers, and pitching benefits platforms (Gympass/Wellhub, Classpass, Peerfit, Forma, Navan-style perk stacks) on listing us.

Different buyer (HR and People Ops), different motion (B2B sales, not sponsorship), different event (a daytime employer-facing open house, not a members' night). Worth its own doc if you want to chase it. Say the word.
