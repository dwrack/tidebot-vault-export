## FAQ SCHEMA

**Q1:**
```
Can you rent out the whole sauna for a bachelorette party?
```
**A1:**
```
Yes. A private session at Ebb & Ember books the entire floating sauna, rooftop deck, and locker lounge for your group only, no other guests. It holds up to 10 people and runs $539 for a 1 hour 45 minute session, plus a 15-minute transition before and after.
```

**Q2:**
```
How many people can go to a private sauna session in Portland?
```
**A2:**
```
Up to 10 guests per private booking. If your bachelorette party is bigger than that, you'd need to split into two sessions or trim the guest list. It's a real limit, not a soft one, the boat and the rooftop deck only hold so many people safely.
```

**Q3:**
```
Can you drink alcohol at a private sauna booking?
```
**A3:**
```
It depends, and it's worth asking directly when you book. Ebb & Ember's safety policy is clear that intoxication and sauna heat don't mix, so alcohol isn't part of the standard booking and isn't allowed inside the sauna itself. Ask the team what's realistic for your group before you plan around it.
```

**Q4:**
```
What should a bachelorette party bring to a private sauna session?
```
**A4:**
```
Swimsuits (private bookings can also go clothing-optional if your group prefers), two towels per person, non-metallic water bottles, and non-alcoholic drinks or snacks for the deck if you want them. Light robes or cover-ups for the walk between the boat and the locker lounge are a nice extra, though not required.
```

**Q5:**
```
Is a floating sauna a good bachelorette party idea?
```
**A5:**
```
For a group that's already done the bar crawl and the brunch, yes. A private floating sauna gives you a rented, photogenic space (rooftop deck, real river views, a genuine cold plunge in the Columbia) for close to two hours, without needing to coordinate a dozen separate reservations across a night out.
```

```markdown
Every bachelorette party photo dump looks the same after a while: matching sashes, a bar with string lights, somebody crying by hour four for reasons nobody remembers by morning. If your bride already knows she doesn't want that, here's the other option nobody tells you about until they've done it: rent the whole sauna.

We book private bachelorette parties often enough that it's a standing GoHighLevel trigger word in our inbox, right next to "birthday" and "corporate." Groups show up expecting a spa day. They leave talking about the cold plunge.

## The Bar Crawl Isn't the Only Option Anymore

A bar crawl is fine. It's also the default, the thing every bachelorette party does because nobody suggested anything else. **A private sauna session flips the whole structure of the day.** Instead of coordinating five reservations, a rideshare budget, and one friend who bails at 9 PM, you book one thing, show up together, and stay together for the entire block of time.

Our floating sauna sits on the Columbia River in North Portland, propane-fired cedar, wet or dry depending on the round, heating to about 190°F and cycling back to 170°F. The rooftop deck looks out over the river. The cold plunge isn't a chilled tub somebody wheeled in for the occasion. It's the actual Columbia, current and all, and it's completely optional. Nobody has to jump. Somebody always does.

## What You Actually Get: The Private Session Breakdown

Here's the real breakdown, no fine print:

- **Whole-boat rental.** Your group only, no strangers sharing the sauna.
- **Up to 10 guests.** A hard cap, not a soft suggestion, so plan your guest list accordingly.
- **$539 for the private session.** One price, covers the group, not per-person.
- **1 hour 45 minutes,** plus a 15-minute transition buffer before and after so you're not rushed in or out.
- **Rooftop deck access** for photos, hanging out between plunges, and the part where everyone just sits and talks.
- **Locker lounge on the dock:** bathroom, heated shower, lockers, and shoe cubbies, so the group isn't fighting over one changing room.
- **Clothing is your call.** Private bookings can go swimsuit or clothing-optional, whatever your group decides ahead of time.
- **Free parking on site.** No garage fee stacked onto the day.

Book it as a standalone, or use it as one stop in a longer day, brunch first, sauna in the afternoon, dinner reservation after everyone's showered and recovered.

## The Part Nobody Puts in the Instagram Caption: Our Alcohol Policy

We're going to be straight with you here instead of dodging it, because a lot of bachelorette planning assumes champagne is automatic at any private booking.

It's not automatic at ours, and here's why: heat and alcohol are a genuinely bad combination. Alcohol messes with your body's ability to regulate temperature and messes with judgment about when to get out of the heat. Our safety policy treats intoxication as a real risk, on par with a heart condition or a recent surgery, not a buzzkill rule we made up. Alcohol isn't part of the standard booking, and it's not allowed inside the sauna itself. If you're planning around drinks for part of the day, ask us directly when you book what's realistic, and plan the actual toast for brunch or dinner instead of the deck.

We'd rather tell you that now than have it be a surprise the morning of. It's a small thing to know ahead of time, and it doesn't take much away from the day, honestly. Most groups don't miss it once they're in the water.

## What the Bride Actually Remembers (Hint: It's the Plunge Face)

Ask anyone who's done a private session what they remember, and it's rarely the sauna heat itself. It's the second right after someone comes up from the plunge, the face they make, the sound that comes out involuntarily. We call it the plunge face internally, because it happens every single time and it's never the same twice.

That's the actual photo you want from this day. Not another posed group shot in front of a neon sign. A real reaction, caught mid-happening, on a boat, on a river, with your closest people watching and laughing.

## Booking It Without It Becoming a Logistics Project

1. **Pick a date and time online.** Private sessions book the same way social ones do, just select "private" and your guest count.
2. **Send the waiver link to your group** ahead of time so nobody's filling it out on their phone in the parking lot.
3. **Confirm your final headcount** a few days out. You can reduce guests on an existing booking, but you'll need everyone re-signed if you add people back.
4. **Show up within your window.** The dock is self-guided, no staff hovering, so arrive anytime inside your session time.
5. **Bring towels, water bottles, and whatever non-alcoholic drinks or snacks you want for the deck.** Two towels per person: one for the sauna, one after.
6. **Decide swimsuit or clothing-optional as a group beforehand,** so nobody's caught off guard at the dock.

That's the whole process. No day-of coordinator required.

## Pair It With: A Full Portland Bachelorette Day

A private session runs under two hours, which leaves the rest of the day open. Groups we've heard from tend to build around it rather than let it be the whole itinerary: brunch on Mississippi Ave or in St. Johns beforehand, the sauna session early-to-mid afternoon when the light on the river is good, dinner reservations somewhere in North or Northeast Portland after everyone's dry and dressed. Hayden Island and the surrounding marina area make a quieter home base than downtown if half your group flew in and wants something that doesn't feel like every other bachelorette city.
```
