## The one lever to fix first: the Counterposition

Lenses 2 and 4 are both at zero, and one rewrite fixes both: say what the studios can't say, to the one person who already books. Don't touch price yet (rule: Veblen last, after 1/2/4 are in place). The Van Westendorp survey (Growth Play #6) and the fake-choice gate (#5) stay queued behind this.

**The line the whole page hangs on:** Studios sell you a cold tub and mood lighting. We put the sauna on a river that's 42 degrees in January and moves. They can't add the river without leaving the building.

### Decision (David, 2026-10-16): ritual STAYS
The word stays. The fix is to make "ritual" mean something only we can mean: three phases that a studio can't do because phase two is a river. The counter-position is against the studio version of the ritual (a tub, a timer, a playlist), not against the word.

**The line the page hangs on, reworked:** The ritual is heat, cold, rest. Studios run it with a tub, a timer and mood lighting. We run it on the Columbia: 42-degree snowmelt with a current for the cold, a deck in the weather for the rest. Same three phases. Only one of them is a river.

**Option B (three phases, flat)**
> **Heat. River. Deck. Repeat until you stop thinking.**
> A three-phase ritual that studios copy with a cold tub and mood lighting. We skipped the tub. The Columbia is the cold phase, 40-plus feet deep and moving, and the deck is the rest phase. Eight people, 1 hour 45, unstaffed, self-guided.

**Option C (one person first)**
> **For the Portlander who's done the studio ritual and wants the river.**
> Same three phases: heat, cold, rest. Different cold. Ours is Cascade snowmelt at 42 degrees in winter, with a current, down a ladder off the back of a floating sauna. 8 guests, 1 hour 45. Bring the group chat.

Rule for every surface from here: "ritual" always travels with its three phases named and with the river in the cold slot. Never "ritualized self-care," never "your ritual," never "intention." The word describes a sequence we run, not a feeling we sell. That keeps the Brand Story ("Ritual over transaction") and the Nemesis ("No wellness guru energy") on the same side.

Keep the Site Updates spec's definition sentence directly under whichever H1 wins (AI-extractable specificity, already approved):
