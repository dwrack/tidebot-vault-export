# Creative Collaborator Search (Aug 2026)

**Status:** Drafts staged, nothing sent. Sourcing research in progress.
**Owner:** Davey. All outreach requires his approval before send.

## The person we're looking for

- Young, hungry, early in their creative career (student or a few years out)
- Shoots AND edits. Phone-first is fine if the eye is there. Owns the whole loop: capture, cut, caption, post
- Not afraid of AI. Uses it as a tool (scripting, cutdowns, generation, iteration) instead of treating it as a threat
- Absorbs fast. We hand them the brand voice doc and the reel recipe once, they run with it
- Wants to be part of something fun, not clock hours

## The deal (approved by Davey, 2026-08-27)

- **Comp:** Starts as trade: free Ember 2 membership (8 guest passes) + a real seat on the team. Pay is real and will happen for the right person who clears the initial hurdles. Hire slowly, test the waters first.
- **Age:** 18+
- **Hours:** Starts small, grows with them if they deliver
- **Vetting:** Past work only. Reel or profile review, then a conversation. No test project required.
- **Reporting:** They join the team Slack and work in the open there, no single boss day-to-day
- **What they get to say:** shot brand content for a floating sauna on the Columbia. That's a portfolio line nobody else in Portland has.
- **To-do before they start posting:** loop in Lemonade PDX (she owns the calendar and doesn't know this role is coming yet)

## Why someone says yes to this

The sauna is the pay. A floating sauna at golden hour is the best free set in Portland, unlimited access to it, creative freedom, an owner who actually ships content, and AI tools already wired up (reel recipe, caption system, publish pipeline). For a student building a reel, that beats $20/hr filming a car dealership.

## What they'd actually do

- Shoot sessions, the dock, the river, the V2 build as it happens
- Edit reels in the house style (hook-first, Inter captions, BASAL end card)
- Experiment: AI-assisted cutdowns, new formats, whatever they want to try
- Work alongside the SMM (Lemonade PDX owns strategy/calendar; this person is the camera + edit muscle and the experimental arm)
