## Shape
1. **Every Tuesday, noon to close: Rising Tide.** Sliding scale ([$20] / [$35] / [$50], pick what fits, no proof). Replaces the SERVICE code. "A rising tide lifts all boats."
2. **Once a month, Tuesday evening: Family Meal.** Industry term for the staff meal before service. Potluck on the deck, guest DJ, ticketed and capped. Once Private Saunas 1 + 2 open, run all three saunas at once (8 + 4 + 4 = 16 seats per round, 2 rounds = ~32 tickets). Sell to the waitlist first.
3. Start monthly so it sells out. Go every other week only after two sellouts in a row.

## Family Meal add-on: the Dock Walk (added 2026-10-19)
A runway moment built into each Family Meal night. Guests walk the dock to the sauna door like a collection drop. Gives every night a content payoff and a reason to post.

- **When:** blue hour, between round 1 and round 2, so it doubles as the reset. 10 to 15 min, DJ drops one walk-off track.
- **The runway:** the dock path to the sauna door. Warm LED strip along both edges, steam rolling out of the door at the end. Ends at the door; no cameras inside the ritual space.
- **The looks:** guests walk in whatever they're sauna-ing in. Robes, towel turbans, felt sauna hats, a blanket over the shoulders, fresh-out-of-the-river slick hair. A rack of loaner statement robes for people who didn't bring a look.
- **The shot:** one locked camera at the end of the dock shoots every walk from the same spot. Post as a grid the next day ("Family Meal, [Month] Collection") and send each guest their clip.
- **Props:** giant thermometer at the start of the walk, umbrella at the end (rain to heat).
- **Rotating collab:** each month a PDX vintage shop or designer dresses 4 to 6 walkers. Free partner slot that brings their audience.
- **Rules:** opt in only, no one is pulled up. Ask before tagging or posting identifiable guests (PRIVACY-HOLD). Wet dock, so walk, don't strut; tether anything freestanding.
