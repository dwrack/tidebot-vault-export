# Blog: Portland Long-Tail Keyword Map (Oct 2026)

Read-only research. Nothing published, nothing sent.

## Data date and what is estimated

- **Pulled 2026-10-20.** DataForSEO (DFS) Google Ads search volume, **Oregon level** (location 21170), 12-month average to Sep 2026. DFS Labs does not support Oregon or Portland, so Portland-only volumes are not available. Oregon is the closest honest number; expect Portland metro to be roughly 40 to 60 percent of it (that ratio is my estimate, not data).
- **"10" means a floor**, not a count. Google Ads rounds tiny terms up. Treat 10 as "someone searches this, maybe 0 to 10 a month".
- **"No data"** means DFS returned nothing. The row is marked ESTIMATE and difficulty is a guess.
- **Difficulty is judged from the live SERP** (Google organic live, Oregon, 2026-10-20, top 10 to 14 results), not from the tool's keyword-difficulty score. Rows marked **SERP NOT pulled** are estimates. 19 SERPs were read.
- **Competition index** in DFS (0 to 100) measures advertisers bidding, not organic difficulty. High values on date and things-to-do terms mostly reflect ad buyers.
- **GSC** = Google Search Console, sc-domain:ebbandember.com, 2026-07-21 to 2026-10-18 (top 400 queries). It has no long-tail yet because there is no blog. Everything we rank for lands on the homepage.
- **Score /25** = volume (1 to 5) + SERP ease (1 to 5) + 2 x intent (1 to 5) + fit (1 to 5). Intent counts double because a booking-intent reader is worth more than a pageview. Scores are judgment calls, not data.
- Builds on, and does not redo: `Content Calendar — Prioritized Blog Queue (Jun 2026).md`, `Website - Best Saunas in Portland Guide (Oct 2026).md` (live at /best-saunas-portland), `Launches/Pulse 2026-10-13 — sauna portland SERP.md`, `Keyword & Content Opportunities (Oct 2026).md`. Drafts already in Marketing/ for bachelorette, Vancouver WA, PDX airport, and cold plunge FAQ should be reused, not rewritten.

## The honest read

1. **Portland-only long-tail volume is small.** Most rows here are 10 to 210 a month. The play is many cheap, specific posts that each convert, plus citations in AI answers, not one big traffic post. The one big number is Hayden Island (3,600), and its searchers want real estate, hotels and Jantzen Beach, so it builds authority more than bookings.
2. **ebbandember.com ranks for none of the 19 SERPs I pulled.** The only Ebb & Ember mention anywhere was a FOX 12 Facebook post at #10 on "cold plunge portland". Earned media is getting the brand into results; the site is not.
3. **Winter is the window.** "sauna portland" runs 720 in Jun/Jul to 1,900 in Dec/Jan (DFS). "rainy day activities portland" peaked at 590 in Oct 2025 and 390 in Nov. Publish the seasonal posts by mid-November.
4. **Weakest SERPs (verified):** rainy day activities, Hayden Island, bachelorette party, what to wear to a sauna. Several results are 2006 to 2016 forum threads, and Portland, Maine pages pollute "bachelorette", "group activities" and "rainy day". Put "Portland, Oregon" in those titles.
5. **Hardest (skip as blog targets):** sauna temperature (retailers + full AI overview), unique things to do in portland (Atlas Obscura, Travel Portland, 2026 blogs), sauna near me (local pack), sauna and cold plunge without a city (shopping SERP).

## Copy rules for whoever writes these
