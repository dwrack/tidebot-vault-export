## The 5 topics

1. **The river is the point** — Cascade snowmelt, 42° in winter, real current, can't be replicated in a building
2. **The ritual, not the service** — heat, cold, recovery. 1 hour 45 because that's how long slowing down takes.
3. **Anti-wellness-industry** — no cortisol claims, no optimization, no guru energy
4. **First-timer nerves** — what the cold actually feels like, what to bring, what happens if you hate it
5. **Portland and the season** — what the Columbia does in January vs July

## The 3 formats

- **Myth-bust** (easy) — the anti-wellness position is a content engine
- **Breakdown** (easy) — the three phases, what to bring, the timeline
- **Cinematic, no talking** (ambitious) — steam, cold water, weather. The product is sensory.

## The 2 layouts

- **A — Static camera, ambient sound, minimal cuts.** Slow the edit down to match the product.
- **B — Text on screen over water/steam b-roll.** Quiet type, lots of negative space.

## Cover type

Quiet lowercase serif, generous negative space. Loud type would break this brand. Everything else in the wellness feed is shouting, so the restraint is the differentiator.
