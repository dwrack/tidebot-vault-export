# Bachelorette Party in Portland, Oregon: Take the Whole Group to a Sauna on the River

For a bachelorette party in Portland, Oregon, one low-key option is a Private Party sauna session on the Columbia River: up to 12 people, the whole dock to yourselves, a cedar sauna and a cold plunge into the river if anyone wants it. It works well as the one anchor activity in a weekend, next to a dinner and a day out. Here's how it works, what it costs, and what else is worth doing.

## Why a sauna works for this

Most bachelorette weekends in Portland follow the same pattern: brunch, a bar crawl, a late dinner. It's fun and it's exhausting. By Saturday night half the group is quietly wishing for a chair.

A sauna session is the opposite, and it's the kind of thing a group can do without a plan. Nobody has to perform. You sit, you talk, you step outside, and you sit again. The bride gets a photo on the dock. The friend who hates loud places gets a quiet room. The friend who loves a dare gets the river.

It also works in the rain, which matters in Portland from October to May.

## What a Private Party session looks like

You book the dock for your group. Up to 12 people. Sessions are self-guided, so there's no host leading you through anything, and you move at whatever pace the group wants.

A typical group does it like this:

1. Arrive, change, put your things in the changing area.
2. Sit in the cedar sauna together.
3. Step out onto the deck. Cool off in the air or take the ladder down into the river.
4. Go back in. Repeat.
5. Take photos on the dock before you leave.

The cold plunge is optional. The Columbia has current, so anyone going in should go with a buddy and use the life jackets on the dock. It's totally fine to sit on the ladder or skip the water altogether.

**One rule.** No alcohol on the dock. Heat and alcohol don't mix, and it's a rule we hold for everyone. Plan the toast for after, off the property, or the night before.

## Where it fits in the weekend

A realistic Portland bachelorette itinerary:

**Friday night.** Dinner somewhere easy. Many groups choose a place in Northeast or Southeast Portland so nobody has to drive downtown.

**Saturday morning.** Brunch. You will find a line at any good spot, so book ahead.

**Saturday afternoon.** The sauna session. Rain or shine. Plan to leave for dinner 30 to 60 minutes after you finish, since the group will be hungry.

**Saturday night.** Dinner, then a show. Darcelle XV Showplace is a Portland classic and a sure bet for a group. Check what's on and book ahead.

**Sunday.** A slow morning. Powell's, a coffee, a walk in Washington Park.

## FAQ

### How many people can come to a bachelorette party at Ebb & Ember?
A Private Party session holds up to 12 people. For smaller groups, we're opening small private saunas for 2 to 4 people on [date].

### How much does a bachelorette party sauna session cost in Portland?
Private Party is [$500/$600] for the group, depending on the day. That's roughly [$50-60] per person at 10 people.

### Can we bring champagne to the sauna?
No. There is no alcohol on the dock. Heat and alcohol are a bad mix, so we ask everyone to wait until after.

### Do we have to get in the Columbia River?
No. The cold plunge is optional. Some guests just use the sauna and the deck. If you do go in, use the ladder, wear a life jacket, and go with a buddy.

### What should the group bring?
Swimsuits, towels, flip flops or shoes that come off easily, and a change of clothes. Bring a water bottle too. Hair ties help.
