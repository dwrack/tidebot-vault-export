# Small-Team Holiday Party Ideas in Portland, Oregon (Up to 12 in the Big Sauna)

If you have a small team in Portland, Oregon and a holiday party to plan, you have more options than a loud restaurant. For up to 12 people, you can book a Private Party session at a cedar sauna on the Columbia River, take a lights walk, or book a dinner in a private room. This list covers the options, costs and the practical stuff a planner needs.

## What small teams actually want

A team of eight or twelve has different needs from a company of eighty. You don't need a ballroom. You need:

- A place everyone can get to, with parking or transit
- Something to do other than stand and talk
- A plan that works for the person who doesn't drink and the person who doesn't like crowds
- A cost that fits a small budget

Most of what people book doesn't meet all four. Here are some that do.

## Options

### A Private Party sauna session on the Columbia

Ebb & Ember is a cedar sauna docked at Elevated Tides Marina, 173 NE Bridgeton Rd, North Portland, next to Hayden Island. A Private Party books the whole sauna for your group, up to 12 people, for a session of [length, confirm]. Price is [$500/$600] depending on the day. That is roughly [$42-50] a person for a full team of 12.

What it's like. You change, sit in the cedar sauna together, step out onto the deck, and take the ladder into the Columbia if anyone wants. The cold plunge is optional. Sessions are self-guided, so you set the pace. It's a good icebreaker, because nobody's pretending. There's nowhere to bring a laptop.

Practical notes:
- **No alcohol on the dock.** Plan the toast for dinner. It makes it easier for the people who don't drink.
- **Swimwear is needed.** Say so in the invite, and let people opt out of the water without comment.
- **The river has current.** Everyone uses the life jackets and goes in with a buddy.
- **December is dark by about 4:30.** An afternoon session ends around dusk, which looks good on the water.

If you want the whole dock for a bigger group, a whole-dock buyout starts at [$1,500]. See the [corporate page](/corporate) for the details.

### A private room at a restaurant

The default for a reason. Many Portland restaurants will hold a back room for 8 to 14 people, often with a prix fixe menu. Book in early November, because the good ones are gone by Thanksgiving. Ask about minimums and whether they can handle dietary needs.

### Portland lights

Two classics. Peacock Lane in Southeast Portland is a residential street where the neighbors decorate for December. ZooLights at the Oregon Zoo is a ticketed event [check current dates]. Both work as a pre-dinner walk, and neither takes a lot of planning.

### A volunteer shift

A half day at the Oregon Food Bank, or a local nonprofit, is a good option for teams that want something less party-like. Contact them early, since slots fill in November.

### A cooking class or workshop

Several Portland studios do private group classes for 8 to 12. Good for teams that like doing something with their hands. Check for minimum group sizes and dietary requirements.
