# Hayden Island, Portland, Oregon: A Local's Guide to the Island, the Marina and the River

Hayden Island is a flat island in the Columbia River at the northern edge of Portland, Oregon, sitting between Oregon and Vancouver, Washington. It's known for Jantzen Beach shopping, floating homes and marinas, and a view of the I-5 bridge. This guide covers how to get there, what's on it, and what's just across the water on Bridgeton Road.

## What Hayden Island is

Hayden Island is part of the city of Portland, but it feels like its own town. It sits in the Columbia River, with the Oregon shore to the south and the Washington shore to the north. Interstate 5 runs across the middle of it on the Interstate Bridge.

Most people know it for one thing: Jantzen Beach. The name comes from the old amusement park and, later, the shopping center that took its place. There is no sales tax in Oregon, which is a big part of why people still drive there to shop.

The rest of the island is quieter than you'd expect. There are marinas, a few restaurants, floating homes tied up along the shore, and a lot of river.

## What's on the island

**Jantzen Beach Center.** Big-box retail and a few restaurants. Practical, not pretty.

**Marinas and moorage.** The water around the island is full of boats from spring through fall.

**Floating homes.** A community of houses on floats lines part of the shore. If you've ever wondered what living on the Columbia looks like, you can see it from the road.

**River views.** The best are from the north shore of the island, where you look across to Vancouver and the bridge.

## What's across from the island: Bridgeton Road

Just east of the island is the Bridgeton neighborhood and Bridgeton Road, which runs along the south shore of the Columbia in North Portland. It's mostly marinas, floating homes and light industry, and a lot of people drive right past without noticing it.

## FAQ

### Is Hayden Island part of Portland, Oregon?
Yes. Hayden Island is part of the city of Portland, even though it sits in the Columbia River and is closer to Vancouver, Washington than to downtown.

### What is Hayden Island known for?
Jantzen Beach shopping, large marinas, floating homes and views of the Columbia River and the I-5 bridge. Oregon has no sales tax, which still draws shoppers across the river.
