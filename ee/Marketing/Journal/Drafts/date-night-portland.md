# Date Night in Portland, Oregon That Isn't Dinner: A Sauna on the Columbia

A good date night in Portland, Oregon doesn't have to be dinner. Try a pinball bar, a repertory movie, a bookstore, or a sauna session on the Columbia River. They all give you something to do with your hands and something to talk about. This list covers the options we like and the one we run.

## Why skip dinner

Dinner is fine. But a table across from each other for two hours is a hard format for a first date, a tired date, or a date between people who already know every story. It's also what everyone else is doing on a Friday.

An activity solves the awkward bits. You have something to react to, and when the conversation goes quiet, it doesn't feel like a problem.

## Ideas that work in Portland

**Ground Kontrol.** An arcade and pinball bar on Burnside. Loud, silly, and easy. Good for a second or third date.

**Hollywood Theatre.** The nonprofit cinema on NE Sandy runs repertory titles and old prints. Check what's playing and sit in the dark together.

**Powell's City of Books.** Pick a section, each pick a book for the other, and meet at the cafe. It's a cheap date and a surprisingly good one.

**Hopscotch.** A bar with games, if you want something more playful than a drink [confirm current format].

**A walk on the Eastbank Esplanade.** Free. Walk the river on the east side, then get a coffee or a cone.

**A sauna session on the Columbia.** The one we run. Details below.

## The sauna as a date

Ebb & Ember is a cedar sauna docked at Elevated Tides Marina, 173 NE Bridgeton Rd, in North Portland, next to Hayden Island. You walk down the dock, change, and sit in the heat. There's no table and nothing to order. If you want to, you step out and take the ladder into the Columbia. If you don't, you don't.

What makes it work for two people:

- **It's slow.** There's no schedule inside the session. You sit and talk, or you don't.
- **The setting does the work.** The river, the light, the weather. Rain on the water is part of it.
- **You can leave the cold plunge out.** The cold plunge is optional, and nobody has to prove anything.
- **It ends on a good note.** After a session you'll want a meal. Dinner afterward feels earned.

A few honest caveats. Regular sessions are social, which means other guests may be on the dock [confirm session size and whether strangers share]. If you want just the two of you, small private saunas for 2 to 4 people are opening [date] at [$200/$250], and until then a Private Party for up to 12 at [$500/$600] is a lot for a pair. Sessions are self-guided, so nobody leads you through anything. And the river has current, so use the life jackets on the dock and go in together.

Prices for standard sessions are $35 for one hour or $50 for two on weekdays, and $60 for a 1 hour 45 minute weekend session. No alcohol on the dock. If you want a drink, make it dinner afterward.

## A full night

If you want to build an evening around it:

1. **Late afternoon.** A sauna session at the marina on Bridgeton Road.
2. **After.** Dinner in North or Northeast Portland. Pick somewhere casual, with a lot of food.
3. **Later.** A movie at the Hollywood, or a few rounds of pinball.

Or in reverse: a movie first, a late sauna session on a weekend.

## FAQ

### What are good date night ideas in Portland, Oregon that aren't dinner?
Pinball at Ground Kontrol, a movie at the Hollywood Theatre, a bookstore date at Powell's, a river walk on the Eastbank Esplanade, or a sauna session on the Columbia River at Ebb & Ember.

### Is a sauna a good first date?
It can be, if you both want something low-pressure. It's quiet, there's no phone, and you can opt out of the cold plunge. If you're not sure about swimwear, pick an easier first activity.
