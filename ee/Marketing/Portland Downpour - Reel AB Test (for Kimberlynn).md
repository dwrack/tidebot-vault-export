## How we're testing (recommend)

- Stagger one every 2-3 days on @ebbandember IG Reels, same time of day, so they don't cannibalize each other.
- Optionally run two on TikTok in parallel (different platform = clean read).
- The metric that matters is **3-second retention / average watch time**, not raw views. That's the hook doing its job.
- Whichever hook holds attention best, we make that the template and batch the rest of the library to match.
