# Portland Downpour - Reel A/B Test

Four cuts of the same Portland Downpour bucket-plunge clip, each with a different opening hook, same sales end card (THE PORTLAND DOWNPOUR + book at ebbandember.com). Goal: find which hook flies, and drive bookings.

Three of the four lean on the Portland-rain joke (a downpour you choose, on a sunny day) so the hook points straight at the product. One is a feeling-based benchmark. We dropped the "66°F" and "river reset" versions: 66 isn't impressively cold in summer (saving it for winter when the Columbia hits ~40°F), and "river reset" sold a vibe without pushing the Portland Downpour.

## How we're testing (recommend)

- Stagger one every 2-3 days on @ebbandember IG Reels, same time of day.
- The metric that matters is 3-second retention / average watch time, not raw views. That's the hook working.
- Whichever hook holds attention best becomes the template, and we batch the rest of the library to match.
- My guess: G or H win on watch time (the joke earns the watch), B converts steadiest. We test instead of guess.
