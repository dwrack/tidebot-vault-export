## The answer in five lines

1. **Don't start new accounts. Build the funnel on the @ebbandember handles you already have.** IG has 9.2K followers and beats Othership, Bathhouse and Löyly on likes per follower. A new faceless sauna account would start at zero right as the winter peak (Oct to Feb) starts.
2. **The viral stuff in sauna is cold, humor, dates and builds, carried by creators, not venues.** Venue accounts almost never go viral. Cascada has 1.64M creator views in Portland and zero from its own account. Ebb's best video ever came from a creator (@biigwaterguy, 95K).
3. **Our proven hook is local plans, not wellness.** Both IG breakouts (2,164 and 2,011 likes) were "what to do in Portland tonight / with friends." Health explainers got 7 to 21 likes.
4. **Floating-sauna content gets saved and shared at about 2x the rate of generic sauna content.** That's trip-planning intent, and YouTube and TikTok have almost nothing on floating or river saunas in the PNW. It's an open lane.
5. **Winter is when the demand shows up.** "sauna portland" searches go from 480/mo in summer to 1,600 in January. Oct 1 to Feb 28 is the window. Spend most of the effort there.

## The funnel

1. **Discover:** Reels/TikTok/Shorts plus creator videos. Hook is local plans or the river-plunge moment.
2. **Get curious:** profile. Pinned 3 = what it is, first-timer walkthrough, a creator video. Highlights for FAQ, pricing, private groups.
3. **Talk:** every reel ends with "comment RIVER and we'll DM you the first-timer guide + booking link." Automated DM, needs a ManyChat-type tool on IG. IG ranks DM shares high, so this helps reach too.
4. **Book:** Periode link with UTM per channel and per creator. The tracking bridge already sends conversions to Meta/GA4.
5. **Come back:** members' nights, seasonal sessions, UGC reshares, "tag your sauna buddy." 58% repeat guests already make 67% of revenue, so the funnel exists to create first visits.

## Five content series (fits the existing 532 grid, doesn't replace it)

1. **"Portland plans"** (weekly): date night, friends, visitors, rainy day, after work. The proven winner.
2. **"The first 10 seconds"** (weekly): first-timer's river plunge reaction with permission. Cold hook, ritual payoff.
3. **"Only on the Columbia"** (biweekly): fog, snowmelt temps, bald eagles, the "other river" angle. Save-and-share bait.
4. **The dragged-along friend** (monthly): scripted comedy bit, same character each time.
5. **V2 build** (YouTube long-form + weekly Shorts): from Grant's Build Diary footage.

Every post: original audio, text on screen in the first second, 10-20 sec cut for Shorts/TikTok, and a comment keyword.

## Creator program (the highest-leverage piece)

- Target 4-6 hosted visits a month Oct to Feb. Priority: Portland/PNW travel and date-idea creators with 5K-100K followers. The data says small accounts outperform.
- Give them the hook lines that already worked: "Portland's only floating sauna," price, "something different to do with friends."
- Ask for a collab post with @ebbandember and a creator-specific UTM or promo code so bookings can be counted.
- FTC: creators must disclose the free session. Put it in the invite.
- Reuse [[Creator Outreach Playbook]] and the follower-personas report for the target list.

## Guardrails

- Never "a float" in guest copy. 9 of 89 TikTok captions currently say "book a float." Fix going forward.
- Leave fuel unsaid. Not wood-fired.
- No detox, longevity, immunity or disease claims, and no testimonials making them either. Talk about how it feels, not what it cures.
- No review incentives tied to 5 stars. Staff reviews must disclose.
- Don't use the "+256% thermal gatherings" stat. It has no traceable source.
