# TikTok, Instagram and Threads: what's going viral in sauna (Sept 2026)

Research only. Nothing was liked, followed, commented, posted or messaged. Data pulled 2026-09-25 through the logged-in Chrome bridge (Profile 9, Ebb & Ember). Raw data: `raw/social_raw.json`.

## Bottom line

- **On TikTok, cold beats heat on raw views.** Cold-plunge-only videos have a median of 21.2K views vs 7.8K for sauna-themed videos (345 vs 1,624 videos). 24 of the top 30 niche videos by views are ice bath / cold plunge.
- **The sauna videos that go huge are humor, a moment, or a date idea, not a venue tour.** Top sauna-themed TikToks of the last 12 months: a "almost died" steam clip (24.3M views, 1.5K-follower account), a DIY sauna build (9.4M), no-caption relatable gym-sauna clips (8.8M, 4.4M). The all-time #3 sauna video is a private spa "Date idea" clip: 12.4M views, 225K shares, from an 8K-follower account.
- **Floating/water saunas get fewer views but more saves and shares.** 250 floating/river/lake/sea sauna TikToks: median 5.4K views (below the 7.8K sauna median), but save rate 0.54% vs 0.37% and share rate 0.48% vs 0.26%. People bookmark and send these. That's trip-planning intent.
- **Creators, not venues, carry the viral posts.** Floating sauna winners are travel creators (coolstays 1.9M, chrislawlor "Hotboat Cruise" 1.3M, mainelycass Maine floating sauna 596K + 574K). In Portland, Cascada shows up in 19 TikToks totaling 1.64M views, median 42K, and none of the 19 are from Cascada's own account.
- **Ebb & Ember is already the Portland floating sauna that creators film.** 23 TikToks in the sample mention Ebb (228K total views). The best one is @biigwaterguy (724K followers), 95K views. The price hook from @sydshohen ("for $49 you get a spot at this amazing floating sauna for 1hr45min") did 46K.
- **Original audio wins on TikTok.** 68% of niche videos use original audio, and they get a median of 12.2K views vs 4.8K for licensed tracks. 72 of the top 100 use original audio. The one licensed sound that keeps showing up in winners is "snowfall" (Øneheart & reidenshi): 13 videos, median 145K, all cold plunge "Day N" clips.
- **On Instagram, Reels beat carousels beat photos** in the discovery sample (median likes 148 / 108.5 / 46). But benchmark accounts' biggest recent posts are events, milestones, giveaways and one scripted comedy series (Othership "Episode 4: The Group Chat", 38.5K likes vs a 603 median).
- **Ebb's IG engagement rate is ahead of most big benchmarks.** 6.3 median likes per 1K followers vs Othership 4.2, Hot Box 5.4, Löyly 1.8, Bathhouse 1.7.
- **Threads could not be properly accessed.** Profile 9 is not logged in to Threads. Logged-out search returned 172 posts (81 on topic, mostly cold plunge) and "No results" for "sauna culture", "floating sauna", "banya" and "saunaculture". Treat the Threads section as a thin sample.

## Method (and limits)

- **TikTok:** 15 search queries (sauna, saunatok, cold plunge, contrast therapy, sauna culture, social sauna, floating sauna, sauna club, ice bath, sauna benefits, portland sauna, sauna date, finnish sauna, banya, aufguss) plus 15 tag pages (#sauna, #saunatok, #coldplunge, #contrasttherapy, #saunaculture, #socialsauna, #floatingsauna, #saunaclub, #icebath, #saunabenefits, #portlandsauna, #saunadate, #finnishsauna, #banya, #aufguss), scrolled about 6 pages each. Data came from TikTok's own search/tag API responses (views, likes, comments, shares, saves, date, author followers, duration, sound). 2,340 unique videos; 1,969 niche-relevant after filtering captions/hashtags for sauna, plunge, ice bath, aufguss, banya etc. and dropping ads and Indonesian "banya" (means "a lot") false positives. 1,386 of the relevant videos were posted in the last 12 months.
- Plus 8 TikTok benchmark profiles (675 videos).
- **Instagram:** 9 hashtag pages (top + recent grid, about 30 posts each) plus IG keyword search for 8 terms (sauna, floating sauna, cold plunge, sauna date, contrast therapy, sauna culture, social sauna, portland sauna). 398 unique posts. Plus 18 benchmark profiles (latest 12 to 24 posts each). Reel view counts come through on hashtag/search posts but NOT on profile grids, so benchmark tables use likes and comments. Wild Haus and Knot Springs hide like counts on every post.
- **Hashtag "top" grids on IG are not date-limited**, so some top posts are years old. The top-20 IG table is limited to the last 24 months; older evergreen hits are listed separately.
- **Format tags** are assigned by caption keywords, so they're rough. A video can carry more than one tag.
- **Outlier ratio = views / author followers** (TikTok only, min 100K views and 500 followers).
- Allas Sea Pool (Helsinki) could not be found on IG under allasseapool or allas_sea_pool. Everett House, Loyly Portland and Soak had no IG search match. Community Sauna Baths has no TikTok account.
