# Benchmark Refresh: sauna venues on Instagram and TikTok (Oct 13 2026)

Read-only research. Nothing was liked, followed, commented, posted or messaged. Data pulled 2026-10-13 with Apify (Ebb & Ember account only). Window: posts from 2026-08-14 to 2026-10-13 (last 60 days). Compares against [[02 TikTok Instagram Threads Findings]] and [[04 Channel Funnel Plan (DRAFT)]] from Sept 24 to 29.

## The answer

1. **Ebb & Ember is in the top group for engagement per follower, and the best on TikTok in this set.** On IG, Ebb's median is 8.6 likes per 1K followers. That beats every account above 40K followers (Othership 3.3, Hot Box 4.2, Bathhouse 1.9, Social Club 3.6, Löyly 1.7). Only two small floating saunas beat it among accounts over 5K (Kiln 12.6, SAUN°A 10.1). On TikTok Ebb has the highest plays per follower of any account with a real sample (1.4 vs Hot Box 0.1, Wildhaus 0.04).
2. **Ebb is weak on absolute reach.** Median reel plays are 3.5K vs 41K for Hot Box, 33K for Othership and Bathhouse, 22K for Löyly. Ebb ranks 10th of 19 on median reel plays. The gap is follower count, not content quality.
3. **The thing that changed in three weeks: Ebb's Oct 6 TikTok "name a better combo. we'll wait."** 21,000 plays and 1,394 likes, about 9x Ebb's TikTok median. The same video on IG did 209 likes and 7.3K plays. Hot plus cold as a dare, 9 seconds, comment bait, no wellness claims.
4. **Venue openings, location reveals and countdowns are the winning format right now.** Four of the biggest non-giveaway posts in the set are "new space coming" content (details in the findings below).
5. **Giveaways still own comments.** Bathhouse 626, Kelowna 1,166, Löyly 776 on top posts. Ebb's last giveaway was Dec 2025.

## What is working now (last 60 days)

1. **New-space reveals and opening countdowns.** Sauna Social Club's "4 days to go" reel from the Peckham build did 74K plays and 1,688 likes (median 11K plays), the biggest in their feed. ## Method and limits

- **Instagram:** `apify/instagram-scraper` (pay per result, $0.0027 per result on this plan). Three kinds of runs: profile details for follower counts (13 main accounts plus 5 user-search runs), a posts run for the 13 benchmark accounts (30 per account, newer than 60 days, 247 results), and a posts run for 9 added venues (plus two guessed Cascada handles). One of the user-search runs was aborted after returning its first 10 profiles to hold cost.
- **TikTok:** `clockworks/tiktok-scraper` ($0.0037 per video plus $0.0013 date-filter add-on and a $0.001 start fee). One run for 12 benchmark handles (94 videos, 4 handles not found) and one run for 8 added venues (cap 30 per profile, 60-day date filter).
- **Added accounts** were found by Apify user search (floating sauna, sauna portland, sauna social club, sauna boat, bathhouse sauna, cold plunge sauna club, sauna in Seattle, Chicago, Los Angeles, New York, Denver). A single snapshot cannot show follower growth, so "fast-growing" was not verified. I picked venues that returned active posting in the last 60 days. Most US results were small mobile or dockside saunas.
- **Limits:** the scraper returns a sample of each feed. Like counts are hidden on Wildhaus, Knot Springs and most Redrock posts. Reel plays are not available for images or carousels. IG saves and shares are not exposed, so share-rate comparisons from the Sept research could not be repeated. Per-1K numbers for accounts under 6K followers are noisy. Hook descriptions come from captions only, I did not watch the videos. Everett House and Soak were not found. Outlier = at least 5x the account's own median.
- **Spend:** about $1.68 in Apify usage (IG roughly $1.15, TikTok roughly $0.52). Budget was $5.
- **Raw data** is saved in `raw/` with the suffix `_20261013`: `ig_posts_main`, `ig_posts_extra`, `ig_details_main`, `ig_details_cascada_guess`, `ig_usersearch_floating`, `ig_usersearch_portland`, `ig_usersearch_socialclub`, `ig_usersearch_misc`, `ig_usersearch_us`, `tiktok_profiles`, `tiktok_profiles_extra`.
