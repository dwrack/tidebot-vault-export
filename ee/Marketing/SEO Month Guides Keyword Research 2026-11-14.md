## Recommended changes

1. Add a "Columbia River water temperature by month" table near the top of /columbia-river/ with one 40 to 60 word answer. Link it from every month page. This is the best win.
2. Add "Is [month] a good time to visit Portland?" to every month FAQ. Google asks it for each month.
3. Christmas Ships: add a section to the December page on where to watch the Columbia fleet, and on watching from the sauna deck. Do it before Dec 1, since the search peak is December.
4. Month pages: add H2s for the big events (Rose Festival in June, cherry blossoms in March, tulips in April, New Year's Eve in December, Fourth of July in July). That gets them into People Also Ask, even though they won't rank for the event name itself.
5. January: add a "New Year's Day plunge in Portland" section.

## Search seasonality (publish or refresh 4 to 6 weeks before each peak)
Searches for each month start climbing about 2 months ahead. "Portland weather in January" peaks in December (2,900); "portland in october" peaks in September.
