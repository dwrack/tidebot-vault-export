# Marketing State of Play

Updated Oct 8. Numbers cover Sept 8 to Oct 7, compared with the 30 days before.

## How we're doing

**Bookings held, revenue dipped.** 331 bookings (was 343) and $19,175 in website revenue (was $23,447). Same number of people booking, but they're spending less per booking: about $58 now vs $68. That's the end-of-summer shift toward social seats over privates, and it should swing back when the new private saunas open.

**Website traffic is down 20%** (1,635 visits). Google search clicks are flat at about 50 a day, and almost all of it is people already searching our name.

**Instagram is picking up.** About 41K accounts reached, roughly the same as last month, but the last week was our strongest stretch since August. Reels are doing the work:

- Fall Equinox reel: 297 likes
- Member Night recap reel: 252 likes
- "name a better combo" reel (Oct 5): 199 likes in two days
- Oslo Sauna Congress carousel: 198 likes, the only carousel that's really landed

**Reviews:** 123 on Google, still 4.9 stars.

## What needs fixing

1. **Google Ads has stopped running.** Both campaigns are switched on but got zero impressions for the whole month. Most likely billing or a policy hold. Someone needs to log in and check.
2. **Seven Google reviews are waiting on a reply** (Oct 8, Oct 6 x2, Oct 5, Sept 20 x2, Sept 16).
3. **Website catch-up with Jess:** Moonlight Sauna listing, the 10pm closing time, retiring the Summer Punch Pass, and the new cancellation policy on the FAQ.
4. **Product launches vs. what customers see.** The Equinox mix-up showed it: when we create a new session, the post, the site and Periode all need to say the same thing before it goes live.
