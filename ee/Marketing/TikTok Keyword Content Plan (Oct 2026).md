# TikTok Keyword Content Plan (Oct 2026)

These are search terms people actually typed before finding our TikToks (TikTok Studio, seen Oct 13 to 19, 2026). Search is about 1 to 8% of our TikTok views, but these terms show what people want. Each idea below has a blog post, an Instagram post and a TikTok aimed at the same words.

**TikTok search rule:** say the exact phrase out loud in the first 3 seconds, put it in the on-screen text, and lead the caption with it. TikTok indexes speech, on-screen text and caption.

**Copy rules:** don't call the sauna wood-fired. Don't quote prices until the new pricing is final. Private saunas hold 2 to 4 people. A Private Party books the big sauna for a group.
