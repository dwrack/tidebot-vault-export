## Anchor text risks

- **"Book Now" used 4+ times sitewide** linking to external Periode URLs. Vary the anchors: "Book Social Session", "Book Private Session", "Reserve Banya Monday".
- **"Become a Member" used 2x in nav + body** with identical anchor. Vary to "View Memberships", "Compare Membership Tiers".
- **No exact-match anchor for "cold plunge Portland" anywhere on site.** Once /cold-plunge ships, ensure 4-6 internal links use that exact phrase or close variants.
