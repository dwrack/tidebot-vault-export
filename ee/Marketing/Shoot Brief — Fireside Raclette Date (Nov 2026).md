# Shoot Brief: Fireside Raclette Date (Nov 2026)

**Talent:** Davey + Jacqueline (the couple)
**Feeds:** rain date, date night, anniversary and birthday blog posts; IG carousel + Reel; GBP winter posts
**Status:** planning. Date, shooter and fire setup not set.

## The idea
After the sauna and plunge, a couple wraps up in robes on the deck and melts raclette by the fire. Steam coming off their shoulders, cheese scraped onto potatoes and bread, river going dark behind them. The post-sauna glow plus food is the date night.

## Timing
- Blue hour, about 4:30 to 5:15pm in early-mid November (sunset ~4:45pm). Warm firelight against the blue sky is the shot.
- Light rain is a bonus, not a cancel: shoot under cover with rain in the frame.

## Setup
- **Raclette:** tabletop electric raclette grill (no open flame needed for the cheese). Or a half-wheel raclette with a heat lamp for the scrape shot if we can borrow one.
- **Cheese:** ask Cow Bell PDX or Speak Your Rind to supply it in exchange for a credit + tag. Turns the shoot into the partner feature for the blog.
- **Sides:** small potatoes, cornichons, pickled onions, crusty bread. Non-alcoholic drinks only in frame (hot cider, tea, NA spritz).
- **Fire:** CONFIRM with the marina first whether an open flame is allowed on the dock. Fallback: gas fire table or a cluster of lanterns/candles.
- **Wardrobe:** E&E robes over swimwear, wool socks, a blanket. Wet hair is good.
- **Props:** wooden board, linen napkins, enamel mugs. Nothing branded except E&E.

## Shot list
1. Wide: couple on the deck, fire + sauna glowing behind, river at blue hour (hero)
2. Cheese scrape close-up, slow-mo, stretch off the knife onto potatoes (Reel hook, first 2 seconds)
3. Steam off shoulders, walking from the sauna to the table
4. Hands passing a mug, fire in the foreground blur
5. Candid laugh across the table
6. Overhead of the spread on the board
7. Plunge splash, then cut to cheese (the hot-cold-hot Reel structure)
8. Detail: robe, wet hair, firelight on faces
9. Vertical versions of 1, 2 and 7 for Stories/Reels

## Rules

- Sauna door stays shut in fire shots so it reads as the deck, not food in the sauna.
