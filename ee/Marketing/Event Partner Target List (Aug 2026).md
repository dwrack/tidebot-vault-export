# Event Partner Target List — August 2026

*Companion to `Event Brand Partnership Framework.md`. Working pipeline for brand partners on the quarterly produced events.*

---

## How this list is tiered

Not by how well a brand fits. By **who can actually write the check.**

A Portland soap maker with four employees is a great brand fit and will never pay $6,000. Tiering by check size is the difference between a pipeline and a wish list.

- **Tier A — Presenting capable ($6,000+).** Real marketing budgets. Big regional or national.
- **Tier B — Supporting or Featured ($2,000 to $3,500).** Mid-size, has a marketing line item, decision-maker is reachable.
- **Tier C — Product Partners (in-kind).** Small local makers. Cost us nothing, make the take-home bag excellent, and they're the easiest yeses. Good for filling out event one.

Pitch Tier A first for every event. There's only one Presenting slot and it funds most of the budget.

## Warm intro paths (use these before cold email)

1. **Early Bird Communications** (Mack John, Jordan Culberson). We already pay them. Brand-partnership introductions are squarely in their lane and we have not asked. **Ask at the next biweekly.**
2. **Existing vault outreach machinery.** Don't rebuild what exists: `Cold Plunge Dealer Outreach`, `Lodging Outreach — Tracker`, `Creator Outreach Playbook`, `UGC Outreach & Repost Playbook`, `Partner Outreach Drafts (Pending)`.
3. **Sesame Collective.** Existing partnership relationship. Ask who else they'd introduce us to.
4. **Members.** With 1,500+ customers and a 58% repeat rate, some of them work at these companies. A single Slack or email asking "do you work somewhere that should be at this?" is the cheapest lead source we have.
5. **Danesh, Linnea, and other programming partners.** They have their own wellness-adjacent networks.

## Who to watch, not pitch

Knot Springs, Löyly, Common Ground, Everett House, and the SWTHZ-style contrast studios are competitors, not partners.

**Othership** (Toronto and NYC) is the closest thing to a national model for what we're doing. Worth tracking: KANE Footwear has run private brand events at their locations, which is exactly the deal we're selling. Use it as proof the model works when a brand hesitates.
