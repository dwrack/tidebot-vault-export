## Sept 8 refresh — channel analytics (Aug 9 – Sep 7 vs prior 30 days)

**Revenue (GA4):** $23,447, up 43%. 343 bookings, up 36%. Sessions actually fell 22% (2,049), so conversion is way up: fewer visitors, better ones. AOV holding at $68.

**Search (GSC):** 1,485 clicks (~50/day), down 28% vs the viral-halo window, but the trend flipped: September is running 55-66 clicks/day vs 35-45 mid-August. Still ~90% branded. "floating sauna portland" at position 2.5 (76 clicks). The generic-term CTR problem from the Aug report is unchanged, and the pillar pages still don't exist.

**Instagram:** ~44.8K reach vs ~244K prior (that window had the Jul 17 viral reel, so the drop reads worse than it is; baseline is now ~600-1,200/day). ~417 new followers, decelerating from 40/day to ~5/day. Standout: Aug 27 "perks of being on the other river" reel, 305 likes and 59 saves/shares. River-comparison reels have now broken out twice; educational carousels keep flopping. September has been mostly static posts, which is why reach is thin.

**YouTube:** the IG-to-Shorts auto-mirror went live Aug 27 and did 22,068 views this window (prior 30 days: 71). Only 9 subscribers, so it's reach, not audience yet, but it costs nothing.

**TikTok — newly measured, first full look ever:** 1,405 followers, 87 posts since Sep 2025, ~256K all-time views. 68% of those views came from the Nov-Dec launch window when we posted 15-17x/month; we're at ~3/month now and views collapsed with volume, not with quality (a July post still pulled 8.7K). Audience is 77% female, 25-44 core, 96% US, and TikTok search is feeding us people typing "things to do in portland" and "sauna girls group". Top 3 videos ever are all winter-escape angles (45.5K / 41.2K / 27K, all Nov-Dec). Full breakdown: [[TikTok Analytics — All Time (Sept 2026)]].

**What this adds to the priority list:**

1. Cross-post every IG reel to TikTok. The mirror model already works on YouTube and the reels exist. Zero production cost, and TikTok is the channel where Portland activity-seekers are actually searching.
2. Start the winter/rain content flip now. It was flagged in the Aug report as coming; TikTok's all-time data confirms winter-escape is our best-performing format on any channel, and V2 lands mid-October. November should look like launch November: 12-15 posts/month.
3. Get a reel out this week. River-comparison angle. It's the only format that has broken the follower bubble twice on IG and it maps to what TikTok search wants.
