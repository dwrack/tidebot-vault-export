# Sauna Near Me: Suburb Landing Pages (Aug 2026)

Nine location pages for the metro suburbs now covered by the 15-mile ads radius. Each targets "sauna near [city]" / "sauna [city]" organically and gives the new Sauna Near Me ad group a matching landing page (which will also pull up the keyword's landing-page quality score).

**Implementation: Squarespace, by Jess.** Each page is a simple stack: H1, intro block, "Getting here" block, what-to-expect block, FAQ block. Reuse the same 3-4 photos site-wide or pull from Assets/Photos (check CATALOG.md for web-sized exports, respect PRIVACY-HOLD tags).

**Schema note:** paste the FAQ JSON-LD via Code Injection per page (Squarespace's auto-schema is broken). Raw JSON only, no script wrapper, no escaped inner double quotes. Verify live with JSON.parse after publishing.

**Address anchor for all pages:** 173 NE Bridgeton Rd, Portland, OR 97211. Right off I-5 at Delta Park / Jantzen Beach. Drive times below are typical off-peak estimates; Jess should sanity-check against Google Maps before publish.

**Internal linking:** every page links to /wellness (sessions), /memberships, and /faq in the body. Add each new page to the footer under a "Nearby" or "Visit" column so crawlers find them.

## Page template (shared bones, per-city fills)

- **H1:** Sauna Near [City]: Ebb & Ember Floating Sauna
- **Intro:** one paragraph, the city-specific hook + what we are (floating cedar sauna and cold plunge on the Columbia River, North Portland).
- **Getting here from [City]:** route + drive time + parking note (free parking at the marina).
- **What to expect:** heat, river plunge, the Portland Downpour bucket shower, social vs private sessions (up to 10), daily 7am to 9pm, memberships.
- **FAQ:** 3 questions, mirrored in JSON-LD.
- **CTA:** Book a session (link to booking), See memberships.

Voice: Brand Story register. Short sentences. Never "a float." Fuel unsaid. "Ebb & Ember" with ampersand.
