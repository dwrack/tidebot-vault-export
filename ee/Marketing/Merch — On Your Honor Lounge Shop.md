# Merch — On Your Honor Lounge Shop

*Curated first pass at an honor-system retail shelf for the Ebb and Ember lounge. Guests take what they want and pay by QR code. No register, no host time. Starting point for discussion with Jonah, Jordan, and the Pals merch contact.*

---

## Why Honor System

- Sessions run on door codes and hosts are busy running the boat. A shelf that sells itself fits how we already operate.
- Phones-away vibe means people are not scrolling. They are sitting in the lounge looking at a shelf.
- Guests trust us with their bodies in extreme heat and cold. Trusting them with a $20 beanie is on brand.
- Shrink happens. Price so a few walk-offs a month do not matter, and treat a lost hat as marketing.

---

## Curation Principles

1. **Solve a forgot-it problem first.** Towels, water bottles, hair ties, and sandals get bought at 4:55 PM by someone who is already here. Those are the anchors.
2. **Sauna-proof.** Nothing metal that goes in the room. Nothing that melts, fades, or smells when wet.
3. **Round-number pricing, tax included.** $5, $10, $20, $35, $45. No math, no coins.
4. **Small run, few SKUs.** Six to eight items. Sell through before adding more.
5. **Design carries it.** Logo, river, ember. No wellness clichés, no "Namaste" energy. Quiet enough that a Portlander would wear it off-site.

## How the Honor System Works

**Payment**
- One QR code, printed large, laminated, on the shelf and on the lounge wall. Points to a single checkout page with every item and a "pay what you took" option.
- Options in order of preference: Square online store, Venmo business, or a Periode product if Erik can add one. Square gives us reporting and inventory.
- Sign copy, keep it in our voice: *"Take what you need. Scan, pay, enjoy. We trust you."*

**Display**
- One shelf or a small cabinet in the lounge. Fits the lounge furniture vision task already on the todo list.
- Price tags on everything. Tax included, round numbers only.
- Fold and restock at the same time the lounge gets reset before a session. Add to the pre-session checklist.

**Tracking**
- Weekly count on the Weekly Review template. Units on shelf vs units paid.
- Shrink target under 10 percent. If it goes higher, move high-ticket items behind the host and keep the essentials self-serve.
- Post anything notable in the channel per "Get It Out of Your Head."

**Members**
- Member price on merch, 10 percent off with a code at checkout. Cheap perk that reinforces membership.

## Design Direction

- Two marks: the full wordmark, and a small icon (ember, or the boat silhouette on a water line).
- Palette from the space: charcoal, river blue-green, one warm ember accent.
- Copy lines worth testing on the back of a tee or the poster: *"Ebb. Ember. Repeat."* / *"Hot. Cold. Quiet."* / *"On the water, off your phone."*
- Avoid: lotus icons, script fonts, the word "wellness" on a shirt.
