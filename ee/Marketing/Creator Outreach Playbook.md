**Owner:** Jonah Moses

## What This Is

Proactive outreach to local content creators, offering them a free floating sauna session in exchange for nothing. No contract, no posting requirements, no talking points. If they make something cool, great. If they just enjoy the heat and the cold plunge, that's fine too.

This is different from the UGC Repost Playbook (which is reactive, finding content that already exists). This is about building relationships with creators before they've ever been on your sauna.

## Why This Works for Ebb and Ember

We sell the experience creators want to film. A floating sauna on the Columbia River with cold plunge into the river itself. The visuals are striking: steam rising off the water, the contrast of heat and cold, the river backdrop, people plunging into the Columbia in winter. That's content.

Portland's wellness/counter-culture scene is full of creators looking for the next thing to try and share. Cold plunge content is massive right now (Huberman effect, Wim Hof crowd). We sit right at the intersection of wellness trending content and Portland weirdness.

The cost is nearly zero. An empty spot on a session that's going out anyway costs us nothing. The content a creator produces from that spot would cost $500-2,000 to produce ourselves.

## Who to Target

### The Sweet Spot: Micro-Influencers (2K-50K followers)

Don't chase big accounts. You want local creators who:
- Live in or frequently visit Portland
- Post wellness, cold plunge, outdoor, or counter-culture content
- Have real engagement (comments from real people, not bot spam)
- Show original storytelling, not just lip-syncs and trending audio reposts
- Would genuinely enjoy a sauna + cold plunge session

## How to Reach Out

### DM from the Business Instagram Account

Not your personal account. Not email. A direct message from the business page.

### First DM: Cold Outreach

Keep it short. No pitch deck energy.

**Template A: Simple invite:**

> Hey [name]! Love your content, especially [reference a specific recent post]. We run a floating sauna on the Columbia River here in Portland, wood-fired, with cold plunge right into the river. We'd love to have you out for a session. No strings, no posting requirements, just come enjoy it. Want us to set something up?

**Template B: For travel creators:**

> Hey [name]! Saw you're hitting Portland soon. We run a floating sauna on the Columbia River, wood-fired heat then cold plunge straight into the river. It's one of those "only in Portland" things. We'd love to have you out while you're here. Free, no catch. Let us know if you want us to hold a spot.

**Template C: For wellness/cold plunge creators:**

> Hey [name]! Saw your cold plunge content. We've got something you might dig: a floating sauna on the Columbia River here in Portland. Wood-fired, then you plunge into the river itself. Different from a tub or barrel. Would love to have you out. On us, no strings. Interested?

### Rules for Outreach
- **Reference something specific** about their content. "Love your content" alone sounds automated.
- **Don't ask them to post.** The moment you say "in exchange for a post," it becomes transactional and the content suffers.
- **One follow-up max.** If they don't reply after 3-5 days, send one casual follow-up. After that, move on.

**Follow-up DM (if no reply after 5 days):**

> Hey just bumping this in case it got buried. No pressure either way!

### If They Say Yes

> Awesome! When works for you? We run sessions [days/times]. Pick whatever works and I'll hold a spot. You can bring a friend too if you want.

Then:
- Hold the spot, confirm the day before
- Let the staff know a creator is coming (just so they're aware)
- After the session, send a quick thank you DM: "Hope you had a good time! No ask from us, just glad you came out."

### If They Post Something

- Like it immediately
- Leave a genuine comment (something specific about their content, not "Thanks for sharing!")
- Repost using the UGC Playbook process (ask permission, download, repost with tag)
- Flag top performers for ads per the UGC playbook
- Add them to your "relationship" tier for future invites

### If They Don't Post

That's fine. Don't follow up asking where their post is. The relationship still exists. They had a good experience, they'll mention you organically at some point, and you can invite them back for a special event later.

### Relationship Tiers

- **One-timers**: Came, enjoyed it, maybe posted, no ongoing relationship. That's fine.
- **Repeat creators**: Loved it, posted, want to come back. Invite them quarterly or for special events (solstice sessions, new moon sessions, whatever fits the brand).
- **Brand friends**: Genuinely love what you do, mention you unprompted, bring friends. These are gold. Give them first access to everything.
