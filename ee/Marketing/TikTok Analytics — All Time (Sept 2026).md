## The story

- **Launch window was the peak.** Nov–Dec 2025 (soft launch + opening) did 175K views across 32 posts — 68% of all-time views. Posting volume was 15-17/month.
- **2026 = volume collapse.** Posting dropped to 3-7/month and views followed. Aug 2026: 3 posts, 2.6K views.
- **When something lands, it still lands.** Jul 18's "something different to do with friends in Portland" pulled 8.7K with zero followers-momentum. Sep 7 spiked ~1.9K views in one day (the "soaking up every last moment" reel, 1,715 views).
- **The winter angle is the proven winner.** Top 3 videos ever are all cold-weather escape angles from Nov/Dec.
