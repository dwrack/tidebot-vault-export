# V2 Build Diary — Content Playbook (Aug 2026)

Drafted 2026-08-27. Owner: Davey. Status: DRAFT, awaiting Kimberlynn feedback.

## The one rule

The build comes first. Content is exhaust from the build, never a task that competes with it. If Grant is mid-pour, mid-cut, or behind schedule, the camera waits. Total daily ask from Grant: under 5 minutes. If it ever creeps past that, we cut the format, not the build time.

## Why we're doing this

1. **Davey + Jonah stay present.** Grant's end-of-day video doubles as the daily status report. Problems surface the day they happen, in a Slack channel we actually watch, instead of a week later. That alone justifies the system even if we never post a frame.
2. **Share the wins.** The audience watches the sauna get born. By launch, V2 already has a following that feels ownership of it.
3. **Grant gets known for his work.** He's on camera, credited, tagged. A year of build content makes him the recognizable craftsman behind Ebb & Ember. That's real currency for him, and it's why he should want to do this rather than tolerate it.

## Grant's daily ask: 2 videos, not 3

Three talking videos a day is where this dies in week two. Two is sustainable:

1. **"The Plan" (morning, 15-30 sec).** Phone in one hand, coffee in the other. "Today we're framing the west wall. If the cedar shows up, even better." One take, no polish. Messy is the aesthetic.
2. **"Win or Wall" (end of day, 30 sec).** Either the best thing that happened or the thing that's blocking him. This is the status-report video. Honest beats pretty: "The stringers came in a quarter inch off and I lost two hours" is exactly what we want, both as managers and as content.

The third slot is NOT another talking video. It's **passive capture**:

3. **Timelapse + b-roll (zero extra energy).** A phone or GoPro on a tripod, hit record, forget it. Grant's only job is to occasionally move the tripod. And when something is genuinely satisfying (a joint seating perfectly, a beam going up), a 10-second clip of just the work, no talking. Aim for 2-3 of those a week, not per day.

Videos go to a dedicated Slack channel (suggest **#v2-build**) same day. Vertical, phone quality, zero editing expected from Grant. Ever.

## How Davey + Jonah use it

- Watch "Win or Wall" daily. React in-channel. A 🔥 on a win costs nothing and matters.
- A "Wall" video gets a same-day response from Davey or Jonah in the channel: unblock, decide, or acknowledge. If walls sit unanswered, Grant stops filming them, and we lose the whole early-warning system.
- Neither of us gives content notes to Grant. Notes go to the SMM, who translates or ignores.

## Posting cadence (capture daily, post weekly)

- **Stories, 2-3x/week:** raw "Plan" or "Win or Wall" clips, near-verbatim. Low stakes, keeps the daily texture.
- **Weekly build recap reel, 1x/week:** 30-45 sec, timelapse + b-roll + Grant's best moment of the week. This is the flagship.
- **Milestone reels:** platform done, walls up, stove in, first steam. These get real edit time and are the ones we'd co-tag suppliers on (SNW Wood juniper decking install is a natural one).
- Nothing time-sensitive: if a week is thin, skip it. No content debt.
- PRIVACY-HOLD rules apply if members appear in any frame.

## B-roll shot list for Grant

One-time / early:
- [ ] Establishing wides: the platform, the marina, the river beyond
- [ ] "Before" state of the dock and build site, walked slowly
- [ ] Material arrivals: lumber coming off the truck, stacked and stickered
- [ ] Wood grain close-ups: cedar, juniper, fir (macro, slow pan)
- [ ] Grant portrait: at the saw, tool belt on, hands
- [ ] Tool flat-lay or the workbench as it actually looks

Ongoing (grab when it happens, never staged):
- [ ] Timelapse of each major phase: platform, framing, sheathing, roof, interior
- [ ] Hands + tools: sawing, drilling, chiseling, driving screws, planing
- [ ] Measure/mark/level moments (tape snap, pencil line, bubble centering)
- [ ] Joints and fits seating together
- [ ] Sawdust, shavings, offcuts hitting the pile
- [ ] Golden hour light on the structure, morning fog on the water
- [ ] Rain-day work (fits the Downpour angle)
- [ ] Stove install, rock cage, first fire, first steam
- [ ] Mistakes and re-dos (with Grant's OK per clip)
- [ ] Final walkthrough POV, door open to the river

## Timelapse plan (the whole build, start to finish)

Two layers:

1. **Fixed hero timelapse (automated, months-long): LIVE as of 2026-08-27.** The always-on Mac runs `com.ebbember.v2-timelapse` (launchd), pulling a still from the wired "Ebb boat" Blink cam every 15 min, 6am-9pm, into `~/ebb-timelapse/frames/YYYY-MM-DD/`. Stitch with ffmpeg monthly. Current framing has two dock pilings blocking center frame; Davey adjusting the cam on 8/28 so the build area sits clear. Rule stands: once adjusted, lock it and never move it. Optional upgrade later: a Brinno-class construction cam (~$200) for a second, higher angle.
2. **Grant's phase timelapses.** Phone/GoPro on tripod per work session (already in the daily system above). These give the close-up texture the fixed cam can't.

Rules: lock the hero framing on day one and never move it. Pull and review a test week of frames before trusting a month of them.
