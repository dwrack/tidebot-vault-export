Read-only research. Nothing was published or changed. Pulled 2026-10-19/20.

Sources, used throughout:
- **GSC** = Google Search Console, sc-domain:ebbandember.com, 2026-07-21 to 2026-10-18 (90 days). Top 400 queries only, so anonymized queries are missing.
- **GA4** = property 524300531, same 90 days.

- CSVs are in the session scratchpad `dash/` folder: gsc_queries, gsc_pages, keyword_opportunities, paa_questions.

## The answer

1. People who already know us find us. Brand queries are about 2,381 of 3,138 GSC clicks (76%, GSC, 90 days). Non-brand is 757 clicks from 6,262 impressions.
2. We own "floating sauna portland" (216 clicks, position 2.4, GSC). We do not own the bigger generic terms. "sauna portland" is position 7.3 with 43 clicks from 1,127 impressions (GSC). "sauna near me" is 1,600 searches a month in Portland (DFS) and we are position 7.7 with 17 clicks.
3. Three pages get huge impressions and almost no clicks: /faq 6,651 impressions at 1.6% CTR, /memberships 6,525 at 0.7%, /wellness 5,875 at 0.1% (GSC). Those are title and meta problems, not ranking problems.
4. The "discovery" searches (date night, things to do at night, rainy day, winter) have low or zero difficulty in DFS and we have no page for any of them. TikTok search is already sending people to us on those terms.
5. Winter is the window. "sauna portland" goes from 480 a month in June and July to 1,600 in January (DFS). Publish and post by mid November.

## The gaps

- **Local pack.** On "sauna portland" and "sauna near me" the three map results are Secret Sauna PDX, Forest Haven Sauna and Koti Sauna / Knot Springs. We are not in them (DFS SERP, 2026-10-20). On "cold plunge portland" the pack is Shiver & Sweat, Core Longevity and Guss. We are not there either. On "floating sauna" we are #1 organic and #1 in the map pack with 4.9 stars and 130 reviews.
- **Discovery searches.** Date night portland (320 a month), date ideas portland (480), things to do in portland at night (210), rainy day activities portland (140), things to do in portland (12,100). We rank for none (DFS, GSC). Ten of those top results are lists from Travel Portland, Eater, Reddit and local blogs. A short, specific "date night on the river" page can sit among them.
- **Bathhouse.** "portland bathhouse" is 480 a month with KD 2 (DFS). We show at position 46.7 for "bathhouse portland" on 3 impressions (GSC). The SERP is Hawks PDX, Steam Portland and Everett House. Nobody is writing "what a social sauna is vs a bathhouse".
- **Contrast therapy.** Real information demand (9 People Also Ask questions, DFS SERP), local volume small (30 a month). Top results are AMA, NIH, Whoop. A local plain-language page can win the Portland version.
- **Competitor note.** Guss (wood-fired, riverside, Oaks Park) uses "Portland's sauna by the river". Do not argue with that. We say "floating sauna on the Columbia" and leave the heater type out.

## Quick wins on existing pages

1. **/faq**: 6,651 impressions, 1.6% CTR, position 2.6 (GSC). Rewrite the title and meta description so the answer-bait in the title is clearer. Cheap, high payoff.
2. **/memberships**: 6,525 impressions, 0.7% CTR, position 2.7 (GSC). Same fix. Add "Portland" and "floating sauna membership" to the title.
3. **/wellness**: 5,875 impressions, 0.1% CTR (GSC). Rename to /experiences is already in the June plan. This is the page most likely to be a bad title match.
4. **Homepage title and H1**: test "Sauna in Portland" next to "floating sauna". "sauna portland" is 880 a month and we sit at position 7.3 with a 3.8% CTR (GSC). Moving to position 3 should roughly double clicks, but that is my estimate, not a measured number.
5. **Google Business Profile**: weekly photo and post, put "sauna and cold plunge" and "private sauna" into services and Q&A. We are missing from the local pack for "sauna near me" and "cold plunge near me" (DFS SERP, 2026-10-20).
6. **Add a "What is a floating sauna" block** to the homepage FAQ. That is the first PAA on the "floating sauna" SERP and Google's AI Overview is answering it with other people's text.
7. **Add alt text**: "sauna boat" and "sauna on the river" already convert at 40 to 50% CTR from position 1.6 (GSC). Alt text and one H2 should keep that.
8. **Private sessions page** gets "private sauna Portland" (99 GSC impressions at position 7.9, KD 15) and "party venues portland" (70 a month, KD 0). Add a group-of-10 section.
9. **GA4**: site search is not recording terms. Turn it on, and make sure periode.no outbound clicks are tracked, per the June plan.

## Not covered / caveats

- GSC covers the top 400 queries. Counts are minimums.
- Bathhouse results include clothing-optional venues. Avoid that angle. We are a family-friendly social sauna.
- PAA questions about weight loss, toxins and "burn belly fat" came back on sauna SERPs. Skip them, we do not make those claims.
