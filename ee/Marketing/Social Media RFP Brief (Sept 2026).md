Purpose: get 2-4 comparable quotes for social media management so the Lemonade PDX SOW ($3K/mo, 9 months, $27.5K) can be judged against the market instead of in a vacuum. Send the same brief to every candidate so the numbers line up.

## The email to send candidates

Subject: Social media management quote - Portland floating sauna

Hi [name],

We run Ebb & Ember, a floating sauna on the Columbia River in Portland (@ebbandember, ~[X]K followers). We're taking quotes for ongoing social media management starting October. Scope we're pricing:

- Instagram primary, TikTok secondary, Facebook cross-posted
- 3-5 posts/week on Instagram
- Reels are the priority: quote us at 2 reels/week (tell us what 1-2/month vs 2/week does to price)
- Monthly content calendar in a shared doc, with our approval before posting
- On-site capture at least monthly (we're a visual brand on the water; quarterly isn't enough)
- Daily community management: comments, DMs triaged to our team, story resharing
- Managing up to 3 hosted influencer/creator visits per month
- Monthly report: follower growth, reach, engagement, link clicks, and bookings attributed where possible

Please send:
1. Monthly price for the scope above, and what you'd cut to hit $2,500/mo if that's over
2. Three accounts you run now that are closest to us (hospitality, wellness, or Portland local), with before/after numbers
3. A sample month of calendar or 2-3 posts you'd make for us
4. Who actually does the work (you, or a team member)
5. Minimum term and notice period

We're deciding by [date, suggest Sept 19]. Happy to do a 20-minute call.

Thanks,
[Connor/Jonah or business signature per outbound rules - not David's name]

## Market reference points (Sept 2026)

- Freelance SMM: $800-2,500/mo, 8-16 posts across 1-2 platforms, basic graphics
- Solo senior / boutique consultant: $2,500-4,500/mo with strategy + light production
- Boutique agency: $4,500-11,000/mo, includes real video production and reporting
- Strategy-only consulting (one-time): $450-2,500
- Portland fractional strategy: $80-250/hr
- Jess's data point: Travel Oregon heavy-content program was $7K+/mo with 4-5 people on it

Verdict baked in: $3K for Lemonade is fair-to-good pricing for her seniority. The open question is shape, not price - she's a strategist selling light production, and Davey wants heavy production. A $1,200-2,000 shooter/editor plus a $1,500-2,000 strategist may beat one $3K generalist retainer. Jess confirmed Hannah K would quote strategy-only (repriced, no bundle discount).
