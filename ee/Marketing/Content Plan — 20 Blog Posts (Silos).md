# Content Plan — 20 Blog Posts (Silos)

*Created 2026-05-09. Long-tail keyword + AI citation strategy for ebbandember.com.*

## Strategic frame

Goal: get cited as a source by Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Gemini for a wide net of long-tail queries that aren't yet owned by Knot Springs, Löyly, or Common Ground.

We're not chasing high-volume head terms. We're stacking 20 long-tail pieces around five cornerstone pillar pages, each silo deeply interlinked. AI cites pages, not domains, so this gives us 20 "doors" into the site instead of one homepage trying to do everything.

**Three rules every post follows:**
1. **Definition first.** First paragraph contains an extractable answer. Brand voice can come after.
2. **One specific number per post minimum.** Stat with source = +37-40% AI citation lift (Princeton GEO research).
3. **Internal links to pillar + 1-2 sibling posts in the silo.** Builds topical authority.

## Silo architecture

| Silo | Pillar Page | Posts | Audience |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Sauna Education | /floating-sauna-portland | 5 | First-time sauna users, curious locals |
| 2. Cold Plunge & Contrast Therapy | /cold-plunge-portland | 4 | Wellness seekers, plunge-curious |

| 4. Group & Special Occasion | /private-sauna-rental-portland | 3 | High-intent commercial buyers |

5 pillars × 1 page each = 5 pillar pages (already in 30-day plan). Plus 20 supporting blog posts = 25 total content pieces over 90-120 days.

## Squarespace blog setup

The site doesn't currently have a blog. Add via **Pages > + Add Page > Blog > "Journal"** or "Notes" or similar (avoid "Blog" — too generic). URL slug `/journal` or `/notes`.

Each post needs:
- SEO title (Page Settings > SEO)
- Meta description (155 chars)
- Featured image with descriptive filename + alt text
- Author name (David Rack, or rotating)
- Last updated date visible on the post
- Internal links wired before publishing

Schema: Squarespace auto-generates BlogPosting schema for blog posts, so no manual JSON-LD needed for posts themselves. FAQ blocks within posts get FAQPage schema added manually via Page Header Code Injection.

## What this plan deliberately doesn't include

- No "Top 10 saunas in [random other city]" content — out of scope, dilutes topical authority
- No celebrity/trend-jacking content (unless naturally relevant)
- No AI-generated drafts shipped without heavy editing — AI-generated content gets cited less
- No daily-update news content — monthly cornerstone pieces beat daily noise

## Success metrics (review at week 10 and week 20)

- Pages indexed in Google (target: 100% indexed within 7 days of publish)
- Impressions per post in GSC at 30, 60, 90 days
- AI citations: re-run the 25-query check and look for new citations referencing blog post URLs
- Clicks to /book from each post (Periode UTM tracking)
- New internal links from each post to pillar pages (link equity flowing where we want)
