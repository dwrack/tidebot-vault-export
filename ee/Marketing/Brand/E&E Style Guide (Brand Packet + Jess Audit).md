## Brand packet rules
- **Colors:** mist #C8DACC, sage #798D7F, cream #EAD3B7, copper #BF7E45, charcoal #212121. "Intentionally muted and natural." Cedar, metal, stone, steam, river water.
- **Type:** Basal (display, caps) + Inter (UI and copy). "Type should always feel intentional and calm, never loud or frantic."
- **Logo:** flame inside a water droplet over ripples. Stacked and horizontal lockups. Approved color pairs: sage on mist, cream on sage, cream on copper, mist on charcoal.

## Jess's audit (what she wants the site to do)
- Use the **full palette to soften the visuals** and reduce the "Adrenaline Sauna!" vibe. Code colors to meaning, and carry that onto signage.
- **Separate text from photos** (or fix the overlay) so both the photo and the CTA land.
- Tighter spacing, alignment and hierarchy for scanning.
- Clarify booking options and how they differ. Copy should map 1:1 to Periode emails and on-site signs.
- **Sitemap:** Home, Book Now, Experiences, What to Expect, Gift Cards, Memberships, FAQ (+ legal).
- **Homepage order:** hero (short benefit + Book), next available sessions, session highlights, membership pitch, testimonials/press, FAQ shortlist, location/hours/contact, footer mirroring the sitemap.
- What to Expect must cover arrival, check-in, deck, river plunge, and **safety/river visibility (boat traffic)**.

## Tension to flag
Round 1's pick (A, Social Ember) is dark brown + bright orange with shouty caps. That is the "loud" look both docs warn against. Round 2 keeps A's layout but moves to the packet palette.
