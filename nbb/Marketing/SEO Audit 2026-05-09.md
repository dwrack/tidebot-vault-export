## Re-prioritized action plan (sale-prep lens)

### This week (literally this week — pick a 90-minute block)

These are the highest signal-to-effort moves a buyer will see in the GSC handoff:

1. **Rewrite the homepage title tag.** Replace "NOLA Bike Bar | Pedal Party Bike Tour in New Orleans" with "Bike Bar New Orleans | Party Bike Tours & Bar Crawls — NOLA Bike Bar" (or close variant). 5 minutes in WordPress. Pushes "bike bar new orleans" CTR from 12.5% toward the 25% it should be at position 4-5.

2. **Add meta descriptions to homepage + 3 tour pages.** 4 pages, ~150 chars each, 20 minutes. Google is currently auto-generating snippets on every commercial page. This alone usually moves CTR 1-2 points.

3. **Fix the "Things to Do" page H2.** Change "Pedal Barge Tours" to "Bike Bar Tours" (or split into separate sections by sister brand). 5 minutes. Removes the single biggest topical-confusion signal on the domain.

4. **Move or de-link the Cajun Queen page.** Either redirect to nolabikebar's sister boat brand domain, OR remove from main nav + activity sitemap and add a noindex tag. 15 minutes either way. Stops the "boat content on a bike-bar domain" semantic problem dead.

Total wall-time: under an hour of CMS work. Cost of not doing it: every additional week is a week the buyer's GSC handoff still shows "audit done, nothing implemented."

### Next 2-3 weeks (publish to show momentum)

The Content Plan in the vault has Week 1's 5 posts fully briefed. **Publishing those posts is the biggest single move for sale-prep optics.** A buyer looking at this asset wants to see:
- An indexed blog with recent content (not 13 posts from 2023 + nothing since)
- Pages ranking for new commercial keywords (bachelorette, French Quarter, etc.)
- A growing impressions curve, not a flat one

Priority order I'd actually publish:
1. Post 1 (Bachelorette) — highest-converting audience, replaces the broken 2020 hub

### This quarter (only if the sale doesn't close first)

These are real plays but lower priority if the goal is a 30-60 day sale. Save them for the buyer's roadmap doc:

1. **Build the bachelorette money page** (`/bachelorette-party-new-orleans/`). Critical content gap, but a money page takes 4-6 hours of design + copy + photos. Post 1 from the Content Plan covers most of the SEO upside in 1500 words.
2. **Spanish landing page or hreflang** for "tour en bicicleta new orleans." The signal is now strong enough (33 impressions / pos 12.5) that this is a legit play, but it needs translation infrastructure that's a real lift.
