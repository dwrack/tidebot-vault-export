# SEO Audit — NOLA Bike Bar
**Date:** 2026-05-04
**Property:** https://www.nolabikebar.com/
**Window:** 90 days (2026-02-03 to 2026-05-04)
**Source:** GSC MCP + live site crawl

---

## Executive Summary

NOLA Bike Bar is showing up for the right brand and "bike bar / pedal bar New Orleans" terms, but the site is leaking demand in two ways. First, only one URL (the homepage) is generating any clicks at all out of 50+ ranked queries — the tour subpages and 17 blog posts are basically invisible to search. Second, every page leans hard into the word "pedal" in titles, H1s, and body copy, which both undersells the experience to party-buying customers and risks attracting the wrong audience (people looking for an athletic bike ride vs people looking for a moving bar with friends).

Total 90-day picture: roughly 20 clicks across 50 ranked queries on 621 impressions, with the homepage carrying everything. The funnel is real (we rank position 1 for "new orleans party bike," "pedal pub nola," "pedal tavern new orleans") but page-level depth and topical content are missing.

**Top three priorities:** kill or rebrand the "Cajun Queen Mega Pedal Bike Barge" page that puts boat language on a street vehicle site, rewrite tour page titles/H1s to lead with "party bike" and "bachelorette / group" intent, and build out missing high-intent landing pages (bachelorette party, French Quarter party experience, group transport).

## CRITICAL FLAGS — Boat & Pedal Language on the Site

### Boat / vessel language present (must fix)

| Issue | Location | Severity |
|-------|----------|----------|
| Page titled "The Cajun Queen — Mega Pedal Bike **Barge**" lives on nolabikebar.com | `/the-cajun-queen-mega-pedal-bike-barge/` | Critical |
| Page describes itself as "pedal party boat tour," "cycleboat," "boat rental," at "Eddie Pinto's Marina" on a "bayou river" | Same page | Critical |
| Sitemap groups this with the bike tours, sending mixed signals to Google about what nolabikebar.com is | activity-sitemap.xml | High |
| `/things-to-do-in-new-orleans/` page uses H2 "Pedal Barge Tours" | `/things-to-do-in-new-orleans/` | High |

This is the single biggest semantic problem. NOLA Bike Bar is a street vehicle (3 bikes, 14-26 people, French Quarter / Mid-City). Hosting a boat product on the same domain confuses Google's topical understanding of the site. Either move the Cajun Queen to its own domain (or to nolaboozecruise.com if that already exists), or pull it out of the main nav and the activity sitemap so it doesn't dilute the bike bar topical authority.

### Pedal language overused (de-emphasize)

| Page | "Pedal" Mentions | Title Tag |
|------|------------------|-----------|
| Homepage | 8 | "NOLA Bike Bar \| Pedal Party Bike Tour in New Orleans" |
| Mid-City Tour | 12 | "New Orleans Pedal Bike Bar - Mid City Tour" |
| Brewery Tour | 7 | "New Orleans Brewery / Bar Tour \| Nola Bike Bar" |
| H2 on homepage | — | "Pedal Bike Bar New Orleans" |

Per user feedback: customers hate the idea of pedaling. The ranking data backs this up — "pedal" terms convert (we rank #1 for "pedal pub nola" and "pedal tavern new orleans," which feed FareHarbor) but the high-volume opportunity sits on terms like "bike bar new orleans" (16 impressions, position 4.5, 12.5% CTR) and "bike tour new orleans" (16 impressions, position 14, 0% CTR). Lead with party / bike bar / group in titles. Keep "pedal" in body for the people specifically searching it.

## 5. Top 3 Critical Issues

1. **The Cajun Queen "Mega Pedal Bike Barge" page on a non-boat domain.** This page describes a "pedal party boat tour" / "cycleboat" at a "marina" on a "bayou river" — the exact maritime language the user told us to never put on the bike bar site. Either remove the page, move it to the boat brand's domain (NOLA Booze Cruise / wherever), or de-link it from the main nav and activity sitemap. As-is, it muddies Google's understanding of what this domain is about and creates customer confusion.

2. **Topical depth is missing across high-intent commercial queries.** The site ranks for ~50 queries but only the homepage gets clicks. We have no money page for bachelorette parties, bachelor parties, French Quarter tours, or group transportation — the four most obvious high-intent commercial queries for this product. This is the single biggest revenue-affecting gap.

3. **Title tags lead with "pedal" instead of customer language.** Per user feedback, customers don't want to hear about pedaling, they want to hear about a moving bar with their friends. Every tour page title and H1 should be rewritten to lead with "Bike Bar," "Party Bike," or "Bar Crawl on Wheels." Keep "pedal" in body copy where it earns long-tail rankings, but stop putting it in the front of every title.

## 6. Quick Wins (this week) + Strategic Plays (this quarter)

### 5 Quick Wins

1. **Rewrite homepage title tag.** From "NOLA Bike Bar | Pedal Party Bike Tour in New Orleans" to "Bike Bar New Orleans | Party Bike Tours & Bar Crawls — NOLA Bike Bar". Leads with the money keyword (position 4.6, 16 impressions, 12.5% CTR — title fix should push CTR up at the same position).

2. **Rewrite Mid-City and Brewery tour title tags.** Drop "Pedal" from the lead, add party / bachelorette / group framing. Example: "Irish Channel Bike Bar Tour | New Orleans Brewery Bar Crawl on Wheels."

3. **Add meta descriptions to every page.** Currently most pages have no meta description, so Google is auto-generating snippets. Write 150-character descriptions with a clear CTA and the target keyword for each tour page.

4. **De-link the Cajun Queen page from the main nav and the activity sitemap.** Even if the page stays live for cross-sell, it should not show up as a sibling of the bike tours in the sitemap.

5. **Add 5 internal links from blog posts to money pages.** Specifically rec #1, #2, #3, #4, #5 from the linking table above. Total work: 30 minutes. Equity push to commercial pages: real.

### 3 Strategic Plays

1. **Build a Bachelorette Party landing page.** The bachelorette guide blog post is already getting some traction implicitly (21+ party group fits). Build a real `/bachelorette-party-new-orleans/` money page with: hero with photos of bachelorette groups on the bike bar, route map, package pricing, FAQ schema, social proof (TripAdvisor reviews filtered for "bachelorette"), and FareHarbor embed. Target keyword: "bachelorette party new orleans bike bar." This has the highest revenue-per-effort ratio of anything on this list.

2. **Build a French Quarter / Group Transportation hub.** Either one page targeting "french quarter party bike tour" with the E-Bike Tours product as the offer, OR a "/group-party-bike/" page targeting "new orleans group transportation" / "party bus alternative" intent. The bike bar IS group party transport — own the frame instead of competing with bike-rental searches.

3. **Refresh and expand the blog cluster around bachelorette / group / Bourbon Street.** The 17 existing posts are a goldmine of orphan equity. Run a content audit: update the 2020 bachelorette post with 2026 data, add a "best group activities for adults in NOLA" pillar, and link every post into the appropriate money page with descriptive anchor text. Goal: convert ~zero-click blog posts into top-of-funnel feeders for the tour pages.
