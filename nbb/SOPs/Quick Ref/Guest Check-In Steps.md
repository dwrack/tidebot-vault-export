# Guest Check-In Steps
**Every guest gets a real welcome, a signed waiver, and a safety briefing before anyone rides.**

---

1. Greet the group as they arrive. Energy starts here. Introduce yourself by name.
2. Verify headcount against the FareHarbor manifest. Confirm who's actually here vs. who booked.
3. Check waiver status in FareHarbor. The manifest shows who signed and who hasn't.
   - Most guests sign via the automated email link before they show up.
   - Anyone unsigned: pull up their booking-specific waiver on your phone or tablet and hand it to them.
   - No generic QR codes. Every signature tied to a specific booking.

> [!warning] No one rides without a signed waiver. Zero exceptions. This is a termination-level issue.

4. Ask if anyone has medical conditions, injuries, or mobility issues that might affect the ride. Adjust seating if needed.
5. Confirm the group's BYOB situation if applicable: what they brought, where to stow it, remind them about open container laws on the street (varies by route/area).
6. Deliver the safety orientation:
   - Stay seated while the vehicle is moving.
   - Keep hands and arms inside the vehicle at all times.
   - Hold onto the rail during stops and starts.
   - If you need to stop for any reason, call out to the driver.
   - Point out the first aid kit location.
   - Driver controls pace and route. No requests to speed up or take unapproved detours.
7. Give the group a photo opportunity before departure. This is their "before" shot and it's great for social.
8. Confirm everyone is seated and holding on before you pull out.
