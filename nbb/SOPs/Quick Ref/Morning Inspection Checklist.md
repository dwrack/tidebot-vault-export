# Morning Inspection Checklist
**Walk every vehicle before the first ride. If it's not safe, it doesn't roll.**

---

**For each assigned vehicle:**

1. Tires: check pressure visually, look for damage, cracks, or low spots. All tires inflated and in good shape.
2. Brakes: test braking function. Firm response, no grinding, no delay.
3. Steering: turn lock to lock. Smooth, no play, no resistance.
4. Headlights: on and functional.
5. Brake lights: have someone watch while you test.
6. Turn signals: left and right, front and rear.
7. Running lights / marker lights: all lit.
8. Reflective safety markings: visible and not peeling or obstructed.
9. Passenger rails and restraints: secure, no wobble, no broken welds or loose bolts.
10. Seats: stable, clean, no damage.
11. Sound system: power on, test audio, confirm Bluetooth pairing works.
12. Cooler: clean, no standing water, no residue from yesterday.
13. First aid kit: stocked (bandages, gauze, antiseptic, gloves, instant cold packs). Replace anything used.
14. Fire extinguisher: accessible, charged, inspection tag current.
15. Reflective vest / safety gear for driver: in the vehicle.
16. Route check: pull up any last-minute road closures, construction zones, or event detours. Adjust route if needed.

> [!warning] Any vehicle that fails inspection on brakes, steering, tires, or lights does NOT go out. Report the issue to management immediately and reassign the ride to another vehicle if available.
