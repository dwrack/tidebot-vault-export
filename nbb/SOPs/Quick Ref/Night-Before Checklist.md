# Night-Before Checklist
**Confirm tomorrow's rides are locked in and nothing will surprise you at the curb.**

---

1. Open FareHarbor and review every booking for tomorrow: headcount, start times, any special requests or notes.
2. Check weather forecast for rain, thunderstorms, extreme heat (heat index >105), or high winds (>30mph sustained). Flag anything that might trigger a cancellation.
3. Confirm driver availability for each scheduled ride. If someone's out, find coverage now, not at 9am.
4. Assign each driver to a specific vehicle (Vehicle 1, 2, or 3). Post assignments in the group chat.
5. Check for any known road closures, parades, construction, or special events that would affect tomorrow's routes. New Orleans always has something going on.
6. Verify cooler supplies are stocked: ice availability, cups, napkins.
7. Confirm sound system batteries are charged or chargers are staged.

> [!tip] If weather looks borderline, make the call by 7am the next morning and communicate to guests immediately. Don't wait until they're standing on the sidewalk.
