# Vehicle Safety Checklist
**Monthly and seasonal deep inspection for all 3 vehicles. This goes beyond the daily morning check.**

---

**Perform monthly or at the start of each season, whichever comes first. All 3 vehicles.**

### Braking System
- [ ] Brake pads/shoes: adequate material remaining
- [ ] Brake cables/lines: no fraying, no leaks, no damage
- [ ] Braking response: firm, immediate, no pulling to one side

### Tires
- [ ] Tread depth: meets minimum safe threshold
- [ ] Tire pressure: within spec for each tire
- [ ] Sidewalls: no cracks, bulges, or dry rot
- [ ] Spare / repair kit available (if applicable)

### Steering
- [ ] Steering mechanism: smooth, no excessive play
- [ ] All linkage and connections tight and secure

### Lighting and Visibility
- [ ] Headlights functional
- [ ] Brake lights functional
- [ ] Turn signals functional (all four)
- [ ] Running lights / marker lights functional
- [ ] All reflective markings intact and visible

### Passenger Area
- [ ] All seats secure, no movement or wobble
- [ ] Passenger rails: no cracks, no loose welds, no rust-through
- [ ] Footrests / foot areas clear of hazards
- [ ] Entry/exit points clear and safe

### Frame and Structure
- [ ] Frame: no cracks, bends, or structural damage
- [ ] All bolts and fasteners torqued and secure
- [ ] Canopy / roof (if applicable): stable, no tears

### Electrical System
- [ ] Battery: charged, terminals clean
- [ ] Wiring: no exposed wires, no chafing, no corrosion
- [ ] Sound system wiring secure

### Documentation
- [ ] Vehicle registration current (expiration date: ___)
- [ ] Commercial vehicle insurance current (expiration date: ___)
- [ ] City of New Orleans business permits current (expiration date: ___)
- [ ] Inspection stickers / tags current (if required)
