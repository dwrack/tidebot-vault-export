# Contractor Onboarding Checklist
**Everything a new driver needs to complete before they take a vehicle out solo.**

---

## Step 0: Culture Doc

1. New hire reads [[How We Run Things — NOLA Bike Bar]] before anything else.
2. Manager walks through any questions or scenarios that come up.

- [ ] Culture doc read
- [ ] Manager conversation completed

---

## Pre-Hire Requirements

- [ ] Valid driver's license (clean driving record, commercial endorsement if required by city)
- [ ] Background check completed
- [ ] CPR/First Aid certification (preferred, not required)

---

## Documents to Collect Before First Shift

- [ ] W-9 (required for all 1099 contractors)
- [ ] Signed Independent Contractor Agreement (ICA) via DocuSeal
- [ ] Photo ID (driver's license or passport copy)
- [ ] Copy of driver's license (separate from photo ID, for driving record verification)
- [ ] Direct deposit / payment info (Venmo, Zelle, or bank ACH)
- [ ] Emergency contact information

---

## Orientation (In-Person)

3. Vehicle walkthrough: all 3 vehicles, where everything is, how each one handles differently if applicable.
4. Equipment tour: sound system operation, cooler setup, safety gear locations, first aid kit.
5. Route training: primary routes, alternate routes, known problem spots (construction, narrow streets, high-traffic intersections), best neighborhoods to highlight.
6. Safety procedures: Morning Inspection Checklist, Guest Check-In Steps, Incident Report Steps.
7. Guest management: how to read a group, managing intoxicated passengers, de-escalation basics.
8. Safety briefing practice: new hire delivers it, manager gives feedback.
9. FareHarbor walkthrough: checking bookings, waiver status, manifest.

---

## Shadow Rides (2-3)

10. New driver rides along while experienced driver operates. Observation only.
11. Debrief after each ride: what they noticed, questions, concerns.

- [ ] Shadow ride 1 completed (date: ___)
- [ ] Shadow ride 2 completed (date: ___)

---

## Assisted Rides (1-2)

12. New driver operates with experienced driver present as backup.
13. Experienced driver only steps in for safety concerns or if the new driver asks.

- [ ] Assisted ride 1 completed (date: ___)
- [ ] Assisted ride 2 completed (date: ___)
