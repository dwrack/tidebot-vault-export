# Contractor Offboarding Checklist
**Clean exit. No loose ends, no lingering access.**

---

1. Confirm the contractor's last working day in writing (text, email, or Slack message).

**Collect within 24 hours:**
- [ ] All vehicle keys returned
- [ ] Any branded gear returned (shirts, hats, etc.)

**Revoke access within 24 hours:**
- [ ] FareHarbor access removed
- [ ] Slack access removed
- [ ] Google Drive access removed
- [ ] Any other shared accounts or tools

**Within 7 days:**
- [ ] Internal roster updated (scheduling rotation, contact list)
- [ ] Team notified of departure (no details needed, just coverage awareness)

**Within 30 days:**
- [ ] Final pay issued
- [ ] 1099 information confirmed for tax reporting

> [!tip] If the departure was involuntary, collect keys and revoke all access the same day. Don't wait 24 hours.
