# Post-Tour Closeout
**Wrap the ride clean so the next group gets the same experience the last one did.**

---

1. Pull into the designated stop point. Confirm the vehicle is fully stopped and secure before anyone stands.
2. Do a sweep for personal items: phones, bags, sunglasses, water bottles. Hand everything back before the group scatters.
3. Thank the group. Be genuine. If they were fun, tell them.
4. Ask for a review: "If you had a good time, a Google review means a lot to us." Keep it casual, not scripted.
5. Note any feedback worth sharing: complaints, compliments, suggestions. Drop it in the group chat or Slack before you forget.
6. Empty and wipe down the cooler. No standing water, no sticky residue.
7. Remove all trash from the vehicle: cups, cans, napkins, anything guests left behind.
8. Wipe down seats and rails.
9. If there's another ride coming, restock the cooler with fresh ice and reset the sound system.
10. If this was the last ride on this vehicle for the day, proceed to End-of-Day Sign-Off.
