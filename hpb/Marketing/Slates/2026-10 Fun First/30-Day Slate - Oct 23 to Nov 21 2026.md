## Stories, every day a boat goes out

Stories are where the selling happens, so the feed doesn't have to.
- Which boat is out, the weather, the marina. 3 frames, phone, 30 seconds of work.
- One sticker a day, rotating: poll ("sunset or 11pm?"), quiz ("how many people fit on the boat?"), slider, question box ("what should we put in the cooler?").
- **"Add Yours" stickers** (these spread to strangers): "your boat day song," "your bach theme," "your cooler MVP."
- Reshare every guest Story we're tagged in, same day.
- Price, the link and open dates live here.

## How we get more engagement (the playbook)

1. **Every caption asks for one thing** that takes 2 seconds: a number, an emoji, a tag. "Comment 1 to 8" beats "let us know your thoughts."
2. **Reply to every comment in the first hour.** That's when the algorithm decides whether to push a post. Whoever posts at 5pm is on comments until 6.
3. **Collab posts with bach groups.** When a group posts their trip, invite them to collab on our reel. It lands on their followers' feeds, who are the exact people planning the next one.
4. **Pin the 3 best posts** (the LIT reel, the costume carousel, the playlist).
5. **Shares are the score, not likes.** HPB is a group-decision product. Every LIT and Dress Code caption says "send this to..."
6. **Name the crew.** Bella, Tom, Abby, Zach, Don, Jade and Ahmad are already famous in our reviews. One of them becomes "the captain" of the Monday series.
7. **Recurring formats, so people come back for them:** Captain Mondays, Cooler Lab Tuesdays, Aux Cord Saturdays. Same cover style every week.
8. **Steal the language.** Customers say "vibe," "boat ride," "the whole boat," "cruise." Never "charter," "barge," "luxury," "memories," or "pedal" (brand name only).
