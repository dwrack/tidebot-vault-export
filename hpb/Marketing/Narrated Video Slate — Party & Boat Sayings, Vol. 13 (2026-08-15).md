## How this was written

Two-pass process, same as every prior volume, run fresh against this batch:

1. **`/copy-mentors`** — master routing by piece:
   - **Schwartz's identification principle** drove the majority of this batch — 197, 200, 201, 202, 203, 204, 205, 206, 207, and 208 all assume the viewer already recognizes the moment (name-tag reunions, a newcomer getting folded into a friend group, ignoring a game score, the "boat mom," a one-word toast, candid photos, a group chat blowing up early, a random flag, an invented handshake, a friend group that doesn't match on paper) and just names it rather than arguing it's funny.
   - **Sugarman's slippery-slide short openers** drove 193, 198, 203, and 206 — each opens with a short, plain, easy-to-swallow line ("She didn't say it was a divorce party," "Public notice," "Public record," "Public log") before the bit builds, matching the same device Vol. 12 used for piece 179.
   - **Halbert's conversational directness** drove 194 and 198's closing lines — "Forty years of alarms. Zero of them go off out here" and "New life, new rule, boat stays exactly the same" are flat, unhedged statements, no qualifier, same register as Vol. 12's piece 186.
   - **Blair Warren's 27 words** (applied during drafting, from copywriting-2.0): piece 194 leans on "let them off the hook" — the retiree doesn't have to perform gratitude, somebody just hands him a drink. Piece 200 leans on "allay their fears" — a newcomer worried about not knowing anyone gets told directly that showing up is enough. Piece 196 leans on "confirm their suspicions" — that the friend-group love matters more than the couple's-holiday version. Piece 199 leans on "encourage their dreams" — the celebration gets to be about joy now that the nerves are over.

2. **`/copywriting-2.0`** — every spoken line run through the 3-test filter (can you visualize it, can you falsify it, could nobody else say it). What that pass caught and fixed in this batch specifically:
   - **Fixed a near-duplicate:** piece 197's closing line, corrected as described in the pass note above — this was the single most important catch in this batch.
   - **Fixed a safety-adjacent overclaim:** piece 198's first draft had the gender-reveal use a powder or smoke cannon, mirroring how these are often done on land. Rewritten to a two-cooler-color reveal instead, since a powder or smoke device on a moving boat is a real fire/mess/safety question this document has no authority to greenlight — flagged again in Open Items as needing an actual answer from Connor, not just a script assumption.
   - **Fixed a false-specificity risk:** piece 201's first draft named an actual team and a specific playoff round. Genericized to "a playoff game" with no team, score, or date named, so the piece doesn't age out or risk stating something that turns out to be wrong (same discipline Vol. 12 applied to piece 180's NASA-distance claim).
   - **Fixed an implied-real-person problem:** piece 194's first draft had the retiree "cry" during the toast, phrased as if describing a specific documented moment. Softened to a general observation ("the ones people cry hardest at") rather than a claim about one identifiable person's real reaction, matching the discipline Vol. 12 applied to piece 189's illustrative tenure number.
   - **Kept two bits honestly flagged as invented illustrations, not documented fact:** piece 206's specific flag list (Texas, Louisiana, Grateful Dead, "one nobody could identify") and piece 208's "seven years running" friend-group tenure are both flagged in their own notes below as illustrative, not logged incidents — same discipline the series has applied since pieces 158/160/163 in Vol. 11 and 185/186/188/189 in Vol. 12.
