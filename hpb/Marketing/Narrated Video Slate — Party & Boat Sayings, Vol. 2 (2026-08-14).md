## How this was written

Same two-pass discipline as Vol. 1, run against this batch specifically:

1. **`/copy-mentors`** — Halbert and Sugarman for structure (one person talking, short opener, momentum before the punchline), Bencivenga for specificity. Blair Warren's three levers still do most of the work: these lines land because they **confirm a suspicion** ("yeah, the playlist really does always end up the same three songs"), **allay a fear** ("no you don't have to hold it for two hours, there's a real bathroom"), or **throw a friendly rock** at something every boat group has lived through (the ice runs out, somebody's cooler gets raided the second it opens, the front-seat scramble at boarding).
2. **`/copywriting-2.0`** — every spoken line run through the 3-test filter: can you visualize it, can you falsify it, could a competitor say it. What that pass caught in this batch:
   - Cut a first draft of piece 22 that had Mama Tried's name origin as "just a fun nickname the crew picked" — vague, not visualizable, not ownable. Rewrote to the actual Waylon Jennings "Mama Tried" nod, which is checkable (ask the crew, it's a real reference) and specific enough nobody else can claim it.
   - Cut a version of piece 20 ("Assigned Seating? Never Heard Of Her") that ended on "there's always a little friendly chaos" — reads like every brand's "chaotic energy" copy. Replaced with "I've seen heels beat sneakers. Don't ask me how," which is a concrete, visualizable, slightly absurd claim nobody else on the lake can say about their specific boats.
   - Cut "an unforgettable golden hour" language from an early draft of piece 26 — same failure as Vol. 1's cut line, generic superlative. Rebuilt around the checkable, funny specific: "forty photos of the same sunset, every single one different somehow."
   - Kept and built piece 31 around a true operational detail (somebody always underpacks ice, somebody else always over-packs it) rather than inventing a generic "come prepared" PSA — the hero-worship angle is what makes it quotable instead of instructional.
   - No dollar figures anywhere in this batch either. The $59-69 vs. $55 discrepancy flagged in Vol. 1 (sourced from `Fresh Clip Batch — Aug 2026.md`) is still open — see Open Items below.

## On-screen text system (unchanged from Vol. 1)

Same three-beat structure, same reasoning: **Impact, yellow `#FFD400`, heavy black outline**, readable on busy sunlit boat footage.
1. **Hook** — funniest/most quotable line, biggest type, first 3-4 seconds, top third of frame
2. **Body** — one supporting line, doesn't repeat the VO word-for-word, carries the joke for sound-off viewers
3. **Tag/CTA** — last 1-2 seconds, small white text, `houstonpedalbarge.com`, present on conversion-relevant pieces (19, 22, 29, 32) and absent on pure-vibe pieces so it doesn't read like every post is a pitch
