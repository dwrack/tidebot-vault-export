# Houston Pedal Barge — Narrated Video Slate: Party & Boat Sayings

**Format modeled on:** Admire NOLA's narrated series (`Content/Narrated/01-poetic-NOTES.txt`, `Content/Script & Caption Bank.md`, `Content/Launch Slate - Daily Posts.md`) — timed script with a timestamp and bracketed visual on every line, one NOTES.txt per piece, voice/music/length header.

**Lane:** funny, loose, quotable — party sayings, boat sayings, toasts, deck-hand slang, bachelorette-party lines, captain lines, the running jokes of a group on a boat. This is the opposite lane from Admire NOLA's poetic swamp narration on purpose. HPB's brand doc is explicit: "loud, inclusive, a little chaotic, and genuinely fun," short sentences, no corporate filler, sell the outcome not the specs. Poetic doesn't fit this brand. Bar-side comedy does.

**Pedaling:** never sold as a feature anywhere in this slate. One piece (06) exists specifically to kill the "do we have to pedal the whole time" misconception, and it does that by making fun of the question, not by pitching the pedals as a draw. Everywhere else, the pedal stations are set dressing for a joke, never the pitch.

**Pass note:** this vault has no prior narrated-video slate on file — searched by filename and by content (`Narrated`, `SCRIPT (timed`, `NOTES`) and found nothing. Prior HPB video work in this vault is hook banks, clip batches, and the FB daily queue, not narrated pieces. So this is genuinely new work in a lane that hasn't been touched here before, not a repeat of an earlier pass.

## How this was written

Ran the whole batch through two passes before locking any line:

1. **`/copy-mentors`** — routed to Halbert (conversational, sounds like one person talking, not a broadcast) and Sugarman (short opener, slippery-slide momentum, curiosity before the reveal) for structure, and Bencivenga for specificity (a Yeti full of White Claw beats "bring your own drinks"). This is a Blair Warren job as much as a copywriting one — most of these lines work because they **confirm a suspicion** ("BYOB really is that much cheaper"), **allay a fear** ("no dress code actually means no dress code," "you don't have to pedal"), or **help the group throw a small, friendly rock** at something relatable (the office Zoom-call energy, the impossible bar tab, the group chat that won't just pick an outfit).
2. **`/copywriting-2.0`** — every spoken line run through the 3-test filter: can you visualize it, can you falsify it, could a competitor say it. Lines that failed got cut or rewritten. A few examples of what that pass caught and fixed:
   - Cut: any version of "unforgettable experience on the water" — passes none of the three tests.
   - Kept and built around: "a cooler that was just entirely deviled eggs" — you can see it, it's a checkable claim (ask the crew), and no other party-boat company has that specific true weird detail.
   - Kept and built around: "quiet for four seconds, loud again forever" — ownable to this exact boat's rhythm, not a generic sunset line.
   - Cut a first draft of the bar-tab piece that stated exact dollar figures — the vault has an **open, unresolved price discrepancy** ($59-69 per the Brand Story doc vs. "$55 a ticket" in an Aug 10 Facebook post, flagged in `Fresh Clip Batch — Aug 2026.md` and still unresolved). No script in this slate states a specific price. Piece 15 makes the savings joke without a number.

## On-screen text system (reused from the existing HPB standard)

Same treatment already locked in `Fresh Clip Batch — Aug 2026.md`, so a viewer scrolling past both batches reads them as one brand: **Impact, yellow `#FFD400`, heavy black outline** — the combo that survives on busy, sunny, high-motion boat footage where a thin white sans-serif disappears. Three-beat structure per piece:
1. **Hook** — the funniest/most quotable line, biggest type, appears in the first 3-4 seconds, top third of frame
2. **Body** — one supporting line burns in mid-piece, smaller, does not repeat the VO word-for-word (viewers reading with sound off need the caption to work as its own joke, same rule Admire NOLA's caption doc uses: the on-screen text and the spoken line each pull their own weight)
3. **Tag/CTA** — last 2 seconds, smaller white text, `houstonpedalbarge.com`, present on pieces built to convert (03, 06, 15, 16) and absent on the pure-vibe pieces (05, 07, 12) so it doesn't read like every post is a pitch

Full captions matter here specifically because these are gag pieces meant to be watched sound-off in a feed — the joke has to land from text alone or the format fails.

**Script:**
```

 3.2s  Doesn't matter what's in it. Water counts. I checked.                    [close on hands raising cups and cans]

 9.0s  To Clear Lake, to Mama Tried, and to whoever's turn it is to pedal for fifteen minutes and then never again.   [wide shot, boat full of raised cups, empty pedal seats in frame]
14.2s  Cheers. Let's go.                                                        [engine starts, boat pulls off the dock, deck lights kick on]
```
**Music:** commercial-library horn-stab pop-country hybrid, ~100 BPM, tempo lift on "cheers." **On-screen text:** Hook = "WATER COUNTS. I CHECKED." · Body = "the toast, every single trip" · no CTA (this is a vibe piece, not a booking pitch).

**Shot list:**

- [CHARTER] Close inserts: hands raising cups/cans/water bottles, mixed drinks visible
