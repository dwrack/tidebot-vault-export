## First: the last giveaway already ran, and it bombed

`HPB Giveaway Draft — Free Cruise for 2.md` was published to FB + IG on **2026-06-21**. Pulled the real
numbers off the page today:

| Post | Date | Engagement |
|---|---|---|
| "Who would you bring on a free cruise?" giveaway | 2026-06-21 | **7 likes, 2 comments, 0 shares = 9** |
| "Giveaway in our last post 👀" | 2025-11-13 | 2 likes, 1 comment, 2 shares = 5 |

Zero shares on a tag-a-friend giveaway is the whole story. The mechanic depends on sharing and nobody
shared it. So the "giveaways own the top of this page" thesis in the old doc is **wrong for 2026** —
it's true of 2022 launch-era posts (45,640 and 13,208 engagements) but those ran when the page was new,
the concept was novel in Houston, and reach was cheap. Two honest attempts since then have returned 9
and 5. That's not a fluke, that's the current reality.

**The real problem isn't the mechanic, it's that the page has no organic reach left.** Baseline right
now is 1 to 10 engagements per post. A giveaway shown to 40 people converts 40 people's worth. You
can't tag-a-friend your way out of zero distribution.

Both 2026 winners are **specific**. Everything in the 1-to-10 range is generic ("Come see us Houston!",
"Looking for plans this weekend?"). The page's problem is that it posts a lot of interchangeable clips.

## So if we run one, run this one

Three changes from the June version:

**1. Make the prize worth sharing.** "Cruise for 2" is a $110 prize on a boat that seats 26. Nobody
rallies their group chat for two seats. **Give away the whole boat.** It costs one off-peak weekday
slot and it's a prize people will actually tag 8 friends for, because the tagging *is* the prize.

**2. Anchor it to the place.** Lead with Seabrook / Clear Lake / ten minutes from Kemah, which is the
single thing that has demonstrably driven shares here in 2026.

**3. Seed it with a small boost.** Organic reach is functionally zero. $75 to $100 behind this post for
5 days targeting Houston metro 21-40 is what makes the difference between 9 engagements and something
real. **This is ad spend and needs your explicit sign-off separately.** Without it I'd expect another
single-digit result and I'd rather say that up front than run it and report a flop.
