## Page copy

### Hero
**Get your group out on the water.**
A private, two-hour boat outing on Clear Lake, just for your group. Up to 26 people, a captain
and crew who handle everything, and nothing for you to plan except who to bring.

[Button: Check Available Dates]

### The idea
Some of the best conversations your group will ever have do not happen in a fellowship hall. They
happen when everyone is out of their routine, phones down, watching the sun drop over the water.

We run a private 26-passenger boat out of Seabrook on Clear Lake. You get the whole boat to your
group, a licensed captain, a relaxed loop around the lake, and about two hours together that nobody
has to organize or clean up after. Bring your own food and drinks (coolers are welcome), pick your
playlist, and we take care of the rest.

### Who it's for
- **Youth groups** - a high-energy end-of-semester or summer-kickoff outing that is fully supervised and easy to chaperone.
- **Young adult and college ministries** - a low-key way for people to actually connect, not just sit in rows.
- **Men's and women's ministries** - a change of scenery for a morning out or an evening on the water.
- **Small groups and life groups** - a simple social that brings everyone together outside of someone's living room.
- **Leadership, staff, and volunteer appreciation** - a genuine thank-you for the people who carry the load.

### Why groups love it
- **The whole boat is yours.** No strangers, no mixed crowd. Just your people.
- **A captain and crew run everything.** You show up, we handle the boat, the route, and the safety.
- **No experience needed, and no pressure.** It is a calm, scenic cruise, not an adventure sport.
- **You set the tone.** Bring your own food and refreshments. Plenty of groups keep it simple with water, sweet tea, and snacks.
- **Easy on the budget.** One flat rate for the whole group, which usually works out to a few dollars a head.
- **Coast Guard certified.** Two restrooms on board, life jackets, the works.

### How it works
1. Pick a date and a time that fits your group's calendar.
2. Reserve the whole boat with a simple deposit.
3. Bring up to 26 people and whatever food and drinks you want on board.
4. Show up at our Seabrook dock. We take it from there.

### Common questions
- **Is alcohol required?** Not at all. You bring whatever your group wants on board, and a lot of our faith groups skip alcohol entirely. It is your outing, your call.
- **How many people fit?** Up to 26 on a private charter.
- **How long is it?** About two hours on the water.
- **Where do we meet?** 2515 East NASA Parkway, Seabrook, TX, on Clear Lake, minutes from the Kemah Boardwalk.
- **What does it cost?** One flat group rate. Reach out and we will send current pricing and open dates.
- **Is it safe for teens?** Yes. The boat is Coast Guard certified with a licensed captain and crew, and chaperones ride free with the group.
