## Executive summary

HPB owns its brand and the Kemah-area party boat searches, and the Maps pack is good: #1 for "houston party boat" and "houston party barge" at the dock, 4.9 stars on 982 reviews. The weak spots are (1) the **"boat rides houston" cluster**, about 2,100 impressions a quarter stuck at positions 7 to 14, (2) a **three-way address conflict** (GBP, schema, money page body all differ), (3) **two hosts splitting signals**: www ranks 3 to 4 places higher than the bare domain for the same queries, and (4) a site that loads 7 to 9 MB and takes 10 to 14 seconds to paint on mobile. Search clicks are down 51% vs the prior 90 days (1,154 vs 2,369) on 27% fewer impressions, which is mostly season, not a loss. GA4 shows **0 conversions on every channel**, so nothing here can be tied to bookings until the FareHarbor events fire.

Top three priorities: confirm and unify the address, fix the www/bare split, and push the boat-rides post onto page 1.
