## 1. REEL — "You don't have to pedal" reassurance — Bachelorette

**Angle / gap filled:** NEW. Direct misconception-buster as reassurance, not joke.
**Evidence:** 4+ Houston competitors named around pedaling that require pedaling the whole route; HPB's own Brand Story: "People assume they have to pedal the whole time... it costs us bookings."
**One reader:** a maid of honor in Katy building the bachelorette weekend, half-scared her friend group will have to physically pedal a boat for two hours in heels.
**Channeling:** Halbert (A-pile pattern interrupt for the open) + Schwartz (Stage 3 — she's already comparing boat options, so we counter a specific doubt, not introduce the category).

**Hook options (first 3 seconds, on-screen text + VO):**
- A. "We get asked this on almost every single booking."
- B. "No, you don't have to pedal the whole time."
- C. "The boat drives itself. The pedals are just there if you're bored."

**Body (framework: reassurance-bridge — name the fear, kill it fast, move to the actual sell):**
> Yes, it's called Houston Pedal Barge. No, nobody's making your group pedal for two hours.
> The boat's fully motorized. A licensed captain drives. The pedal stations are there if someone wants a photo or five minutes of "we're doing this," then everybody sits back down.
> What you're actually booking: your group, your music, your coolers, two hours on Clear Lake with the Kemah skyline behind you.

**Objection handled:** "Do we have to pedal the whole time?"
**CTA (button):** Book Now — copy above button: "Check Your Date's Availability"

**Format spec / asset:** 15-20 sec vertical Reel. Open on tight shot of pedal stations (the thing people worry about), cut wide to reveal guests lounging, drinks in hand, boat clearly moving on its own. One guest hops on a pedal seat for a laugh, then gets off. Use existing Assets folder barge-cruising b-roll; needs one new clip of a guest briefly pedaling for the visual gag, if not already shot.

**Ready-to-paste block:**
> **Primary text:** We get asked this on almost every single booking: no, you don't have to pedal. The boat's fully motorized, captain drives, pedals are just there if you want a photo. Bring your coolers, bring your playlist, book your Houston bachelorette.
> **Headline:** You don't have to pedal, promise
> **CTA button:** Book Now
