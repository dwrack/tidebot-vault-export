# Creator Outreach Playbook: Houston Pedal Barge

**Owner:** Connor Champaign
**Business Instagram:** @houstonpedalbarge (or current handle)

---

## What This Is

Proactive outreach to local content creators, offering them a free pedal barge cruise in exchange for nothing. No contract, no posting requirements, no talking points. If they make something cool, great. If they just have a good time pedaling and partying on the water, that's fine too.

This is different from the UGC Repost Playbook (which is reactive, finding content that already exists). This is about building relationships with creators before they've ever been on your barge.

## Why This Works for Houston Pedal Barge

We sell the experience creators want to film. A pedal-powered party boat where your group is the engine and the entertainment. It's active, it's social, it's inherently funny and shareable. Groups of friends pedaling a boat while drinking: that's TikTok content without trying.

Most Houston "things to do" content is the same restaurants and bars. A pedal barge stands out because it's different. Creators looking for something their audience hasn't seen yet gravitate toward this.

The cost is nearly zero. An empty spot on a cruise that's going out anyway costs us nothing. The content a creator produces from that spot would cost $500-2,000 to produce ourselves.

## Who to Target

### The Sweet Spot: Micro-Influencers (2K-50K followers)

Don't chase big accounts. You want local creators who:
- Live in or frequently visit Houston
- Post nightlife, group activities, bachelorette, or city-life content
- Have real engagement (comments from real people, not bot spam)
- Show original storytelling, not just lip-syncs and trending audio reposts
- Would genuinely enjoy a pedal barge with friends

### Creator Types to Prioritize

| Type | Why | Example |
|------|-----|---------|
| Houston nightlife/party creators | Direct alignment with the product | "Houston nightlife" accounts, weekend event reviewers |
| Bachelorette/group activity creators | Top booking segment | Bachelorette planners, girls' trip accounts |

| Couples/friend group content | Matches the booking unit (groups) | "Houston date ideas" or friend group activity accounts |

## How to Reach Out

### DM from the Business Instagram Account

Not your personal account. Not email. A direct message from the business page.

### First DM: Cold Outreach

Keep it short. No pitch deck energy.

**Template A: Simple invite:**

> Hey [name]! Love your content, especially [reference a specific recent post]. We run a pedal-powered party barge here in Houston. Your group pedals the boat, BYOB, great energy on the water. We'd love to have you and some friends out. No strings, no posting requirements, just come have a good time. Want us to set something up?

**Template B: For travel creators:**

> Hey [name]! Saw you're hitting Houston soon. We run a pedal barge on the water, your group literally pedals the boat, BYOB, it's a party on the bayou. Totally different from the usual Houston stuff. We'd love to have you out while you're here. Free, no catch. Let us know if you want us to hold spots.

**Template C: For creators who posted about a competitor or similar experience:**

> Hey [name]! Saw your [post about group activity/similar experience]. We do something different here: a pedal-powered party barge where your crew is the engine. BYOB, on the water, great for groups. Would love to have you out. On us, no strings. Interested?

### Rules for Outreach
- **Reference something specific** about their content. "Love your content" alone sounds automated.
- **Don't ask them to post.** The moment you say "in exchange for a post," it becomes transactional and the content suffers.
- **One follow-up max.** If they don't reply after 3-5 days, send one casual follow-up. After that, move on.

**Follow-up DM (if no reply after 5 days):**

> Hey just bumping this in case it got buried. No pressure either way!

### If They Say Yes

> Awesome! When works for you? We run cruises [days/times]. Pick whatever works and I'll hold spots. Bring a few friends, it's better with a group.

Then:
- Hold the spots, confirm the day before
- Let the captain know a creator is coming (just so they're aware)
- After the cruise, send a quick thank you DM: "Hope you had a good time! No ask from us, just glad you came out."

### If They Post Something

- Like it immediately
- Leave a genuine comment (something specific about their content, not "Thanks for sharing!")
- Repost using the UGC Playbook process (ask permission, download, repost with tag)
- Flag top performers for ads per the UGC playbook
- Add them to your "relationship" tier for future invites

### If They Don't Post

That's fine. Don't follow up asking where their post is. The relationship still exists. They had a good time, they'll mention you organically at some point, and you can invite them back for a special event later.

### Relationship Tiers

- **One-timers**: Came, enjoyed it, maybe posted, no ongoing relationship. That's fine.
- **Repeat creators**: Loved it, posted, want to come back. Invite them for special events, holiday cruises.
- **Brand friends**: Genuinely love what you do, mention you unprompted, bring friends. These are gold. Give them first access to everything.

| Task | How often | Time |
|------|-----------|------|

| Post-cruise thank you DM | Day after their cruise | 2 min |

**Total: ~1-2 hours per month.** This is not a heavy lift.
