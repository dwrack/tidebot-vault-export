# Houston Pedal Barge — Video Scripts (Top 10 Hooks)

Full short-form scripts for the top 10 ranked hooks from `Marketing/Video Hook Bank (2026-08-15).md`. Hook wording and numbering below is quoted verbatim from that file — confirmed against the source doc, nothing shifted.

**Process:** every hook was already sharpened in the Hook Bank via `/copy-mentors` + `/copywriting-2.0`. This pass ran the same two skills on the *new* lines — build copy, VO, on-screen text, CTAs — that didn't exist yet. Each new line was checked against the 3-test filter (can I visualize it, can I falsify it, can nobody else say it) before it made the final cut. Seedance 2.0 prompts were built with `/seedance-video`, pulling the Viral Hook and Testimonial Story toolkits plus the shared cinematography/sound reference.

**Hard rule held throughout:** pedaling is never shown or narrated as the activity. No pedal-station close-ups, no "get your steps in" copy. Where the boats' pedal stations appear in b-roll at all (none of the 10 scripts below require it), they're incidental background, not the subject. Hook #18 is the only script that says the word "pedaling," and it does so to kill the objection, not sell the mechanic — matching the Hook Bank's own finding.

**Brand facts used for specificity (per `Brand Story & Values.md`):** two boats, Delta Dream (white/green) and Mama Tried (black/gold), both 30-ft, USCG Subchapter T certified, 26-person capacity, BYOB, Clear Lake / Seabrook-Kemah corridor, 4.9 stars across 400+ reviews, private charters $800-950, public tours $59-69/person.

## Script 1 — Hook #3 (Ranked #1)

> "She said no strangers, no bouncer, no last call — so we rented the whole boat."

**Occasion:** Bachelorette / private charter positioning. **Audience stage:** Solution-aware — she's already decided on a group night out, comparing venues (Schwartz Stage 3). **Format:** Text-on-screen negation list building line by line over real deck/dancing b-roll.

### Shot list & pacing (15s)

| Time | Shot | On-screen text | Sound |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0-1.5s | Cold open, handheld deck shot, group already dancing, golden hour | Hook line types on: "no strangers." | Music cold-opens at volume, no intro |
| 1.5-3s | Cut to cooler being opened, drinks passed | Text adds: "no bouncer." | Music continues, ice/can sounds cut through |
| 3-4.5s | Cut to bride laughing, sash visible, full group in frame | Text adds: "no last call." | Music holds |
| 4.5-6s | Whip pan to wide shot, full boat, Clear Lake in background | Text adds: "so we rented the whole boat." | Bass swells into drop |
| 6-12s | Fast-cut montage: BYOB cooler, aux cord in someone's hand, sunset, group singing | No text — let it breathe | Music at full energy, real crowd audio underneath |
| 12-15s | Settle on wide shot, boat silhouetted against Clear Lake sunset | CTA card: "Check dates for your group — link in bio." | Music tail, fades under VO-free CTA card |

**VO/caption copy (caption text under the video, not on-screen overlay):** "The whole boat. Just us. BYOB, our music, no dress code. Delta Dream, Clear Lake, whatever night you want it."

**Why the new lines hold up:** "The whole boat. Just us." — visualizable (empty boat but your people), falsifiable (either the charter is private or it isn't — it is), and ownable (a bar can't say "just us," a shared-tour operator can't either).

**CTA:** "Check dates for your group — link in bio." (Call-to-value: the value is checking availability for *her* group, not a generic "book now.")
