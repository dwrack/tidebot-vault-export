## The 5 topics

1. **"There's nothing to do in Houston"** — the most-said, most-wrong sentence in the city, and our whole opening
2. **The two boats** — Delta Dream (white/green) and Mama Tried (black/gold), and how to pick
3. **BYOB and the group runs it** — playlist, drinks, pace, no dress code, ages 8+
4. **Clear Lake / Kemah / Galveston Bay** — Houston is a water city and nobody knows it
5. **Who this is for** — bachelorettes, birthdays, corporate, the person stuck planning

## The 3 formats

- **Myth-bust** (easy) — the pedaling question alone is a month of content
- **Ranking / tier list** (easy) — best trip times, best occasions, boat picks
- **Guest reaction** (ambitious) — the payoff format

## The 2 layouts

- **A — On-boat handheld, loud, unpolished.** Matches the product.
- **B — Text-on-screen over drone/b-roll of Clear Lake.** Sound off.

## Cover type

Chunky block caps. Black/gold for Mama Tried rows, white/green for Delta Dream rows. The livery is free brand consistency, use it.

## Notes

- **Row 13 is the single most important video on this list.** The brand story says the pedaling question "comes up constantly." A question that comes up constantly is a page that failed and a video that hasn't been made. Answer it on the site first, then post it, then keep posting it.
- Do not frame pedaling as a feature or a selling point anywhere. It's an optional novelty, and the copy should always be de-escalating it.
- Safety is the baseline, not the pitch. USCG certification belongs in the FAQ, not the cover line.
- Boost the highest-**share** post as a social-proof ad. Group decision product.
