# Customer Voice - 981 Google Reviews (Oct 2026)

Source: all 981 Google reviews pulled 2026-10-12 (782 have text, 199 are star-only). Raw file: Marketing/Reviews/google-reviews-2026-10-12.json. Shares are of the 782 text reviews; buckets are regex counts, so treat them as floors.

Stars: 950 five (96.8%), 12 four, 1 three, 0 two, 18 one. Average 4.9. Reviews per year: 2022: 304, 2023: 331, 2024: 152, 2025: 160, 2026: 34 (to Oct 12).

## 1. The verdict

Customers describe a private dance party on the water that they run themselves: their playlist on the Bluetooth speaker, their cooler and snacks, their friends, with a captain and host who play along instead of policing. The captain or crew shows up in 38% of text reviews, more than every scenic word combined (sunset 1.4%, dolphins and birds 0.1%, Kemah/Seabrook/view 5%). People rate the people first and the lake second. The occasion is nearly always a celebration (birthday leads), the feeling words are 'fun', 'vibe', 'blast' and 'amazing', and the praise that keeps coming back is crews who 'let us be ourselves'. Pedaling is a footnote.

## 7. Complaints, plain (31 reviews under 5 stars: 18 one-star, 1 three, 12 four)

All 18 one-stars are about what happened before boarding or around it, not the ride itself. Nobody who got a normal trip complained.

| Complaint | Reviews |
|---|---|
| Cancelled, rescheduled, no captain, or boat down, often after the guest drove 45 to 60+ minutes or was told the day before the birthday | 8 |
| One storm day (Jun 6, 2026): leaking roof, soaked seats, stood or sat wet, felt pressured to go out in lightning | 8 |
| Offered a gift card instead of the refund the staff said they would give (out-of-state guests) | 6 |
| Manager rude, blocked their number, or stopped answering | 5 |
| 'Seats 26' but only about 12 real seats for a party of 16 | 3 |
| Expectations: bring your own drinks, $65 felt steep, no signs or check-in directions, $10 per person to reschedule | 2 |

In plain words: guests were stranded after a long drive, told one thing by the manager and another by the captain, handed a gift card they could not use, then ghosted. The Jun 6 storm alone is 8 of 18 one-stars and includes a Better Business Bureau threat.

**How the 4-stars hedge (12 reviews: 4 have no text, 4 are pure praise, 4 hedge):** one says the passenger-phone playlist ran vulgar and not to their taste and lightning turned the boat back at the bridge so they missed the Kemah Boardwalk view; one says the speakers were 'not so great'; one warns 'the heat is OUT THERE'; one disliked waiting on a late guest and says only 12 can use the pedal seats while everyone pays the same. The 3-star just says 'Relaxing ride'. Also, 'I wish the ride was longer than 2 hours' (12 reviews of any star say 2 hours felt short). One 5-star mentions a restroom that was not working.
