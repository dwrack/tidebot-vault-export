# HPB Organic Analytics Readout
*Houston Pedal Barge — Facebook (Page 109758021560620, ~37k fans) + Instagram (@houstonpedalbarge). Generated 2026-06-21.*

Source: full account history pulled live via Graph API v21.0. FB = 458 posts (Dec 2021 → Jun 2026). IG = 158 media (Jan 2022 → Jun 2026). Raw cached at `/tmp/hpb_fb_all.json` and `/tmp/hpb_ig_all.json`. "Engagement" = likes + comments + shares (FB) / likes + comments (IG). **Every number below is real.** Median is the headline number because two 2022 viral giveaway posts skew every average; both are shown where it matters.

---

## The one-paragraph verdict

Both channels peaked in **2022** and have been declining since, and it's not subtle. FB median engagement went 26 (2022) → 12.5 (2023) → **3** (2024) → 5 (2025) → 1.5 (2026 so far). IG went 26 → 29 → 7.5 → 7.5 → 6. The decline is partly Meta reach decay, but it's also self-inflicted: 2024 was the worst year on FB *and* had the most posts (204), almost all of them near-identical `#highlightreel #tourhighlights #partyboat` clips with no hook. We posted more and said less. **IG is the healthier per-post channel right now** (since-2024 median 7 vs FB's 3) but **FB has the audience and the viral ceiling** (a single 2022 giveaway hit 45,640 engagements / 14k shares; IG's all-time best is 129). The play is different per platform, and a couple of angles that worked for NOLA actively *hurt* here. Details below.

## 6. Cadence & timing (times converted to Central)

- **Volume:** FB median 3 posts/week (164 active weeks), IG median 1/week (95 active weeks). FB has been over-posted in bursts (204 in 2024) with declining quality; IG is under-posted. The fix isn't more volume, it's better posts at a steady cadence — which is exactly what a 5x/week queue enforces.

⚠️ **Flag:** the inherited NOLA cadence skips **Wed + Sun** — but on FB those are HPB's two *best* days (median 14 each), and Monday (the worst, median 1) is kept. This is an HPB-specific mismatch. See the "what differs from NPB" note at the bottom — I kept the skip-Wed/Sun pattern for now to mirror NOLA exactly as asked, but the data says HPB should skip **Mon + Thu** instead. Easy one-line change in `post.py` (`SKIP_WEEKDAYS`) when you want it.

**Hour-of-day (CT), best windows:**
- FB: 9am is the workhorse (n=118, median 16) — most posts go then and it holds up. Late afternoon 2pm/5pm (22–23) and evening 7–8pm also strong. The 7am median 165 is n=3 noise, ignore it.
- IG: **5pm (median 48)**, 9am (40, n=4 noise), 1pm (29), 4pm (27.5), 2pm (27). IG's real sweet spot is **early-to-late afternoon, peaking ~5pm.**

The FB poster runs 10am and IG reels 11am (inherited). 10am FB is fine. **IG at 11am is leaving engagement on the table — 1–5pm is materially better.** I set the IG job to 13:00 (1pm CT) for HPB instead of 11am. Noted below.

## 7. Hashtags, emoji, URLs

- **IG hashtags:** present median **14** vs none **4**. Hashtags help a lot here — keep 5–10 relevant tags on every IG post.
- **IG emoji:** ≥3 emoji median **33** vs ≤1 emoji median **12**. More emoji = more engagement on IG. (n is small on the high-emoji bucket, but the direction is consistent with the platform.)
- **FB URLs:** caption *with* a booking URL median **13** vs *without* median **5**. The usual "FB suppresses link posts" effect does **not** show up on this page — posts with the booking link actually engage *better* (they're the real promo posts vs filler). **Keep the booking link in FB captions.** This is another HPB-specific reversal worth noting.

## 8. Cross-platform verdict

| | FB | IG |
|---|---|---|
| All-time median eng | 6 | 12.5 |
| Since-2024 median | 3 | **7** |
| Audience | ~37k fans | smaller |
| Viral ceiling | **45,640** | 129 |

**IG is the healthier per-post channel** (more than double FB's current baseline) and **should get the higher-effort creative** — it's where a good hook actually compounds. **FB is the reach-and-conversion channel**: bigger audience, much higher viral ceiling, and it's where booking links and giveaways pay off. Don't abandon FB; feed it the proven practical content (book-now + giveaway + dance trends) and let the 37k base do the work.

**Do the two platforms reward different content?** Yes, and differently than NOLA:
- **FB → practical + offer.** Booking links (help, not hurt), giveaways, "weekends filling up," location/Clear Lake, dance trends. Older, conversion-minded audience.
- **IG → hook + format.** Question-hook openers, video/carousel over stills, hashtags, emoji, named groups (bachelorette/bridal/corporate). 5pm posting.

**Angles to lead with:** question hooks (both), giveaways (both, underused), bachelorette/group (IG), book-now urgency (FB + IG), Clear Lake/location (FB), dance trends (both).
**Angles to drop:** emotional "moments/strangers/memories" (worst on both — opposite of NOLA), social-proof/#1/award bragging (collapses on both), single still photos (IG floor), lazy one-line captions.

## What differs from NOLA (HPB-specific)

1. **Emotional/personality content loses here.** NOLA's IG won on emotion; HPB's emotional posts are dead last on both platforms. Lead HPB with questions and offers, not feelings.
2. **Booking URLs help FB, don't hurt it.** No link suppression on this page — opposite of the usual assumption.
3. **Best FB days are Wed + Sun**, which the inherited cadence skips. Mirrored NOLA's skip for now; data argues for skip Mon+Thu instead.
4. **IG should post ~1pm, not 11am** (5pm is the true peak; 1pm is the practical compromise with the FB slot).
5. **Brand voice:** per your June 7 decision, **no "pedal" in any caption** — lead with Houston Party Barge / BYOB booze cruise / Clear Lake / fully captained. (Note: several of the historical top posts literally say "pedal-powered party boat," which is exactly the language we're retiring — they won on novelty in 2022, not on the word.)
6. **Giveaways are even more dominant for HPB than NOLA** — they own the all-time top of both platforms. Section 4 is the single highest-leverage move in this whole build.
