<h2>2. The Kemah Boardwalk, Before or After</h2>
<p>Our dock is in Seabrook, minutes from the Kemah Boardwalk, which makes the obvious combo move: cruise first, boardwalk after. Ride the Boardwalk Bullet if you're feeling brave, grab a frozen drink, walk the water's edge, play the dumb carnival games. Kemah at night with the lights on is genuinely a good time, and it gives the date a second act so the night doesn't end at 8:30.</p>
<p>If you'd rather flip it, do the boardwalk in the late afternoon and time your cruise for sunset. That order is better in summer, honestly, since the boardwalk concrete radiates heat until the sun gets low.</p>
<h2>3. Dinner on the Seabrook Waterfront</h2>
<p>Seabrook's waterfront restaurants are the underrated play here. You're eating fresh Gulf seafood with boats going by, at a fraction of the fuss of a downtown reservation. The stretch along the Seabrook channel has casual spots where you can show up in shorts after a day on the water and nobody blinks. Pair it with a cruise and you've built an entire evening within a few minutes' drive: boat, sunset, seafood, home by 10.</p>
<h2>4. A Galveston Day Trip</h2>
<p>For a full-day version, keep driving 30 more minutes to Galveston. Walk the Strand, hit the Pleasure Pier, eat oysters somewhere on the seawall. It's the classic Houston couples' escape for a reason. The smart itinerary if you want maximum water: Galveston during the day, then catch an evening cruise on Clear Lake on the way back north. You pass right by our Seabrook dock heading home on 146.</p>
