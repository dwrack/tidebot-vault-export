<p>Fireworks over water exist for one reason: the reflection. From a boat, you get the show twice, once overhead and once mirrored across the bay. From the Boardwalk, you get the show filtered through a crowd, light poles, and whoever decided to hold their phone above their head for the full twenty minutes.</p>
<p>Then there's the part nobody budgets for. The drive home. Kemah on a normal Saturday night is already slow.
