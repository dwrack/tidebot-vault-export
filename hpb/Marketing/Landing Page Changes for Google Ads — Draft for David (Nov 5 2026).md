## Why we're asking

Every Houston Pedal Barge Google ad carries the policy flag **"Alcohol information — ads with this issue can run, but won't show to some people."** It is a restriction, not a disapproval.

On 2026-11-05 we published three ads with **zero alcohol words** (headlines, descriptions, sitelinks, callouts all clean). All three came back with the same flag. So the ad text is not the cause. The ads all point to **houstonpedalbarge.com/houston-party-barge-cruise/**, and that page is full of alcohol wording (list below). Google reads the landing page, so the page is the most likely trigger.

## Please read before changing anything

- **BYOB is a real selling point and it works.** The ad groups built around "booze cruise" are among our best (lifetime ~6x return). Changing the main pages could cost us those searchers and the page's search ranking for "booze cruise houston".
- The flag only limits who sees the ad; in the US our ads still ran and earned clicks. We have not proven the flag costs us much.
- So the safe plan is a **test**, not a rewrite (Option A). Only do Option B if the test shows it's worth it.
- **Do not hide the BYOB policy from customers.** Whatever the ad-only page says, the full "what you can bring" policy must stay in the FareHarbor booking policies and the confirmation email.
