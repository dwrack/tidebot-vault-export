## CONTENT (Markdown body)

*Written by the Houston Party Barge dock crew, Seabrook, TX*

We get this question at the dock more than you'd think: "Wait, are y'all the same as Houston Party Boats?" No. Different company, different boats, different party entirely. Both are real options if you're searching "party boat Houston" and trying to figure out where your money should go. So let's just settle it.

**Search "houston party boats" and you'll find yacht-style charters built for big groups.** Search "party barge houston" and you land on us: two 30-foot boats on Clear Lake, capped at 26 people, BYOB, captain included. Neither one is the objectively better boat. They're built for different parties.

## Two Different Boats for Two Different Parties

Here's the honest split. Houston Party Boats and similar yacht operators run bigger vessels, 49 to 75 guests, usually with a bar service and a more formal event structure. We run Delta Dream and Mama Tried, both 30-foot Coast Guard certified barges, private charters capped at 26 people. Neither of us is trying to be the other.

If your group is 40, 50, 60 people and you want an open bar and a night that feels like an event, a bigger yacht is the right call. We're not going to pretend a 26-person cap works for a 60-person wedding party. It doesn't.

If your group is 8 to 26 people and you want the whole boat to yourselves, your own music, your own coolers, and a captain who just runs the boat while you run the party, that's us. That's the gap in the Houston market we built this whole thing to fill.

## Where Houston Party Boats Wins

Give credit where it's due. Bigger boats mean bigger groups, and if you've got a wedding block of 55 or a company party with the whole department invited, a 26-person cap just doesn't fit. A licensed bar on board also means nobody's in charge of buying ice, and for some groups that's worth paying for.

Gulf Coast Party Boats runs a similar model. If you're comparing three or four operators before you book, that's the right instinct. Just match the boat to your headcount before you match it to the photos.

## Where We Win

Small and mid-size private groups. That's the whole pitch. A bachelorette party of 15, a birthday crew of 20, a team outing of 22 people from the office. On a 75-guest yacht, a group that size rattles around a half-empty boat and pays yacht prices for it.

**Private charter on Delta Dream or Mama Tried is $800 to $950 flat, up to 26 guests, locked in with a $150 deposit.** Split 15 ways for a bachelorette, that's around $53 to $63 a person for the whole boat. BYOB means the drinks cost H-E-B prices, not bar prices, and there's no minimum spend hanging over the night.

Last month we had a 20-person birthday group that had priced out a yacht first. They came back to us because the yacht wanted a $1,600 minimum for a Saturday slot that size, and they'd have been paying for capacity they weren't using.

## The Real Numbers, Side by Side

| | Houston Party Barge | Larger Party Boats (49-75 guests) |
|---|---|---|
| Group size | Up to 26 | 49-75 |
| Private charter cost | $800-$950 flat | $800-$2,000+ |
| Cost per head at capacity | ~$31-$37 | Varies, often higher for smaller groups |
| Drinks | BYOB, no bar tab | Often a licensed bar, added cost |
| Boat access | You get the whole boat | Depends on booking, sometimes shared |
| Best fit | Bachelorette, birthday, small corporate | Weddings, large corporate, 50+ guest events |

The math only works one way once you know your headcount. Under 26 people, a barge is almost always the better dollar-for-dollar move. Over 30, start pricing the bigger boats.

## What Nobody Tells You About the Big-Boat Bar Tab

Here's the part that catches people off guard. A licensed bar sounds convenient until the invoice shows up. Drink packages on larger charters often run per-person, on top of the base charter price, and a 4-hour open bar for 50 guests adds up fast. Ranch water and canned cocktails from H-E-B cost a fraction of that, and on our boat, that's exactly what shows up in the cooler.

None of that makes the bigger boats a bad choice. It just means the sticker price on a yacht charter usually isn't the final number. Ask for the all-in total, drinks included, before you compare it to our flat $800.

## So Which One Do You Book

Count your group first. Under 26, book the barge and keep every dollar for the party instead of the boat. Over 30, go price the bigger charters and get the drink package cost in writing before you sign anything.
