# Night-Before Checklist

Confirm tomorrow's tours are locked in so nothing surprises you at the dock.

---

1. Open FareHarbor and review all bookings for tomorrow
2. Note headcount for each tour, flag any special requests or large groups
3. Check weather forecast: wind speed, lightning probability, visibility
4. Confirm captain is scheduled and has acknowledged the shift
5. Verify gear is staged: PFDs, first aid, cleaning supplies, cooler ice
6. Check that Bluetooth speaker is charged (if applicable)
7. Review any notes from today's tours that affect tomorrow

> [!tip] Wind forecasts can shift overnight. Check again in the morning before leaving the house.

- [ ] Bookings confirmed in FareHarbor
