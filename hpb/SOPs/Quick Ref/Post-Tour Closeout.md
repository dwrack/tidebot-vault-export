# Post-Tour Closeout

Wrap up the tour properly before you move on to the next one or call it a day.

---

1. Confirm guest head count off the vessel (match to boarding count)
2. Return all gear to its designated storage
3. Rinse any salt-exposed equipment
4. Store PFDs properly (hung to dry, not wadded up)
5. Complete an incident report if anything happened: injury, equipment issue, guest complaint, near-miss
6. Trigger review request to guests (automated or manual depending on setup)
7. Note any feedback, complaints, or observations in the team channel
8. Check fuel level for next tour or next day

> [!tip] A 30-second message in the team channel about how the group went can save the next captain a headache.
