# End-of-Day Sign-Off

Lock everything down at the end of the last tour of the day.

---

1. Secure vessel: lines tied, bumpers in place, engine off, battery switch off
2. Store all loose gear and equipment in designated locations
3. Rinse and hang PFDs to dry
4. Clean the boat: wipe seats, sweep deck, empty trash
5. Restock anything used: first aid supplies, cleaning supplies, trash bags
6. Check fuel level and note if a fill is needed before next operating day
7. Report any maintenance issues, equipment concerns, or damage to management same day
8. Lock any on-shore storage areas
9. Final walk-around: nothing left on the dock, nothing hanging off the boat

- [ ] Vessel secured (lines, bumpers, engine, battery)
- [ ] Equipment stored
- [ ] Boat cleaned
- [ ] Supplies restocked
- [ ] Issues reported to management
- [ ] Site locked and clear
