# Contractor Onboarding Checklist

Every new captain follows this exact sequence. No steps skipped, no shortcuts.

---

### Step 1: Culture Doc
1. New hire reads [[How We Run Things — Houston Pedal Barge]]
2. New hire completes [[Culture Quiz — Houston Pedal Barge]]
3. Manager walks through any quiz scenarios worth discussing

### Step 2: Collect Credentials
4. Verify USCG Captain's License (OUPV / 6-pak minimum)
5. Verify current CPR/First Aid certification
6. Verify TWIC Card
7. Confirm background check is cleared
8. Confirm drug test is completed and passed

### Step 3: Collect Documents
9. W-9 (signed)
10. Independent Contractor Agreement via DocuSeal
11. Government-issued photo ID (copy on file)
12. Copy of USCG license
13. Copy of CPR/First Aid cert
14. Copy of TWIC Card
15. Direct deposit or payment info
16. Emergency contact (name, phone, relationship)

### Step 4: Orientation (In-Person)
17. Full vessel walkthrough: engine, safety equipment locations, storage
18. Walk through Morning Inspection Checklist together
19. Walk through Guest Check-In Steps
20. Practice delivering the safety briefing out loud
21. Review emergency procedures and incident report process
22. Review weather hold thresholds and cancellation procedure

### Step 5: Shadow Shift
23. New captain observes an experienced captain for a full tour
24. No independent guest interaction
25. Debrief after: questions, feedback, go/no-go

### Step 6: Assisted Shift(s)
26. New captain runs the tour with experienced captain present as backup
27. Post-shift feedback from experienced captain

### Step 7: Solo Sign-Off
28. Manager or senior captain confirms all of the following:

- [ ] Culture doc read and quiz completed
- [ ] All credentials verified and on file
- [ ] All documents collected and stored
- [ ] Orientation completed
- [ ] Shadow shift completed
- [ ] Assisted shift(s) completed
- [ ] Safe departure and docking demonstrated
- [ ] Safety briefing delivery confirmed
- [ ] Weather assessment competency confirmed
- [ ] Guest management competency confirmed
- [ ] Waiver verification process confirmed
- [ ] Emergency procedure knowledge confirmed
- [ ] Post-tour cleanup standards confirmed
- [ ] Added to scheduling rotation
