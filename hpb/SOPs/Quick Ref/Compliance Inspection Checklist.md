# Compliance Inspection Checklist

USCG Subchapter T (Small Passenger Vessel) full equipment and documentation audit.

---

### Life-Saving Equipment
1. PFDs (Type I, II, or III): one per max capacity plus crew
2. Throwable PFD (Type IV): minimum one, on deck, accessible
3. Ring life buoy: if vessel is 26 ft or greater

### Fire Safety
4. Fire extinguishers: charged, inspection tags current, per COI requirements
5. Flammable liquid storage: compliant containers, away from ignition sources

### Navigation Equipment
6. Navigation lights: port (red), starboard (green), stern (white)
7. Sound-producing device: horn or whistle, functional
8. VHF marine radio: operational, tuned to Ch 16
9. Anchor: appropriate for vessel size, accessible

### Visual Distress Signals
10. Flares or approved signals: present, not expired (check dates)

### First Aid
11. First aid kit: stocked, accessible, checked within the last week

### Documentation On Board
12. Certificate of Inspection (COI): posted visibly, current
13. Certificate of Documentation or state registration
14. Captain's USCG license: on captain's person
15. Stability letter (if applicable)
16. Manning certificate

> [!warning] The COI is your operating license. Without it, the boat does not leave the dock.

### Annual Inspection Prep
17. Contact USCG Marine Safety Unit to schedule annual inspection
18. Confirm all equipment above passes before the inspector arrives
19. Have previous inspection reports available for reference
20. Review any deficiencies from last inspection and confirm corrected

### Compliance Calendar Key Dates

| Month | Task |
|---|---|
| January | Crew certification renewals (CPR, First Aid, USCG license) |
| February | Schedule USCG annual inspection |
| March | Insurance renewal review |
| April | Pre-season safety equipment audit |
| May | State/local permit renewals |
| June | Mid-season spot check |
| October | End-of-season gear inspection and storage |
| December | Document all certifications for next year |
