# Contractor Offboarding Checklist

When someone leaves, voluntary or otherwise. Nothing gets missed, nothing lingers.

---

### Day of Decision
1. Confirm in writing (or verbal, documented) that the working relationship is ending
2. If termination: document brief reason internally
3. Confirm final shift date
4. Collect company property: keys, radios, PFDs, branded gear

### Within 24 Hours
5. Remove from FareHarbor scheduling and dashboard access
6. Deactivate affiliate link if applicable
7. Remove from Slack workspace or relevant channels
8. Remove from group texts / WhatsApp
9. Remove from Google Drive shared folders
10. Remove from scheduling tool
11. Remove from DocuSeal (revoke access, keep signed docs)

### Within 7 Days
12. Update any state/local permits if the departing person was listed
13. Update Crew Roster and Credentials Tracker (mark departed + date)
14. Return or note expiration of any permit cards

### Within 30 Days
15. Calculate outstanding pay for completed shifts
16. Calculate any pending affiliate commissions
17. Issue final payment
18. If they earned over $600 YTD: flag for 1099 at year-end
19. Document final payment date and amount

### Vault Cleanup
20. Remove from any "active" rosters in the vault
21. Add to offboarding log
22. Archive (never delete) ICA and credentials in Drive

> [!tip] If someone might come back seasonally, mark them as INACTIVE instead of fully offboarding.

- [ ] Communication completed
- [ ] All system access removed (24hrs)
- [ ] Permits and roster updated (7 days)
- [ ] Final pay issued and documented (30 days)
- [ ] Vault updated and docs archived
