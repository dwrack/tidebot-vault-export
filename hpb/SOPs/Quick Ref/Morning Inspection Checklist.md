# Morning Inspection Checklist

Make sure the vessel is safe, legal, and ready before anyone boards.

---

### Engine and Systems
1. Check oil level
2. Check fuel level, top off if below half
3. Inspect and test bilge pump
4. Check battery charge and connections
5. Test navigation lights (port, starboard, stern)
6. Test horn / sound-producing device
7. Test VHF radio, confirm Ch 16 reception

### Safety Equipment
8. Count PFDs: USCG Type I, II, or III for max capacity plus crew
9. Confirm throwable PFD (Type IV) is on deck and accessible
10. Check fire extinguisher: charged, inspection tag current
11. Check visual distress signals / flares: not expired
12. Verify first aid kit is stocked and accessible

### Documentation
13. Confirm vessel documentation is on board
14. Confirm COI is posted and visible
15. Confirm captain's USCG license is on your person

### Boarding Area
16. Clear dock and boarding area of trip hazards
17. Wipe down seats if needed
18. Empty trash, replace bags

> [!warning] Do not depart with any failed item on this list. Fix it or cancel the tour.

- [ ] Engine and systems: all pass
- [ ] Safety equipment: all present and current
- [ ] Documentation: vessel docs, COI posted, captain's license on person
- [ ] Boarding area: clean and clear
