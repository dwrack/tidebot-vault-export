# Guest Check-In Steps

Get every guest checked in, briefed, and safely on board. No shortcuts.

---

1. Greet guests by name when possible as they arrive at the dock
2. Verify headcount against the FareHarbor manifest
3. Check waiver status in FareHarbor for each guest under their booking
4. If a guest hasn't signed, pull up their booking-specific waiver link on your phone or tablet and have them sign on the spot

> [!warning] Every waiver must be tied to the guest's specific booking. No generic QR codes. No boarding without a signed waiver. No exceptions.

5. Fit PFDs: check each guest individually for proper fit
6. Deliver the safety briefing (see Safety Briefing Script in Deep Read)
7. Ask about any medical or physical limitations
8. Offer a photo opportunity at the dock before boarding
9. Board guests and do a final head count before departure

> [!tip] Most guests sign the waiver pre-trip via the automated FareHarbor email. You're catching the ones who didn't.
