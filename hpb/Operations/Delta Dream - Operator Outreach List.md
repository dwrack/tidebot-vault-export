*Built 2026-08-12. Companion to `Delta Dream — Vessel Sale Packet (July 2026).md`. Live listing: https://www.boattrader.com/boat/2021-trident-3510-catamaran-10282194/*

> Hey [name],
>

>
> 2021 Trident 3510 Catamaran, 35 ft, USCG certified for 26 passengers plus 2 crew, COI in effect through May 2030. It's running commercially right now, not a project boat. Single Suzuki 90 outboard, covered roof, head, sound system, lights. Hull was repainted last month.
>
> Asking $179,500. Next drydock comes due February 2027 and I'm saying that up front.
>
> Full listing with photos and a walkthrough video: https://www.boattrader.com/boat/2021-trident-3510-catamaran-10282194/
>
> Happy to answer anything. Survey welcome.
>
> Connor Champagne, Manager
> Houston Party Barge
> (832) 974-0365
> info@houstonpedalbarge.com

One thing to say plainly in every conversation so nobody is surprised at closing: the COI does not transfer with the boat. The new owner files with their own OCMI, and the clean inspection history is what makes that fast instead of painful.
